Buying Clothes

You want a new jacket. It is a little too small. And you have an old jumper to bring back. Time to shop — and be polite.

The Scene 🎬

It is Saturday. You are in a clothes shop. You have two jobs today. First, you want a new jacket. There is a friend's wedding soon, and you need something nice.

You see a jacket you like. But there is a problem — this one is too small for you. You want to ask for a bigger size, and you want to try it on before you buy it. Second job: you have a jumper in your bag. You bought it here last week, but it is not really you. You want to bring it back.

The shop assistant is friendly. They want to help you — but they also want to sell you more things! So you must be polite, but also say "no, thank you" when you need to.

Warm up — talk for one minute

  1. Do you like buying clothes? Do you go to shops, or do you buy online? Why?
  2. Do you try clothes on before you buy them? Have you ever bought something and then taken it back?

Who's who

You
The Shopper 🧥

Friendly and polite. You want a jacket that fits, and you want to bring back an old jumper — with no stress.

The other person
The Shop Assistant 🛍️

Kind and helpful. They can find your size and take the jumper back — but they will also try to sell you more!

From memory — don't look up!

1. The shopper likes a jacket, but there is a problem. What is the problem, and what do they ask for?

2. The assistant tries to sell one more thing. What is it, and what does the shopper say?

3. The shopper wants to return the jumper. What does the assistant ask for? And how do they fix it at the end?

1. The jacket is too small. So the shopper asks for it in a bigger size — and the assistant finds a large one.
2. The assistant tries to sell a shirt too ("with the jacket"). The shopper says "No, thank you — just the jacket today."
3. The assistant asks for the receipt. Because the shopper is buying the jacket, the assistant takes the jumper's price off the total — an easy fix for both.

The Echo 🕰️

Five hundred years ago, in the time of the Tudors, you could not just buy any clothes you liked. There were laws about clothes. Only the king and queen could wear purple silk. Only rich, important people could wear red and fine velvet. Everyone else wore simple wool.

Your clothes told people who you were. They showed if you were rich or poor, important or not. You could not choose. And you could not change it.

Today it is very different. You can try a jacket on, buy it or not, and bring an old jumper back — all in one afternoon. A Tudor person could only dream of that!

Two questions to talk about

  1. The Tudors could tell if you were rich or poor from your clothes. Can we still do that today? Do clothes show who a person is?
  2. Today we can buy something and then take it back. Is this a good thing? Or do we buy too many things we do not need?
