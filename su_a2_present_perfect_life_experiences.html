Have you ever...?

A2 Level · Talking About Your Life Experiences

Last lesson, you talked about the past

You learned how to talk about things that finished a long time ago:

  • "When I was a child, I used to walk to school every day."
  • "My grandmother would make bread every morning."

Today, we learn a different way to talk about your life — for things you have done at any time, from when you were born until today.

Two questions to start

1. Think about your life. What is one interesting thing you have done?

2. What is one thing you have never done, but you would like to do?

Key Words — click to flip

These words help us talk about life experiences.

ever at any time in your life — used in questions: "Have you ever...?"
never not at any time in your life: "I have never been to Spain."
been to visited a place: "I have been to Paris."
tried tested or eaten something for the first time: "I have tried sushi."
abroad in a different country: "I have lived abroad for two years."
delicious very tasty, very good food

Common Past Participles — quick reference

Regular verbs: add -ed

  • visit → visited    cook → cooked    live → lived    try → tried

Irregular verbs: learn these — they don't follow a rule

  • be → been    go → gone / been    do → done
  • see → seen    eat → eaten    drink → drunk
  • have → had    meet → met    take → taken
  • buy → bought    lose → lost    win → won
  • give → given    ride → ridden    swim → swum

The Present Perfect — for life experiences

We use this when the exact time is not important. We just say it happened — sometime in your life.

The Pattern

have / has + past participle (the 3rd form of the verb)
  • I have visited London.
  • She has tried sushi.
  • We have lived in two countries.

Questions with "ever"

Have / Has + you / he / she + ever + past participle ?
  • "Have you ever been to Paris?" — "Yes, I have." / "No, I haven't."
  • "Has he ever tried Thai food?" — "Yes, he has."

Negative with "never"

subject + have / has + never + past participle
  • "I have never eaten snails."
  • "She has never seen snow."

Important: Past Simple vs Present Perfect

"I went to London last year." OR "I have been to London."?

Both are correct — but they mean different things!

• "I went to London last year." → Past simple. We say WHEN.
• "I have been to London." → Present perfect. The time is not important — just the experience.

Rule: If you say a finished time (yesterday, last week, in 2020) → use past simple.
If the time is not important → use present perfect.

Find the Mistake

Each sentence has one mistake. Can you find it and fix it?

1. "I have went to Paris last year."

"I went to Paris last year." → Use past simple, not present perfect, when you say a finished time ("last year").

2. "She has never ate sushi."

"She has never eaten sushi." → "ate" is past simple. We need the past participle "eaten".

3. "Have you ever been in London?"

"Have you ever been to London?" → We say "been TO" a place, not "been IN".

4. "I have see that film three times."

"I have seen that film three times." → "see" needs the past participle "seen".

5. "He have visited many countries."

"He has visited many countries." → He / She / It uses HAS, not HAVE.

Build a Question

Make a "Have you ever...?" question from these words.

1. you / ever / fly / in a helicopter ?

Have you ever flown in a helicopter?

2. you / ever / break / a bone ?

Have you ever broken a bone?

3. you / ever / lose / your phone ?

Have you ever lost your phone?

4. you / ever / meet / a famous person ?

Have you ever met a famous person?

5. you / ever / win / a competition ?

Have you ever won a competition?

Tell Me About Your Life

Answer in full sentences. Use "I have..." or "I have never...". Then I will ask you follow-up questions in the past simple — give me details!

1. Have you ever travelled to a different country? (Where? When? Who with?)

2. Have you ever cooked food from a different country? (What? Was it good?)

3. Have you ever lost something important? (What? Did you find it?)

4. Have you ever met someone famous? (Who? Where?)

5. Have you ever had a bad accident? (What happened?)

6. Have you ever stayed up all night? (Why? How did you feel the next day?)

7. Have you ever moved to a new city? (Where? Was it difficult?)

8. Have you ever tried a sport you didn't like? (What? Why didn't you like it?)

Game 1 — Two Truths and a Lie

Tell me three sentences about yourself using "I have...". Two are true, one is a lie. I will ask you questions and try to guess the lie!

Example: "I have ridden a camel. I have eaten frog legs. I have met the king."

Then we swap — I will tell you three things about my life, and you ask the questions to find my lie.

Game 2 — Find Someone Who...

Imagine you are at a party. You want to find people who have done these things. Ask me the questions:

Find someone who has...

1. ...climbed a mountain

2. ...swum in the sea this year

3. ...broken a bone

4. ...slept in an airport

5. ...eaten something very strange

6. ...won a prize

7. ...ridden a horse

8. ...written a poem or a song

Example: "Have you ever climbed a mountain?" → If I say yes, ask: "Which mountain? When?"

Speaking Task — Your Life Map

Speaking time: 3:00

Talk for 3 minutes about your life. Try to say at least 5 things using the present perfect. Mix in some past simple to give details.

Try to talk about:

  • Places you have lived or visited
  • Jobs you have had
  • People you have met
  • Food you have tried
  • Something special you have done

Writing Task — A Message to a Friend

Imagine you have an old friend. You haven't seen them for 5 years. Write a short message (4–6 sentences) telling them about your life. Use at least 3 sentences with the present perfect.

Start: "Hi! It's been a long time. A lot has happened in my life..."
