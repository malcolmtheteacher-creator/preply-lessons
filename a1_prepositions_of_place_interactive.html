📚 Prepositions of Place

Where is your phone? Where are your keys? 🔑 Use prepositions of place to describe where things are!

🎯 The Pattern

Subject + is/are + preposition + the + place

My phone is on the table.

The books are in my bag.

The cat is under the bed.

💡 Remember!

ON = touching the surface (on the table, on the wall, on the floor)

IN = inside something (in the box, in my pocket, in the room)

UNDER = below something (under the bed, under the table)

💡 Answering "Where" Questions

Where is...? → It's + preposition + place

Where are...? → They're + preposition + place

Where is my phone? → It's on the table.

Where are my keys? → They're in my bag.

🗣️ Describe Where Things Are!

🛏️ Your Room Tour

Give your teacher a tour of your room! Describe where everything is:

  • Where is your bed? (next to the wall? under the window?)
  • Where is your desk? What's on it?
  • Where is your TV/computer?
  • Where are your books?
  • Where do you keep your phone?
  • Where are your clothes?

🔍 The "Where Is It?" Game

Your teacher will ask you about things. Answer quickly!

  • Where is your pencil case?
  • Where are your headphones?
  • Where is your school bag?
  • Where is your favorite thing in your room?
  • Where is something you lost recently? 😅

🏫 Your Classroom

Describe your classroom at school:

  • Where do you sit? (near the window? next to a friend?)
  • Where is the teacher's desk?
  • Where is the board?
  • What's on the walls?

🌟 Combine with Lesson 10!

Use There is/There are + Prepositions:

"There is a poster on the wall."

"There are books on my desk."

"There is a cat under my bed!"

🏆 Final Challenges

🎯 Challenge 1: Find My Things!

Imagine you lost these things. Describe where you found them!

Example:

"I lost my keys! I looked everywhere. They were under the sofa! My phone was in my jacket pocket. My headphones were behind my desk!"

🎯 Challenge 2: Describe a Picture

Look around your room and describe 5 things and their locations:

Example:

"My computer is on my desk. My bed is next to the window. There is a poster on the wall. My shoes are under the bed. My bag is behind the door."

🌟 SUPER CHALLENGE: Complete Room Description!

Use EVERYTHING to describe your room!

  • ✅ Lesson 10: There is / There are
  • ✅ Lesson 11: Present Continuous (what's happening now)
  • ✅ Lesson 12: Prepositions (where things are)
  • ✅ Lesson 1: I like... (what you like about it)

MEGA Example:

"In my room, there is a big bed next to the window. There are posters on the wall. My desk is in front of the window. Right now, my cat is sleeping on my bed! There are books on my desk because I'm studying. I like my room because it's comfortable!"

🎮 GAME: Give Directions!

Imagine your friend wants to find something in your room. Give them directions!

Example:

"My PlayStation controller? OK, look on my desk. No, not there? Then look under the bed. No? Check next to the TV. Behind the books? Ah, it's probably between the sofa cushions!"
