Arranged Marriage: Forced or Family-Guided?

Arranged marriage and forced marriage are not the same.

Key words

Read the sentence. What does the bold word mean? Then click Reveal.

1. Their marriage was a big family event.
marriage (n) — the legal union of two people. Example: “Their marriage was a big family event.”
2. Her family came to the wedding.
family (n) — parents, children and other relatives. Example: “Her family came to the wedding.”
3. Young people can choose their partner.
choose (v) — to pick from many things. Example: “Young people can choose their partner.”
4. Both people must agree.
agree (v) — to say yes. Example: “Both people must agree.”

crime (n) — something the law says is wrong. Example: “A forced marriage is a crime.”

The stereotype: “In South Asia, all marriages are forced.”

The story

Part 1 — Two different things

In an arranged marriage, the family introduces two people. Then the two people decide. In a forced marriage, one person does not agree. This is a crime in India and in many other countries. The big question is simple: can the person say no?

Quick check:

1. What happens in an arranged marriage?

2. Is a forced marriage a crime?

1. The family introduces two people, and then they decide.

2. Yes.

Discuss:
  • Why is it important to say yes freely?

Part 2 — A changing practice

In India, many marriages are arranged. But today more young people choose their partner, or they choose and the family says OK. There are also websites that help people meet. In Thailand, Vietnam and the Philippines, most people choose their own partner, but they still want the family to like them.

Quick check:

1. What do more young people do today?

2. Do people in Thailand mostly have arranged marriages?

1. They choose their own partner.

2. No, most choose their own partner.

Discuss:
  • Do you want your family to like your partner?

Part 3 — Different ideas

People have different ideas. Some people say a family helps you find a good partner. Other people say it puts pressure on young people. Some couples are very happy, and others are not. The most important idea is that the choice is free.

Quick check:

1. What do some people say a family does?

2. What is the most important idea?

1. Helps you find a good partner.

2. The choice is free.

Discuss:
  • What do you think?

Grammar — questions with do and does

Do + I / you / we / they: “Do they choose their partner?”
Does + he / she / it: “Does the family agree?”
Short answers: “Yes, they do.” “No, she doesn't.”
