Something to think about

Tipping customs differ widely. In some countries tips are a significant part of workers' income and are almost expected; in others they are a small bonus, or can even cause offence. Defenders say tipping rewards good service, while critics say employers should simply pay fair wages.

Two sides of the same story

Read both, then tell me which argument is stronger and why.

Supporters argue…

Tipping rewards good service directly and gives staff an incentive to do well. Customers can show thanks, and workers in some places earn more than basic wages would allow.

Critics counter…

Employers should pay fair wages, and income should not depend on customers' mood or bias. Tipping can be confusing, uneven between jobs and uncomfortable for customers.
