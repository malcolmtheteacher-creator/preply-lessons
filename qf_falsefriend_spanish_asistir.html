🎭 Asistir ≠ Assist

B1 • Spanish/French/Portuguese speakers • False friends: asistir (attend) ≠ assist (help)

🔍 Let's notice what we see

Look at this sentence. What's the problem?

"I assisted the concert last night."

(The speaker wants to say they went to the concert, not that they helped run it!)

Does this sentence sound wrong? Why?

⚠️ The False Friend:

The Spanish word "asistir" looks like English "assist", but they mean DIFFERENT things!

In Spanish: asistir = to attend, be present (asistir a una reunión = attend a meeting)

In English: assist = to help (Can I assist you?)

Also applies to French "assister" and Portuguese "assistir"

🛠️ Understand the difference

📊 Spanish asistir vs English assist:

🎭 Spanish: ASISTIR

  • To attend/be present
  • Asistir a una reunión
  • Asistir a un concierto
  • Be at an event

🤝 English: ASSIST

  • To help/support
  • Assist a customer
  • Assist with a project
  • Provide aid/help

🚨 Attend vs Help

Key distinction:

asistir → English: "attend"
Example: "Asistí al concierto" = "I attended the concert" ✓
NOT "I assisted the concert" ✗
assist → Spanish: "ayudar"
Example: "Can I assist you?" = "¿Te puedo ayudar?" ✓
This is about providing help.

🚀 Now you try — no help!

Choose the correct word: attended or assisted. Check your answers when you're done.

1. "She _____ three classes and two meetings yesterday."
Answer: "She attended three classes and two meetings yesterday."

→ This is about being present at events.

2. "The doctor _____ the patient during her recovery."
Answer: "The doctor assisted the patient during her recovery."

→ This is about providing care/help.

3. "How many people _____ the annual fundraiser?"
Answer: "How many people attended the annual fundraiser?"

→ This is about being present at an event.

4. "The team will _____ with the project to ensure success."
Answer: "The team will assist with the project to ensure success."

→ This is about providing support/help.

✅ I can say 'attend' instead of 'assist' when meaning 'be present at'
