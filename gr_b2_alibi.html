Language Focus

When you tell stories under pressure, which tense do you reach for?

↓ Past simple vs past continuous in storytelling

Past Simple = completed actions, the main events

"I arrived at 7:15. I ordered a pizza. I left at 10:45."

Past Continuous = the background, what was happening

"While I was eating, Marco was telling me about his job. As I was leaving, it was raining."

Good alibi stories use both: past simple for your actions, past continuous for context and atmosphere.
