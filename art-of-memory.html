The Art of Memory

Thirty lessons on the oldest memory technique in the world. Each one opens with a true story, teaches a method you can learn in an evening, and ends with practice that proves it works. Written in clear English for learners and for anyone who wants a better memory.

Start with lesson 1 →

Two and a half thousand years ago, a poet walked out of a banquet hall a moment before the roof fell in. The guests inside could not be recognised. The poet could name every one of them, because he remembered where each person had been sitting. That story is the seed of everything in this course: we remember places far better than lists, so we put what we want to keep in a place.

The first five lessons teach the basics: how memory works, how to make a picture stick, and how to use your body, a building and a story as places for what you want to keep. Then come numbers, people, cards, speeches, words, formulas and ideas, and finally the habits that keep it all alive. Take one lesson at a time. Each takes about an hour. How every lesson works

1 · The Story

A short true story, or a scene, that shows the idea at work, with a question after each part.

2 · The Method

The technique in clear steps, with a worked example and a diagram.

3 · Try It

You build the pictures yourself. Models appear only after you have tried.

4 · Drill

Flash cards, a quick quiz and a timed memory challenge.

5 · Look Back

Questions from earlier lessons, what you can now do, and one thought to keep.
