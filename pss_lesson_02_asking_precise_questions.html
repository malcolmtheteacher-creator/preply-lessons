Lesson 02 — Asking Precise Questions

B1+ to C1 50 minutes Diagnostic focus

🔥 Hook: When questions fail

Situation: You ask questions but still don’t understand the problem.
Cause: The questions are too broad or polite.

Micro-task (2 minutes)

Ask questions to understand this situation:

“The meeting didn’t go well.”

Today’s output:
Control a conversation through questioning until the core problem is clear.

📚 Discover: Useful vs useless questions

“What happened?”
“What part of the meeting failed: preparation, discussion, or decision?”
Precise questions reduce the answer space.

Sorting task

Click the questions that would actually help.

“Can you explain?”
“Who made the final decision?”
“Why was it bad?”
“What was the original goal?”

🧰 Question tools

1. Narrowing
“Is the issue X or Y?”
2. Constraint
“What exactly was missing?”
3. Sequencing
“What happened just before that?”
4. Outcome
“What result were you expecting?”
5. Verification
“So the problem was timing, not content — is that right?”

🎯 Guided Practice

Click generate to get a vague situation.

Rules

  • Questions only
  • No advice
  • No opinions
  • Force specifics

🎭 Pressure Drill

Continue questioning while the situation becomes less clear.
Your goal is to keep reducing uncertainty.

⏱️ Timed Output

Control the conversation with questions for 5 minutes. End by stating the core problem in one sentence.
5:00

✨ Review

Key skill: good questions do the thinking for you.
Self-check:

Carryover:
Use verification questions in real conversations.
