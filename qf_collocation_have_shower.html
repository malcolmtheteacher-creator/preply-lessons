🎯 Have a Shower / Have Lunch

HAVE for daily routines | A2 Level

Let's see what you notice...

Read this sentence. What feels wrong?

I make a shower every morning.

What's the correction?

✓ Correction

I have a shower every morning.

📌 The Rule

English uses HAVE for daily routines: have a shower, have breakfast, have a break, have a nap.

Italian 'fare la doccia', Spanish 'tomar una ducha', French 'prendre une douche' — different verbs.

📚 The Rule (Dual Coding)

✅ HAVE + routine

🚿

have a shower/bath

have breakfast/lunch/dinner

have a break

have a nap

have a rest

❌ NOT these

🚫

make a shower ✗

do a shower ✗

take the breakfast ✗

do lunch ✗

💡 Tip: 'Take a shower' is also correct (more American English). 'Have a shower' is more British.
