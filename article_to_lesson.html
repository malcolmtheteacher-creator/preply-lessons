What you'll get back

  • 8 vocabulary items at the level you chose, each with a definition and a fresh example sentence
  • 5 reading comprehension questions — a mix of factual and inferential
  • 5 discussion / debate questions — open-ended, opinion-based, ideally provocative
  • 3 grammar / language focus points spotted in the article (passives, hedging, conditionals, etc.)
  • 1 "hot take" sentence — a single provocative claim drawn from the article that the student can argue for, then flip and argue against
