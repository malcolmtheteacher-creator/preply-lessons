[FORMULA] The 4-Paragraph Structure

PARAGRAPH 1: INTRODUCTION
"The GRAPH TYPE SHOWS/ILLUSTRATES/COMPARES WHAT in WHERE WHEN."

PARAGRAPH 2: OVERVIEW (CRITICAL!)
"Overall, MAIN TREND 1. Additionally, MAIN TREND 2."

PARAGRAPH 3: BODY 1
"DETAILED DATA POINT 1 + COMPARISON + SPECIFIC NUMBERS"

PARAGRAPH 4: BODY 2
"DETAILED DATA POINT 2 + COMPARISON + SPECIFIC NUMBERS"

CRITICAL: The Overview is 25% of Your Score!

The overview is NOT optional. Without an overview, your maximum score is Band 5. With a good overview, you guarantee Band 7+ in Task Achievement.

Introduction Phrases

[FORMULA] The Perfect Introduction

"The GRAPH TYPE ACTION VERB WHAT in WHERE WHEN."

PARAPHRASE THE QUESTION!

Never copy the question word-for-word. Change the structure and vocabulary.

Graph Type Words Use Instead Of...
The line graph... The chart / The graph
The bar chart... The chart / The diagram
The pie chart... The chart / The diagram
The table... The data / The information
The diagram... The process / The illustration
The maps... The two maps / The plans
Action Verbs (Choose One) Best For...
...illustrates... Any graph type (safest choice)
...shows... Any graph type (simple)
...compares... Bar charts, tables, multiple lines
...presents... Any graph type
...provides information about... Any graph type
...gives data on... Tables, bar charts

Time Period Phrases

  • ...between 2000 and 2020
  • ...from 2000 to 2020
  • ...over a 20-year period
  • ...during the period 2000-2020
  • ...over two decades

Location Phrases

  • ...in the UK
  • ...in five different countries
  • ...in a European city
  • ...across various regions
  • ...in selected nations

[EXAMPLE] Complete Introduction

Question says: "The chart below shows the number of tourists visiting Australia from 2010 to 2020."

You write: "The bar chart illustrates tourist arrivals in Australia over a ten-year period from 2010 to 2020."

Changed: chart --> bar chart, shows --> illustrates, number of tourists visiting --> tourist arrivals, 2010 to 2020 --> ten-year period from 2010 to 2020

Overview Phrases

CRITICAL FOR BAND 7

NO OVERVIEW = MAXIMUM BAND 5

The overview summarizes the MAIN TRENDS. It does NOT include specific numbers. It must identify 2-3 key features that stand out.

[FORMULA] The Perfect Overview

"OVERVIEW STARTER, MAIN TREND 1. CONNECTOR, MAIN TREND 2."
Overview Starters (Choose ONE) Notes
Overall, Simplest and safest
In general, Alternative to "Overall"
It is clear that For obvious trends
It is evident that Slightly more formal
The most striking feature is that For dramatic data
What stands out is that Alternative to above
Connectors for Second Point Use When...
Additionally, Adding similar information
Furthermore, Adding similar information
In contrast, Opposite trend
On the other hand, Contrasting information
It is also notable that Second important feature
Another key observation is that Second important feature

Overview Templates by Graph Type

LINE GRAPH Overview

  • Overall, [CATEGORY] experienced the most significant growth, while [CATEGORY] showed a declining trend.
  • It is clear that there was an overall upward/downward trend in [WHAT] over the period.

BAR CHART Overview

  • Overall, [CATEGORY] had the highest figures, whereas [CATEGORY] recorded the lowest.
  • It is evident that [CATEGORY] dominated throughout the period.

PIE CHART Overview

  • Overall, [CATEGORY] accounted for the largest proportion, while [CATEGORY] represented the smallest share.
  • It is clear that the majority of [WHAT] was [CATEGORY].

PROCESS Overview

  • Overall, the process involves [NUMBER] main stages, beginning with [FIRST] and ending with [LAST].
  • It is clear that this is a [linear/cyclical] process with [NUMBER] key steps.

MAP Overview

  • Overall, the area underwent significant development, with [KEY CHANGE 1] and [KEY CHANGE 2].
  • It is evident that the town became considerably more urbanized over the period.

TABLE Overview

  • Overall, [CATEGORY] consistently had the highest values, while [CATEGORY] remained the lowest.
  • It is clear that there were significant variations between [WHAT].

[EXAMPLE] Complete Overview

Line Graph about transport use:

"Overall, car usage increased significantly over the period, while bus and train travel showed a declining trend. It is also notable that cycling remained relatively stable throughout."

Describing Trends

THE MAGIC FORMULA

Trend = VERB + ADVERB or ADJECTIVE + NOUN. Mix both structures for grammatical range!

[FORMULA] Two Ways to Describe Trends

Structure 1: VERB + ADVERB
"Sales increased dramatically."

Structure 2: ADJECTIVE + NOUN
"There was a dramatic increase in sales."

USE BOTH structures in your essay for higher grammar score!
Movement Verbs Nouns
UP (big) increased, rose, grew, climbed, soared, surged, rocketed an increase, a rise, growth, a climb, a surge
UP (small) rose slightly, edged up, crept up a slight rise, a marginal increase
DOWN (big) decreased, fell, dropped, declined, plummeted, plunged a decrease, a fall, a drop, a decline
DOWN (small) fell slightly, dipped, edged down a slight fall, a marginal decrease
NO CHANGE remained stable, stayed constant, leveled off, plateaued stability, no change, a plateau
UP AND DOWN fluctuated, varied, oscillated fluctuation, variation
HIGHEST peaked, reached a peak, hit a high a peak, the highest point, the maximum
LOWEST bottomed out, reached a low, hit a trough a low point, the minimum, a trough
Speed/Size Adverbs (with verbs) Adjectives (with nouns)
DRAMATIC dramatically, sharply, significantly, substantially, considerably dramatic, sharp, significant, substantial, considerable
MODERATE moderately, noticeably, markedly moderate, noticeable, marked
GRADUAL gradually, steadily, progressively gradual, steady, progressive
SMALL slightly, marginally, fractionally slight, marginal, fractional, minor
FAST rapidly, quickly, swiftly rapid, quick, swift

[EXAMPLES] Complete Trend Sentences

Structure 1 (Verb + Adverb):
"Car ownership increased dramatically from 2000 to 2010."
"House prices rose steadily throughout the period."
"Unemployment fell sharply after 2015."

Structure 2 (Adjective + Noun):
"There was a dramatic increase in car ownership between 2000 and 2010."
"The period saw a steady rise in house prices."
"2015 witnessed a sharp fall in unemployment."

Numbers & Approximation

BE SPECIFIC BUT FLEXIBLE

Use exact numbers when clear, approximations when not. Never say "about 47.3%" - round it!

Type Phrases Example
Exactly exactly, precisely Sales were exactly 500 units.
Just Under just under, slightly below, nearly, almost Exports were just under 1 million.
Just Over just over, slightly above, a little more than The figure was just over 50%.
Approximately approximately, roughly, around, about Approximately 30% chose option A.
Range between X and Y, from X to Y Values ranged from 10 to 50.

"By 2010, this had increased to just under 50,000."

Process Diagrams & Maps

PROCESS = PASSIVE VOICE

Process diagrams almost always use passive voice. Maps describe location and change.

[FORMULA] Process Diagram Structure

INTRODUCTION: "The diagram illustrates the process by which [WHAT] is [PRODUCED/MADE]."

OVERVIEW: "Overall, there are [NUMBER] main stages, beginning with [FIRST] and ending with [LAST]."

BODY: Describe each stage using sequence markers and passive voice.
Sequence Markers Use For...
First, / Firstly, / To begin with, / At the first stage, Starting the process
Then, / Next, / After that, / Following this, / Subsequently, Middle stages
Finally, / Lastly, / At the final stage, / In the last step, Ending the process
At this point, / At this stage, / During this phase, Specific moments
Before this, / Prior to this, / Once this is complete, Connecting stages
Passive Voice Phrases Example
...is collected / ...is gathered Raw materials are collected from the field.
...is transported / ...is moved / ...is transferred The mixture is transported to the factory.
...is processed / ...is treated The water is processed to remove impurities.
...is heated / ...is cooled / ...is dried The product is heated to 100 degrees.
...is packaged / ...is stored / ...is distributed Finally, it is packaged and distributed.
