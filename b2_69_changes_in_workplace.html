Employment Vocabulary

Click each card to reveal the definition.

Redundancy Dismissal from employment when a job is no longer needed; severance payment given to dismissed worker
Zero-hour contract Employment with no guaranteed hours; worker is called in only when needed
Fixed-term contract Employment for a specific period that ends on a set date
Trade union Organization of workers that represents employee interests and negotiates with employers
Maternity leave Paid or unpaid absence from work granted to a mother after giving birth
Sick pay Compensation paid to employee when absent from work due to illness
Give notice Formally inform employer of intention to quit a job, usually with advance warning
Demotivated Lacking enthusiasm or motivation; discouraged or disheartened

Employment Meeting

You will participate in a meeting to discuss employment practices. Duration: 5 minutes.

5:00

Meeting Scenario

You are either a staff representative or a senior manager at a company. You need to discuss employment practices and reach agreements on improvements.

Staff Representative Perspective

You represent staff interests. Your concerns:

  • Staff dissatisfaction with current labour practices
  • Need for better working conditions
  • Desire for job security and fair treatment

Senior Manager Perspective

You are aware of criticisms about employment practices. You must balance:

  • Cost-cutting measures for business sustainability
  • Employee motivation and retention
  • Fair and legal employment practices

Meeting Goals: Ensure you leave the meeting with clear outcomes and action points agreed by all parties.

Useful Meeting Expressions

  • "I appreciate your concerns about..."
  • "We need to find a solution that..."
  • "What if we tried..."
  • "That's a fair point. Let me consider..."
  • "So, to summarize, we've agreed to..."
