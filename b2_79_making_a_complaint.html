Making a Complaint

B2 - Career Development - Apologising and Problem Resolution

Opening Discussion

Do you usually complain when you receive bad service? Why or why not?

Lesson Objectives

In this lesson, you will:

  • Make a complaint about a problem
  • Learn ways to explain problems and apologise politely
  • Learn vocabulary related to delivery services and phrases with look
  • Practise sounding sincere when apologising

Useful Apology Phrases

Use these phrases to apologise sincerely and politely:

I am really sorry
A direct, sincere apology
I am sorry to hear that
Showing sympathy for someone else's problem
Oh dear, I must apologise
Formal, expressing regret
I do apologise again
Repeated apology for emphasis

Pronunciation Practice

Which word in each phrase receives the main stress? This emphasizes sincerity:

I am SORRY to hear that

SORRY is stressed to show genuine sympathy for the person and their problem.

I must APOLOGISE

APOLOGISE is stressed, showing that you feel obligated to express regret.

Watch apologise again

DO is stressed to emphasize that you are genuinely apologising, not making excuses.

Customer Service Call Language

Three key sections to a complaint call:

Asking for Information:

  • How may I help you?
  • Can you give me the details, please?

Giving Information:

  • I am calling about some service issues
  • I will give you an update within two days

Apologising Politely:

  • I am sorry to hear that
  • Oh dear, I must apologise
  • I do apologise again

Phrases with "Look"

Look at the problem this way...

This means to consider the issue from a different perspective or point of view.

I will look into this for you

This means to investigate the problem and find out what happened.

Role Play Scenarios

Choose a scenario and perform the dialogue with me. One person is the customer, the other is the service representative.

Scenario A: Broken Equipment

Customer Role Card

You ordered a new photocopier and in the first week it has broken down three times. You want a replacement.

Service Rep Role Card

You work for customer service for a photocopier company. You can offer the customer the newest model.

Scenario B: Restaurant Damages

Manager Role Card

You are the manager of a restaurant. A company had a staff party at your restaurant and were very rude to your staff. They damaged three tables, five chairs, lots of glasses and plates. You want an apology and payment for damages.

Company Manager Role Card

You are the manager of a large company. You recently had a staff party at a restaurant and it was a disaster. You will pay for the damages.

Scenario C: Cleaning Costs

Office Manager Role Card

You hired a catering company for a staff lunch. The lunch was fine but the caterers did not clean up. You hired cleaners and want payment for them.

Catering Service Role Card

You work for customer service for a catering company. You have recently had new employees. You will pay for the cleaners.

Scenario D: Transport Failure

Office Manager Role Card

You hired a bus company to take a group of employees to a conference. The bus broke down and everyone missed the conference. You want a refund.

Transport Company Role Card

You work for customer service for a transport company. You have had mechanical problems with the buses lately and are getting new ones. Promise this will not happen again and offer a full refund.

Scenario E: Printing Error

Company Role Card

You hired a printing company to print 1000 copies of your company brochure. They printed a large number of pages upside down. You want a refund.

Printing Company Role Card

You work for customer service for a printing company. You have had problems with the printers and are getting new ones. Promise your customer that this will not happen again and offer a full refund.

Your Complaint Conversation

Prepare to make a phone call to complain about something. Write notes about:

  • What product or service you are complaining about
  • What the problem is
  • When it happened
  • What solution you want (replacement, refund, repair)

Key Tips for Making a Complaint Call

Stay calm and speak clearly
Explain the problem completely before asking for a solution
Use polite apology phrases when the company apologises
Get the name of the person helping you
Ask when you can expect a response or resolution
Write down reference numbers or important details
