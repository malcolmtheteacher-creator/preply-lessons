🎯 Strong Opinion / Heavy Traffic

Adjective + noun collocations | B1 Level

Let's see what you notice...

Read this sentence. What feels wrong?

I have a big opinion about this.

What's the correction?

✓ Correction

I have a strong opinion about this.

📌 The Rule

Certain adjectives collocate with certain nouns: STRONG opinion/accent/coffee. HEAVY traffic/rain/smoker. DEEP sleep/breath.

Students often use 'big', 'much', or 'very' where a specific adjective collocates naturally.

📚 The Rule (Dual Coding)

💪
