GROW Model: Goal Setting Framework

A structured approach to professional development

What is the GROW Model?

GROW is a simple framework for setting and achieving professional goals. It guides you through four key stages:

G = Goal

What do you want to achieve?

Define your target clearly. Be specific about what success looks like.

R = Reality

Where are you now?

Assess your current situation honestly. What skills do you have? What obstacles exist?

O = Options

What could you do?

Brainstorm different ways to reach your goal. Consider multiple strategies and solutions.

W = Will

What will you do?

Choose one option and commit to action. Create a concrete plan with specific steps.

Why Use GROW?

The GROW model helps you:

  • Think clearly about your objectives
  • Understand your starting point
  • Explore creative solutions
  • Make practical commitments

Speaking Task: Apply GROW to Your Goal

Time Remaining
10:00

Your Task

Choose a professional goal and speak for 10 minutes using the GROW model to structure your response.

Suggested Goals:

  • Improve your English speaking skills
  • Advance to a leadership position
  • Learn a new technical skill
  • Start your own business
  • Get a promotion within 6 months

Structure Your Speech

Spend about 2–3 minutes on each stage:

  1. Goal: What is your professional goal? Why is it important to you?
  2. Reality: Where are you now? What skills and resources do you have? What obstacles might you face?
  3. Options: What are 2–3 different strategies you could use? What are the pros and cons?
  4. Will: Which option will you choose? What specific actions will you take? When will you start?

Practice Questions

Use these questions to build your GROW conversation skills.

Goal Questions

  • What is your biggest professional goal for this year?
  • Why is this goal important to you?
  • How will you know when you have achieved it?

Goals should be specific, measurable, and meaningful to you personally. Share what drives you.

Reality Questions

  • What challenges might stop you from reaching your goal?
  • What skills and strengths do you already have?
  • What resources do you have access to?

Be honest about your current position. Understanding reality helps you plan better and avoid surprises.

Options Questions

  • What are three possible ways to overcome these challenges?
  • Who could help or mentor you?
  • What have other successful people done?

Brainstorm freely. Consider creative solutions and learn from others' experiences.

Will Questions

  • Which option will you commit to and why?
  • What are your next three concrete steps?
  • When will you take these steps?

Commitment and action are key. Be specific about what you will do and when.
