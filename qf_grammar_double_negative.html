❌ Double Negatives

Use ONE negative, not two | A2 Level

Let's see what you notice...

Read this sentence. What feels wrong?

I don't have nothing.

What's the correction?

✓ Correction

I don't have anything.

📌 The Rule

English uses ONE negative per clause, not two.

don't + anything (NOT don't + nothing)

can't + anywhere (NOT can't + nowhere)

Choose: negative verb OR negative word, but not both!

📚 The Rule (Dual Coding)

✅ CORRECT

👍

don't have anything

can't see anybody

didn't go anywhere

One negative only

❌ INCORRECT

👎

don't have nothing

can't see nobody

didn't go nowhere

Double negative (wrong!)

💡 Note for Multilingual Speakers: In Italian (Non ho niente), Spanish (No tengo nada), French (Je n'ai rien), and Russian (У меня нет ничего), double negatives are CORRECT! But English is different.
