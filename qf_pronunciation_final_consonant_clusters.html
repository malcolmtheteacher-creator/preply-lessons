🔗 Final Consonant Clusters

B1 • Italian/Spanish/Portuguese • Don't add vowels after final consonants

🔍 Let's notice the extra vowel problem

Listen to these common mistakes:

❌ "tex-a"

Should be: "text" (one syllable, close cleanly)

❌ "nex-a"

Should be: "next" (one syllable, don't add a vowel!)

Why do speakers add extra vowels? Are they creating new syllables?

🎯 The Problem:

Italian, Spanish, and Portuguese speakers add a vowel sound after final consonant clusters because their languages NEVER end words with consonant groups. They always close a word with a vowel.

But English words can end with multiple consonants:

🔹 -xt: text, next

🔹 -cts: facts, acts

🔹 -nds: hands, friends

🔹 -lps: helps, alps

Don't add a phantom vowel sound after these!

🛠️ Understanding final consonant clusters

💡 The key:

English can END a word with multiple consonants (a "cluster"). When you pronounce these words, CLOSE the word cleanly — don't add an extra vowel sound!

Common final consonant clusters:

-XT (consonant + consonant at end)

Close the word cleanly with both consonants.

text
next
context
sextant

-CTS (three consonants!)

Say all three consonant sounds without a vowel between.

facts
acts
objects
subjects

-NDS (consonant + consonant)

No extra vowel — close cleanly.

hands
friends
lands
stands

-LPS (consonant + consonant)

Close with both sounds.

helps
alps
yelps

❌ WRONG vs ✅ RIGHT:

❌ WRONG (adds vowel)

✅ RIGHT (no vowel)

text

❌ WRONG

nex-a

✏️ Guided practice

Say these one-syllable words (NO extra vowel!):

🔵 text — TEKST (one syllable, both consonants at end)

🔵 next → NEKST (one syllable, close cleanly)

🔵 facts → FAKTS (say all three consonants: k-ts)

🔵 hands → HANDZ (both consonants: n-dz)

🚫 Common mistakes to AVOID

❌ tex-a instead of text (adding vowel after xt)

❌ nex-a instead of next (adding vowel)

❌ ask-ed instead of asked (treating -sked as a new syllable)

❌ fac-a-ta instead of facts (adding vowels between consonants)
