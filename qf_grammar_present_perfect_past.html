🎯 Present Perfect vs Past Simple

Choose the right past tense | A2 Level

Let's see what you notice...

Read this sentence. What's the timing problem?

I have been to Rome last year.

What's the correction?

✓ Correction

I went to Rome last year.

📌 The Issue

"Last year" is a FINISHED time. When you mention a finished time, use Past Simple, not Present Perfect.

The structure "have + past participle" doesn't go with "last year", "yesterday", "in 2020"—use "went" instead.

📚 The Wall Timeline

Imagine a wall between the past and NOW. Which side are you talking about?

⬅️ Past Simple

📅 [PAST]

Finished time:

  • last year
  • yesterday
  • in 2020
  • last week

"I went to Rome last year."

➡️ Present Perfect

[PAST] NOW 🎯

Unfinished time or no time:

  • ever, never
  • already, just
  • no time mentioned
  • recently

"I have been to Rome."

Practice: Past Simple or Present Perfect?

1. I __________ that movie yesterday. (watch)

2. She __________ three countries. (visit)

3. They __________ in London last summer. (live)

4. I __________ ever __________ sushi? (eat) [Bonus with ever]

💡 Bonus Interleave: For/Since always go with Present Perfect (I've lived here for 5 years / since 2020).
