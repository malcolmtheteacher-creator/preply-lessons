Prepare Your Charity Appeal

You will prepare and deliver a persuasive appeal for a charity of your choice. Your presentation should be 2-3 minutes and include:

  • What the charity does and why it matters
  • A compelling reason people should support it
  • Specific actions you want listeners to take
  • At least three persuasion techniques from the language focus

Reflection & Discussion

Question 1

Have you attended a charity event, such as a fundraising gala, marathon, or community fundraiser? What was the experience like?

Question 2

What do you think makes charity events effective at raising money and awareness? Is it the event itself, the cause, or the people involved?

Question 3

Do you think involving a celebrity in charity campaigns makes them more successful? Why or why not?

Question 4

Do you know of any NGOs (non-governmental organisations) active in your country? What issues do they address?

Question 5

Do you think charities' work should actually be done by governments instead? What are the pros and cons of each approach?
