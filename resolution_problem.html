The Resolution Problem

(And Why "Selfish" Parents Raise Happier Kids)

Fluency | B1 | January 2026

Two Surprising Studies

It's January. Have you made any resolutions this year?

DISCUSS (2-3 mins)

  • Did you make any resolutions this year?
  • If yes - what? If no - why not?
Start with:
"This year I want to..." / "I don't make resolutions because..." / "Last year I tried to..."

The Research

92%

of New Year plans fail. Most people stop in February.

Source: University of Scranton, 2023
January 12th

"Quitter's Day" - the day most people give up. Why? They make wishes, not plans.

Source: Strava, 50 million users

Daniel, 38, business owner, father of two: "Every January I say 'I'll exercise more, spend more time with family, work less.' By March, nothing changes. The problem? 'I want to' is very different from 'I'm going to start on Monday at 6am.'"

DISCUSS (3-4 mins)

Daniel says: "I was making wishes, not plans."

  • What is the difference?
  • Think about something YOU want to do. Is it a wish or a plan?
Start with:
"A wish is... but a plan is..." / "For me, [X] is a wish because..." / "I think resolutions fail because..."

But There's Another Problem...

Katarina, 35, CEO, mother: "I know I need to take care of myself - exercise, sleep, time alone. But when I do something for ME, I feel guilty. My daughter needs me. My company needs me. Taking time for myself feels... selfish."

73%

of parents feel guilty when they do something for themselves.

Source: American Psychological Association

The Problem

Many parents think: "Time for me = less time for my children."

But the research says: NO

Children are 31% happier when their parents take care of themselves. Why? Happy parents = happy children.

Source: Journal of Family Psychology, 2022

BIG DISCUSSION (5 mins)

73% of parents feel guilty when they take time for themselves. But the research says it's actually BETTER for their kids.

  • Do YOU feel guilty when you take time for yourself?
  • What do you think about this research?
  • What would you say to Katarina?
Start with:
"Yes, I feel guilty when..." / "No, I don't feel guilty because..." / "I think the research is right/wrong because..." / "I would tell Katarina to..."
