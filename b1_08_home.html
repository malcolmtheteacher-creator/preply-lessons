Asking Polite Questions

Direct vs. Indirect Questions

In formal situations like booking with a travel agent, we use indirect questions because they are more polite.

Direct question: "What time does the flight leave?"
Indirect question: "Could you tell me what time the flight leaves?"

How to Make Indirect Questions

Pattern: Could you tell me + question word + subject + verb

Example: Could you tell me when you would like to travel?

Pattern: Do you know + question word + subject + verb

Example: Do you know what price the accommodation is?

Pattern: I'd like to know if + subject + verb (for yes/no questions)

Example: I'd like to know if breakfast is included.

Important: Word Order

In indirect questions, the word order is like an affirmative sentence:

Direct: "When would you like to travel?"

Indirect: "Could you tell me when you would like to travel?"

Notice: In indirect questions, we use subject + verb, not verb + subject

Practice: Write Indirect Questions

Turn these direct questions into indirect questions:

1. Direct: "Is there a gym in the hotel?"

Indirect: "Could you tell me if there is a gym in the hotel?" OR "Do you know if there is a gym?"

2. Direct: "How much does the aisle seat cost?"

Indirect: "Could you tell me how much the aisle seat costs?" OR "Do you know how much the aisle seat costs?"

3. Direct: "Does the flight have a transfer?"

Indirect: "Could you tell me if the flight has a transfer?" OR "I'd like to know if the flight has a transfer."

Travel Vocabulary

Flight Terms

Direct flight
A flight that goes from the starting place to the final destination without stopping.
Return flight
A flight that goes both ways: from one place to another, and back again.
Aisle seat
A seat next to the walkway (aisle) in the plane, making it easy to get up.
Window seat
A seat next to the window of the plane.
Transfer
When you change planes during your journey to reach your final destination.
Departure
The time when a flight leaves.
Arrival
The time when a flight reaches its destination.

Accommodation Terms

Self-catering
Accommodation where you cook your own meals (kitchen available).
Luxury hotel
An expensive, high-quality hotel with excellent facilities and services.
Budget accommodation
Inexpensive accommodation with basic facilities.
Facility / Facilities
Things provided by the hotel (gym, swimming pool, restaurant, spa, etc.).
Breakfast included
When breakfast is part of the hotel price, you don't pay extra.
Excursion
A short trip to see interesting places, organized by the hotel or tour company.

Dialogue: Booking a Hotel

Customer: "Could you tell me if breakfast is included in the price?"
Agent: "Yes, breakfast is included. Would you like a room with a view?"
Customer: "I'd like to know if the hotel has a gym."
Agent: "Yes, we have a modern gym and a swimming pool."
Customer: "Do you know how many excursions are available?"
Agent: "We offer five different excursions. Would you like to book any?"

Role-Play Activity

Booking a Holiday

You will role-play booking a flight and hotel with a travel agent. Choose your role and prepare.

Your Situation:

You want to book a 5-day trip. Decide:

  • Where are you going?
  • When (what month)?
  • Direct flight or transfer OK?
  • Luxury or budget hotel?
  • What facilities are important (gym, pool, restaurant)?
  • Do you want meals included?
Your Questions:

Ask the agent at least 3 indirect questions:

  • About flight times
  • About hotel facilities
  • About price or included services

3-Minute Preparation Timer

Prepare your questions and notes. Use the timer:

Preparation Time

3:00
Your Role:

You work for a travel agency. You have flights and hotels available. Remember:

  • Ask questions about what they want
  • Recommend the best options for them
  • Answer their questions about flights and accommodation
  • Be helpful and professional
Sample Options:

Flights: Direct flights €500-800, flights with transfer €300-500

Hotels: 5-star €150/night, 3-star €60/night, budget €30/night

Facilities: gym, pool, restaurant, spa, free WiFi, breakfast included

Useful Phrases:
  • "What are your preferences for..."
  • "I can recommend..."
  • "Would you like..."
  • "That would be perfect for you because..."

Preparation Notes

Think of different customer types and good recommendations for each.
