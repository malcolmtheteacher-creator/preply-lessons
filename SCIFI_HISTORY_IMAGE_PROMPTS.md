### 1. `FIG:hero-part1` — pathway hero banner *(16:9)*

## PART 2 — Evolution & the Writer's Craft

### 6. `FIG:hero-part2` — Part 2 hero banner *(16:9)*
> A wide hero image showing science fiction "growing up and outward": a single silhouette dissolving into many — different body shapes and eras — walking toward a horizon that splits into several futures (a wave/ocean for inner space, a neon city, a green climate-future, distant worlds). Indigo-to-orchid gradient, amber horizon line. Plural, expansive, hopeful-yet-uncertain.

### 7. `FIG:new-wave` — The New Wave / inner space *(16:9)*
> A surreal, psychological image evoking 1960s New Wave SF: a human head in profile opening like a doorway onto an inner landscape — a drowned, half-submerged city, drifting clocks, entropy. Ballard-esque melancholy. Muted violet and teal-grey, one amber light in the depths. Dreamlike, literary, unsettling.

### 8. `FIG:new-voices` — New voices / Afrofuturism & the global turn *(16:9)*
> A luminous Afrofuturist-inflected image: a figure in silhouette wearing headwrap-and-starlight, standing between an ancestral landscape and a gleaming future city, patterns of African textile becoming circuit-board and constellation. Rich indigo and violet with radiant amber-gold. Dignified, celebratory, forward-looking. (Semi-abstract, not a specific person.)

### 9. `FIG:cyberpunk-city` — Cyberpunk *(16:9)*
> A rain-soaked neon megacity at night, seen from a narrow street looking up — towering corporate towers, holographic signs (no readable text), a lone hooded figure below. "High tech, low life," the "used future": grubby, wet, alive. Violet/indigo base with electric-cyan and amber neon. Cinematic, moody.

### 10. `FIG:prose-anatomy` — INFOGRAPHIC: the writer's toolkit *(square or 4:5)*
> A clean infographic titled **"The SF Writer's Toolkit."** Four labelled panels, each a minimalist icon + short label: **NEOLOGISM** (an invented word on a glowing tag), **INCLUING** (scattered puzzle pieces forming a world), **THE 'USED FUTURE'** (a worn, patched robot/boot), **TONE** (a dial between a star of "wonder" and a storm of "dread"). Line-art, indigo/violet on cream, amber highlights, elegant type, airy layout.

### 11. `FIG:frankenstein-to-neuromancer` — INFOGRAPHIC: one fear across 200 years *(16:9, timeline)*
> A horizontal timeline infographic titled **"The Creation Turns on Us — 1818 to Now."** Five elegant nodes left→right, each a tiny emblem + year + one word: **1818 Frankenstein** (a stitched hand), **1920s Robots** (a clockwork figure), **1968 Androids** (a face half-human half-circuit), **1984 Neuromancer** (a glowing network node), **Now AI** (a smartphone glow). A single connecting line evolving from ink-stroke to circuit-trace. Indigo/violet on cream, amber nodes, refined and legible.
