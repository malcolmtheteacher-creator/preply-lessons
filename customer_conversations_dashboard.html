Difficult Customer Conversations

18 real workplace situations in 18 different industries — a declined loan, a late delivery, a client who wants something “safer” — each rehearsed out loud.

Nobody finds it hard to explain good news. The conversations people remember are the ones where you have to say no, not yet, or not what you hoped — to a client, a customer or a colleague you want to keep. Each lesson gives you the key words, the phrases professionals actually use, and a scenario to plan and play out loud.

Choose your own sector, or a different one: the language of delivering bad news travels. 10 lessons are at B2 (customers and clients) and 8 at C1 (senior advice, negotiation and pushback).
