Duolingo English Test — Your Practice Path

Five lessons that build to test day · Target: 120 (about IELTS 6.5)

The Duolingo English Test is a one-hour online test taken at home, scored 10–160. It mixes many short tasks — reading a sentence aloud, describing a photo, typing what you hear, following a conversation, writing, and more. There is no separate grammar paper: everything is speaking, listening, reading and writing. Your result is one overall score plus four sub-scores:
Literacy — reading + writing
Comprehension — reading + listening
Conversation — listening + speaking
Production — speaking + writing
Work through the lessons in the order below. The full mock is deliberately last.
