What's in the Box?

There are 6 sealed black boxes. Each one contains something real — a law, a fact, a statistic, a phenomenon, or a real-world situation. Something that actually exists or happened.

For each box, you get 3 clues, revealed one at a time. After each clue, you can guess what's inside — or ask for the next clue.

Once you open the box, the REAL challenge begins: you have to give your opinion on what you found. Is it fair? Is it smart? Is it surprising? Would you change it?

Quick Warm-Up
