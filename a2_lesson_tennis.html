Reading time 05:00

A Day at Wimbledon

Maria is sixteen years old. She lives in Italy. She loves tennis. Every Saturday, she plays with her brother on a court near her house.

Last summer, Maria went to England with her mother. They went to a famous tennis tournament called Wimbledon. It is in London. It is more than 140 years old.

At Wimbledon, the players wear white clothes. The grass is green. The balls are yellow. Maria thought it was very beautiful.

She watched two big matches. In the first match, an Italian player won. Maria was very happy! In the second match, an American player lost. Maria was a little sad for him.

The players are very fast. They hit the ball hard. Sometimes the ball moves at 200 kilometres an hour. Maria cannot hit the ball that fast — not yet.

Now Maria practises every day. She wants to be a tennis player, like the ones at Wimbledon. Her dream is to play there one day. Her mother says, "Practise, practise, practise. Then we will see."

Comprehension

1. How old is Maria?

b) sixteen

2. Where is Wimbledon?

c) in London

3. What colour are the players' clothes at Wimbledon?

4. Who won the first match?

a) an Italian player

5. What does Maria do every day now?

Present simple vs past simple

In the reading, we used two tenses. Today, Maria practises tennis (present simple). Last summer, she went to England (past simple).

Fill the gaps with the correct tense

1. Maria tennis every Saturday. (play)
2. Last year, she to Wimbledon. (go)
3. She two matches in London. (watch)
4. The players white clothes at Wimbledon. (wear)
5. Maria's brother tennis with her every weekend. (play)
6. Last summer, the Italian player his match. (win)
7. The American player very sad. (be)
8. Now Maria very hard every day. (practise)
1. plays   2. went   3. watched   4. wear   5. plays   6. won   7. was   8. practises

Quick rules

  • Every day, every week, usually, always → present simple (I play)
  • Yesterday, last week, in 2024 → past simple (I played)
  • Past simple of go = went
  • Past simple of win = won
  • Past simple of be = was / were
Speaking time 10:00

Let's talk

Part A — about you

  1. Do you like tennis? Why or why not?
  2. What sports do you play?
  3. How often do you play sport — every day, every week, or sometimes?
  4. Do you watch sport on TV? Which sport?
  5. Who is your favourite player or sportsperson?

Part B — past experiences

  1. When did you last play a sport? Where were you?
  2. Did you win or lose?
  3. Have you ever been to a big sports event? Tell me about it.
  4. What was your favourite sport when you were a child?

Part C — Maria's story

  1. Why do you think Maria wants to be a tennis player?
  2. Is it easy or difficult to become a professional player? Why?
  3. What advice would you give Maria?
Writing time 12:00

Writing task

Choose one task. Write about 80–100 words.

Option 1 — My favourite sport

Write about a sport you like. Answer these questions:

  • What is the sport?
  • When and where do you play it?
  • Why do you like it?
  • Who do you play with?

Option 2 — A sports day I remember

Write about a day when you played or watched sport. Use the past simple.

  • When was it?
  • Where were you?
  • What did you do?
  • How did you feel?

Option 3 — An email to Maria

Write a short email to Maria. Tell her about a sport you like. Ask her two questions about tennis.
