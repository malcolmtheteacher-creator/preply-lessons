Future Plans with "GOING TO"

Use "going to" to talk about your future plans and intentions.

How to Make Future Plans

🔮
I'm going to play video games tomorrow.
I'm going to watch a movie this weekend.
I'm going to study English tonight.

Pattern for Future Plans

I + am going to + verb
You/We/They + are going to + verb
He/She/It + is going to + verb
Examples:
• I am going to eat pizza tonight. 🍕
• She is going to visit her grandmother tomorrow. 👵
• We are going to play football on Saturday. ⚽
• They are going to watch a movie this evening. 🎬

Short Forms (Contractions)

I am going to = I'm going to
You/We/They are going to = You're/We're/They're going to
He/She/It is going to = He's/She's/It's going to
In speaking, we almost always use the short form!
• "I'm going to study" (NOT "I am going to study")
• "She's going to come" (NOT "She is going to come")

Question Form

Are you going to + verb?
Examples:
• "What are you going to do tomorrow?"
→ "I'm going to play video games."

• "Are you going to study tonight?"
→ "Yes, I am!" / "No, I'm not."

Specific Days

You can also use specific days:
• I'm going to study on Monday.
• I'm going to go shopping on Saturday morning.
• We're going to have a party on Friday night.
• She's going to visit me on Sunday afternoon.

More Weekend Questions

1. Are you going to study this weekend?
Answer: "Yes, I'm going to study on Sunday." / "No, I'm not going to study this weekend!"
2. Are you going to see your friends this weekend?
Answer: "Yes, I'm going to hang out with my friends on Saturday!"
3. What's the most exciting thing you're going to do this weekend?
Example: "I'm going to go to the cinema with my friends! We're going to watch a new movie!"

Speaking Practice

Discussion 1

What are you going to do after this lesson?
Talk about your immediate plans - what will you do in the next few hours?
Example: "After this lesson, I'm going to eat lunch. Then I'm going to do my homework."

Discussion 2

What are you going to do tomorrow?
Describe your plans for tomorrow from morning to evening.

Discussion 3

What are your plans for next month?
Think about any special plans, events, or trips coming up!
Example: "Next month, I'm going to go on holiday with my family!"

Discussion 4

What are you going to do during your next school holiday?
Talk about your holiday plans and what you're looking forward to!

Combining with ALL Previous Lessons!

Add more details to your future plans:

• What you like: "I'm going to play video games tomorrow because I love it!"
• How often: "I usually play on weekends, so I'm going to play on Saturday."
• Who with: "I'm going to watch a movie with my friends tomorrow."
• What time: "I'm going to study at 4:00 PM tomorrow."
• What you can do: "I can speak English now, so I'm going to watch movies in English!"

Challenge Questions!

1. What's something you CAN'T do now, but you're going to learn?
Example: "I can't cook now, but I'm going to learn next year!"
2. Compare your plans with your routine: What do you usually do on Saturdays? What are you going to do THIS Saturday?
Example: "I usually stay home on Saturdays, but this Saturday I'm going to go to the cinema!"
