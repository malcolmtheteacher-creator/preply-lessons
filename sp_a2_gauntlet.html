Warm-up

8 Structures — Quick Reference

Five from the Grammar Bingo, three new from the Grammar Flip. Check these before you start. You'll need all eight in the Gauntlet.

1 · Modal Verbs

modal + base form (NO "to")
I must finish. / She can drive.

2 · Present Perfect

have/has + past participle with already/just/yet
I've already booked the tickets.

3 · -ed vs -ing Adjectives

-ing = the thing / -ed = the person
The film was boring. I was bored.

4 · Reported Speech

said/told + tense shifts back
She told me that she didn't want to come.

5 · Used To

used to + base form (NOT past form)
I used to live there. (NOT lived)

★ A · Comparatives

short: -er than / long: more… than
bigger than / more expensive than

★ B · Too / Enough

too + adj / adj + enough / enough + noun
too tired / old enough / enough time

★ C · Question Tags

positive → negative tag / negative → positive tag
It's cold, isn't it? / She can't drive, can she?
