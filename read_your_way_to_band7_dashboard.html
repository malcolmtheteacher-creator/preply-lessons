Read Your Way to Band 7

Full IELTS workouts built from genuinely interesting texts — read closely, then practise the writing and speaking that grow out of each one.

Most IELTS practice is dry. This series isn't. Each lesson starts from a text worth reading in its own right — a classic short story, a piece of behavioural science, an essay on a lost human skill — and turns it into a complete exam workout.

You meet the real IELTS reading question types (Matching Headings, True / False / Not Given, Summary & Sentence Completion, Multiple Choice), then carry the topic straight into a Writing task and the three parts of the Speaking test. Vocabulary and the grammar that lifts a band are built in. Everything is pitched at Band 7.
