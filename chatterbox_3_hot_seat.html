The Hot Seat

Defend the indefensible — and win

You're on trial for an opinion

One motion. One minute. Total conviction. Nobody in this room agrees with you — your job is to change that, out loud, without stopping.

The motion

“Pineapple belongs on absolutely everything.”
Defend it like your life depends on it

No hedging, no “well, maybe.” Full conviction from the first word. When the timer starts, go — and don't stop until it ends.

01:00

Four motions. Win every one.

Take these one at a time. For each one: state your position, back it up, and knock down every objection thrown at you. Don't let a single one land.

Motion 1
“Mondays are the best day.”
Motion 2
“Cats are overrated.”
Motion 3
“Everyone should nap at work.”
Motion 4
“Small talk is a noble art.”
Go through all four, back to back

One motion runs into the next — no breaks. Every objection you hear gets an answer, not a shrug.

03:00
Reach for these: “The evidence is clear…” · “Consider this…” · “You might think… but…”

Argue against yourself

Pick an opinion you genuinely hold — something you really believe. Now argue the exact opposite. Not half-heartedly: make it sound like you've believed it your whole life.

Convince the room of the opposite

Name your real opinion first in one sentence, then spend the rest of the time destroying it from the other side.

02:00

Invent your own indefensible motion

Something gloriously ridiculous, something nobody in their right mind would agree with. The more indefensible, the better the defence.

Name it, then defend it to the death

State your motion in one clear sentence. Then build the case: reasons, examples, a rebuttal to the objection you know is coming.

02:00

Your finest closing argument

Two minutes to the jury. Pick your strongest motion from today, or bring back the very first one. This is the moment you leave everything on the table.

Deliver your closing statement

Build to a finish. Say the most ridiculous, most convincing thing you've got — and land it before the timer runs out.
