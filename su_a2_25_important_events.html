Negative Form (Past)

did + not + base form

  • I didn't come to school last week.
  • She didn't go to the party.
  • They didn't meet last year.

Regular Verbs (Past)

base form + -ed

  • play → played
  • watch → watched
  • study → studied
  • work → worked

Key Points to Remember

  • Irregular verbs do not follow the -ed rule: go → went, eat → ate, make → made
  • Use didn't + base form for negatives: I didn't go, she didn't eat
  • Time expressions with past tense: last week, last month, three years ago, recently, when I was...
  • Practice listening to vowel sounds in past verbs — they help you recognize the word quickly
  • Personal stories are powerful ways to practice and remember language
