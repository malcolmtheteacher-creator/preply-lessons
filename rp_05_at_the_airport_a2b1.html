At the Airport

You are at the airport. You need to check in, drop your bag, and get a good seat. But then the board says your flight is late. Stay calm, be polite, and talk it through.

The Scene 🎬

It is Friday evening. The airport is very busy. There are lots of people, and the big board shows many flights. You are flying to Lisbon tonight, and you need to be there by morning.

You go to the check-in desk with your bag. You want three easy things: to check in, a good seat, and your bag to go all the way to Lisbon. This part is simple. You are calm and polite.

Then you see a problem. The board says your flight is late — delayed. You are not happy, but you do not shout. You ask calm questions, and the person at the desk helps you. When you are polite, people help you more.

Warm up — talk for one minute

  1. Do you like flying? What is good about it, and what is not so good?
  2. When your bus or train is late, how do you feel? Do you get angry, or do you stay calm?

Who's who

You
The Traveller ✈️

Tired, but calm and polite. You want to check in, get a good seat, and — when the flight is late — some help and a clear plan.

The other person
The Check-in Agent 🛄

Friendly and busy. They can give you a good seat, take your bag, and help with the delay — if you stay calm and kind.

The Conversation 💬

Read it out loud, one person each, then change. Find the phrases from your toolkit. Listen for the calm, polite words.

AgentGood evening! Where are you flying today?
YouGood evening. I am flying to Lisbon, on the eight-forty. But I got a text about a delay.
AgentLet me look for you. Can I have your passport and your booking, please?
YouHere you are. I have one bag to check in. And can I have an aisle seat, please?
AgentOf course. You have seat 22C — that is on the aisle. Now, please put your bag on the scale.
YouHere it is. I think my bag is a little heavy. Is that a problem?
AgentLet me see… twenty-three kilos. That is fine! It is not too heavy.
YouGood, thank you. Can you send my bag to Lisbon, please? I don't want to take it in Madrid.
AgentYes, no problem. Now, about the delay — your flight is now at half past ten.
YouOh. That is a shame, but these things happen. How long is the delay — about two hours?
AgentYes, about two hours. I am sorry. Here is a voucher — you can get some food while you wait.
YouThank you very much! That is very kind.
AgentYou're welcome. Your gate is number 14. Please watch the board. Have a good flight!

From memory — don't look up!

1. At the desk, the traveller asks for two things. What are they?

2. The traveller thinks the bag is too heavy. Is there a problem? What does the agent say?

3. The flight is late. What does the agent give the traveller for the wait?

1. An aisle seat, and to send the bag all the way to Lisbon (so they don't have to take it in Madrid).
2. No, there is no problem. The bag is twenty-three kilos — the agent says that is fine, not too heavy.
3. A voucher for some food while they wait. The agent also tells them the gate (number 14) and says to watch the board.

The Echo 🕰️

In 1937, a pilot called Amelia Earhart tried to fly all the way around the world. She was two-thirds of the way there when her plane went missing over the sea. There was no board, no gate, no kind agent to help her find a new flight.

She flew with an old radio and small maps. She flew into big storms. Today, you wait safely inside, warm and dry, while other people work to keep you safe.

So next time the board says your flight is late, try to think of it a different way. A late flight is not so bad. Amelia would love the small problem you have tonight.

Two questions to talk about

  1. Flying is very safe now, but people often get angry about small things, like a late flight. Why do you think that is?
  2. Amelia knew flying was dangerous, but she went anyway. Would you like to do something big and a little scary? What?
