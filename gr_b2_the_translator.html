How This Works

Every language has idioms that sound completely bizarre when translated literally into English. You'll see the literal translation — and try to figure out what it actually means.

Each round: You'll see 3 idioms from different languages, translated literally into English. Discuss what you think each one means before revealing the answer.
Then: Try to think of the closest English equivalent — is there an English idiom that means the same thing?

Language Debrief

Idioms reveal how cultures see the world differently — even when they're describing the same human experiences.

Speculating about meaning:
"I think it might mean..."
"It could be their way of saying..."
"My best guess is..."
"It sounds like it's about..."
Comparing across languages:
"In English we'd say..."
"We have something similar: ..."
"There's no direct equivalent, but the closest would be..."
"It's like our expression '...' but more..."
Discussing cultural differences:
"It's interesting that they use [image] to describe [concept]..."
"That tells you something about the culture..."
"English tends to use [X] metaphors, but this language uses [Y]..."
