📁 Carpeta ≠ Carpet

A2 • Spanish speakers • False friends: carpeta (folder) ≠ carpet (floor covering)

🔍 Let's notice what we see

Look at this sentence. What's the problem?

"Put the papers on the carpet."

(The speaker wants to say put the papers in a folder, not on the floor!)

Does this sentence miss the real meaning? Why?

⚠️ The False Friend:

The Spanish word "carpeta" looks like English "carpet", but they mean DIFFERENT things!

In Spanish: carpeta = folder (una carpeta de documentos = a folder of documents)

In English: carpet = floor covering, rug

In English: folder = document storage container

🛠️ Understand the difference

📊 Spanish carpeta vs English carpet:

📁 Spanish: CARPETA

  • Folder for documents
  • File storage
  • Document container
  • Office supply

🟥 English: CARPET

  • Floor covering
  • Rug/mat
  • On the floor
  • Home furnishing

🚨 Documents vs Floors

Key distinction:

carpeta → English: "folder"
Example: "Pon los papeles en la carpeta" = "Put the papers in the folder" ✓
NOT "Put the papers on the carpet" ✗
carpet → Spanish: "alfombra/moqueta"
Example: "The living room has a red carpet" = "La sala tiene una alfombra roja" ✓
This is about a floor covering.
