Grammar Focus: Present Simple for Habits

Study these sentences. Notice how we use the present simple and frequency expressions:

"I log in to the website throughout the day."
"I also have a video chat with my tutor once a week."
"They only print articles once in a while."
"It's three times a week."
"I only cancel once or twice a year."

Check Your Understanding

1. What tense is used in these sentences?
Present simple
2. Why do we use the present simple here?
To describe routines and habits
3. What are the frequency expressions in these sentences?
throughout the day, once a week, once in a while, three times a week, once or twice a year
4. Where do frequency expressions come in the sentence — before or after the verb?
After the verb, usually at the end of the sentence
