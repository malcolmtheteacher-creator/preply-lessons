Travel Vocabulary

Click the cards to reveal definitions. These are essential words for talking about travel.

go sightseeing To visit interesting places and attractions as a tourist
take photos To use a camera or phone to capture pictures of things you see
go camping To stay outdoors and sleep in a tent, usually in nature
try local food To taste and eat traditional dishes from the place you visit
go by train To travel using a train as your transportation
holiday A period of time away from work or school for rest and enjoyment
trip A journey from one place to another, usually for a specific purpose
accommodation A place to stay, such as a hotel, hostel, or apartment

Travel Expressions in Sentences

Read these example sentences:

  • Go sightseeing: Last summer, I went sightseeing in Paris and visited the Eiffel Tower.
  • Take photos: I took many photos of the beautiful beaches in Thailand.
  • Try local food: When I traveled to Italy, I tried local pasta and risotto.
  • Go by train: I went by train from London to Scotland last year.
  • Go camping: My family enjoys going camping in the mountains during summer.

Warm-Up Questions

These are common conversation starters about travel:

Think about these questions:

  • What is your favorite travel memory?
  • Do you like traveling alone or with friends? Why?
  • Beach or mountains? Which do you prefer?

Grammar Focus: Past Tense Stories

Past Simple — Completed Actions

Use past simple for finished actions in the past:

  • Example: "I visited Bangkok last year."
  • Structure: Subject + past verb + object
  • Other examples: "I went camping in Canada." "We took photos at the museum."

Past Continuous — Actions in Progress

Use past continuous for actions happening at a specific time in the past:

  • Example: "I was swimming when it started to rain."
  • Structure: Subject + was/were + verb + -ing
  • Other examples: "We were eating dinner when the phone rang." "I was walking through the market when I saw my old friend."

Remember: Use past continuous to set the scene or show what was happening when something else occurred (past simple).

Understanding Travel Preferences

Modern Travel

Think about these questions about how people travel today:

  • Do you think people travel more than before?
  • What kinds of holidays are most popular?
  • How do people find accommodation? (hotels, booking websites, etc.)
  • What is your perfect holiday? Beach? City? Adventure?

Helpful phrases: "I think...", "For me...", "In my opinion...", "I believe..."

Dealing with Travel Problems

Common Scenarios

Here are situations that sometimes happen when traveling:

  • Scenario A: Your hotel room is not ready when you arrive.
  • Scenario B: Your flight is delayed or late.
  • Scenario C: Your luggage/bag is missing.

Polite phrases to use: "Excuse me...", "Can you help me please?", "I'm sorry, but...", "Could you please...?"

Cultural Awareness While Traveling

When you travel to a new country, it's important to show respect:

  • Learn about local customs and traditions
  • Be respectful if you make a cultural mistake (e.g., forgetting to remove shoes when required)
  • Know how to apologize politely: "I'm sorry, I didn't know about that custom."
  • Show genuine interest in how people live and what they do

Speaking Activity 1: Travel Conversation

Have a conversation with your teacher about travel. Try to answer in full sentences and ask questions:

  • Where would you like to go on holiday?
  • What's your favorite travel memory?
  • Do you like traveling alone or with friends?
  • What activities do you enjoy on vacation?

Target: Use complete sentences and the pattern "I like... because..."

Speaking Activity 2: Tell Your Travel Story

Tell a 2-3 minute story about a trip you took. Include:

  • Where you went
  • When you went
  • What you did (use past simple)
  • What was happening when something interesting occurred (use past continuous)
  • How you felt about the experience

Target: Use past simple and past continuous naturally to describe your experience.

Speaking Activity 3: Role Play A

Scenario: Travel Problem at Hotel

You are: A guest who arrived at the hotel and the room is not ready.

Your teacher is: The hotel staff.

What to say:

  • "Excuse me, I have a reservation..."
  • "Could you please help me with this problem?"
  • Ask when your room will be ready
  • Politely express your concern

Speaking Activity 4: Role Play B

Scenario: Cultural Awareness

You made a cultural mistake abroad (for example, you forgot to remove your shoes). Your teacher is a local person.

What to say:

  • Apologize politely: "I'm sorry, I didn't know about that custom."
  • Show genuine interest: "Can you tell me more about your traditions?"
  • Explain that you are learning
  • Thank them for teaching you

Speaking Activity 5: Your Perfect Holiday

Describe your perfect holiday in 90-120 seconds. Include:

  • The destination (beach, mountains, city, countryside)
  • Transportation (plane, train, car)
  • Accommodation type
  • 3-4 activities you would do
  • Who you would travel with
  • Why this would be perfect for you

Target: Use "I would...", "I'd like to...", "I'd love to..." and descriptive adjectives.
