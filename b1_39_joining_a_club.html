Predicting Information

Before listening, think about what kind of information you need. For example:

What kind of information goes in "Meeting Days"?

Show Answer
Days of the week: Monday, Wednesday, etc. You can listen for these specific words.

What kind of information goes in "Meeting Time"?

Show Answer
Times: 8 p.m., 6:30 a.m., etc. Listen for numbers and time expressions.

What kind of information goes in "Contact Phone"?

Show Answer
Phone numbers with digits: like "07700 913 284." Listen carefully for all the numbers.

IELTS Listening Skills

Let's learn about the IELTS Listening test and develop your comprehension skills:

IELTS Listening Test: Key Facts

How long is the IELTS Listening test?

Show Answer
Approximately 30 minutes of listening, plus 10 minutes to transfer your answers.

How many questions do you have to answer?

Show Answer
40 questions total (10 questions in each of the 4 parts).

Is spelling important?

Show Answer
Yes! Spelling is important in IELTS. If you misspell a word, it is marked wrong.

What kinds of conversations will you hear?

Show Answer
Everyday social conversations AND academic/professional discussions. Not just casual chat.

How many marks do you need for a band score of 6?

Show Answer
Approximately 23-24 correct answers out of 40 (about 58-60%).

Listening Strategies

Before Listening: Read the questions carefully. Predict what information you need.

While Listening: Focus on keywords. Write short notes. Don't write complete sentences.

After Listening: Check spelling. Verify numbers and names match what you heard.
