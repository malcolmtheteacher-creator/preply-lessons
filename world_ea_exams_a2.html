Exam Hell: Do East Asian Students Only Study?

Big exams and extra classes. But students also have clubs, friends and fun.

Talk first:
  • Do you like exams?
  • Do students go to extra classes in your country?
  • What do you do after school?

Key words

Read the sentence. What does the bold word mean? Then click Reveal.

1. The exam is in June.
exam (n) — a test at school or university. Example: “The exam is in June.”
2. Every student wants a good result.
student (n) — a person who studies. Example: “Every student wants a good result.”
3. He has a private class in the evening.
class (n) — a group of people learning together. Example: “He has a private class in the evening.”
4. The exam is very hard.
hard (adj) — difficult. Example: “The exam is very hard.”
5. She is in the music club.
club (n) — a group of people with the same hobby. Example: “She is in the music club.”
6. Too much stress is bad for students.
stress (n) — a feeling of worry and pressure. Example: “Too much stress is bad for students.”
Talk it through:
  • Which word is new for you? Say a sentence with it.
The stereotype: “East Asian students only study.”

The story

Part 1 — Big exams

China has a very big exam for university. It is called the gaokao. It is every June, and more than thirteen million students take it. In South Korea, the big exam is in November. On that day, shops open late, and police help students. Planes do not take off or land during the English listening test.

Quick check:

1. When is the gaokao?

2. What happens to planes in Korea during the English listening test?

1. Every June.

2. They do not take off or land.

Discuss:
  • What does your country do on exam day?

Part 2 — Extra classes

Many students have extra classes after school. About three in four students in South Korea go to them. Japan has extra schools called juku. Students say the stress is a problem. Governments are trying to help. China made new rules for private classes in 2021.

Quick check:

1. How many students in South Korea have extra classes?

2. What did China do in 2021?

1. About three in four.

2. It made new rules for private classes.

Discuss:
  • Do you think extra classes are a good idea?

Part 3 — More than books

School is not only exams. In Japan, students clean their classrooms and serve lunch. They also join clubs after school. Schools have sports days and festivals. Many parents want a lot from their children, but they also love them very much.

Quick check:

1. What do students in Japan clean?

2. What do students do after school in Japan?

1. Their classrooms.

2. They join clubs.

Discuss:
  • Which club would you like to join?

Grammar — more … than and the most

more + adjective + than: “The gaokao is more difficult than a normal test.”
the most + adjective: “It is the most famous exam in China.”
Short adjectives: harder than, the hardest.

1. Choose the word

1. The gaokao is than a normal test. (harder / more hard)

2. It is famous exam in China. (the most / more)

3. Korean students have classes than students in my country. (more / most)

1. harder

2. the most

3. more

2. Make your own

1. My school is ______ than ______.

2. The easiest subject for me is ______.

3. The most difficult exam I took was ______.

1. ...bigger than my old school. / ...older than the library.

2. ...English. / ...music.

3. ...maths. / ...my driving test.
