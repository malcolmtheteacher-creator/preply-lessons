Food Choices & Culture

Healthy eating means different things in different cultures. In the Mediterranean, it means olive oil and fresh vegetables. In Asia, it often means rice and lean proteins. In Latin America, beans and whole grains are essential.

Key Nutrition Vocabulary

Balanced Diet Eating correct amounts of all food groups
Processed Food Food that has been industrially treated or manufactured
Nutritious Containing nutrients that are good for your body
Portion Size The amount of food you eat in one meal
Whole Grains Grains with all three parts intact; higher in fiber
Organic Grown without chemical pesticides or fertilizers
Supplements Additional vitamins or minerals taken as pills or powders
Food Intolerance Difficulty digesting certain foods; different from allergies

A Nutritionist's Advice

Maria works as a nutritionist in a busy health clinic. She sees many clients who want to eat healthier but don't know where to start. "The most important thing," she says, "is not perfection. It's consistency."

Her golden rules are simple: eat more vegetables and fruits, choose whole grains instead of white bread or rice, and reduce sugary drinks. "Don't eliminate foods you love," Maria explains. "Just eat them less often and in smaller portions."

She also warns against falling for diet trends. "Every month there's a new 'superfood' or 'miracle diet,'" she laughs. "But the truth is, a balanced diet of real, unprocessed foods works best. And remember to exercise regularly and drink plenty of water."
