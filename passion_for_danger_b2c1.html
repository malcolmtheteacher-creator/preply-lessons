Verb Patterns Reference

Three Main Verb Patterns:
1. Verb + infinitive: "I decided to leave early."
2. Verb + object + infinitive: "She persuaded me to stay."
3. Verb + -ing form: "He avoided answering the question."

Prepare Your Monologue

Your task: Prepare a 2-3 minute monologue about something you're passionate about. Use the prompts below to help you plan what to say. Click each card for additional ideas.
🌱
When did you start doing this activity? How did you become interested in it?
Think about: a specific moment, a person who introduced you, a book/film that inspired you...
👥
Have there been any people who have encouraged you in your interest?
Think about: family members, teachers, friends, online communities, famous figures...
🔧
What does this activity involve?
Think about: equipment needed, skills required, time commitment, where you do it...
🌍
What is its role in society? (if appropriate)
Think about: how many people do it, professional aspects, cultural significance...
📖
Can you think of any interesting stories that have resulted from your interest?
Think about: memorable moments, funny incidents, challenges you've overcome...
❤️
Why is it important to you?
Think about: emotional connection, stress relief, sense of achievement, identity...
🤝
Does it involve you interacting with others? Have you made friends as a result?
Think about: clubs, teams, online communities, events, competitions...
🔮
What does the future hold, as far as your interest is concerned?
Think about: goals, improvements, things you want to try, teaching others...

Remember:

  • These questions are only suggestions - use your own ideas too!
  • Try to use some verb patterns from the Language Focus
  • Aim for 2-3 minutes of speaking
