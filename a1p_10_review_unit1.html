✅ Lesson 2: Where Are You From?

  • Countries and locations: I'm from Brazil
  • Nationalities: Brazilian, Japanese, French

✅ Lesson 3: What Do You Do?

  • Jobs and occupations: teacher, doctor, engineer
  • How to say your job: I'm a..., I work as a...

✅ Lesson 4: Numbers & Basics

  • Numbers 1-100 in English
  • Ages, phone numbers, prices

✅ Lesson 5: REVIEW

  • Consolidated lessons 1-4
  • Practiced integration and spaced recall

✅ Lesson 6: Family & People

  • Family members: mother, father, sister, brother
  • Words to show who: My, Your, His, Her
💡 Watch out: In English we say "my sister", never "the my sister". You use "my" or "the" — never both together.

✅ Lesson 7: Daily Routines

  • Action words: Wake up, shower, work, eat, sleep
  • Time expressions: Morning, afternoon, always, never

✅ Lesson 8: Food & Drinks

  • Food vocabulary: Pizza, chicken, pasta, salad
  • Likes/Dislikes: I like..., I don't like...

✅ Lesson 9: Where Is It?

  • Position words: IN, ON, UNDER, NEXT TO, BETWEEN
  • Location descriptions: "The book is on the table"
