Think Inside the Box

The grids, pyramids, loops and diagrams the world uses to think — and the true stories of the people who drew them.

A manager sorts a whole team into nine boxes. A president divides his day into four. A fighter pilot turns a dogfight into a loop, and a Japanese chemist draws a fish. Every one of these is a thinking tool — a simple shape that turns a messy problem into a few clear questions.

Each lesson tells the true story of one tool: who drew it, what problem they had, and what happened next. Then you use the tool yourself, learn one grammar point the tool needs, and finish by applying it to your own life and work. Start with the map, or pick any card — the lessons can be taken in any order.
