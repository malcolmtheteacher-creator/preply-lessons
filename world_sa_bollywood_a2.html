Bollywood: Song, Dance and So Much More

India makes a lot of films. Songs and dances are only one part.

Today: you will learn about Indian films, and use adjectives to describe them.
Talk first:

  • Which films from India do you know?

Key words

Read the sentence. What does the bold word mean? Then click Reveal.

1. She loves a funny film.
film (n) — a story on a big screen. Example: “She loves a funny film.”
2. The song is very popular.
song (n) — words and music. Example: “The song is very popular.”
3. The dance has bright colours.
dance (n) — movement to music. Example: “The dance has bright colours.”
4. He is a famous star.
star (n) — a very famous actor or singer. Example: “He is a famous star.”
5. Films are made in many languages.
language (n) — the words people speak. Example: “Films are made in many languages.”
6. The director won a prize.
director (n) — the person who leads a film. Example: “The director won a prize.”

  • Which word is new for you? Say a sentence with it.
The stereotype: “All Indian films are the same: songs and dances.”

The story

Part 1 — What is Bollywood?

Bollywood is the Hindi film industry in Mumbai. The name comes from “Bombay” and “Hollywood”. But India makes films in more than twenty languages. Every year, India makes about two thousand films. That is more than any other country.

Quick check:

1. Where is Bollywood?

2. How many languages are used in Indian films?

1. In Mumbai.

2. More than twenty.

Discuss:
  • Which language would you like to hear in a film?

Part 2 — Why songs?

Indian films have had songs since the first sound film in 1931. There is an old tradition of music and stories. Actors often move their lips to the voice of a famous singer. Films are long, and there is a break in the middle. A film often has romance, comedy, drama and action together.

Quick check:

1. When was the first Indian sound film?

2. What is in a typical film?

1. 1931.

2. Romance, comedy, drama and action.

Discuss:
  • Do you like long films?

Part 3 — More than Bollywood

There are also quiet, serious films. A famous director, Satyajit Ray, made the film Pather Panchali in 1955. In 2023, the film RRR won an Oscar for a song. There are also good films in Thailand, Indonesia and the Philippines. Bollywood is only one part of the story.

Quick check:

1. Who made Pather Panchali?

2. Which film won an Oscar for a song in 2023?

1. Satyajit Ray.

2. RRR.

Discuss:
  • Which film would you like to see?

Grammar — adjectives

Adjectives go before a noun or after be.
“a colourful film” · “The song is famous.”
We do not add -s to adjectives: “two famous stars”.

1. Choose the word

1. It is a film. (colourful / colourfuls)

2. The songs are very . (famous / famously)

3. She is a director. (good / goods)

1. colourful

2. famous

3. good

2. Make your own

1. My favourite film is ______.

2. A good film has ______ songs.

3. The people in the film are ______.

1. ...funny. / ...exciting.

2. ...beautiful. / ...happy.

3. ...friendly. / ...interesting.
