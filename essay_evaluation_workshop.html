Criterion B: Analysis & Evaluation

5 marks

"Does this essay analyze HOW the author's choices create meaning?"

This is where most marks are lost. Students name techniques but don't explain their effect. "The author uses a metaphor" = band 2. "The metaphor creates X effect, suggesting Y" = band 5.

Band Comparison

Band 1-2

"Descriptive. Little relevant analysis. May identify techniques but doesn't explain effect."

Band 4-5

"Consistently insightful analysis. Evaluates HOW authorial choices shape meaning."

Self-Assessment Checklist

The Test

Find ONE sentence in your essay where you analyze a technique. Does it follow this pattern?

[Technique] + [Effect] + [Meaning]

"The metaphor creates a sense of suffocation, suggesting guilt is inescapable."

Criterion D: Language

5 marks

"Is the language clear, accurate, and appropriate for academic writing?"

Band Comparison

Band 1-2

"Language rarely clear. Many errors. Little sense of register."

Band 4-5

"Clear, varied, precise. Very few errors. Effective register and style."

Self-Assessment Checklist

Language Red Flags

Check your essay for these common issues:

Avoid:

• "This shows..." (too vague)

• "The reader feels..." (assumes reader response)

• "In today's society..." (cliche)

• "Since the beginning of time..." (filler)

• First person "I think..." (unless specifically allowed)
