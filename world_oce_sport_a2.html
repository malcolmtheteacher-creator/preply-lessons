Sport as a Way of Life: More Than Cricket and Surfing

Sport is very important in Oceania. People play many different sports.

Talk first:
  • Which sport do you like?
  • Do you play sport or watch it?
  • Who is your favourite player?

Key words

Read the sentence. What does the bold word mean? Then click Reveal.

1. Rugby is very popular in New Zealand.
rugby (n) — a team sport with an oval ball. Example: “Rugby is very popular in New Zealand.”
2. Many Australians play cricket in summer.
cricket (n) — a sport with a bat and a ball. Example: “Many Australians play cricket in summer.”
3. The team wins many games.
team (n) — a group of people who play together. Example: “The team wins many games.”
4. The match starts at three o'clock.
match (n) — a game between two teams. Example: “The match starts at three o'clock.”
5. A fan shouts and sings.
fan (n) — a person who loves a team or a player. Example: “A fan shouts and sings.”
6. The best player comes from Fiji.
player (n) — a person who plays a sport. Example: “The best player comes from Fiji.”

The stereotype: “Everybody in Australia and New Zealand is a surfer.”

The story

Part 1 — Many sports

Sport is very popular in Australia and New Zealand, but people do not all play the same sport. In Australia, many people play cricket. In Melbourne, people love Australian football. In New Zealand, people love rugby. Not everybody surfs. Most people live in cities, and they play many different sports.

Quick check:

1. Which sport do people love in New Zealand?

2. Does everybody surf?

1. Rugby.

2. No.

Discuss:
  • Which sport is popular in your country?

Part 2 — Small islands

The Pacific Islands have very good rugby players. Fiji has fewer than a million people. In 2016, the Fiji team won an Olympic gold medal in rugby sevens. It was the first Olympic medal for the country. Many players in the New Zealand and Australian teams have family from Samoa, Tonga and Fiji.

Quick check:

1. What did Fiji win in 2016?

2. Which islands have family of many players?

1. An Olympic gold medal.

2. Samoa, Tonga and Fiji.

Discuss:
  • Which small country is good at sport?

Part 3 — Sport and people

In 2000, Cathy Freeman, an Aboriginal runner, lit the flame at the Olympic Games in Sydney. Then she won the 400 metres. Today, the women's teams are also growing. A fan is watching a match now. A girl is playing netball in the park. Sport is for everybody.

Quick check:

1. Who lit the flame in Sydney in 2000?

2. What is the girl playing?

1. Cathy Freeman.

2. Netball.

Discuss:
  • Who do you play sport with?

Grammar — present simple and present continuous

Present simple for habits: “She plays netball every Saturday.”
Present continuous for now: “She is playing netball at the moment.”
Words: every day, usually (simple) and now, at the moment (continuous).
