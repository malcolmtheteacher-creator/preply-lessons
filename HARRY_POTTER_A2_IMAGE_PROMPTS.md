**Look for the whole set:** refined editorial illustration, like a magazine or a good travel guide. Muted jewel colours (deep purple, forest green, burgundy, gold, cream), soft shading, clean lines, white or warm cream background. Adult characters only. No cartoon faces, no bubbly fonts, no stickers, no childish decoration. Labels in a clean, elegant sans-serif font in dark grey.
