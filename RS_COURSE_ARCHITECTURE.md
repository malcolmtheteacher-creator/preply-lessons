## PROJECT OVERVIEW

A complete, interactive Religious Studies curriculum from Year 7 to Year 13, delivered as self-contained HTML lesson files. Each lesson is designed for classroom use (teacher-led with student interaction) or independent study. Lessons are pedagogically grounded in retrieval practice, dual coding, and active student engagement.

### What Makes This Different

These are not textbook summaries with comprehension questions. Each lesson:

- Opens with a genuine question students want to answer
- Presents beliefs/practices through primary sources (scripture, testimony, lived experience)
- Requires students to think theologically, philosophically, and ethically
- Builds exam skills progressively from Y7 (informal evaluation) to Y13 (synoptic essays)
- Treats every worldview with intellectual seriousness and respect
- Includes diverse voices within traditions (not monolithic "Christians believe...")

## CURRICULUM MAP

### Phase 1: KS3 Foundation (Years 7–9)
**~108 lessons | 36 per year | 12 per term | 1 per week**

KS3 builds the knowledge base and thinking skills that GCSE and A-Level depend on. It covers all six major world religions plus Humanism, organised thematically with increasing sophistication.

### Phase 2: GCSE (Years 10–11)
**~72 lessons | 36 per year | ~2 per week across the year**

Directly mapped to AQA RS A (8062): Christianity + Islam beliefs/teachings/practices, plus four thematic studies. Exam technique is embedded from lesson 1, not bolted on at the end.

### Phase 3: A-Level (Years 12–13)
**~72 lessons | 36 per year | Plus independent study guides**

Mapped to AQA RS (7062): Philosophy of Religion, Ethics, Study of Christianity, and Dialogues. Requires genuine philosophical engagement, not recall of positions.

## PHASE 1: KS3 (YEARS 7–9)

### Design Principles for KS3
- **Thematic approach**: Organised by Big Questions, not "here's everything about Buddhism"
- **Progressive complexity**: Y7 = encounter & describe; Y8 = compare & explain; Y9 = evaluate & connect
- **All six religions + Humanism**: Depth over breadth — 3-4 traditions per unit, rotated to ensure full coverage by end of Y9
- **No assumed faith position**: Lessons are academically rigorous, not devotional
- **Disciplinary methods**: Students learn to think theologically, philosophically, and through social science — not just "learn about religions"

## LESSON STRUCTURE (All Years)

### HTML Lesson Template

Every lesson follows a consistent structure adapted for RE:

```
┌─────────────────────────────────────────┐
│  HEADER: Series badge, Lesson title,    │
│  Year group, Unit, Lesson X of Y        │
│  Learning Intention (one sentence)       │
├─────────────────────────────────────────┤
│  TAB BAR: [Think] [Encounter] [Explore] │
│  [Apply] [Evaluate] [Review]            │
├─────────────────────────────────────────┤
│  TAB CONTENT (see below)                │
└─────────────────────────────────────────┘
```

### Tab Structure

| Tab | Name | Purpose | Time |
|-----|------|---------|------|
| 1 | **Think** | Retrieval & activation — what do you already know/think? | 5 min |
| 2 | **Encounter** | New content: beliefs, sources, testimony, data. Dual-coded (visual + text). Small chunks. | 10 min |
| 3 | **Explore** | Guided investigation: scripture analysis, case studies, diverse perspectives within traditions | 10 min |
| 4 | **Apply** | Student production: discussion questions, creative tasks, real-world connections | 10 min |
| 5 | **Evaluate** | Critical thinking: "To what extent...?" questions, building evaluation skills progressively | 10 min |
| 6 | **Review** | Retrieval from memory + metacognition: "What did you learn? What helped?" | 5 min |

### Progressive Evaluation Skill

| Year | Evaluation Expectation |
|------|----------------------|
| Y7 | "I think... because..." (opinion with one reason) |
| Y8 | "Some people think... Others think... I think... because..." (multiple perspectives) |
| Y9 | Structured paragraph with evidence from teachings + counter-argument |
| Y10 | AQA 12-mark structure: argument + counter + evaluation with scripture |
| Y11 | Polished 12-mark answers under timed conditions |
| Y12 | Extended essay: sustained argument with scholars, 25-mark format |
| Y13 | Synoptic essays connecting philosophy, ethics, and theology |
