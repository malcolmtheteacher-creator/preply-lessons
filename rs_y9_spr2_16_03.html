Divorce and Family Breakdown

Christian Perspectives on Divorce

Catholic: Divorce forbidden. "What God has joined together, let no one separate."

  • Indissoluble lifetime sacrament.
  • Cannot remarry; only annulment possible.

Protestant: Most accept divorce as regrettable reality.

  • Bible permits divorce for infidelity/abandonment.
  • God's grace allows forgiveness and new relationships.

Islamic Perspectives on Divorce

Islam permits divorce but: "Of all permissible things, divorce is most disliked by God."

Process: Spoken three times over months, allowing reconciliation.

Women's protection: Retain mahr; custody of young children; men provide support.

Jewish Perspectives on Divorce

Get (divorce document): Husband gives to wife; both must accept freely.

Problem: If husband refuses, woman cannot remarry in Jewish law.

Modern: Reform/Conservative Judaism protect women by allowing women rabbis and civil recognition.

Secular/Legal Perspectives

Divorce is a legal process. Children's welfare is priority. No moral judgment; divorce is normal part of relationships.

No-fault divorce: Many countries allow divorce without proving wrongdoing.

Deeper Investigation: Family Breakdown & Suffering

Conservative Religious View: Divorce Causes Damage

"Divorce tears families apart. Children suffer. Marriage vows are sacred. Support troubled marriages, not abandonment."

Progressive Religious View: Compassion Over Rules

"Forcing people to stay in broken marriages violates dignity. Sometimes the loving act is to let go."

Secular View: Practical & Individual

"Relationships end. Goal is fair management: protecting children, just asset division, helping move forward."

A Case Study: Jamal & Sofia

Jamal (Muslim) and Sofia (Catholic) married civilly. After 5 years, unhappy and considering divorce.

Islamic: permissible but discouraged, try mediation. Catholic: offer support but no remarriage approval. Secular: both can divorce via civil law, focus on child/assets.
