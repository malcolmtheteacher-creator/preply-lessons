Which one is stronger?

A: "The workers stopped work. They stood outside the building and said, 'We will not go back until you talk to us.'"

B: "The workers wrote a letter. They asked the boss to talk to them. They waited two weeks."

SPEAK Which action is stronger? What can go wrong with each one?
A is stronger because the work stops now, and the boss must listen. But the workers lose money, and the boss can get angry. B is safer, but the boss can say no or do nothing.

    Two opinions

    Read both. Which opinion is stronger for you? Why?

    Some people say…

    Ordinary people need a way to be heard. A protest makes leaders listen. Many good changes began with a protest.

    Other people say…

    A protest can hurt people who did nothing wrong. It is better to vote and talk. Sometimes anger makes the problem worse.
