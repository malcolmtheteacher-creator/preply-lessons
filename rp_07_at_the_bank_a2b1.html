At the Bank

Your card stopped working in a shop yesterday. You have money in the bank, so why? This morning, you go to the bank to ask — and to get help.

This is the easier version. Want the harder one? → Full B2/C1 version →

The Scene 🎬

Yesterday you were in a shop. You wanted to pay with your card. But the card said "no" — it did not work! You had money in the bank, so you did not understand. It was not a nice feeling.

You do not know why your card stopped. Maybe the bank stopped it. Banks sometimes do this to keep your money safe. But you need your card, so you must ask.

This morning, you go to the bank. A person works there — the bank clerk. They are friendly and want to help. You ask a simple question: "Why doesn't my card work?" And you ask for help. Stay calm and polite — it is an easy problem to fix.

Warm up — talk for one minute

  1. Do you use a card, or cash, or your phone to pay? Which do you like best? Why?
  2. Has a card or a machine ever said "no" to you at a bad time? What did you do?

Who's who

You
The Customer 💳

Calm and polite. Your card stopped working. You want to know why, and you want to use your card again.

The other person
The Bank Clerk 🏦

Kind and helpful. They can look at your account, tell you why, start your card again, and send you a new one.

First: read the conversation in the next tab. Look at the polite words: please, thank you, can you help me, can I ask. These small words help a lot.

The Conversation 💬

Read it out loud, one person each — then change. Listen for the polite words.

Bank clerkGood morning! How can I help you?
YouGood morning. Can you help me, please? There is a problem with my card.
Bank clerkOh, I am sorry. What is the problem?
YouMy card doesn't work. Yesterday, I was in a shop, and my card said "no". But I have money in the bank.
Bank clerkLet me look. Can I have your card and your name, please?
YouYes, here you are. My name is Costa.
Bank clerkThank you, Mr Costa. Ah, I see it. The bank stopped your card. It was a safety check. Someone used your card online, and we were not sure it was you.
YouOh! Can you tell me why? I only bought a train ticket online.
Bank clerkYes, that was the train ticket. It is fine. It was a small mistake by the computer. I can start your card again now.
YouThank you. Can I have a new card, please? I want to be safe.
Bank clerkOf course. I can send you a new card. Is that OK?
YouYes, please. How long does it take?
Bank clerkFive days. It will come to your home. Your old card works again today.
YouThat's great. Thank you so much! You are very kind.

From memory — don't look up!

1. Why did the bank stop the card?

2. Was there a real problem, or a mistake? What did the customer really buy?

3. The customer asks for two things at the end. What are they?

1. The bank stopped the card for safety. Someone used the card online, and the bank was not sure it was really the customer.
2. It was a mistake by the computer — there was no real problem. The customer only bought a train ticket online, and that was fine.
3. A new card, and how long the new card takes to come (five days).

Useful Phrases 🧰

Eight phrases you can use at any bank or shop. Say each one out loud BEFORE you look at the answer. Guessing first helps you remember.

Stay calm and polite. A card problem is not your fault, and it is easy to fix. You do not need to be angry. Speak slowly, use please and thank you, and ask simple questions. Calm and polite works best.

🏦 Saying hello

Phrase 1

You come to the desk. You want to ask, politely, for help. Complete it: "Hello. Can you ___ me, please?"

"Hello. Can you help me, please?"
"Can you help me, please?" is a soft, polite way to start. It works everywhere — a bank, a shop, a station. The clerk wants to help you.
Phrase 2

You want to ask the clerk a question. Which sentence is polite?

A. "Tell me now."
B. "Can I ask you something?"
C. "I need answers."
B — "Can I ask you something?"
"Can I…?" makes it soft and polite. A and C sound like an order, not a question — and people help you more when you are polite.

💳 The problem

Phrase 3

You want to tell the clerk that something is wrong with your card. Complete it: "There is a ___ with my card."

"There is a problem with my card."
"There is a problem with…" is the calm, friendly way to start. You are not angry — you just need help. Use it for anything: "There is a problem with my phone", "There is a problem with my room".
Phrase 4

Now say what is wrong: your card is not working. Put the words in order:

doesn'tMy cardwork.
"My card doesn't work."
"… doesn't work" is perfect for any broken thing: "The machine doesn't work", "My phone doesn't work", "The key doesn't work".

🙋 Asking questions

Phrase 5

You want to know why your card stopped — politely, not angrily. Which one is polite?

A. "Why you stop my card?"
B. "Can you tell me why?"
C. "This is very bad!"
B — "Can you tell me why?"
"Can you tell me why?" is short, correct, and polite. It asks for a reason in a calm way. A has a grammar mistake, and C is not a question — it just sounds angry.
Phrase 6

You want a new card, to be safe. Ask for it politely. Complete it: "Can I have a ___ card, please?"

"Can I have a new card, please?"
"Can I have a …, please?" is a very useful way to ask for something: "Can I have a bag, please?", "Can I have a receipt, please?"
Phrase 7

You want to know how many days you must wait for the new card. Put the words in order:

does itHow longtake?
"How long does it take?"
"How long does it take?" asks about time — days, minutes, hours. Use it a lot: "How long does the bus take?", "How long does the food take?"

💛 Saying thank you

Phrase 8

The clerk helped you a lot. You want to say a big, warm thank you. What do you say? (Say it out loud first.)

"Thank you so much! You are very kind."
Add "so much" or "very much" to make your thank you bigger and warmer. "You are very kind" tells the person you saw their help. A smile helps too!

🔑 Make them yours

These phrases work at a bank, a shop, a café — everywhere. Write your own sentences. Say each one out loud first.

"There is a problem with ___." — Think of a thing that can go wrong. What is it?

"Can I have ___, please?" — What do you want to ask for next time you go out? (a bag, a coffee, a new card…)

"How long does ___ take?" — Think of a thing you sometimes wait for.

Quick test: close your eyes and say the whole visit out loud — say hello → tell them the problem → ask why → ask for a new card → say thank you. When it is easy, go to Your Turn.

Your Turn 🎭

Now it is your bank. Say your answers out loud FIRST, then write them. Use the timer if you like.

Round 1 — you are the customer 💳

The clerk speaks — you answer. Be polite: use please, thank you, and can you help me.

Bank clerkGood morning! How can I help you?
Bank clerkOh, I am sorry. What is the problem?
Bank clerkLet me look. Ah — the bank stopped your card, for safety.
Bank clerkIt was a small computer mistake. I can start your card again now.
Bank clerkOf course. I can send you a new card.
Bank clerkFive days. It will come to your home.

Round 2 — now YOU are the bank clerk 🏦

Change places. You work at the bank. Be friendly and helpful. Say "How can I help you?", "I am sorry", "Let me look", "Of course".

CustomerGood morning. Can you help me, please? There is a problem with my card.
CustomerMy card doesn't work. But I have money in the bank!
CustomerCan you tell me why? I only bought a train ticket.
CustomerThank you. Can I have a new card, please?
The Twist

🐢 The computer is slow

One more time — no help this round. Today the bank's computer is very slow. The clerk says: "I am sorry. The computer is slow today. I cannot start your card now. Can you come back this afternoon?" What do you say? Stay calm and polite. Ask a simple question — maybe "Can I use my card today?" or "What time this afternoon?" Try to leave happy. Start the timer and speak!

Before you finish…

Think about what you said. Can you say "yes" to these?

  • I said hello and asked for help politely.
  • I told them the problem in a calm way.
  • I asked "Can you tell me why?" to understand.
  • I asked for a new card and how long it takes.
  • I said "thank you" warmly at the end.

Can't say yes to one? That is your practice — try Round 1 again.

The Echo 🕰️

Six hundred years ago, in a city called Florence in Italy, there was a family called the Medici. They were bankers — they kept people's money safe and lent it out. They became very rich and very famous.

The Medici made many banking ideas that we still use today. They had banks in many cities. They wrote everything down carefully, so they always knew where the money was. This is why a bank can look at your card and see the problem — like the clerk did for you this morning.

A bank is really just a promise: you give them your money, and you trust them to keep it safe and give it back. When your card stopped, the bank was keeping that promise — checking your money was safe. It was a small problem, but the idea is very old.
