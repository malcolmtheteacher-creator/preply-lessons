At the Bank

Your card stopped working in a shop yesterday. You have money in the bank, so why? This morning, you go to the bank to ask — and to get help.

The Scene 🎬

Yesterday you were in a shop. You wanted to pay with your card. But the card said "no" — it did not work! You had money in the bank, so you did not understand. It was not a nice feeling.

You do not know why your card stopped. Maybe the bank stopped it. Banks sometimes do this to keep your money safe. But you need your card, so you must ask.

This morning, you go to the bank. A person works there — the bank clerk. They are friendly and want to help. You ask a simple question: "Why doesn't my card work?" And you ask for help. Stay calm and polite — it is an easy problem to fix.

Warm up — talk for one minute

  1. Do you use a card, or cash, or your phone to pay? Which do you like best? Why?
  2. Has a card or a machine ever said "no" to you at a bad time? What did you do?

The Conversation 💬

Read it out loud, one person each, then change. Find the phrases from your toolkit. Listen for the polite words.

Bank clerkGood morning! How can I help you?
YouGood morning. Can you help me, please? There is a problem with my card.
Bank clerkOh, I am sorry. What is the problem?
YouMy card doesn't work. Yesterday, I was in a shop, and my card said "no". But I have money in the bank.
Bank clerkLet me look. Can I have your card and your name, please?
YouYes, here you are. My name is Costa.
Bank clerkThank you, Mr Costa. Ah, I see it. The bank stopped your card. It was a safety check. Someone used your card online, and we were not sure it was you.
YouOh! Can you tell me why? I only bought a train ticket online.
Bank clerkYes, that was the train ticket. It is fine. It was a small mistake by the computer. I can start your card again now.
YouThank you. Can I have a new card, please? I want to be safe.
Bank clerkOf course. I can send you a new card. Is that OK?
YouYes, please. How long does it take?
Bank clerkFive days. It will come to your home. Your old card works again today.
YouThat's great. Thank you so much! You are very kind.

From memory — don't look up!

1. Why did the bank stop the card?

2. Was there a real problem, or a mistake? What did the customer really buy?

3. The customer asks for two things at the end. What are they?

1. The bank stopped the card for safety. Someone used the card online, and the bank was not sure it was really the customer.
2. It was a mistake by the computer — there was no real problem. The customer only bought a train ticket online, and that was fine.
3. A new card, and how long the new card takes to come (five days).

The Echo 🕰️

Six hundred years ago, in a city called Florence in Italy, there was a family called the Medici. They were bankers — they kept people's money safe and lent it out. They became very rich and very famous.

The Medici made many banking ideas that we still use today. They had banks in many cities. They wrote everything down carefully, so they always knew where the money was. This is why a bank can look at your card and see the problem — like the clerk did for you this morning.

A bank is really just a promise: you give them your money, and you trust them to keep it safe and give it back. When your card stopped, the bank was keeping that promise — checking your money was safe. It was a small problem, but the idea is very old.
