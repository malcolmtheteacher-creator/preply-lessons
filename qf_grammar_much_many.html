🎯 Much vs Many

Master countable and uncountable nouns | A2 Level

Let's see what you notice...

Read this sentence. What feels wrong?

I have much friends.

What's the correction?

✓ Correction

I have many friends.

📌 The Rule

Much = for uncountable nouns (water, advice, information, time)

Many = for countable nouns (friends, apples, cars, hours)

Countable nouns = can be counted (1 friend, 2 friends, 3 friends...). Uncountable = cannot be counted individually.

📚 The Rule (Dual Coding)

🔢 MANY (Countable)

🍎 🍏 🍎

many friends

many apples

many hours

Can count: 1, 2, 3...

💧 MUCH (Uncountable)

🥛 💧 🍵

much water

much time

much advice

Cannot count individually

💡 Bonus: A lot of works with BOTH countable and uncountable! (a lot of friends, a lot of water)
