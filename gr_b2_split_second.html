No time to think

An either/or choice appears. You have 10 seconds to pick one AND give one reason. No essays. No "it depends." Just pick and justify — NOW.

Then the twist: The other person has to argue the OPPOSITE of what you chose — in 20 seconds. Even if they agree with you. Especially if they agree with you.

3 rounds

Quick Round: 8 rapid choices. 10 seconds each. Pure gut instinct.
Argue Back: Same format, but after each choice the other person argues the opposite for 20 seconds.
Impossible: The hardest choices. Both truly terrible. Deeper discussion.

Reflection

Which choice was hardest? Was there one where you genuinely couldn't decide?
Did arguing the opposite ever convince you? Did you change your mind on any choice after hearing the counter-argument?
What does your choice pattern say about you? Did you tend to pick safety or adventure? Money or time? People or independence?
