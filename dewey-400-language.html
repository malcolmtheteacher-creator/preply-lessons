Elaboration: Language Families & Geography

Understanding connections helps long-term memory.

Language Families Represented

  • Indo-European (7/10): English, Hindi, Spanish, French, Bengali, Portuguese, Russian. Descended from ancient "Proto-Indo-European"
  • Sino-Tibetan (1/10): Mandarin Chinese. Ancient language family from East Asia
  • Afro-Asiatic (1/10): Arabic. Connected to Hebrew, Amharic
  • Japonic (1/10): Japanese. Unique language family (debated origins)

Why Language Distribution Matters

English's dominance: Not the most native speakers, but spread through colonialism and now global commerce/technology. 1.5 billion total speakers make it the lingua franca.

Mandarin's challenge: 1.1 billion native speakers but 50,000+ characters and tonal complexity limit global adoption.

Hindi/Spanish's regions: Hindi dominates India (but faces English/regional languages). Spanish unified Latin America despite indigenous languages.

Arabic's dialects: Classical Arabic (Quranic) vs. Egyptian, Gulf, Moroccan dialects — speakers may struggle to understand each other!

Japanese's writing: THREE writing systems make it one of hardest languages to learn (2,500+ characters needed for literacy).
