The Ticket

I run a travel agency with one small difference — we sell trips through time. Any moment in history, or any moment still to come. One-way or return, your choice.

Pick a moment. Anywhere, any century — ancient, medieval, next Tuesday, or a thousand years from now. Then sell me the trip of a lifetime: why this place, why this time, and why I'd be a fool to book anything else.

Sell me the trip

One minute. Convince me. Go.

01:00
Try one of these, or bring your own: the court of a Renaissance king · the first Moon landing, from the control room · a floating city two hundred years from now · the opening night of a legendary concert · the building of an ancient wonder, brick by brick.

The Brochure

The ticket is sold — now write the brochure out loud. Walk me through the whole trip before we've even left: what we'll see, what we'll eat, what we'll wear, and what we risk by going.

Don't just list it — make me feel it. I want the sounds, the smells, the crowd around us, the heat or the cold on our skin.

Talk me through the whole trip

Three minutes. Sights, sounds, food, clothes, danger — all of it.

03:00
Keep going with: what does it smell like? · what's the danger? · what will we wear so we don't stand out? · what's the one thing tourists always get wrong here?

The Tour

We've landed. This is happening right now — not a memory, not a plan. Guide me through one scene, live, as it unfolds around us. Point things out as we see them. React to what happens.

Guide me, live, through one scene

Two and a half minutes. We're there. Show me around.

02:30
Try: “Look — over there…” · “Careful, mind the…” · “Can you hear that? That's…” · “Quick, follow me before…” · describe it as it happens, not as it happened.

The Incident

Every trip through time has one. Something historic goes wrong — a chase, a storm, a case of mistaken identity, a rule we accidentally broke. Narrate what happens and how we get out of it.

Something goes wrong — narrate it

Two minutes. What happens, and how do we escape it?

02:00
Something goes wrong when… we're mistaken for someone important (or dangerous) · the weather turns without warning · we say or wear the wrong thing in front of the wrong person · the way home isn't where we left it.

Five Stars

We're home. Now give the trip its review, out loud, the way you'd leave it for the next traveller. Be honest — the good, the bad, whether you'd book it again.

And tell me the one single moment on the whole trip you'd have stayed inside forever, if you could.

Your five-star review

Two minutes. The verdict, and the one moment you'd never leave.

02:00
A good review says: what you'd recommend · what you'd warn the next traveller about · whether it was worth the risk · and the one moment you'd live inside forever, and why.
