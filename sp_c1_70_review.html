Key Mindset for Continued Growth

  • Sophistication is not complexity. Simpler, more precise language is actually more sophisticated.
  • Fluency comes from repetition, not perfection. Use these skills in real conversations. Awkwardness is part of the journey.
  • Reading your audience is as important as knowing techniques. The best skill unused is worthless. The mediocre skill perfectly timed is gold.
  • C1 is a spectrum, not a finish line. Even native speakers are always learning. You're now at the sophisticated end—keep refining.
