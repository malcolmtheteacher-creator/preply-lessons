Live to Work? Vacation and the Hustle

Americans are said to work all the time. What does the law say? And what about Canada?

Key words

Read the sentence. What does the bold word mean? Then click Reveal.

1. We took a vacation in July.
vacation (n) — time away from work or school. Example: “We took a vacation in July.”
2. He has two weeks of paid holiday.
paid (adj) — with money for the time. Example: “He has two weeks of paid holiday.”
3. The law says you must have a day off.
law (n) — a rule from the government. Example: “The law says you must have a day off.”
4. My employer is a big company.
employer (n) — a person or company that gives you work. Example: “My employer is a big company.”
5. 1 July is a holiday in Canada.
holiday (n) — a day with no work, for a special reason. Example: “1 July is a holiday in Canada.”
6. He is very tired after work.
tired (adj) — needing sleep or rest. Example: “He is very tired after work.”

The stereotype: “Americans never take holidays. They live to work.”

The story

Part 1 — No national rule

The USA is the only big rich country with no national law for paid vacation. Most full-time workers get some paid time off from their employer, often about two weeks a year at first. But part-time and low-paid workers often get little or none. Public holidays are not guaranteed by national law.

Quick check:

1. Does the USA have a national law for paid vacation?

2. How much vacation do many workers get at first?

1. No.

2. About two weeks.

Discuss:
  • Do you think a law is a good idea?

Part 2 — Taking time off

Even when workers have time off, they often do not take it. A 2023 survey found that almost half of US workers with paid time off took less than they had. They worried about falling behind. Some people love “hustle culture”, where constant work is good. Others say it causes burnout.

Quick check:

1. What did almost half of US workers do?

2. What is burnout?

1. Took less time off than they had.

2. Being very tired from too much work.

Discuss:
  • Do you take all your holidays?

Part 3 — Canada

In Canada, each province sets the rule. Usually, workers get two weeks after a year, and more later. Public holidays such as Canada Day on 1 July are protected. Canada is between the US and European systems. In much of Europe, workers get four or five weeks.

Quick check:

1. How much vacation do Canadian workers usually get?

2. When is Canada Day?

1. Two weeks after a year.

2. 1 July.

Discuss:
  • How many weeks is a good number?

Grammar — have to and don't have to

have to = it is necessary: “Workers have to ask for time off.”
don't have to = it is not necessary: “You don't have to work on a public holiday in Canada.”
Use has to for he / she / it.
