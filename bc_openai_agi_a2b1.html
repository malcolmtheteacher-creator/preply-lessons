The Trillion-Dollar Maybe

OpenAI makes ChatGPT. Millions of people use it every day. But the company spends much more money than it makes. Investors are still giving it billions. Will the bet pay off?

The story, in three parts

Read each part. Then answer the questions before you read the next part.

Part 1 — From lab to ChatGPT

OpenAI started in 2015. It was a research lab, not a normal company. Its goal was to build very powerful AI that helps all people.

But AI needs very big, very expensive computers. So in 2019 OpenAI changed. It started a company that could take money from investors. Microsoft invested 1 billion dollars.

In 2020 OpenAI started selling its AI to other companies. Then, in November 2022, it launched ChatGPT. About two months later, around 100 million people were using it. In January 2023 Microsoft invested billions more.

True or false? Click your answer.

1. OpenAI started as a normal company.

2. OpenAI sold its AI to companies before ChatGPT.

3. ChatGPT got about 100 million users in two months.

Talk about it
  • Why do you think ChatGPT became popular so fast?
  • What was the first time you used an AI tool? What did you think?

Part 2 — Fired, then back

On 17 November 2023, OpenAI’s board fired the boss, Sam Altman. The board said he was not always honest with them.

Almost all of the 800 workers said: “Bring him back, or we will leave.” Four days later, Altman was the boss again, with a new board.

After that, OpenAI started to spend more and more. It signed deals to build huge data centres. In 2025 Altman said the company planned to spend about 1.4 trillion dollars on computers in the next eight years.

True or false? Click your answer.

1. The board fired Sam Altman in 2023.

2. The workers were happy that Altman left.

3. OpenAI plans to spend about 1.4 trillion dollars on computers.

Talk about it
  • Who should be more powerful in a company: the board or the boss? Why?
  • Would you leave your job to follow a boss you like?

Part 3 — Big money, big losses

In 2025 OpenAI made about 13 billion dollars. That is a lot. But it spent much more, and its loss from running the business was about 21 billion dollars.

Investors still believe in it. In March 2026 they gave OpenAI 122 billion dollars. They said the company was worth 852 billion dollars. ChatGPT now has more than 900 million users every week.

But reports say OpenAI expects to lose about 25 billion dollars of cash in 2026, and it does not expect to make a profit in cash until about 2030. Will the bet pay off? Nobody knows yet.

True or false? Click your answer.

1. In 2025 OpenAI made more money than it spent.

2. Investors gave OpenAI 122 billion dollars in 2026.

3. OpenAI expects to make a profit in 2026.

Talk about it
  • Why do investors give money to a company that is losing money?

Talking about the future: will be doing and will have done

OpenAI talks a lot about the future — especially 2030. Here are two useful future forms.

will be + -ing — something in progress at a time in the future:
In 2028, OpenAI will be building new data centres. · This time tomorrow, I’ll be working.

will have + past participle — something finished before a time in the future. We often use by:
By 2030, OpenAI will have spent billions of dollars. · By Friday, I’ll have finished the report.

Not sure? Use might or probably:
By 2030, OpenAI might have made a profit. · In 2027, it will probably be spending more.

FormMeaningExample
will + verba simple predictionOpenAI will need more money.
will be + -ingin progress thenIn 2028 it will be losing money.
will have + past participlefinished by thenBy 2030 it will have built new data centres.
might have + past participlemaybe finished by thenBy 2030 it might have made a profit.
