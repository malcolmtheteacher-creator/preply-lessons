Making and Responding to Requests and Offers

Asking for Help / Making Requests

  • Would you be willing to help out at our fundraising event next week?
  • Can I give you a hand with...? (moving a table, making posters, etc.)
  • Do you mind if I ask you to...?
  • Could you possibly help me with...?
  • Shall I buy some snacks for the guests?

Offering Help

  • I"d be happy to help print the marketing leaflets.
  • Would you like me to prepare the presentation?
  • Can I give you a hand with that?
  • Let me know if you need any help organizing.

Responding to Requests

  • Sure, I"d be delighted to help!
  • I"m sorry, I"ve got a business trip that day.
  • I"d love to help, but I have another commitment.
  • Yes, absolutely. What do you need?

About Charities and Events

Think about these questions before planning your event.

1. Do you know any charities or non-profit organisations?

Think about organisations in your country or around the world that help people.

2. What do you know about charity events?

Have you ever attended a charity event? What was it like? What was the event trying to achieve?

3. What makes a successful event?

Consider: location, timing, activities, volunteers, budget, publicity, goals.

4. Types of Charity Events

Think about different types of events: sports events, galas, auctions, concerts, runs, bake sales, or awareness campaigns.

5. What is a famous charity event in your country?

What does the event support? How much money does it raise? Who attends?

Speaking Task: Planning a Charity Event

You have 5 minutes to plan a charity event together. Click "Start Timer" when ready.

5:00

Step 1: Choose a Charity

Decide which type of charity to support:

  • Animals
  • Children
  • Research (medical, scientific, etc.)
  • Local issues (homeless, food banks, etc.)
  • Poverty and development

Step 2: Plan Event Details

Work through these planning areas:

Planning Area Questions to Consider
Event Type What kind of event will raise money? (dinner, concert, race, etc.)
Date & Time When will the event happen? How long will it last?
Location Where will the event be held? Is it suitable?
Budget How much money do you need? Where will it come from?
Activities What will happen at the event? What will keep people interested?
Volunteers Who will help? What will each person do?
Marketing How will you tell people about the event?
Goal How much money do you want to raise?

Step 3: Present Your Event

Present your charity event plan. Share:

  • What charity will the event support?
  • What is the event and why will it work?
  • When and where will it happen?
  • Who will attend?
  • How much money do you expect to raise?

Use the phrases you learned to ask for and offer help with different tasks. Discuss who is responsible for what, and how you"ll coordinate all the details.
