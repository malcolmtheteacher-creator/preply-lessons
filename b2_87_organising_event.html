Organising a Charity Event

Plan an event and practise making offers and requests

Learning Objectives

  • Plan a charity event from start to finish
  • Practise making and responding to offers
  • Practise making and responding to requests
  • Develop communication and planning skills

Let's Start

Do you know any charities?

What do they do? How do they help people? What organizations make a difference in your country?

What happens at a famous charity event in your country?

Think about fundraising events, benefit dinners, telethons, marathons, or concerts.

Have you ever participated in a charity event?

What did you do? Did you enjoy it? Would you do it again?

Offers and Requests

Complete the sentences with the correct word:

1. Would you be _______ to help out at our fundraising event?

willing or able

2. I'm _______ I've got a business trip.

afraid or sorry

3. Can I give you a _______ with the posters?

hand

4. Do you _______ me to contact the CEO?

want or need

5. _______ I buy some snacks for the guests?

Shall, Should, or Can

Dialogue Examples

Study these exchanges and use them as models for your event planning conversations:

Offering Help

A: Can I give you a hand moving the table?

B: Sure! That would be great. Can you move it over there?

Making a Request

A: Do you need me to print the leaflets?

B: Yes, please! We need 200 copies by Friday.

Declining Politely

A: Would you be willing to help with the presentation?

B: I'm afraid I can't. I'm away that weekend. But I can help you practice it beforehand?

Offering Alternative Help

A: Can I give you a hand with the decorations?

B: I appreciate that, but I've got that under control. Can you help check the equipment instead?

Common Tasks for Charity Events

Use offers and requests language to discuss these:

  • Help moving tables or equipment
  • Print leaflets or programs
  • Prepare a presentation
  • Check documents or materials
  • Borrow equipment or items

Plan Your Charity Event

Work through these steps to plan a complete charity event:

Step 1: Choose Your Charity

What type of charity interests you?

Animals — Supporting animal welfare, wildlife conservation, or animal shelters
Children — Supporting orphanages, education, or child health
Research — Supporting medical research, scientific discovery, or technology
Local Issues — Supporting homeless services, food banks, or community centers
Poverty — Supporting poverty relief, education programs, or fair trade

Step 2: Plan Your Event Details

Answer these questions:

  • Name of the charity: What will you call your event?
  • What area does it work in? Where does the money help people?
  • What type of event? Dinner, concert, marathon, bake sale, auction?
  • Where will it be? Restaurant, park, school, office?
  • When? Date and time?
  • Food and drink: What will you serve?
  • Activities: What entertainment or activities?

Step 3: Present and Discuss

Now present your event to me:

  • Describe your charity and why it's important
  • Explain the event type and why you chose it
  • Share the location, date, and time
  • Discuss the food, drinks, and activities
  • Ask for my help or offers in organizing it

Remember to:

  • Use offers: "Can I give you a hand with...?"
  • Use requests: "Do you need me to...?" or "Can you...?"
  • Respond politely to offers and requests
  • Explain your choices clearly
