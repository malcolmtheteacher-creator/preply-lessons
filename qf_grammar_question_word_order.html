📌 The Rule

Question word + AUXILIARY + SUBJECT + main verb

Where ARE YOU going?

What DO YOU want?

When DID SHE arrive?

The auxiliary verb (are, do, did, have, etc.) comes BEFORE the subject.

📚 The Rule (Dual Coding)

✅ CORRECT

QW + Aux + Subject

Where ARE YOU going?

What DOES SHE want?

When DID THEY arrive?

Auxiliary comes before subject

❌ WRONG

QW + Subject + Aux

Where YOU are going?

What SHE does want?

When THEY did arrive?

Subject before auxiliary (incorrect)

💡 Bonus: This is called inversion. In statements: "You are going." In questions: "Are you going?"
