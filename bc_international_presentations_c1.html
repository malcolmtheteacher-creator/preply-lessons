Opening Discussion: Presentations Around the World
Consider these questions about speaking and presentations in different cultures.

Have you seen a really good or bad speaker? What was the difference?

Think about the structure, delivery, engagement, or how they used visual aids. What made them effective or ineffective?

What's more important — knowledge or public speaking skill?

Can someone with excellent knowledge but poor speaking skills still be a good presenter? Or is delivery equally crucial?

Are there cultural differences in how people present?

Compare presentation styles in Europe, North Africa, and Asia. How do audiences react differently? How do speakers use gestures, eye contact, or pauses?

What challenges do international speakers face?

Think about language barriers, cultural misunderstandings, or audience expectations. What makes presenting to international audiences difficult?

Reflection and Discussion

Do you enjoy delivering presentations?

What do you like about it? What makes you nervous? How does it feel when your talk goes well?

What are the characteristics of an effective presenter?

Think about confidence, structure, engagement, body language, or tone. Which are most important?

How do you manage nerves before presenting?

Do you practise? Do you have breathing techniques? Do you arrive early? What helps you feel ready?

What do you think of PowerPoint or visual aids in presentations?

Are they helpful or distracting? When are they necessary? Can you present well without them?

How different is presenting to an international audience?

What extra skills do you need? Is it harder or just different? What did you learn about cross-cultural communication?
