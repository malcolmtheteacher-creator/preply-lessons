Today's Goal

You'll learn how to build a "keyword map"—a special group of connected words. When you speak, these keywords help you:

  • Say more interesting things
  • Remember ideas when speaking
  • Connect your ideas together

Let's build your first keyword map!

How It Works

Making a keyword map is simple. Here's what we do:

Step 1: Pick 3 Starting Words

You choose 3 words that are important to your answer. For example, if the question is "What is the perfect weekend?", you might pick: relax, family, adventure

Step 2: Find Connected Words

The computer finds words that connect to your words. You might see similar words, opposite words, or related words. Look at all the options!

Step 3: Pick More Words

From all the connected words, choose the ones that help you talk about the question. You can pick as many as you want!

Step 4: Speak for 45 Seconds

Use your keyword map to speak. Try to use as many of your keywords as possible. The timer helps you talk for the right amount of time.

Recall: What Did You Learn?

What is a keyword?

A word that is important to your answer. It helps you speak more and remember ideas.

What are connected words?

Words that are similar, opposite, or related to your main keywords. They give you more choices for speaking.
