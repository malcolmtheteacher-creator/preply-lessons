Sub-Saharan Africa: Nearly Fifty Countries, Not One

Many countries, many languages and a very young population. Africa is not one place.

Key words

Read the sentence. What does the bold word mean? Then click Reveal.

1. Nigeria is a big country.
country (n) — a land with its own government. Example: “Nigeria is a big country.”
2. The Congo is a long river.
river (n) — a long line of water that flows to the sea. Example: “The Congo is a long river.”
3. People speak many languages in Africa.
language (n) — the words people speak in a country. Example: “People speak many languages in Africa.”
4. Many people in Africa are young.
young (adj) — not old. Example: “Many people in Africa are young.”
5. Lagos is a huge city.
city (n) — a very big town. Example: “Lagos is a huge city.”
6. Africa has a long history.
history (n) — the story of the past. Example: “Africa has a long history.”
Talk it through:
  • Which word is new for you? Say a sentence with it.
The stereotype: “Africa is one country, and it is all the same.”

The story

Part 1 — A big place

Africa is about three times bigger than Europe. South of the Sahara desert, there are nearly fifty countries. More than a billion people live there. Nigeria has the most people. The landscape is very different from place to place: forests, grassland, mountains, lakes and desert.

Quick check:

1. How many countries are there south of the Sahara?

2. Which country has the most people?

1. Nearly fifty.

2. Nigeria.

Discuss:
  • Which country do you want to visit?

Part 2 — A long history

Africa has a very long history. A long time ago, there were big kingdoms, such as Aksum, Mali and Benin. Great Zimbabwe was a big stone city. Later, Europeans ruled many countries. Ghana was the first country in the region to become free again, in 1957.

Quick check:

1. Which country was first to be free in 1957?

2. Name one old kingdom.

1. Ghana.

2. For example Aksum, Mali or Benin.

Discuss:
  • What do you know about the history of your country?

Part 3 — Many Africas

There are about 2,000 languages in Africa. That is nearly a third of all the languages in the world. There are also many religions. Most people live in villages, but the cities are growing fast. Africa has the youngest people in the world. Many are under twenty.

Quick check:

1. How many languages are there?

2. Who is the youngest group in the world?

1. About 2,000.

2. People in Africa.

Discuss:
  • How many languages can you speak?

Grammar — the biggest, the oldest, the most

Short adjective: the + -est: “Nigeria is the biggest country.”
Long adjective: the most …: “Lagos is the most crowded city.”
Irregular: good → the best, bad → the worst.

1. Choose the word

1. Nigeria is country in the region. (the biggest / bigger)

2. Great Zimbabwe is stone city in the south. (the oldest / older)

3. Lagos is city in Nigeria. (the most famous / more famous)

1. the biggest

2. the oldest

3. the most famous
