Review: Key Teachings

Lesson 1: Conflict Debate

Four models: Conflict (enemies), Independence (separate), Dialogue (learn), Integration (complementary). Most scientists favour Independence or Dialogue.

Lesson 2: Creation

Religions teach creation differently. Interpretations: literal, metaphorical, framework, day-age. Many integrate evolution.

Lesson 3: Evolution

Evolution is unifying biology principle. Theistic evolution (most common), Intelligent Design, Young Earth (minority). Buddhism, Islam, Catholicism accept evolution.

Lesson 4: Miracles

Hume: doubt miracles (testimony unreliable). Aquinas: God works through nature. Science explains most; "medically inexplicable" ≠ divine.

Lesson 5: Limits of Science

Science excellent at "how"; struggles with "why" (meaning, morality, consciousness). NOMA: science and religion answer different questions.

Unit 14 Summary

The Answer: It Depends on the Model

Religion and science are not necessarily in conflict:

  • Conflict model: They cannot both be true (Galileo vs Church)
  • Independence model: Different questions, both valid (Theistic evolution)
  • Dialogue model: Can learn from each other (Buddhist-science)
  • Integration model: Complementary (Francis Collins)

Key Learning

Religion answers "Why?" and meaning. Science answers "How?" Science is powerful for factual questions about physical world. But meaning, purpose, consciousness, morality are beyond science's scope. Both science and religion offer value.

Final thought: Many of the world's best scientists are people of faith. Being scientific and faithful are compatible.
