Weekends, Holidays and Plans

B1 Level · Tell easy stories and talk about plans

Warm-up

Today we're working on talking about the past and the future so you can tell a short, easy story about your weekend, a trip or your plans.
Have a go, there are no wrong answers. What did you do last weekend? Tell me in three or four sentences.
Where would you like to go on your next holiday? Why?

A short story has four parts

📍Click after you answerWhere / when
"On Saturday we went to..."
🎬Click after you answerWhat happened
"First we..., then we..."
😀Click after you answerHow it was
"It was lovely / a bit crazy."
🔁Click after you answerAsk back
"What about you?"
Name each part before you click. Then tell me your weekend again using all four parts.

Tell a short story

Small words that help a story flow. Say your own sentence first, then click.

OrderWords to use
StartFirst, To begin with, On Saturday morning
NextThen, After that, Later
SurpriseSuddenly, Unfortunately, Luckily
EndIn the end, Finally, At the end of the day
Make a story from: train late / missed meeting / lunch with a friend
"Unfortunately my train was late, so I missed the start of the meeting. After that I had lunch with a friend, which was lovely. In the end it was a good day."
Make a story from: rain / museum / great coffee
"First it started to rain, so we went to a museum. Later we found a small café with great coffee. In the end it was a perfect afternoon."
Make a story from: new restaurant / long wait / delicious food
"On Friday we tried a new restaurant. Unfortunately we waited for forty minutes, but the food was delicious, so we were happy."
Choose any picture in your head: a trip, a party, a day at work. Tell me a short story with at least three of the linking words.

Quick check: Which sentence sounds most natural?

Talking about plans

Three easy ways to talk about the future.

UseExample
I'm going to… (a plan)"I'm going to visit my family next month."
I'm …ing (arranged)"I'm meeting a friend on Friday."
I might… (not sure)"I might go to the coast if the weather's good."
Ask me about my plans: how many different questions can you make?
"What are you doing at the weekend?"
"Are you going away this summer?"
"Have you got any plans for the holidays?"
"What are you looking forward to?"
Tell me your plans for this weekend, one thing you are sure about and one thing you might do.
Now tell me a plan for the summer and a plan you have for a year from now.

Quick check: "I'm not sure yet, but I ___ go to the lake."

Catch-up chat

A role-play. You are two colleagues who haven't spoken for a couple of weeks. Choose a situation and talk for about three minutes.

A: Monday morning

Ask about the weekend and share yours.

B: After the holidays

Your colleague has just come back from time away. Find out how it was.

C: Before a long weekend

Talk about what you are both going to do.

D: Video call

The meeting hasn't started yet. Make friendly conversation while you wait.
