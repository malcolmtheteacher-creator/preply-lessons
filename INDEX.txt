B1 INTERMEDIATE ACADEMIC ENGLISH LESSONS
========================================
Created: March 2026
Purpose: 1-to-1 Preply screen-shared lessons for students preparing for academic study
Level: B1 Intermediate (Foundational Study Skills)

LESSON STRUCTURE:
- 4 tabs per lesson (Content, Vocabulary, Practice, Examples/Review)
- 8 vocabulary flip cards per lesson
- 3+ practice exercises with answer reveals
- 5-minute timer (click-to-start)
- All files: 13-18KB each
- Pure HTML/CSS/JavaScript (no dependencies)

15 LESSONS:
===========

1. acad_b1_01_classroom_english.html
   "Classroom English: Asking for Help"
   - Asking questions, requesting clarification
   - Participating in class discussions
   - Key phrases for academic participation

2. acad_b1_02_taking_notes.html
   "Taking Notes in English"
   - Common abbreviations and symbols
   - Note-taking structure and organization
   - Academic shorthand techniques

3. acad_b1_03_reading_textbooks.html
   "Reading Textbooks Effectively"
   - Skimming and scanning techniques
   - Using textbook features (glossary, index, summaries)
   - Active reading strategies

4. acad_b1_04_writing_paragraphs.html
   "Writing Clear Paragraphs"
   - Paragraph structure (topic, supporting, concluding sentences)
   - Paragraph unity and coherence
   - Transition words and flow

5. acad_b1_05_giving_opinions.html
   "Giving and Supporting Opinions"
   - Opinion introduction phrases
   - Agreeing and disagreeing politely
   - Supporting claims with reasons and examples

6. acad_b1_06_understanding_instructions.html
   "Understanding Assignment Instructions"
   - Key instruction words (analyse, compare, evaluate, discuss)
   - What teachers expect from different task types
   - Planning before writing

7. acad_b1_07_basic_presentations.html
   "Your First Presentation in English"
   - Presentation structure (intro, body, conclusion)
   - Signposting language
   - Managing nerves and confidence-building

8. acad_b1_08_writing_emails_teachers.html
   "Emailing Your Teacher"
   - Formal email structure and tone
   - Polite requests and professional greetings
   - Sign-offs and common request scenarios

9. acad_b1_09_working_in_pairs.html
   "Working in Pairs and Groups"
   - Turn-taking and listening skills
   - Making suggestions and asking for opinions
   - Reaching agreement and dividing tasks

10. acad_b1_10_describing_graphs.html
    "Describing Charts and Graphs"
    - Trend vocabulary (rise, fall, peak, fluctuate, plateau)
    - Describing change speed (gradual, dramatic, sharp)
    - Example sentences for data description

11. acad_b1_11_summarising.html
    "Summarising What You Read"
    - Finding main ideas and key points
    - Paraphrasing in your own words
    - Keeping summaries brief and accurate

12. acad_b1_12_comparing_contrasting.html
    "Comparing and Contrasting"
    - Comparison transition words (similarly, both, whereas)
    - Contrast expressions (on the other hand, however)
    - Academic language for analysis

13. acad_b1_13_using_dictionary.html
    "Using a Dictionary Like a Pro"
    - Word class and parts of speech
    - Pronunciation guides and collocations
    - Finding the right meaning for context

14. acad_b1_14_exam_preparation.html
    "How to Prepare for Exams"
    - Revision strategies and time management
    - Understanding mark schemes
    - Managing exam anxiety

15. acad_b1_15_learning_from_mistakes.html
    "Learning from Your Mistakes"
    - Error correction and self-assessment
    - Keeping error logs
    - Growth mindset in language learning
