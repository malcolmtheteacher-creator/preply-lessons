Date: _______________

Key Vocabulary

Cancel culture - the practice of withdrawing support for public figures after objectionable behavior

To call someone out - to publicly criticize someone for something they have done

Accountability - taking responsibility for your actions and facing consequences

Mob mentality - when people act as a group without thinking individually

Public shaming - humiliating someone in front of many people

To pile on - when many people attack or criticize one person at the same time

Redemption - making amends for past mistakes and being forgiven

Due process - fair investigation and procedures before making a judgment

Useful Phrases

Acknowledging Complexity:

  • It depends on the context...
  • There are nuances to consider...
  • It is not as simple as...
  • On the one hand... but on the other...
  • While I understand... I also think...

Expressing Conditions:

  • It would be justified IF...
  • I could support this PROVIDED THAT...
  • This would only be fair AS LONG AS...
  • UNLESS there is evidence, we should not...
  • It might be different SUPPOSING...

For Debates:

  • One could argue that...
  • The problem with that logic is...
  • How do you account for...?
  • Playing devil's advocate here...
  • That raises the question of...

Answers from Today's Lesson

Vocabulary Matching:

1. Cancel culture = Fans stop buying tickets to a comedian's shows after controversial jokes
2. Call out = Someone publicly criticizes a politician's offensive comment on Twitter
3. Accountability = Taking responsibility for your actions and facing consequences
4. Mob mentality = Thousands retweeting the same outrage without reading the full story
5. Public shaming = A trending hashtag exposes someone's past mistakes to millions
6. Redemption = A celebrity apologizes, does charity work, and slowly rebuilds their career
7. To pile on = Thousands of people attacking one person on social media at the same time
8. Due process = A university fires a professor without investigating the allegations first

Complete the Sentences:

1. She was called out for her insensitive comments.
2. The company needs to take accountability for its actions.
3. After the scandal, thousands of people began to pile on her.
4. Without proper due process, we cannot judge if the accusations are true.
5. After years of apologizing and changing his behavior, he finally found redemption.

Practice at Home

Task 1: Write 5 sentences using the vocabulary above. Try to use different words in each sentence.

Task 2: Find a news article about a cancel culture incident. Write a short paragraph (100 words) explaining:

  • What happened?
  • Do you think the response was justified?
  • Use at least 4 vocabulary items from this lesson

Task 3: Practice the useful phrases by completing these sentences:

  • On the one hand, social media gives people a voice, but on the other hand...
  • I would support boycotting a company provided that...
  • While I understand the desire for accountability...

Key Discussion Points from Today

Questions we explored:

  • Where is the line between accountability and cancel culture?
  • Should punishment be proportionate to the offense?
  • Is there a path to redemption?
  • Does cancel culture give power to victims or create mob mentality?
  • Is it fair to judge old behavior by modern standards?

Remember: The goal is not to have all the answers, but to express your ideas clearly and consider different perspectives!
