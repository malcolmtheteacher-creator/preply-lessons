What does B do that A doesn't? Find two things before you check.
Check
In B the speaker answers, adds a little extra (a bit tired / it was easy), and asks a question back. In A every answer is one word, so the chat stops.
The simple formula: Answer + Add + Ask.

Answer + Add + Ask

Say your answer out loud, then check an example.

“Did you have a good journey?”
Yes, it was easy, thanks. The train was on time for once! Did you come by car?
“How's your week going?”
Quite busy, but good. I've got a lot of meetings. How about you?
“Have you been here before?”
Once, a few years ago. I remember the coffee was great. Is there a good place nearby?
“What did you do at the weekend?”
Nothing special. I met some friends and we had dinner. Did you do anything nice?

When you don't understand

Small talk can be fast. Say: "Sorry, could you say that again?" and "Sorry, what do you mean by …?" Practise both out loud.

Careful. Too personal. Avoid it.

Safe. Everyone has an answer.

Careful. Opinions can divide people. Avoid it with new people.
A good restaurant nearby
Safe. Food is friendly and easy.
