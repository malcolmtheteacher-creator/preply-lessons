Common Instruction Words

What Teachers Expect

Analyse: Break something into parts and examine each part carefully

Describe: Give a detailed picture with characteristics and features

Compare: Find similarities between two or more things

Contrast: Find differences between two or more things

Evaluate: Make a judgment about the value or quality

Discuss: Consider different points of view with reasoning

Explain: Make something clear by giving reasons or examples

Summarise: Give a brief statement of main points

Identify: Recognise or point out specific things

Justify: Give reasons to support your position

Before You Start

Read Instructions Carefully

✓ Circle or highlight key instruction words

✓ Check the word count or length requirement

✓ Note the due date and submission method

✓ Find any specific requirements (format, source types, etc.)

✓ Ask your teacher if you don't understand

Essential Vocabulary

Analyse Examine in detail; break into parts and study
Evaluate Judge the worth or value of something
Justify Give reasons to support your claim
Interpret Explain the meaning of something
Assess Evaluate or judge something carefully
Critique Examine critically and point out strengths and weaknesses
Synthesise Combine information from different sources
Illustrate Give examples to clarify or explain

Practice Exercises

Exercise 1: Match Instructions to Tasks

Assignment: "Analyse the causes of climate change."

What should you do?

Break climate change into different causes (greenhouse gases, deforestation, etc.) and examine each one in detail.

Exercise 2: Identify the Instruction

"Compare and contrast traditional and online education methods."

How many tasks are there?

Two tasks: 1) Compare (find similarities), 2) Contrast (find differences)

Exercise 3: Plan Before Writing

"Evaluate the effectiveness of recycling programmes in your city."

What questions should you ask yourself?

What are the goals of recycling? How well are they working? What evidence supports this? What are the strengths and weaknesses?

Sample Answers

Instruction: Describe the water cycle

What NOT to do: "The water cycle is important."

What TO do: "The water cycle consists of four stages. First, evaporation, where water rises from oceans and lakes. Second, condensation, where water cools and forms clouds. Third, precipitation, where water falls as rain or snow. Finally, collection, where water gathers in bodies of water again."

Instruction: Analyse the causes of World War II

What NOT to do: "World War II happened because countries fought."

What TO do: "Several interconnected causes led to WWII. The Treaty of Versailles created economic hardship and resentment in Germany. Rising fascism in Germany, Italy, and Japan... etc."

Key Takeaways

✓ Identify instruction words before starting

✓ Different words require different approaches

✓ "Discuss" is different from "Describe"

✓ Always follow the specific instructions given

✓ Ask for clarification if unsure

✓ Plan your response before writing
