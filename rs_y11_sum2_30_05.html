What is the total time and marks available for Paper 2?

🔒Reveal answer

Answer: Paper 2 is 1 hour 45 minutes (105 minutes) for 96 marks total. 4 themes (A, B, D, E): Theme A (24 marks), Theme B (24 marks), Theme D (24 marks), Theme E (24 marks). SPaG (5 marks) added to one answer.

What are the command words and marks for each question structure?

🔒Reveal answer

Answer: Theme A: 1 mark (what is), 1 mark (what is), 4 marks (explain), 6 marks (evaluate), 12 marks (evaluate). Total per theme: 24 marks. Same structure for B, D, E.

What does a 1-mark question require?

🔒Reveal answer

Answer: Define or name something briefly. "What is marriage?" Answer: "Permanent union between man and woman" or similar. One clear point sufficient. No lengthy explanation needed.

What should a 4-mark "explain" answer contain?

🔒Reveal answer

Answer: Identify teaching (1 mark), explain why it matters or what it means (1 mark), give biblical/Quranic example (1 mark), comment on practice or significance (1 mark). Show understanding, not just facts.

What should a 6-mark answer include?

🔒Reveal answer

Answer: Two contrasting perspectives or aspects. Each perspective needs: identification (1 mark), explanation with reason (1-2 marks), example (1 mark). Use connectives: "On the other hand," "In contrast," "Furthermore."

What is the structure for a 12-mark evaluation question?

🔒Reveal answer

Answer: Point 1 for statement (3-4 marks): perspective + explanation + example. Point 2 against statement (3-4 marks): contrasting view + explanation + example. Analysis of values (2-3 marks). Conclusion (1-2 marks).

How should you manage time for Paper 2 (105 minutes)?

🔒Reveal answer

Answer: Estimate 15 minutes per theme (60 minutes), 15 minutes for checking/SPaG. Breakdown: 5 min (2x1-mark), 10 min (4-mark), 15 min (6-mark), 25 min (12-mark) per theme. Adjust if running short.

What is SPaG and how is it awarded?

🔒Reveal answer

Answer: Spelling, Punctuation and Grammar (5 marks). Awarded on ONE extended answer (usually 12-mark). Use complex sentences, spell religious terms correctly (Muhammad, Jesus, Shariah, Qur'an), check punctuation.

What are common errors in 12-mark answers?

🔒Reveal answer

Answer: Only one perspective (needs two), repetition without analysis, vague statements without examples, no conclusion, poor spelling/grammar, irrelevant material, failing to address the specific statement/question.

What techniques improve marks in exam answers?

🔒Reveal answer

Answer: Use subject terminology precisely. Quote scripture/Quran. Explain not just assert. Show TWO perspectives on evaluation questions. Address the question precisely. Use PEE (Point, Evidence, Explanation). Check for typos.

Exam Structure & Strategy

Paper 2 Overview

Duration: 1 hour 45 minutes (105 minutes)

Total marks: 96 marks + 5 SPaG = 101 possible

4 Themes: Theme A (Relationships), Theme B (Religion & Life), Theme D (Peace & Conflict), Theme E (Crime & Punishment)

Each theme: 24 marks (1+1+4+6+12)

Time allocation: Roughly 20 minutes per theme (24 marks in ~20 min). Leave 5 minutes for checking.

Command Word Guide

"What is" (1 mark): Define or name. One clear, concise point.

"Explain" (4 marks): Identify + explain reason + example + significance. Show understanding.

"Outline" (6 marks): Two contrasting views/aspects. Each: identification + explanation + example. Use comparative language.

"Evaluate" (12 marks): Discuss statement critically. Two perspectives (for/against), analysis of underlying values, reasoned conclusion. Most demanding question type.

1+1+4-Mark Section: Worked Example

Q1 (1 mark): What is the Christian definition of marriage? Answer: "Permanent union between man and woman ordained by God." (1 mark)

Q2 (1 mark): Name one Islamic term for marriage. Answer: "Nikah" (1 mark)

Q3 (4 marks): Explain Christian teaching on divorce. Answer: "Christians traditionally teach marriage is indissoluble (Mark 10:9) [teaching stated - 1 mark]. This is based on the belief that God joins husband and wife permanently [reason - 1 mark]. The Catholic Church teaches no divorce is possible; annulment only (declares marriage invalid) [example - 1 mark]. This reflects the sanctity and permanence Christians place on marriage as a sacrament and covenant [significance - 1 mark]."

6+12-Mark Section: Worked Example

Q4 (6 marks): Compare Christian and Islamic teaching on contraception. Answer: "Christianity (Catholic) teaches contraception is immoral (Humanae Vitae 1968) and separates the procreative purpose from the unitive purpose of marriage [Christian view - 2 marks]. In contrast, Islam permits contraception with mutual spousal consent; it is not forbidden in the Quran and many scholars approve it for family wellbeing [Islamic view - 2 marks]. This shows Christianity emphasizes procreation while Islam prioritizes spousal agreement and wellbeing [comparison - 2 marks]."

Q5 (12 marks): "Religious teaching on punishment should emphasize retribution." Evaluate.

Exam Technique Summary

Key Exam Reminders

Before exam: Know the 4 themes and their key teachings. Memorize key quotes/Quranic verses. Practice timed answers.

During exam: Read every question twice. Underline command words. Plan your answer briefly. Show your reasoning step-by-step. Use subject terminology correctly. Give examples from scripture/Quran.

Time management: 1 hour total for 4 themes (15 min each). 5 minutes checking. Spend longer on 6 and 12-mark questions. Do NOT skip any question.

Common mistakes to avoid: Answering only one perspective in 6-mark comparison, no examples, vague language, poor spelling of key terms, not addressing the specific question, writing too little on high-mark questions, writing too much on low-mark questions.

What is the mark distribution for each theme on Paper 2?

🔒Reveal answer

Answer: Theme A, B, D, E each worth 24 marks: 1-mark question (definition), 1-mark question (name/term), 4-mark "explain" question, 6-mark comparison question, 12-mark evaluation question. Total: 96 marks across 4 themes.

What should you do if you run short of time?

🔒Reveal answer

Answer: Prioritize: complete 1 and 1-mark questions first (easy marks), then 4-mark, then 6-mark. If truly short, outline points for 12-mark (shows thinking). Do not leave any question blank—attempt something.

How many SPaG marks are available and which answer receives them?

🔒Reveal answer

Answer: Five SPaG marks available (Spelling, Punctuation, Grammar). Usually awarded on the 12-mark evaluation answer. Write carefully, use complex sentences, spell religious terms correctly (Muhammad, Jesus, Shariah, Quran, Hadith).
