What does 'Name' mean in a 1-mark question?

🔒Reveal answer

Answer: Identify ONE point, briefly. Example: 'What is cohabitation?' Answer: 'Living together without marriage.' No explanation needed for 1 mark.

What does 'Explain' mean in a 4 or 6-mark question?

🔒Reveal answer

Answer: State teaching, then give evidence/reason WHY. Example: 'Explain a Christian teaching on marriage.' Needs: teaching + Bible quote + explanation = full mark.

What does 'Evaluate' mean?

🔒Reveal answer

Answer: Weigh different viewpoints and reach reasoned conclusion. Not just 'both sides are valid.' Show which view is stronger and why.

What does 'Discuss' mean?

🔒Reveal answer

Answer: Present different perspectives, analyse strengths/weaknesses, reach conclusion. Similar to evaluate but broader — can discuss multiple issues.

What does 'Consider' mean in a question?

🔒Reveal answer

Answer: Examine the statement critically. Look at evidence for AND against. Example: 'Consider the view that...' — examine it carefully from multiple angles.

Paper 2 Timing Strategy (1h 45m = 105 minutes, 96 marks total)

4 themes × (1+1+4+6+12 marks) = 4 × 24 = 96 marks. Plan: 5 mins planning | Theme A: 20 mins (26 marks) | Theme B: 22 mins (26 marks) | Theme D: 24 mins (26 marks) | Theme E: 24 mins (26 marks) | 5 mins proofread. Allocate time by marks: ~1 min per mark, but 4-mark Q = 4-5 mins, 12-mark = 13-15 mins.

1-Mark Questions: Quick & Sharp

Just NAME the concept. No explanation. 20 seconds per question. Example Q: 'Name one Christian teaching about marriage.' A: 'Marriage is a lifelong commitment.' Done. Move on.

4-Mark Questions: Explain with Evidence

TWO points, each 2 marks. Each point needs: statement + evidence. 4-5 minutes. Example: 'Christians teach marriage is lifelong (Mk 10:9). Secondly, Islam teaches marriage is a legal contract (Nikah). Mark: 2+2=4.

6-Mark Questions: Evaluate Both Sides

Viewpoint 1 (2 marks) | Viewpoint 2 (2 marks) | Conclusion (2 marks). 6-8 minutes. Show balance, then explain which you think stronger.

12-Mark Questions: Essay with Structure

Intro (what you'll discuss) | Para 1 (viewpoint A with evidence) | Para 2 (viewpoint B with evidence) | Conclusion (reasoned judgment). 13-15 minutes. Aim for 250-350 words.

Worked Example: 4-Mark Question

Q: Explain two Christian teachings about sexual ethics. A: 'Christians teach that sex is reserved for marriage because it expresses love and commitment between partners (2 marks). Secondly, the Catholic Church teaches artificial contraception is immoral because it separates sex from procreation, violating natural law (Humanae Vitae) (2 marks).' Total: 4 marks.

How much time for Theme A (26 marks)?

Answer: 20-22 minutes. Allocate: 1 min Q1+Q2, 5 mins Q3, 7 mins Q4, 10 mins Q5. Rough guides; adjust if any question is tricky.

What if you run out of time?

Answer: Finish what you're writing, then briefly outline remaining questions. Outline answers score partial credit. Full, incomplete answers score more than no answer.

How much to write?

Answer: 1-mark: one sentence. 4-mark: 4-5 sentences (2 points). 6-mark: 6-8 sentences. 12-mark: full essay (250-350 words).

Final Checklist Before Exam

Brought calculator? NO — not needed for RS. Brought pens + pencil? Yes. Reread questions carefully — don't assume. Check you answered all five questions per theme. Proofread: no spelling/grammar errors. SPaG counts in 12-mark essays.
