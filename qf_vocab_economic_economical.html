📊 Economic vs. Economical

Two different adjectives — know the difference | B2 Level

Let's see what you notice...

Read this sentence. What feels wrong?

The government launched an economical recovery plan after the recession.

What's the correction and why?

✓ Correction

The government launched an economic recovery plan.

📌 The Rule

economic = relating to the economy, trade, or finance

economical = cost-saving; uses little money or resources; thrifty

Quick test: Can you replace the word with "financial" or "relating to the economy"? → Use economic. Does it mean "efficient" or "cheap to run"? → Use economical.

📚 The Difference (Dual Coding)

📊 ECONOMIC

🏦

= about the economy

economic growth

economic policy

economic crisis

economic data

→ Think: finance, trade, GDP

💰 ECONOMICAL

🐷

= cost-saving / efficient

an economical car

an economical solution

economical use of space

more economical to bulk-buy

→ Think: saves money or resources

💡 Why this happens: In many European languages (Italian economico, Spanish económico, French économique), one word covers both meanings. English splits them into two distinct adjectives.
