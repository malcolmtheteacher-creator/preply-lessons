Common Phrases
Click to see example sentences.
Poverty leads to unemployment
Shows direct connection: poverty causes unemployment
Stress is caused by poor work-life balance
Shows effect receiving the cause: poor balance creates stress
Lack of education results in limited job opportunities
Shows outcome: education lack creates fewer job chances
Loneliness is a consequence of social isolation
Shows result: isolation causes feeling alone
Racism is responsible for minority group discrimination
Shows responsibility: racism causes discrimination
Online bullying results in feelings of isolation
Shows effect: bullying causes isolation feelings
Modal Verbs and Adverbs: Softening Opinions
Strong cause-effect claims can be softened with modal verbs and adverbs to show less certainty. Click to reveal.

Modal Verbs (Possibility/Likelihood)

Smoking can lead to lung cancer
Possible but not certain: it's possible but not guaranteed
Stress may result in health problems
Possible outcome: might happen but not definite
Poverty could cause limited education
Possible consequence: reasonable but not absolute

Adverbs of Frequency (Frequency/Tendency)

Smoking often leads to lung cancer
Frequent outcome: happens regularly but not always
Isolation sometimes results in loneliness
Occasional outcome: happens in some cases
Poor diet usually causes weight gain
Common pattern: typical but not universal

Why soften opinions?

In formal writing, softening language shows academic caution. Not everything causes something 100% of the time. Using modal verbs and adverbs shows nuance and sophistication.

Paragraph Checklist
Wider Discussion

1. Most serious effect of sedentary lifestyles?

Think about physical health, mental health, social connection, and work performance. Which effect is most serious and why?

2. What causes pollution?

Consider sources like transportation, industry, agriculture, and consumer habits. Which cause has the biggest environmental impact?

3. Impact of investing in health services?

Think about both positive and negative effects. How do health services affect the economy, society, and individuals?

4. Effects of internet and social media on global society?

Consider communication, education, mental health, misinformation, and social connection. What are the major effects?

5. How does understanding cause and effect help in writing?

Why is clear cause-effect language important for formal writing? How does it strengthen your arguments?
