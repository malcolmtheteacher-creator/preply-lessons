📺 Classic Apprentice Moment

A candidate once paid £75 for fish that should have cost £30, then tried to make up the loss by aggressively haggling over 50p with a customer — and lost the sale entirely. The lesson? Know what matters and what doesn't.

The Two Deadly Mistakes

Most people fall into one of two traps:

  • The Pushover: Accepts the first offer, afraid to ask for more
  • The Bulldozer: Pushes so hard they damage the relationship or lose the deal

Today, you'll learn to find the sweet spot: confident, collaborative negotiation that gets results.

🎯 The TRADE Framework

Successful negotiation isn't about "winning" — it's about finding value for both sides. The TRADE framework keeps you strategic.

T Target & Limits

Know your numbers BEFORE you start. What's your ideal outcome? What's your walk-away point? What's your BATNA (Best Alternative To Negotiated Agreement)?

R Research & Rapport

Understand the other party. What do they need? What pressures are they under? Build connection before getting to business — people do deals with people they like.

A Anchor & Ask

Make the first offer when you can — it sets the "anchor" for the whole discussion. Ask for more than you expect to get, but stay credible.

D Discover & Develop

Ask questions. Listen more than you talk. Look for creative options that expand the pie — volume discounts, payment terms, add-ons, future business.

E Exchange & Exit

Trade concessions strategically — never give without getting. Know when to close, and always leave the door open for future deals.

🧠 Essential Negotiation Tactics

These are the moves that separate amateurs from pros. Learn to use them — and to spot when they're being used on you.

🎯 The Anchor
The first number mentioned heavily influences the final outcome. Make it yours.
"Based on market rates, I'm looking at £5,000 for this project."
🤫 The Pause
Silence is powerful. After making an offer or hearing one, pause. Let them fill the space.
They say £3,000. You say nothing. Wait. Watch them squirm.
🔄 The Trade
Never give something for nothing. Every concession should get something back.
"I can do that price IF you can confirm the order today."
📦 The Bundle
When stuck on price, change what's included. Add or remove items to shift value.
"The price stays, but I'll include free delivery and a 12-month warranty."
🚶 The Walk-Away
Be genuinely willing to leave. Nothing is more powerful than not needing the deal.
"I appreciate your time, but at that price it doesn't work for me. Thanks anyway."
🎭 The Flinch
React visibly to their first offer, even if it's reasonable. It signals they need to do better.
*Sharp intake of breath* "Wow... that's quite a bit more than I was expecting."

👆 Click to see: Tactics to WATCH OUT for (when used against you)

Defend Against These:

  • "Good Cop, Bad Cop": One person is friendly, another is tough. It's a game — negotiate with the decision-maker.
  • "The Deadline": "This offer expires today!" Often fake pressure. Test it.
  • "Higher Authority": "I'd love to, but my boss won't allow it." Ask to speak to the boss.
  • "Take It or Leave It": Rarely true. Ask "Is there any flexibility at all?"

💬 The Language of Negotiation

The words you use matter enormously. Here are the phrases that get results.

Opening & Anchoring

YOU "Based on my research, I'm looking at around £X for this."
YOU "To make this work for us, we'd need to be in the region of..."
YOU "What kind of flexibility do you have on price?"

Asking Questions (Discover)

YOU "Help me understand — what's driving that price?"
YOU "What would it take to get to £X?"
YOU "If price is fixed, what else could you include?"

Making Trades (Exchange)

YOU "I could agree to that IF you could..."
YOU "That's a big move on my side. What can you offer in return?"
YOU "I'm willing to be flexible on timing if you can move on price."

Closing & Walking Away

YOU "If we can agree on X, I'm ready to shake hands today."
YOU "I appreciate your time, but at that level it doesn't work for us."
YOU "Let me think about it and come back to you tomorrow."

👆 Click to see: Phrases to AVOID

❌ Weak Language

"I was kind of hoping for maybe around..."
"I don't suppose you could...?"
"Sorry, but would it be possible...?"
"That's probably the best I can do."

✅ Strong Language

"I'm looking for..."
"What flexibility do you have?"
"To make this work, I'd need..."
"My final position is..."
