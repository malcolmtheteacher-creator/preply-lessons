The Story Bank

Every professional storyteller needs ready stories. Here's what should be in yours.

A story bank is a collection of pre-shaped, rehearsed, deployable stories. You've learned how to build them. Now you need to audit which ones you actually have. Because the stories you don't have yet? Those are your practice priorities.

Category 1

Your Origin Story (Lesson 2)

Category 2

Your Thought Leadership Narrative (Lesson 11)

Your throughline + 2-3 signature stories that prove it. Deployable in keynotes, interviews, networking.

Do you have this one? (You should from Lesson 11.)

Category 3

A Crisis Story (Lesson 9)

How you handled difficulty. What you learned. Built with Acknowledge-Own-Redirect, ready to deploy when trust is challenged.

Do you have this one? If not, build one.

Category 4

A Data Story (Lesson 6)

Numbers that matter. Built with Context-Contrast-Clarity-Conclusion. Ready to explain why the data means something.

Do you have this one? If not, build one.

Category 5

A Reframe Success (Lesson 5)

A time you changed how people saw something. Built with the Reframing Ladder. Ready to deploy when mindset shift is needed.

Do you have this one? If not, build one.

Category 6

A "Show, Don't Tell" Moment (Lesson 4)

A time you let an action, interaction, or detail speak louder than explanation. Precise. Observed. Affecting.

Do you have this one? If not, build one.
