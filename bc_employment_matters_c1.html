Describing Tendencies in Team Members

Use these structures to discuss how people typically behave or contribute to teams.

Structure: Subject + tend(s) to + verb

Examples:

  • Mary tends to take on challenging problems.
  • Ajeet tends to organize his work systematically.
  • They tend to delegate too much responsibility.

Structure: Subject + may/might + verb (possibility)

Examples:

  • He may be uncomfortable with change.
  • She might find criticism difficult to handle.
  • They might lose focus without clear direction.

Structure: Subject + can be + adjective

Examples:

  • Felix can be overly optimistic sometimes.
  • Jennifer can be too focused on details.
  • Creative thinkers can be impractical at times.

Team Member Profiles

Study these profiles to understand different team roles and how to work with them effectively.

Group A: Action-Oriented Roles

Mary-Jo Singer - The Shaper

Characteristics: Dynamic, extrovert, challenges people to improve, finds best approaches for solving problems. Considers all possibilities before deciding.

Weakness: Can be argumentative and might offend others.

Ajeet Patel - The Implementer

Characteristics: Gets things done, turns ideas into results, conservative background, strong work ethic, very well organized. People count on him.

Weakness: May be uncomfortable with routine changes.

Farah Sinclair - The Completer-Finisher

Characteristics: Ensures things are done properly, excellent at editing and proofreading, meets deadlines, pushes team to complete work on time. Orderly.

Weakness: May be seen as a stress head or control freak.

Group B: People-Oriented Roles

Pilar Amado - The Team Worker

Characteristics: Supporter, ensures team works effectively, good at negotiating, diplomatic, very capable herself, prioritizes helping others get along.

Weakness: Sometimes sits on the fence; avoids taking clear positions.

Annelie Saunders - The Resource Investigator

Characteristics: Curious, innovative, explores all options, enthusiastic, outgoing, extrovert. People outside the group respond positively.

Weakness: Can be overly optimistic; gets bored quickly.

Felix le Grand - The Coordinator

Characteristics: Likes leading role as chairman, guides team toward objectives, excellent listener, recognizes value each member brings. Calm and good-natured.

Weakness: May delegate away too much personal responsibility.

Group C: Thinking-Oriented Roles

Stuart Freeman - The Plant

Characteristics: Creative innovator, thinks up new ideas and approaches, enjoys praise, introverted, prefers working alone.

Weakness: Ideas can be impractical; tends to ignore constraints.

Jennifer Smith - The Monitor-Evaluator

Characteristics: Analyzes and evaluates ideas from others, objective, carefully thinks through pros and cons, critical thinker.

Weakness: Can be unemotional; not a very good motivator.

Ciaron Byrne - The Specialist

Characteristics: Has specialized knowledge, very interested in professional development, strives to be expert in area.

Weakness: Gets too involved in details, loses sight of the bigger picture.

Team Roles Discussion

How do the different team roles complement each other? Consider how different personalities and strengths work together.

What role do you think you naturally fit into? Explain your answer with examples of how you work in groups.

Do you avoid working in teams or put yourself forward? Why do you think this is your tendency?

What qualities do you feel you bring to a team? Consider both your strengths and areas where you're still developing.

Give an example of when teamwork has failed. What went wrong? How could the team have been better balanced?

What are the advantages and disadvantages of working alone versus in teams? When is each approach preferable?

What are the pros and cons of using Belbin's roles to form teams? Does categorizing people help or limit them?
