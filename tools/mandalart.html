How to use MandalArt

  1. Write your main topic in the center cell
  2. Fill the 8 surrounding cells with related ideas
  3. Double-click any outer cell to expand it into its own grid
  4. Drag cells to reorder within the current level
  5. Right-click a cell to move it to another project
