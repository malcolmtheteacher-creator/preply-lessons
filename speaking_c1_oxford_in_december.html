Key words

quadrangle (noun) — a square or rectangular courtyard with buildings on all four sides, especially in a college. Example: "We crossed the frosty quadrangle to reach the chapel."
spire (noun) — a tall, pointed structure on top of a church or tower. Example: "From the hill you can see dozens of spires above the rooftops."
cobbled (adjective) — paved with small rounded stones fitted closely together. Example: "Watch your step: the cobbled lanes get slippery in a frost."
porter (noun) — the person at a college or hotel entrance who looks after the gate and answers visitors' questions. Example: "At the lodge we were told the college would reopen after the New Year."
reputedly (adverb) — according to what people say, though it may not be true. Example: "The pub was reputedly a favourite of several famous writers."
renowned (adjective) — famous and admired for a particular quality. Example: "The city is renowned for its libraries."
atmospheric (adjective) — creating a strong mood, often mysterious or romantic. Example: "The narrow lanes are at their most atmospheric after dark."
bustling (adjective) — full of busy, noisy activity. Example: "The market was bustling with shoppers buying last-minute gifts."
secluded (adjective) — quiet and hidden away from other people. Example: "Behind the gate lay a garden that few tourists ever see."
festive (adjective) — cheerful in a way that suits a celebration, especially Christmas. Example: "Strings of lights gave the whole street a cheerful, holiday feel."
