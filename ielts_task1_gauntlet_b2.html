Three ways, same fact — this time you write

Three chart facts. For each, write THREE versions: (A) verb + adverb, (B) "There was…" + adj + noun, (C) "saw / experienced" + adj + noun. Then reveal the models.

The Write — 20-minute timed Task 1

Exam conditions. Study the chart, plan for 2 minutes, write for 18. Target: 150+ words, with at least one verb+adverb sentence, one adj+noun sentence, one contrast linker.

Time remaining: 20:00

The task

The chart below shows the percentage of households in one country with access to three technologies between 2000 and 2020.

Summarise the information by selecting and reporting the main features, and make comparisons where relevant.

Household access to technologies, 2000–2020 (% of households)

Smartphone: 0% → 95% · Broadband: 5% → 92% · Landline: 95% → 20%

Words: 0

Model (176 words)

The line graph shows the percentage of households with access to three technologies — smartphones, broadband, and landlines — in one country between 2000 and 2020.

Overall, smartphone and broadband access rose dramatically over the 20-year period, while landline use fell sharply. By 2020, smartphones had overtaken both older technologies.

In 2000, smartphones were essentially non-existent (0%), and broadband reached only 5% of households, whereas landlines dominated at 95%. Over the next two decades, smartphones saw the most dramatic increase of the three, surging from 0% to 95%. Broadband followed a similar pattern, climbing steadily from 5% to 92%.

Meanwhile, landline access experienced a considerable decline. After remaining stable at around 85% until 2005, it fell steeply, reaching only 20% by 2020 — a drop of roughly three-quarters.

By the end of the period, smartphones and broadband had changed from being marginal technologies to near-universal, while landlines had moved in the opposite direction.

Target language: rose dramatically (V+adv), saw the most dramatic increase (V + adj+N), experienced a considerable decline (V + adj+N), while and meanwhile (contrast linkers), had changed from being (ranking change). That's the full toolkit.
