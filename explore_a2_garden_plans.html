Talking about plans: be + going to + verb

🌦️ → 🧑‍🌾

I'm going to plant carrots.   He's going to cut the lawn.   They aren't going to pick the apples.

Find the pattern. Look at the three sentences. What comes after "going to"? What changes when the person changes? Say the rule in your own words. Then make a new sentence about tomorrow.

After going to we use the base verb (plant, cut, pick). Only am / is / are changes: I am, he/she is, you/we/they are. For no: am not / isn't / aren't going to.

Without looking: Close your eyes. Say six weather words and one garden job for each. 30 seconds.

How To…

An instruction starts with a verb. Dig a hole. Don't forget your gloves.

Words that show the order: first · then · next · after that · finally

Model: First, choose a sunny place. Then, make a hole. Next, put in the plant. Finally, give it water.

Put the steps in order

How do you plant a seed? Write 1 to 5 next to each step.

  • Cover the seed with soil.
  • Water the soil.
  • Choose a sunny place.
  • Put the seed in the hole.
  • Dig a small hole.

Now you teach

Choose one job: water a plant · cut the lawn · make a cup of tea · wash your hands. Give instructions with first, then, next, finally. Speak first, then write.

Warnings: Don't + verb.

  • Don't water the plants at noon – the sun is too hot.
  • Don't walk on the wet lawn.
  • Think of one more warning for a garden.

A Busy Weekend

It is Friday evening and Tom is looking at the weather forecast. Saturday is going to be sunny and warm. Sunday is going to be rainy and windy. So Tom has a plan. On Saturday morning, he is going to cut the lawn and dig the flowerbed. In the afternoon, he is going to plant tomato seeds in pots. On Sunday, he is not going to work outside. He is going to stay inside and make a list of seeds for next month.

True or false?

1. Saturday is going to be rainy.

2. Tom is going to cut the lawn on Saturday.

3. He is going to plant the seeds on Sunday.

4. On Sunday, he is going to stay inside.

5. He is going to buy seeds on Sunday.
