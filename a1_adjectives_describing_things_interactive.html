🎨 Describing Things

Learn adjectives to describe everything around you!

📚 What Are Adjectives?

Adjectives are describing words. They tell us what something is like! Is it big? Small? Beautiful? Old? New? 🤔

🎯 Two Ways to Use Adjectives

1. After BE (is/are/am):

Subject + is/are + adjective

My room is big.

The games are fun.

2. Before a NOUN:

Subject + have/has + adjective + noun

I have a new phone.

She has long hair.

📝 Word Order: Adjective BEFORE Noun

I have
→
a big
→
room

✅ a big room   |   ❌ a room big

🔄 Opposites - Learn Adjectives in Pairs!

💡 French vs English

In French, adjectives often come AFTER the noun: "une maison grande"

In English, adjectives come BEFORE the noun: "a big house"

⚠️ Don't forget to change the order!

🗣️ Describe Everything!

🛏️ Describe Your Room

Tell your teacher about your room using adjectives:

  • Is your room big or small?
  • Is it clean or messy?
  • What color are the walls?
  • Do you have a new bed or an old bed?
  • Is your room cool? Why?

📱 Describe Your Things

Talk about things you own:

  • What kind of phone do you have? (new/old, big/small, fast/slow)
  • What about your computer/tablet?
  • Do you have a favorite jacket? What is it like?
  • What's your school bag like?

👨‍👩‍👧 Describe People

Describe people in your life:

  • Is your best friend tall or short?
  • Does your teacher have long or short hair?
  • Are your parents young or old? (careful! 😅)
  • Is your pet big or small?

🏆 Final Challenges

🎯 Challenge 1: Opposites Game

Your teacher says an adjective. You say the OPPOSITE!

Example:

Teacher: "big" → You: "small!"

Teacher: "hot" → You: "cold!"

Teacher: "new" → You: "old!"

🎯 Challenge 2: Describe a Picture

Look around your room. Use 5 adjectives to describe what you see!

Example:

"I can see a big window. There's a small cat on my comfortable bed. I have a new computer on my old desk. My room is beautiful!"

🌟 SUPER CHALLENGE: Complete Description!

Use EVERYTHING to describe your ideal day!

  • ✅ Adjectives to describe things
  • ✅ There is/are to say what exists
  • ✅ Present Continuous for actions
  • ✅ Prepositions for locations
  • ✅ I like... for preferences

MEGA Example:

"It's a beautiful day! The weather is hot and sunny. I'm sitting in my big room. There is a comfortable sofa next to the window. I'm playing a fun game on my new computer. I have a cold drink on my small table. I'm happy because I like relaxing days!"

🎮 GAME: 20 Questions with Adjectives!

Think of an object. Your teacher asks yes/no questions using adjectives!

Example:

🤔 (You think of: your phone)

Teacher: "Is it big?" → "No, it's small."

Teacher: "Is it new?" → "Yes, it is!"

Teacher: "Is it expensive?" → "Yes, it is!"

Teacher: "Is it your phone?" → "Yes! 🎉"
