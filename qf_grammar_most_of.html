🎯 Most / Most of the (Not 'The Most Of')

Correct quantifier patterns | B1 Level

Let's see what you notice...

Read this sentence. What feels wrong?

The most of students passed the exam.

What's the correction?

✓ Correction

Most students passed the exam. / Most of the students passed.

📌 The Rule

Most + noun (general). Most of the + noun (specific group). Never 'the most of'.

L1 transfer: Spanish 'la mayoría de', French 'la plupart de', Italian 'la maggior parte di'.

📚 The Rule (Dual Coding)

MOST + noun (general)

👥

Most people like coffee

Most students work hard

Most countries have...

MOST OF THE + noun (specific)

🎯

Most of the students in my class

Most of the cake is gone

Most of the people here

💡 Tip: 'The most' = superlative: 'the most expensive' (different meaning!).
