Everyday Echoes

Thirty everyday conversations to speak your way through — and every one carries a small echo of a moment from history.

How it works 🎭

Each lesson drops you into a real situation — a ticket desk, a doctor's room, a market stall — and hands you the language that turns "no" into "well, maybe". You read a model conversation, learn the phrases that make it work, then play both roles yourself.

🎬 The SceneWho you are, what you want, and who you're up against.
🧰 The PhrasesTen grown-up, real-life phrases — guess first, then check.
🎭 Your TurnPlay it out, both sides, against the clock.
🕰️ The EchoA wink to the history lesson hiding inside the situation.
Choose your level: Every situation comes at two levels — switch any time, and the cards below update.
