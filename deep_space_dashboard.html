Deep Space

You know the planets. This is everything beyond them: how stars die, what a black hole really is, why the universe is stretching, and what the other 95% is.

Each lesson starts with a number that should not be true, then explains it properly, with the actual equations and the people who found them. Every lesson has labs you can play with: build a star, squeeze the Earth into a black hole, fit a galaxy's dark matter, fire Newton's cannon.

Where scientists still disagree, the lessons say so. There are open problems in every one, and some of them could be solved by whoever reads this. Start with the map or pick any card. They can be taken in any order.
