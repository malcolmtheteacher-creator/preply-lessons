Key Health Issues

Your school is planning a health awareness event. Choose one health issue to focus on.

Health Problems to Address

  • Fast food & poor diet: Causing obesity, heart disease
  • Smoking: Damages lungs, increases cancer risk
  • Sedentary lifestyle: Lack of physical activity increases health risks
  • Chemical additives: Found in processed foods
  • Sun exposure: Without protection, causes skin damage
  • Lack of sleep: Affects mental and physical health
  • Stress: Can cause serious physical and mental health problems
  • Too much salt: Linked to high blood pressure and heart disease

Reflection:

Which of these health issues do you think has the most serious impact? Why?

Cause & Effect Language

Click to see examples of phrases that express cause and effect, and describe "too much" or "too little".

Expressing Cause & Effect

Show Phrases
• lead to — A diet high in fast food can lead to obesity.
• contributes to — Smoking contributes to lung disease.
• causing — Stress is causing serious health problems.
• bring on — Sedentary lifestyles can bring on depression.
• provoked by — Heart disease is provoked by high stress.
• aggravated further by — Sleep problems are aggravated further by caffeine.

Expressing Too Much / Too Little

Show Phrases
Too Much:
• An excessive amount of sugar
• High in salt
• Surplus energy

Too Little:
• Lacking in vitamins
• An absence of exercise
• Not enough sleep
• Insufficient physical activity

Discourse Markers for Presentations

Use these markers to organize your presentation and connect ideas.

Common Discourse Markers

Show Examples
To introduce: First, To begin with
→ "First, we\'re going to look at the effects of stress."

To move forward: Then, Next, After that
→ "Then, we\'ll talk about solutions."

To introduce reason: Given, Because
→ "Given our demanding jobs, it\'s no surprise we can\'t slow down."

To add emphasis: But, However
→ "But research shows that stress can cause illness."

To show importance: Especially
→ "This is important, especially since workloads are increasing."

To acknowledge difficulty: Of course
→ "Of course, we all know reducing hours is easy to say but hard to do."

To conclude/advise: So, Therefore
→ "So, speak to your boss about stress with your colleagues."

Hold Your Meeting

Discuss and decide which health issue should be the focus of your school\'s awareness campaign.

Meeting time: 10:00

Your Task

As a group, you need to:

  1. Discuss why your assigned issue is important
  2. Explain the effects and consequences
  3. Listen to others\' ideas about their issues
  4. Decide together which issue should be the focus of the campaign

What to Remember During the Meeting

  • Use cause and effect language (leads to, contributes to, brings on, etc.)
  • Use discourse markers to organize your points (First, Then, Given that, etc.)
  • Provide statistics and facts to support your arguments
  • Ask questions and listen to your partners\' perspectives
  • Work together to reach a group decision

Meeting Notes

Reflection after the meeting:

What was the most persuasive argument you heard? How did you reach your group\'s decision?
