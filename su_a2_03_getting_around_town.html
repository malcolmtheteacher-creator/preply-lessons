Direction Verbs

Read Mike's letter to Jana. The direction verbs are highlighted. These are the key words for giving directions.

Dear Jana,

I'm so glad you're coming to our party! There are two ways to get to our house from the train station.

By bus:
Take bus 17 and get off just after the library in Dolphin Street. It takes about 5 minutes. Buy your ticket from the driver. Our house (number 8) is opposite the bus stop and has a yellow door.

By foot:
Or if it's a nice day and you don't have a heavy bag, you can walk—it's about 20 minutes. When you leave the station, turn left and walk down Fish Street. At the school turn right into Ship Street. Carry on for about 200 metres until you get to the traffic lights. Then turn left into Dolphin Street and go straight on and go past the library. Our house is on the right, next to a restaurant called Othello and as I said it's opposite the bus stop. Give me a call if you get lost!

We're all really looking forward to seeing you, and remember you don't have to bring any food or drink—just yourself!

Love,
Mike

Key Direction Verbs

take Get on (a bus, train, etc.)
get off Leave (a bus, train, etc.)
leave Go away from a place
turn left / right Change direction at an angle
walk down Move along a street
carry on Continue in the same direction
get to Arrive at a place
go straight on Continue without turning
go past Pass by something

Where Things Are

Use these phrases to describe where things are in relation to other places.

📍 opposite
Example: Our house is opposite the bus stop.
Meaning: On the other side, facing each other
🏪 next to
Example: The house is next to a restaurant.
Meaning: Beside, touching or very close
➡️ on the right / left
Example: Our house is on the right.
Meaning: Direction when you're facing forward
📚 just after
Example: Get off just after the library.
Meaning: Immediately following something
🚦 at the traffic lights
Example: Turn left at the traffic lights.
Meaning: Exactly at that location (a landmark)
🏙️ in [Street Name]
Example: Just after the library in Dolphin Street.
Meaning: Located on that street

Quick Practice

Complete these sentences with a location phrase:

  1. The bus stop is ______ the library. (opposite / next to)
  2. The restaurant is ______ the house. (next to / on the right)
  3. Turn left ______ the traffic lights. (at / in)
  4. Get off ______ the library. (just after / at)

Get Ready to Speak

You're going to give directions from your house to a place you know well. Let's prepare.

Your Task

  • Think of a route you know very well (your house → school, work, a shop, a friend's house, etc.)
  • What are the key landmarks on the way? (streets, buildings, traffic lights, parks, etc.)
  • What direction verbs will you use?
  • What location phrases will help describe the route?

Useful Phrases to Review

  • turn left / right
  • go straight on
  • carry on / keep going
  • go past the library
  • it's opposite the bus stop
  • next to the restaurant
  • on the right / left
