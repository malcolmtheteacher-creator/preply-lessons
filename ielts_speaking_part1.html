What is IELTS Speaking Part 1?

7 minutes

The Basics

Time: 4-5 minutes

Topics: You, your life, your interests (familiar things!)

Questions: Simple questions about home, work, studies, hobbies

Number of questions: About 12 questions on 3 different topics

Why is Part 1 Perfect for You?

Part 1 is the BEST place to start your IELTS journey because:

  • Questions are about YOU - things you know!
  • Answers are SHORT - just 2-4 sentences
  • Topics are FAMILIAR - home, family, food, hobbies
  • You can prepare common topics in advance

Band 4 vs Band 5: What's the Difference?

Look at these two answers to the same question:

Examiner asks:
"Do you like cooking?"
Band 4

Too Short

"Yes, I like cooking."

Only 4 words! The examiner learns nothing about you.

Band 5

Extended Answer

"Yes, I like cooking. I usually cook dinner for my family. I enjoy making pasta because it's easy and delicious."

3 sentences! Shows more language and personality.

The Secret to Band 5

You don't need perfect grammar or difficult words. You just need to say more! In this lesson, you'll learn 3 simple strategies to extend your answers.

Strategy 1: Extend Your Answer

Never give a one-sentence answer. Always add more information!

8 minutes

The Simple Rule

1 Sentence = Bad | 2-3 Sentences = Good

After your first answer, add ONE more piece of information:

  • When? - "I usually...", "Every day...", "On weekends..."
  • Where? - "...at home", "...in the park", "...near my house"
  • Who with? - "...with my friends", "...with my family", "...alone"
  • What exactly? - "I like reading novels", "I enjoy playing football"

Practice 1: Which is Better?

Examiner asks:
"Do you like reading?"

Activity: Choose the better answer

A) "Yes, I like reading."
B) "Yes, I like reading. I usually read before bed. I enjoy mystery novels."

Practice 2: Which is Better?

Examiner asks:
"Do you play any sports?"

Activity: Choose the better answer

A) "Yes, I play badminton. I usually play on Saturday mornings with my colleagues. We go to a sports centre near my office."
B) "Yes, I play sports. I like sports very much."

Build Your Own Extended Answer

Examiner asks:
"Do you like music?"

Sentence Builder - Click to Build!

Start
Yes, I like music. Yes, I love music. Yes, I enjoy music.
+ When?
I usually listen when I'm working. I listen every day. I often listen on the bus.
+ What?
I like pop music. I enjoy classical music. I prefer rock music.

Common Mistake

Don't say only "Yes" or "No" - this is the biggest mistake in Part 1! Even if you're nervous, always add at least one more sentence.

Useful Phrases for Extending

I usually... Every day... On weekends... When I have time... I really enjoy... I often...

Strategy 2: Add a Reason

Tell the examiner WHY - it shows you can connect ideas!

8 minutes

The Magic Words

Use These Connectors

because so that's why

These simple words help you extend your answer and show your English ability!

See the Difference

Without Reason
"I like summer."
With Reason
"I like summer because I can go to the beach."
Without Reason
"I don't like cooking."
With Reason
"I don't like cooking because it takes a long time, so I usually eat out."

Examiner asks:

This answer has no reason. Which version is better?

A) "Yes, I like my neighbourhood. It's nice. Very nice."

Pro Tip

You can use "because" for positive AND negative answers!

Strategy 3: Give an Example

Make your answer real and specific with examples!

8 minutes

Why Examples Help

Examples Make Your Answer...

  • Longer - More words = more language to assess
  • More interesting - The examiner learns about YOU
  • Easier to say - You're talking about real things you know

Phrases for Giving Examples

Use These Phrases

For example, like such as For instance, especially

See the Difference

General
"I like sports."

What sports? We don't know!

With Example
"I like sports, especially football. For example, I play every Saturday with my friends."

Now we know exactly what you do!

Practice 1: Add an Example

Examiner asks:
"What do you usually do on weekends?"

Activity: Which answer has a good example?

A) "I do many things on weekends. I have fun."
B) "I usually relax on weekends. For example, I watch movies or go to the park with my dog."
C) "Weekend is good. I like weekend."

Practice 2: Make It Specific

Examiner asks:
"What kind of music do you like?"

Which answer is more specific?

A) "I like all kinds of music. Music is good for relaxing."
B) "I really like K-pop, especially BTS. For example, I listen to their songs when I'm exercising or studying."

Build a Complete Answer: All 3 Strategies!

Examiner asks:
"What kind of food do you like?"

Use All 3 Strategies Together!

1. Answer
I like Italian food. I enjoy Asian food. I prefer healthy food.
2. + Reason
because it's delicious. because it's good for me. because I grew up eating it.
3. + Example
For example, I love pasta and pizza. Especially, I enjoy sushi and noodles. For example, I eat a lot of salads and fruit.

Model Answer Using All 3 Strategies

"I like Italian food Answer + Detail because it's delicious and reminds me of holidays. Reason For example, I really love pasta, especially carbonara. Example I try to cook it at home sometimes. Extra detail"

Spot the Strategy!

7 minutes

Now let's test if you can identify which strategies are used in real answers. This will help you remember to use them yourself!

Analysis 1

Question: "Do you enjoy your work?"

"Yes, I really enjoy my work. I'm an accountant and I work for a small company. I like it because I have flexible hours and my colleagues are friendly."

Which strategies does this answer use?

See Answer ▼

Extend - Yes! Added details about being an accountant and working for a small company.

Reason - Yes! Used "because" to explain why they like it.

Example - No, there's no "for example" with specific instances.

This is a good answer, but adding an example would make it even better!

Analysis 2

Question: "What do you like to do in your free time?"

"I like watching movies in my free time, especially action films. For example, last weekend I watched a new Marvel movie. I enjoy them because they help me relax after work."

Which strategies does this answer use?

See Answer ▼

Extend - Yes! Specified "especially action films"

Reason - Yes! Used "because" to explain why they enjoy movies.

Example - Yes! Used "for example" with a specific movie.

This is an excellent Band 5+ answer! It uses all 3 strategies.

Analysis 3

Question: "Do you like your hometown?"

"Yes, I like my hometown. It's nice."

Which strategies does this answer use?

See Answer ▼

This answer uses NONE of the strategies!

Better version: "Yes, I like my hometown because it's quiet and peaceful. I grew up there, so I know many people. For example, my best friends from school still live there, and we meet every month."

Key Learning

Good IELTS answers use multiple strategies together. Try to use at least 2 of the 3 strategies in every answer!

Full Practice

10-15 minutes

Now use ALL THREE strategies together! We'll do several rounds of practice.

Round 1
Examiner asks:
"Do you live in a house or an apartment?"
Remember Your 3 Strategies!
1
Extend: House or apartment? How long? Where?
2
Reason: Why do you like/dislike it? (because...)
3
Example: Specific detail about your home (for example...)
See Model Answer ▼

"I live in an apartment in the city centre. I've been living there for about two years now. I like it because it's very convenient - there are shops and restaurants nearby. For example, I can walk to my favourite coffee shop in just five minutes."

Round 2
Examiner asks:
"How do you usually travel to work or school?"
Include:
1
What transport? How long does it take?
2
Why this transport? (because...)
3
What do you do during the journey? (for example...)
See Model Answer ▼

"I usually take the metro to work because it's the fastest way. The journey takes about 30 minutes. I like it because I can use the time to read or listen to music. For example, this morning I listened to a podcast about technology."

Round 3
Examiner asks:
"What do you usually do after work or school?"
Include:
1
What activities? When? With who?
2
Why these activities? (because...)
3
Specific example (for example...)
See Model Answer ▼

"After work, I usually go to the gym or relax at home. I try to exercise because it helps me feel less stressed. For example, yesterday I did a yoga class online. Sometimes I also cook dinner with my partner, which I really enjoy."

Review and Next Steps

5 minutes

Well Done!

You've learned the 3 key strategies for IELTS Speaking Part 1. These simple techniques can take you from Band 4 to Band 5!

The 3 Strategies - Summary

1
EXTEND
Never give just 1 sentence. Add when, where, who, what.
2
REASON
Use "because" or "so" to explain why.
3
EXAMPLE
Use "for example" or "like" to be specific.

Realistic Goals

Your Target

With practice, you can realistically aim for Band 4.5 - 5.0 in Speaking Part 1. This is a great achievement!

Focus on:

  • Using these 3 strategies every time
  • Speaking clearly (pronunciation)
  • Staying calm and natural

Remember

Band 5 is a good score! Many universities accept Band 5 for some courses. Don't compare yourself to Band 7 or 8 - that's for very advanced speakers.
