Lesson Objective

Ask and answer questions about big events and celebrations in your life using past simple tense. Learn celebrations vocabulary and practise telling personal stories.

Celebrations Vocabulary

Click each word to reveal its meaning.

celebration A special event or gathering to mark an important occasion
anniversary The date when something important happened in a previous year (like a wedding anniversary)
occasion An important event or a particular time when something happens
memorable Worth remembering; something that makes a strong impression
tradition A custom or belief that is passed down from one generation to another
