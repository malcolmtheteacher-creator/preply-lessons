How This Works

Each round gives you 6–8 items. You rank them by tapping an item, then tapping the slot where you want it to go. Gold, silver, bronze for your top 3.

Once you've ranked them all, speak about why — your top picks, your bottom picks, and whether anything surprised you.

There's also a spinning wheel later. It picks a bonus topic at random.

Useful Language

"For me, the most important..." "I'd put X above Y because..." "What people underestimate is..." "On reflection..." "I couldn't live without..." "X is overrated because..." "It depends on..." "If I had to choose..."

Reflection

Ranking vs. Justifying: Which was harder — deciding the order, or explaining why? What made it difficult?

Surprises: Did any ranking surprise you? Something you expected to be high that ended up low?

The Sacrifice Round: How different was choosing 3 to keep versus ranking all 8? Did it change your priorities?

The Spin Round: Was it harder when you didn't know the topic in advance? Did you feel more or less confident?
