Today's Goal

In this lesson, you'll:

  • Rank items on a scale of 1–8 (or fewer)
  • Justify your top 3 choices by speaking about why they matter
  • Justify your bottom 3 — explain why you put them there
  • Think aloud about trade-offs and priorities

No perfect answers. Only YOUR answers — with reasons behind them.

Useful Language

"For me, the most important..." "I'd put X above Y because..." "What people underestimate is..." "On reflection..." "I couldn't live without..." "X is overrated because..."

Reflection

Ranking vs. Justifying: Was it easier to rank the items, or to explain WHY you ranked them that way? What made the explanation harder or easier?

Priorities: Did any of your rankings surprise you? Was there something you thought was important until you ranked it?

The Sacrifice Round: That's real decision-making. How different was it from just ranking? Did it change your priorities?
