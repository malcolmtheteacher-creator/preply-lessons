Describing Charts and Graphs

Master vocabulary for trends: rise, fall, peak, fluctuate, and more

5:00

Upward Trends

Rise/Increase: Go up gradually (noun: rise, increase)

Climb/Soar: Go up quickly or dramatically

Peak: Reach the highest point

Rocket/Surge: Go up very suddenly and sharply

Downward Trends

Fall/Decline: Go down gradually

Drop/Plummet: Go down quickly or dramatically

Dip: Go down slightly before rising again

Slump/Crash: Go down very sharply

Stable Trends

Remain stable/steady: Stay the same

Level off: Stop going up or down and become flat

Plateau: Reach a level that stays constant

Fluctuate: Change up and down repeatedly

Describing Change

Dramatic/Sharp: Sudden and large change

Gradual/Gentle: Slow change over time

Slight/Minor: Small change

Significant/Notable: Important or noticeable change

Essential Vocabulary

Trend A general direction or tendency of change
Fluctuate Change frequently and unpredictably
Plateau A level area where values stay the same
Volatile Changing rapidly and unpredictably
Dramatic Sudden and striking; noticeable change
Precipitate Cause something to happen suddenly
Correlation Relationship between two variables
Axis A reference line on a graph (plural: axes)

Practice Exercises

Exercise 1: Choose the Right Word

The line shows: 2020: 100, 2021: 150, 2022: 200. What happened?

Values increased/rose/climbed steadily over the two-year period. Or: There was a consistent upward trend.

Exercise 2: Describe a Fluctuation

Stock prices went: high, low, high, low, high. What's happening?

Stock prices are volatile/fluctuating significantly. Or: The market shows considerable fluctuation.

Exercise 3: Describe a Plateau

Sales went up 2015-2018, then stayed flat 2018-2020. Describe it.

Sales increased from 2015 to 2018, then plateaued/leveled off between 2018 and 2020, remaining stable.

Example Sentences

Describing Trends

"Global temperatures have risen steadily over the past century."

"Unemployment rates fluctuated significantly during the recession."

"The company's profits soared in the third quarter."

"Internet usage has remained stable among all age groups."

"The graph shows a sharp decline in pollution levels."

"Population growth is slowing and beginning to plateau."

Key Takeaways

✓ Use specific trend verbs (rise, fall, peak, etc.)

✓ Describe the speed (gradual, dramatic, sharp)

✓ Note when change begins and ends

✓ Compare trends across time periods

✓ Use numbers or percentages when available

✓ Explain what trends mean or imply
