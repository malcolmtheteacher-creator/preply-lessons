Today's aim: Today we're learning to explain what happens when things melt, freeze and boil, so you can say what happens and why.

Words you'll need

solid (n) — something that keeps its shape, like ice or wood. Example: "Ice is a solid."
liquid (n) — something that flows, like water or milk. Example: "Water is a liquid."
gas (n) — something that spreads out to fill all the space, like air. Example: "Steam is a gas."
ice (n) — water that is frozen. Example: "Put some ice in my drink."
steam (n) — water as a hot gas. Example: "Steam comes out of the kettle."
particle (n) — a very, very small piece of something. Example: "Water is made of tiny particles."
evaporate (v) — to change from a liquid into a gas. Example: "Water evaporates from the sea."
shape (n) — the form of something, like round or square. Example: "A ball has a round shape."
fill (v) — to take all the space in something. Example: "Air fills the balloon."
cool (v) — to become less hot. Example: "Let the soup cool."

A question to start

You put a glass of water in the freezer. The next day it is a block of ice. You put the ice in a hot pan and it turns back into water, and then into steam.

Say it aloud: Is it the same stuff each time? What do you think changes?

It is the same stuff: water. What changes is how the tiny particles move. In ice they stay in place. In water they slide past each other. In steam they fly about freely.

Solid, liquid and gas

🧊 Solid

The particles are close together and stay in place. A solid keeps its shape.

💧 Liquid

The particles are close, but they can slide past each other. A liquid takes the shape of its container.

💨 Gas

The particles are far apart and move fast. A gas fills all the space it is in.

Language for explaining

Facts: the present simple

Ice melts at 0 °C.
Water boils at 100 °C.

Giving a reason: because and so

Water turns into steam because the particles move faster.
The particles move faster, so the water turns into steam.

If sentences

If you cool water to 0 °C, it freezes.
If you heat ice, it melts.

How is rain made?

The sun heats the sea. The water evaporates and goes up as a gas. High in the sky it is cold, so the gas turns back into tiny drops. The drops make clouds. When the drops are big and heavy, they fall as rain.

1 · Evaporate ☀️🌊
The sun heats the sea and the water turns into a gas.
2 · Cool ☁️
High in the sky it is cold, and the gas turns back into drops.
3 · Rain 🌧️
Heavy drops fall as rain.
