☀️ Estate ≠ Estate

A2 • Italian speakers • False friends: estate (summer) ≠ estate (property)

🔍 Let's notice what we see

Look at this sentence. What's the problem?

"In the estate it was very hot."

(The speaker wants to say it was hot during summer, not on a property!)

Does this sentence sound confusing? What's the issue?

⚠️ The False Friend:

The Italian word "estate" looks like English "estate", but they mean DIFFERENT things!

In Italian: estate = summer (l'estate scorsa = last summer)

In English: estate = property/land OR a person's possessions after death

In English: summer = the warm season (June, July, August)

🛠️ Understand the difference

📊 Italian estate vs English estate:

☀️ Italian: ESTATE

  • Summer season
  • June-August
  • Warm season
  • L'estate scorsa

🏠 English: ESTATE

  • Land/property
  • Country estate
  • Real estate
  • Possessions after death

🚨 When to use which

Important distinction:

estate → English: "summer"
Example: "In estate fa molto caldo" = "In summer it's very hot" ✓
NOT "In the estate it's very hot" ✗
estate → Italian: "proprietà/possesso"
Example: "The real estate agent sold the property" = "L'agente immobiliare ha venduto la proprietà" ✓

Choose the correct word: summer or estate. Check your answers when you're done.

1. "My grandparents own a country _____ with beautiful gardens."
Answer: "My grandparents own a country estate with beautiful gardens."

→ This is about property/land.

2. "During _____, schools are closed for vacation."
Answer: "During summer, schools are closed for vacation."

→ This is about the season.

3. "The lawyer handled the _____ after my uncle passed away."
Answer: "The lawyer handled the estate after my uncle passed away."

→ This is about possessions/property after death.

4. "We always go camping in _____ when the weather is warm."
Answer: "We always go camping in summer when the weather is warm."

→ This is about the warm season.
