📊 Comparative & Superlative Errors

Don't double the form | A2 Level

Let's see what you notice...

Read this sentence. What feels wrong?

She is more taller than me.

What's the correction?

✓ Correction

She is taller than me.

📌 The Rule

Short adjectives (1-2 syllables): add -ER/-EST (taller, tallest)

Long adjectives (3+ syllables): use more/most (more interesting, most beautiful)

NEVER combine both! NOT "more taller" or "most biggest"

📚 The Rule (Dual Coding)

📏 Short: -ER / -EST

➕

tall → taller → tallest

big → bigger → biggest

fast → faster → fastest

1-2 syllables only

📚 Long: MORE / MOST

🔡

beautiful → more beautiful → most beautiful

interesting → more interesting → most interesting

expensive → more expensive → most expensive

3+ syllables

💡 Irregular Forms: good → better → best, bad → worse → worst, far → farther/further → farthest/furthest
