Cause and Effect Expressions

These expressions help us show the relationship between a cause and an effect.

Match the Cause and Effect

A lack of education leads to unemployment.
Poor work-life balance is responsible for stress.
Obesity is a consequence of a sedentary lifestyle.
Lung cancer can result in smoking.
Air pollution is responsible for respiratory problems.
Car accidents are a result of dangerous driving.

Softening Your Opinion with Modal Verbs

When discussing causes and effects, we often use modal verbs to show uncertainty or show that something is not always true.

Can / Could / May / Might (possibility):
Smoking can lead to lung cancer.
Stress could result in sleep problems.
Online bullying might cause anxiety.
Often / Sometimes / Usually (frequency):
Poverty often leads to poor health outcomes.
A sedentary lifestyle sometimes causes obesity.
Social isolation usually results in depression.
Strong Statement (Present Simple):
Smoking leads to cancer.
Unemployment causes financial problems.
Racism is responsible for social division.
💡 Tip: Use modal verbs to sound more academic and respectful when discussing opinions about social issues. It shows you understand that causes and effects can be complex.

Write Your Paragraph

Choose a social or health issue and write a paragraph explaining its causes and effects. Use the target language you learned!

Topic Ideas: poverty, stress, online bullying, racism, isolation, unemployment, sedentary lifestyle, poor nutrition

Paragraph Structure

  • Topic Sentence: Introduce the issue (1 sentence)
  • Causes: What causes this problem? (1-2 sentences with cause/effect expressions)
  • Effects: What are the consequences? (1-2 sentences with cause/effect expressions)
  • Conclusion: Sum up your main idea (1 sentence)

Discussion Questions

Think about these questions and discuss them. There are no "right" answers—it's about expressing your opinion using cause and effect language.

1. Social Issues

What is the most serious social or health issue in your country right now? What causes it?

2. Causes

Think about unemployment. What are the main causes? Can you explain using "leads to" or "can result in"?

3. Effects

What are the consequences of social isolation? How does it affect people's health and happiness?

4. Solutions

Choose one issue you discussed. What could be done to solve it? What might be the consequences of these solutions?

5. Personal Connection

Have you ever experienced the effects of any of these issues? How did it affect you? (You don't have to share if you're not comfortable.)

6. Global Perspective

Are some of these issues more serious in some countries than others? Why do you think that is?
