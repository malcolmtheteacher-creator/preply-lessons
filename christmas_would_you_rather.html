🎄 Welcome to Christmas Would You Rather! 🎄

🎅 🎄 ⛄ 🎁 ❄️

How to Play:

  1. Choose one option from each "Would You Rather" question
  2. Explain your choice using the grammar we've learned
  3. Discuss with your teacher for 2-3 minutes per question
  4. Have fun and practice speaking naturally!

Grammar Review - Use These in Your Answers:

CONDITIONALS MODAL VERBS ABILITIES PAST TENSES FUTURE FORMS

💡 Example Answer:

Question: Would you rather spend Christmas in a hot or cold place?

Great Answer: "I would choose a cold place because if it snows, I can build a snowman. I have never seen snow before, so it would be amazing! If I go to a hot place, I will miss the traditional Christmas feeling."

🎤 Warm-Up Question

Would you rather:
Spend Christmas with your family at home
OR
Spend Christmas traveling somewhere exciting?

Discuss for 2 minutes. Use: would, if, can, will, have been...

❄️ Classic Christmas Dilemmas

Question 1

🌴

Hot Christmas

Spend Christmas on a tropical beach (30°C)

VS
❄️

Cold Christmas

Spend Christmas in a snowy mountain cabin (-10°C)

💬 Discussion Questions:

  • Why would you choose this option?
  • If you went there, what would you do?
  • Have you ever spent Christmas somewhere unusual?
  • What can you do in a hot/cold place at Christmas?

Question 2

🎁

Give Only

Only GIVE presents (never receive any)

VS
🎀

Receive Only

Only RECEIVE presents (never give any)

💬 Discussion Questions:

  • If you could only do one, which makes you happier?
  • Why might giving be better than receiving?
  • What's the best present you have ever given?
  • How would you feel if you never received gifts?

Question 3

🍗

VS
🎁

Gift Paradise

  • What's your favorite Christmas food? Have you ever tried...?
  • If you could choose any present, what would it be?

🗣️ Christmas Debates!

Debate Topic 1

"Christmas is TOO commercialized these days."

Do you agree or disagree?

💭 Think about:

  • If there were no advertisements, would Christmas be better?
  • What should Christmas really be about?
  • Have things changed since you were a child?
  • If you could change one thing, what would it be?

Debate Topic 2

"Everyone should spend Christmas with family, not friends."

Do you agree or disagree?

💭 Think about:

  • If someone doesn't have family, what should they do?
  • Can friends be like family?
  • What makes Christmas special - people or traditions?
  • Would you ever spend Christmas alone? Why/why not?

Debate Topic 3

"It's better to give expensive gifts than handmade ones."

Do you agree or disagree?

💭 Think about:

  • Which would you prefer to receive?
  • If you make something, does it show more love?
  • What's the best gift you have ever received?
  • Should we judge gifts by their price?

🎤 Final Debate Challenge (5 minutes)

Choose ONE topic and give a full argument:

  • State your position clearly
  • Give 3-4 reasons with examples
  • Use conditionals and modals
  • Conclude with your strongest point
