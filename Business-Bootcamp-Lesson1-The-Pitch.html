🔥 The SPARK Framework

Forget everything you've been told about pitching. No more "start with your background" or "build up slowly." SPARK cuts straight to what matters.

S Situation

Set up the problem or opportunity. Make your audience feel the pain or see the gap. "Right now, 40% of small businesses fail because..."

P Proposition

Your solution in one clear sentence. What are you offering? "We've created an app that..." — No jargon, no complexity.

A Advantage

Why you? Why now? What makes this different from everything else? "Unlike competitors, we..." — This is your edge.

R Results

Proof it works. Numbers, testimonials, traction. "In 6 months, we've achieved..." — Concrete evidence builds trust.

K Kicker

Your memorable close + clear ask. What do you want them to do? "I'm asking for £50,000 for 20% equity." End strong.

🔍 Mastering Each Element

S — Situation: The Hook That Grabs

Your first 10 seconds determine if people keep listening. Start with:

  • A surprising statistic: "Did you know that 73% of..."
  • A provocative question: "What if I told you..."
  • A relatable problem: "We've all been there — you're trying to..."

👆 Click to see a Situation done RIGHT vs WRONG

❌ Weak Situation

"So, um, we've been working on this idea for about 18 months now, and basically what happened was my co-founder and I were chatting one day and we realised that..."

✅ Strong Situation

"Every year, UK restaurants throw away £3 billion worth of food. That's not just waste — that's profit walking out the door."

P — Proposition: Crystal Clear Solution

One sentence. No jargon. A child should understand it.

👆 Click to see examples

❌ Jargon Overload

"We're a B2B SaaS platform leveraging AI-driven analytics to optimise supply chain inefficiencies through predictive modelling."

✅ Crystal Clear

"We help restaurants sell their leftover food at half price instead of throwing it away."

A — Advantage: Your Secret Weapon

Answer: "Why should I choose YOU over the 10 other options?"

Strong advantages are: specific, defensible, and meaningful to the audience.

👆 Click to see the difference

❌ Generic

"We have great customer service and really care about quality."

✅ Specific

"We're the only app partnered with all major UK supermarket chains, giving us exclusive access to 50,000 locations."

R — Results: The Proof

Numbers talk. Stories convince. Use both.

Types of proof: revenue, growth %, customer numbers, testimonials, awards, pilot results.

K — Kicker: The Memorable Close

Two parts: (1) A memorable line they'll remember, and (2) A clear ask — exactly what you want them to do.

📋 Full SPARK Examples

Let's see the complete framework in action. Study these, then you'll create your own.

Example 1: Food Waste App

SITUATION: "Every year, UK restaurants throw away £3 billion worth of perfectly good food. Meanwhile, millions of people are looking for affordable meals."

PROPOSITION: "FoodRescue connects restaurants with customers who buy surplus meals at 50% off, just before closing time."

ADVANTAGE: "Unlike competitors, we've secured exclusive partnerships with three major restaurant chains, giving us instant access to 2,000 locations."

RESULTS: "In our pilot across 50 restaurants, we saved 12 tonnes of food and generated £180,000 in revenue for our partners."

KICKER: "Good food deserves a second chance. I'm asking for £75,000 for 15% equity to take this nationwide."

Example 2: Leadership Training

SITUATION: "Companies spend £42 billion on leadership training every year. Yet 75% of employees say their manager is the worst part of their job. Something's not working."

PROPOSITION: "LeadRight delivers bite-sized leadership coaching through an app — 5 minutes a day, based on real scenarios from your actual workplace."

ADVANTAGE: "We don't teach theory. We capture real challenges from your team and turn them into micro-coaching moments. It's personalised in a way workshops can never be."

RESULTS: "After 90 days with our pilot companies, employee satisfaction with managers increased by 34%, and staff turnover dropped by 22%."

KICKER: "Better leaders aren't made in conference rooms — they're made in the moments that matter. Let me show you how we can transform your management team."

🔍 What Makes These Work?

  • Problem is felt, not just stated
  • Solution is immediately understandable
  • Advantage is specific and defensible
  • Results include real numbers
  • Kicker is memorable AND includes a clear ask

🏆 The Boardroom Challenge

It's time to face Lord Sugar. Deliver your complete SPARK pitch under pressure.

📺 Your Brief

You've made it to the boardroom. Lord Sugar is giving you 90 seconds maximum to pitch your idea. No waffle. No going over time. This is your moment.

Before You Pitch — Self-Check

  • My Situation hooks attention in the first 10 seconds
  • My Proposition is one clear sentence
  • My Advantage is specific (not generic)
  • My Results include at least one real number
  • My Kicker ends strong with a clear ask
  • The whole pitch is under 90 seconds
