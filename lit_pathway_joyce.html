Meet the writer · B1

James Joyce

1882-1941 · Ireland

An Irish writer who left Dublin and then wrote about nothing else for the rest of his life.

Guess first

Joyce's novel Ulysses takes place over...

A life in a few steps

  1. 1882Born in Dublin.
  2. 1904Leaves Ireland with Nora Barnacle. They live in Trieste, Zurich and Paris.
  3. 1914Dubliners: fifteen stories about ordinary people in Dublin.
  4. 1922Ulysses, a huge novel set in Dublin on one single day, 16 June 1904.
  5. 1941Dies in Zurich, aged 58.

Why people still read Joyce

Ulysses changed what a novel could do: it shows every thought in a character's head. Many writers say it is the most important novel of the twentieth century.

Did you know?

Every 16 June is 'Bloomsday'. Fans in Dublin and around the world dress up and follow the route of the novel's hero, Leopold Bloom.
