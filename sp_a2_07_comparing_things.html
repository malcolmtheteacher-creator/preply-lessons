Micro-Skill: 5 Comparison Structures

Click each structure to see how it works:

...er than / more...than Click to expand

The basic comparison - showing one thing is MORE than another:

Short adjective + ER + than

"The beach is quieter than the city."

"Trains are faster than buses."

MORE + long adjective + than

"Flying is more expensive than driving."

"Reading is more relaxing than watching TV."

🎤 NOW YOU TRY

Compare: Coffee vs Tea. Use "...er than" or "more...than"

not as...as Click to expand

A softer way to compare - saying something is LESS:

A is not as [adjective] as B

"The movie wasn't as good as the book."

"Cooking at home isn't as expensive as eating out."

"Monday isn't as busy as Friday."

Pro tip: This sounds more polite than "worse than" or "less than"!

🎤 NOW YOU TRY

Compare: Your city vs a bigger city. Use "not as...as"

similar to / different from Click to expand

Talking about how things relate:

A is similar to B (in that...)

"Spanish is similar to Italian."

"Working from home is similar to being at the office in that you still have meetings."

A is (completely/quite/very) different from B

"City life is completely different from country life."

"This restaurant is quite different from the one we went to last week."

🎤 NOW YOU TRY

Compare: Your job/studies now vs 5 years ago. Use "similar to" or "different from"

whereas / while Click to expand

Showing contrast in one sentence:

A..., whereas/while B...

"I'm a morning person, whereas my partner prefers evenings."

"Coffee gives you quick energy, while tea is more gradual."

"City apartments are convenient, whereas houses give you more space."

Note: "Whereas" is slightly more formal than "while".

🎤 NOW YOU TRY

Compare: Yourself vs a friend/family member. Use "whereas" or "while"

On the other hand... Click to expand

Showing both sides - great for balanced comparisons:

[Point A]. On the other hand, [Point B].

"Living in the city is exciting. On the other hand, it can be stressful."

"Cars give you freedom. On the other hand, public transport is better for the environment."

Perfect for: Showing you've thought about both sides before giving your opinion!

🎤 NOW YOU TRY

Compare: Having children vs not having children. Use "On the other hand"
