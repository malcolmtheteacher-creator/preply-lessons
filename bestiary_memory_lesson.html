The Impossible Challenge
Let's see what your brain can do right now...
Your Mission

You will see 8 famous people and what they did. You have 45 seconds to memorize them. Then they disappear. Ready?

The Secret
Your brain isn't bad at memory. It just needs the right system.
The Problem

Your brain is terrible at remembering lists of words. Names, dates, facts -- they just fall out.

But your brain is incredible at remembering pictures, stories, and weird things.

The Trick

Every name starts with two letters. Those two letters match a creature from the Bestiary -- an ancient book of beasts.

You turn the name into a creature. Then you make the creature DO something connected to the person. The weirder the picture, the stronger the memory.

Build Your Scenes
Today: 5 people you'll actually want to remember. Write a scene for each one.

Today we're learning about 5 people who changed the world -- and you're going to remember all of them using the bestiary.

Writing tip: The best memory scenes are specific, weird, and use action. Don't write "A fish swims." Write "A fish slaps a calculator off the desk with its tail, and numbers fly everywhere."
