Whole Unit Recall: GCSE RS Foundations

What are the two papers in AQA GCSE RS A?

🔒Reveal answer

Answer: Paper 1: Religion (Christianity & Islam). Paper 2: Thematic Studies (Relationships, Religion & Life, Peace & Conflict, Crime & Punishment).

Name three key Christian beliefs.

🔒Reveal answer

Answer: Any of: God is omnipotent/omnibenevolent/omniscient; Trinity (Father, Son, Holy Spirit); Incarnation (God becoming human); Resurrection (Jesus rose from the dead); Salvation (forgiveness through faith).

What is Tawhid in Islam?

🔒Reveal answer

Answer: Tawhid is the oneness and unity of God (Allah). It's the foundation of Islam: no god but Allah, and Allah is one and indivisible.

What are the four parts of a PEEL paragraph?

🔒Reveal answer

Answer: Point (state your idea), Evidence (specific example), Explain (why it matters), Link (back to the question).

Assessment Review Cards

What command word requires you to make a judgment with multiple perspectives?

🔒Reveal answer

Answer: Evaluate (6 marks or 12 marks on extended responses). Use "Some... However... Therefore" structure.

What's the difference between AO1 and AO2?

🔒Reveal answer

Answer: AO1 = knowledge (facts and definitions). AO2 = understanding and evaluation (explaining why and making judgments).

Name two of the Four Themes studied in Paper 2.

🔒Reveal answer

Answer: Any of: Theme A (Relationships & Families), Theme B (Religion & Life), Theme D (Peace & Conflict), Theme E (Crime & Punishment).

What's the most important skill you'll develop in GCSE RS?

🔒Reveal answer

Answer: Evaluation — learning to judge ideas, consider multiple perspectives, and write reasoned arguments. This appears in 6-mark and 12-mark questions.
