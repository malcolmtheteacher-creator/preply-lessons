Grammar: There's / There are

To say what is in a place, English uses there is and there are.

There's = There is. One thing: "There's a bathroom." "There's a big table in the living room."
There are. Two or more: "There are three bedrooms." "There are two cars in the garage."
Questions: "Is there a garage?" — "Yes, there is. / No, there isn't." · "How many rooms are there?" — "Five."
live in / live with. "I live in a house." "I live in a small town." "I live with my family." Alone? "I live alone."
