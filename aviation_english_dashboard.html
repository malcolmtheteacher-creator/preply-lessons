How to use this pathway

  1. One lesson per session, in order — each one trains a different section of the exam and assumes the previous.
  2. Speak everything aloud. The timers only measure what your voice actually does — reading silently trains nothing the exam tests.
  3. For the radio dialogues, the drills work best when someone reads the exchange to you at natural speed, once — that is the closest thing to the real recordings.
  4. Finish with Lesson 6 a few days before the test, and let its self-check send you back to whichever lesson needs a second pass.
