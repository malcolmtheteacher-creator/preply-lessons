Year 8: Comparing & Connecting

"How do beliefs shape the way people live?"

What Year 8 Is About

In Year 7, you encountered the world's major religions and worldviews for the first time. Now it's time to go deeper. Year 8 is about comparing and connecting — looking at how different traditions respond to the same big questions, and discovering what happens when beliefs meet real life. You'll explore the Abrahamic faiths, wrestle with the problem of evil, examine rites of passage, and consider whether religion is a force for peace or conflict. By the end of this year, you'll be able to make thoughtful comparisons across traditions and build well-supported arguments.

How This Course Works

  • One lesson per week — Each unit takes 6 weeks, with lesson materials, activities, and discussion prompts.
  • Compare and connect — Year 8 builds on Year 7 by comparing how different traditions respond to the same questions. You'll develop the skill of balanced, evidence-based analysis.
  • Learn from sources — We study religious texts, art, rituals, and stories. You'll work with primary sources and learn to interpret them across traditions.
  • Respectful inquiry — We ask big questions: What does this belief mean? Why is it important? How do different traditions compare? No belief is mocked; all are explored with intellectual seriousness.
  • Assessment is ongoing — Each unit ends with a task that checks your understanding: knowledge checks, extended writing, and evaluation essays.

Year 8 deepens your understanding. By comparing traditions, you develop the analytical skills that prepare you for GCSE and beyond.
