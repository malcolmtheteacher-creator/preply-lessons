What is the Christian view on the purpose of marriage?

🔒Reveal answer

Answer: Christians teach marriage has two purposes: (1) unitive (love and commitment), (2) procreative (children). Marriage is a lifelong covenant. Mark 10:9 says "no one should separate."

What is the Islamic view on marriage (Nikah)?

🔒Reveal answer

Answer: Nikah is a legal contract based on mutual consent between man and woman. Marriage is half of faith (Hadith). Emphasizes responsibility and companionship, not passion.

What is cohabitation and Christian views?

🔒Reveal answer

Answer: Living together without marriage. Traditional Christianity opposes it (sex outside marriage is sin). Modern Christians more divided. Marriage required for sexual relations traditionally.

What are Christian and Muslim views on divorce?

🔒Reveal answer

Answer: Christian: Marriage indissoluble; some allow for adultery. Muslim: Divorce (Talaq) permitted but "most disliked of permissible things" (Hadith). Three pronouncements make it final.

What is Roman Catholic teaching on contraception?

🔒Reveal answer

Answer: Humanae Vitae (1968): contraception is immoral; only natural family planning allowed. Separates procreation from unitive purpose. Most Catholics use contraception anyway.

What is Islamic permission on contraception?

🔒Reveal answer

Answer: Islamic scholars permit contraception for family wellbeing. Not forbidden by Quran. Various methods acceptable if both spouses agree. Differs from Catholic absolute ban.

What does zina mean and why is it condemned?

🔒Reveal answer

Answer: Zina means any sexual relations outside marriage. Condemned in Quran 17:32. Severe penalties in Shariah (flogging, stoning). Protects family and social order.

What are Christian views on gender roles in families?

🔒Reveal answer

Answer: Traditional: Ephesians 5 (wives submit to husbands). Modern: equality and mutual submission. Complementarian vs egalitarian debate continues in Christian churches.

What does Quran 4:34 teach about gender roles?

🔒Reveal answer

Answer: Men are "maintainers" of women (Qawwamun). Interpreted traditionally as male headship; modern scholars emphasize mutual responsibility and historical context.

What is the Christian teaching on gender equality?

🔒Reveal answer

Answer: Galatians 3:28: "neither male nor female" in Christ. Modern churches: women priests and bishops. Biblical basis for equality despite patriarchal culture of biblical times.

Key Concepts

Marriage in Christianity

Sacramental view: Marriage is a sacrament (sign of God's grace). Two purposes: unitive (bonding) and procreative (children). Indissoluble covenant.

Biblical basis: Genesis 2:24 (becoming one flesh), Ephesians 5:25-33 (Christ and Church), Mark 10:9 (no separation).

Modern challenges: Cohabitation, same-sex relationships, divorce rates. Different denominations respond differently.

Marriage in Islam (Nikah)

Legal contract: Nikah is a binding agreement between man and woman with witnesses. Both must consent freely.

Purpose: Companionship, procreation, mutual support. "Half of faith" (Hadith). Not a sacrament but divinely guided institution.

Key features: Mahr (gift from groom), mutual rights and duties, can be dissolved through Talaq.

Christian Divorce

Catholic: Indissoluble; annulment only (declares never valid).

Protestant: Generally allow for adultery (Matthew 19:9). Increasingly compassionate on other grounds.

Mark 10:9: "What God joined, let no one separate."

Islamic Divorce (Talaq)

Three types: Talaq (revocable), Talaq Bain (irrevocable).

Process: Husband pronounces "I divorce you" three times (though disputed).

Waiting period: Iddah (3-4 months) before finalized. Maintenance (Nafaqah) for wife.

Key Terms & Concepts

Essential Vocabulary

Sacrament: Outward sign of inward grace; marriage as sacred covenant.

Nikah: Islamic marriage contract.

Talaq: Islamic divorce; husband pronounces "I divorce you".

Zina: Forbidden sexual relations outside marriage in Islam.

Mahr: Gift from groom to bride in Islamic marriage.

Iddah: Waiting period (3 months/4 cycles) after divorce in Islam.

Contraception: Birth control. Humanae Vitae forbids; Islam permits with consent.

Complementarianism: Men and women have equal worth but different roles.

Egalitarianism: Complete equality in all roles and responsibilities.

Cohabitation: Unmarried couples living together; traditionally forbidden in Christianity.

What is the key difference between annulment and divorce?

🔒Reveal answer

Answer: Annulment declares marriage never valid (Catholic); divorce dissolves valid marriage. Annulment allows remarriage in Church; divorce historically did not.

Why do Muslims emphasize consent in Nikah?

🔒Reveal answer

Answer: Both man and woman must freely agree. Protects women rights and ensures ethical marriage founded on mutual agreement.

How do modern Christians and Muslims differ on gender equality?

🔒Reveal answer

Answer: Christianity: Many embrace full equality; women priests. Islam: Traditional interpretation emphasizes male guardianship; modern scholars reinterpret as mutual responsibility.
