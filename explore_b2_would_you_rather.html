Structures that make you sound balanced

The upside is … , but the downside is …Shows both sides. "The upside is the freedom; the downside is the uncertainty."
All things considered, … / On balance, …Gives your final judgement after weighing everything.
Even if …, I'd still …Shows a strong preference. "Even if it were free, I'd still say no."
Having said that, …Introduces a contrast with what you just said.

Explain it your way

What is an opportunity cost? Explain it in your own words with an example of your own.

The opportunity cost of a choice is the best alternative you give up. For example, if I spend Saturday working, the opportunity cost is the time I could have spent with friends.

What can you remember?

Answer out loud first. Then click to check.

1. Give me three ways to soften an opinion.
I'd say … · It seems to me that … · To some extent … · In my experience … · I tend to think …
2. What is the difference between the present perfect simple and continuous?
Simple focuses on the result ("I've written three emails"). Continuous focuses on the activity and its duration ("I've been writing emails all morning").
3. How can you interrupt politely?
"Sorry to interrupt, but …" · "Can I just add something?" · "If I could jump in here …"
4. Give me three ways to talk about the future with different levels of certainty.
I'll definitely … · I'm likely to … · I might … · I'm planning to … · I'm meeting … on Friday (arrangement).
5. When do we use the passive voice?
When the action matters more than who did it, when we don't know who did it, or when we want a more formal tone. "The decision was made last week."
