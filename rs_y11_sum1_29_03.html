What is Tawhid?

🔒Reveal answer

Answer: The oneness and uniqueness of God (Allah); central principle of Islam; belief that nothing equals God.

Name the Six Articles of Belief in Islam.

🔒Reveal answer

Answer: 1. God (Allah), 2. Angels, 3. Holy Books (Torah, Psalms, Gospel, Qur'an), 4. Prophets, 5. Day of Judgment, 6. Predestination.

What are the Five Roots of Usul ad-Din (Shi'a)?

🔒Reveal answer

Answer: 1. Tawhid (God's oneness), 2. Adl (God's justice), 3. Nubuwwah (Prophethood), 4. Imamah (Divine leadership), 5. Akhirah (Afterlife).

Who is Muhammad in Islamic belief?

🔒Reveal answer

Answer: The final prophet and messenger of God; brought the Qur'an; example (sunnah) for all Muslims.

What is Predestination (Qadr) in Islam?

🔒Reveal answer

Answer: God's complete knowledge and control of all events; humans have free will within God's plan.

Define Akhirah.

🔒Reveal answer

Answer: The Islamic concept of the afterlife; Day of Judgment; paradise (Jannah) or hell (Jahannam).

What is Risalah?

🔒Reveal answer

Answer: Prophethood; the belief that Allah sends prophets to guide humanity; Muhammad is the seal of prophets.

What is the Qur'an?

🔒Reveal answer

Answer: The holy book of Islam; believed by Muslims to be God's final, perfect revelation; revealed to Muhammad.

What is the Sunnah?

🔒Reveal answer

Answer: The example and teachings of Muhammad recorded in hadith; source of Islamic law alongside the Qur'an.

Name one key difference between Sunni and Shi'a Islam.

🔒Reveal answer

Answer: Leadership: Sunni believe in elected caliphs; Shi'a believe in divinely guided Imams with special knowledge.

Understanding Islamic Structure

The Relationship Between Articles and Roots

Six Articles of Belief (Sunni): Core beliefs all Muslims share.

Five Roots (Shi'a): Expand and deepen Sunni belief with emphasis on justice and leadership.

Key point: Both traditions affirm belief in God, prophets, and afterlife; they differ on political/spiritual leadership.

Guidance

Exam tip: The AQA paper tests both beliefs. Be ready to explain Sunni views, and note where Shi'a differ (especially on Imamah).

Quick-Fire Assessment

What is Adl?

Answer: God's justice; belief that God is fair and merciful; emphasised in Shi'a theology.

Name the Muslim concept of paradise.

Answer: Jannah (Garden); reward for the righteous on the Day of Judgment.

What does the Sunnah include?

Answer: The words, actions, and example of Muhammad; recorded in hadith collections; guides Muslim law and behaviour.

Sunni vs Shi'a at a Glance

Key Distinctions for Paper 1

  • Leadership: Sunni—elected caliphs; Shi'a—divinely guided Imams.
  • Imamah: Not in Six Articles; central to Five Roots.
  • Ijtihad: Interpretation of law; Shi'a emphasise scholar reasoning; Sunni vary.
  • Population: ~85% Sunni, ~15% Shi'a (majority in Iran, Iraq).
