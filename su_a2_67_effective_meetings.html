Language Focus: Opinions and Agreement

Asking for Opinions

Choose the best option:

1. What"s your opinion?

2. What about you?

3. What"s your position on hiring?

4. What do you think?

Expressing Your Opinion

1. I believe that experience is important.

2. I feel that energy is essential.

3. Personally, I prefer confident people.

Discussion: Making Decisions Together

Discuss these questions. Use the language from this lesson. Agree or disagree with your partner"s ideas.

1. Sales Manager for Fashion:

We need someone for ION Fashion Agency. The company values creativity and team leadership. What qualities matter most? Do you agree with the hiring managers?

2. Your Workplace:

In your workplace or school, what qualities do the best employees or students have? Which adjectives from the lesson describe them?

3. Disagreement in Meetings:

You disagree with your boss about hiring someone. How do you politely express your disagreement? When is it difficult to disagree?

4. Dream Team:

If you could hire four people for any job, what qualities would each have? Why would they be good together?

5. Your Opinion:

What job would you like? What qualities do you have? What qualities do you want to develop?
