Hannah Arendt

1906-1975 · Germany and New York

A German-Jewish political thinker who watched a Nazi organiser on trial and wrote about 'the banality of evil'.

A life in a few steps

  1. 1906Born near Hanover, Germany, and grows up in Königsberg.
  2. 1933Arrested by the Gestapo. She escapes to Paris.
  3. 1941Escapes again, this time to New York.
  4. 1951The Origins of Totalitarianism. She becomes an American citizen.
  5. 1961Reports on the trial of Adolf Eichmann in Jerusalem.
  6. 1975Dies in New York.

Why Arendt still matters

She argued that terrible things can be done by ordinary people who simply stop thinking for themselves.

Main works

The Origins of TotalitarianismThe Human ConditionEichmann in Jerusalem

Did you know?

For fourteen years, after Nazi Germany took away her citizenship, she was not a citizen of any country.
