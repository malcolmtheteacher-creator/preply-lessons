Beyond Oil: A Region That Is Changing

Some countries have oil, and many do not. Now many countries are building new industries.

Today: you will talk about the future of the region, and use will to make predictions.
Talk first:
  • What does your country make or sell?
  • Is solar power common where you live?
  • What job do you want in the future?

Key words

Read the sentence. What does the bold word mean? Then click Reveal.

1. Some countries have a lot of oil.
oil (n) — a thick liquid from under the ground, used for fuel. Example: “Some countries have a lot of oil.”
2. The sun gives energy for solar panels.
sun (n) — the bright star that gives us light and heat. Example: “The sun gives energy for solar panels.”
3. Young people want a good job.
job (n) — the work that you do to earn money. Example: “Young people want a good job.”
4. The new university is very modern.
university (n) — a place where you study after school. Example: “The new university is very modern.”
5. A factory in Morocco makes cars.
factory (n) — a building where people make things. Example: “A factory in Morocco makes cars.”
6. In the future, there will be more solar power.
future (n) — the time that is coming after now. Example: “In the future, there will be more solar power.”
Talk it through:
  • Which word is new for you? Say a sentence with it.
The stereotype: “The whole region is rich from oil.”

The story

Part 1 — Some oil, some not

Some countries, like Saudi Arabia, Qatar and Kuwait, have a lot of oil. Other countries, like Jordan, Lebanon, Tunisia and Morocco, have very little. Some people are very rich and some are poor. Qatar has high incomes. Yemen is one of the poorest countries.

Quick check:

1. Which countries have a lot of oil?

2. Does Morocco have a lot of oil?

1. For example Saudi Arabia, Qatar and Kuwait.

2. No.

Discuss:
  • Does your country have oil?

Part 2 — New industries

Oil does not last forever, so many countries are building new industries. Dubai has a very big solar park. Morocco has a big solar power station. It also has factories that make cars. Many countries have more tourists, new universities and technology companies.

Quick check:

1. What does Dubai have?

2. What do Moroccan factories make?

1. A very big solar park.

2. Cars.

Discuss:
  • What new building is there in your city?

Part 3 — Young people

More than half of the people in the region are under 30. They want good jobs. Many young people study science and engineering. In the future, there will be more solar power and more technology companies. It will not be easy for everyone, but many people are working hard.

Quick check:

1. How many people in the region are under 30?

2. What will there be more of in the future?

1. More than half.

2. Solar power and technology companies.

Discuss:
  • What will your town be like in ten years?

Grammar — will for predictions

Will + verb for predictions: “There will be more solar power.”
Negative: won't: “Oil won't last forever.”
Questions: “Will there be more jobs?” — Yes, there will. / No, there won't.

1. Choose the word

1. In the future, there more solar power. (will be / is)

2. Oil last forever. (will / won't)

3. young people have good jobs? (Will / Do)

2. Make your own

1. In ten years, my city will ______.

2. Next year, I will ______.

3. People won't ______.

2. ...learn a new skill. / ...visit a new place.
