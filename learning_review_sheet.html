Key Vocabulary & Expressions

pick something up - learn informally or easily
Example: I picked up some Spanish while traveling in Mexico.

get the hang of it - start to understand how to do something
Example: Learning to drive was difficult at first, but I got the hang of it after a few weeks.

trial and error - learn by trying different ways
Example: I learned to cook through trial and error - I made many mistakes!

steep learning curve - something very difficult to learn
Example: Using the new software had a steep learning curve.

practice makes perfect - the more you do it, the better you get
Example: Don't worry about mistakes - practice makes perfect!

learn the ropes - learn the basic procedures
Example: It took me a month to learn the ropes at my new job.

it's second nature - so automatic you don't think about it
Example: After 10 years of driving, it's second nature to me now.

master a skill - become very skilled at something
Example: It takes years to master a musical instrument.

self-taught - learned without a teacher
Example: Many successful programmers are self-taught.

hands-on experience - learn by doing, not just reading
Example: The best way to learn is through hands-on experience.

Grammar Review

Past Perfect - for actions that happened before another past action:

  • By the time I was 10, I had already learned to swim.
  • I had never driven a car before I moved to the countryside.
  • Had you studied English before you came here?

Structure: had + past participle

Used to - for past habits and states that are no longer true:

  • I used to be afraid of speaking English in public.
  • My teacher used to give us homework every day.
  • I didn't use to practice regularly, but now I do.

Structure: used to + base verb
