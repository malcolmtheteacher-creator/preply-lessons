Used to - Past Habits

We use "used to" to talk about things we did regularly in the past but don't do now. It shows a change from the past to the present.

Affirmative Sentences

I used to wear braces when I was at school.
She used to have problems with self-confidence.
We used to have homework every day.

Form: Subject + used to + base verb

Negative Sentences

I didn't use to like sports.
They didn't use to have computers at school.
She didn't use to enjoy social events.

Form: Subject + didn't use to + base verb

Questions

Did you use to wear a uniform?
Did he use to play sports?
What did you use to do at break time?

Form: Did + subject + use to + base verb

Conversation Tips

When you present your backstory, try to add details and show interest:

  • Speak naturally - don't just read the sentences
  • Add more information: "I used to be very shy, so I didn't use to have many friends."
  • Ask questions back: "Did you use to play sports too?"
  • Show interest in the other person's story

Reflection & Discussion

Think about your own school experiences and discuss these questions.

❯ What was your school like?
Think about your childhood school. Describe it using "used to": What did you use to do? What was the school like? How did you use to feel?
❯ Tell us about your best school friend
Did you have a close friend at school? What used to you two do together? Do you still use to see them? Why or why not?
❯ What's changed since school?
What didn't you use to do then that you do now? What used you to love that you don't care about anymore? How have you changed?
❯ If you could talk to your younger self...
What would you say? Think about the things you used to worry about. Would you change anything about your school experience?
❯ Most interesting fact about school days
What's the most interesting or surprising thing you learned about the other person's school days today? How was it different from your own experience?
