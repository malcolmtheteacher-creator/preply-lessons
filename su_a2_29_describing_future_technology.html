The grammar rule

Before "will": Definitely/Probably/Possibly, [subject] will...

Before main verb: [Subject] will definitely/probably/possibly [verb]...

See it all together — click to reveal each example

Opinion + adverb + reason
I think electric cars will ___...
"I think electric cars will definitely become cheaper. The technology is improving every year — that's what I think, anyway."
Opinion + prediction + reason
In my opinion, people will probably...
"In my opinion, people will probably work from home more, for one key reason — technology is getting better every year."
Uncertain opinion + hedging
I reckon Mars holidays...
"I reckon Mars holidays will possibly happen in the future, but for a number of reasons, they will be extremely expensive."

Your Country in 20 Years

Choose 2 or 3 topics below. Click each one to get a question to think about. Then use the timer to prepare your ideas.

🤖
Robots
Will robots be part of daily life?
Think about: housework, factories, hospitals, teachers. Which jobs will robots do — and which will they never do?
🚗
Transport
How will people travel?
Will everyone drive an electric car? Will there be flying taxis? Will people travel less because of remote work?
💼
Work & Jobs
What will jobs look like?
Will most people work from home? Will AI replace many jobs? Will people work fewer hours per week?
🏥
Lifespan & Health
How long will people live?
Will scientists find cures for cancer and Alzheimer's? Will the average lifespan reach 100? What problems might this cause?
🎓
Education
How will people learn?
Will children still go to school? Will AI be a personal tutor for everyone? Will universities still exist in the same form?
☀️
Energy
What energy sources will we use?
Will solar and wind power replace oil and gas completely? Will nuclear energy come back? Will energy become very cheap?
