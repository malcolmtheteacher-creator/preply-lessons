Telephone Conversation Role Play

You will practise making and answering telephone enquiries about a city.

Your Roles

Role A: Travel Agency Employee

You work for a local travel agency. Answer questions about your city. Help the caller with information about places to visit, costs, opening times, transport, restaurants, and hotels.

Role B: Caller

You want to visit a city. Get details about these things from the travel agency:

  • Places to visit
  • Costs
  • Opening times
  • Transport
  • Restaurants and hotels

How to Do This Activity

Try both roles! Start as the caller, then switch and be the travel agency employee.

Remember to use these phrases:

  • "I'm calling about..."
  • "Do you provide...?"
  • "Could you recommend...?"
  • "How may I help you?"
  • "Certainly..."

Role Play Timer

Spend time on each role. Switch when the timer ends.

Practice Tips

  • Speak naturally and slowly
  • Use the telephone phrases you learned
  • Listen carefully to what your conversation partner says
  • Ask for clarification if you don't understand: "Could you say that again?"
  • Be polite and professional
