Part 1 — I have the contract

A week later. Monday morning. The contract is on Leo's desk. He signs it. Then he calls London.

Mr Hill: Leo! Good to hear you.

Leo: Good morning, Mr Hill. I have the contract. I'll sign it today and send it this afternoon.

Mr Hill: Excellent.

Leo: And… I'd like to invite you to my city. In March?

Mr Hill: March! Yes. I'd love to.

Quick check — answer from memory, then reveal:

1. What does Leo do with the contract?

2. What does he invite Mr Hill to?

1. He signs it today and sends it this afternoon.

2. To his city, in March.

Talk about it:
  • And you? Do you invite customers to your city? What do you show them?

Part 2 — The twelfth

Leo: When is good for you?

Mr Hill: Let me look at my calendar. The first week of March is busy… What about the twelfth?

Leo: The twelfth of March. One moment. Yes, the twelfth is fine.

Mr Hill: Tuesday the twelfth. I'll fly in the morning.

Leo: I'll meet you at the airport.

Mr Hill: You don't have to.

Leo: I know. But I will.

He writes it in his calendar: 12 March — Mr Hill — airport, 10:30.

Quick check — answer from memory, then reveal:

1. What date do they choose?

2. Where will Leo meet him?

1. Tuesday the twelfth of March.

2. At the airport, at half past ten.

Talk about it:
  • And you? What is a busy month for you? What is a quiet month?

Part 3 — Carla will cook

Mr Hill: Where will I stay?

Leo: I'll book a hotel near the port. Small, quiet, good breakfast.

Mr Hill: Like the Park Hotel?

Leo: Better than the Park Hotel. And on Tuesday evening, dinner. Carla will cook.

Mr Hill: Carla will cook?

Leo: Fish. It's delicious.

Mr Hill: Then I'll bring the wine.

Leo: Deal. See you in March.

Mr Hill: See you in March, Leo.

He puts the phone down. Carla is in the doorway. "I will cook?" "You will cook." "Then you will do the plates." "Deal."

Quick check — answer from memory, then reveal:

1. Where will Mr Hill stay?

2. What will Carla cook, and what will Mr Hill bring?

1. In a small, quiet hotel near the port. Leo will book it.

2. Fish. Mr Hill will bring the wine.

Talk about it:
  • And you? What do you cook for guests? Who does the plates?

Grammar: I'll… / Will you…? / dates and months

Plans and promises use will. Then you need a date to put them on.

I'll = I will. A plan or a promise, now: "I'll sign it today." "I'll book a hotel." "I'll meet you at the airport." "Carla will cook."
Questions and no: "Where will I stay?" "Will you come in March?" — "Yes, I will. / No, I won't." (won't = will not)
Dates: "the twelfth of March" (12 March) · "on Tuesday" · "on the twelfth" · "in March" · "at 10:30" · first, second, third, fourth, fifth, sixth, seventh, eighth, ninth, tenth, eleventh, twelfth, twentieth, thirtieth.
Months: January, February, March, April, May, June, July, August, September, October, November, December. "What about the fifth of May?" "When is good for you?"
