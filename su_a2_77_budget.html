💡 Think about what voters wanted and what the winning candidate promised

Key idea: Politicians make promises about the FUTURE. Today you'll learn the language they use to talk about what they WILL, PLAN TO, or HOPE TO do.

Future Plans Language: How Politicians Talk About Tomorrow

Click each phrase to see examples and how to use it:

"will" (promise) Click to expand

The strongest future promise. Certain, definite.

"We will lower taxes"
"We will build new schools"
"We will fix the roads"
"We will create more jobs"

Try: What will your government do in the next year?

"plan to" (intention) Click to expand

A serious intention. Thought-out, organized.

"We plan to improve healthcare"
"We plan to reduce pollution"
"We plan to invest in technology"
"We plan to cut costs"

Try: What do you plan to do this week?

"hope to" (wish/desire) Click to expand

Optimistic but less certain. It's a dream or goal.

"We hope to make everyone feel richer"
"We hope to reduce crime"
"We hope to have zero unemployment"
"We hope to clean the rivers"

Try: What do you hope to achieve in your life?

"promise to" (commitment) Click to expand

A personal commitment. You're taking responsibility.

"I promise to keep interest rates low"
"I promise to never raise taxes"
"I promise to listen to ordinary people"
"I promise to be honest"

Try: What can you promise to do in your next lesson?

"want to" (desire) Click to expand

Simple desire or goal. Personal and direct.

"We want to reduce VAT"
"We want to support small businesses"
"We want to help young people"
"We want to make life easier"

Try: What do you want to do after this lesson?

Mayor for a Day: Your Mayoral Proposals

You are now the mayor of your city. What problems will you solve?

Pre-Task: If you were the mayor of your city, what problem would you address?

💡 Think: traffic, pollution, schools, parks, safety, jobs, housing...

Preparation & Speaking: 3 Minutes

3:00

Your Task: Present Your Mayoral Proposals

Use these sentence starters to present your ideas:

"I'd like to focus on the problem of..."

"I propose that we..."

"We will/plan to/hope to + solution"

Identify one problem in your city
Propose at least one solution
Use future language: will / plan to / hope to / want to / promise to
Speak for at least 2-3 minutes

Example: "I'd like to focus on the problem of traffic in our city. I propose that we build more bike lanes and improve public transport. We will invest in new buses and trains. We hope to reduce car use by 30% within two years. I promise to make our city cleaner and more modern."
