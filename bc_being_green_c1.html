Expressing Attitudes to Green Behaviour

These phrases help you discuss environmental attitudes:

I feel strongly that...
I'm all for...
I'm a very conscious...
...is key

Practice: Complete the Sentences

1. I feel strongly that (complete with a belief about the environment): ...supporting organic farming is important.

2. I'm all for helping the environment, but I don't: ...want to spend significantly more money on everything.

3. One thing that drives me crazy is (describe annoying behaviour): ...people using plastic bags for single items.

4. For me, is key (complete with what matters): ...reducing overall consumption is key.

Green Myths and Facts

Test Your Knowledge

1. Appliances use no energy when turned off. False. Many appliances use energy for clocks and remote sensors. Unplug devices when possible.

2. Washing by hand always uses less water than modern dishwashers. False. Energy Star dishwashers often use less water (up to 50% less) than hand washing.

3. Paper and plastic bags are equally harmful. True. Both are harmful to manufacture and dispose of. Reuse is the best option.

4. Hybrid cars perform worse than conventional cars. False. Modern hybrids perform as well as or better than conventional vehicles.

5. Most global energy comes from renewable resources. False. Only 8% of global energy is renewable; the rest comes from fossil fuels.

Discussion Topics

1. Your Environmental Habits

What do you personally do (or not do) to help the environment? Think about your home, workplace, and shopping habits.

2. Anti-Green Behaviours

What behaviours in other people annoy or frustrate you when it comes to environmental issues?

3. Government Action

How much does your government do to help with environmental problems? Is it enough?

4. Recycling Effectiveness

How does your local government support or hinder recycling efforts?

5. Balancing Progress with Protection

Can we protect the environment and improve people's standard of living simultaneously?

Action Lists Task

Create two lists of environmental actions with justifications:

Part A: Personal Actions

List 5 actions you could personally take to help the environment. Use the collocations and attitude phrases you learned.

Part B: Government/Societal Actions

List 5 actions you would like to see implemented at local or national government level. What is your reasoning?

Reflection

Which actions from Part A are you genuinely willing to implement?

Which government actions would make the biggest difference in your opinion?

What obstacles prevent more people from being consciously green?
