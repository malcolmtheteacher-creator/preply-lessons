Part 1 — A scarf for Carla

The flight home is at six in the evening. Leo has the morning free. A present for Carla, a present for the children. He goes to a big shop on Oxford Street.

Assistant: Can I help you?

Leo: Yes. I'm looking for a scarf. For my wife.

Assistant: What colour?

Leo: Green. She likes green.

Assistant: We have this one — green and blue. It's wool.

Leo: It's beautiful. How much is it?

Assistant: Forty-five pounds.

Leo: Hmm. It's a little expensive.

Assistant: There's a twenty per cent discount today. Thirty-six pounds.

Leo: OK. I'll take it.

Quick check — answer from memory, then reveal:

1. What does Leo want, and who is it for?

2. How much does he pay? Why?

1. A green scarf, for his wife Carla.

2. Thirty-six pounds. There is a 20% discount.

Talk about it:
  • And you? Do you buy presents when you travel? For whom?

Part 2 — Marco and Sofia

Now: Marco and Sofia. A football shirt for Marco. A T-shirt for Sofia.

Leo: Do you have this shirt in a small size?

Assistant: Small… yes. Here you are.

Leo: And this T-shirt — do you have it in pink?

Assistant: In pink, only medium.

Leo: Medium is too big. She's ten.

Assistant: What about this one? Pink, size small.

Leo: Perfect. Can I have a gift bag?

Assistant: Of course. Is that everything?

Leo: Yes. Can I pay by card?

Quick check — answer from memory, then reveal:

1. What does Leo buy for Marco?

2. Why is medium not good for Sofia?

1. A football shirt, size small.

2. It is too big. She is ten.

Talk about it:
  • And you? What do you buy for your family when you travel: clothes, chocolate, something from the city?

Part 3 — Something for Leo

One more thing. A shop with a hundred umbrellas. Leo laughs. Of course. London.

Leo: Excuse me, how much is this umbrella?

Assistant: Twelve pounds.

Leo: Do you have it in black?

Assistant: Black, blue, red…

Leo: Black, please.

Assistant: Would you like to try it?

Leo: Try an umbrella?

Assistant: Everybody says that. And then they try it.

He tries it. It works. He is ready for London — on the last day.

Quick check — answer from memory, then reveal:

1. What does Leo buy for himself?

2. Why is it funny?

1. A black umbrella.

2. It is his last day in London. Now he is ready for the rain.

Talk about it:
  • And you? What do you always forget when you travel?

Grammar: I'm looking for… / Do you have this in…? / too big

Four sentences do almost all the work in a shop. Learn them as one set.

I'm looking for + a thing. "I'm looking for a scarf." "I'm looking for a present for my son." The assistant asks: "What colour?" "What size?"
Do you have this in…? "Do you have this in blue?" "Do you have it in a small size?" Sizes: small, medium, large (S, M, L).
too + adjective = more than you want: "It's too big." "It's too small." "It's too expensive." Softer: "It's a little expensive."
Decide: "I'll take it." (yes) · "I'll think about it." (no, politely) · Prices: £12.50 = "twelve pounds fifty" · "Is there a discount?"
