A busy Saturday 📖

Read it slowly. Say the words out loud.

Ana is at home again now. Mia's room is quiet. But the café? The café is NOT quiet! ☕

Yesterday was Saturday — the café's biggest day ever. So many people! Coffee, tea, cake, more coffee…

Mia worked all day. She carried a big tray with three cups — and she didn't drop it! 🎉

She helped Rosa at the coffee machine. She helped a little boy with his hot chocolate. She helped everyone!

The people smiled at Mia, and Mia smiled at the people. It was a very busy day — and a very happy day.

In the evening, Mia and Rosa cleaned the tables and the floor. Rosa smiled. "You worked so well today, Mia. Thank you!"

At home, Mia was so tired. She closed her eyes in the big chair. The cat closed its eyes too. 🐈

"What a Saturday," she says to the cat. "I worked, I helped, I smiled… and now — I sleep!" 😴

Notice: Mia worked, helped, smiled and cleaned. That little -ed at the end means it happened yesterday — in the past. Learn the words next.

The pattern: -ed = yesterday 🧩

To talk about the past, add -ed to the verb. Same word for I, you, he, she, we, they!

Today I work. → Yesterday I worked.
Today she helps. → Yesterday she helped.
Today we clean. → Yesterday we cleaned.
Does the verb end in e, like smile? Just add d:
smile → smiled  ·  close → closed
Remember lesson 12? was and were are the past too: "Yesterday was Saturday. Mia was tired. The people were happy — and Mia worked, helped and smiled."

Act it out 🎭

Now YOU are in the story — at the end of the biggest Saturday ever.

🎬 The scene: Evening at the café. The last people go home. You and Rosa clean the last table.

Round 1 — you are Mia

Your teacher is Rosa. Speak your lines — don't write them.

Rosa: "Phew! The last table is clean. What a Saturday! How was the day for you?"

You: (say how the day was — use the word for "so much to do")

Rosa: "So many people! Tell me two or three things from your day."

You: (say what you did — the big tray, the coffee machine, the tables… all with that little -ed)

Rosa: "Yes! And the people? Were they happy?"

You: (say what the people did with their faces — and what you did back)

Rosa: "Big smiles all day! Thank you, Mia. Go home now — sleep well!"

You: (say how you feel after this long day — and say good night)

Round 2 — swap!

Now YOU are Rosa, and your teacher is Mia. But this time it is a busy market Monday, and her jobs were different.
