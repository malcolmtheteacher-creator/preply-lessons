⚔️ English Conquest

Conquer the world with the power of English

How To Play

🎯
Goal: Conquer every territory. Eliminate the AI Empire — or each other!
👨‍🎓
Student turn: Answer an English question to attack. Correct = conquer. Wrong = lose troops.
👨‍🏫
Teacher turn: Free strategic moves — reinforce and attack without answering questions.
🤖
AI Empire: Reinforces and attacks automatically each round. Keep your borders defended!
📈
Adaptive: Get 3 right in a row → harder questions. Get 2 wrong → easier ones. The game adjusts to you.
🌍
Continents: Own every territory in a continent for bonus troops each turn.
🔥
Streaks: 3+ correct in a row = bonus troops. Keep the streak alive!
⏱️
Timer: Each question is timed. Run out of time and you lose the battle.
Tip: The teacher can play strategically to pressure the student — or team up against the AI!
