How To Play

🎯
Goal: Conquer every territory on the map by answering English questions correctly. Eliminate the AI Empire to win!
🛡️
Reinforce: Each turn starts here. Click your own territories to deploy new troops. More land = more troops per turn.
⚔️
Attack: Click a glowing enemy territory next to yours. Answer the question correctly to conquer it. Get it wrong and you lose troops.
🌍
Continents: Own every territory in a continent for bonus reinforcements each turn. Check the sidebar to track progress.
🔥
Streaks: Answer 3+ questions correctly in a row to earn bonus troops. The longer your streak, the stronger you get!
📖
Questions: Vocabulary, grammar, idioms, collocations, false friends, and reading comprehension. Every answer includes an explanation.
⏱️
Timer: Each question has a time limit. Standard = 45s, C1 Challenge = 30s, Blitz = 15s. If time runs out, you lose the battle!
🤖
AI Empire: The enemy reinforces and attacks each turn too. Don't leave your borders undefended or they'll push back!
Tip: Conquer entire continents early for powerful troop bonuses every turn.

Goal: Conquer all territories!
