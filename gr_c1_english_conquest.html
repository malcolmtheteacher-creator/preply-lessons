How To Play — Classic Risk Rules

🎯
Goal: Conquer all 42 territories. Eliminate every opponent.
🛡️
Reinforce: Get troops = territories ÷ 3 (min 3) + continent bonuses. Click your territories to deploy.
⚔️
Attack: Click enemy territory next to yours. Answer correctly to win the battle. You can attack multiple times per turn!
🎲
Combat: Questions replace dice. Correct = attacker wins. Wrong = defender wins (ties go to defender, like real Risk!).
🃏
Risk Cards: Capture a territory → earn 1 card. Collect sets of 3 to trade for bonus troops (values escalate!).
🚚
Fortify: End of turn, move troops from one territory to an adjacent one. Must leave at least 1 behind.
📈
Adaptive: 3 correct in a row → harder questions. 2 wrong → easier. The game adjusts to you.
👥
3 Players: Student answers English Qs. Teacher attacks freely. AI Empire fights back each round.
Classic Risk continent bonuses: Asia +7, N. America +5, Europe +5, Africa +3, S. America +2, Australia +2
