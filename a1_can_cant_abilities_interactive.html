What Can You Do?

Talk about your abilities and skills

CAN & CAN'T

Use CAN and CAN'T to talk about abilities - what you are able to do and what you are not able to do.

✅ CAN = I am able to do this

💪
/kæn/
I can swim.
I can speak French.
I can play the guitar.

❌ CAN'T = I am NOT able to do this

🚫
/kɑːnt/ or /kænt/
I can't swim.
I can't speak Spanish.
I can't play the piano.

Grammar Rules

✅ I/You/He/She/We/They + CAN + verb
❌ I/You/He/She/We/They + CAN'T + verb
Important:
• CAN never changes! (not "cans" or "canning")
• After CAN, use the base verb (swim, speak, play)
• CAN'T = cannot (negative form)

Question Form

CAN + you + verb?
Examples:
• "Can you swim?" → "Yes, I can!" / "No, I can't."
• "Can you speak English?" → "Yes, I can!"
• "Can you cook?" → "No, I can't, but I want to learn!"

✅ I CAN

I am good at this!
I know how to do this!

❌ I CAN'T

I don't know how to do this.
I am not able to do this.

📚 I'M LEARNING

I can't do this YET,
but I'm learning!

Speaking Practice

Discussion 1

What are 3 things you CAN do?
Tell your teacher 3 abilities or skills you have!
Example: "I can swim, I can speak French, and I can play video games!"

Discussion 2

What are 3 things you CAN'T do?
Tell your teacher 3 things you can't do.
Example: "I can't drive a car, I can't cook, and I can't speak Spanish."

Discussion 3

What CAN'T you do now, but you WANT to learn?
Think about skills you want to have in the future!
Example: "I can't cook now, but I want to learn!"

Discussion 4

What can you do VERY WELL?
Talk about your best skills!
Example: "I can speak French very well!"
Or: "I can play video games very well!"

Combining with Past Lessons!

Add MORE information to your sentences:

• How often: "I can swim, and I usually go swimming on weekends."
• Who with: "I can cook, and I sometimes cook with my family."
• When: "I can play guitar, and I usually practice in the evening."
• Likes: "I can draw, and I love drawing!"
Challenge: Tell your teacher about 5 different abilities using CAN or CAN'T, and add extra information about when, how often, and who with!

About Me - Complete Profile

Tell your teacher about yourself using EVERYTHING you've learned!

Create Your Complete Profile!

Talk about yourself using ALL the structures:
Include:
✓ What you LIKE and DON'T LIKE (Lesson 1)
✓ WHEN you do activities (Lesson 2)
✓ HOW OFTEN you do them (Lesson 3)
✓ WHO you do them with (Lesson 4)
✓ Your daily ROUTINE (Lesson 5)
✓ What you CAN and CAN'T do (Lesson 6)

Example Complete Description:

"Hi! My name is Sarah. I'm 14 years old and I live in France.

I love playing video games! I usually play in the evening with my friends. I also like listening to music. I always listen to music when I do homework.

My daily routine: I wake up at 7:00 AM and I eat breakfast at 7:30 AM with my family. I go to school at 8:00 AM. After school, I usually do homework at 4:00 PM. I eat dinner with my family at 7:00 PM, and I go to bed at 10:00 PM.

I can speak French and English! I can also swim very well. I can't cook yet, but I want to learn! I can't drive a car because I'm too young!

I don't like waking up early, but I have to go to school! 😊"

Now It's YOUR Turn!

Tell your teacher about yourself!
Use the example above as a guide, but talk about YOUR life!
Include everything you've learned in all 6 lessons!

Bonus Questions:

1. What new skill do you want to learn this year?
Example: "I want to learn how to cook Italian food!"
2. Who in your family can do something you can't do?
Example: "My mom can cook very well, but I can't!"
3. What's something you can do now that you couldn't do when you were younger?
Example: "I can speak English now! When I was 10, I couldn't speak English."
