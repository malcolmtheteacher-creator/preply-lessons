First Impressions: Can You Judge a Book by Its Cover?

Research shows we form first impressions in just 7 seconds!

Let's Discuss:

  1. How important are first impressions?
  2. Have you ever had a bad first impression of someone who turned out to be wonderful?
  3. What do you think people's first impression of you usually is?
  4. How do you make a good first impression in professional settings?
  5. Can you really judge a book by its cover? Or a person by their appearance?

Vocabulary Practice

Complete each sentence with ONE word:

1. You should never a book by its cover.
2. He made a terrible first at the interview.
3. Don't to conclusions about people too quickly.
4. She comes as very confident and professional.
5. Appearances can be - people aren't always what they seem.
6. It's important to dress for important meetings.

Grammar in Context:

Notice the phrases: "I would say..." and "It depends on..."

  • How do you use these in conversation?
  • When is it natural to use them?
  • Can you create examples using both phrases?

Books That Changed the World

Some books have had an incredible impact on history, culture, and society. Let's explore a few and discuss which ones interest you most.

Select 2-3 Books That Interest You:

On the Origin of Species
Charles Darwin (1859)
Explained evolution through natural selection. Challenged religious views and laid foundations for modern science.
1984
George Orwell (1949)
Dystopian novel warning against surveillance states and totalitarian regimes. "Big Brother" entered the English language.
The Diary of Anne Frank
Anne Frank (1947)
A teenager's diary during Holocaust. Personal link to history that resonates strongly with young people.
Communist Manifesto
Karl Marx (1848)
Called for overthrow of capitalism. Driving philosophy behind Russian Revolution. "Workers of the world, unite!"
Harry Potter
J.K. Rowling (1997)
Rekindled reading for a generation. Best-selling book series in history with 450+ million copies sold.
Silent Spring
Rachel Carson (1962)
Documented harmful effects of pesticides. Kick-started the environmental movement as we know it today.
