The Story of the Mission

Read this short text. Then close it and tell the story from memory.

On April 1st, a rocket called the Space Launch System took off from Florida, USA. Inside were four astronauts: Reid Wiseman, Victor Glover, Christina Koch, and Jeremy Hansen.

They flew around the Moon on April 6th. They didn't land — they flew past it. They took more than 7,000 photos of the Moon's surface.

On April 10th, their capsule splashed down in the Pacific Ocean near San Diego. The mission lasted about 10 days.

SPEAK: Now — without looking! Tell the story. What happened first? Then what? Use past simple: "They took off... They flew... They landed..."

Helpful sentence starters:

"First, they took off from..."

"After that, they flew around..."

"They took more than..."

"Finally, they came back and..."

Space Vocabulary

Click each card to see the meaning.

an astronaut A person who travels to space
a rocket A vehicle that takes people into space — it goes very fast and very high
to take off To leave the ground — "The rocket took off at 6:35 pm."
to splash down When a spacecraft lands in the ocean — "They splashed down in the Pacific."
a mission An important trip with a goal — "The mission lasted 10 days."
to break a record To do something better/more/faster than anyone before — "They broke the distance record."

Discussion Cards

Choose 2 cards. For each one, give your opinion and explain why.

1

The Money

This mission cost $4.1 billion. Is it OK to spend this much money on space? What else could you do with that money?

2

Would You Go?

Imagine you can go to the Moon for 10 days. Would you say yes? What would be exciting? What would be scary?

3

Living on the Moon

Some people want to build houses on the Moon in the future. Would you like to live there? What would you miss about Earth?

4

Who Should Go?

Only astronauts can go to space now. In the future, normal people might go too. Who should go first — scientists, teachers, artists, or someone else?
