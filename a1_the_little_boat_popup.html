Words

water (noun) — you drink it. It has no colour. Example: "I am thirsty. I need the water."
bread (noun) — food. You eat it with butter or cheese. Example: "I am hungry. I need the bread."
hat (noun) — you wear it on your head. Example: "The sun is hot. I need the hat."
umbrella (noun) — you hold it over your head in the rain. Example: "It rains. I need the umbrella."
phone (noun) — you call people with it. Example: "I need the phone. I want to call my friend."
ball (noun) — a round thing. You play football with it. Example: "I don't need the ball."
TV (noun) — you watch films and football on it. Example: "I don't need the TV."
chair (noun) — a seat for one person. Example: "I don't need the chair."
need (verb) — must have. Example: "I am thirsty. I need water."
heavy (adjective) — not easy to carry, like a car or a piano. Example: "The TV is heavy."
