The Situation

You're trapped. Five locked rooms stand between you and freedom. Each room has a different challenge — and the only way through is to speak.

The rules are simple: Each room gives you a speaking task. Complete it well enough and you escape that room. Fail or run out of time, and you're stuck.

You have 25 minutes total. The clock starts when you enter Room 1.

The 5 Rooms

Each room tests a different speaking skill.

Room 1 — The Description Room
Describe something so precisely that I can guess the password.
Room 2 — The Persuasion Room
Convince the guard to let you through. You'll need a good argument.
Room 3 — The Story Room
Tell a story using 5 random words. All 5 must appear in your story.
Room 4 — The Explanation Room
Explain a complex idea so simply that a child could understand it.
Room 5 — The Negotiation Room
You and the captor want different things. Find a deal — or stay locked in.

Before You Start

Quick warm-up: Describe the room you're sitting in right now. You have 30 seconds. Be as specific as possible — colours, objects, textures, sounds. Go.

Room 2 — The Persuasion Room

There's a guard at the door. The guard is not unreasonable — but they need a good reason to let you through. You have 2 minutes to convince them.

The Scenario

Tap to reveal your scenario

Situation: You're at an airport. Your flight leaves in 20 minutes. The gate is closed. The gate agent (your teacher) has the power to reopen it — but they're not supposed to.

Your goal: Convince the gate agent to reopen the gate and let you board.

The catch: The agent has heard every excuse before. "My grandmother is dying" won't work. You need to be creative, honest, and persuasive.

Think for 30 seconds. Then speak for 2 minutes. The teacher will push back.
