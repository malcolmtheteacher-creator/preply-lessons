Sharing Opinions Politely

B1 Level · Give your view and disagree in a friendly way

Warm-up

Today we're working on giving opinions in a friendly way so you can share your ideas and disagree politely.
Have a go, there are no wrong answers. What's your opinion: is it better to work from home or in an office? Give me two reasons.
Is it difficult to disagree with someone at work? Why, or why not?

Friendly opinions have three parts

Name each part before you click. Then give me an opinion on coffee or tea using all three.

Give an opinion

Some ways to say what you think. Say your own, then click.

Strong, but friendly
"I really think...", "In my view...", "I'm convinced that..."
Softer
"I tend to think...", "It seems to me that...", "I'd say..."
Not sure
"I'm not sure, but I think...", "Maybe...", "I haven't decided yet, but..."
Adding a reason
"...because...", "...for one thing,...", "...that's why..."
Give me your opinion on each: a four-day working week, text messages or phone calls, and big or small cities. Use a different phrase each time.

Quick check: Which opinion sounds the most friendly?

Agree and disagree

Agree

"I agree."
"That's a good point."
"Exactly!"
"I couldn't agree more."

Disagree politely

"I see what you mean, but..."
"That's true, although..."
"I'm not sure I agree."
"Maybe, but have you thought about...?"

Play it

I'll give an opinion. You answer: agree or disagree politely, with a reason. Then click.

"Everyone should learn to cook."
"I couldn't agree more. It saves money, and it's good for you."
or "I see what you mean, but not everyone enjoys cooking."
"Holidays should always be abroad."
"I'm not sure I agree. A holiday at home can be just as relaxing."
"Working from home is better than the office."
"That's true, although I miss seeing people. What about you?"
"Social media makes life easier."
"In some ways, yes, but it also takes up a lot of time."
Now you give me a surprising opinion and I'll disagree politely. Then we swap.

Talk

Choose a card. Say your opinion, give a reason and ask me what I think. We can agree or disagree, politely.

05:00

Talk for the whole time. Use at least three opinion phrases and disagree politely at least once.
Pick a second card and start it yourself.

Discuss

A role-play. Choose a situation and talk for about three minutes. You disagree a little, but you stay friendly.

A: Choosing a restaurant

You want different places. Agree on one.

B: Team lunch

Decide where to go and what time. You each have a different preference.

C: Planning a team day

Choose between a walk, a workshop and a dinner.

D: News of the week

Chat about a light news story and share your views.

Opinion → Reason → Ask → Agree or disagree → Compromise

If you disagree

Finding a compromise
"How about we...?"
"Why don't we...?"
"We could do both."
"Let's meet in the middle."
Do the role-play again with another situation. This time you disagree first.
