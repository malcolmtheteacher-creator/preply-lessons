The Argument Flow

These five work as a sequence: state your position → cite evidence → explain the implication → handle the objection → reinforce. But each also works independently.

When to Use Each One
Polar Bear — "My position is..." When you need to state where you stand, clearly and without hedging. Meetings, interviews, panels. After you've FRAMEd the topic, this is how you commit.
Elephant — "The evidence suggests..." When you need to back your claim with facts or data. Instantly elevates your argument from opinion to analysis. Essential in professional and academic settings.
Praying Mantis — "What this means in practice is..." When you need to translate abstract reasoning into concrete implications. Bridges theory and reality. Examiners and decision-makers love this move.
Tapir — "I take your point, however..." When someone challenges you. Acknowledges their position (shows you're listening) then pivots. Diplomatic but firm. The adult version of "Yes, but..."
Stingray — "To strengthen that argument..." When you want to add another layer of support. Builds momentum. Works after your own point OR after agreeing with someone else's — either way, you're adding force.
