Beliefs Shape Practice and Daily Life

Islamic beliefs are not abstract. They shape how Muslims live:

1. Tawhid - Muslims pray to one God, avoid idolatry, treat all equally

2. Akhirah - Muslims live ethically, avoid haram, show patience

3. Risalah - Muslims follow Muhammad's Sunnah and Hadith

4. Holy Books - Muslims base law on Qur'an

5. Angels - Muslims aware of Allah's presence

The Shahadah: Islamic Beliefs in One Statement

"There is no god but Allah, and Muhammad is the messenger of Allah."

This expresses: (1) Rejection of idolatry, (2) Tawhid - oneness, (3) Belief in Muhammad as final prophet.
