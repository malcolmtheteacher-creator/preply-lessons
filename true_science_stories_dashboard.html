True Science Stories

Twelve real discoveries that changed the world — and the stubborn people the world tried to ignore.

A doctor who drank his own experiment. A man destroyed for asking surgeons to wash their hands. A map that beat a two-thousand-year-old theory. A dirty dish, a leaking shed, a photograph shown to the wrong person, a teddy bear full of banknotes, a virus hunted to extinction, a spacecraft saved with a sock and tape, and a cure hidden in a book from 340 CE. Every story here is true — and every one turns on the same moment: the evidence was there, and somebody had to be stubborn enough to make the world look at it.

Each story is a 50-minute B2 lesson: ten key words unlocked first, the story in three parts with discussion as it builds, one grammar point the story itself teaches — passives, conditionals, reported speech, narrative tenses — and timed speaking tasks: retell it, debate it, and argue the moment when you would have had to decide.
