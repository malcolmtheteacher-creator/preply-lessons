Twelve lessons · six themes · two levels

The Physics Lab

Learn English by explaining how the world works. Run the experiment, say what happened, and say why.

There are two series. Everyday Physics (A2/B1) starts from things you can see and touch: a cooling cup of tea, a sliding box, a thunderstorm. Physics You Can Argue About (C1) takes the same six themes to the big open questions, where the aim is to say exactly what is known, what is disputed, and how sure we can be.

The two lessons in each theme match, so you can start with the one that fits you and come back to the other later.

How every lesson works

1 · Say what you thinkA few questions to answer aloud before anything is explained.
2 · Try the experimentOne interactive to play with. Predict first, then watch.
3 · Explain itFinish sentences, then say it in your own words and write one sentence.
4 · Test and rememberA short quiz, questions from earlier lessons, and a check of what you can now do.
