Eight words for today

Read the sentences. Say what you think the word means. Then open the meaning.

1 My biggest strength is that I learn fast. That is the best thing about me at work.
What does it mean? Say your idea first.
1. Show the meaning
strength (n) — something you are very good at. Example: "My strength is working with people."
2 My weakness is that I speak too fast. I want to be better at this.
What does it mean? Say your idea first.
2. Show the meaning
weakness (n) — something you are not good at yet. Example: "My weakness is that I am sometimes late."
3 I have five years of experience. I worked in two shops and one hotel.
What does it mean? Say your idea first.
3. Show the meaning
experience (n) — the things you did and learned in jobs before. Example: "I have three years of experience in a restaurant."
4 Driving is a useful skill. I learned it when I was 18.
What does it mean? Say your idea first.
4. Show the meaning
skill (n) — something you can do well because you learned it. Example: "Cooking is my best skill."
5 There are six people in my team. We work together every day.
What does it mean? Say your idea first.
5. Show the meaning
team (n) — a group of people who work together. Example: "I like working in a small team."
6 Ana is very reliable. She is never late and she always finishes her work.
What does it mean? Say your idea first.
6. Show the meaning
reliable (adj) — always doing what you say you will do. Example: "I am reliable. I come to work on time every day."
7 I want to improve my English. I study for 20 minutes every day.
What does it mean? Say your idea first.
7. Show the meaning
improve (v) — to get better at something. Example: "I want to improve my computer skills."
8 My goal is to be a manager in two years. I am working hard for it.
What does it mean? Say your idea first.
8. Show the meaning
goal (n) — something you want to do in the future. Example: "My goal is to work in a big hotel."

A good answer has three steps

💬
1 · AnswerSay yes, no, or the main thing.
➕
2 · ReasonSay why. Use because.
⭐
3 · ExampleSay one real thing. Use for example.

Look at one

"What are you good at?"

I am good at working with people because I am friendly and I listen. For example, in my last job I helped new workers on their first day.

Which step is missing?

Say which step is missing. Then say the full answer.

1 "I am good at cooking because I cook every day at home."
Check

The example is missing. Add one real thing: "For example, last week I cooked dinner for ten people."

2 "Because I like people. For example, I help my neighbours."
Check

The answer is missing. Start with the main thing: "Yes, I like working in a team."

3 "I want this job. For example, I worked in a shop before."
Check

The reason is missing. Say why: "…because I like talking to customers."
