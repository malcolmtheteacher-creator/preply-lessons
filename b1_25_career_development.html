Phrases with "Work"

In English, "work" combines with prepositions and other words to create useful expressions. Here are the most common:

Work towards

Move in the direction of a goal; try to achieve something

She is working towards a promotion by taking extra courses.
Work out

Find a solution to a problem; figure out

The team had to work out a new schedule that suited everyone.
Work well with

Have a good relationship with; cooperate effectively

He works well with people from different backgrounds.
Work on

Continue doing or developing something; improve

I need to work on my communication skills.
Work through

Deal with something difficult step by step; solve

We had to work through some conflicts before the project succeeded.

The STAR Method

STAR is a simple framework for answering interview questions about your experience. It helps you tell a clear, complete story.

S
Situation

Describe the situation or context. When and where did this happen? Who was involved?

T
Task

What was the task or challenge? What needed to be done?

A
Action

What did YOU do? What steps did you take? Focus on your own contribution.

R
Result

What was the outcome? What did you achieve or learn?

Example STAR Answer

Tell me about a time you worked successfully in a team.
Situation: I worked at a marketing company with five team members. We had three weeks to design a new campaign for a tech client.

Task: The challenge was that everyone had different ideas about the campaign direction, and we had a tight deadline.

Action: I suggested that we organize a meeting to listen to everyone's ideas. I then created a simple comparison of the three strongest suggestions. I helped the team compromise by combining elements from each idea, and I volunteered to coordinate the timeline so we stayed on schedule.

Result: We finished on time with a campaign that everyone felt proud of. The client loved it and gave us two more projects. The experience taught me that listening to others and creating compromise solutions leads to better results.

The Interview

Practice answering common interview questions about teamwork. You have 3 minutes to prepare each answer. Use the STAR method to structure your response.

03:00

Question 1

Can you tell me about a time you worked successfully in a team?
Prepare: Think about a specific example. How did you contribute? What was the result?

Question 2

Describe a situation where you had to deal with a conflict in your team. How did you handle it?
Prepare: What was the conflict? What action did you take? What was the outcome?

Question 3

Tell me about a time when you had to work with someone very different from you.
Prepare: What made them different? How did you cooperate? What did you learn?
