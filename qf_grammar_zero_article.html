📌 The Rule

No article for general statements about uncountable or plural nouns.

"I like music" (music in general) vs. "I like the music in this café" (specific music)

No article: sports (I play football), meals (I had lunch), languages (She speaks French), most countries.

📚 The Rule (Dual Coding)

🎯 THE = Specific

👉

the book on the table

the music in this café

the meal we had yesterday

A particular thing

🌍 Ø = General

✨

Books are educational

I like music

Breakfast is important

All things of that type
