🎯 Lesson 1: Buying Time

What 5 phrases can you use to buy thinking time? Reveal

The 5 time-buying phrases:

"Let me think..." "That's a good question..." "Well..." "I'd say..." "If I'm honest..."
Which phrase is best for formal situations like interviews? Reveal

"That's a good question..." - Shows you're taking it seriously

🎨 Lesson 2: Describing Photos

What 5 phrases help you describe a photo? Reveal

The 5 description phrases:

"I can see..." "In the background/foreground..." "It looks like..." "They seem to be..." "It reminds me of..."
Which phrase makes your description personal? Reveal

"It reminds me of..." - Connects the image to your experience

💭 Lesson 3: Simple Opinions

What's the "power formula" for giving opinions? Reveal

Opinion + because + reason + Also/Plus + second reason

Name 3 ways to start an opinion. Reveal
"I think..." "I'd say..." "Personally, I..."

📖 Lesson 4: Telling Stories

What's the story structure? (5 parts) Reveal
So,... First,... Then/After that,... Suddenly,... In the end,...
Which phrase creates drama in a story? Reveal

"Suddenly,..." / "All of a sudden,..." - Use max once for impact!

The Ultimate Challenge

🎯 Challenge 1: The Photo Story

Think of a photo from a trip or event. Describe it AND tell the story behind it.

Use: Description phrases (L2) + Story structure (L4) + Time-buyers when needed (L1)

🎯 Challenge 2: The Opinion Story

"What's the best decision you ever made? Tell me the story and why you think it was the right choice."

Use: Story structure (L4) + Opinion formula (L3) + Time-buyers (L1)

🎯 Challenge 3: The Dream Day

Describe your perfect day from morning to night. What would happen? What would you see? Why would you enjoy it?

Use ALL 4 skills: Time-buyers + Description + Opinion + Story structure

Goal: Speak for the full 5 minutes without long pauses. Use at least 10 different phrases from Lessons 1-4!
