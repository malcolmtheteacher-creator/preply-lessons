Power-Ups
Click each card to unlock a writing superpower
SHOW DON'T TELL 👁️
Don't just say how someone feels. Show it with actions and details!
EXAMPLE:
"He was scared."
"His hands trembled. He couldn't breathe. Every shadow looked like a monster."
SAID IS DEAD 💀
"Said" is boring! Use better words for dialogue.
INSTEAD OF "SAID" TRY:
whispered, shouted, demanded, admitted, insisted, muttered, exclaimed, sighed, groaned, laughed
ADJECTIVE STACKING 📚
Use 2-3 adjectives before a noun for vivid descriptions. Order: Opinion - Size - Age - Color - Material
EXAMPLE:
"A car drove past."
"A sleek, vintage, cherry-red sports car roared past."
SHORT + LONG 〰️
Mix short punchy sentences with longer flowing ones. Creates rhythm!
EXAMPLE:
"The door opened. Standing there, illuminated by the pale moonlight that streamed through the broken windows, was a figure I thought I'd never see again. My heart stopped."
