Year 10: GCSE Religious Studies

"Understanding beliefs, practices, and how to evaluate them."

What Year 10 Is About

Year 10 is the first year of your GCSE Religious Studies course (AQA Specification A, 8062). You'll study Christianity and Islam in depth, covering both beliefs and practices. By the end of Year 10, you'll have a thorough understanding of what Christians and Muslims believe, how they worship, and how their faith shapes daily life. You'll also develop the evaluation and essay skills needed to excel in your GCSE exams.

How This Course Works

  • AQA Specification A (8062) — This course follows the AQA GCSE Religious Studies A specification. You'll study Christianity and Islam as your two religions.
  • Two papers — Paper 1 covers the study of religions (beliefs, teachings, and practices). Paper 2 covers thematic studies (you'll tackle these in Year 11).
  • Beliefs before practices — Units 19–20 cover Christian beliefs, then Units 21–22 cover Christian practices. Units 23–24 cover Islamic beliefs. Islamic practices follow in Year 11.
  • Evaluation is key — The highest-mark questions (12 marks) ask you to evaluate a statement. Every unit practises this skill with structured guidance.
  • Assessment every unit — Lesson 6 of each unit is a formal assessment combining recall, extended writing, and evaluation practice.

Year 10 builds the foundations of your GCSE. Master the beliefs and practices this year, and you'll be ready for the thematic studies and final revision in Year 11.
