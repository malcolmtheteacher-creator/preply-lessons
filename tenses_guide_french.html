English Tenses - Simple Guide 🇬🇧

For French Speakers 🇫🇷

1. PRESENT SIMPLE

VERB (add -s for he/she/it)

When to use: Habits, routines, general truths, permanent situations

English: I work every day.
French: Je travaille tous les jours. (Présent)

English: She lives in Paris.
French: Elle habite à Paris. (Présent)

English: Water boils at 100 degrees.
French: L'eau bout à 100 degrés. (Présent)

2. PRESENT CONTINUOUS

AM/IS/ARE + VERB-ing

When to use: NOW! Right at this moment, temporary actions

English: I am working now.
French: Je suis en train de travailler maintenant. (Présent continu)

English: She is eating lunch.
French: Elle est en train de manger. (Présent continu)

English: They are studying English this year.
French: Ils étudient l'anglais cette année. (action temporaire)

3. PAST SIMPLE

VERB + -ed (regular) OR irregular form

When to use: Finished actions in the past (with specific time)

English: I worked yesterday.
French: J'ai travaillé hier. (Passé composé)

English: She went to Paris last week.
French: Elle est allée à Paris la semaine dernière. (Passé composé)

English: I lived in London for 5 years.
French: J'ai vécu à Londres pendant 5 ans. (Passé composé)

4. PAST CONTINUOUS

WAS/WERE + VERB-ing

When to use: Action in progress in the past

English: I was working when you called.
French: J'étais en train de travailler quand tu as appelé. (Imparfait)

English: She was sleeping at 10pm.
French: Elle dormait à 22h. (Imparfait)

English: They were studying all night.
French: Ils étudiaient toute la nuit. (Imparfait)

5. PRESENT PERFECT

HAVE/HAS + PAST PARTICIPLE

When to use: Past action connected to NOW (no specific time!)

English: I have worked here for 3 years.
French: Je travaille ici depuis 3 ans. (Présent!)

English: She has been to Paris.
French: Elle est allée à Paris (dans sa vie). (Passé composé)

English: I have just eaten.
French: Je viens de manger. (Passé récent)

⚠️ ATTENTION FRENCH SPEAKERS!

For actions that CONTINUE to now, English uses Present Perfect, but French uses Present!

English: I have lived here for 5 years. (still living here)
French: J'habite ici depuis 5 ans. (présent!)

6. PAST PERFECT

HAD + PAST PARTICIPLE

When to use: Action that happened BEFORE another past action

English: I had worked there before I moved.
French: J'avais travaillé là-bas avant de déménager. (Plus-que-parfait)

English: She had already left when I arrived.
French: Elle était déjà partie quand je suis arrivé. (Plus-que-parfait)

English: I had never seen snow before.
French: Je n'avais jamais vu la neige avant. (Plus-que-parfait)

7. FUTURE SIMPLE

WILL + VERB

When to use: Predictions, decisions made NOW, promises

English: I will work tomorrow.
French: Je travaillerai demain. (Futur simple)

English: It will rain later.
French: Il pleuvra plus tard. (Futur simple)

English: I will help you!
French: Je vais t'aider! (Futur proche ou futur simple)

8. GOING TO (Future)

AM/IS/ARE + GOING TO + VERB

When to use: Plans, intentions, something about to happen

English: I am going to work tomorrow.
French: Je vais travailler demain. (Futur proche)

English: She is going to study medicine.
French: Elle va étudier la médecine. (Futur proche)

English: Look at the clouds! It is going to rain!
French: Regarde les nuages! Il va pleuvoir! (Futur proche)

🚨 BIGGEST MISTAKES FOR FRENCH SPEAKERS

  1. Present Perfect vs Present:
    ❌ I live here since 5 years
    ✅ I have lived here for 5 years
  2. Forgetting continuous forms:
    ❌ I work now
    ✅ I am working now
  3. Using Present Perfect with specific time:
    ❌ I have worked yesterday
    ✅ I worked yesterday
