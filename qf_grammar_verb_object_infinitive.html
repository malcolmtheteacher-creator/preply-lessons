🔗 Quick Fix: Verb + Object + To-Infinitive

Ask/tell/want/expect/allow someone TO do something

Level B1/B2

What's the error?

Original sentence:

"My teacher said me to study more."

said me to study — SAY cannot take this pattern. You cannot say "said me to do something."

Corrected:

My teacher told me to study more.

The Rule

Some verbs take the pattern: VERB + OBJECT + TO-INFINITIVE

✓ These verbs work:

  • tell someone to do: "She told me to wait."
  • ask someone to do: "I asked him to help."
  • want someone to do: "They want us to leave."
  • expect someone to do: "She expects me to be on time."
  • allow someone to do: "They allowed us to enter."
  • advise, encourage, persuade, remind, warn, invite, order

✗ These do NOT work:

  • say someone to do → Use TELL instead
  • suggest → Use "suggest + -ing" or "suggest that + clause"
  • explain → Use "explain to someone"

Important: No "to" between the verb and the object!

WRONG: "He asked to me for help"

CORRECT: "He asked me for help"

How to Fix It

✓ VERB + OBJECT + TO

✔

Pattern: V + person + to + base verb

tell me to wait

ask her to help

want them to leave

expect us to finish

✗ NOT SAY + OBJECT + TO

❌

Never use SAY:

said me to do

said to me to do

Use TELL instead:

told me to do

Common Verbs That Take This Pattern

tell someone to do
ask someone to do
want someone to do
expect someone to do
allow someone to do
advise someone to do

Also: persuade, encourage, remind, warn, invite, order

Memory trick: Think of common requests: "Tell him to come", "Ask her to help", "Want you to finish". These all follow the pattern naturally.

Practice

Rewrite each sentence to correct the error. Type the corrected version.

1. "She said me to be quiet."

2. "He asked to me for help."

3. "They want that we finish by Friday."

4. "The doctor advised to me to rest."

Produce

Create sentences as requested. Use the verb + object + to-infinitive pattern correctly.

1. Your boss wants you to send a report. Write the sentence.

2. Report this: the teacher said "Come to class on time." Use TELL.

3. You want your friend to lend you their notes. Use ASK.

4. Someone made sure you didn't forget your keys. Use REMIND.
