🤔 Would You Rather?

Make choices • Give reasons • Level up your English!

🎮 How This Works

You'll get impossible choices. Pick one. Explain why. That's it!
Would you rather...
🦖 Have a pet dinosaur
🐉 Have a pet dragon
Click your choice! Then tell your teacher WHY. The more you explain, the more points you get!

Why "Would You Rather" is great:

✅ There's NO wrong answer
✅ You get to be creative
✅ It's actually fun
✅ You practice giving reasons in English

🗣️ 4 Power Phrases

Click each card to open it. Try to say the example out loud!
"I'd rather... because..."
This is your main answer! Say what you choose + why.
"I'd rather have a dragon because it can fly AND breathe fire!"
YOUR TURN: Dinosaur or dragon? Say it with "I'd rather..."
"That depends on..."
Use this when you need more info before deciding!
"That depends on... is the dinosaur friendly or will it eat me?"
YOUR TURN: What would YOU want to know about the dinosaur?
"The thing is..."
Use this to explain your thinking or add a problem.
"The thing is, a dinosaur is too big for my apartment!"
YOUR TURN: What's a problem with having a dragon?
"No way! I'd never..."
Strong reaction when one option is DEFINITELY not for you!
"No way! I'd never pick the dinosaur — they're scary!"
YOUR TURN: What's something you'd NEVER do?
