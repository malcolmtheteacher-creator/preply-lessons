Why Past Simple?

When you tell a story, you use the past simple for completed actions. The story is finished — it happened and it's over.

Example: "I had an accident. My car crashed into a tree. I was very scared, but the ambulance came quickly."

Pronunciation: Linked Sounds

In stories, verbs and words flow together. Notice how the final sound connects to the next word:

Narrative Linkers: Connecting Your Story

These phrases help you organize a story and show how events connect. Click to see examples and how to use each one.

OPENING
Last week, I had an interesting experience.
Sets up the story. The time is clear, and you're starting to tell what happened.
WHAT HAPPENED NEXT
At first, everything was normal.
Describes the situation before the main event. Helps your listener understand the context.
SURPRISING EVENT
Then suddenly, something went wrong.
Introduces the turning point or the most interesting part of the story.
IMMEDIATE REACTION
In that moment, I didn't know what to do.
Describes how you felt or what you did right after the event. Shows emotion and urgency.
WHAT HAPPENED AFTER
Eventually, the situation improved.
Shows how the event resolved or what the final result was.
CONCLUSION/REFLECTION
Looking back, I learned a lot from that experience.
Ends the story by reflecting on what it meant or what you learned. Gives the story meaning.

Complete Story Example

Last Tuesday, I had an accident. I was driving home from work when suddenly a car in front of me stopped without warning. I tried to stop, but I couldn't — I crashed into the back of their car. At that moment, I was terrified. But then I realized everyone was okay. We called the police, they came quickly, and eventually everything was sorted out. Now I am more careful when I drive, especially in heavy traffic.

Tell Your Story

Choose a personal story to tell. Take a few minutes to make notes and think about how to organize it.

Choose Your Story Topic

Pick one of these story types, or think of something similar from your own life:

Option 1: A funny or annoying incident at school

Option 2: A bad weekend or day

Option 3: A problem on holiday

Option 4: A party or family celebration

Option 5: A time when you did something naughty (and got caught!)

Prepare Your Story Notes

Write brief notes for each part. You don't need complete sentences — just key words to help you remember.

When?

Where?

Who was involved?

What happened? (Main events in order)

How did it end? What did you learn?

Remember to use:

  • Past Simple: "I had", "We ran", "She fell", "I got stuck"
  • Narrative Linkers: "Last week", "Then", "Suddenly", "Eventually", "Looking back"
  • Time expressions: "At first", "In that moment", "After that", "In the end"
