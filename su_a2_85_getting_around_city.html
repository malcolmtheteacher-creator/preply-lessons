Key Vocabulary: Places & Directions

Click on each card to reveal the definition. Master these words to talk about getting around a city.

bus stop A place where buses regularly pick up and drop off passengers
traffic lights Red, yellow, and green lights that control traffic at intersections
turn left/right Change direction by going to the left or right side
go straight on Continue in the same direction without turning
carry on Continue or keep going in the same direction or doing the same thing
intersection A place where two or more roads cross or meet
train station A building where trains pick up and drop off passengers
opposite On the other side of something; facing something across a space
next to Beside something; on the side of something else
pass by / go past Go in front of or move beyond something without stopping
get off Leave a vehicle like a bus, train, or plane
landmarks Buildings or natural features that are easy to recognize and help identify a location

Reading: Letter with Directions

Read this letter from Mike to Jana about how to get to a party. Notice the language used to give directions.

Dear Jana,

I'm so glad you're coming to our party! There are two ways to get to our house from the train station.

By Bus: Take bus 17 and get off just after the library in Dolphin Street. It takes about 5 minutes. Buy your ticket from the driver. Our house (number 8) is opposite the bus stop and has a yellow door.

On Foot: Or if it's a nice day and you don't have a heavy bag, you can walk. It's about 20 minutes. When you leave the station, turn left and walk down Fish Street. At the school, turn right into Ship Street. Carry on for about 200 meters until you get to the traffic lights. Then turn left into Dolphin Street and go straight on past the library. Our house is on the right, next to a restaurant called Othello and opposite the bus stop.

Give me a call if you get lost!

We're all really looking forward to seeing you. Remember, you don't have to bring any food or drink – just yourself!

Love,
Mike

Comprehension Questions

1. How many ways can Jana get to Mike's house from the train station?

Show Answer
There are two ways: by bus or on foot.

2. What is special about Mike's house?

Show Answer
It has a yellow door and is number 8 on the street. It's opposite a bus stop and next to a restaurant called Othello.

3. How long does the bus ride take?

Show Answer
About 5 minutes.

4. Which direction should Jana turn when she leaves the train station?

Show Answer
Turn left and walk down Fish Street.

Language Focus: Direction Verbs

Find these verbs in the letter above. They are all used to give directions:

  • Take - use public transport
  • Get off - leave a bus or train
  • Turn - change direction
  • Walk - move on foot
  • Carry on - continue in the same direction
  • Go straight on - continue without turning
  • Go past - pass by something

Task 2: Partner Pair Work

Work with a partner. Take turns asking for directions.

Situation 1 - Asking for Directions:

You are at the train station and want to get to the library. Ask your partner (the local) for directions.

Useful phrases:

  • "Excuse me, how do I get to...?"
  • "Could you tell me the way to...?"
  • "Is it far from here?"
  • "Can you repeat that?"

Situation 2 - Giving Directions:

A visitor asks you for directions to the closest bus stop. Give clear, step-by-step directions.

Remember to use:

  • Turn left/right
  • Go straight on
  • Carry on for...
  • At the... turn...
  • It's opposite/next to...
