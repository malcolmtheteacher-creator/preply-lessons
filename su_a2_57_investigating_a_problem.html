How and Why Questions

To investigate a problem, you need to ask questions. Use "how" to find out what happened, and "why" to find out the reasons.

The Investigation Pattern

IDENTIFY THE PROBLEM
There aren't enough parking spaces at work.
State what the problem is clearly
ASK WHY
Why isn't there enough parking?
Find out the reason for the problem
ANSWER — DEEPER WHY
There are too many cars.
This leads to another why question
ASK WHY AGAIN
Why are there too many cars?
Continue investigating the root cause
ANSWER — ROOT CAUSE
Most staff drive to work.
Get to the real reason
ASK HOW TO SOLVE
How can we solve this?
Move towards finding a solution
SUGGEST A SOLUTION
The company could provide a shuttle bus service.
Offer a practical way to solve the problem

Key Question Patterns

Do you know how this happened? — Ask about the event or situation

What kind of information was stored on the flash drive? — Get details

Why was that? — Investigate the reason

How can we stop this happening again? — Find solutions and prevention

Solve These Workplace Problems

For each problem, think about how and why questions. What caused it? What is the solution?

Scenario 1: The Photocopier

Problem: The photocopier doesn't work.

Your questions:

  • How does it not work? (Is it broken? Out of toner?)
  • Why did this happen?
  • How can we fix it?

Scenario 2: Unhappy Employees

Problem: The employees are very unhappy.

Your questions:

  • Why are they unhappy?
  • What caused this situation?
  • How can management improve things?

Scenario 3: Security Issue

Problem: Our computer network has been hacked.

Your questions:

  • How did this happen? (Was it a weak password?)
  • What data was accessed?
  • How can we prevent this in the future?

Scenario 4: Theft

Problem: Someone stole four laptops from the office.

Your questions:

  • When did this happen?
  • Why weren't they secured?
  • How can we prevent theft in future?

Scenario 5: Financial Crisis

Problem: Our profits have dropped by 50%.

Your questions:

  • Why have profits dropped?
  • What caused this decline?
  • How can we increase income?

Scenario 6: Time Pressure

Problem: We have to find a new office in three days.

Your questions:

  • Why do we need to move so urgently?
  • What are the key requirements?
  • How can we speed up the search?

Speaking Task

You will investigate a workplace problem using how and why questions. Your goal is to understand the problem deeply and suggest solutions.

What to do:

Situation

Imagine a serious problem has occurred at your workplace. You need to investigate it by:

  • Asking how the problem happened
  • Asking why it happened (keep asking "why" to find root causes)
  • Asking how to prevent it or solve it
  • Suggesting practical solutions

Remember to use:

  • How questions: "How did this happen?", "How can we solve this?"
  • Why questions: "Why is this a problem?", "Why did that happen?"
  • Follow-up: "And why was that?", "What kind of…?"
  • Solutions: "We could…", "The company should…", "We might…"

Speaking Task — 3 Minutes

3:00

After your investigation, write a summary:

What was the problem? What caused it? What solutions did you suggest?
