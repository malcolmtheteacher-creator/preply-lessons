The words for today's story

Each new word is hidden in a sentence. Read it aloud and say what you think the bold word means from the rest of the sentence — then reveal it and see how close you were.

1. James was openly Catholic.
Catholic (adj) — belonging to the Roman Catholic Church.
2. His Declaration of Indulgence offered toleration.
a declaration (n) — an official statement.
3. He offered religious toleration.
toleration (n) — allowing different beliefs.
4. Seven bishops were acquitted.
to acquit (v) — to declare not guilty.
5. His son became the heir.
an heir (n) — the person who will inherit.
6. Nobles chose to invite William of Orange.
to invite (v) — to ask to come.
7. James decided to flee to France.
to flee (v) — to run away.
8. He threw the Great Seal into the Thames.
the Great Seal (n) — the seal used on official documents.
9. His supporters were Jacobites.
a Jacobite (n) — supporters of James and his heirs.
10. Supporters called it the Glorious Revolution.
glorious (adj) — wonderful, worthy of praise.

The story, in three parts

Read each part. After it, there's a quick comprehension check (try the questions from memory first), then one thing to discuss.

Part 1 — The Catholic king

James II, brother of Charles II, became king in 1685. He was openly Catholic. After defeating Monmouth's Rebellion, he placed Catholics in powerful positions and issued a Declaration of Indulgence giving religious toleration.

Quick check — answer from memory, then reveal:

1. What was James's religion?

2. What did his Declaration do?

1. Catholic.

2. Offered religious toleration.

Discuss:
  • Was toleration a good idea or a political tool?

Part 2 — The heir

In 1688 seven bishops refused to read the Declaration and were tried but acquitted. In June, James's wife gave birth to a son, meaning a Catholic dynasty. Leading nobles invited William of Orange, James's Protestant son-in-law, to come.

Quick check — answer from memory, then reveal:

1. What happened to the seven bishops?

2. Why did the birth matter?

1. They were tried and acquitted.

2. It meant a Catholic dynasty.

Discuss:
  • Why did a baby change everything?

Part 3 — The flight

William landed at Torbay in November 1688. James's support collapsed. In December he fled, reportedly throwing the Great Seal into the Thames. He was caught, released and escaped to France. Parliament declared that he had abandoned the throne.

Quick check — answer from memory, then reveal:

1. Where did William land?

2. What did James do with the Great Seal?

1. Torbay.

2. Reportedly threw it into the Thames.

Discuss:
  • Why didn't James fight?

Grammar — Past Perfect & Sequencing

History is a chain of causes. To show that one thing happened before another in the past, English uses the past perfect.

How it works:
• Past simple tells the main line: "The Danes attacked at Christmas."
• Past perfect (had + past participle) reaches further back: "By then, Alfred had already escaped into the marshes."
• Signal words: by the time, already, before, after, when, until.

1. Past simple or past perfect?

Complete each sentence about James II. Use the word in brackets.

1. Before William landed, James (lose) much support.

2. The bishops (refuse) to read the Declaration.

3. After the prince (be) born, nobles acted.

1. had lost

2. had refused

3. had been

2. Put the earlier event in the past perfect

1. By December the army (desert).

2. James (flee) before Parliament met.

3. Parliament said James (abandon) the throne.

1. had deserted

2. had fled

3. had abandoned

Going deeper

The Glorious Revolution led to the Bill of Rights in 1689, limiting royal power. In Ireland and Scotland, however, it led to war and bitter divisions.

Worth noticing: "glorious" was the winners' word; in Ireland the events were far from bloodless.
Learning from the past — for today: Read the room. James pushed ahead without allies. Change works best with support — build it first.
Dig into it — talk these through:
  • Was it glorious?
  • Why did James lose so fast?
  • When did you push change without support?
