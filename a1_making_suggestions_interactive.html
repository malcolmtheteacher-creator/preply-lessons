💡 Making Suggestions

Learn to suggest activities and make plans with friends!

📚 How to Make Suggestions

Want to invite your friends to do something? 🎉 Learn three easy ways to make suggestions in English!

🚀
Let's...
Allons... / On...
"Let's play video games!"
🤔
Shall we...?
On... ? (plus poli)
"Shall we go to the cinema?"
❓
How about...?
Et si on... ?
"How about watching Netflix?"

🎯 The Patterns

Let's + verb (base form)

Let's go to the park!

Let's eat pizza!

Shall we + verb (base form)?

Shall we watch a movie?

Shall we play football?

How about + verb-ING?

How about going to McDonald's?

How about playing video games?

✅ ❌ How to Respond

👍

Saying YES

  • ✅ "Yes, let's!"
  • ✅ "Good idea!"
  • ✅ "Great idea!"
  • ✅ "Sure!"
  • ✅ "Sounds good!"
  • ✅ "OK, let's do it!"
👎

Saying NO (politely)

  • ❌ "Sorry, I can't."
  • ❌ "I'd rather not."
  • ❌ "Maybe later."
  • ❌ "I'm not sure..."
  • ❌ "How about ... instead?"

💡 Which One Should I Use?

Let's = casual, with friends, enthusiastic 🎉

Shall we = more polite, asking permission 🤝

How about = suggesting an alternative idea 💭

💡 Tip: Be Polite!

When saying no, always be polite and maybe suggest something else:

"Sorry, I can't. How about tomorrow?"

"I'd rather not. Let's do something else!"

🗣️ Make Plans Together!

📅 Weekend Planning

Make suggestions for what to do this weekend:

  • What do you want to do on Saturday morning?
  • Suggest something fun for Saturday afternoon.
  • What about Saturday evening?
  • Make a plan for Sunday too!

🎭 Scenario 1: After School

Situation: It's 4pm. School is finished. You're with your friend. Make 3 suggestions!

Example: "School is finished! Let's go to the park! Or shall we get ice cream? How about going to my house to play video games?"

🎭 Scenario 2: Rainy Day

Situation: It's raining outside. You can't go to the park. What indoor activities do you suggest?

Example: "It's raining! Let's stay inside. How about watching Netflix? Or shall we play board games?"

🎭 Scenario 3: Birthday Party

Situation: It's your friend's birthday soon. Help plan the party! Where? What activities? What food?

Example: "For your birthday, let's have a party at your house! Shall we order pizza? How about playing video games and watching a movie?"

🏆 Final Challenges

🎯 Challenge 1: The Suggestion Chain

Take turns making suggestions with your teacher. Each person must respond AND make a new suggestion!

Example:

Teacher: "Let's play football!"

You: "Good idea! And shall we get ice cream after?"

Teacher: "Sure! How about going to the park too?"

You: "Sounds good! Let's bring a picnic!"

🎯 Challenge 2: The Picky Friend

Your teacher will say "no" to everything! Keep making new suggestions until they say "yes"!

Example:

You: "Let's watch TV!"

Teacher: "Sorry, I can't. The TV is broken."

You: "How about playing video games?"

Teacher: "I'd rather not. I'm tired of games."

You: "Shall we go for a walk?"

Teacher: "Great idea! Let's go!"

🌟 SUPER CHALLENGE: Plan the Perfect Day!

Plan a complete day with your friend using ALL the suggestion forms!

  • ✅ Use Let's... at least 2 times
  • ✅ Use Shall we...? at least 2 times
  • ✅ Use How about...? at least 2 times
  • ✅ Include: morning, afternoon, AND evening plans

MEGA Example:

"OK, tomorrow is Saturday! In the morning, let's sleep late - we're tired! Then, shall we have a big breakfast? How about making pancakes? In the afternoon, let's go to the park and play football. Shall we invite more friends? In the evening, how about watching a movie at my house? Let's order pizza too! It's going to be a great day!"

🎮 GAME: Yes, And...

Every suggestion must be accepted with "Yes! And..." then add something more!

Example:

A: "Let's go to the cinema!"

B: "Yes! And let's get popcorn!"

A: "Yes! And shall we sit in the front row?"

B: "Yes! And how about getting drinks too?"
