Short vs Extended Answers

In IELTS Speaking Part 1, you should speak for about 15-20 seconds per question. Short answers won't demonstrate your ability!

Click each card to see how to extend the answer:

Do you enjoy your job?
Yes, I do. I like it a lot.
Yes, I really enjoy my job. I work as a software developer, and what I love most about it is the problem-solving aspect. Every day brings new challenges, and I find it really satisfying when I manage to fix a difficult bug or create something useful.

What's the best thing about your job?
We get a lot of holidays.
I'd say the best thing is probably the flexible working hours. I can start and finish when I want, as long as I complete my tasks. This gives me a much better work-life balance, and I can spend more time with my family.

Do you prefer working alone or with others?
I much prefer working on my own.
I much prefer working on my own, to be honest. I find I'm more productive when I can focus without interruptions. That said, I do enjoy collaborating with colleagues on big projects because it's good to share ideas and get different perspectives.

Useful Vocabulary for Work & Study

Click each card to reveal the meaning. Which words describe your situation?

apprentice
(noun)
Someone who works for a skilled person to learn a trade or job. Often younger and learning on the job.
part-time
(adjective)
Working fewer hours than full-time, typically less than 35 hours per week.
full-time
(adjective)
Working the standard number of hours, typically 35-40 hours per week.
salary
(noun)
A fixed regular payment, usually monthly, for professional or office work.
benefits
(plural noun)
Extra advantages provided by an employer, such as health insurance, pension, holidays, etc.
degree
(noun)
An academic qualification given by a university after completing a course of study.
qualification
(noun)
An official record showing you have completed a course or have the skills for something.
course
(noun)
A series of lessons or lectures on a particular subject, often leading to a qualification.

Self-Assessment: Grammatical Range & Accuracy

Use the official IELTS band descriptors to assess your level. Click the band that best describes YOUR speaking today.

9
Structures are precise and accurate at all times, apart from 'mistakes' characteristic of native speaker speech.
8
Wide range of structures, flexibly used. The majority of sentences are error free. Occasional inappropriacies and non-systematic errors occur. A few basic errors may persist.
7
A range of structures flexibly used. Error-free sentences are frequent. Both simple and complex sentences are used effectively despite some errors. A few basic errors persist.
6
Produces a mix of short and complex sentence forms and a variety of structures with limited flexibility. Though errors frequently occur in complex structures, these rarely impede communication.
5
Basic sentence forms are fairly well controlled for accuracy. Complex structures are attempted but these are limited in range, nearly always contain errors and may lead to the need for reformulation.
4
Can produce basic sentence forms and some short utterances are error-free. Subordinate clauses are rare and, overall, turns are short, structures are repetitive and errors are frequent.

🎯 Key Takeaways:
1. Extend your answers with reasons, examples, and details
2. Use topic-specific vocabulary (salary, benefits, degree, qualification, etc.)
3. Mix simple and complex sentences
4. Aim for 15-20 seconds per Part 1 answer
