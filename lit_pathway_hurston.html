Meet the writer · B1

Zora Neale Hurston

1891-1960 · Alabama and Florida

A writer and collector of folk tales who celebrated the voices of Black communities in the American South.

A life in a few steps

  1. 1891Born in Alabama. She grows up in Eatonville, Florida, one of the first all-Black towns in America.
  2. 1920sBecomes a leading voice of the Harlem Renaissance in New York.
  3. 1926'Sweat'.
  4. 1928Graduates from Barnard College, where she studies anthropology.
  5. 1937Their Eyes Were Watching God.
  6. 1960Dies in Florida, aged 69, poor and almost forgotten.

Why people still read Hurston

She wrote her characters' speech the way people really spoke. She was rediscovered in the 1970s, and her novel is now an American classic.

Did you know?

In 1973 the writer Alice Walker found Hurston's unmarked grave and paid for a headstone.
