Model Cover Letter

Read this cover letter. Notice the structure, vocabulary, and how it is organized. Look at how the writer describes themselves and their experience.

Dear Sir / Madam,

I am interested in applying for the position of teaching assistant, which was posted on the website 'jobs.com'. I believe I have the necessary skills and training to become an excellent member of your teaching team.

I see myself as an enthusiastic person who is self-motivated and hard-working. I enjoy working with different students and being in a classroom environment.

In June 2020, I graduated with a degree in History from Liverpool University and I have recently completed my teacher training certificate at a school in London. My previous work experience includes working in children's retail and being a primary school assistant.

I would welcome the opportunity to discuss my application and experience with you and I look forward to hearing from you soon.

Yours Faithfully,

Emma Wallace

Letter Structure

A good cover letter has these parts:

PARAGRAPH 1
State why you are writing and which job you want
Example: "I am interested in applying for the position of teaching assistant..."
PARAGRAPH 2
Describe your character and why you are good for the job
Example: "I see myself as an enthusiastic person who is self-motivated and hard-working..."
PARAGRAPH 3
Explain your qualifications and work experience
Example: "I graduated with a degree in... My previous work experience includes..."
PARAGRAPH 4
Express interest in discussing the role and close politely
Example: "I would welcome the opportunity to discuss... I look forward to hearing from you soon."

Plan Your Cover Letter

Now you will plan your own cover letter. Choose one of the job advertisements from the previous section. Think about what you want to say in each paragraph.

Step 1: Choose Your Job

Which job have you chosen?

Step 2: Plan Your Content

Think about what to include in each paragraph of your letter:

Paragraph 1: Why you are writing

Mention the specific job position and where you found it.

Paragraph 2: Your character and why you suit the job

Use positive adjectives. Explain why you would be good at this job.

Paragraph 3: Your qualifications and experience

Mention your education and previous jobs. Connect them to the position.

Paragraph 4: Closing and call to action

Express your interest in discussing the role. End politely.

Success Criteria

When you write your full cover letter, remember these points:

Your letter should:

  • Use an appropriate greeting (Dear Sir / Madam)
  • State clearly which job you want
  • Describe your character using positive adjectives
  • Include your qualifications and work experience
  • Explain why you are the right person for the role
  • Be organized into clear paragraphs
  • Use polite formal language and key phrases
  • End with "Yours Faithfully" or similar closing
