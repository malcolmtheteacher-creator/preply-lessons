Something to think about

Modern life is full of noise: traffic, phones, music and TV. Some people say they cannot think or sleep well with so much sound. Others feel uncomfortable when it is completely quiet.

SPEAK Do we need more quiet in our lives? Give your first idea, even if you are not sure.

Two opinions

Read both. Which opinion is stronger for you? Why?

Some people say…

Silence is good for us. It helps us relax, sleep and think. In a quiet place we can hear our own ideas.

Other people say…

Some people feel uncomfortable in silence. Music and sounds can make us happy and help us work, and noise is a normal part of life.
