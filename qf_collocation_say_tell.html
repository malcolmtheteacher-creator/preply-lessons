📢 Say vs Tell

A2 • Universal • Choose the right word

🔍 Let's notice what we see

Look at this sentence. What's the problem?

"He said me a story."

Can you spot the error? What should the word be instead of "said"?

🎯 The Rule:

SAY + (that) + clause, OR just SAY something. No person directly after SAY.

TELL + person + something. TELL is always followed by a person.

In this sentence, we need a person after the verb (me), so we use TELL: "He told me a story."

🛠️ Understand the difference

Two speech bubbles:

SAY 💬 — Words going OUT to everyone (or no one in particular)

Example: "He said hello." / "She said that it was raining."

TELL ➡️👤 — Words directed AT a PERSON (arrow points to someone)

Example: "He told me a story." / "She told us that it was raining."

📌 Fixed expressions

SAY:
• say hello
• say goodbye
• say sorry
• say a prayer
TELL:
• tell a story
• tell a joke
• tell a lie
• tell the truth

✏️ Guided practice

1. "She _____ me the truth." (say / tell)
2. "I _____ hello to my teacher." (said / told)
3. "He _____ us a joke." (said / told)
4. "Can you _____ me about your weekend?" (say / tell)

🔗 Related error: Speak vs Talk

Similar confusion:

• I speak English (name a language) — also "speak about/of/to"

• Let's talk about it (have a conversation) — more informal than "speak"

🚀 Now you try — no help!

Complete these sentences with the correct form. Check your answers when you're done.

1. "Mother _____ me to always tell the truth."
Answer: "Mother told me to always tell the truth."

→ Needs a person (me), so TELL.

2. "He _____ that he was very tired."
Answer: "He said that he was very tired."

→ No person after, just "that + clause", so SAY.

3. "Don't _____ anyone my secret!"
Answer: "Don't tell anyone my secret!"

→ Person (anyone) + object (my secret), so TELL.

4. "They _____ goodbye and left the party."
Answer: "They said goodbye and left the party."

→ Fixed expression "say goodbye" — no person after.
