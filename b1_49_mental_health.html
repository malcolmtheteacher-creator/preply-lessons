Looking After Your Mental Health

B1 Level · Health & Wellbeing

Understanding Mental Health

Mental health is as important as physical health. Many people experience stress, anxiety, or sadness at some point. The key is recognizing when you need help and knowing what strategies work for you.

Let's talk about mental wellbeing:

• How do you currently manage stress?

• What makes you feel anxious or overwhelmed?

• Do you talk about your feelings with others?

• What activities help you relax?

• How do you balance work/study and personal time?

Mental Health Vocabulary

Anxiety Feeling nervous or worried about something; persistent fear
Mindfulness Paying attention to the present moment without judgment
Self-care Activities you do to maintain your physical and mental health
Burnout Physical or mental collapse from overwork and stress
Resilience The ability to recover from difficulties; emotional strength
Overwhelmed Buried under too much emotion or responsibility
Coping Strategy A technique to manage stress or difficult emotions
Therapy Professional treatment to improve mental health

Five Evidence-Based Wellbeing Strategies

1. Regular Exercise

Physical activity releases endorphins, which improve mood. Even 20 minutes of walking can reduce anxiety. Exercise also helps regulate sleep and increases confidence.

2. Sleep & Routine

Poor sleep damages mental health. Aim for 7–9 hours nightly. Consistent bedtimes and routines signal to your brain that it's time to rest. Good sleep improves memory and emotional regulation.

3. Social Connection

Humans are social creatures. Spending time with friends and family reduces loneliness and depression. Even brief conversations can boost mood. Don't isolate when stressed—reach out.

4. Mindfulness & Meditation

Practicing mindfulness reduces anxiety and increases self-awareness. Start with just 5 minutes daily. Apps like Calm or Headspace guide beginners. Meditation doesn't require silence or emptying your mind.

5. Limiting Social Media

Excessive social media use increases anxiety and depression. Set daily time limits. Unfollow accounts that trigger negative feelings. Remember: social media shows highlight reels, not reality.

Your Wellbeing Practices

Reflect on Your Mental Health

1. What's one thing that negatively affects your mental health?

Be honest with yourself. Identifying the problem is the first step to addressing it.

2. What's one strategy you could try this week?

Start small. Even one simple change can make a difference over time.

3. How is mental health discussed in your culture?

Mental health awareness varies globally. In some places, it's becoming more open; in others, it remains taboo.

4. Who do you talk to when you're struggling?

Having a support system is crucial. This could be friends, family, or a professional.

Important Resources:

• If you're struggling, please reach out to someone you trust

• Many countries have free mental health helplines available 24/7

• Therapy and counseling are available and can help

• Mental health is not a weakness; it's part of being human

• Recovery is possible, and you don't have to handle everything alone
