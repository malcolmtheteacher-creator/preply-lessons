A Vendetta

Guy de Maupassant — an old woman makes a plan to avenge her son.

Today: you'll read a dark, clever story about a mother, her lost son and a careful plan — and you'll practise to + verb to say why someone does something.
Before you read — talk a little. In some old cultures, families took "revenge" when someone hurt them. Is revenge ever a good idea? What should people do instead? Say one or two sentences.

The words for today's story

Each word is in a sentence. Read it and guess the meaning, then click Reveal.

1. When her son was killed, the woman wanted revenge — to pay back the man who did it.
revenge (n) — hurting someone because they hurt you or your family first.
2. A man murdered her son — he killed him on purpose.
to murder (v) — to kill a person on purpose. (A person who does this is a murderer.)
3. The old woman was very angry and could not forget what happened.
angry (adj) — feeling strong bad feelings about something.
4. She was old and weak, so she could not fight the man herself.
weak (adj) — not strong; not powerful.
5. So she made a clever plan to get her revenge.
a plan (n) — an idea about how to do something, step by step.
6. She used her big dog — a strong animal that could bite.
a dog (n) — a common animal that people keep; it can guard and bite.
7. She kept the dog hungry and did not give it food.
hungry (adj) — needing or wanting to eat.
8. She trained the dog every day to teach it what to do.
to train (v) — to teach a person or animal to do something by practising.
9. After months, the dog became fierce — wild and dangerous.
fierce (adj) — angry, wild and ready to attack.
10. She took a boat across the water to find the man.
a boat (n) — a small ship for travelling on water.

Now use them

Talk before you read:
  • Use revenge, weak and plan: how could an old, weak woman get revenge on a strong man?
  • The story is dark but clever. What kind of plan do you think she makes?

The story, in three parts

Read each part. Then answer the quick questions (try from memory first), and talk about one thing.

Part 1 — A terrible night

On the island of Corsica, an old woman lived with her son and their dog. She loved her son very much. But one night, a man called Ravolati killed him. Then Ravolati ran away across the sea to another island, to be safe.

The old woman was full of grief and anger. In Corsica, there was an old rule: when someone kills a member of your family, you must take revenge. But the woman was old and weak, and she had no other sons. How could she do it? She had no idea — yet.

Quick check — answer, then reveal:

1. What did Ravolati do, and where did he go?

2. Why was revenge difficult for the old woman?

1. He killed the woman's son, then ran away across the sea to another island to be safe.

2. She was old and weak, with no other sons, so she could not fight a strong man herself.

Discuss:
  • The old rule says the family must take revenge. Is it fair to ask a weak old woman to do this?

Part 2 — The plan

The old woman thought for a long time. Then she had an idea. She had a big dog called Semillante. She made a doll from straw and old clothes, so it looked like a man. Around the doll's neck, she tied pieces of meat.

Every day, she kept the dog hungry. Then she showed it the doll, and the dog jumped up to eat the meat at the doll's neck. Day after day, she trained the dog to attack the neck. After three months, the dog was fierce and strong, and it jumped at the doll's throat every time.

Quick check — answer, then reveal:

1. What did the woman make, and why?

2. How did she train the dog to attack the neck?

1. A straw doll dressed like a man, with meat tied around its neck, to teach the dog to attack a man's throat.

2. She kept the dog hungry and put the meat at the doll's neck, so the dog jumped at the neck to eat.

Discuss:
  • The plan is patient and very clever. Does the woman's cleverness make you admire her, or frighten you?

Part 3 — The revenge

When the dog was ready, the old woman took a boat across the sea to find Ravolati. She did not eat, and she did not feed the dog, to keep it very hungry.

She found the man at his door. She let the hungry dog go, and it jumped straight at his throat. He could not fight it. In a moment, it was over. The old woman went home quietly. That night, for the first time in months, she slept well.

Quick check — answer, then reveal:

1. Why did she not feed the dog before she went?

2. How does the story end for the old woman?

1. To keep the dog very hungry, so it would attack the man fiercely.

2. She gets her revenge, goes home quietly, and sleeps well for the first time in months.

Discuss:
  • The last line says she slept well. What does that quiet ending make you feel about her?

Grammar — Purpose: to + verb

This whole story is about why the woman does things. We use to + verb to explain the purpose — the reason for an action: she trained the dog to kill the man.

The rule:
• Use to + base verb to answer the question "Why?": "She made a doll to train the dog." "He ran away to be safe."
• It comes after the main action: [main action] + to + [purpose].
• We do not use for + verb here. Say "She went to find him" (not "for find him").

1. Match the action to its purpose — complete with to + verb

1. Ravolati ran away safe. (be)

2. The woman made a doll the dog. (train)

3. She kept the dog hungry it fierce. (make)

4. She took a boat the man. (find)

1. to be · 2. to train · 3. to make · 4. to find

2. Answer "Why?" with a full purpose phrase

Write a short answer beginning with to…

1. Why did she tie meat to the doll's neck?  

2. Why did she not feed the dog before the journey?  

1. e.g. To teach the dog to attack a man's neck.

2. e.g. To keep the dog very hungry (so it would attack fiercely).

3. Complete the paragraph

The old woman did everything for one reason: revenge. She trained her dog (attack) a man. She kept it hungry (make) it fierce. Then she crossed the sea (find) her son's killer.

to attack · to make · to find

Going deeper — talk more

Talk about these questions out loud. There are no wrong answers — just say what you think.

Useful language: "I think…", "In my opinion…", "It's right / wrong because…", "In the film…".
1 · Is revenge ever right?

The woman follows an old tradition of revenge. Is taking revenge ever the right thing to do? What should people do instead when someone hurts their family? Give your reasons.

2 · The law or the tradition?

The woman breaks the law, but she follows her culture's old rule. When a law and an old tradition disagree, which should win? Why?

3 · Revenge in stories

Many films and books tell revenge stories. Do you know one? What usually happens to the person who takes revenge — do they feel better or worse? What do these stories teach us?
