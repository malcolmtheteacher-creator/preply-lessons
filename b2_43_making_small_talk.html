Making Small Talk

B2 Level · People & Interaction

Why Small Talk Matters

"You can make more friends in two months by being interested in other people than in two years by trying to get people interested in you." — Dale Carnegie

What does this quote mean to you?

Small talk is not just about filling silence. It's about showing genuine interest in the other person. The best conversationalists ask questions and listen—they make the other person feel valued.

Present Continuous for Polite Questions

The present continuous (are you + -ing?) is perfect for small talk because it sounds warm, friendly, and interested in what someone is doing RIGHT NOW.

Why we use it:

• Ask polite, friendly questions — It shows you're interested in the person's current activities.

• Talk about short-term actions or projects — Work happening now, this week, this month.

• Keep a conversation informal and warm — Less formal than simple present questions.

Examples from Small Talk

Are you working on anything exciting at the moment? Polite question about current projects
What are you currently helping out with? Question about what they're focused on now
Are you still working from home? Question about their current situation
I'm currently helping out with a new project. Your answer explaining what you're doing now
