The Recipe

Simple Pasta Recipe

How to Make Pasta with Tomato Sauce

Ingredients: 400g pasta, 2 tins of tomatoes, 1 onion, 2 cloves of garlic, salt, pepper, oil

Steps:

First, chop the onion and garlic. Heat oil in a large pan. Fry the onion and garlic for 2 minutes. It smells delicious! Add the tins of tomatoes. Stir it well. Leave it for 15 minutes. Add salt and pepper.

While the sauce is cooking, boil water in a big pot. Add salt. When the water is hot, add the pasta. Stir it. Cook for 8-10 minutes. The pasta should be soft.

Drain the pasta. Put it on a plate. Pour the sauce over it. Enjoy your dinner!

Cooking Tips

Good cooking habits:

I read the recipe before I start cooking
I prepare all ingredients first
I cook on medium heat, not too hot
I taste the food while cooking
I clean up while I cook
I ask family for help sometimes
