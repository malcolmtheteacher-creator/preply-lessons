➕ Quick Fix: Not Only... But Also (Inversion)

Add emphasis — and invert the verb when not only starts a sentence

Level C1

What's the error?

Original sentence:

"Not only she sings beautifully, but she also plays the piano."

Not only she sings — When NOT ONLY starts a sentence, the auxiliary verb must invert before the subject, like in a question.

Corrected:

Not only does she sing beautifully, but she also plays the piano.

The Rule

MID-SENTENCE: No inversion — the subject comes before the auxiliary

She not only sings but also plays.

SENTENCE-INITIAL (for emphasis): Inversion — the auxiliary comes before the subject (like a question)

Not only does she sing, but she also plays.

Which auxiliary? Use the same auxiliary that would be used in the main verb:

  • Present simple → does/do
  • Past simple → did
  • Present perfect → has/have
  • Past continuous → was/were
  • Modal verbs → can, could, will, would, etc.

How to Fix It

✓ MID-SENTENCE (no inversion)

➡

Pattern: Subject + not only + verb + but also + verb

She not only reads but also writes poetry.

He not only passed but also scored the highest grade.

✓ SENTENCE-INITIAL (inversion)

🔄

Pattern: Not only + auxiliary + subject + verb...

Not only does she sing beautifully, but she also plays the piano.

Not only have they won, but they have broken the record.

More Examples

Past tense inversion:

Not only was she late, but she also forgot the documents.

Present perfect inversion:

Not only have they apologised, but they have also offered a refund.

Modal inversion:

Not only can she speak English, but she also speaks French fluently.
Test for inversion: Does NOT ONLY start the sentence? If yes → invert like a question. If NOT ONLY is mid-sentence → no inversion needed.
