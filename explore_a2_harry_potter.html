Find the mistake

Each sentence has one mistake. Write the correct sentence. Then click Check.

Hermione have brown hair.

Hermione has brown hair. With she, we say has.

Harry can to fly.

Harry can fly. After can we use the verb with no "to".

Ron has very tall.

Ron is very tall. Tall is an adjective, so we use is.

Who Is It?

Look at these three sentences. What is the pattern?

  • Hermione has brown hair.
  • Hagrid is very big.
  • Harry can fly.

When do we use has, is and can? Say your idea first.

has + a thing: has black hair, has glasses, has a beard

is + a describing word: is brave, is old, is funny

can + a verb: can fly, can swim, can do magic

Careful: he can fly (not "he cans fly"), and she has (not "she have").

A day at a wizard school

At Hogwarts, the students live in four houses: Gryffindor, Hufflepuff, Ravenclaw and Slytherin. In the morning they eat breakfast in the Great Hall, and owls bring letters. Then they go to lessons. They learn about potions, plants and spells. In the afternoon, some students play Quidditch. They fly on broomsticks and try to catch a small golden ball. In the evening, they do their homework.
