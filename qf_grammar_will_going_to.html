📌 The Rule

GOING TO = plans and evidence (you have proof NOW)

Example: "Look—she's going to fall!" (I can see evidence NOW)

WILL = spontaneous decisions and predictions WITHOUT evidence

Example: "I think it will rain tomorrow" (prediction, no evidence now)
