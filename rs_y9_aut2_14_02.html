Creation Stories Across Religions

Christianity: The Genesis Account

Genesis 1–2 describes creation. God creates over six days, culminating in humans made in God's image. This emphasises purpose and humanity's special place.

Christian interpretations: Literal (young Earth creationism), metaphorical (symbolic truths about purpose), framework (Genesis explains "who" and "why," not "how"), or day-age (days = geological epochs).

Islam: Quranic Creation

The Qur'an describes creation in several chapters. God created the heavens and Earth, and humans from clay. Many Muslims see this as compatible with evolutionary science—evolution is God's method.

Buddhism: Dependent Origination

Buddhism has no creation myth. Dependent Origination teaches that everything arises from causes and conditions. No creator God needed. This aligns naturally with evolutionary science.

How Religions Interpret Creation

Literal Interpretation

Creation story is literally true. Young Earth Creationists believe Earth is 6,000–10,000 years old.

Metaphorical Interpretation

Sees spiritual truths in creation stories—"in God's image" means moral worth, not physical similarity.

Framework Approach

Genesis explains spiritual truth about purpose; science explains the actual mechanisms.

Day-Age Theory

The "six days" represent six geological ages, aligning biblical with scientific timescales.

Literal or Metaphorical?

The Earth was created in exactly six 24-hour days, about 6,000 years ago. "In God's image" means we have capacity for reasoning and morality. "Days" in Genesis represent long geological epochs matching evolutionary timescales. Genesis explains why God created (for relationship) but not how scientifically.

Key Concepts

What do Christians claim about humans?

Answer: Humans are made "in the image of God"—special value and purpose.

How do Muslims interpret creation?

Answer: God created all things. Many Muslims see this as compatible with evolution.

Why is Buddhism compatible with evolution?

Answer: Buddhism teaches natural causation, not a creator God.

What is the "Framework Approach"?

Answer: Genesis explains spiritual "who" and "why"; science explains "how."

Can evolution and faith coexist?

Answer: Yes. Evolution is the mechanism; faith addresses meaning.
