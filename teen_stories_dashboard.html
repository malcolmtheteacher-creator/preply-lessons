Short Stories for Teens

Twelve classic stories worth arguing about — each in two levels, B2/C1 and A2/B1.

A girl who invents a ghost story on the spot. A boy punished for being right. A child offered more money than her family has ever held, for one piece of information. A promise kept for twenty years, at a terrible price. A school lesson that turns out to be the last one. And a firework who is absolutely certain the royal wedding is about him. Saki, Jewett, O. Henry, Daudet and Wilde — comic, humane, and safe to discuss with any class.

And six with sharper teeth: a charm that grants three wishes and takes something for each one; two enemies pinned under the same tree; a kidnapping that goes catastrophically right for the boy; a man alone in seventy below; a lawyer who bets fifteen years of his life; and a signalman who keeps seeing a figure at the mouth of a tunnel. Jacobs, Saki, O. Henry, London, Chekhov and Dickens — still frightening, still funny, and still worth an argument.

Every story comes as a pair: a B2/C1 version and an easier A2/B1 retelling, linked to each other at the top of every lesson — so you can switch level mid-lesson. The speaking tasks always offer non-personal alternatives: nobody has to talk about themselves.
