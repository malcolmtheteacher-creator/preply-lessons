Year 11: GCSE Religious Studies

"Islam practices, thematic studies, and mastering the exam."

What Year 11 Is About

Year 11 completes your GCSE Religious Studies course (AQA Specification A, 8062). You'll study Islamic practices (completing your study of Islam from Year 10), then move to the thematic studies that span Christianity and Islam. In the summer term, you'll revise both religions and exam papers intensively in preparation for your final GCSE exams. This is the year where evaluation skills truly matter, and where you'll become confident in handling complex questions about belief, practice, ethics, and contemporary issues.

How Year 11 Works

  • Complete Islamic studies — Units 25–26 finish your study of Islam with practices. Combined with Year 10's beliefs, you'll have comprehensive knowledge of Christianity and Islam for Paper 1.
  • Thematic studies (Themes A, B, D, E) — Units 27–28 introduce how Christians and Muslims respond to real-world ethical and social issues. These form the basis of Paper 2 questions.
  • Paper 1: Study of Religions — 192 marks covering Christianity and Islam beliefs, teachings, and practices. Assessed in Unit 29 mock.
  • Paper 2: Thematic Studies — 192 marks covering four themes (you choose one). Assessed in Unit 30 mock.
  • Evaluation and application matter — Both papers test your ability to analyse, evaluate, and apply religious teachings to contemporary situations.
  • Summer revision is intensive — Units 29–30 focus on bridging gaps, practising exam technique, and building confidence before your final exams.

Year 11 is the culmination of your GCSE journey. Master the Islamic practices, embrace the thematic studies, revise thoroughly, and you'll be ready to excel in your final exams.
