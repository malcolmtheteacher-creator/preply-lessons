Connectors for Giving Explanations

These connectors help you explain why something happens or explain reasons for cultural differences.

On account of — formal; often followed by a noun phrase
Due to — slightly formal; cause and effect (often negative outcomes)
The reason being that — explanatory; introduces a full reason
Since — causal; can be formal or informal
Because — most common; informal and direct
So — shows result or consequence
Because of — followed by noun; middle formality

Cultural Dimensions

Every culture sits on different spectrums. Use the sliders below to position your culture on each scale. Then think of a culture at the opposite end.

Individualism vs Collectivism
Individualist Collectivist
Individualist: People prioritise personal goals, independence, and individual rights. Collectivist: People prioritise group harmony, family, and community needs.
Direct vs Indirect Communication
Direct & Explicit Indirect & Subtle
Direct: People say exactly what they mean; silence means nothing. Indirect: People hint and imply; context and silence matter.
Formal vs Informal Society
Formal & Structured Informal & Flexible
Formal: Clear rules, hierarchy, protocols, and etiquette matter. Informal: Relaxed rules, egalitarian, casual interaction.
Monochronic vs Polychronic Time
Linear & Sequential Fluid & Simultaneous
Monochronic: Time is linear; schedules are strict; one thing at a time. Polychronic: Time is flexible; relationships matter more than schedules; multitasking is normal.
High Power Distance vs Low Power Distance
Hierarchical & Deferential Egalitarian & Direct
High Power Distance: Clear hierarchy; respect for authority; inequality accepted. Low Power Distance: Flat structures; question authority; inequality questioned.
Task-Oriented vs Relationship-Oriented
Task & Results First Relationships & Trust First
Task-Oriented: Goals and efficiency are paramount; get to business quickly. Relationship-Oriented: Building trust and connection comes first; relationships enable business.
