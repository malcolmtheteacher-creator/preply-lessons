Today's Goal

You're going to build stories from random words. Think fast, speak fluently, and make it fun.

The Challenge

You'll see 5 words on screen. You have 60 seconds to tell a story that uses ALL 5 words.

Rules:

  • You must use every word.
  • The story must make sense.
  • It doesn't have to be true.

Storytelling Tips

Click each tip to reveal the example.

Tip 1: Start with a person and a place

"Last summer, my friend decided to..."

Tip 2: Create a problem

"But then something unexpected happened..."

Tip 3: Use the words as plot points, not decoration

Don't just mention them — make them important to the story.

Tip 4: End with a twist or a lesson

"And that's why I never..."
