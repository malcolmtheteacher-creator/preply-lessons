The story, in three parts

Read each part. Then try the questions from memory, and talk about the last one.

Part 1 — A girl becomes queen

Her name was Victoria. She was born in London in 1819. Her father died when she was a baby. Her mother was very strict. As a child, Victoria was never alone. She slept in her mother's room. She could not walk down the stairs without help. It was a lonely life for a young girl.

One morning in 1837, when Victoria was only eighteen, someone woke her early. Her uncle, the King, had died in the night. Now Victoria was the queen. On her first day, she asked to be alone for one hour. It was the first time in her life! That same day, she moved her bed out of her mother's room. The young queen wanted to be free.

Victoria was very young to rule a big country. At first, an older man, the Prime Minister, helped her. But everyone wanted the same thing for the new queen. They wanted her to marry and to have children.

Quick check — answer from memory, then reveal:

1. What was Victoria's life like when she was a child?

2. How old was Victoria when she became queen, and what did she do on her first day?

1. It was lonely and strict. She was never alone, slept in her mother's room, and could not go downstairs without help.

2. She was eighteen. On her first day, she asked to be alone for one hour and moved her bed out of her mother's room.

Talk about it:
  • Victoria was controlled as a child, and then she was suddenly free and powerful. Do you think this was easy or hard for her? Why?

Part 2 — Albert and the big empire

In 1840, Victoria married a young German prince called Albert. Because she was the queen, she had to ask him to marry her! They loved each other very much. On her wedding day, Victoria wore a simple white dress. This was new and different. After this, many women wanted a white dress for their wedding too. We still do this today.

Victoria and Albert had nine children. Albert helped the queen with her work. In 1851, he made a big show called the Great Exhibition. It was in a beautiful building made of glass, called the Crystal Palace. Millions of people came to see the new machines. This was a time of factories, trains and big cities in Britain.

At this time, Britain had a very big empire. It grew and grew. Victoria ruled many countries all around the world — about one quarter of all the people on Earth! In 1876, she also became the Empress of India. Some men tried to hurt the queen, but they always failed. Every time, the people loved their queen even more.

Quick check — answer from memory, then reveal:

1. Why did Victoria have to ask Albert to marry her, and what did she wear?

2. How big was Victoria's empire?

1. Because she was the queen, so she had to ask him. She wore a simple white dress, and this started the fashion for white wedding dresses.

2. Very big. She ruled about one quarter of all the people on Earth, and she became the Empress of India in 1876.

Talk about it:
  • Victoria's white wedding dress started a fashion that is still popular today. What other old traditions do people still follow now?

Part 3 — The queen in black

In 1861, Albert became ill and died. He was only forty-two years old. Victoria was very, very sad. She wore black clothes every day for the rest of her life — for forty years! She did not want to see people. For many years, the queen almost disappeared. Some people asked, "Do we still need a queen?"

Slowly, Victoria came back to her work. She had some new friends who helped her. One was a Scottish man called John Brown. Another was an Indian man called Abdul Karim, who taught her his language. Her family did not like these friends, because they were not rich or important. But Victoria did not care.

When Victoria was old, the people loved her again. They had big parties for her — fifty years as queen, then sixty years as queen. She was like a grandmother to the whole country. Victoria died in 1901, when she was eighty-one. She was queen for almost sixty-four years. That is why we still call this time in history the "Victorian" age.

Quick check — answer from memory, then reveal:

1. What did Victoria do after Albert died?

2. Why did Victoria's family not like her friends John Brown and Abdul Karim?

1. She was very sad. She wore black clothes for the rest of her life (forty years) and did not want to see people.

2. Because they were not rich or important, and one was Scottish and one was Indian. But Victoria did not care.

Talk about it:
  • Victoria was sad for forty years. Is it good to remember someone we love for a long time, or is it better to be happy again? What do you think?

Grammar: adjectives before nouns

An adjective is a word that describes a noun — young, big, white, sad. In English, we usually put the adjective before the noun.

adjective + noun
a young queen · a big empire · a white dress · a sad woman
We say "a young queen", NOT "a queen young".
The adjective does not change. It is the same for one or many:
one big palace → two big palaces (NOT "bigs palaces").
You can also use it after is/are/was/were: "The queen was young." "The palaces are big."
Careful — word order:
✔ a beautiful white dress   ✘ a dress beautiful white
In English, the describing word comes first, then the noun.

1. Put the words in order

Make a correct phrase: adjective + noun. Write the two words.

1. (queen / young) → a

2. (empire / big) → a

3. (dress / white) → a

1. a young queen

2. a big empire

3. a white dress

The adjective always comes before the noun.

2. Choose the correct sentence

One sentence is correct. Write the letter (A or B).

1. A) She wore a dress black.   B) She wore a black dress. →

2. A) Victoria was a girl young.   B) Victoria was a young girl. →

3. A) They lived in a big palace.   B) They lived in a palace big. →

1. B — She wore a black dress.

2. B — Victoria was a young girl.

3. A — They lived in a big palace.

Remember: adjective + noun.
