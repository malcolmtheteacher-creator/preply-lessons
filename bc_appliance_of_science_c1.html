Coating A thin layer of substance applied to the surface of something.
Freeze-dry To remove water from a substance by freezing it and then evaporating the ice.

How Things Are Made

Read about how four different products are manufactured:

Jam Production

Step 1: Quality Inspection — Fruit arrives at the plant and undergoes quality inspection for colour, ripeness, and taste.

Step 2: Cleaning and Crushing — The fruit is pushed through pipes where water sprays clear away surface dirt. Paddles push the fruit through small holes to remove stems and debris.

Step 3: Cooking — The fruit pulp is cooked with sugar to thicken and develop flavour.

Step 4: Bottling and Sealing — The hot jam is poured into sterilized jars and sealed.

Instant Coffee Production

Method A: Freeze-Drying

Step 1: Roast and grind coffee beans.

Step 2: Immerse the coffee in water. The water evaporates, leaving a concentrated coffee solution.

Step 3: Freeze the concentrated solution to around -40°C. Any remaining water turns into ice crystals.

Step 4: Sublimation — the ice crystals turn directly into water vapor, leaving instant coffee granules.

Chewing Gum Production

Step 1: Melt and Purify — The gum base (synthetic rubber) is melted and purified.

Step 2: Mix Additives — Sweeteners and flavours are added to the gum base in a mixer.

Step 3: Compress and Shape — A large loaf of gum passes through a series of rollers. Each pair of rollers is closer together than the previous pair, creating a long, thin ribbon.

Step 4: Dust and Cut — Overhead sprays dust the ribbon with powder (to prevent sticking), and machines cut it into individual sticks.

Passive Voice in Manufacturing

When describing manufacturing processes, we use passive voice because the focus is on what happens to the product, not who does it.

Understanding Passive Structures

Active: "Workers wash the potatoes with drum washers."

Passive: "The potatoes are washed with drum washers."

Why passive? In manufacturing descriptions, we care about what happens to the product. The person or equipment doing the action is less important.

Key Passive Structures for Manufacturing

"First, the ingredients are weighed out and mixed together."

"The mix is pasteurised, which involves heating it to a high temperature to ensure that harmful bacteria are killed off."

"The mix is then left for at least 4 hours to allow the fat to cool and form into crystals."

"The fruit is screened for large particles, which are then filtered and finally treated with chlorine."

Task Requirements

When delivering your description:

  • Speak for at least 2 minutes without stopping
  • Use at least 5 passive structures
  • Use at least 3 manufacturing vocabulary words
  • Organize your description clearly (first, then, next, finally)
  • Explain why each step is important (where possible)

Useful Language for Your Description

Sequencing: First… Then… Next… After that… Finally…

Explaining purpose: This ensures that… To remove… In order to… So that…

Passive structures: is mixed… are removed… are coated… is cooled… is packaged…
