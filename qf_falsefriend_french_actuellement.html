⏰ Actually vs Currently

B1 • French/Portuguese speakers • False friends: actuellement ≠ actually

🔍 Let's notice what we see

Look at this sentence. What's the problem?

"I am actually working at a bank."

(But they mean: at this moment, right now)

Does this mean they're telling you a surprising fact? Or does it mean they're working there NOW? What's confusing?

⚠️ The False Friend:

The word "actually" LOOKS like French "actuellement", but they mean DIFFERENT things!

In French: actuellement = RIGHT NOW, at this moment, currently

In English: actually = in fact, to tell you something surprising

In English: currently = right now, at this moment

🛠️ Understand the difference

📊 Two different words:

🇫🇷 French "actuellement"

⏰ CURRENTLY / RIGHT NOW

At this moment, at the present time

"Je travaille actuellement à la banque" = I am currently working at the bank

🇬🇧 English "actually"

💡 IN FACT / SURPRISINGLY

To reveal something unexpected or true

"I don't actually agree" = In fact, I don't agree

🎯 Key Rule:

French actuellement → English CURRENTLY / RIGHT NOW

English actually → French en fait (in fact)

When you want to say "I'm currently working" → use CURRENTLY, not actually
