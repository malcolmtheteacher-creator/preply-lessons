The Order

Lucia's First Online Order

A Funny Story

Lucia never shopped online before. Last month, she wanted a new blue jacket. She went to her computer and searched for "blue jacket". Suddenly, 500 blue jackets appeared! Which one was good? She read reviews from other customers. They said, "This jacket is very pretty! Good quality. I love it!"

Lucia chose one. She put it in her basket. She went to checkout. She paid with her credit card. Then she got confused! The website said "Delivery: 5 days". She refreshed the page 10 times. She was so excited!

Five days later, a big box arrived! She opened it carefully. Inside was... the jacket! But it was VERY big! It was not her size. But she loved the colour, so she kept it. Now she wears it every day!

Comprehension Questions

1. Had Lucia shopped online before?

No, she had never shopped online before.

2. What did she want to buy?

She wanted a new blue jacket.

3. How many jackets did she see?

She saw 500 blue jackets.

4. How long was the delivery?

The delivery took 5 days.

5. Was the jacket the right size?

No, it was very big and not her size. But she kept it because she loved the colour.

Role-Play: Customer Service

Situation: You have a problem with your online order. The item arrived, but it's the wrong size or color.

Your task: Call customer service and explain your problem. Ask for a refund or exchange.

Use these phrases:

"Hello, I have a problem with my order."

"The jacket arrived yesterday, but it's the wrong size."

"Can I return it and get a refund?"

"Can I exchange it for a different size?"

"Thank you for your help."

Shopping Safety Tips

Stay safe when shopping online:

Only use websites you know and trust
Look for the lock symbol in the address bar
Never give your full credit card details in an email
Read customer reviews before you buy
Check the delivery cost and time
Know your rights if something goes wrong
Use secure payment methods (credit card, PayPal)
