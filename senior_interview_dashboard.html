Senior Interview Toolkit

Five lessons for anyone interviewing at director, partner or vice-president level: your story, your leadership, your clients, your thinking and your close.

A senior interview is not a test of English. It is a test of judgement, delivered in English, in front of people who have heard a hundred polished answers. These five lessons prepare you for that conversation: the answers panels actually want, the vocabulary of the role, the grammar that makes you sound precise and calm, and a timed mock interview you can score against the same five criteria panels use.

Work through them in order, or choose the one you most fear. Each is a 50-minute lesson in five parts: key words, a scenario told in three parts, one grammar focus, a closer look at what the panel is really assessing, and a speaking tab with a rehearsal method, hostile questions and a full mock interview. Every lesson works for any sector, and you add your own details.

How to use the toolkit

1 · Say it first

Answer every question aloud before you reveal anything.

2 · Build it

Write your own answer with your own numbers, then compare with a model.

3 · Rehearse it

Record yourself, listen back, fix one thing, repeat at 90 and 45 seconds.

4 · Score it

Run the full mock interview and mark yourself on the five criteria.
