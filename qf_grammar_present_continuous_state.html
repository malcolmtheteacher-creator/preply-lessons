📌 The Rule

State verbs describe feelings, knowledge, and states—NOT actions. They do NOT use the continuous (-ing) form.

State verbs: know, believe, want, need, like, love, hate, understand, remember, belong, prefer, mean

I know (not "I am knowing") | She believes (not "She is believing")
