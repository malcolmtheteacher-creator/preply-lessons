🎯 Look Forward TO + -ING

Gerund after 'look forward to' | B1 Level

Let's see what you notice...

Read this sentence. What feels wrong?

I look forward to meet you tomorrow.

What's the correction?

✓ Correction

I look forward to meeting you tomorrow.

📌 The Rule

After look forward to, use the -ING form (gerund). The 'to' here is a preposition, not part of an infinitive.

Students see 'to' and assume infinitive follows. But here 'to' = preposition, so gerund (-ing) follows.

📚 The Rule (Dual Coding)

❌ WRONG (infinitive)

🚫

look forward to meet

look forward to see

look forward to hear

look forward to work

✅ CORRECT (-ing)

✅

look forward to meeting

look forward to seeing

look forward to hearing

look forward to working

💡 Tip: Same pattern: be used to + -ing, be accustomed to + -ing, object to + -ing.
