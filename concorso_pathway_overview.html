The Oral-Exam Pathway

AB24 is the competitive state exam that qualifies teachers for English posts in Italy's scuola secondaria di II grado. The whole oral is conducted in English — and this pathway walks you through three lessons that build toward a confident, top-scoring performance.

What is AB24?

Classe di concorso AB24 — Inglese (II grado)

The Italian public-sector competition for "Lingue e culture straniere negli istituti di istruzione secondaria di II grado (Inglese)". Candidates need a minimum of B2 English to be assessed, but top scorers operate comfortably at C1. Up to 30 of 100 oral points go purely on your English comprehension and production.

Oral-Exam Shape

Up to 45 minutes · Two integrated parts

A simulated lesson on a topic drawn 24 hours in advance (max ½ of the total time) plus a colloquio disciplinare on questions drawn at the start of the exam. Three commissioners score you out of 100; pass mark is 70/100.

The 100-point oral: how the three ambiti add up

The commission scores the oral out of 100 against three named ambiti. Two of them (Ambito 1 & 2) are assessed through the simulated lesson and colloquio; the third (Ambito 3) is assessed continuously throughout — anytime you open your mouth in English.

Oral score composition (100 pts total · 70 to pass)
Ambito 1 · Didactic design · 40 pts
Ambito 2 · Subject + method · 30 pts
Ambito 3 · English interaction · 30 pts

What actually happens on exam day

The two moments that matter most are the 24-hour drop and the opening ten minutes of the oral itself.

T − 24 hrs
Lesson topic is extracted The commission draws your lezione simulata topic exactly 24 hours before your scheduled slot. You have that day to plan the lesson — context, aims, staging, assessment, inclusion strategies.
T − 0
Disciplinary questions drawn at the start At the beginning of the oral itself the commission extracts your colloquio questions. You answer them without prep time — but in English.
0-22 min
Lezione simulata (max ½ the total oral) You deliver the lesson you prepared the day before — aims, staging, methodology, differentiation, assessment. The commission acts as both observer and, if asked, as your "class".
22-45 min
Colloquio disciplinare The commission pursues your drawn questions and probes the choices you made in the simulated lesson. Expect ELT theory, SLA, literature, linguistics, and pedagogy questions — all in English.
After
Verdict: 70/100 or above = pass The commission deliberates and publishes a score out of 100. 70 is the floor for passing; higher scores improve your ranking on the graduatoria.

The pass line: 70/100

The oral colloquium is graded out of 100 and passed by candidates who reach at least 70 points. The same 70-point threshold applies to the written test.

Below the line 0-69 · non idoneo
Pass 70-100 · idoneo
0-69 points Candidate is not judged idoneo. No place on the graduatoria di merito for this cycle.
70-100 points Candidate is idoneo. The exact score feeds into the ranking that determines teaching posts.

Why this pathway is built this way. Ambito 1 (didactic design, worth 40 of 100 points) is the single largest lever on your oral score — more than subject knowledge, more than English itself. That is why the pathway front-loads the AB24 masterclass and the interactive lesson builder: if your lezione simulata is sharply planned and clearly staged, you are already halfway to 70. The English oral lesson then rounds out Ambito 3 (the 30 points awarded for comprehension and production in English) so nothing gets left to chance on exam day.
*Note on the "10 points" figure for the general oral lesson. That figure refers to the English-interaction weighting in the generic Concorso Docenti oral for non-language classes. For AB24 specifically, English is the medium of the whole oral and Ambito 3 alone is worth 30 of 100.
