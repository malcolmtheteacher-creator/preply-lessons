Questions and answers

Say what is there

  • There are two chairs.
  • There isn't a TV.
  • There aren't any books.

Ask

  • Is there a cat?
  • Are there any cups?
  • How many books are there?
  • What colour is the sofa?
  • Where is the lamp?

Say where

  • on the table
  • under the table
  • next to the sofa
  • between the sofa and the TV
  • on the wall

Check

  • Sorry, can you say that again?
  • In my picture, …
  • Me too!

Key words

sofa (noun) — a long, soft seat for two or three people. Example: "We sit on the sofa and watch TV."
cushion (noun) — a small soft bag you put on a seat. Example: "There are two blue cushions on my bed."
curtain (noun) — a long piece of cloth in front of a window. Example: "Please close the curtains. It's dark outside."
shelf (noun) — a flat piece of wood on the wall for books and things. Example: "My books are on the shelf."
rug (noun) — a small carpet on the floor. Example: "The cat is sleeping on the rug."
lamp (noun) — a small light you can move. Example: "I read with a lamp next to my bed."
plant (noun) — a green living thing in a pot. Example: "There is a plant on the windowsill."
clock (noun) — a thing on the wall that shows the time. Example: "The clock says six o'clock."
next to (preposition) — very near, at the side of something. Example: "The lamp is next to the sofa."
under (preposition) — lower than something, below it. Example: "My bag is under the table."
