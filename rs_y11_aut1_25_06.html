Name the Five Pillars of Islam.

🔒Reveal answer

Answer: Shahadah (declaration of faith), Salah (prayer), Zakah (charitable giving), Sawm (fasting), Hajj (pilgrimage to Makkah).

What are the two parts of the Shahadah?

🔒Reveal answer

Answer: Part 1: 'La ilaha illallah' (There is no god but Allah) — affirms Tawhid. Part 2: 'Muhammadun rasulullah' (Muhammad is His messenger) — affirms Risalah.

Name the five daily prayers and their times.

🔒Reveal answer

Answer: Fajr (dawn), Dhuhr (midday), Asr (afternoon), Maghrib (sunset), Isha (night).

What is wudu?

🔒Reveal answer

Answer: Ritual washing before prayer: hands, mouth, nose, face, arms to elbows, wiping head, feet. Symbolises spiritual purification.

What is taqwa and which pillar develops it most directly?

🔒Reveal answer

Answer: Taqwa means God-consciousness. Sawm (fasting) develops it most directly — the Qur'an states fasting is prescribed 'that you may achieve taqwa' (2:183).

What is the nisab?

🔒Reveal answer

Answer: The minimum threshold of wealth above which zakah must be paid — roughly the value of 85g of gold or 595g of silver.

What is the difference between zakah and Khums?

🔒Reveal answer

Answer: Zakah is 2.5% of savings (Sunni and Shi'a). Khums is 20% of surplus income (Shi'a only). Khums goes partly to Sayyids and partly to the marja.

What is Laylat al-Qadr?

🔒Reveal answer

Answer: The Night of Power — when the Qur'an was first revealed. Falls in the last ten nights of Ramadan. 'Better than a thousand months' (Surah 97:3).

What is Jummah?

🔒Reveal answer

Answer: Friday congregational prayer at the mosque, replacing Dhuhr. Includes a khutbah (sermon). Obligatory for adult Muslim men.

What does 'zakah' literally mean?

🔒Reveal answer

Answer: 'To purify.' Paying zakah purifies remaining wealth by removing greed and selfishness. It also purifies the soul.

Quick Review: The Five Pillars

Shahadah — Declaration of faith: 'No god but Allah, Muhammad is His messenger.' Foundation of all practice. Affirms Tawhid and Risalah.

Salah — Five daily prayers (Fajr, Dhuhr, Asr, Maghrib, Isha). Preceded by wudu. Involves rak'ahs facing the qiblah. Jummah on Fridays.

Zakah — 2.5% of savings above nisab. Eight categories of recipients. Purifies wealth. Shi'a also pay Khums (20%).

Sawm — Fasting dawn to sunset in Ramadan. Develops taqwa. Exemptions for children, ill, pregnant. Laylat al-Qadr. Ends with Eid ul-Fitr.

Hajj — (Coming in Unit 26!) Pilgrimage to Makkah, once in a lifetime if able.

Answer: The minimum threshold of wealth above which zakah must be paid.

Extended Writing: 12-Mark Question

'The Five Pillars are equally important — no single pillar matters more than the others.' Evaluate this statement.

In your answer you should refer to Muslim teaching and belief, give developed arguments to support this statement, give developed arguments to support a different point of view, and reach a justified conclusion. [12 marks + 3 SPaG]

Guidance

For equal importance: All are commanded by Allah — who are humans to rank God's commands? Each addresses a different need: belief (Shahadah), worship (Salah), social justice (Zakah), self-discipline (Sawm), unity (Hajj). Missing any one creates a gap.

Against — some are more important: The Shahadah is the foundation — without it, nothing else counts. Salah is daily; the Prophet called it the first thing asked about on Judgement Day. Some Muslims cannot perform Hajj or are exempt from Sawm, but the Shahadah and Salah are universal.
