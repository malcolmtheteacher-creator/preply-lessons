Spark → Speech

Bring anything you want to talk about. We'll build it up with you — from a single word to two fluent minutes.

Most speaking lessons hand you a topic. This one does the opposite: you bring whatever you want to talk about — a word, a feeling, an opinion, a question — and the page builds it up with you, one small step at a time, until you can speak about it for a full two minutes.

It works at any level, because the language is yours: the same page meets an A2 learner and a C1 speaker exactly where they are. And when the words run dry, the built-in word bank lets you climb from plain to polished — from "I like it" all the way to "there's something about it I find quietly irresistible."
