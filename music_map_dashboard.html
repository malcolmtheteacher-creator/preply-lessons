The Music Map

One map, many kinds of music. Find out where each style came from, tell its story, and talk about it in English.

Every branch of the map is a lesson. Each one tells the story of a style or an idea as a 3D pop-up book and gives you plenty to say: how to describe a style, how one grew out of another, and what you really think about it.

Start with Jazz, then take the train north with the Blues. New branches open as new lessons arrive.
