Unit Recall

Quick questions from whole unit.

What is UDHR?

🔒Reveal answer

Answer: Universal Declaration of Human Rights, 1948 after WWII; 30 articles protecting all humans rights.

Name three categories?

🔒Reveal answer

Answer: Civil or political, economic or social, cultural.

What is imago Dei?

🔒Reveal answer

Answer: Christian or Jewish belief humans made in God image; inherent dignity.

What is zakah?

🔒Reveal answer

Answer: Obligatory charity: 2.5 percent wealth to poor annually; Five Pillar.

What is langar?

🔒Reveal answer

Answer: Sikh community kitchen; free food to all as equals; Guru Nanak established.

Unit Review: Key Concepts

Structural causes of poverty?

🔒Reveal answer

Answer: Unequal education, wage inequality, unemployment, discrimination, geography.

What is liberation theology?

🔒Reveal answer

Answer: Christian theology through lens of oppressed; Jesus liberator; structural justice.

What is Fair Trade?

🔒Reveal answer

Answer: Certification ensuring fair wages, safe conditions, direct relationships.

Prejudice vs discrimination?

🔒Reveal answer

Answer: Prejudice: opinion. Discrimination: unfair treatment. Attitude vs action.

Modern justice campaigns?

🔒Reveal answer

Answer: Climate justice, refugee crisis, food poverty.

Unit Summary: Human Rights & Social Justice

Key Learning

L1: Universal moral principles; UDHR 1948; religious and philosophical grounds. L2: Christianity prophetic; Islam structures justice; Sikhism rejects hierarchy; Humanism grounds in reason. L3: Structural poverty; wealth cautious but not sinful; liberation theology; fair trade. L4: Prejudice attitude, discrimination action; all religions teach against; MLK and Malala show faith motivating justice. L5: Modern campaigns; Christian Aid, Islamic Relief, Khalsa Aid; inspire but address structures.

Big Question

"What responsibilities do we have?" Powerful protect vulnerable. Resources shared justly. Discrimination violates teaching. Justice requires changing hearts AND systems. Differ on motivation, method, priority. Challenge: balance compassion for individuals with systemic demand.

Next Steps

Year 9 closes unit. Future: build institutions, detailed traditions, analyse policies, your ethical framework. Foundation learned; deepen thinking and live these principles.
