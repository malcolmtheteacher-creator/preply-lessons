🏆 Éxito ≠ Exit

B1 • Spanish speakers • False friends: éxito (success) ≠ exit (way out)

🔍 Let's notice what we see

Look at this sentence. What's the problem?

"The project was a big exit."

(The speaker wants to say the project was a success, not that it was a door!)

Does this sentence sound confusing? Why?

⚠️ The False Friend:

The Spanish word "éxito" looks like English "exit", but they mean DIFFERENT things!

In Spanish: éxito = success (tener éxito = to be successful)

In English: exit = the way out, a door or passage

In English: success = achievement/accomplishment

🛠️ Understand the difference

📊 Spanish éxito vs English exit:

🏆 Spanish: ÉXITO

  • Success/achievement
  • Tener éxito
  • A great success
  • Accomplishment

🚪 English: EXIT

  • Way out/door
  • Emergency exit
  • Exit sign
  • Leave/depart

🚨 Success vs Way Out

Key distinction:

éxito → English: "success"
Example: "Tuvo un gran éxito" = "It was a great success" ✓
NOT "It was a great exit" ✗
exit → Spanish: "salida"
Example: "Where is the exit?" = "¿Dónde está la salida?" ✓
This is about a door/way out.

Choose the correct word: success or exit. Check your answers when you're done.

1. "The startup's _____ was remarkable — they grew from zero to a billion-dollar company."
Answer: "The startup's success was remarkable — they grew from zero to a billion-dollar company."

→ This is about achievement/accomplishment.

2. "In case of fire, use the _____ on your left."
Answer: "In case of fire, use the exit on your left."

→ This is about a way out/door.

3. "The film was a commercial _____ — it earned hundreds of millions worldwide."
Answer: "The film was a commercial success — it earned hundreds of millions worldwide."

→ This is about achievement.

4. "The parking garage _____ is at the end of the ramp."
Answer: "The parking garage exit is at the end of the ramp."

→ This is about a way out/direction.
