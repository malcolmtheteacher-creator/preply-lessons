Words

bed (noun) — you sleep on it at night. Example: "The bed is in the bedroom."
wardrobe (noun) — a big, tall box for your clothes. Example: "My coat is in the wardrobe."
bath (noun) — you sit in it, in warm water, and wash. Example: "The bath is in the bathroom."
toilet (noun) — a white seat with water in it, next to the shower. Example: "The toilet is in the bathroom."
sofa (noun) — a long, soft seat for two or three people. Example: "The sofa is in the living room."
TV (noun) — you watch films and football on it. Example: "The TV is in the living room."
table (noun) — you eat your dinner at it. Example: "The table is in the kitchen."
chair (noun) — a seat for one person. Example: "The chair is in the kitchen."
fridge (noun) — a cold box for milk and food. Example: "The fridge is in the kitchen."

Rooms

  • bedroom: you sleep here.
  • bathroom: you wash here.
  • living room: you sit and watch TV here.
  • kitchen: you cook here.
