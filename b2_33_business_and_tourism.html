Phrases for Presenting Your Ideas

Use these structures to present your business proposal clearly and confidently.

Your Ideas

We think we should increase our marketing budget.
We think a greener business plan is preferable.
We plan to build an underground car park to resolve the traffic problem.
In our opinion, upwardly mobile people in their thirties should be attracted.
We believe that renewable energies reduce pollution.

Presentation Phrases

Our idea is...
It is our belief that...
The best way... is...
Our plan is...
As far as we"re concerned...

Examples

Our idea is to increase the marketing budget.

It is our belief that a greener business plan is preferable.

The best way to resolve the traffic problem is to build an underground car park.

Our plan is to attract upwardly mobile people in their thirties.

As far as we"re concerned, renewable energies reduce pollution.

Your Environment: Has your government invested in tourism? What kind? What"s your opinion on this?

Weak Forms & Stressed Words

Notice how certain words are spoken lightly (schwa ə) to sound more natural and professional.

Words with Weak Forms

to → tə (sounds like "duh")

the → thə (sounds like "duh")

a → ə (sounds like "uh")

of → əv or ə (sounds like "uh")

and → ənd or ən (sounds like "un")

Examples with Natural Pronunciation

Show Examples

Our answə tə thə problem is development.

What we intend tə do is expand.

Tə understand why we are investing...

Thə best way tə deal with the problem is...

Our idea fə development is to build...

There are ə number əv reasons for this problem.

Our aim is tə make ə profit, ənd so we propose...

Key point: Stress important words (development, expand, profit, invest) and use weak forms for function words (to, the, a, of). This sounds professional and natural.

Advantages & Disadvantages of Tourism

Before presenting your proposal, think about the broader impact.

Advantages of Tourism

Show
  • Creates local jobs and economic growth
  • Increases revenue from visitors
  • Promotes cultural exchange
  • Improves infrastructure (roads, hotels, services)
  • Can fund conservation of natural areas

Disadvantages of Tourism

Show
  • Habitat destruction and environmental damage
  • Traffic congestion and pollution
  • Cultural dilution and loss of local identity
  • Seasonal employment (not stable year-round)
  • Over-tourism can harm natural sites
  • Local housing becomes unaffordable

Reflection: Do these disadvantages apply to tourism in your country? Have you experienced them personally?

The Sandy Cove Development

You will propose a tourism development for a local seaside area called Sandy Cove.

Planning Your Proposal

Consider these key points:

  • Type of holiday you"d offer (luxury, eco, budget, adventure)
  • Type of people you"d like to attract
  • Type of accommodation you"d provide
  • How to resolve parking and traffic problems
  • Shops, facilities, and services (on the beach, in the woods, etc.)
  • How you will market your idea

Option 1: Eco-tours

Show

Focus: Ecologically friendly development offering special eco-tourist packages.

Features to consider: Ways of saving energy and resources, recycling systems, avoiding habitat destruction, renewable energy sources.

Target market: Environmentally conscious tourists looking for sustainable travel.

Option 2: Luxury Tours

Show

Focus: Five-star package for upscale, wealthy clients.

Features to consider: Premium accommodation, fine dining, exclusive services, spa facilities, VIP treatment.

Target market: Rich and glamorous tourists seeking high-end experiences.

Option 3: Budget Tours

Show

Focus: Economical package for backpackers and budget-conscious tourists.

Features to consider: Affordable accommodation (hostels, shared rooms), self-catering options, free or low-cost activities, camping facilities.

Target market: Young backpackers and travelers on tight budgets.

Practice & Role-play

Reflection Questions:

2. Have you experienced any disadvantages of tourism mentioned earlier?

3. What are the main industries being developed in your country?

4. What are the advantages and disadvantages of these industries?
