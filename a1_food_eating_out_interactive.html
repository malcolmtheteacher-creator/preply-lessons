💬 Essential Restaurant Expressions (Part 1)

🙋 I'd like... / Can I have...?

Meaning: You use these to order food politely at a restaurant.
Forms:
• "I'd like a coffee, please." (more formal)
• "Can I have a burger?" (more casual)
• "I'd like the pasta with chicken."
"I'd like" = /aɪd laɪk/
"Can I have" = /kæn aɪ hæv/
🍽️
Picture yourself: You're sitting at a table in a restaurant, looking at the menu. You see something delicious and you say, "I'd like the fish and chips, please!" The waiter writes down your order. These phrases work in ANY restaurant in the world!
💡 TIP: Using "I'd like" sounds MORE polite than "I want." At restaurants, politeness is always better!

🤔 What do you recommend?

Meaning: You ask the waiter/waitress for their opinion about what's good.
Forms:
• "What do you recommend?" (most common)
• "What's good here?" (more casual)
• "What do you suggest?"
"recommend" = /ˌrekəˈmend/
"suggest" = /səˈdʒest/
👨‍💼
Real Situation: You're in a new restaurant and you don't know what to order. You ask the waiter, "What do you recommend?" He might say, "The salmon is amazing today!" Now you know what to order!
💡 TIP: Waiters LOVE when you ask them! They know the food best and are happy to help you choose.

⚠️ I'm allergic to...

Meaning: You tell someone that your body has a bad reaction to a food. This is VERY important for your health!
Forms:
• "I'm allergic to peanuts." (most important)
• "I'm allergic to seafood."
• "I'm allergic to milk."
• "I can't eat..." (for preferences, not allergies)
"allergic" = /əˈlɜːrdʒɪk/
"allergy" = /ˈælɜːrdʒi/
🚫
Safety First! If you have a food allergy, ALWAYS tell the restaurant staff. Say clearly: "I'm allergic to..." This keeps you safe! The staff will check the ingredients carefully.
💡 IMPORTANT: An allergy is dangerous (your body can't have it). A preference is just what you like or don't like. Know the difference!

💬 Essential Restaurant Expressions (Part 2)

💳 Could we get the bill/check?

Meaning: You ask for the bill (UK) or check (USA) when you're ready to pay for your meal.
Forms:
• "Could we get the bill, please?" (UK - most polite)
• "Could we get the check?" (USA - most polite)
• "Can I have the bill?" (more casual)
• "The check, please!" (very casual)
"bill" = /bɪl/ (British English)
"check" = /tʃek/ (American English)
💰
At the End of the Meal: You finished your delicious dinner. You catch the waiter's eye and say, "Could we get the bill, please?" The waiter brings you a piece of paper showing how much you need to pay. Then you can pay and leave!
💡 USEFUL: "Bill" is British English. "Check" is American English. Both are correct! Learn both.

😋 This looks delicious!

Meaning: You compliment the food. This makes the chef and server happy!
Forms:
• "This looks delicious!" (before you eat - about appearance)
• "This tastes delicious!" (after you eat - about flavor)
• "This is delicious!" (general compliment)
• "Mmm, this is amazing!" (informal)
"delicious" = /dɪˈlɪʃəs/
"tastes" = /teɪsts/
👌
Making People Happy: When the waiter brings your food and it looks beautiful, say "This looks delicious!" When you taste it, say "This tastes delicious!" This makes people feel good about their work.
💡 CULTURE TIP: In many countries, complimenting the food and service is very appreciated. It's polite and makes everyone happy!

✅ Summary: 5 Key Expressions You Learned

  1. I'd like... / Can I have...? - To order food
  2. What do you recommend? - To ask for the waiter's suggestion
  3. I'm allergic to... - To tell about food allergies (IMPORTANT!)
  4. Could we get the bill/check? - To ask for the bill at the end
  5. This looks/tastes delicious! - To compliment the food

⏱️ Deep Dive: Speaking Activity (3 Minutes)

3:00

🎯 Your Ideal Meal

Imagine you can eat ANYTHING you want for one special meal. Describe your ideal meal in detail:

  • What's the main dish? (chicken, fish, vegetables, etc.)
  • What sides do you want?
  • What drink would you have?
  • What's for dessert?
  • Where would you eat this meal? (restaurant, home, beach, mountains?)
  • Who would you eat with?

💭 A Food Memory

Tell a story about a special meal or food experience you remember:

  • When was it? (last week, childhood, holiday?)
  • What did you eat?
  • Who was with you?
  • Why was it special or memorable?
  • How did it make you feel?
🎤 How to Do This Activity

1. Choose ONE prompt above (either "Your Ideal Meal" or "A Food Memory")
2. Click "Start Timer" when you're ready
3. Talk OUT LOUD - don't write, SPEAK! Use your voice and the new expressions you learned
4. Keep talking until the timer runs out
5. Don't stop! Even if you make mistakes, KEEP SPEAKING. Use gestures, sounds, anything!

⭐ Challenge: Try to speak for the FULL 3 minutes without stopping!

💡 Why This Matters: Speaking is the most important skill for real communication! The more you speak, even when you make mistakes, the faster you improve. This timer helps you practice sustained speaking, which is exactly what you need for real conversations!
