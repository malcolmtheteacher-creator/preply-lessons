Key words

tone (noun) — the way something sounds, which shows how you feel about the listener. Example: "It wasn't what she said, it was her tone."
blunt (adjective) — saying exactly what you think, without caring how it sounds. Example: "He was so blunt that his friend walked off."
tactful (adjective) — careful to say things in a way that does not upset people. Example: "Be tactful when you tell Grandad you can't come."
come across (phrasal verb) — seem a certain way to the people listening. Example: "My email came across as bossy, but I didn't mean it."
bossy (adjective) — always telling other people what to do. Example: "Stop being so bossy with your little sister."
persuade (verb) — make somebody agree to do something by giving good reasons. Example: "I persuaded my parents to let me stay up later."
considerate (adjective) — thinking about other people's feelings and needs. Example: "It was considerate of you to knock first."
sincere (adjective) — really meaning what you say. Example: "Her apology sounded sincere, so I forgave her."
offend (verb) — make somebody upset or annoyed, often without meaning to. Example: "I didn't want to offend her, so I chose my words carefully."
compromise (noun) — an agreement where each side gets part of what it wants. Example: "Ten o'clock at weekends was a good compromise."
