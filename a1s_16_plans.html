The big weekend 📖

Read it slowly. Say the words out loud.

It is Friday. Mia's sister Ana is here! They sit at a little table in Rosa's café. ☕

Mia opens a big map on the table. "Look, Ana. This is my town. And this is our plan for the weekend!"

"Tomorrow morning, we are going to ride bikes," says Mia. "Tom has two bikes for us."

"Then we are going to see the castle on the hill. It is very, very old!"

Ana claps her hands. "I love it! And food? I am always hungry!" 😄

"We are going to have a picnic in the park," says Mia. "Bread, cheese and apples."

Rosa looks at the map too. "A picnic? Then I am going to make a big cake for you!" They all laugh.

Saturday morning. The sun is out. Mia and Ana ride their bikes down a little road. The big weekend starts now! 🌞

Notice: Mia says "We are going to ride bikes" and "We are going to have a picnic". We say going to for our plans. Learn the words next.

The pattern: going to 🧩

For a plan, we say am / is / are + going to + an action word.

I am going to ride a bike.
We are going to see the castle.
She is going to make a cake.
Question: Are you going to come? — Yes, I am!
No plan? I am not going to sleep all weekend! 😄
Picture it: a plan in your head 💭 → say going to: I'm going to ride 🚴 · we're going to eat 🧺 · she's going to bake 🎂.
