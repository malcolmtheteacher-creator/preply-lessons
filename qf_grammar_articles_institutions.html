🏛️ Articles with Organisations

When to use THE, A/AN, or nothing | B2 Level

Let's see what you notice...

Read this sentence. What's missing?

UN has called for immediate international action on climate change.

What needs to be added and why?

✓ Correction

The UN has called for immediate international action on climate change.

📌 The Rule

Use the before named international organisations and unique institutions:

the UN · the WHO · the EU · the IMF · the Red Cross · the BBC · the government

These are unique, specific bodies — "the" signals we are talking about one known, identifiable institution.

📚 The Rule (Dual Coding)

✅ USE "the"

🏛️

the UN, the WHO

the EU, the IMF

the Red Cross

the government

the United States

→ Named, unique, specific institutions

✅ USE "a / an"

🔎

a humanitarian organisation

a democratic government

an international body

a small NGO

an independent charity

→ One of many — not a specific named body

💡 Note for speakers of Russian, Polish, Finnish and others: Languages without articles make this tricky — there is no equivalent word in those languages. In English, "the" before an institution signals "you know which one I mean." Treat these as fixed phrases to memorise.
