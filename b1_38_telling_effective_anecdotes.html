Gradable vs Non-Gradable Adjectives

When telling anecdotes, we use adjectives to make our stories more interesting. But some adjectives work differently:

The Rule

Gradable adjectives can be made stronger with "very" (funny, scared, embarrassed).

Non-gradable adjectives are already extreme, so we use "absolutely" instead (hilarious, terrified, mortified).

Practice: Choose the Correct Word

1. The film I watched last night was absolutely _ / hilarious

Show Answer
Answer: hilarious — "Hilarious" is non-gradable (already very funny), so we use "absolutely."

2. I am always very / absolutely scared when I stay home alone at night

Show Answer
Answer: very — "Scared" is gradable (you can be a little scared or very scared), so "very" is correct.

3. She must have been very / mortified when she realized she had forgotten his name

Show Answer
Answer: absolutely — "Mortified" is non-gradable (it means extremely embarrassed), so we use "absolutely."

4. They were very / absolutely stunned when they discovered their house was burgled

Show Answer
Answer: absolutely — "Stunned" is non-gradable (you are either stunned or not), so "absolutely" is correct.

Tell Your Anecdote

Now it is your turn to tell your own anecdote. An anecdote is a short story about something that happened to you. Follow these steps:

Time remaining: 5:00

Step 1: Plan Your Story (2 minutes)

Think of a funny, embarrassing, scary, or surprising situation you have been in. Make notes about:

Where were you?

Why were you there?

What happened?

How did you feel about it at the time?

How did it end?

Step 2: Tell Your Story

Now tell your story to a partner or imagine telling it to someone. Try to:

  • Use adjectives like "very," "absolutely," "mortified," or "hilarious" to describe how you felt
  • Include the 5 parts from Step 1 in order (where, why, what, how you felt, how it ended)
  • Make it clear and interesting so your listener understands
  • Use clear pronunciation and natural pauses

Step 3: Reflect on Your Partner's Story

Recap your partner's story, checking any information you are not sure about

Useful Phrases for Storytelling

Starting your story: "This happened to me last month..." / "I'll never forget the day when..."

Adding details: "It was absolutely hilarious because..." / "I was very embarrassed when..."

Finishing: "In the end..." / "The funny thing is..."

Asking for response: "Do you have a similar story?" / "Have you ever experienced something like that?"
