Event 1
Fall of Rome
476 AD
Click to reveal details...

What Happened: Last Western Roman Emperor (Romulus Augustulus) deposed by Germanic tribes

Significance: End of classical antiquity. Marked beginning of Middle Ages.

Consequences: Loss of Roman law, infrastructure, literacy. Europe fragmented into kingdoms.

Memory Anchor: ROME'S FINAL EMPEROR FALLS (476 AD) - Picture the eternal city crumbling. Barbarian tribes (Visigoths, Vandals) sacking Rome. Aqueducts breaking. Books burning. Emperor deposed. 1,000-year Roman civilization ends. 476 = "4-7-6" = encode as Major System.
Event 2
Norman Conquest (Battle of Hastings)
1066 AD
Click to reveal details...

What Happened: William the Conqueror (Duke of Normandy) defeated English King Harold II

Significance: Norman/French rule imposed on England. Changed English language, culture, law.

Consequences: English became hybrid of Norman French and Anglo-Saxon. Feudal system imposed.

Memory Anchor: WILLIAM DEFEATS HAROLD AT HASTINGS (1066) - Picture William of Normandy on horseback leading Normans across English Channel. King Harold takes an arrow to the eye. Norman victory. French language infuses English forever. 1066 = memorable date!
Event 3
Magna Carta (Great Charter)
1215 AD
Click to reveal details...

What Happened: English barons forced King John to sign document limiting royal power

Significance: First written limit on monarch's power. Foundation of constitutional law.

Principles: Rule of law, due process, no taxation without representation

Memory Anchor: BARONS FORCE KING JOHN'S HAND (1215) - Picture angry English nobles with swords surrounding weak King John. He signs the Magna Carta on parchment. His power is limited by law. Birth of constitutional government. 1215 = "1-2-1-5" = Major System.
Event 4
Columbus Reaches Americas
1492 AD
Click to reveal details...

What Happened: Christopher Columbus sailed across Atlantic, reaching Caribbean islands

Significance: Initiated European colonization of Americas. Triggered the Columbian Exchange.

Consequences: Indigenous civilizations decimated. New World resources flowed to Europe. Global trade networks formed.

Memory Anchor: COLUMBUS SAILS BLUE OCEAN (1492) - Picture Columbus on the Santa Maria sailing into unknown ocean. Three ships on horizon. Sailors see land. "In 1492, Columbus sailed the ocean blue." Native Americans welcome him. Tragic beginning of colonization. 1492 = memorable year!
Event 5
American Independence
1776 AD (July 4)
Click to reveal details...

What Happened: American colonies declared independence from British rule with Declaration of Independence

Significance: Birth of American nation. Revolutionary idea: government by consent of the governed.

Consequences: Inspired revolutions worldwide. Created model for democratic government.

Memory Anchor: LIBERTY BELL RINGS IN PHILADELPHIA (1776) - Picture the Liberty Bell ringing. Declaration read publicly. "We hold these truths to be self-evident, that all men are created equal..." Colonists declare freedom from King George. Revolutionary idea: democracy. 1776 = America born!
Event 6
French Revolution (Storming of Bastille)
1789 AD (July 14)
Click to reveal details...

What Happened: Parisian mob stormed Bastille prison, demanding bread and freedom from monarchy

Significance: Overthrow of absolute monarchy. Declaration of Rights of Man and Citizen.

Consequences: Aristocracy abolished. Radical decade of revolution, terror, war. Reshaped European politics.

Memory Anchor: BASTILLE STORMED BY HUNGRY PARISIAN MOB (1789) - Picture poor Parisians storming fortress prison. Bastille falls. King Louis XVI captured. Aristocrats flee. Revolution spirals into Reign of Terror. "Liberty, Equality, Fraternity." 1789 = France transformed!
Event 7
World War I
1914-1918 AD
Click to reveal details...

What Happened: Global conflict started by assassination of Archduke Franz Ferdinand. Trenches, poison gas, millions died.

Significance: First modern industrial war. Redrew map of Europe. Ended empires (Russian, Ottoman, Austro-Hungarian).

Consequences: 20 million deaths. League of Nations formed. Set stage for WWII.

Memory Anchor: TRENCHES AND POISON GAS SLAUGHTER (1914-1918) - Picture soldiers in muddy trenches. Poison gas clouds. Machine guns mowing down thousands. No one winning, just attrition. Map of Europe redrawn in blood. 1914 = Western civilization nearly destroyed itself.
Event 8
World War II
1939-1945 AD
Click to reveal details...

What Happened: Nazi Germany under Hitler invaded Poland, triggering global war. Holocaust. Atomic bombs.

Significance: Deadliest war ever (70+ million deaths). Holocaust industrialized genocide. Atomic weapons changed warfare forever.

Consequences: United Nations formed. Cold War began. Nuclear age dawned. Europe divided.

Memory Anchor: HITLER'S SWASTIKA DARKENS WORLD (1939-1945) - Picture Hitler's rise. Nazis invade. Holocaust horrors. Concentration camps. D-Day beaches. Atomic bombs on Hiroshima and Nagasaki. 70 million dead. Worst moment in human history. 1939-1945 = humanity's darkness revealed.
Event 9
Moon Landing (Apollo 11)
1969 AD (July 20-21)
Click to reveal details...

What Happened: Apollo 11 astronauts Neil Armstrong and Buzz Aldrin landed on the Moon

Significance: First humans on another celestial body. Peak of Space Race. Demonstrated human capability.

Famous Words: "One small step for man, one giant leap for mankind" (Neil Armstrong)

Memory Anchor: ARMSTRONG STEPS ON MOON (1969) - Picture the lunar lander touching down. Dust blowing. Neil Armstrong descending ladder. Boot touching lunar surface. Footprint in moon dust. Whole world watching. "One small step..." 1969 = humanity reaches beyond Earth!
Event 10
Fall of the Berlin Wall
1989 AD (November 9)
Click to reveal details...

What Happened: Berlin Wall separating East and West Berlin demolished by jubilant crowds

Significance: Symbol of Cold War's end. East and West Germany reunified. Soviet Union's collapse imminent.

Consequences: End of communism in Eastern Europe. New world order began. Age of globalization.

Memory Anchor: HAMMERS SMASH BERLIN WALL (1989) - Picture Germans on both sides hammering the wall to rubble. Concrete chunks flying. Families reuniting. Champagne celebrations. "Mr. Gorbachev, tear down this wall!" (Reagan 1987 finally answered). 1989 = Cold War ends!

Elaboration: How History Unites All Dewey Classes

Each historical event depended on knowledge from Dewey 0-800. Here's the web of connections:

Classical to Medieval (476-1215)

Fall of Rome (476) - Marked end of classical learning. Dark Ages = loss of technology (Roman engineering), science (Greek philosophy), and literature (manuscripts burned). Set back human progress centuries.

Middle Ages to Renaissance (1066-1492)

Norman Conquest (1066) - Norman culture infused French-language knowledge into England. Enabled cross-cultural learning.
Magna Carta (1215) - Established that laws (not kings) rule. Foundation for democratic philosophy.
Columbus (1492) - Navigation technology (astronomy, math, ships) enabled exploration. New resources fueled Renaissance learning.

Modern Era (1776-1789)

American Independence (1776) - Enlightenment philosophy (Locke, Rousseau) inspired "unalienable rights." Democratic government based on intellectual ideas.
French Revolution (1789) - Radical application of philosophy. "Liberty, Equality, Fraternity" put Enlightenment ideas into violent practice.

Industrial & World Wars (1914-1945)

World War I (1914-1918) - Industrial technology (tanks, poison gas, artillery) industrialized death. Science weaponized at unprecedented scale.
World War II (1939-1945) - Technology (radar, code-breaking, atomic physics) determined victory. Science weaponized to ultimate degree. Holocaust showed technology could enable genocide.

Space Age & Cold War (1969-1989)

Moon Landing (1969) - Apogee of science and technology. Rocket science (Newton, Einstein), computing, engineering all converged. Humans reached beyond Earth.
Berlin Wall Fall (1989) - Collapse of ideological empire. Marked triumph of democratic/capitalist ideas over communism. History's end-of-ideology moment (Fukuyama).

The Grand Synthesis

Why does history matter? History shows how science (0), technology (6), arts (7), and literature (8) shape events (9). Columbus needed maritime technology. Enlightenment philosophers wrote ideas. Industrial Revolution applied science to production. World Wars weaponized technology. Moon landing combined all knowledge. Berlin Wall fall proved ideas matter. When you memorize these 10 events, you're memorizing how human knowledge and choices shape civilization. You're memorizing why the entire Dewey Decimal System exists—to organize humanity's accumulated wisdom.
