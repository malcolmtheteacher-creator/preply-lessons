How This Course Works

Each lesson follows a six-part structure: Think (retrieval & activation) → Encounter (new content & ideas) → Explore (guided investigation & analysis) → Apply (student production & creativity) → Evaluate (critical thinking & debate) → Review (retrieval + metacognition). Content returns at +3, +7, and +14 lesson intervals through spaced repetition, ensuring deep mastery of concepts, key terms, scripture passages, and evaluative frameworks across the seven traditions.

Exam Specifications & Alignments

GCSE: AQA Religious Studies A (8062) — Christianity & Islam as core content, with thematic units woven throughout.

A-Level: AQA Religious Studies (7062) — Philosophy of Religion, Ethics, Christianity, plus dialogues with other worldviews and contemporary issues.
