Idioms: Nelson Mandela

These sentences are taken from a feature article about Nelson Mandela. Click each card to reveal the meaning of the highlighted expression.

"A constant thorn in the side of the authorities, he was eventually imprisoned in 1962 due to his activism and spent the following 28 years in prison."
Meaning: Someone who repeatedly annoys or causes problems for those in a position of power. A "thorn in the side" is something that constantly irritates.
"A moral force to be reckoned with, yes, but also a man of great courage, empathy, dignity and grace..."
Meaning: A person whose ethical influence and principles deserve to be respected and taken seriously. Someone who cannot be ignored.
"...a man of great courage, empathy, dignity and grace, who stood up for what he believed – and won."
Meaning: To defend or support something that is being criticised or attacked. To publicly express your beliefs despite opposition.

Discussion:

  • Can you think of anyone else who was "a thorn in the side" of authorities?
  • Who in your country is "a moral force to be reckoned with"?
  • Have you ever had to "stand up for what you believed"?

Expressions

1 Jenny is such a vivacious and energetic persona live wire – she keeps us all entertained with her jokes and stories.
2 I had no idea he used to be a well-known sportsperson in his youth. He can be hiding his skillsa dark horse at times!
3 Even when she was a child, directors could see she had the qualities and potential that could be developedhad the makings of a great star.
4 In total he won 3 Olympic medals – truly an athlete unequalled and better than anyone elsein a class of his own.

Planning Your Talk

📝 Your Task

You are going to present a short talk about a famous person you admire. First, brainstorm ideas and make notes.

Gather information about:

📅
Biographical Details
Year/place of birth, family background, education, career
👤
Appearance
How do they look? Any distinctive features?
💭
Personality
Use the idioms and vocabulary from today!
🏆
Achievements
What have they accomplished? Awards? Impact?
❤️
Why You Admire Them
Personal connection, inspiration, values

Suggested Talk Structure:

Opening: Introduce who they are – grab attention!
Background: Key biographical details
Personality & Achievements: What are they like? What have they done?
Conclusion: Why you admire this person

Discussion with partner:

  • Share your information with a partner
  • Can they add anything to your notes?
  • Which information is most interesting?
