A Wedding Invitation

A good friend calls you with big news: they are getting married! Now you say congratulations, say yes, and ask a few simple questions.

This is the easier version. Want the harder one? → Full B2/C1 version →

The Scene 🎬

It is evening. Your phone rings. It is a good friend — someone you have known for a long time. You can hear in their voice that they are very happy. They have good news!

They are getting married. And they want you to come to the wedding. So first you say congratulations. Then you say yes. Then you ask a few small questions: Can I bring my partner? What should I wear? Is there a hotel near the wedding?

And sometimes the answer is no — maybe you are busy that day, or it is very far. That is OK too. You can still say no in a kind and warm way. Your friend will understand.

Warm up — talk for one minute

  1. Do you like weddings? What is your favourite part — the food, the music, or the people?
  2. A friend invites you to a party, but you are busy. Do you say yes, or do you say no? How do you say no nicely?

Who's who

You
The Good Friend 💌

Happy and warm. You want to say congratulations, say yes to the wedding, and ask a few simple questions.

The other person
The Friend Getting Married 💍

Very excited! They call you with the big news. They really want you to come to their wedding.

First: read the conversation in the next tab. Look at the warm words: congratulations, I am so happy for you, I would love to come. These words make your friend feel good.

The Conversation 💬

Read it out loud, one person each — then change. Listen for the warm, happy words.

FriendHi! I have big news. I have to tell you first — I am getting married!
YouWow! Congratulations! I am so happy for you! That is wonderful news.
FriendThank you! The wedding is in September. And I really, really want you to be there.
YouYes! I would love to come. I will be there — of course!
FriendThat makes me so happy. It is at a big garden, just outside the city.
YouIt sounds lovely. Can I ask a question? Can I bring my partner?
FriendOf course! Your partner is very welcome. Please bring them.
YouThank you. And what should I wear? Is it smart, or is it relaxed?
FriendNot too smart. It is in a garden, so wear nice, comfortable shoes. No high heels!
YouGood idea, thank you. One more question — is there a hotel near the garden?
FriendYes, there is a small hotel, five minutes away. I will send you the name.
YouPerfect. Thank you so much! I am so happy for you. See you in September!
FriendThank you! I will send you all the details tonight. Bye for now!

Useful Phrases 🧰

Eight phrases for happy news. Say each one out loud BEFORE you look at the answer. Guessing first helps you remember.

Say it with a big smile. Weddings are happy! Use a warm, happy voice. You do not need long sentences — short and kind is perfect. And it is OK to ask questions; your friend wants to help you.

🎉 Happy news

Phrase 1

Your friend says they are getting married. You want to show them you feel their joy. Complete it: "Congratulations! I am so ___ for you!"

"Congratulations! I am so happy for you!"
"I am so happy for you" tells your friend you feel their joy. Use it for any good news: a new baby, a new job, a new home.
Phrase 2

Your friend has just told you their big news. Which answer sounds warm and excited?

A. "Oh, ok."
B. "That is wonderful news!"
C. "When is it?"
B — "That is wonderful news!"
Big news needs a big, warm answer. A is cold, and C jumps to questions too fast. Say the happy words first, then ask your questions later.

💌 Saying yes

Phrase 3

Your friend asks you to come to the wedding. You really want to go. Complete it: "Yes! I would ___ to come."

"Yes! I would love to come."
"I would love to…" is a warm, happy way to say yes. Use it for any invitation: "I would love to come to dinner", "I would love to help".
Phrase 4

You want to promise your friend that you will go. Put the words in order:

beI willthere
"I will be there."
Short, strong, and warm. It tells your friend: you can count on me. You can add "of course!" to make it even warmer: "I will be there, of course!"

❓ Asking simple questions

Phrase 5

You want to take your partner to the wedding, but first you must ask. Complete it: "Can I ___ my partner?"

"Can I bring my partner?"
"Can I bring…?" is a polite way to ask. You ask first — you do not just say "My partner is coming". You can also ask "Can I bring a friend?"
Phrase 6

You do not know what clothes to put on. You want to ask your friend. Put the words in order:

should IWhatwear?
"What should I wear?"
"What should I…?" asks for good advice. It is useful everywhere: "What should I bring?", "What should I do?", "What should I say?"

🙏 Saying no in a kind way

Phrase 7

Sometimes you can't go. You must say no — but in a kind, warm way. Which one is kind?

A. "No, I can't."
B. "I am so sorry, but I can't come. I am busy that day."
C. "I'm not coming."
B — "I am so sorry, but I can't come. I am busy that day."
Say "I am so sorry" first — the sorry comes before the no. Then give a short, real reason. A and C are cold and can hurt your friend's feelings.
Phrase 8

You can't go, but you still love your friend. You want to end the call in a warm way. What can you say? (Say it out loud first.)

"I will be thinking of you! Send me lots of photos!"
Even when you say no, you can stay warm. "I will be thinking of you" and "send me photos" tell your friend you still care about their big day.

🔑 Make them yours

These phrases work for any happy news — a wedding, a party, a new baby. Write your own sentences. Say each one out loud first.

"I am so happy for ___!" — Think of a person with good news. Who is it?

"I would love to ___." — What is an invitation you would say yes to? (dinner, a trip, a party…)

"Can I bring ___?" — Who or what would you like to bring?

Quick test: close your eyes and say the whole call out loud — say congratulations → say yes → ask your three questions → say a happy goodbye. When it is easy, go to Your Turn.

Your Turn 🎭

Now it is your phone ringing. Say your answers out loud FIRST, then write them. Use the timer if you like.

Round 1 — you are the good friend 💌

Your friend calls with big news — you answer. Be warm: say congratulations, say yes, and ask nicely.

FriendHi! I have to tell you first — I am getting married!
FriendThe wedding is in September. I really want you to be there!
FriendOh, I am so glad! It is at a big garden, near the city.
FriendOf course, bring them! They are very welcome.
FriendWear something relaxed, and there is a small hotel very near. I will send you everything!

Round 2 — now YOU are getting married 💍

Change places. You are getting married! You call your friend to tell them and to invite them. Be happy and kind.

FriendHello! It's late — is everything OK?
FriendOh, congratulations! That is wonderful! When is it?
FriendI would love to come! Can I bring my partner?
FriendLovely! And what should I wear?
FriendThank you for asking me. It means a lot.
The Twist

💔 You can't go

One more time — a harder one. Your good friend calls with the happy news… but you can't go. Maybe you are away that weekend, or it is very far. First, be happy for them and say congratulations. Then say sorry before you say no: "I am so sorry, but I can't come." Give a short, kind reason. And end in a warm way — say you will be thinking of them, and ask for lots of photos. Your friend must feel loved, not sad. Start the timer and speak!

Before you finish…

Think about what you said. Can you say "yes" to these?

  • I said congratulations with a happy voice.
  • I said yes clearly, using "I would love to…".
  • I asked my questions in a polite, gentle way.
  • When I said no, I said "sorry" first and gave a kind reason.
  • I stayed warm and friendly the whole time.

Can't say yes to one? That is your practice — try Round 1 again.

The Echo 🕰️

In 1840, a young queen got married. Her name was Queen Victoria. On her wedding day, she wore a simple white dress. This was new and different — before this, people often wore bright colours to get married.

People loved the picture of the queen in her white dress. Soon, women all over the world wanted a white dress too. Little by little, "the white wedding" became normal.

So today, when your friend plans their wedding, some of the old ideas come from one queen, a long time ago. You only have to choose your shoes!
