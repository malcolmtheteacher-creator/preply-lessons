Life in English

The conversations that actually matter — with the people you love, the people you're stuck with, and the ones you'll only have once.

How it works 🌍

Each lesson drops you into one real conversation — asking your father to stop driving, telling a friend the truth, saying the thing you've been not-saying for months — and hands you the ten phrases that make it possible. You read it, learn the phrases, then play both sides yourself.

🎬 The SceneWho you are, what you want, and what's at stake.
🧰 The PhrasesTen real phrases — guess first, then check.
🎭 Your TurnPlay it out, both sides, against the clock.
🔭 The Bigger PictureA thought or two on why this one is hard.
