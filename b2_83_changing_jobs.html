Changing Jobs

Practise talking about changing jobs and giving advice

Learning Objectives

  • Practise talking about changing jobs
  • Learn expressions for asking for advice about work
  • Learn expressions for giving advice about jobs

Let's Discuss

Why do people change jobs in your country?

Think about reasons people might leave their job. What are the most common reasons you can think of?

Have you ever thought about changing jobs or careers?

What would make you consider a change? What would you want in a new job?

Key Phrases for Advice

Asking for Advice

I'm thinking of... (doing something)
What do you think I should do?
Do you think that's a good idea?

Responding to Advice

I think that's good advice.
It sounds very difficult.
I think you should... (give suggestion)

Practice Scenarios

Use the phrases above to discuss these situations:

Situation 1: Work-Life Balance

You don't have time for exercise because of work.

Situation 2: Noisy Workplace

Your coworkers are too noisy; you can't concentrate.

Situation 3: Equipment Issues

You need a new car for your job, but you can't afford one.

Situation 4: Job Satisfaction

You're bored with your routine; it's the same every day.

Situation 5: Time Off

You want a holiday but haven't had time off in months.

Situation 6: Technical Problems

Your laptop doesn't work well; you can't do your job properly.

Role Play Practice

Use the phrases you've learned to discuss your reasons for wanting to change jobs.

Your Reasons to Change Jobs

Choose one or more of these reasons and ask for advice about changing jobs:

  • The work is too difficult
  • There's too much stress
  • Your colleagues are unfriendly
  • You want to work abroad

How to Have This Conversation

  1. Start by saying what you're thinking: "I'm thinking of..."
  2. Explain your reason: "The work is..." or "My colleagues are..."
  3. Ask for advice: "What do you think I should do?" or "Do you think that's a good idea?"
  4. Listen to the response and discuss
  5. Respond to the advice: "I think that's good advice" or "It sounds very difficult"

Discussion Questions

After your conversation, think about:

  • Is changing jobs the best solution?
  • What other options could you try first?
  • What advice would you give to a friend in this situation?
