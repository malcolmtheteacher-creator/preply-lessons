Asking for and Giving Advice

Asking for Advice

  • I'm thinking of... (changing jobs, moving abroad)
  • What do you think I should do?
  • Do you have any suggestions?
  • What would you do in my situation?

Giving Advice

  • I think you should... (be more flexible, seek new challenges)
  • You might want to consider... (learning new skills, taking a course)
  • Have you thought about... (a different industry, starting your own business)
  • It might be worth... (updating your CV, networking)

Responding to Advice

  • That's good advice.
  • That sounds sensible.
  • I hadn't thought of that.
  • It sounds very difficult.

Discussion Questions

Think about these questions and share your thoughts.

1. Why do people change jobs?

Consider factors such as salary, career development, work environment, and personal satisfaction.

2. What are the advantages of changing jobs?

Think about new skills, better working conditions, higher pay, or career growth.

3. What are the challenges of changing jobs?

Consider starting over, losing seniority, adapting to new teams, or uncertainty.

4. Have you ever considered changing careers?

If yes, what would make you consider a change? If no, why are you satisfied with your current path?

5. What makes an ideal job for you?

Describe the elements that matter most: flexibility, salary, meaningful work, team, location, etc.

Scenario A: Work is Too Difficult

You want to change your job because:

  • Your work is too difficult
  • You are under a lot of stress and pressure
  • Your colleagues are unfriendly
  • You would like to work in another country

Scenario B: Work is Not Interesting

You want to change your job because:

  • Your work is not interesting
  • Your work is too easy
  • You are not learning anything new
  • You have been doing the same thing for 10 years

Your task: Listen to the other person's situation. Give them advice using the phrases you learned. Ask questions and show interest.
