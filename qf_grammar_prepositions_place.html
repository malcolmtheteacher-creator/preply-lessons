Let's see what you notice...

Read this sentence. What feels wrong?

I am waiting in the bus stop.

What's the correction?

✓ Correction

I am waiting at the bus stop.

📌 The Rule

AT = specific point or location (at the bus stop, at home, at school, at work)

IN = inside an enclosed space (in the room, in the car, in the city)

ON = on a surface (on the table, on the wall, on the bus)
