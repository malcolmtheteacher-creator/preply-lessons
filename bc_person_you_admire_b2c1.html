The "But" Factor

Master six different ways to express contrast and complexity using contrastive linkers.

Pattern Hunt

Read these examples. What structures connect contrasting ideas? How many different ways can you say "but"?

1. and yet
He is not from a rich family and yet he became prime minister.
2. not because... but because
He got into university not because daddy was rich but because he studied hard.
3. Even though (+ clause)
Even though he earns $25,000 a month as President, he only keeps $1000.
4. Despite + -ing (+ noun/gerund)
He still drives his little Volkswagen car despite being President.
5. whereas
He has remained true to his values whereas other politicians get involved in corruption.
6. Despite + -ing (+ noun/gerund)
Despite working in difficult conditions, he takes excellent photos.

Grammatical Patterns

Notice the difference:

Even though + full clause: Even though she had every advantage, she worked harder than anyone.
Despite + -ing (gerund/noun): Despite having every advantage, she worked harder than anyone.

Same meaning, different structure.

Transformation Task

Rewrite each sentence using a different contrastive linker. Keep the same meaning:

  1. He comes from a poor background. He is now very successful.
    Try: "Despite coming from a poor background, he is now very successful." Or: "He comes from a poor background and yet he is now very successful."
  2. She had natural talent. She still practised every day.
    Try: "Despite having natural talent, she still practised every day." Or: "Even though she had natural talent, she still practised every day."
  3. Other athletes take shortcuts. He has stayed true to his principles.
    Try: "He has stayed true to his principles whereas other athletes take shortcuts."

Someone I Admire

Present information about someone you admire using collocations and contrastive structures.

Your Mini-Presentation

Prepare and deliver a talk about someone you admire. Address all four points:

  1. Who is this person? (Name, profession, background)
  2. What qualities do they have? (Use collocations: seriously, insanely, bitterly, etc.)
  3. How do they compare to others? (Use contrastive linkers: and yet, despite, whereas, etc.)
  4. How have they influenced or impressed you? (Personal connection or impact)

Structure Your Talk

Example opening: "The person I admire most is my grandmother. She grew up in a village where girls didn't go to university. And yet, she became a teacher. Despite having very little money, she was entirely convinced that education could change lives..."

Contrastive Linkers

Name 4 different contrastive linkers and use each in a sentence.
1. and yet: He had no formal training and yet he became world-famous.
2. Despite + -ing: Despite facing opposition, she pursued her dreams.
3. Even though: Even though she was tired, she kept going.
4. whereas: She remained humble whereas others became arrogant.
What's the grammatical difference between "despite" and "even though"?
Despite + noun/gerund (-ing): "Despite working hard, she failed the exam."
Even though + full clause (subject + verb): "Even though she worked hard, she failed the exam."
They mean the same thing but have different structures.
