Understanding Human Rights

What Are Human Rights?

Universal moral principles protecting dignity and freedom of all people. Key: universal, equal, inalienable, indivisible. Categories: civil/political (voting, speech), economic/social (education, healthcare), cultural (religion, participation).

History

1689 English Bill of Rights; 1776 American Declaration; 1789 French Declaration; 1948 UDHR after WWII; 1966 International Covenant. UDHR created to prevent future abuses.

Religious Foundations

Christianity/Judaism: Imago Dei (image of God). Islam: khalifah (trustee). Sikhism: Ik Onkar (one God, spiritual equality). Buddhism: Buddha-nature in all. Humanism: rational capacity. All teach fundamental human worth.

Universal vs Cultural Relativism

Universal: rights reflect dignity transcending culture; core rights (no torture, slavery) are non-negotiable. Culturally relative: rights vary by culture; collective wellbeing may be valued over individual rights. Challenge: respect diversity while protecting people.
