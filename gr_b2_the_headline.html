The game

A real news headline appears. It sounds insane — but it's 100% true. Before you see the story, you and your partner speculate: what on earth happened? Then reveal the truth. It's almost always stranger than what you imagined.

How it works: Read the headline together. Both of you speculate for 2 minutes — what's the story behind this? Build a theory. Then tap to reveal the real story. Discuss: were you close? Which version is better?

Useful language

Speculating: "It could be that...", "My theory is...", "What if it turns out that...", "I bet what happened is...", "There's no way this is about..."
Reacting: "That's even crazier than I thought!", "I was way off.", "How is that even possible?", "That makes no sense.", "I actually guessed something similar!"

Reflection

Which story surprised you the most? Was reality stranger than your theory, or were you close?
Would any of these stories make the news in your country? What kinds of stories go viral where you're from?
Do you trust headlines? How often do you read past the headline? Have you ever been completely misled by one?
Write your own headline. Think of something real that happened to you. Write it as a sensational headline. Read it out. Let the other person speculate.
