My Story Arc — Becoming a Lawyer

Ordinary village life → a chance meeting → total commitment → the long becoming → the gift

1The World Before
Who were you? What was the situation?
  • Ordinary — small village, simple life
  • Local school, blue-collar surroundings
  • A quiet, predictable road ahead
  • Mother pushing the academic path
2The Crack
Where were you? What happened?
  • End of high school
  • Indecision — unsure of next step
  • A chance meeting at the gym
  • That conversation changed everything
3The Struggle
What was hard? What did it cost?
  • Loss of free time
  • Not a natural reader
  • "If I do it, I do it 100%"
  • Friends lost along the way
  • Commitment as a daily choice
4The Becoming
How did it shape who you are today?
  • Not in one moment — slow but sure
  • Learned to sit with complexity
  • Became precise, detail-orientated
  • The practice shaped the person
5The Gift
Why should the listener care?
  • If you're not 100% convinced — don't start
  • Becoming a lawyer is so much effort
  • Without commitment, you'll quit
  • Know your why before you begin

Story arc: ordinary → the gym meeting → commitment → slow becoming → the gift
