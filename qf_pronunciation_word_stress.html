🎵 Word Stress Patterns

B1 • French/Italian/Polish • Master English stress for clarity

🔍 Let's notice the stress problem

Listen to these common mistakes:

❌ "phoTOgraphy" (wrong stress on TO)"

Should be: "PHOtography" (stress on FIRST syllable)

❌ "I reCORD the meeting"

Should be: "I REcord the meeting" (stress on first syllable for the verb)

Why do these sound wrong? Does the stress placement change meaning?

🎯 The Problem:

French, Italian, and Polish speakers often stress the FINAL syllable because that's how their languages work. But English stress is unpredictable and changes meaning!

English stress changes:

🔹 Meaning: REcord (noun: a document) vs reCORD (verb: to capture sound)

🔹 Word class: PREsent (noun: a gift) vs preSENT (verb: to show)

🛠️ English stress rules

📌 Rule 1: Nouns and Verbs (same word)

Many English words are both noun and verb. The stress shifts:

✓ Nouns: stress on 1st syllable (REcord, PREsent, OBject)

✓ Verbs: stress on 2nd syllable (reCORD, preSENT, obJECT)

NOUN:
RECord
(a document)
VERB:
reCORD
(to tape)
NOUN:
PRESent
(a gift)
VERB:
preSENT
(to show)
📌 Rule 2: Suffixes affect stress

-tion, -sion: Stress the syllable BEFORE the suffix

✓ informATION (not INformation)

✓ decISION (not DEcision)

-ic: Stress the syllable BEFORE

✓ fanTAStic (not FANtastic)

✓ draMATic (not DRAmatic)

-ity: Stress the syllable BEFORE

✓ univerSITy (not UNIversity)

✓ possiBILity (not POSSibility)
