Innovation Creating new ideas and solutions that drive progress
Autonomy Freedom to work independently and make decisions within your role
Initiative Acting without being told; taking the first step
Recognition Acknowledging and praising effort or success
Inclusion Making everyone feel welcome regardless of background
Accountability Taking responsibility for your actions and outcomes

Working Conditions

  • Micromanagement: Excessive control over small tasks, limiting creativity and independence
  • Flat Hierarchy: Few management levels, enabling faster decisions and open communication

Language Focus: Modals and Conditionals

Making Suggestions with Modals

  • We should... — Direct suggestion with medium strength
  • It might be a good idea to... — Gentle, tentative suggestion
  • Let's consider... — Collaborative, inclusive suggestion
  • Perhaps we could... — Polite, open-ended suggestion

Visioning with Conditionals

Use conditionals to express possibilities and potential benefits:

  • If the company had flexible hours... people would be more productive and balanced.
  • If we worked remotely... we could attract more talent from different locations.
  • If we want innovation... we should allow more freedom and creative time.

Practice

Try forming your own sentences using these patterns. Use them when describing your ideal workplace.

Speaking Task: Design Your Culture

Time Remaining
10:00

Your Task

Speak for 10 minutes about your ideal company culture. Use specific values and language structures.

Structure Your Presentation

  1. Select Core Values (2–3 min): Choose at least three values that define your ideal workplace. Explain why each matters to you using examples.
  2. Create Suggestions (2–3 min): Develop two suggestions using modal verbs to implement these values. Example: "We should create mentorship programs."
  3. Form Conditional Vision (2–3 min): Craft conditional sentences showing potential benefits. Example: "If we had flexible hours, employees would achieve better work-life balance."
  4. Prepare Examples (remaining time): Use concrete examples showing these values in action. Talk about a workplace you've experienced or one you've read about.

Things to Consider

  • What conditions help people do their best work?
  • What kind of office space appeals to you (quiet, social, remote)?
  • How should leaders communicate with employees?
  • What role should diversity and inclusion play?
