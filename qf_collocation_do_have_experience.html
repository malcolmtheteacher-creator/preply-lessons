✨ Have an Experience (Not Do)

Master this essential collocation | B1 Level

Let's see what you notice...

Read this sentence. What's the error?

I did a great experience in Italy.

What's the correction?

✓ Correction

I had a great experience in Italy.

📌 Why?

English uses HAVE with experiences, conversations, meetings, problems, arguments, fun, difficulty.

Italian/Spanish/French speakers often say "fare un'esperienza" / "hacer una experiencia" (DO an experience), but English uses HAVE.

Examples: Have a conversation, have a meeting, have an argument, have fun, have a good time, have difficulty.
