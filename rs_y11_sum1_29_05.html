How long is Paper 1 and how many marks?

🔒Reveal answer

Answer: 1 hour 45 minutes (105 minutes); 96 marks total; 4 sections × 24 marks each.

What is the mark breakdown per section?

🔒Reveal answer

Answer: Each section: 1 + 1 + 4 + 6 + 12 = 24 marks.

What does 'identify' mean in exam questions?

🔒Reveal answer

Answer: Name or state (1 word–2 words); no explanation needed; e.g., 'Identify one Christian value.'

What does 'explain' require?

🔒Reveal answer

Answer: Give reason(s) why something is true or matters; include detail and reference (belief, practice, teaching).

What does 'evaluate' mean?

🔒Reveal answer

Answer: Weigh arguments for and against a statement; present both sides; reach a reasoned conclusion.

How much time for 1-mark and 4-mark questions per section?

🔒Reveal answer

Answer: 1-mark: 1–2 minutes. 4-mark: 4–5 minutes. Total: 6–7 minutes per section.

How much time for 6-mark and 12-mark questions?

🔒Reveal answer

Answer: 6-mark: 6–7 minutes. 12-mark: 12–15 minutes (longest question). Total: 18–22 minutes per section.

What are SPaG marks?

🔒Reveal answer

Answer: Spelling, Punctuation, and Grammar; 3 bonus marks available across the paper; use in 12-mark questions.

What is the most common student mistake on 12-mark questions?

🔒Reveal answer

Answer: Stating one side only without evaluation; spending too long on knowledge and rushing the conclusion.

How should you structure a 12-mark answer?

🔒Reveal answer

Answer: Intro (restate claim), Para 1 (for), Para 2 (against), Para 3 (synthesis), Conclusion (judgment).

Command Words: Complete Guide

Identify (1 mark)

Name or state one point. No explanation.

Example: 'Identify one attribute of God in Christianity.'

Answer: 'Omnipotent' OR 'All-powerful'

Outline (1 mark)

Brief description; 1–2 sentences max.

Example: 'Outline the Trinity.'

Answer: 'God as three persons in one substance: Father, Son, Holy Spirit.'

Explain (4 marks)

Give reason(s) and show understanding. Link to belief/practice.

Example: 'Explain two reasons why prayer is important to Christians.'

Key: Two reasons, each explained (not just named).

Evaluate (12 marks)

Weigh arguments; show both sides; reach conclusion.

Example: 'Evaluate: The Hajj is the most important pillar.'

Key: For + Against + Synthesis + Judgment.

Worked Example: 4-Mark Explanation

Question: Explain two Christian beliefs about God's nature. (4 marks)

Model Answer (4 marks):

Belief 1: Omnipotence. Christians believe God is all-powerful and able to do anything. This is shown in the creation account where God speaks the world into existence with no limitations. It gives believers confidence that God can answer prayer and intervene in the world.

Belief 2: Omnibenevolence. Christians believe God is wholly good and loving. This is demonstrated in Jesus' teachings about God as a caring Father. It reassures believers that God acts in their best interest even in suffering.

Marking: Knowledge of two beliefs (2), explanation of why they matter (2) = 4 marks.

Plan a 12-Mark Evaluation

Use this outline to draft your own 12-mark answer:

Guidance

Statement: 'The Five Pillars are equally important to all Muslims.'

Your task: Plan an evaluation. List 3 points FOR and 3 AGAINST.

Words: 0/250

Common Pitfalls & How to Avoid Them

Pitfall 1: Vague Answers

Wrong: 'Christians believe in God.'

Right: 'Christians believe God is omnipotent (all-powerful), shown through creation and answered prayer.'

Pitfall 2: Identify vs Explain

Wrong (1-mark Q, explains): 'Identify one Christian practice: Prayer is talking to God.'

Right: 'Prayer.' (Just state it.)

Pitfall 3: Missing the 'So What'

Wrong: 'Muslims fast during Ramadan.'

Right: 'Muslims fast during Ramadan because it develops discipline and empathy for the poor, strengthening their faith.'

Pitfall 4: One-Sided Evaluation

Wrong: 'The Hajj is important because it's one of the Five Pillars.' (No counter-argument.)

Right: 'While the Hajj is significant, Salah is arguably more important as it's performed daily. However, the Hajj's once-in-a-lifetime nature makes it spiritually unique.'

Final Revision: Mark Your Timing

Paper 1 Time Allocation (105 minutes)

  • Per section (4 sections): ~26 minutes each
  • 1-mark × 2: 2 minutes
  • 4-mark × 1: 5 minutes
  • 6-mark × 1: 7 minutes
  • 12-mark × 1: 12 minutes
  • Buffer: ~20 minutes for checking/review

Guidance

Pro tip: Start with the 12-mark questions first if you're confident in knowledge; or tackle them when alert (not last). Don't spend more than 15 minutes on any single 12-mark answer.
