Paper 1 Structure & Command Words

Paper 1 Overview

Duration: 1 hour 45 minutes (105 minutes total).

Total marks: 96 marks (plus 4 SPaG) = 100.

Sections: Four, each worth 24 marks.

Structure per section: 1+1+4+6+12 = 24 marks.

Questions: All compulsory. No choice within sections.

Content: No biblical references required by mark scheme, BUT evidence strengthens answers significantly.

Four Sections Breakdown

Section A (Christianity Beliefs - 24 marks): Nature of God, Trinity, Creation, Incarnation, Crucifixion, Resurrection, Ascension, Afterlife, Sin, Salvation, Atonement.

Section B (Christianity Practices - 24 marks): Worship types, Prayer, Baptism, Eucharist, Pilgrimage, Celebrations, Church community, Mission, Evangelism, Persecution, Worldwide Church.

Section C (Islam Beliefs - 24 marks): Tawhid, Six Articles, Five Roots, Angels, Predestination, Akhirah, Risalah, Holy Books, Imamate.

Section D (Islam Practices - 24 marks): Five Pillars, Ten Obligatory Acts, Shahadah, Salah, Sawm, Zakah, Hajj, Jihad, Festivals.

Define (1 mark)

State precise meaning. One sentence. No explanation needed for full mark. Example: "Omnipotence: all-powerful; able to do anything."

Explain (4 marks)

Two developed points. Why is it significant? What does it mean? Use examples. Well-chosen quotes strengthen.

Question Formats & Command Words

1 mark: "Give a name/term for..." Single word or short phrase. Precision matters. No development needed.

4 mark: "Explain why..." or "Explain what..." Two clear points. Each developed with example or evidence. Use "because," "therefore."

6 mark: "Evaluate..." or "Assess..." Weigh both sides. Consider different views. Reasoned conclusion. Balanced argument. 3-4 paragraphs.

12 mark: Extended essay. Thesis in intro. 3-4 developed paragraphs. Counterargument acknowledged. Conclusion reaffirms thesis.

Worked Examples at Each Mark Level

Worked Example: 4-Mark Question

Question: "Explain what Christians mean by the Incarnation."

Weak answer (1-2 marks): "It's when God became human. Jesus was born."

Good answer (3-4 marks): "The Incarnation means God became human in Jesus Christ. Christians believe Jesus was fully God and fully human, uniting divine and human nature. This shows God's love by joining in human suffering and experience. It makes salvation possible because Jesus' sacrifice carries divine power and human obedience."

Key moves: Define (1), develop point 1 (2), develop point 2 (3), add significance (4).

Worked Example: 6-Mark Question

Question: "Evaluate the claim that the Five Pillars are the most important aspect of Islam."

Strong answer structure:

Para 1 (Support): Five Pillars are fundamental obligations. Framework of Muslim life. Equal importance for all believers regardless of culture or sect.

Para 2 (Development): But theological beliefs (Tawhid) enable Pillars. Belief comes before practice. No Pillar without faith foundation.

Para 3 (Counterargument): Some argue Greater Jihad (spiritual struggle) is more important than Pillars. Inward faith matters more than outward practice.

Conclusion: Both matter interdependently. Pillars express beliefs; beliefs motivate Pillars. Equally important as system.

Worked Example: 12-Mark Question

Question: "To what extent do you think the Resurrection is the most important Christian belief?"

Structure: Intro (2-3 sentences): state position. Thesis: yes/no/partly.

Para 1: Why Resurrection IS most important. Proves Jesus' divinity. Guarantees eternal life for believers. Validates all other beliefs.

Para 2: Why other beliefs matter equally. Incarnation precedes Resurrection; God becoming human is unique. Crucifixion is the sacrifice moment.

Para 3: Counterargument. Some argue faith (Incarnation) or atonement (Crucifixion) are more important theologically.

Conclusion: Resurrection is pivotal (makes faith meaningful) but interconnected with other beliefs. Hard to separate them. Strongest answer: "Resurrection is most important because ___, but cannot be separated from ___."

Common Mistakes & SPaG Focus

Time Management Strategy (105 minutes)

Reading & planning: 3 minutes. Read all questions. Identify 6-mark and 12-mark Qs (most time-heavy).

1-mark questions: ~1 minute per question (4 total = 4 min). Single word or short phrase.

4-mark questions: ~5 minutes per question (4 total = 20 min). Two developed points each.

6-mark questions: ~8 minutes per question (4 total = 32 min). Three paragraphs, balanced evaluation.

12-mark questions: ~15 minutes per question (4 total = 60 min). Full essay with intro/conclusion.

Review & SPaG: 5-10 minutes. Check spelling, grammar, clarity.

Common Exam Mistakes to Avoid

1-mark Q: Giving too much information. "All-powerful" is enough for omnipotent. Extra words waste time.

4-mark Q: Only one point explained. Marker gives max 2 marks. ALWAYS aim for two distinct, developed points.

6-mark Q: One-sided argument. Fails to evaluate properly. MUST weigh evidence and consider alternative views.

12-mark Q: No clear thesis. Essay rambles. Conclusion weak. MUST have clear position and support it consistently.

SPaG: Sloppy writing loses marks. Check: Capital letters, full stops, subject-verb agreement, spelling. Legibility matters.

SPaG Strategy (4 marks)

Spelling: Religious terms matter most. Get Incarnation, Eucharist, Tawhid, Hajj right. Common words more forgiving.

Punctuation: Full stops at end of sentences. Apostrophes in contractions ("don't" not "dont"). Commas in lists.

Grammar: Subject-verb agreement. "The Trinity is..." not "are." "Beliefs shape practice" not "shapes." Consistency in tense.

Tip: Read answer aloud mentally. If it sounds awkward, grammar might be wrong. Take last 3 minutes to proofread carefully.

Exam Strategy Summary

Paper 1 Success Checklist

Before exam: Know all four sections content. Practice mark scheme questions. Time yourself.

During exam: Read all questions first. Manage time by mark value. Plan 12-mark essays (2 min). Check spelling/grammar (5 min).

Quality markers: 1-mark: precise, concise. 4-mark: two points, each developed. 6-mark: balanced evaluation. 12-mark: clear thesis, supporting paragraphs, conclusion.

Final 5 minutes: Proofread. Check capitalization, punctuation, spelling. Add missing words if spotted.

Guidance

Key insight: Mark value determines answer length. More marks equals more time equals more development needed. A 4-mark answer is NOT mini-essay. A 12-mark answer IS full essay. Respect the format.
