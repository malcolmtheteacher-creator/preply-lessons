Q1: What is akhirah?

Lock

Answer: Akhirah is the Islamic belief in the afterlife and the Day of Judgement.

Q2: Name the Five Pillars

Lock

Answer: Shahada, Salah, Zakat, Sawm, Hajj

Q3: What does "Seal of Prophets" mean?

Lock

Answer: Muhammad is the final prophet. No prophets after him.

Q4: How do Sunni and Shi'a differ on leadership?

Lock

Answer: Sunni: community chooses leader. Shi'a: Allah chose Ali.

Q5: What are the four holy books before Qur'an?

Lock

Answer: Scrolls, Torah, Psalms, Gospel

Review - Unit 24 Deep Dive

Q1: How does akhirah belief motivate ethical behaviour?

Lock

Answer: Deeds recorded by angels, weighed on Judgement Day. Fear of hell, hope for paradise motivate righteous living.

Q2: Why do Muslims follow the Sunnah and Hadith?

Lock

Answer: Qur'an says Muhammad is excellent example. Sunnah shows how to live. Hadith provide guidance.

Q3: How is Qur'an perfectly preserved?

Lock

Answer: Revealed in Arabic, immediately memorized, written on materials, every copy identical to original.

Q4: What is Shahadah?

Lock

Answer: "There is no god but Allah, Muhammad is messenger." First Pillar, summarizes core beliefs.

Q5: What unites the global Muslim Ummah?

Lock

Answer: One Qur'an, Tawhid, Muhammad as final prophet, Five Pillars, prayer toward Makkah, shared beliefs.

Write - Extended Response (GCSE Practice)

12-Mark Evaluate Question

In Islam, belief in the Qur'an's perfection is more important than belief in Muhammad as final prophet. Evaluate.

Approximately 750 words.

Structure

  1. Introduce both beliefs and importance
  2. For claim: Qur'an is final word, complete guidance, basis of law
  3. Counter: Muhammad essential - delivered revelation, Muslims follow Sunnah
  4. Synthesise: inseparable - Qur'an is Muhammad's miracle
  5. Conclude: Both foundational, neither "more important"
