Different Media Channels

Television & Radio: Broadcast media that reaches mass audiences with news, entertainment, and information
Newspapers & Magazines: Print media providing in-depth reporting and analysis
Online News: Digital platforms offering real-time news and global coverage
Social Media: Platforms where users share and receive information directly
Podcasts: Audio content on various topics and current affairs

Linking Words & Phrases

These words help connect ideas in your writing. Fill in the blanks using appropriate linking words:

Complete the paragraph:

"1. ______ there are many advantages to social media, there are also several disadvantages. 2. ______, traditional media is facing challenges. 3. ______, young people rely heavily on social media for news. 4. ______, this raises concerns about misinformation."

1. Although / While
2. However / Nevertheless
3. For example / For instance
4. Therefore / As a result / Consequently

Common Linking Phrases for Essays

  • To show contrast: However, Nevertheless, In spite of, Whereas, Although
  • To add information: Moreover, Furthermore, In addition, Besides
  • To show cause/effect: Therefore, As a result, Consequently, Because
  • To give examples: For example, For instance, Such as, Like
  • To conclude: In conclusion, To summarize, Finally, In summary

Essay Statement

"Traditional media: on their way out?"

You will write a 140-180 word essay arguing for and/or against this statement.

Brainstorming: Arguments For and Against

Arguments In Favour (Traditional Media is Declining)

  • Declining newspaper circulation
  • Cord-cutting and streaming replacing TV
  • Rise of digital-native outlets
  • Younger generations prefer online sources
  • Cost of maintaining print infrastructure

Arguments Against (Traditional Media Still Matters)

  • In-depth journalism and investigative reporting
  • Credibility and editorial standards
  • Wide audience reach across demographics
  • Accessibility for less tech-savvy populations
  • No algorithm bias (unlike social media)

Add your own ideas:

Essay Structure Plan

Introduction

General idea about media. Rewrite the question in your own words. State your opinion.

Body Paragraph 1: Arguments For

Topic sentence stating your first main point. Supporting sentences with examples.

Body Paragraph 2: Arguments Against

Topic sentence for the opposing view. Supporting sentences with examples and evidence.

Conclusion

Summarize both sides. Restate your position. Final thought about the future of media.

Remember: Your essay should be 140-180 words, clearly divided into paragraphs with ideas connected using linking phrases. Use supporting examples and present arguments in a balanced, persuasive way.

Success Criteria

  • Present arguments in favour and against the statement
  • Include relevant examples for each argument
  • Clearly divide content into paragraphs
  • Connect ideas using linking words and phrases
  • Maintain formal, persuasive tone
  • Use accurate grammar and spelling
  • Stay within 140-180 word count
