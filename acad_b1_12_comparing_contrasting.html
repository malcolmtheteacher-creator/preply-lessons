Comparing and Contrasting

Key Vocabulary for Comparing and Contrasting

Click each card to see the definition.

similarity a way in which things are the same or alike
difference a way in which things are not the same
whereas used to show a contrast between two things
however introduces a statement that contrasts with what was said before
both the two things together; showing similarity
neither not one nor the other; none of the two
unlike different from; not similar to
on the other hand phrase used to show an opposite or different point of view

Comparison Methods

Method 1: Block Method

Structure: Write all details about Topic A, then all details about Topic B.

Example:

"London is the capital of England. It has over 9 million people and is famous for Big Ben, the Thames River, and red buses. Paris is the capital of France. It has around 2 million people and is known for the Eiffel Tower, museums, and cafes."

When to use: Longer texts, comparing many features, giving background information

Method 2: Point-by-Point Method

Structure: Compare the same feature for both topics one at a time.

Example:

"Both London and Paris are European capitals, but London is larger with 9 million people whereas Paris has 2 million. However, both cities are major tourist destinations. London is famous for Big Ben, whereas Paris is known for the Eiffel Tower."

When to use: Short comparisons, academic writing, highlighting differences more clearly

Common Comparison Phrases

  • Both ... and ... — "Both London and Paris have historic buildings"
  • Similarly — "Similarly, both cities attract millions of tourists"
  • In comparison — "In comparison, New York is younger than Paris"
  • Likewise — "London has excellent public transport. Likewise, Paris has extensive metro systems"

Common Contrast Phrases

  • Unlike ... — "Unlike Paris, London has more green spaces"
  • Whereas — "London is crowded, whereas Edinburgh is more compact"
  • On the other hand — "Tokyo is expensive. On the other hand, Bangkok has lower costs"
  • In contrast — "In contrast to London"s cold winters, Dubai has hot weather year-round"

Practice Exercises: Gap-Fill

Complete each sentence using a comparison or contrast word from the Key Language tab.

Exercise 1

A smartphone and a laptop are useful devices, but they serve different purposes.

Answer: both — Both devices are useful because they both serve important functions.

Exercise 2

Coffee contains caffeine, tea has very little caffeine.

Answer: whereas — This shows a clear contrast between the caffeine content of the two drinks.

Exercise 3

The new office building is modern and spacious. , the old one was cramped and outdated.

Answer: However — This contrasts the qualities of the new and old buildings.

Exercise 4

swimming, cycling is a low-impact exercise that is easy on your joints.

Answer: Unlike — This shows the difference between swimming and cycling.

Exercise 5

Barcelona has beaches and mountains. , Madrid is inland with no coastline.

Answer: On the other hand or However — These phrases introduce the contrasting information about Madrid.

Exercise 6

Winter is cold and dark, summer is warm and sunny.

Answer: Whereas — This creates a parallel structure comparing two opposite seasons.
