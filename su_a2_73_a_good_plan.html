Grammar Review: The First Conditional

Look at these sentences from the audio. They use the first conditional. Click to reveal the analysis:

  • If we move to cheaper offices, we will be harder to find.
    First conditional: if + present simple, will + infinitive. Used for real future possibilities.
  • We will be able to choose more modern offices if we move.
    First conditional (reversed order): will + infinitive, if + present simple. Same meaning, different word order.
  • If we reduce the size of our office, we won"t have enough space.
    First conditional with negative result: if + present simple, will + not + infinitive.
  • We won"t have enough space for all our staff unless we start hot-desking.
    First conditional using "unless" (= if not): unless + present simple, will + not + infinitive.

How Do We Use It?

We use the first conditional to talk about:

  • Things that might happen in the future (real possibilities)
  • The results or consequences of those actions
  • Solutions to problems in work or business situations

Business Meeting Task

The Situation

A lot of your company staff are unhappy. This year, 15% of employees have left the company.

Why are they unhappy?

  • Staff think salaries are low and haven"t changed for four years.
  • They don"t feel part of a team.
  • People only speak to each other in meetings.
  • People often don"t know the names of colleagues on the same floor.

Your task: The CEO has asked you to think of ways to improve staff happiness. Discuss the options below.

Focus on Money & Recognition

Consider these three options (and add two more of your own):

  • Option 1: An "employee of the month" prize. The best employee wins a weekend trip to Paris.
  • Option 2: A bonus for all staff at the end of the month if they meet targets.
  • Option 3: A 10% increase in salary for all staff at the end of the year.

Your own ideas:

Think: How will each option help? Will there be any problems?

Focus on Team & Belonging

Consider these three options (and add two more of your own):

  • Option 1: Have a one-hour social event every Friday afternoon for all staff.
  • Option 2: Have a Training Week in a five-star hotel for the best 24 employees.
  • Option 3: Change the basement offices into a fitness centre and café for staff.

Your own ideas:

Think: How will each option help? Will there be any problems?
