Psychology Questions

Six famous experiments about how the mind works: waiting, attention, the crowd, memory, nature and nurture, and money. Each one comes at two levels, so the same story can be told at A2/B1 or argued at B2/C1.

A child is left alone with a sweet. A gorilla walks through a basketball game. A whole room gives the wrong answer. Each of these is a real experiment that changed what people thought about the mind.

Each lesson starts from one of them. You meet the key words, read the story in three short parts, practise one grammar structure the story needs, test your thinking on five cases, and then speak. Pick a question, then pick your level.
