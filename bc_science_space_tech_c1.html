Persuasive Language Techniques

Imperatives

Direct commands that create urgency:

Example: "Don't wait another second!"

Rhetorical Questions

Questions that make people think about the answer:

Example: "What could be better than a quick dip in the pool before breakfast?"

Example: "How will you know if you never try it?"

Conditionals

Create scenarios using "if":

Example: "If you buy this camera, you'll never miss a perfect moment."

Positive Adjectives

Use strong, appealing descriptors: sleek, ultra-light, ground-breaking, innovative, revolutionary

Inversion

Reverse normal word order for emphasis:

Example: "No sooner do you arrive home than the lights go on."

Modal Verbs

Show possibility and opportunity:

Example: "You won't want to miss out on this chance."

Your Sales Pitch Challenge

You will present one of these innovative gadgets and try to convince with persuasive language, adjectives, and techniques you've learned.

Option A: Spherical Camera

Appearance: A small sphere with 36 lenses

Purpose: Takes enhanced 360-degree images

Problem solved: No need to exclude yourself from group photos

Features: Captures everything in 360-degree high resolution, includes you in the photo

Specs: 108-megapixel image, Android app, tough exterior

How it works: Toss in the air and catch—accelerometer calculates highest point and shoots

Option B: Door Robot

Appearance: Small security camera

Purpose: Monitor your front door from anywhere

Problem solved: Watch and communicate with visitors while away

Features: Smartphone alerts, allows you to address visitors, smart lock control

Specs: Adjustable camera, night vision, weather resistant

How it works: App connects your smartphone wirelessly to the robot; rings bell or motion triggers alert

Option C: Kind Waker

Appearance: Modern design, digital watch

Purpose: Ensure the best possible sleep

Problem solved: Wake during light sleep instead of deep sleep

Features: Selects optimum time to eliminate grogginess

Specs: Sleep monitor, sleep analyser, snooze feature

How it works: Monitors sleep activity, detects lightest sleep stage, wakes you

Option D: Smart Toothbrush

Appearance: Modern electric toothbrush

Purpose: Optimise your brushing experience

Problem solved: Get feedback on brushing technique and duration

Features: Advanced sensor technology, real-time feedback

Specs: Smartphone and Android compatible

How it works: Advanced sensors collect data on duration and frequency, communicate via app
