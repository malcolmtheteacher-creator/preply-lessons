Language for Talking About the Future

Match the predictions to their grammatical forms:

a) will + infinitive (prediction)
"In a few years, we'll be replacing manufacturing with 3D printers."
b) going to (prediction based on evidence)
"We're going to have to find ways of preventing AI abuse."
c) Present simple (scheduled future)
"The first self-driving cars are set to appear by 2027."
d) could/might (future possibility)
"The rise of machines could lead to the destruction of humanity."
e) Future continuous
"We'll be replacing a significant part of manufacturing."
f) Future perfect
"By 2030, programmers will have created full-immersion VR games."
g) Future passive
"Nanobots will soon be injected into our bodies."
h) "Future in the past" (would)
"A favourite prediction was that translating phones would replace interpreters."

Useful Phrases for Debating

Stating position: "I firmly believe that...", "It's my contention that..."

Adding points: "Furthermore...", "What's more...", "Additionally..."

Countering: "While that may be true...", "On the other hand..."

Concluding: "In conclusion...", "To sum up..."
