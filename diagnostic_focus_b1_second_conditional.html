B1 Focus Diagnostic — Second Conditional

Welcome to The Wish Engine. You will build second conditional sentences — the grammar of imaginary worlds, regrets, and advice.

The second conditional talks about imaginary or unlikely situations in the present or future. The shape never changes:

If + past simple + would / could / might + infinitive

Notice two classic traps: we never say "If I would...", and with the verb be, careful writing uses "if I were" — not "if I was".
