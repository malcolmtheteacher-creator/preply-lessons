Meet the thinker · B1

David Hume

1711-1776 · Edinburgh

A cheerful Scottish sceptic who asked how we can know anything, even that the sun will rise tomorrow.

Guess first

In his lifetime Hume was most famous for...

A life in a few steps

  1. 1711Born in Edinburgh.
  2. 1739A Treatise of Human Nature. Almost nobody reads it, and he says it 'fell dead-born from the press'.
  3. 1754Begins his History of England, which makes him famous and rich.
  4. 1776Dies calmly in Edinburgh.

Why Hume still matters

He showed that much of what we believe comes from habit, not proof, and that 'reason is the slave of the passions'.

Main works

A Treatise of Human NatureAn Enquiry Concerning Human UnderstandingThe History of England

Did you know?

Immanuel Kant said that reading Hume woke him from his 'dogmatic slumber'.
