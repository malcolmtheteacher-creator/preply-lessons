☕ The Joy of Small Things

Why Simple Pleasures Make Life Good

🎯 What's the Best Part of Your Day?

We often think happiness comes from big things—promotions, holidays, winning something. But science tells us a different story. The small, everyday moments might be what really matters. That first coffee. A quiet evening. A good meal. Let's explore why.

The Morning Coffee Effect ☕

Researchers found that small daily pleasures—like your morning coffee, a favourite song, or 10 minutes of peace and quiet—have a bigger effect on happiness than salary, job title, or material possessions.

Source: University of British Columbia, Journal of Positive Psychology
8 Small Pleasures a Day

The average person experiences about 8 small good moments every day—but most people only notice 2 or 3 of them. The others pass without attention. Simply noticing can double your daily satisfaction.

Source: Harvard Study of Adult Development
The "Enough" Principle

Studies of the happiest people show they share one thing: they appreciate what they have instead of wanting more. It's not about having more—it's about enjoying what's already there.

Source: Positive Psychology Research, 2022
🌍 Simple Pleasures Around the World Different cultures have words for this. In Italy, "dolce far niente" means the sweetness of doing nothing. In Denmark, "hygge" is about simple comfort—a warm drink, good company, no rush. In Japan, "ikigai" includes finding joy in daily routines. What do you call it in your language?

Real Stories

Marco, 58, Italy: "After my heart surgery, everything changed. I used to work 12-hour days, always busy. Now? I sit on my balcony every morning with coffee and watch the street wake up. Those 20 minutes are worth more than any business deal I ever made."

David, 45, Scotland: "I spent years thinking I'd be happy when I got the next thing—the car, the house, the promotion. Then my dad died suddenly. He was a simple man. Loved his garden, his dog, his pint on Friday. I realised he understood something I didn't. Now I try to live more like him."

Kenji, 67, Japan: "Every morning for 40 years, same routine: wake up, stretch, make tea, read the newspaper. My son says this is boring. But for me, this routine is like an anchor. It keeps me calm. The pleasure is in the repetition."

🗣️ Expressions for Talking About Enjoyment

Click each expression to learn more and practice!

"to savour something"

+
Meaning
To enjoy something slowly and completely; to really appreciate the experience
Form
savour + noun (savour the moment, savour the taste)
Pronunciation
SAY-ver (British) / SAY-vor (American)
"I like to savour my first coffee of the day—no phone, no rush."
"After working all week, I savour those quiet Saturday mornings."
Similar words: enjoy, appreciate, relish
🎯 NOW YOU TRY: What do you like to savour? A drink? A meal? A moment of peace? Tell me about it.

"it's the little things"

+
Meaning
Small, everyday moments are what really make life good (not big events)
Form
It's the little things (that make life good / that matter / in life)
Register
Informal, warm, conversational—used a lot!
"A cold beer after finishing a job. It's the little things."
"I don't need expensive holidays. A good book and some quiet—it's the little things."
🎯 NOW YOU TRY: Give me an example of a "little thing" that makes you happy. Start with: "It's the little things—like..."

"to take pleasure in something"

+
Meaning
To enjoy doing something; to get satisfaction from an activity
Form
take pleasure in + noun / -ing verb
Register
Slightly formal but very natural; sounds thoughtful
"I take pleasure in cooking a proper meal—not rushing, just taking my time."
"My father takes great pleasure in his garden. It keeps him busy."
Grammar note: take pleasure in cooking ✓ (not "to cook" ✗)
🎯 NOW YOU TRY: Complete this sentence about yourself: "I take pleasure in..."

🗣️ More Expressions for Simple Pleasures

"there's nothing like..."

+
Meaning
This is the best; nothing else compares; this is truly special
Form
There's nothing like + noun / -ing verb
Register
Informal, warm, enthusiastic—shows real appreciation
"There's nothing like a hot shower after a long day."
"There's nothing like the peace and quiet of early morning before anyone else wakes up."
Stronger version: "There's absolutely nothing like..." or "There's really nothing like..."
🎯 NOW YOU TRY: Complete this: "For me, there's nothing like..."

"to make someone's day"

+
Meaning
To make someone happy; a small thing that creates unexpected joy
Form
make + possessive + day (make my day, make his day, made their day)
Register
Informal, very common in conversation
"My son called just to chat. No reason. Made my day."
"Finding 20 euros in an old jacket—that made my day!"
Also common: "You've made my day!" (when thanking someone)
🎯 NOW YOU TRY: What's something small that can make your day? When did a small thing last make your day?

Quick Review: All 5 Expressions

  1. to savour something – enjoy slowly and completely
  2. it's the little things – small moments matter most
  3. to take pleasure in – get satisfaction from something
  4. there's nothing like... – this is the best
  5. to make someone's day – create happiness with something small
