W. Somerset Maugham

1874-1965 · England (born in Paris)

A doctor who became one of the best-selling writers of the twentieth century.

A life in a few steps

  1. 1874Born in Paris, at the British Embassy. French is his first language.
  2. 1884Both his parents have died, and he is sent to England to live with an uncle.
  3. 1897Qualifies as a doctor. His first novel is published the same year.
  4. 1915Of Human Bondage, partly about his own life.
  5. 1965Dies in France, aged 91.

Why people still read Maugham

He wrote clear, simple English and he loved a story with a twist at the end. His books were best-sellers for fifty years.
