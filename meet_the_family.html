Key words

genre (noun) — a style or type of music, film or writing. Example: "Jazz is my favourite genre, but I listen to almost anything."
lyrics (noun) — the words of a song. Example: "I love the tune, but I can never remember the lyrics."
melody (noun) — the main tune of a piece of music, the part you could hum. Example: "It has such a simple melody that children can sing it."
rhythm (noun) — a regular pattern of beats. Example: "A complex rhythm underlies the song."
sing in harmony (verb) — to sing different notes together so that they sound good. Example: "The three sisters sang in perfect harmony."
chorus (noun) — the part of a song that repeats after each verse. Example: "Everyone in the stadium joined in with the chorus."
catchy (adjective) — easy to remember and hard to stop singing. Example: "That advert has such a catchy tune."
improvise (verb) — to make something up while you are performing, without planning it. Example: "The pianist forgot the music, so she had to improvise."
sampling (noun) — taking a short piece of an old recording and using it in a new song. Example: "Early hip-hop was built on sampling old soul and funk records."
guilty pleasure (noun) — something you enjoy but feel slightly embarrassed about. Example: "Eighties power ballads are my guilty pleasure."
