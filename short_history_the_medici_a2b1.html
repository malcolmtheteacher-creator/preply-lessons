The Medici

A family of bankers in Italy who used their money to pay for beautiful art.

Want more detail? Read this lesson at B2/C1 level →

Today: tell the story of the Medici, a rich family in Italy who paid artists to make beautiful things. Talk about money, art and power. Grammar: the past simple (finished actions in the past).
Warm up — talk before we read.
  • The Medici were a rich family in Italy long ago. They paid artists to make beautiful things. Do you know any famous art or buildings from Italy?
  • If you were very rich, would you pay for art, new buildings, or something else? Why?
  • Can a family be powerful without being kings or queens? How?

B. Guess the word from the sentence

Read the sentence. What does the bold word mean? Say your idea, then reveal.

7. The Medici gave a lot of money to young artists. Because of this support, the artists could work and make beautiful things.

8. The city was full of new art, new ideas and new books. This great time in Italy was the Renaissance.

The story, in three parts

Read each part. Then try the questions from memory, and talk about the last one.

Part 1 — A rich family with a bank

The Medici were a family in Florence, a beautiful city in Italy. Long ago, about 600 years ago, they started a bank. The bank became very big and very strong. The Medici lent money to kings, to rich people, and even to the Pope in Rome. They became one of the richest families in all of Europe.

One man in the family was very clever. His name was Cosimo de' Medici. Florence had no king. The people wanted to choose their leaders. But Cosimo had a lot of money, so he had a lot of power. He helped his friends and gave money to important people. In this quiet way, he became the real leader of the city.

Cosimo did not only want power. He also loved beautiful things. He gave money to artists and to builders. He paid for a very big, round roof — a "dome" — on the top of the big church in Florence. People said it was impossible to build. But the builder finished it, and that dome is still on the church today.

Quick check — answer from memory, then reveal:

1. How did the Medici family become rich?

2. How did Cosimo become the leader of Florence, when there was no king?

1. They started a bank and lent money to kings, rich people and the Pope. The bank became very big.

2. He had a lot of money, so he had power. He helped his friends and gave money to important people, and became the real leader.

Talk about it:
  • Cosimo was the leader, but he was not a king. Do you think money can give a person power? How?

Part 2 — Lorenzo and the artists

Cosimo's grandson was called Lorenzo. People called him "Lorenzo the Magnificent" because he was so great. He loved art more than anything. He invited many artists to his house and gave them money and food. A young boy called Michelangelo lived with the family for some time. Later, Michelangelo became one of the greatest artists in the world.

But the Medici had enemies too. Another rich family, the Pazzi, did not like them. In 1478, the Pazzi made a secret plan. During a church service, some men attacked Lorenzo and his brother. They killed Lorenzo's brother, but Lorenzo ran away and was safe. The people of Florence were very angry and helped Lorenzo. After this, Lorenzo was more powerful than before.

Lorenzo loved art, but he was not good with the bank. He spent a lot of money and did not watch the business. Slowly, the family bank started to lose money. But the beautiful art did not stop. When Lorenzo died in 1492, Florence was full of wonderful paintings and statues.

Quick check — answer from memory, then reveal:

1. Who was Lorenzo, and which famous artist lived with his family?

2. What did the Pazzi family do in 1478?

1. He was Cosimo's grandson, "Lorenzo the Magnificent". He loved art. The young Michelangelo lived with his family.

2. They made a secret plan and attacked Lorenzo and his brother in a church. They killed his brother, but Lorenzo was safe.

Talk about it:
  • Lorenzo loved art, but he was not good with money. Is it a problem to love one thing and forget another?

Part 3 — Popes, and art for everyone

The Medici family became very powerful. Some men in the family became the Pope, the leader of the Church in Rome. In total, four Medici men became popes. Two Medici women became queens in France. The family from the small bank was now one of the most important families in all of Europe.

The Medici family ruled Florence for a very long time — about 300 years. But then the family became smaller and smaller. In the end, there were no more children. The last Medici died, and the great family was finished.

But the story has a happy end. The last person in the family gave all the family's art to the city of Florence. She said the art must stay in the city forever. Because of this gift, you can still go to Florence today and see the beautiful paintings and statues. The Medici family is gone, but the art they paid for is still here.

Quick check — answer from memory, then reveal:

1. How did the Medici become powerful outside Florence?

2. Why can people still see the Medici art in Florence today?

1. Four Medici men became popes in Rome, and two Medici women became queens in France.

2. The last person in the family gave all the art to the city of Florence and said it must stay there forever.

Talk about it:
  • The last Medici gave the art to the city. Was this a good idea? Should beautiful art be for everyone?

Grammar: past simple

This story happened long ago and is finished. For finished actions in the past, we use the past simple.

Regular verbs: add -ed.
start → started · love → loved · help → helped · call → called · want → wanted
"The Medici started a bank." "Cosimo helped his friends."
Irregular verbs: learn them one by one.
give → gave · make → made · become → became · run → ran · die → died · pay → paid · build → built
"Cosimo gave money to artists." "Four men became popes."
Time words help you. We often use words like in 1478, long ago, then, later, yesterday with the past simple.

1. Write the past simple (regular verbs)

Change the verb in brackets to the past simple. Add -ed.

1. The Medici (start) a bank in Florence.

2. Cosimo (help) his friends and got power.

3. The people (love) Lorenzo and his art.

1. started

2. helped

3. loved

These verbs are irregular. Write the correct past simple form.

3. Tell a short part of the story

Fill each gap with the past simple of the verb in brackets. Read it aloud when you finish.

Long ago, the Medici (be) a rich family in Florence. Cosimo (pay) for a big dome on the church. His grandson Lorenzo (invite) many artists to his house. The last Medici (give) all the art to the city.

Remember: regular verbs take -ed; irregular verbs you must learn one by one.
