Politics: Systems, Issues & Debates

Lessons that mix how power works, hot topics, how people argue, participation and media — for respectful discussion.

Politics looks different in every country. These lessons mix systems (how power is organised), one hot topic, debate styles, participation (voting, protests, trust) and media. Start with the Background, then explore the places. Talk about tendencies, not every person.
