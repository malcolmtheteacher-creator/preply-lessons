The king of the surprise ending, who wrote hundreds of stories about ordinary New Yorkers.

A life in a few steps

  1. 1862William Sydney Porter is born in North Carolina.
  2. 1882Moves to Texas. He works on a ranch, in a chemist's shop and in a bank.
  3. 1898Goes to prison for taking money from the bank. He begins writing stories seriously there.
  4. 1902Moves to New York and soon writes a story every week for a newspaper.
  5. 1905'The Gift of the Magi'.
  6. 1910Dies aged 47.

Why people still read O. Henry

His stories turn round in the very last lines. A surprise ending like this is still called 'an O. Henry twist'.
