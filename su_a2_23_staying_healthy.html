Giving Advice

We use three main structures to give advice about health:

Structure 1: "You should + verb"

Examples:

• You should do some exercise seven days a week.

• You should eat vegetables every day.

• You should drink lots of water.

Structure 2: "Make sure you + verb"

Examples:

• Make sure you get lots of Vitamin C.

• Make sure you eat healthy food.

• Make sure you take breaks when you exercise.

Structure 3: "Don't + verb" (negative advice)

Examples:

• Don't eat too much meat.

• Don't skip meals.

• Don't drink too much coffee.

Pronunciation: Sentence Stress

In advice sentences, we stress the important words. Listen carefully:

Example 1

"You should do some exercise seven days a week."

Stressed: DO, EXERCISE, SEVEN DAYS. We stress the action word and the time.

Example 2

"You should do many types of exercise."

Stressed: DO, TYPES, EXERCISE. We stress different types and the variety.

Example 3

"Don't take breaks when you exercise."

Stressed: TAKE, BREAKS, EXERCISE. We stress what NOT to do and the action.

Example 4

"Make sure you get lots of Vitamin C."

Stressed: GET, VITAMIN C. We stress what to get and the specific nutrient.

Key point: In advice sentences, we stress the content words (verbs, nouns, adjectives, numbers) more than small words (a, the, of, to).

📖 Language Reference ▼

Advice Structures

  • You should + V1: You should do some exercise. You should eat vegetables.
  • Make sure you + V1: Make sure you get lots of Vitamin C. Make sure you drink water.
  • Don't + V1: Don't eat too much meat. Don't skip meals.

Health & Fitness Collocations

  • go: swimming, jogging, hiking, running, to the gym
  • eat: vegetables, fruit, healthy food
  • drink: water, orange juice, green tea
  • play: badminton, volleyball, tennis
  • do: exercise, sport, yoga, karate
  • lift: weights
  • take: breaks

Counter-Advice (If they make an excuse)

  • "I'm too busy" → You should find 30 minutes a day.
  • "I love fast food" → Make sure you eat healthy food most days.
  • "I don't like vegetables" → You should try different vegetables.
  • "I'm too tired" → You should exercise—it gives you energy!

When you finish: Sum up your advice with: "The most important advice for me is… because…"
