Say It Clearly

Three moves that make what you say easy to hear: give the meaning, answer honestly, and say numbers so they stick.

Clear speaking is mostly a matter of choosing what the listener needs to hear first. Each lesson here takes one real story, one tool, and a lot of speaking practice. The tools are small enough to use the same day: a one-sentence summary of what your data means, a three-part reply to a difficult question, and a way to say a figure so that it is remembered.

The lessons stand alone, so take them in any order. They work best after Begin With the End, which teaches the habit of landing your point first.

Who it is for

Anyone who speaks English at work and already speaks it well: managers, specialists, people who present results, answer questions and are asked for numbers. No grammar from scratch; the language here is the language of clarity.
