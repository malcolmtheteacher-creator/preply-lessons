The security system uses a Caesar cipher — an ancient Roman encryption where each letter is replaced by a letter a fixed number of positions down the alphabet.
💡 HOW TO SOLVE: Each letter has been shifted FORWARD by 3 positions.
To decode: shift each letter BACK by 3.

So if you see "G", it really means "D".

The vault has a biometric lock, temperature sensors, and a pressure-sensitive floor. Any sudden movement will trigger the containment protocol.

This is the critical moment. One wrong move and the facility locks down. The antidote will be destroyed.
PROCEDURAL PRECISION 🔬
Break down complex actions into granular, sequential steps. Medical procedures require precision — so does your writing.
EXAMPLE:
"I opened the vault and grabbed the antidote."
"I positioned my thumb on the biometric scanner. Held my breath. The light flickered from red to green. The pneumatic seal released with a soft hiss. I eased the vault door open, centimetre by centimetre."

EXAMPLE:
"The vial was cold against my palm. Frost crystallised on its surface. The fluorescent hum of the refrigeration unit was the only sound. I could taste the metallic tang of recycled air."
