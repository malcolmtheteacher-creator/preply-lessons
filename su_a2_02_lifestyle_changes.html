Last year, I decided I needed to save money, but I kept ____1____ making a budget. Without a plan, I eventually ____2____ spending too much on coffee. Every morning on my way to work, I would ____3____ at my favorite café.

Then I sat down and ____4____ how much I was actually spending. I ____5____ buying expensive coffee and started making it at home. On weekends, I ____6____ and saved money by not going out for breakfast. Most evenings I ____7____ and cooked meals instead of eating at restaurants.

By the end of the month, I realized I hadn't ____8____ of money! I decided to ____9____ on entertainment expenses. Now when I watch films at home, I always ____10____ the volume so my friends can enjoy it too.

Phrasal Verb Meanings
work out = calculate / exercise
give up = stop doing something
stop off = make a brief stop on a journey
cut down = reduce
put off = postpone / delay
sleep in = sleep late
turn up = arrive / increase volume
end up = finally be in a situation
run out = have no more of something
stay in = remain at home

Discuss these questions with focus on pronunciation and natural speaking.

1

Do you spend a lot of time working out how to save money?

2

Have you ever given up something to save money?

3

If you are shopping in town do you often stop off for a coffee?

4

Is it a good idea to put off holidays if you don't have much money?

5

Have you ever slept in because you had nothing else to do?

Tip: Pay attention to stress and intonation. Phrasal verbs often have the stress on the particle (the second word): "GIVE UP", "WORK OUT", "STOP OFF".
