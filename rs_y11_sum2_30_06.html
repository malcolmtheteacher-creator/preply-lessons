Paper 2 Structure (All 4 Themes)

Answer: Each theme has 5 questions: 1 mark + 1 mark + 4 marks + 6 marks + 12 marks = 24 marks per theme × 4 themes = 96 marks total. 1h 45m = 105 minutes. Average ~11 minutes per theme.

MOCK EXAM: Set Timer to 1h 45m. Start Now.

THEME A: Relationships & Families (96 minutes from start)

Q1 (1 mark): Name one Christian or Islamic teaching about marriage. ___________

Q2 (1 mark): What is cohabitation? ___________

Q3 (4 marks): Explain two Christian or Muslim teachings about sexual ethics. [Space for 4-5 sentences]

Q4 (6 marks): Evaluate: 'Divorce is never morally justified.' How far do you agree? [Space for 6-8 sentences with balanced view]

Q5 (12 marks): 'Christian and Islamic teachings on family roles are the same.' Evaluate this statement. [Write full essay, 250-350 words, with intro/paras/conclusion]

THEME B: Religion & Life (54 minutes remaining)

Q1 (1 mark): Name one scientific explanation for the universe's origin. ___________

Q2 (1 mark): What is meant by 'sanctity of life'? ___________

Q3 (4 marks): Explain two Christian or Muslim teachings about abortion. [Space for 4-5 sentences]

Q4 (6 marks): Evaluate: 'Euthanasia is never justified.' Discuss. [Space for balanced 6-8 sentences]

Q5 (12 marks): 'Belief in the Big Bang is incompatible with belief in God.' Evaluate. [Write full essay, 250-350 words]

THEME D: Peace & Conflict (30 minutes remaining)

Q2 (1 mark): What is terrorism? ___________

Q3 (4 marks): Explain two Christian or Islamic teachings about warfare. [Space for 4-5 sentences]

Q4 (6 marks): Evaluate: 'Nuclear weapons can never be justified.' How far do you agree? [Space for balanced 6-8 sentences]

THEME E: Crime & Punishment (Final 16 minutes: finish strong!)

Q1 (1 mark): Name one cause of crime. ___________

Q4 (6 marks): Evaluate: 'The death penalty is sometimes justified.' Do you agree? [Space for balanced 6-8 sentences]

Q5 (12 marks): 'Christian and Islamic approaches to justice are fundamentally different.' Discuss. [Write full essay, 250-350 words]

Model Answers & Self-Assessment

After you finish, check these model answers:

  • Theme A, Q5 Model: Introduce comparison. Para 1: Christian teaching (hierarchy, mutual love) with evidence (Eph 5:23-24). Para 2: Islamic teaching (guardianship, motherhood) with evidence (Q4:34, Hadith). Para 3: Evaluate — both emphasize different things (Christian = mutuality, Islamic = complementarity). Conclusion: Similar goals (family stability, love), different frameworks. Grade: A would be 10-12 marks.
  • Theme B, Q4 Model: Some argue sanctity of life means no euthanasia (2 marks). However, others say suffering, autonomy matter (2 marks). I conclude: sanctity of life is important but doesn't absolutely forbid euthanasia where suffering is extreme (2 marks). Grade: 6 marks.
  • Theme D, Q3 Model: Christians teach Just War requires just cause, e.g., defence against aggression (2 marks). Muslims teach Jihad must be declared by authority and never harm civilians, e.g., Q6:151 forbids killing innocents (2 marks). Grade: 4 marks.
