🌍 On a Scale / At a Level

Fixed academic preposition phrases — memorise them | B2 Level

Let's see what you notice...

Read this sentence. What's the preposition error?

Plastic pollution is a serious environmental problem in global scale.

What's the correction?

✓ Correction

Plastic pollution is a serious environmental problem on a global scale.

📌 The Rule

These are fixed academic collocations — the preposition and noun are locked together:

on a ___ scale  ·  at a ___ level  ·  in the ___ term

You cannot swap the prepositions — these must be memorised as complete phrases.

📚 Three Phrases to Memorise

📐
ON a ___ scale

global · large · small
massive · national
on a global scale

📊
AT a ___ level

local · national
individual · international
at a local level

⏳
IN the ___ term

long · short · medium
 
in the long term

💡 Why this happens: Many languages use a general preposition ("in") for all three. In English academic writing, each phrase has its own fixed preposition. Treat them as vocabulary chunks, not grammar rules.
