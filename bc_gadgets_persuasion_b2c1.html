🗣️ The Language of Persuasion

Advertisers and salespeople use specific linguistic techniques to convince us to buy. Master these techniques to become more persuasive—and more aware of when others are trying to persuade you!

1. Imperatives (Commands) ▼

Direct commands create a sense of urgency and action. They tell the listener exactly what to do.

"Buy now!" • "Don't miss out!" • "Experience the future!" • "Transform your life today!" • "Get yours before they're gone!"
2. Rhetorical Questions ▼

Questions that don't need an answer—they make the listener think and engage with your message.

"Wouldn't you love to wake up feeling refreshed every morning?" • "Why settle for less when you can have the best?" • "Isn't it time you treated yourself?"
3. Conditional Sentences ▼

Show cause and effect—what will happen IF someone takes (or doesn't take) action.

"If you order today, you'll receive a 30% discount." • "If you don't act now, you'll regret it later." • "Once you try this, you'll never go back."
4. Positive Adjectives ▼

Powerful descriptive words that create positive associations and emotions.

"revolutionary" • "ground-breaking" • "state-of-the-art" • "cutting-edge" • "innovative" • "sleek" • "premium" • "unrivalled" • "effortless"
5. Inversion for Emphasis ▼

Changing normal word order to create dramatic effect. Very common in formal advertising.

"Never before has technology been so accessible." • "Rarely will you find such quality at this price." • "Not only is it powerful, but it's also beautiful."
6. Modal Verbs ▼

Create possibility, ability, and advice—making claims while avoiding absolute statements.

"This could change your life." • "You'll be able to accomplish more than ever." • "You might just fall in love with it." • "You should definitely consider this."

🎤 Your Pitch: Sell a Gadget!

Time to put your persuasive language skills into action!

📋 The Challenge

You are a product designer pitching your latest invention to potential investors. You have 2 minutes to convince them your gadget is worth backing!

  1. Choose or invent a gadget — It can be real or imaginary (the more creative, the better!)
  2. Prepare your pitch — Use at least 4 different persuasive techniques
  3. Include the vocabulary — Try to use 5+ expressions from the vocabulary section
  4. Deliver your pitch — You have exactly 2 minutes!
  5. Face the panel — Answer questions from your "investors" (classmates)

⏱️ Pitch Timer

Your Pitch Time
2:00

✅ Pitch Checklist

Make sure your pitch includes:

At least ONE imperative command
At least ONE rhetorical question
At least ONE conditional sentence
Multiple positive adjectives (state-of-the-art, cutting-edge, etc.)
ONE example of inversion for emphasis
5+ vocabulary items from the lesson

💬 Useful Phrases for Your Pitch

"Introducing the revolutionary..." "Imagine a world where..." "What if I told you..." "Never before has..." "This isn't just a gadget—it's..." "Say goodbye to..." "The future is here, and it's called..." "Don't miss your chance to..." "Join the thousands who already..." "At this price, it's a no-brainer."
