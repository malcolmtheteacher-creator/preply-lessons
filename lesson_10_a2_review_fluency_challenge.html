📚 Tense Master Review

Click each card to review the tense. Can you explain it and give an example?

🔵 Past Simple

When do we use it?

Use: Completed actions in the past
Example: "I visited Paris last summer."
Time words: yesterday, last week, in 2020, ago

🟣 Past Continuous

When do we use it?

Use: Background action or action in progress in the past
Example: "I was walking home when it started to rain."
Time words: while, when, at 8pm yesterday

🟢 Used To / Would

When do we use it?

Use: Past habits or states that are no longer true
Example: "I used to live in London." / "We would play in the park."
Note: "Would" = repeated actions only, not states

🟡 Present Perfect

When do we use it?

Use: Life experiences, recent events, unfinished time
Example: "I have visited 10 countries."
Time words: ever, never, already, yet, just, this week

🟠 Present Perfect Continuous

When do we use it?

Use: Actions that started in the past and continue now (duration/recent activity)
Example: "I have been studying English for 3 years."
Time words: for, since, all day, lately, recently

🔴 Going To + Will

When do we use each one?

Going to: Plans and intentions / "I'm going to study medicine."
Will: Predictions & spontaneous decisions / "I think it will rain."
Difference: Going to = already decided; Will = deciding now

⚫ Present Continuous for Future

When do we use it?

Use: Fixed arrangements (usually with other people)
Example: "I'm meeting my friend at 6pm tomorrow."
Note: More definite than "going to" - it's arranged!

⚪ Future Continuous & Future Perfect

When do we use these?

Future Continuous: Actions in progress at a future time
"This time tomorrow, I will be flying to Japan."

Future Perfect: Actions completed before a future time
"By 2030, I will have graduated from university."
