How you say it changes everything

The same message — said to your best friend, your boss, a child, or a stranger — uses completely different words, tone, and grammar. That shift is register, and getting it right is what makes you sound natural.

Example: "I don't understand" → to a friend: "Wait, what?" → to a professor: "Sorry, could you explain that again?" → to a child: "Hmm, can you show me what you mean?"

3 rounds

Say It Four Ways: A situation appears. Both of you say it to 4 different audiences. Compare your versions.
Guess the Audience: One person says it in a specific register. The other guesses WHO they're talking to.
Register Roulette: Random audience + random situation. You have 15 seconds. No time to plan.

Reflection

Which audience was hardest to speak to? Was it the very formal one? The child? Why?
What actually changes between registers? Think about: vocabulary, sentence length, directness, politeness markers, body language.
When have you got the register wrong in real life? Too formal with friends? Too casual in a meeting? What happened?
Is your native language the same? Do you shift register as much in your first language? More? Less?
