🔥 The Environmental Crisis

❓ If we continue living the way we do now, what will the world be like in 50 years?

1.5°C
Global temperatures have already risen by 1.5°C since pre-industrial times
🌡️
Climate Change
Temperatures are rising faster than ever. Ice caps have been melting for decades. If we don't act now, sea levels will rise dramatically, which will affect millions of people.
🗑️
Plastic Pollution
Over 8 million tons of plastic are dumped into the ocean every year. Marine animals are dying because they eat plastic that looks like food. By 2050, there could be more plastic than fish in the ocean!
🌳
Deforestation
Forests are being cut down at an alarming rate. The Amazon rainforest, which produces 20% of the world's oxygen, has lost an area the size of France. Animals that live in these forests are losing their homes.
1 million
Species are at risk of extinction due to human activity

🗣️ Discuss: Which environmental problem worries you the most? Why?

📚 Essential Environmental Vocabulary

🌱 Key Terms

Learn these words to discuss environmental issues effectively!

🌡️ Global warming
The Earth getting hotter due to greenhouse gases
♻️ Recycle
Process materials to use them again
🌿 Sustainable
Can be maintained without harming the environment
💨 Carbon footprint
Amount of CO2 you produce through daily activities
🌊 Renewable energy
Power from sources that don't run out (solar, wind)
🛢️ Fossil fuels
Coal, oil, gas - non-renewable energy sources
🌳 Deforestation
Cutting down forests on a large scale
🐟 Biodiversity
Variety of plant and animal life
☠️ Pollution
Harmful substances in air, water, or land
🌍 Climate change
Long-term changes in global weather patterns
💧 Conserve
Use less of something; protect from harm
🔋 Eco-friendly
Not harmful to the environment

💬 Useful Phrases for Discussion

  • "In my opinion, we should..."
  • "The government must take action by..."
  • "If we don't do something, then..."
  • "I think the biggest problem that we face is..."
  • "Something which really worries me is..."
  • "We have already seen the effects of..."
  • "Plastic is being thrown away every day."
  • "People who care about the environment..."

🎯 Discussion Tips

Use your B1 grammar naturally:

  • Conditionals: "If everyone recycled, we would reduce waste."
  • Passive: "Forests are being destroyed."
  • Present Perfect: "We have already seen the effects."
  • Modals: "The government should/must/could..."
  • Relative Clauses: "People who care about..."

⚖️ Environmental Debates

🥊 Take a Side!

Choose a position and defend it with your teacher. Use strong arguments!

✅ FOR

"Plastic bags should be banned completely."

  • Save marine life
  • Reduce pollution
  • Force people to use reusable bags
  • Already done in many countries

❌ AGAINST

"Plastic bags should be banned completely."

  • Inconvenient for shoppers
  • Reusable bags also have environmental cost
  • Better to recycle than ban
  • Affects poor people more
