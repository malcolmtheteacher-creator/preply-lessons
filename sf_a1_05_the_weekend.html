Grammar: like + -ing / can, can't

Two small tools for free time: like with -ing, and can.

like / love / don't like + -ing. "I love cooking." "She likes reading." "I don't like running." (swim → swimming, run → running)
Questions with like: "Do you like football?" "Do you like cooking?" — "Yes, I do. / No, I don't. / I love it!"
can / can't = it is possible for you, or not: "I can swim." "I can't play the guitar." "Can you cook?" — "Yes, I can. / No, I can't." No -s with he: "He can sing."
How often? "every Saturday" · "on Sunday" · "three times a week" · "a lot" · "never"
