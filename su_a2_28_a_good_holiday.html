What Makes a Good Holiday?

Let's think about holidays:

What are the most popular holiday destinations for people from your country?

Planning a holiday:

What's important when deciding where to go on holiday?

  • Weather: Hot and sunny, or cold and snowy?
  • Activities: Beach, mountains, culture, adventure?
  • Cost: Budget-friendly or luxury?
  • Who with: Family, friends, or solo?
  • Distance: Near home or far away?

Holiday timing:

Do people usually have a summer holiday, a winter holiday, or both?

Personal preference:

What kind of weather do you prefer on holiday?

Holiday Questions & Answers

Match Questions with Answers

These questions and answers come from real holiday conversations. Study the patterns so you can ask and answer about holidays:

Questions Answers
How was your holiday? It was great! I had a lot of fun!
Where did you go? We went to London.
What was the weather like? It was hot and sunny.
What time did you arrive? We got there at 7pm.
Did you see anything interesting? Yes, we did. We saw Buckingham Palace, the London Eye and Tower Bridge.
What was the food like? It was delicious! We had traditional English food like fish and chips.

Key Holiday Vocabulary

Destination
The place you are going to on holiday
Summer/Winter holiday
Holiday during summer or winter season
Weather
Hot, sunny, cold, rainy, windy, etc.
Arrive
Get to a place; past tense = arrived
Interesting
Worth your attention because it's exciting or unusual
Traditional
Doing something the way it has been done for a long time
Delicious
Very tasty and enjoyable to eat

Grammar Focus: Past Simple Questions

Question Pattern: Did + you/we + verb + ...?
Example: "Did you see anything interesting?" = asking about a completed action in the past
Short Answer: Yes, we/I did. OR No, we/I didn't.
Example: "Yes, we did. We saw Buckingham Palace."
Other Question Words: Where, What, When, How
Examples: "Where did you go?" / "What was the weather like?" / "How was your holiday?"

Create Your Own Questions

Now try to make 3 more questions about holiday experiences:

Question 1: Did you... ?
Think about activities: swim, hike, visit museums, try local food, etc.
Question 2: How long... ?
Think about duration: How long did you stay? How many days...?
Question 3: What was the best part... ?
Think about memories: What was your favorite moment?

Common Holiday Experiences

Beach Holiday
You enjoy swimming, sunbathing, and relaxing by the sea.
City Break
A short holiday visiting a city to see sights, museums, and try new restaurants.
Mountain Holiday
You go hiking, enjoy nature, and see beautiful views.
Adventure Holiday
You do active things like rock climbing, surfing, or exploring.

Reflection & Summary

3-2-1 Reflection

Take a moment to think about what you've learned:

3 things: List 3 new words or phrases about holidays that you learned today.
  • Destination — the place you're going
  • Traditional food — food made the way it has been made for a long time
  • City break — a short holiday in a city
2 things: Write 2 questions you could ask someone about their holiday.
  • "Where did you go on holiday?"
  • "Did you see anything interesting?"
  • "What was the weather like?"
  • "How long did you stay?"
1 thing: What is the most important thing to talk about when you describe a holiday?
  • The destination (where you went)
  • What you did and what you saw
  • Whether you had fun and enjoyed it
  • The weather or special experiences

Discussion Questions to Think About

Popular destinations:

What are some holiday destinations you want to go in the future?

Holiday plans:

Have you got any holiday plans this year?

City breaks:

Do you like city breaks (short holidays that visit a city)? Why or why not?

Return visits:

Is there one place that you have visited that you want to go back to again? Why?

Holiday preferences:

Do you prefer to go on holiday with friends, with family, or by yourself? Why?
