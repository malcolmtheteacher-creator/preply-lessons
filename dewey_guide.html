How the shelves work

Every lesson has a place, the way every book in a library does.

The idea

Libraries all over the world file books by subject using the Dewey Decimal system. There are ten big classes, numbered 000 to 900, and each one holds one kind of knowledge. Once you know the numbers, you can walk into almost any library and find what you want.

This map does the same with lessons. Pick a shelf, then a topic, then your level, and keep going until you reach a lesson.

The eight shelves

Ten classes are squeezed into eight so they fit on the map: philosophy and religion share a shelf, and so do arts and literature.

Using the map

Tap a picture to go deeper. The middle picture always takes you back up one step. Inside a big topic, lessons are grouped by level, so you can start where you are comfortable and move up.

If you are working towards an exam or a goal such as IELTS, an interview or a first conversation, the first three pictures on the map give you a step-by-step route instead.
