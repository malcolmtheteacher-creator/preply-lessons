Pattern Hunting

Look at all the verb phrases above. What pattern do you notice? They all follow: subject (she) + verb (plays/teaches/connects/researches) + object or complement. This is a clear way to describe what someone does.

Pronunciation of Third-Person Endings

In English, when we talk about what one person does (he, she, it), we add -s or -es to the verb. But the pronunciation changes depending on the verb!

/z/ sound
plays, supports, defends, connects
/ɪz/ sound
teaches, uses, researches
/s/ sound
helps, works, speaks
