💡 Teacher's Explanation

⏰The Key Difference

Sentence 1 (Habit/Routine): Your action is something you do regularly - it's part of your life. The tense we use is Simple Present. ✨

Sentence 2 (Right Now): Your action is happening AT THIS MOMENT - it's happening now while you read this! The tense we use is Present Continuous. 🎯

📚 Formula Boxes

📌SIMPLE PRESENT Formula
Subject + Base Verb (+ s/es for 3rd person singular)
✅ I play football.
✅ She plays tennis.
✅ They eat breakfast.
⏳PRESENT CONTINUOUS Formula
Subject + (am/is/are) + Base Verb + -ing
✅ I am playing football.
✅ She is playing tennis.
✅ They are eating breakfast.

🎨 Color-Coded Examples

SIMPLE PRESENT 📌
  • I walk to school every day
  • She drinks tea in the morning
  • They study English twice a week
  • The sun rises in the east
PRESENT CONTINUOUS ⏳
  • I am walking home now
  • She is drinking tea right now
  • They are studying English at this moment
  • The sun is rising right now
