Diogenes

c. 412-323 BC · Sinope (now in Turkey)

The Cynic who lived in a large clay jar in Athens and laughed at money, manners and power.

A life in a few steps

  1. c. 412 BCBorn in Sinope, on the Black Sea coast.
  2. Forced to leave after a scandal about the city's coins, he moves to Athens.
  3. He lives in a large clay jar and owns almost nothing.
  4. c. 323 BCDies in Corinth.

Why Diogenes still matters

He showed that people need very little to be free, and he was not afraid to tell powerful people the truth.
