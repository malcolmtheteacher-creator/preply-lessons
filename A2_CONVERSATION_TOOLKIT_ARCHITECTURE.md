# A2 Conversation Toolkit
## Architecture Document

---

## The Problem

The Speaking Pathway teaches **how to speak** (strategies, fluency, interaction skills).
The General English Pathway teaches **grammar** — but it's pitched at B1–C1.
The Quick Fix grammar files are reactive one-offs, not a systematic path.

**What's missing:** A systematic way for A2 students to build the grammar structures and vocabulary they need for conversation, delivered through casual, talk-heavy lessons that feel like chatting — not studying.

---

## The Solution: Grammar & Vocabulary Through Conversation

Each lesson has:
- A **conversation topic** the student actually wants to talk about
- A **grammar structure** that emerges naturally from the topic
- **10–15 target vocabulary items** organised by the topic
- A **game or activity** that forces production of the grammar + vocab
- **70% student talking time** (same principle as the Speaking Pathway)

The student thinks: "We talked about food today, that was fun."
The teacher knows: countable/uncountable nouns, some/any, much/many, and 12 food vocabulary items were systematically practised.

## Proposed Lessons: A2 Conversation Toolkit (25 lessons)

### Design Principles

1. **Topic-first, grammar-second:** The topic drives the lesson. Grammar emerges from the conversation, never the other way round.
2. **Vocabulary load: 10–15 items per lesson**, with at least 5 recycled in later lessons.
3. **Each lesson includes a mini-game or activity** — these students want to TALK and PLAY, not study.
4. **70% speaking time** maintained throughout.
5. **Can be used standalone or interleaved** with Speaking Pathway lessons.
6. **4th Wall Rule applies** — no grammar labels, no teacher notes, no "today we're learning present simple."

### Lesson Format (5-Tab Structure)

| Tab | Name | Time | Purpose |
|-----|------|------|---------|
| 1 | **Warm-Up Chat** | 5 min | Casual question + vocab preview (images/matching) |
| 2 | **The Conversation** | 10 min | Guided discussion on the topic; grammar patterns emerge naturally through prompts |
| 3 | **Word Power** | 8 min | Vocabulary building activity (matching, categorising, gap-fill, picture-based) |
| 4 | **The Game** | 12 min | Activity that forces production of target grammar + vocab |
| 5 | **Free Talk + Recall** | 10 min | Open conversation on topic + recall from previous CT lessons |

### The 25 Lessons

#### Block 1: The Basics (CT 01–08)
*Grammar: present simple, present continuous, question formation*

| # | Topic | Grammar Focus | Vocab Set | Game/Activity |
|---|-------|---------------|-----------|---------------|
| CT-01 | **My Typical Day** | Present simple (routine) + adverbs of frequency | Daily routine verbs (wake up, commute, grab lunch...) | "My Perfect Day vs My Real Day" — describe both, spot differences |
| CT-02 | **What's Happening Right Now?** | Present continuous (now) | Actions & activities (scrolling, cooking, waiting...) | "Describe the Photo" — timed photo descriptions using present continuous |
| CT-03 | **Hobbies & Free Time** | Present simple (likes/habits) + like/love/hate + gerund | Hobbies & interests (hiking, binge-watching, DIY...) | "Find Someone Who..." adapted for 1-to-1: guess teacher's answers, compare |
| CT-04 | **My Job / My Studies** | Present simple vs continuous | Work/study vocab (deadline, shift, overtime, assignment...) | "A Day in the Life" — role-play someone else's job for 2 min |
| CT-05 | **REVIEW + GAME** | Present simple vs continuous recap | Recycled from CT 01–04 | "Two Timelines" — describe your routine AND what you're doing differently this week |
| CT-06 | **Eating Out** | Questions: Do you...? / Are you...? / What do you...? | Food, drink, restaurant vocab (starter, bill, tip, spicy...) | "The Restaurant Game" — order, complain, send food back (role-play) |
| CT-07 | **My Neighbourhood** | There is/are + some/any + prepositions of place | Places & location (bakery, roundabout, pedestrian zone...) | "Draw My Neighbourhood" — describe while partner draws (or imagines) |
| CT-08 | **Weekend Plans** | Going to (future plans) | Weekend activities (have a lie-in, catch up with, chill out...) | "The Weekend Swap" — plan each other's ideal weekend |

## Integration with Existing Resources

### How It Connects to the Speaking Pathway

The CT lessons give students the **language building blocks**. The SP lessons give them the **speaking strategies**. Together:

```
Week 1: CT-01 (Daily Routine / Present Simple)  →  SP-01 (Buying Time)
Week 2: CT-02 (Present Continuous)               →  SP-02 (Describing Photos)  →  Game
Week 3: CT-03 (Hobbies / like + gerund)          →  SP-03 (Simple Opinions)
Week 4: CT-04 (Work / Pres. Simple vs Cont.)     →  SP-04 (Telling Stories)    →  Game
Week 5: CT-05 (Review Game)                      →  SP-05 (Review)
...
```

### How It Connects to Quick Fix Grammar Files

The qf_grammar files become **repair tools**. If a student struggles with articles during CT-07 (My Neighbourhood), the teacher can assign qf_grammar_articles.html as homework. The CT lesson does the teaching; the QF does the fixing.

### How It Connects to the Game Room

The 3 existing A2 games slot in as bonus sessions between CT/SP blocks. New A2 games could be built to match CT topics (see below).

## For the Casual Student: The Recommended Path

For a student who "just wants to talk" and does 1–2 lessons per week:

1. **Primary:** CT lessons in order (conversation + grammar + vocab)
2. **Secondary:** SP lessons interleaved (speaking strategies)
3. **Fun sessions:** Game Room (A2 games, including new ones)
4. **Homework:** QF grammar files when errors need fixing

This gives them structure they don't even notice, vocabulary that grows systematically, grammar that sticks because it came from real conversation, and the casual chatty vibe they want.
