Three principles that run through the whole pathway

  1. Your own words. If you can't paraphrase a claim, you don't yet understand it. This applies to notes, to writing, to AI-assisted work.
  2. One idea, one note, one paragraph. Atomicity beats density. Findability beats completeness.
  3. Hedge appropriately. PhD-level writing rarely makes flat assertions. The language of likely, suggests, tends to, in most cases, on the whole is what separates research writing from undergraduate writing.
