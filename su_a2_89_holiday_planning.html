Planning a Holiday

A2 Level · Travel

Holiday Dreams

Let's talk about holidays and travel. Answer these questions:

Question 1: Where did you go on your last holiday?

You can say:

"I went to Spain last summer. It was beautiful."

"I went to the beach. I stayed with my family."

"I haven't had a holiday. I stayed at home."

Question 2: Do you prefer beach or mountains?

You can say:

"I prefer the beach. It's hot and I like swimming."

"I like mountains. They are quiet and peaceful."

"I like both. It depends on the season."

Question 3: Do you like planning holidays?

You can say:

"Yes, I love planning. It's exciting!"

"It's OK. I like it, but it takes time."

"Not really. It's stressful. Too many decisions."

Travel Words

Resort A big hotel with lots of activities and fun
Currency The money of a country. Like euros or dollars

Holiday Planning Checklist

Things to do before you go:

Check your passport. It must be valid.
Book your flight and accommodation
Check the weather and pack clothes
Buy travel insurance
Check currency and money exchange
Buy a guidebook or read about the place online
Tell your bank you are travelling
Present Your Plan: 3:00

Task: Describe your holiday plan. Where are you going? What's your accommodation? What activities will you do? How many days will you stay?

Holiday Discussion

Let's talk about holidays and travel experiences.

1. What was your favourite holiday memory?

Tell me where you went, who you were with, and what you did.

2. Do you prefer travelling alone or with other people? Why?

With family? With friends? Alone? What are the advantages and disadvantages?

3. How do you like to travel? By plane, train, car, or bus?

Which is more comfortable? Which is cheaper?

4. What's the best time of year for a holiday?

Summer? Autumn? Winter? Spring? When do you go on holiday?

5. Do you like relaxing or active holidays?

Sitting on a beach or visiting museums and hiking?

Holiday Tips

Smart travel tips:

Book early for cheaper flights
Pack light. You don't need everything
Make a list of places to visit before you go
Try local restaurants, not just tourist places
Take photos and videos to remember
Keep important documents in a safe place
