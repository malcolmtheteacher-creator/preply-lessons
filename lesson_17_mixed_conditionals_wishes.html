🔍 Discover: Wishes & Mixed Conditionals

Part 1: "I WISH" - Three Types

Type Structure Example
Present Wish I wish + past simple "I wish I spoke French." (but I don't)
Past Regret I wish + past perfect "I wish I had studied harder." (but I didn't)
Future/Annoying I wish + would "I wish it would stop raining." (but it won't)

🔎 Click to Discover the Pattern

"I wish I had more free time."

👆 Click to see the analysis

Type: Present wish
Reality: I don't have much free time now
Grammar: wish + past simple (had)
Key insight: We use PAST tense to talk about NOW!
"I wish I had listened to my parents' advice."

👆 Click to see the analysis

Type: Past regret
Reality: I didn't listen to their advice
Grammar: wish + past perfect (had listened)
Key insight: We use PAST PERFECT to talk about past regrets!
"I wish you would stop making noise!"

👆 Click to see the analysis

Type: Wish about annoying situations
Reality: You're making noise and I'm frustrated
Grammar: wish + would + verb
Key insight: Often used when we're annoyed or frustrated!

Part 2: Mixed Conditionals - Connecting Past & Present

Type 1: Past situation → Present result
If + past perfect, ... would + verb
"If I had studied medicine, I would be a doctor now."
Type 2: Present situation → Past result
If + past simple, ... would have + past participle
"If I were taller, I would have played basketball."
"If I had saved money when I was young, I would own a house now."

👆 Click to see the analysis

Type: Mixed conditional (past → present)
Past situation: I didn't save money
Present result: I don't own a house
Key insight: A past action affects my present situation!
"If I weren't so shy, I would have asked her to dance."

👆 Click to see the analysis

Type: Mixed conditional (present → past)
Present situation: I am shy (always)
Past result: I didn't ask her to dance
Key insight: My personality affected a past decision!

🎯 Practice: Building Wishes & Conditionals

Exercise 1: Choose the Correct Form

Situation: You can't play the guitar, but you really want to.

Complete: "I wish I ___________"

could play the guitar
can play the guitar
will play the guitar
Situation: You didn't study for yesterday's exam and failed it.

Complete: "I wish I ___________"

studied harder
had studied harder
would study harder
Situation: You moved abroad when you were young. Now you speak the language perfectly.

Complete: "If I ___________, I wouldn't speak this language so well."

didn't move abroad
hadn't moved abroad
wouldn't move abroad
Situation: Your neighbor plays loud music every night. It's really annoying!

Complete: "I wish my neighbor ___________"

stopped playing loud music
would stop playing loud music
had stopped playing loud music

📚 Lesson Review

🎯 Key Grammar: "I Wish"

Present: I wish + past simple
"I wish I knew the answer."
Past regret: I wish + past perfect
"I wish I had studied more."
Annoying things: I wish + would
"I wish you would listen!"

🔄 Key Grammar: Mixed Conditionals

Past → Present:
"If I had learned coding, I would be a developer now."
Present → Past:
"If I weren't so lazy, I would have finished it."

💬 Useful Expressions

"If only I had known..."
"I really regret not..."
"Looking back, I should have..."
"If I could do it again, I would..."

📝 Common Mistakes

❌ "I wish I can fly."
✅ "I wish I could fly."
❌ "I wish I studied harder." (past)
✅ "I wish I had studied harder."
