Vocabulary: Key Terms

Click each card to flip and reveal the definition. Try to remember each word before flipping.

icon
A famous, admired person; someone who is widely respected and followed as a role model.
favourite
The athlete or team expected to win a competition; the one who is predicted to succeed.
underdog
A team or athlete nobody expects to win; someone in a weaker position with little chance of victory.
against all odds
When something unexpected happens despite the circumstances seeming impossible; success despite difficulties.
gutted
Really disappointed; extremely upset about a loss or failure. A strong emotional reaction to bad news.
idolise
To really admire someone; to regard someone as perfect or to make them your ideal. (British spelling)

Discussion Questions

Explore these questions to deepen your thinking about sports and athletes.

Do you prefer winter or summer sports? What's the appeal of each for you?

Tell me about an athlete who succeeded against all odds. What made their story inspiring?

Why do you think some people stop playing sports they once loved? What would motivate someone to continue?

What does it mean when people idolise athletes? Is it always positive?

For many athletes, is the Olympic Games the peak of their career? Does it represent the ultimate achievement in sport?

Can you think of a time when the underdog became the favourite? How did that happen?

How do you feel when your favourite team loses unexpectedly? What's the difference between being disappointed and being gutted?
