Hot Takes: Steelman Showdown

Build the strongest opposite. Find the real truth.

What is a steelman?

A strawman is the weakest, easiest-to-knock-down version of an argument. A steelman is the strongest, smartest, most generous version. Today, you're going to build steelmen — for positions you don't agree with.

The format (every round)

You'll see a hot take. You'll do three things:

  • Step 1 — Steelman the opposite (90s): Argue the strongest possible case against the take.
  • Step 2 — Spot your strawman (60s): Catch yourself. What's the weak version of your side that you might lazily reach for?
  • Step 3 — Synthesise (90s): Where does the truth actually sit? What does each side get right?

Three steelman moves

1. Generous reading: "The strongest version of this argument is..."

2. Concede a real point: "What this side genuinely gets right is..."

3. Identify the value: "Underneath the argument is a value worth taking seriously: ..."
