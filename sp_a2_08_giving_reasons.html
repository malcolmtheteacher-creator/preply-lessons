Giving Reasons

Beyond "because" - make your opinions convincing

💭 Opinion & Argument
📖 Do Prep First →

The Spark: Controversial Questions

Some questions have no "right" answer. What matters is HOW you explain your thinking.

📱 Should children have smartphones?
🎓 Is university worth the cost?
🏠 Should adult children live with parents?

Reason Strength Meter

Not all reasons are equally convincing:

❌ Weak 😐 Okay ✅ Strong

Weak: "I think children should have phones because... it's good."

Okay: "I think children should have phones because they need to contact their parents."

Strong: "I think children should have phones because of safety. For example, if there's an emergency, they can call for help immediately. This means parents feel more relaxed too."

Today you'll learn: 5 ways to make your reasons stronger and more convincing.

Micro-Skill: 5 Reason Strengtheners

Click each technique to see how it makes reasons more powerful:

For example, ... / For instance, ... Click to expand

Give a concrete example to support your point:

Without: "Social media is bad for teenagers."

With: "Social media is bad for teenagers. For example, many studies show it increases anxiety and affects sleep."

Note: "For instance" is slightly more formal than "For example".

🎤 NOW YOU TRY

Opinion: "Exercise is important for health." Add "For example,..."

This means (that)... Click to expand

Show the consequence or result of your reason:

Without: "Learning a language takes a long time."

With: "Learning a language takes a long time. This means you need to be patient and practice every day."

Power: Connects your reason to a real-world impact!

🎤 NOW YOU TRY

Opinion: "Working from home saves commute time." Add "This means..."

The main reason is (that)... Click to expand

Highlight your strongest argument:

"I prefer renting to buying. The main reason is that it gives me flexibility to move for work."

Variations:

  • "The biggest reason is..."
  • "The most important thing is..."
  • "What matters most is..."

🎤 NOW YOU TRY

Why do you like your favorite hobby? Use "The main reason is..."

Another reason is (that)... Click to expand

Add additional supporting points:

"I think we should ban plastic bags because they harm the environment. Another reason is that there are many alternatives available now, like reusable bags."

Variations:

  • "Besides that,..."
  • "What's more,..."
  • "On top of that,..."

🎤 NOW YOU TRY

Give 2 reasons why learning English is useful. Use "Another reason is..."

That's why... / So that's why... Click to expand

Conclude by connecting back to your opinion:

"Electric cars are better for the environment and cheaper to run in the long term. That's why I think governments should encourage people to buy them."

Perfect for: Finishing a longer explanation with a clear conclusion.

🎤 NOW YOU TRY

Give reasons why sleep is important, then conclude with "That's why..."

Guided Practice: Build Strong Arguments

For each opinion, add reasons using the structure given. Say it OUT LOUD!

Opinion: "Everyone should learn to cook basic meals."

Add: "The main reason is..." + "For example,..." + "This means..."

Opinion: "People spend too much time on their phones."

Add: "For instance,..." + "Another reason is..." + "That's why..."

Opinion: "Living in a big city is better than living in the countryside."

Add: "The biggest reason is..." + "What's more,..." + "This means..."

The Full Formula

Opinion + Main reason + Example + Another reason + Conclusion

"I think... The main reason is... For example,... Another reason is... That's why..."

🎤 Now try the FULL formula on this topic:

"Should schools teach financial skills like budgeting?"

Speak for at least 45 seconds using all the phrases!
