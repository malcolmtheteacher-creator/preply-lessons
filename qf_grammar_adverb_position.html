⏰ Adverb Position (Frequency)

Place frequency adverbs correctly | B1 Level

Let's see what you notice...

Read this sentence. What feels wrong?

I go always to the gym.

What's the correction?

✓ Correction

I always go to the gym.

📌 The Rule

Frequency adverbs (always, usually, often, sometimes, never) go BEFORE the main verb.

I always go (NOT "I go always")

She never eats meat (NOT "She eats never")

EXCEPTION: After the verb "be" (is, are, am, was, were), put the adverb AFTER: "She is always late", "They are never happy"

📚 The Rule (Dual Coding)

✅ BEFORE Main Verb

👉 ⏰ 🎬

I ALWAYS go

She USUALLY eats

They NEVER work

Adverb before main verb

✅ AFTER "Be"

be ⏰ ✨

She IS ALWAYS late

They ARE NEVER happy

I AM OFTEN tired

Adverb after "be" verb

💡 Bonus: With auxiliaries (have, will, etc.): "I have always liked ice cream." The adverb goes after the first auxiliary.
