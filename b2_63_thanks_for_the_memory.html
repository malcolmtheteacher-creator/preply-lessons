Verb Patterns

Focus on these common verb pattern structures:

Verb + Infinitive (with "to")

advise, want, suggest + to + base verb

Example: "The doctor advised you to take vitamins."

Verb + -ing (Gerund)

keep, suggest + -ing verb

Example: "Is there anything you keep forgetting to do?"

Verb + Object + Infinitive

let, help + object + base verb

Example: "Do you usually let your colleagues help you?"

Verb + -ing or Infinitive

imagine can use either form with slight meaning differences

Example: "Where do you imagine working in 10 years?"

Why this matters: Mastering these patterns helps you express intentions, suggestions, and experiences more naturally in conversation.

The Memory Palace Technique

A time-tested strategy for remembering information:

How It Works

Imagine a familiar place you know well (your home, school, or a favourite park). Mentally place the items or words you want to remember in different spots within that space.

Example

Your home:

  • Front door: a fruit bowl full of grapes
  • Living room: butter sitting on the coffee table
  • Kitchen: someone peeling potatoes

As you mentally walk through each area, you associate items with locations. This technique can help you remember up to 100 words at a time!

Your Task

Step 1: Choose a familiar place (home, school, park, workplace)

Step 2: Select 5-8 vocabulary words from today's lesson

Step 3: Create vivid mental images linking each word to a specific location

Step 4: Describe your memory journey aloud

Reflection Questions

Do you have other techniques for memorising words?

Think about: Flashcards, repetition, songs, grouping by category, or creating sentences. What works best for you?
