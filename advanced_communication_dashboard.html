The Art of Being Understood

Five lessons in saying difficult things clearly, precisely and convincingly — for people who know too much to be easily understood.

Fluency is not the finish line. At the top of the language, the real difficulty is not grammar or vocabulary — it is the gap between what an expert knows and what an audience can follow. This short series is about closing that gap on purpose: a set of advanced communication skills for anyone who has to explain, persuade, or perform in English at a high level.

Each lesson takes one big idea about communication — the curse of knowledge, the engineering of an analogy, the precise calibration of certainty, the architecture of persuasion, the art of reading a room — and turns it into concrete, learnable language: the moves, the phrases, and the practice. They build in order, but each one stands alone.
