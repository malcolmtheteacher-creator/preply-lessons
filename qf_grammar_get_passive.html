🔄 Get vs Be — Passive Structures

📌 The Rule

Never mix "was" and "get" together. Choose one:

  • GET + past participle = change, often informal or unexpected. "I got confused. She got fired. The window got broken."
  • BE + past participle = state or formal passive. "I was confused. She was promoted. The window was broken."

The key difference:

  • GET = something HAPPENS to you (dynamic, change)
  • BE = a STATE or FORMAL description (passive voice)

Examples:

  • I got lost in the city. ✓ (NOT: was get lost)
  • She got promoted last week. ✓ (NOT: was get promoted)
  • The report was completed by the team. ✓ (formal passive)
