II. Your Brain on Love

Anthropologist Helen Fisher spent 30 years scanning people's brains. She found love isn't an emotion — it's a drive, like hunger. And it comes in three stages:

🔥
LUST
Sex hormones. Physical pull. Gets you looking.
🥰
ATTRACTION
Dopamine rush. Obsession. Can't stop thinking about them. 1–3 years.
🤝
ATTACHMENT
Oxytocin. Deep bonding. Comfort. Can last a lifetime.

Over to you:

In your own words, describe the three stages. Try using: "First... then... eventually..."

The danger zone

Many relationships die between Stage 2 and Stage 3. The dopamine fades. People think they've "fallen out of love." But Fisher says they've just moved to a different kind of love.

What do you think?

Have you ever seen this happen — in your own life or someone else's? Is the "boring" stage actually deeper love, or is it just... boring?

III. The Big Questions

Two more pieces of research, then we talk.

5 : 1
Gottman's "Magic Ratio"
Psychologist John Gottman predicted divorce with 94% accuracy. His rule: couples need at least 5 positive moments for every 1 negative. Below that? Almost always fails.
36 ?s
Aron's 36 Questions
Arthur Aron gave strangers 36 increasingly personal questions + 4 minutes of eye contact. Several pairs fell in love. One married 6 months later. Vulnerability = intimacy.

IV. Aron's 36 Questions

In 1997, psychologist Arthur Aron found that answering increasingly personal questions with a stranger could create real closeness in under an hour. Here are some of the best ones. Take turns. Answer honestly.

How it works

The original experiment had three sets — each more personal than the last. The magic isn't in any single question. It's in the pattern: small openness leads to bigger openness.

♥ ♥ ♥

Set 1 — Getting Started

🌟

Q1

If you could invite anyone in the world to dinner, living or dead, who would you choose?

🌐

Q2

Would you like to be famous? In what way?

📞

Q3

Before making a phone call, do you ever rehearse what you're going to say? Why?

🌞

Q4

What would make a "perfect" day for you?

🎤

Q5

When did you last sing to yourself? To someone else?

⏳

Q6
