Friendly Chat at Work

B1 Level · Keep a relaxed, friendly conversation going

Warm-up

Today we're working on friendly conversation so you can keep a relaxed conversation going with someone at work.
Have a go, there are no wrong answers. When you chat with a colleague before or after a meeting, what do you usually talk about? Tell me three topics.

Start the conversation

Three easy ways to begin a friendly chat. First say your own question, then click to see another way to say it.

Starter 1: Ask about the journey or the day
"How was your journey?"
"How's your day been so far?"
"How's your week going?"
Starter 2: Ask about the weekend or plans
"Did you have a good weekend?"
"Do you have any plans for the holidays?"
"Did you do anything nice at the weekend?"
Starter 3: Ask about work or life
"How's the project going?"
"Is it a busy time for you?"
"What have you been working on lately?"

Your turn

Imagine we are colleagues and we haven't spoken for a few weeks. Start a conversation with me, using a different starter each time. Do it three times.
Now I'll ask you the questions. Answer in two or three sentences, not one word.

Quick check: Someone asks, "Did you have a good weekend?" Which answer keeps the conversation going best?

Keep it going

The secret is the follow-up question. Listen to the answer, then ask about one detail.

Someone says…ReactFollow up
"I went to the mountains at the weekend.""Oh, nice!""Do you go often?"
"I'm learning to cook.""That sounds fun.""What's the first thing you learned to make?"

Follow-up game

I'll read the sentence. You react and ask a follow-up question. Then click to compare.

"I've just moved to a new flat."
"Oh, congratulations! Do you like the area?"
"I'm really tired. The week has been busy."
"I know the feeling. What's been keeping you busy?"
"I love long train journeys."
"Really? What do you do on the train?"
"We have two children."
"How lovely! How old are they?"
"I'm going to Italy next month."
"Lucky you! Which part are you visiting?"

Talk

Choose a topic card. We'll have a real conversation, and you can ask me questions too.

🏖️WeekendWhat did you do last weekend? What do you usually do?
🍜FoodWhat's your favourite dish? Where is the best place to eat near you?
✈️TravelWhere have you been recently? Where would you like to go next?
🎬Free timeWhat are you watching or reading at the moment?
🏙️Where you liveWhat do you like about your town? What would you change?
💼Work weekWhat's your week like? What is the best part of your day?
05:00

Talk about your chosen topic for the whole time. Ask at least three follow-up questions. Try not to stop.
Pick a second card and do it again. This time start the conversation yourself.

Coffee break

A role-play. Pick one situation. You are two colleagues who know each other a little but don't work together every day. The conversation should last about three minutes.

Situation A: the kitchen

You are both making coffee on a busy morning.

Situation B: before a meeting

You arrive five minutes early. A senior colleague is already in the room.

Situation C: the lift

You share a lift with a colleague. You have thirty seconds. Then you meet again at lunch.

Situation D: after work

The team is having a drink after work. You sit next to a colleague from another office.

Start → React → Add → Ask more → Say goodbye nicely

If you get stuck

I can't find the word
"It's a kind of..."
"I mean the thing that..."
"How do you say ... in English?"
I need a second to think
"Let me think..."
"That's a good question."
"Well, ..."
Ending the conversation
"It was really nice talking to you."
"I'd better get back to work. See you later!"
"Let's catch up again tomorrow."
Do the role-play again with a different situation. Use two expressions from the "stuck" list.
