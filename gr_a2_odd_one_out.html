Odd One Out

Which one is different? Explain your choice!

Warm-Up

Today's goal: Explain differences and give reasons for your choices.

Which one is different? Click a word to pick it, then click the button to see the answer.

Example 1:
apple
banana
car
orange
Common answer: Car — it's not a fruit. The others are all fruits you can eat.

Other possible answer: Orange could be different because it's also a colour!
Example 2:
Monday
Friday
December
Wednesday
Common answer: December — it's a month, not a day of the week. The others are all days.
Remember: There isn't always ONE right answer. The important thing is your reason!
SPEAK: "Pick one of the words from the examples and explain why it's different."

Useful Language

Use these phrases to explain your choices:

"I think _____ is different because..."
"The others are all..."
"_____ doesn't belong because..."
"You could also say _____ because..."
"I chose _____ because it's the only one that..."
Practice: Try using each phrase in a sentence about the warm-up words. For example:
  • "I think car is different because it's not a fruit."
  • "The others are all days of the week."
  • "December doesn't belong because it's a month."
  • "You could also say orange because it's a colour."
  • "I chose car because it's the only one that has wheels."

Round 2 — Tricky Ones

These have multiple good answers! The discussion is the most important part.

Challenge: These are harder because there's more than one correct answer. Think carefully!

Set 1

love
friendship
family
money
Possible answer 1: Money — it's not an emotion or relationship.

Possible answer 2: Love — it's more abstract and complicated than the other relationships.

Set 2

phone
book
newspaper
magazine
Possible answer 1: Phone — it's electronic, the others are paper.

Possible answer 2: Book — it's not about news, the others give you information about events.

Set 3

rain
snow
wind
cloud
Possible answer 1: Cloud — it's not weather you feel, it's something you see in the sky.

Possible answer 2: Wind — it's air, not water like rain and snow.

Set 4

breakfast
lunch
snack
dinner
Common answer: Snack — it's not a main meal, it's something small you eat between meals.

Other possible answer: Breakfast — it's the first meal, and some people eat it at different times.

Set 5

car
house
holiday
clothes
Possible answer 1: Holiday — you can't touch it, it's an experience, not an object.

Possible answer 2: House — it's the only place you live in; the others are things you use or do.

Set 6

English
maths
history
homework
Common answer: Homework — it's not a school subject, it's work you do at home.

Other possible answer: English — it's a language; the others are about facts and events.
SPEAK: "Which set did we disagree on? Make your case — why did you choose that answer?"
