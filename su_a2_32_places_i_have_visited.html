Places I Have Visited

A2 Level English • Storytelling

Objective

Practise talking about places you've visited. Learn adjectives and their opposites to describe locations. Write and discuss hotel reviews.

Let's Start

Think about these questions:

What is your favourite city or town to visit?

What do you like about it?

Do you ever read hotel or restaurant reviews on websites?

Do you ever write reviews?

Do you believe reviews? How do they influence your decisions?

Hotel Reviews

Fill in the gaps with adjectives from the word list. Click each blank to see the answer.

The hotel is in a quiet area, which was a bit disappointing. It is convenient the underground station so everything is accessible. It is a short walk to the national museum and it's only minutes away from the main square. The staff were polite and the rooms were clean. The breakfast was wonderful but the views from the restaurant were breathtaking. Overall, I would recommend this hotel.

SPEAK:

Read the review out loud. Did you use similar words to describe your experience?
