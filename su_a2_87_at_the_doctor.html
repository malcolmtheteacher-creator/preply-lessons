At the Doctor

A2 Level · Health

Feeling Unwell

Let's talk about going to the doctor. Answer these questions:

Question 1: When was the last time you went to the doctor?

You can say:

"I went to the doctor last month. I had a cold."

"I went two weeks ago. I had a sore throat."

"I don't remember. Maybe three months ago."

Question 2: Do you like going to the doctor? Why or why not?

You can say:

"Yes, I like it because the doctor helps me feel better."

"No, I don't like it. I'm nervous. But it's important."

"Not really. I don't like waiting. But I go when I'm sick."

Question 3: What do you do when you feel ill?

You can say:

"I stay at home and rest. I drink water and sleep."

"I go to the doctor if it's serious."

"I take medicine and eat healthy food."

The Doctor's Visit

Lucia's Story

Last week, Lucia had a bad cold. She had a headache, a sore throat, and a fever. She called the doctor and made an appointment. On Monday, she went to the clinic at 10 o'clock.

The receptionist gave her a form. Lucia wrote her name and address. Then she waited 15 minutes. The nurse took her temperature. It was 38 degrees.

The doctor asked, "How do you feel?" Lucia said, "My throat hurts. I have a headache and I'm very tired." The doctor looked at her throat and listened to her chest. "You have a cold," he said. "Take these tablets. Drink lots of water. Rest at home."

Lucia got a prescription. She took it to the pharmacy. She bought the medicine for 12 euros. Now she feels much better.

Comprehension Questions

1. What was wrong with Lucia?

She had a bad cold. She had a headache, a sore throat, and a fever.

2. What time was her appointment?

Her appointment was at 10 o'clock on Monday.

3. What was her temperature?

Her temperature was 38 degrees.

4. What did the doctor tell her to do?

The doctor told her to take tablets, drink lots of water, and rest at home.

Role-Play Practice

Situation: You are at the doctor. You are not feeling well.

Your task: Describe your problem to the doctor. Say what hurts, how long you've had it, and what you've done to feel better.

Use these phrases:

"I have a [headache/sore throat/sore stomach]."

"I feel [sick/tired/hot]."

"I've had it since [yesterday/two days ago]."

Staying Healthy

Let's talk about staying healthy and what you do when you feel ill.

Speaking Task: 3:00

Discussion Questions

1. What do you do to stay healthy?

Talk about exercise, food, sleep, and other healthy habits.

2. What is the first thing you do when you feel ill?

Do you see a doctor? Do you rest? Do you take medicine?

3. How often do you go to the doctor?

For check-ups? Only when you're sick? Why?

4. What are some natural ways to feel better when you have a cold?

Water, tea, rest, vitamins, honey, etc.

Good Health Habits

Check the habits you do regularly:

I eat healthy food (fruits and vegetables)
I exercise (walk, run, play sports)
I get 7-8 hours of sleep
I drink a lot of water
I wash my hands before eating
I don't smoke
