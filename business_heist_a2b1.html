Phase 1: Gear Up
What do you need to bring?
Before you go to the building, you need to pack your bag. Think about what you will need! What tools will help you get into the building? What will help you stay hidden?
USE SEQUENCE WORDS 📝
Use words like first, then, next, after that, finally to explain what you will do.
EXAMPLE:
"I have a keycard."
"First, I will use my special keycard to open the door. Then I will walk inside quietly."
DESCRIBE YOUR ITEMS 🎨
Don't just name things — describe them. What colour is it? Is it big or small? Why is it useful?
EXAMPLE:
"I have a torch."
"I have a small black torch. It is very quiet and the light is not too bright."

Phase 2: Break In
How do you get inside?
You are outside the PetMax building. It is late at night. The streets are empty and dark.

There are three ways to get in: the back door, the window on the second floor, or the underground car park.

You must be very quiet. If anyone hears you, you will be caught!
USE SHORT SENTENCES 😰
When something exciting happens, use short sentences. This makes the reader feel excited too!
EXAMPLE:
"I went through the door."
"I opened the door slowly. It made a noise. I stopped. I waited. Silence. I went inside."
DESCRIBE FEELINGS 👁️
Don't just say "I was scared." Describe what your body does when you are scared!
EXAMPLE:
"I was nervous."
"My hands were shaking. My heart was beating very fast."

Phase 3: The Grab
The moment of truth
You're in the CEO's office. It's dark and quiet. The computer is on the desk.

And there it is. On the wall behind the desk. A safe. The secret recipe is inside.

The safe has a number code and a motion sensor nearby. If you move too fast, an alarm will go off.

This is the most important part. Be very careful. One mistake and it's over.
STEP BY STEP 🔬
Describe each step one at a time. Write what you do first, then second, then third. Be very clear.
EXAMPLE:
"I opened the safe and took the recipe."
"First, I walked slowly to the safe. Then I put my hand on the dial. Carefully, I turned it to the right number. I heard a click. The door opened."
USE YOUR SENSES ✨
What do you see, hear, and feel? Is it dark? Cold? Can you hear your heart?
EXAMPLE:
"The paper was old and yellow. It felt soft in my hands. I could smell coffee and old books. My heart was beating fast."
