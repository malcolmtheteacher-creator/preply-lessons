Conversation at the Shop

Here are useful phrases you'll hear and use:

Common Shop Phrases

  • Shop Assistant: "Hello, can I help you?"
  • Customer: "I'm just looking, thanks." OR "Yes, I'd like some..."
  • Customer: "Can I have some of that special Italian balsamic vinegar?"
  • Shop Assistant: "How many would you like?" OR "How much would you like?"
  • Shop Assistant: "We haven't got any beef today, but we have some fresh lamb chops."
  • Customer: "How much is a kilo?"

Countable vs. Uncountable Nouns

Why This Matters

In English, we use different words depending on whether something is countable or uncountable:

Countable Nouns (plural forms, can be counted)

  • Many: "How many apples do you want?" / "I want many apples."
  • A few: "I need a few croissants." (small number)
  • Several: "I'll take several lamb chops."

Uncountable Nouns (cannot be easily counted, stay singular)

  • Much: "How much milk do you want?" / "I want much milk."
  • Some: "I'd like some butter and some cheese."
  • A little: "I need a little sugar." (small amount)

Key Rule: With countable nouns, use "many" and "a few". With uncountable nouns, use "much" and "a little" or "some".

Speaking Activity 1: Warm-Up Conversation

Discuss these questions with your partner:

  • When was the last time you went to a shop?
  • How often do you go shopping?
  • What kind of shopping do you enjoy? (for books, clothes, groceries, window shopping online)
  • What kind of shopping don't you like?

Target: Speak naturally, use past tense, and give reasons for your answers.

Speaking Activity 2: Shop Role Play - Customer

Scenario: You are at a specialty shop with a budget of £100. Your partner is the shop assistant.

Your task: Buy items for a dinner party for 6 people. Include:

  • Fruit or fresh vegetables
  • Meat or fish (enough for 6 people)
  • Specialty items (cheese, wine, chocolate)
  • Staples (bread, butter, oil)

Key phrases to use:

  • "Can I have some..."
  • "How much is...?"
  • "How many would you recommend?"
  • "That's too expensive for my budget."

Target: Use countable and uncountable quantifiers correctly in conversation.

Speaking Activity 3: Shop Role Play - Shop Assistant

Scenario: Your partner is the customer. You are the shop assistant at a specialty food shop.

Your task:

  • Greet customers warmly
  • Ask how many or how much they want
  • Suggest specialty items
  • Tell them prices (make them up)
  • Handle questions about availability

Sample phrases:

  • "Can I help you today?"
  • "We have some excellent Scottish steak on special offer."
  • "How many would you like?"
  • "That costs £15 per kilo."
  • "We haven't got any of that today, but we have..."

Speaking Activity 4: Budget Shopping Challenge

You have £50 to spend at a shop. Tell your partner:

  • What you are shopping for (a meal, a picnic, groceries for the week)
  • What items you need to buy
  • How much of each item you want (using quantifiers correctly)
  • Why you chose these items
  • Whether you stayed within your budget

Target: Use "I want many..." with countable nouns and "I want much..." with uncountable nouns.

Speaking Activity 5: Interview About Shopping Habits

Your partner will ask you about your shopping preferences. Be prepared to answer:

  • Where do you usually shop?
  • What specialty items do you like to buy?
  • Do you prefer supermarkets or specialty shops? Why?
  • What makes a good shopping experience for you?
  • Are there any products you find difficult to find?

Target: Speak for 2-3 minutes with good pronunciation and natural rhythm.
