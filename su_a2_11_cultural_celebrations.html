Let's Talk About Celebrations

Festivals and celebrations happen all around the world. They bring people together and show us the traditions that make different cultures special.

Useful Phrases for Describing Festivals

Use these phrases and patterns to describe celebrations and festivals.

It's a ___ event
Example: It's a religious event.
It's famous for ___
Example: It's famous for fireworks and music.
It has taken place since/for ___
Example: It has taken place for over 200 years.
On this day people ___
Example: On this day people wear colourful costumes.
People usually decorate ___ with ___
Example: People usually decorate streets with colorful lanterns.
There are ___
Example: There are exciting performances and parades.

Talk About Your Favourite Festival

Choose one festival that you like or know well. Prepare your ideas using these questions, then tell us about the festival. You have 3 minutes.

Preparation Questions

  1. Which festival are you going to talk about?
  2. How long has it been celebrated? (How many years or centuries?)
  3. Where does it happen? (Which country or city?)
  4. What happens in the morning, afternoon, and evening?
  5. How many people usually come to this festival?
  6. What do people wear, eat, or drink?
  7. What decorations, music, or performances are there?
  8. Why is it famous? What makes it special?
