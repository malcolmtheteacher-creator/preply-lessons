Food is more than fuel — it's culture, drama, creativity, and connection. English speakers use food in everyday language too. Idioms like "a tough nut to crack" and "go pear-shaped" reflect how food language shapes the way we talk about challenges, success, and life itself.

Your Stories

Pick 3 prompts. Tell a true or imaginative story using the idiom naturally.

1. A Tough Nut to Crack

Tell about a problem or person that was difficult to understand or solve. What was the challenge? How did you handle it?

2. The Apple of Your Eye

Who or what is very special to you? Why do they mean so much? What would you do for them?

3. Go Pear-Shaped

Tell about a time something went wrong. What was the plan? How did it fall apart? What happened next?

4. Have Egg on Your Face

Describe an embarrassing moment. What happened? How did it feel? Did you laugh about it later?

5. Big Cheese

Tell about someone important or powerful you know. What makes them influential? How do people treat them?

6. Take It with a Pinch of Salt

Describe a claim or story you weren't sure about. Why didn't you fully believe it? What did you do?

Your task: Plan a treatment for a new TV cookery series. It's your chance to pitch an idea producers would love.

A TV treatment is a short pitch document. It includes: a striking title, a 1-2 sentence logline that grabs attention, a 2-3 paragraph synopsis, and brief episode descriptions.
