🤫 Silent Letters

A2 • Italian/Spanish/Polish/Russian • Stop saying letters that aren't pronounced

🔍 Let's notice the silent letter problem

Listen to these common mistakes:

❌ "k-nife" (saying the k)"

Should be: "nife" (the K is SILENT)

❌ "lis-TEN-a" (saying the T)"

Should be: "LIS-uhn" (the T is SILENT, and no extra vowel!)

Why are there letters you don't pronounce? Isn't that confusing?

🎯 The Problem:

English has MANY silent letters — letters written but NOT pronounced. This is especially confusing for Italian, Spanish, Polish, and Russian speakers whose languages pronounce every letter.

Common silent letter patterns:

🔹 KN = just /n/ (knife, know, knee, knock)

🔹 WR = just /r/ (write, wrong, wrist, wrap)

🔹 GH = silent (light, night, thought, daughter)

🔹 T = silent sometimes (listen, castle, whistle, often)

🔹 MB = no /b/ (climb, bomb, lamb, thumb)

🛠️ Common silent letter patterns

🔤 KN- = /n/ (the K is SILENT)

At the beginning of a word: say only the /n/ sound.
knife (nife)
know (no)
knee (nee)
knock (nock)
knit (nit)

🔤 WR- = /r/ (the W is SILENT)

At the beginning of a word: say only the /r/ sound.
write (rite)
wrong (rong)
wrist (rist)
wrap (rap)
wreck (rek)

🔤 -MB = just /m/ (the B is SILENT at the end)

At the end of a word: don't pronounce the B.
climb (klime)
bomb (bom)
lamb (lam)
thumb (thum)
dunb (dum)

🔤 -GH = SILENT (often at the end or middle)

These letters make NO sound.
light (lite)
night (nite)
thought (thot)
daughter (dau-ter)
right (rite)

🔤 -T in the middle = sometimes SILENT

Often when T is between vowels or in unstressed syllables.
listen (LISS-uhn)
castle (CASS-uhl)
whistle (WHISS-uhl)
often (OFF-uhn)
fasten (FASS-uhn)

✏️ Guided practice — Say these words

Say these KN- words (JUST the /n/):

🔵 knife → NIFE (not "k-nife")

🔵 know → NO (not "k-no")

🔵 knee → NEE (not "k-nee")

Say these WR- words (JUST the /r/):

🔵 write → RITE (not "w-rite")

🔵 wrong → RONG (not "w-rong")

🔵 wrap → RAP (not "w-rap")

🚫 Common mistakes to AVOID

❌ k-nife instead of nife (pronouncing the K)

❌ lis-TEN-a instead of LIS-uhn (saying the T and adding vowel)

❌ clim-B instead of klime (pronouncing the B)

❌ li-GHT instead of lite (saying the GH)
