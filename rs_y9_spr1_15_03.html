Poverty and Wealth

Causes of Poverty

Structural inequality: unequal education, healthcare. Economic systems: wage inequality, unemployment, inadequate nets. Historical injustice: colonialism, slavery effects persist. Geographic: fewer resources. Individual: illness, disability. Systemic discrimination: racism, sexism.

Religious Teachings on Wealth

Christianity: "love of money root of evil"; wealth is test; not sinful if generous. Islam: permissible if honest and shared; wealth is trust. Sikhism: honest earning valued but wealth worthless without sharing. Judaism: tzedakah obligatory. Buddhism: greed is poison.

Liberation Theology and Fair Trade

Liberation theology: Jesus was liberator; God sides with oppressed; sin is structural. Oscar Romero: archbishop assassinated defending poor. Fair Trade: fair wages, safe conditions, direct relationships; justice in exchange, not charity. Limitation: individual help, not system change.

Can Religious Person Be Wealthy?

Jesus: "harder for rich to enter kingdom than camel through needle." Interpretation 1: wealth spiritually dangerous. Interpretation 2: okay if honest, used generously, doesn't replace faith. Interpretation 3: problem is inequality not wealth. Islamic finance: riba (interest) forbidden; profit-sharing fair; zakah ensures distribution.
