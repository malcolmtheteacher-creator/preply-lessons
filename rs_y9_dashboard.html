Year 9: Evaluating & Preparing for GCSE

"What do I think, and can I defend it?"

What Year 9 Is About

Year 9 is where everything comes together. You've encountered the world's major religions in Year 7 and compared traditions in Year 8. Now you'll tackle the biggest questions in philosophy, ethics, and theology — and learn to build structured arguments that defend your own position. This year develops the evaluation skills you'll need for GCSE, while exploring fascinating topics from the existence of God to the meaning of life. By the end of Year 9, you'll be ready to begin your GCSE Religious Studies course with confidence.

How This Course Works

  • One lesson per week — Each unit takes 6 weeks, with lesson materials, activities, and discussion prompts.
  • Evaluate and argue — Year 9 builds on Years 7 and 8 by developing your ability to evaluate competing claims, construct reasoned arguments, and defend your own position with evidence.
  • Philosophy and ethics — You'll tackle some of the biggest questions in human thought: Does God exist? Is religion compatible with science? What do we owe each other? What happens when we die?
  • GCSE preparation — The final unit introduces you to the AQA GCSE RS specification, exam technique, and the evaluation skills you'll need to excel.
  • Assessment is ongoing — Each unit ends with a task that checks your understanding: knowledge checks, extended writing, and evaluation essays.

Year 9 is your bridge to GCSE. The evaluation skills you develop this year — weighing evidence, considering multiple perspectives, and defending your position — will serve you throughout your academic career and beyond.
