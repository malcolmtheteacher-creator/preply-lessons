Different Treatments Explained

Read about these treatments. What do you think about each one?

Acupuncture
Alternative treatment — Chinese medicine

Very thin needles are placed on specific points on the body. Many people use it for pain relief, stress, and sleep problems. Some studies show it can help, but others say the results are not clear.

Herbal Products
Alternative treatment — natural medicines

Plants and herbs are used to make medicines. Examples include echinacea for colds and ginger for stomach problems. Many people find them helpful, but some contain no active ingredients. Be careful — some can cause side effects or react badly with drugs.

Homeopathy
Alternative treatment — diluted substances

Substances are diluted many times in water. Supporters believe it helps the body heal itself. Most doctors and scientists say it does not work better than a placebo (fake treatment).

Nutritionist Advice
Can be conventional or alternative

A nutritionist studies food and how it affects health. Good nutrition can prevent illness and improve health. Registered nutritionists have professional training. Some people claiming to be nutritionists may not have real qualifications.

Yoga
Alternative practice — exercise and relaxation

Physical poses (asanas) combined with breathing control and meditation. Studies show yoga can reduce stress, improve flexibility, and help with back pain. It is becoming more accepted by conventional medicine.

Meditation
Alternative practice — mental exercise

Focusing the mind on breathing and thoughts to relax and reduce stress. Scientific research shows it changes the brain and helps anxiety and depression. Many hospitals now teach meditation.
