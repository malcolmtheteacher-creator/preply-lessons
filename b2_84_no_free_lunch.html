About School Meals

Think about these questions before the discussion.

1. School Meals in Your Country

Do children have meals at school in your country? What are they like? Are they free?

2. Personal Experience

Have you ever had free school meals? At what age? How was that experience?

3. Your Opinion

Do you think free school meals are a good idea? Why or why not?

4. Health and Nutrition

How important is it for schools to provide healthy meals? What role should government play?

Debate Positions

Consider these arguments for and against free school meals.

Position 1: Universal Free Meals (For)

Argument: Giving free school meals to disadvantaged children could be seen as preferential treatment and might lead to bullying. To avoid this, everybody should receive free school lunches, regardless of their family situation.

Key points to consider:

  • Social equality and reducing stigma
  • Improved focus and academic performance
  • Better nutrition for all children
  • Reduced bullying based on socioeconomic status

Position 2: No Free Meals (Against)

Argument: It is not the job of government to feed people. School meals should not be free. People should provide for their own children, not rely on taxpayers.

Key points to consider:

  • Government responsibility and cost
  • Personal and family responsibility
  • Tax burden on society
  • Market-based solutions

Position 3: Health Benefits (For)

Argument: The health benefits of providing future generations with a healthy diet outweigh the costs. This is an investment in public health.

Key points to consider:

  • Long-term health outcomes
  • Prevention of obesity and disease
  • Cost savings in healthcare later
  • Development of healthy eating habits

Ideas for School Health Initiatives

Consider these proposals for improving health and fitness in schools:

  • Pre-school running clubs
  • Regular "stand and talk" breaks during class
  • Double the number of formal PE classes
  • "Fruit and stretching" breaks twice a day
  • "Healthy cooking" classes in schools
  • "How to make a lunch box" guidance for parents
  • All vegan school lunches
  • Banning vending machines
  • "Water only" policy in schools
  • Weekly classes on diet and fitness with exams

Your task: Choose two ideas that you think would work well. Explain why. Then choose two ideas you think wouldn't work. Explain what changes would be needed. Use the vocabulary and discussion phrases you've learned.
