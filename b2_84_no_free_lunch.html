No Such Thing as a Free School Lunch

Debate the pros and cons of free school meals

Learning Objectives

  • Debate the pros and cons of free school meals
  • Use language for checking understanding
  • Use language for preventing interruptions
  • Express agreement and disagreement

Let's Discuss

Do children have meals at school in your country?

Think about what happens in schools where you live. Do all children eat at school?

Are school meals free?

Who pays for school meals? Do families pay for them, or does the government?

Do you think free school meals are a good idea?

What would be the advantages? What could be the disadvantages?

Debate Language

Expressing Agreement

I agree
I take your point
That's true
I would agree that... (add your point)
That's probably true

Expressing Disagreement

I think you're looking at it the wrong way
Are you sure...? (challenge the point)

Checking Understanding

Hold on, let me get this straight (then restate what you understood)

Preventing Interruptions

If I could just finish...
I'll just pick up on... (refer to something someone said earlier)

Key Vocabulary

Complete each sentence with a word from the box: provision, shortsighted, rolled out, well-off, marginally

1. When trial health schemes are successful there is often call for them to be _______ nationally.

rolled out — means to introduce or make available widely

2. The _______ of free school meals means children from less _______ backgrounds can access similar food.

provision and well-off — provision means supply or service; well-off means having enough money

3. Some argue it is _______ to stop providing free school meals as it results in more healthcare spending.

shortsighted — means lacking foresight; not thinking about future consequences

4. Exercise alone only results in _______ better health stats.

marginally — means only slightly; not very much

Debate Statements

Discuss these statements. Use the debate language to express agreement, disagreement, and check understanding.

Statement 1

"Giving free school meals to disadvantaged children could be seen as preferential treatment and lead to bullying. Everyone should get free lunches."

Do you agree with this? Why or why not?

Statement 2

"It is not the job of the government to feed people. Parents should provide for their children."

What do you think about this argument? Is it realistic?

Statement 3

"The health benefits of providing future generations with a healthy diet outweigh the costs."

How would you respond to this? What evidence would support or contradict this?

Activity: Health and Fitness Ideas

Imagine your school wants to improve student fitness. Discuss these ideas using debate language:

  • Pre-school run club — Students run together before school starts
  • Regular stand-and-talk sessions — Short breaks during lessons to stand and move around
  • Lunchtime sports activities — Organized games and activities at lunch
  • Walking/cycling to school incentives — Rewards for sustainable transport

Discuss: Which ideas would work best? What challenges might there be? How would you persuade people to participate?
