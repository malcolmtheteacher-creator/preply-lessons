My Story Arc — A Job That Makes You Happy

Two moments of certainty → loving the critically ill → the cost of switching paths → 30 years of fit → the gift

1The World Before
Who were you? What was the situation?
  • Before 1989 — resident in family practice outside Madrid
  • The speciality wasn't my ideal vocation
  • Earlier: started uni in biology, not medicine
  • Realised biology wasn't the right path either
  • Two moments of certainty — knew the path I wanted
2The Crack
Where were you? What happened?
  • 1989 — working as a family practice doctor
  • At the same time: critical-care rotations
  • Realised I loved the critically ill patients
  • The most severe cases were the ones I preferred
  • Slow realisation through the family practice
  • The high-stakes environment was where I belonged
3The Struggle
What was hard? What did it cost?
  • Biology → medicine: lost a year of study
  • Long talks with parents — think carefully
  • A longer path, but I was determined
  • Family practice → critical care: more exams
  • 5 more years of residence
  • Had to change city — and life
4The Becoming
How did it shape who you are today?
  • PPV — completely satisfied with both decisions
  • 30 years of professional career
  • Interesting, demanding, important
  • A beautiful area of medicine
5The Gift
Why should the listener care?
  • Don't doubt a change in your professional trajectory
  • If you'll be happier on a different path — go
  • Sacrifice is necessary — and worth it in the end
  • Health, Family, Work
  • A job that makes you happy.
