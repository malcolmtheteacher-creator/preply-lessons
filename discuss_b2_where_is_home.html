Three Lives

SPEAK: Read these three situations. Which one is closest to your experience?
A: The Returner
Marta moved to Ireland for 8 years. Good job, good money. But she always planned to go back. She returned to Riga last year. Now she feels like a stranger in her own city. Her friends moved on. Her favourite café closed. She says: "I came home, but home had changed. Or maybe I had."
B: The Settler
Andris came to London 12 years ago "for one year." He married a British woman. His kids speak English at school and broken Latvian at home. His mother in Liepāja asks when he's coming back. He says: "I am home. But I also left home. Both things are true."
C: The In-Between
Ieva has been in Manchester for 3 years. She video-calls her parents every Sunday. She sends money home. She hasn't decided if she's staying or going. She says: "When I'm here I miss Latvia. When I visit Latvia I miss my life here. I'm never fully anywhere."
SPEAK — 3 minutes:
• Which person's experience is closest to yours?
• What would you say to each of them?
• Is one of these choices "better" than the others?
