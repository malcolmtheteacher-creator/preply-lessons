Modern Life

Speaking lessons for the conversations the modern world actually throws at us — screens, group chats, and all.

How it works 💡

Each lesson drops you into a very modern moment — bowing out of a group chat, leaving an honest review, drawing a line around your own time — and hands you the language that gets you through it with your dignity and your friendships intact. You read a real conversation, learn the phrases that make it work, then play both sides yourself.

🎬 The SceneWho you are, what you want, and what's quietly at stake.
🧰 The PhrasesTen grown-up, real-life phrases — guess first, then check.
🎭 Your TurnPlay it out, both sides, against the clock.
