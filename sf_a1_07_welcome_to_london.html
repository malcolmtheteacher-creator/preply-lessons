Part 1 — Passport control

Twelve thirty. The plane lands at London Heathrow. Leo walks and walks — the airport is very big. Then, a queue. A long queue. Passport control.

Officer: Good afternoon. Passport, please.

Leo: Here you are.

Officer: What's the purpose of your visit?

Leo: Business. I have a meeting.

Officer: How long are you staying?

Leo: Three days.

Officer: Where are you staying?

Leo: At the Park Hotel, in the city.

Officer: Thank you. Enjoy your stay.

Quick check — answer from memory, then reveal:

1. Why is Leo in London?

2. How long is he staying, and where?

1. For business. He has a meeting.

2. Three days, at the Park Hotel.

Talk about it:
  • And you? When you travel, is it for business or a holiday? How long do you usually stay?

Part 2 — The taxi

Outside, it is raining. Of course — it's London. Leo finds a black taxi.

Leo: The Park Hotel, please.

Driver: The Park Hotel? Which one? There are two.

Leo: Sorry?

Driver: Do you have the address?

Leo: Yes, one moment. 20 Park Street.

Driver: Ah, Park Street. OK.

Leo: How long is it?

Driver: Forty minutes. Maybe fifty. The traffic is bad.

Leo: In London too!

Quick check — answer from memory, then reveal:

1. Why does the driver ask for the address?

2. How long is the journey?

1. There are two Park Hotels.

2. Forty or fifty minutes. The traffic is bad.

Talk about it:
  • And you? Do you take taxis? Is the traffic bad in your city?

Part 3 — Paying

At the hotel, the taxi stops. The meter says £62.

Leo: How much is it?

Driver: Sixty-two pounds.

Leo: Can I pay by card?

Driver: Yes, of course.

Leo: Can I have a receipt, please? For my company.

Driver: Here you are. Have a good meeting.

Leo: Thank you! How do you know?

Driver: The suitcase and the face. Business face!

Quick check — answer from memory, then reveal:

1. How much is the taxi? How does Leo pay?

2. What does Leo ask for? Why?

1. Sixty-two pounds. He pays by card.

2. A receipt, for his company.

Talk about it:
  • And you? Do you pay by card or cash? Do you ask for receipts?

Grammar: Where…? How long…? How much…? How many…?

At passport control and in a taxi, four question words do most of the work. Learn the question and the short answer together.

Where…? = a place. "Where are you staying?" — "At the Park Hotel." · "Where are you going?" — "To the city."
How long…? = time. "How long are you staying?" — "Three days." · "How long is the journey?" — "Forty minutes."
How much…? = money. "How much is it?" — "Sixty pounds." · How many…? = a number. "How many bags?" — "Two."
Your plans: "I'm staying at the Park Hotel." "I'm leaving on Friday." "I'm here for business / for a holiday / for three days."
