Fitness Vocabulary

Health Benefits and Effects

When we talk about fitness, we describe what effect activities have on our bodies and minds. Click on each word to see its meaning:

Stay in shape
Meaning: Maintain good physical condition. You're healthy and fit.
Tone your muscles
Meaning: Make your muscles firmer and more defined through exercise.
Keep your weight down
Meaning: Maintain a healthy weight. Prevent weight gain.
Build up muscles
Meaning: Develop and increase muscle strength and size through exercise.
Strengthen your heart and lungs
Meaning: Make your cardiovascular system stronger through aerobic activity (running, swimming, cycling).
Develop your sense of balance
Meaning: Improve your ability to stay upright and stable (useful in yoga, trampolining, rock climbing).
Improve agility
Meaning: Become able to move quickly and change direction easily. Important in sports like climbing and dancing.
Improve stamina
Meaning: Build the ability to exercise for longer periods without getting tired.
Improve coordination
Meaning: Develop the ability to use different parts of your body together smoothly (important in dancing, tennis, rowing).
All-round workout
Meaning: Exercise that works your entire body, not just one part.

Mental Health Benefits

Stress relief
Meaning: Reduction of emotional tension and worry through physical activity.
Exercise your concentration skills
Meaning: Activities that require and improve focus and attention (rock climbing, precision sports).

Persuasive Language for Fitness

When we describe sports and activities, we use special language to convince people that they should try them. These are called persuasive phrases. They make activities sound attractive and beneficial.

Group 1: Emphasizing Guaranteed Results

Pattern: "[Activity] is guaranteed to [verb]"
Example: "Walking is guaranteed to tone your leg muscles."
Meaning: We are certain this will happen. It's reliable.
Pattern: "[Activity] will give you a brilliant [noun]"
Example: "Fencing will give you a brilliant all-round workout."
Meaning: The benefit is excellent and comprehensive.

Group 2: Emphasizing Effectiveness

Pattern: "It's [adverb] effective for [gerund/noun]"
Example: "Irish dancing is incredibly effective for improving coordination."
Meaning: This activity works very well for this purpose.
Pattern: "[Activity] is really amazing for [gerund]"
Example: "Rowing is really amazing for developing physical endurance."
Meaning: This activity excels at achieving this goal.
Pattern: "[Activity] works wonders for [noun]"
Example: "Hula hooping works wonders for stress relief."
Meaning: This activity produces excellent, almost magical results.

Group 3: Adding Opportunity and Benefit

Pattern: "[Activity] helps [pronoun] + verb / helps keep [pronoun] [adjective]"
Example: "Walking helps me stay in shape and helps keep my weight down."
Meaning: The activity assists you in achieving this benefit.
Pattern: "[Activity] really strengthens your [body part]"
Example: "Walking really strengthens your heart and lungs."
Meaning: The effect is noticeable and important.
Pattern: "[Activity] gives you the chance to + verb"
Example: "Rock climbing gives you the chance to exercise your concentration skills."
Meaning: This activity provides an opportunity to develop something valuable.

Group 4: Adding Certainty and Expert Opinion

Pattern: "Experts say [activity] is ideal for [noun]"
Example: "Experts say dancing is ideal for all ages."
Meaning: Professional opinions support this claim.
Pattern: "[Activity] always adds fun and excitement to your training"
Example: "Hula hooping always adds fun and excitement to your training."
Meaning: This consistently brings enjoyment in addition to fitness benefits.
Pattern: "[Activity] definitely works wonders for [noun]"
Example: "Trampolining definitely works wonders for toning and conditioning the whole body."
Meaning: No doubt — this activity is excellent for this purpose.

Describe Your Activity

Now it's time to prepare a short description of a fitness activity. Choose an activity (real or hypothetical) that interests you:

📝 Your Selected Activity:

Preparation checklist:

  • What does it involve? Briefly describe what you do in this activity.
  • Physical benefits: What does it do for your body? (strength, balance, endurance, etc.)
  • Mental benefits: How does it affect your mind? (stress relief, concentration, fun, etc.)
  • For whom? Is it for all ages or certain people? Beginners or experienced?
  • Your persuasive phrases: Use at least 3 phrases to describe why this activity is beneficial.

Reflect on Your Learning

Key Takeaways

Vocabulary for Health Benefits: Can you name at least 5 ways that fitness activities affect our bodies or minds?
Stay in shape, tone muscles, build strength, strengthen heart/lungs, improve balance, improve agility, improve stamina, improve coordination, stress relief, exercise concentration.
Persuasive Language Patterns: What are the main patterns for persuasive phrases?
1. "... is guaranteed to ..." (certainty)
2. "... is incredibly effective for ..." (emphasis on effectiveness)
3. "... works wonders for ..." (excellent results)
4. "... gives you the chance to ..." (opportunity)
5. "Experts say ... is ideal for ..." (external authority)
Application: When would you use these persuasive phrases in real life?
When recommending an activity to a friend, writing a gym or sports club advertisement, trying to convince someone to join your fitness class, or describing why you enjoy a particular sport.
