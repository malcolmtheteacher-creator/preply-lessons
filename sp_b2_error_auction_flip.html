Spot the Difference

Each pair has one correct sentence and one with an error. Which is which?

A: "I've lived here for three years."

B: "I've lived here since three years."

Which one is correct? Why is the other one wrong?
↓ Check

A is correct. Use for + duration (three years), since + point in time (since 2023).

A: "If I had more time, I would travel the world."

B: "If I would have more time, I would travel the world."

Which one is correct? What's the rule?
↓ Check

A is correct. Second conditional: if + past simple, NOT if + would. "Would" only goes in the result clause.

The Flip

You'll see a statement. First, argue FOR it for 90 seconds. Then immediately argue AGAINST it for 90 seconds.

The challenge: your arguments against must be DIFFERENT from simply reversing your arguments for. Find genuinely new angles.
