✅ You Are Right (Not Have Reason)

Common Romance language transfer error | A2 Level

Let's see what you notice...

Read this sentence. What's wrong with it?

You have reason.

What's the correction?

✓ Correction

You are right.

📌 Why?

Romance languages (Italian, Spanish, French, Portuguese) use "have" + noun: "hai ragione" (have reason), "tienes razón" (have reason), "tu as raison" (have reason).

But English uses BE + adjective: "be right," "be wrong," "be lucky." This is a classic false friend!

Not English: have reason, have luck, have wrong

📚 English Structure: BE + Adjective

✓ ENGLISH ✅ BE + Adjective

✅

be right

be wrong

be lucky

be afraid

be happy

be sure

= Use BE + adjective form

❌ NOT ENGLISH ❌ HAVE + Noun

❌

have reason

have wrong

have luck

have fear

have happiness

have certainty

= Don't use in English!
