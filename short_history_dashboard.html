Short History, Long Echoes

Ninety-six of history's most dramatic true stories — retold for English learners, at two levels, with the real podcast as your homework.

Real history makes the best language lessons: a gripping story to read, rich vocabulary to learn, and endless things to argue about. Each lesson here takes one extraordinary true story — a sinking ship, a queen's downfall, a city buried in a day — and turns it into a complete speaking lesson.

Every story comes at two levels (B2/C1 and a simpler A2/B1 twin), and every one teaches one grammar focus — the ninety-six are arranged below by that focus, so they build into a full course. The lessons stand completely on their own; the brilliant "Short History Of…" podcast sits at the end of each as optional listening — listen before for a head start, or after to stretch your ears.

🎭 Now go and speak it — Everyday Echoes Thirty B2/C1 conversation role plays — a museum, a market, a doctor's room — each carrying a small echo of one of these histories. →
