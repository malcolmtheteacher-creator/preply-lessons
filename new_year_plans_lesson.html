🎆 New Year, New Plans?

How to talk about the future (without the pressure)

🗣️ "So, any resolutions?"

And somehow, we feel pressure to sound impressive, motivated, or at least... interesting.

The Certainty Scale

English has many ways to talk about future plans — and they show how certain you are.

Click each expression to see how committed it sounds:

Planning to
Definitely doing
1. "I'm toying with the idea of..."
Very Tentative 💭
Meaning: You're just starting to think about it. No commitment at all. Perfect when you don't want to be held to anything!
"I'm toying with the idea of learning to cook properly — like, actually following recipes instead of just improvising."
"I'm toying with the idea of maybe doing a half marathon... we'll see."
NOW YOU TRY: What's something you're "toying with the idea of"?
2. "I've been meaning to..."
Ongoing Intention 🔄
Meaning: Something you've wanted to do for a while but haven't started. Slightly self-deprecating — admits you've been procrastinating!
"I've been meaning to call my grandmother more often. I always say I will, and then weeks go by."
"I've been meaning to sort out my closet since... 2019, honestly."
NOW YOU TRY: What have YOU been meaning to do?
3. "I'm hoping to..."
Soft Hope ✨
Meaning: You want it to happen, but you're not 100% in control. Leaves room for life to get in the way.
"I'm hoping to travel somewhere new — maybe Portugal or Japan, depending on work."
"I'm hoping to read more this year. At least one book a month."
NOW YOU TRY: What are you hoping to do in 2025?

Complete Your Toolkit

4. "Ideally, I'd like to..."
Aspirational but Realistic 🎯
Meaning: This is what you WANT, in a perfect world. Shows ambition but also awareness that life isn't perfect.
"Ideally, I'd like to get promoted this year, but I'm also just focused on doing good work."
"Ideally, I'd like to spend less time on my phone. Realistically? We'll see."
NOW YOU TRY: "Ideally, I'd like to..." — what's your ideal scenario?
5. "We'll see how it goes..."
Flexible / Hedge 🤷
Meaning: The perfect way to stay open. Neither committing nor refusing. Great for ending a topic you don't want to discuss deeply!
"I might try to exercise more, but... we'll see how it goes."
"I'm thinking about changing jobs. We'll see how it goes — I'm keeping my options open."
NOW YOU TRY: Use this to respond vaguely to "Any big plans for 2025?"

🧰 Your 5 Expressions:

  • "I'm toying with the idea of..." — just a thought, no commitment
  • "I've been meaning to..." — wanted to for ages, still haven't
  • "I'm hoping to..." — want it, but not 100% in control
  • "Ideally, I'd like to..." — in a perfect world...
  • "We'll see how it goes..." — keeping options open
The most common answer to "Any resolutions?" is "Not really" — which tells the other person nothing and kills the conversation. These expressions keep it interesting!

Your Turn to Talk!

Pick 2-3 topics that interest you. You'll use the expressions to discuss them.

🎯 The Resolution Debate
Are New Year's resolutions useful motivation or just setting yourself up to fail?
Great for: The Anti-Resolution type
🌍 The Dream Trip
Where would you go in 2025 if money and time weren't an issue? What's stopping you?
Great for: Dreamers & Planners
📵 The Digital Detox
Everyone says they want to use their phone less. Why is it so hard? Would you actually do it?
Great for: Everyone (we're all guilty!)
🔄 Same Resolution, Different Year
Is there something you've tried to change for years? Why does it keep appearing on your list?
Great for: The Repeater type
🛋️ In Defence of Doing Nothing
What if your goal for 2025 is just... peace? Less hustle, more rest. Is that valid?
Great for: Realists & Improvisers
📅 2024 in Review
What's one thing you did this year that you're proud of? What would you do differently?
Great for: Everyone (reflection mode)
