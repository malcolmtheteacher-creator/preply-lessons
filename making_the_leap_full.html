The Language of Uncertainty

When we talk about big life decisions, we need language for:

  • Things that are almost certain to happen
  • Things that are likely but not guaranteed
  • Possibilities we can imagine
  • Complete uncertainty

Non-native speakers often use "maybe" and "probably" for everything. Native speakers have more precise tools - and they sound completely different.

Today's focus: Five native-speaker phrases for speculation - with the exact pronunciation patterns that make them sound natural, not textbook.

Three phrases, from high certainty to possibility. Focus on the sound, not just the meaning.

Sound Check: Intensive Drilling

Exercise 1: Mark the Stress

Click the stressed words. Check your answers.

SENTENCE 1
Making a career change is bound to be stressful.
MAKing a caREER change is BOUND to be STRESSful.
"a", "is", "to", "be" = weak
SENTENCE 2
Your old colleagues may well think you're crazy.
Your OLD COLleagues MAY WELL THINK you're CRAzy.
Note: Both MAY and WELL stressed!
SENTENCE 3
It remains to be seen whether this will work.
It reMAINS to be SEEN whether this will WORK.
Two peaks: MAINS and SEEN

Exercise 2: Weak Forms

These words almost disappear in natural speech:

"to" → /tə/ — "bound to happen" = "bound tə happen"
"a" → /ə/ — "such a risk" = "such ə risk"
"are" → /ə/ — "you are likely" = "you ə likely"
"the" → /ðə/ — "take the leap" = "take ðə leap"
"could" → /kəd/ — "you could conceivably" = "you kəd conceivably"
WEAK FORM DRILL

Say each sentence. Make the weak forms WEAKER each time:

"You're bound to face challenges." (tə)
"It's a big risk to take." (ə... tə)
"Things are likely to change." (ə... tə)
"You could conceivably succeed." (kəd)

Exercise 3: Chunking Passage

Read aloud. Pause at |. Stress the CAPS.

When you're THINKing about a caREER change | you're BOUND to have DOUBTS. | The first few MONTHS | are LIKEly to be HARD. | You MAY WELL wonder | if you've made a misTAKE. | But staying in a job you HATE | could conCEIVably | be the BIGger risk. | It reMAINS to be SEEN | whether you'll sucCEED | but at LEAST you'll KNOW | you TRIED.
3x DRILL

Read the passage 3 times. Each time: weaker weak forms, stronger stressed syllables.

What Makes Native Sound

  • Stress the key syllables - signals your certainty level
  • Weaken function words - "to", "a", "the" almost disappear
  • Chunk naturally - pause between thought groups
  • Connect words - "bound to" = "boundtə"
