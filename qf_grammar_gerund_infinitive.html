🔄 Gerund vs Infinitive

Choose -ing or to after verbs | B1 Level

Let's see what you notice...

Read this sentence. What feels wrong?

I enjoy to play tennis.

What's the correction?

✓ Correction

I enjoy playing tennis.

📌 The Rule

Different verbs take different forms:

Enjoy + GERUND (-ing): I enjoy playing, not "enjoy to play"

Want + INFINITIVE (to): I want to play, not "want playing"

Some verbs can take BOTH, but with different meanings (stop, remember, try, forget).

📚 The Rule (Dual Coding)

🔄 VERB + -ING

🎬🔙

enjoy playing

avoid eating

finish working

suggest going

Also: imagine, mind, keep, consider

➡️ VERB + TO

→🎯

want to play

decide to go

hope to win

plan to travel

Also: need, promise, agree, refuse

💡 Both forms: Stop smoking (quit) vs Stop to smoke (pause in order to). Remember doing (recall past) vs Remember to do (don't forget future)
