Profit The financial gain made in a transaction or business operation (income minus costs)
Loss A negative financial outcome where costs exceed revenue or profits
Break even The point where profits equal costs; neither profit nor loss
Fair deal An exchange or agreement that is equitable and satisfies all parties fairly
Stake A share of ownership or interest in a business or outcome
Volatile Liable to sudden or unpredictable change; unstable or fluctuating
Smallholder A farmer with a small area of land, often facing economic challenges
Supply chain The entire sequence of processes involved in producing and delivering a product

Coffee Quiz - Test Your Knowledge

Q1: At what latitude does coffee grow?

Between the Tropics of Cancer and Capricorn (either side of the equator)

Q2: What are the two main species of coffee grown today?

Arabica and Robusta

Q3: Is most of the coffee-growing process done by machine?

No, it's all done by hand

Q4: Where does most processing happen?

In the consuming countries

Q5: What factor most affects coffee production?

The weather

Q6: Which country is the largest coffee producer?

Brazil

Real Fair Trade Percentages

Here is how the price of a cup of coffee is distributed through the supply chain:

Farmers: 5–12%
Local Traders: 2–5%
Exporters/Shippers: 5–10%
Roasters: 55–65%
Retailers: 25–30%

Fair Trade Negotiation

You will negotiate to agree on fair profit percentages for each role in the coffee supply chain. Duration: 5 minutes.

5:00

Your Role

You are assigned one of these roles in the coffee supply chain. Work together to decide what percentage of the final price should go to each person/organization.

Role 1: Farmer

You are a smallholder with two hectares of land. Your wife and two children help you farm. You make your living growing coffee trees. Recent unreliable rain patterns have affected your crops.

Role 2: Local Trader

You travel to buy coffee cherries from local farmers. You are often the only contact farmers have with the outside world. Your vehicle is the only transportation in and out of the region.

Role 3: Exporter/Shipper

You buy green coffee beans from traders and transport them by ship to consuming countries. You have high costs for fuel, maintenance, and shipping.

Role 4: Roaster

You buy green coffee beans, roast and blend them to create unique flavours, package the products, and sell them. You have significant branding and marketing costs.

Role 5: Retailer

You buy roasted coffee, store it, and make cups of coffee to sell to customers. You have high costs for premises, equipment, and staff.

Your Task: Negotiate together to agree on the proportion each role should receive. Take notes for feedback to the class.
