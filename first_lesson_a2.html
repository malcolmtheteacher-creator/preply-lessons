First Lesson

Getting to Know Each Other

A2 Level

Nice to Meet You

Today is our first lesson together. I want to know more about you, and you can learn about me too.

Tell me about yourself:

  • What is your name?
  • Where do you live?
  • What is your job?
  • Why do you want to learn English?
You can say:
"My name is..."
"I live in..."
"I work as a..." / "I am a..."
"I want to learn English because..."

Some more questions:

  • What do you like to do in your free time?
  • Do you have any hobbies?
  • What kind of person are you?
You can say:
"In my free time, I like to..."
"My hobby is..." / "I like..."
"I am a [friendly/quiet/...] person."

Your Turn

Talk About Yourself

Step 1: Choose 3 words that describe YOU.
Step 2: Tell me why you chose these words.
Step 3: What words do you think describe ME? Tell me!
You can say:
"I am [adjective]."
"I think I am [adjective] because..."
"My friends say I am [adjective]."
"I think you are [adjective] because..."

More questions:

  • Are you more quiet or talkative?
  • Are you organised or not organised?
  • Are you patient or impatient?
You can say:
"I am more [quiet/talkative]."
"I am quite [adjective]."
"I am not very [adjective]."

Useful Language

Here are some useful phrases to talk about yourself and other people.

Talking about yourself:
"I am a friendly person."
"I am quite shy."
"I am not very organised."
Talking about other people:
"My mother is very kind."
"My friend is quite funny."
"My boss is hard-working."
Useful words:
very = a lot (She is very friendly.)
quite = a little (He is quite shy.)
not very = not much (I am not very patient.)
