Short Stories, Long Shadows · Volume II

Ten more of the sharpest stories ever written — a second shelf for readers who finished the first forty-nine wanting more.

Volume II opens ten new doors. A sniper who wins his duel and then crosses the street to see who he has killed. A child's letter posted to an address no postman can find. A rocking-horse that names real winners, and a house that only whispers louder. Four old friends handed their youth back for one afternoon. A train carriage full of parents arguing about grief until a single question ends the argument. Three winning cards whispered by a ghost. O'Flaherty, Chekhov, Lawrence, Mansfield, Hawthorne, Pirandello, Tolstoy, Joyce, Irving and Pushkin — each story turns on its final sentence, and each becomes a workshop: key words first, then the story in parts, one grammar structure it teaches by example, a look at how the writer pulls it off, and three timed speaking tasks.
