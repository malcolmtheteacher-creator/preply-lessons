Grammar - Used to / Present Simple

Used to - talking about past habits and states:

  • I used to eat a lot of candy when I was a child.
  • My grandmother used to cook for the whole family every Sunday.
  • I didn't use to like vegetables, but now I do.

Present Simple - talking about current habits:

  • I eat breakfast every morning.
  • She cooks dinner three times a week.
  • We don't eat meat - we are vegetarian.

Grammar Practice

Complete the sentences with "used to" or present simple:

1. When I was young, I __________ (hate) fish, but now I __________ (love) it.

2. My mother __________ (make) all our meals, but now we __________ (cook) together.

3. I __________ (not/drink) coffee before, but now I __________ (drink) it every day.

4. We __________ (eat) out every weekend when I was a student.

5. My dad __________ (not/like) spicy food, but now he __________ (eat) it all the time.

Speaking Practice

Discussion Questions

Discuss these questions:

About Your Eating Habits:

  • How often do you cook at home?
  • What is your favorite meal of the day? Why?
  • Do you prefer eating at home or eating out? Why?
  • Are you a good cook? What can you make?

Past vs Present:

  • What foods did you use to eat as a child?
  • Is there any food you didn't use to like, but you like now?
  • How is your diet different now compared to when you were younger?
  • Who used to cook in your family when you were a child?

Food Culture:

  • What is a typical breakfast in your country?
  • What is the most popular dish in your country?
  • Do you have any special foods for celebrations?
  • Have you ever tried cooking food from another country? What was it?

Describe Your Favorite Dish

Think about your favorite dish and describe it:

  • What is it called?
  • What ingredients are in it?
  • How do you cook it? (boil, fry, bake, etc.)
  • When do you usually eat it?
  • Why is it your favorite?
  • Does it remind you of any special memories?

Role Play: At a Restaurant

Practice this conversation. Then create your own version:

Waiter: Good evening! Are you ready to order?
Customer: Yes, please. What do you recommend?
Waiter: The grilled fish is very popular today.
Customer: That sounds good. How is it cooked?
Waiter: It's grilled with lemon and herbs.
Customer: Perfect! I'll have that, please.
Waiter: Would you like a side dish with that?
Customer: Yes, I'll have the mixed vegetables.
Waiter: Excellent choice!

Useful Phrases:

  • I'd like... / I'll have...
  • What do you recommend?
  • How is it cooked?
  • Does it come with...?
  • Can I have... instead of...?
  • That sounds delicious!

Wrap-up

  • What new food vocabulary did you learn today?
  • Tell me one thing you used to eat as a child.
  • What will you cook or eat this week?

Homework: Write about a special meal or food memory from your childhood (100 words). Use "used to" at least 3 times!
