How This Works

You're a time traveller. You've arrived at an auction where 8 of history's most important inventions and discoveries are being sold.

You have $1,000 to spend. Each item has a minimum price. You decide how much to bid on each — or whether to skip it entirely.

The twist: After the auction, you must sell your purchases back by giving a 60-second sales pitch for each one. Your teacher decides whether to "buy" or "reject."

Strategy matters. Persuasion matters more.

Useful Language

"This changed everything because..." "Without this, we wouldn't have..." "What makes this unique is..." "Consider the impact on..." "Before this existed, people had to..." "This single invention led to..."

Reflection

Strategy: Looking back, did you spend your money wisely? Which purchase was the best value? Which was a waste?

Missing out: Is there an item you DIDN'T buy that you now wish you had? What would you change about your strategy?

Real world: If you could actually travel back in time and witness one of these inventions being created, which would you choose? Why?
