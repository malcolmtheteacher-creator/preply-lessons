Verb Collocations: How You Talk About Sports

In English, we don't always use the same verb with every sport. Notice the patterns:

PLAY + ball sports
"I play football." "She plays tennis." "We play basketball." "Do you play volleyball?"
DO + individual sports & exercises
"I do yoga." "He does karate." "They do gymnastics." "Do you do boxing?"
GO + -ing sports (for activities you do for movement/adventure)
"I go swimming." "She goes running." "We go cycling." "Do you go skiing?" "They go surfing."

Quick tip: These patterns help you sound natural. Don't say "I do football" or "I go tennis" — native speakers will notice the difference.

How Often? Frequency Adverbs

Frequency adverbs tell us how often we do something. Arrange them from most often to least often.

Here are the adverbs (not in order):

Always • Often • Sometimes • Rarely • Never

From MOST OFTEN to LEAST OFTEN:

1
Always — 100% of the time
2
Often — most of the time, regularly
3
Sometimes — occasional, not regular
4
Rarely — not often, infrequent
5
Never — 0% of the time, not at all

Using Frequency Adverbs in Sentences

I ______ play football on weekends.
You mean every weekend. "Always" = 100% of the time.
She ______ goes swimming in summer.
She swims multiple times in summer, regularly. "Often" = most of the time.
I ______ do yoga in the morning.
Not every morning, just occasionally. "Sometimes" = not regular.
We ______ go skiing because it's expensive.
Not often — maybe once a year or less. "Rarely" = not frequent.
I ______ play boxing. It's too dangerous for me.
Not at all, 0% of the time. "Never" = not at all.

Important placement: In affirmative sentences, the adverb goes after the verb "to be" or before the main verb:

  • "I am always tired." (with "be")
  • "I always play football." (before the main verb)
  • "I don't ever play boxing." (in negatives, often use "ever")

2:00

What to talk about:

  • What sports or games do you like? How often do you play?

  • Do you do any exercise regularly? What is it?
  • Did you do a different sport when you were younger?

Frequency Adverbs + Sports Sentences

I always play football on weekends. She often goes swimming in summer. We sometimes do yoga together.

Verb Collocations

Play + football, basketball, tennis, volleyball, badminton, table tennis Do + yoga, gymnastics, boxing, karate, martial arts, exercise Go + swimming, running, cycling, skiing, surfing

Frequency Adverb Order

Always → Often → Sometimes → Rarely → Never

Negative Forms

I rarely go skiing. I never play boxing. I don't often do gymnastics.

Questions

How often do you play football? Do you ever go swimming? What sports do you like?

Helpful sentence starters:

  • "I always..." (or often, sometimes, rarely, never)
  • "I like to play/do/go..." (what sport? how often?)
  • "I don't really enjoy..." (what sport? why not?)
  • "When I was younger, I used to..." (past habit — optional)
