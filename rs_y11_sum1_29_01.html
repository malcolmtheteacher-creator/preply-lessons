What does 'omnipotent' mean when describing God?

🔒Reveal answer

Answer: All-powerful; able to do anything. Christian belief that God has unlimited power.

Explain the Trinity in one sentence.

🔒Reveal answer

Answer: God exists as three persons (Father, Son, Holy Spirit) in one substance; a core Christian doctrine.

What is the Incarnation?

🔒Reveal answer

Answer: The belief that God became human in Jesus Christ; 'the Word made flesh' (John 1:14).

Why is the Crucifixion significant in Christianity?

🔒Reveal answer

Answer: Jesus' death paid for humanity's sins; it achieved redemption and shows God's sacrificial love.

What is the Resurrection?

🔒Reveal answer

Answer: Jesus rose from the dead three days after crucifixion; proved victory over sin and death; foundation of Christian hope.

Define 'Ascension'.

🔒Reveal answer

Answer: Jesus' return to heaven 40 days after resurrection; marks the end of his earthly ministry.

What do Christians believe about the afterlife?

🔒Reveal answer

Answer: Eternal life in heaven for believers; some traditions teach purgatory or hell; judgment based on faith and deeds.

What is original sin?

🔒Reveal answer

Answer: The sin of Adam and Eve; inherited by all humanity; explains human imperfection and need for redemption.

How do Christians view salvation?

🔒Reveal answer

Answer: Being saved from sin through Christ's death; comes by faith, grace, or faith + works (varies by tradition).

What is God's 'omnibenevolence'?

🔒Reveal answer

Answer: God is wholly good and loving; all-good; a key attribute emphasised in Christian theology.

Spot the Question Type

Here are the most common ways Paper 1 asks about beliefs:

1-Mark 'Identify' Questions

Example: "Identify two attributes of God."

Strategy: Name two qualities (omnipotent, omnibenevolent, omniscient). One word or phrase per point.

4-Mark 'Explain' Questions

Example: "Explain two reasons why the Resurrection is important to Christians."

Strategy: Give two reasons. For each: name the belief + explain why it matters.

Worked: Reason 1: Resurrection proves Jesus is God's son (validates his teachings). Reason 2: It offers hope of eternal life to believers (comfort in grief).
