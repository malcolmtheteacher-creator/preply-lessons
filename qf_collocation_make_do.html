🎯 Make vs Do

Master these essential collocations | A2 Level

Let's see what you notice...

Read this sentence. Which verb is wrong?

I need to make my homework.

What's the correction?

✓ Correction

I need to do my homework.

📌 Why?

DO = routine/work activities you already know about (homework, exercise, housework)

MAKE = create something new or produce an output (a cake, a decision, a mistake)

Homework is a routine task, so you "DO" it, not "MAKE" it.

📚 The Two Categories

✓ DO (Routine/Work)

🔄

do homework

do exercise

do housework

do the dishes

do research

do a favour

= activities you perform repeatedly

✓ MAKE (Create/Produce)

🎨

make a cake

make a decision

make a mistake

make noise

make money

make progress

= create or produce something new

Practice: Make or Do?

1. I __________ the dishes every day.

2. She __________ a beautiful cake for the party.

3. He __________ a lot of noise at night.

4. I need to __________ exercise for my health.

💡 Bonus Interleave: TAKE is another verb that works with different collocations: take a photo, take a break, take time (neither make nor do).
