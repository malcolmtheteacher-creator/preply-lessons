📌 The Rule

WISH + PAST SIMPLE = unreal present wish (what I want NOW but don't have)

Example: "I wish I could fly" (I can't fly now) | "I wish I were taller" (I'm not tall now)

WISH + PAST PERFECT = regret about the past (I want to change something that already happened)

Example: "I wish I had studied harder" (I didn't study hard, and now I regret it)

NEVER use present tense with wish for unreal wishes!
