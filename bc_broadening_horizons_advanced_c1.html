Grammar & Language Focus

In discussions about education, you often need to discuss benefits and challenges. Practice these structures:

One benefit of studying abroad is...

Example: One benefit of studying abroad is gaining independence and new perspectives.

A challenge you might face is...

Example: A challenge you might face is adapting to a different educational system.

While it's true that..., I also think...

Example: While it's true that studying abroad is expensive, I also think the experience is invaluable.

Speaking Task: Group Discussion

You'll participate in a discussion about one of these topics. Choose the one that interests you most, or let your teacher assign you a group.

Discussion Topics

Topic 1: Multinational University Groups

What are the pros and cons of studying in a multinational group at places like Kaplan, International House, or British Council programs? Think about: diversity, support systems, quality of teaching, cost.

Topic 2: Studying Subjects in English

What are the benefits and drawbacks of studying subjects in English (rather than your native language)? Consider: language development, understanding of content, career opportunities, challenge level.

Topic 3: Foreign Degree and Local Employment

What are the pros and cons of a foreign degree for getting a job in your home country? Think about: credential recognition, language skills, international experience, local job market expectations.

Discussion Guide

Remember to:

  • Share your prepared points clearly
  • Listen to what others say and respond to their ideas
  • Use discussion management phrases to keep on track
  • Encourage others to contribute
  • Summarize the main points at the end

After the Discussion
