Why "heavy rain" but not "heavy wind"?

In English, certain words just GO together — and others don't. "Make a decision" but "do homework." "Strong coffee" but not "powerful coffee." There's no grammar rule for this. You just have to know.

By the end of this lesson, you'll know 8–10 new collocations well enough to use them in your own sentences — not just recognise them.

4 rounds

Discover: Options flash on screen then vanish. Which collocation was correct? Say it, then use it in a sentence.
Distinguish: Two confusable words. Which goes in the sentence? Then make your own examples with both.
Discuss: The weirdest collocations in English. Why do they exist? What's the pattern?
Use It: The collocations come back — but this time YOU have to use them in sentences about your real life.

What sticks?

Without looking back — how many collocations from today can you say from memory? Try to list as many as possible.
The real test: Pick 3 collocations from today. For each one, describe a specific moment from your life where you could have used it. Be specific — not "I make mistakes" but "Last week I made a huge mistake at work when I sent the email to the wrong person."
