About This Course

Commanding Discourse is an advanced speaking series designed for C1-C2 level learners who want to master sophisticated communication strategies in high-stakes professional environments. From framing complex ideas to handling intense disagreements, this course teaches the discourse management skills that separate competent communicators from true command of English.

Each of the 6 lessons focuses on a critical aspect of advanced discourse: mastering fluency and coherence, hedging strategies for nuance, building persuasive arguments, navigating disagreement diplomatically, using speculation and modality precisely, and maintaining composure under pressure. Perfect for professionals, academics, and advanced learners preparing for leadership roles.

Course Lessons

🗣️ 1 Fluency C1
Discourse Mastery
Master the foundations of sophisticated discourse. Learn how to frame complex ideas clearly, maintain coherence across extended turns, and deliver ideas with professional polish and eloquence.
🎯 2 Nuance C1
Hedging Strategies
Develop sophisticated qualification techniques. Learn when and how to use hedging language to express appropriate uncertainty while maintaining credibility and authority in professional contexts.
💡 3 Persuasion C1
Argument Structure
Build compelling, evidence-based arguments. Master the architecture of persuasion including thesis statements, supporting evidence, counterargument handling, and powerful conclusions that convince.
🤝 4 Diplomacy C1
Handling Disagreement
Navigate conflict gracefully and professionally. Learn language for respectfully opposing views, finding common ground, and turning disagreement into collaborative problem-solving moments.
🔮 5 Probability C1
Speculation & Modality
Master the precise expression of probability, possibility, and hypothetical scenarios. Use advanced modality structures to communicate nuanced predictions and conditional reasoning in professional settings.
💪 6 Resilience C1
Pressure Moments
Maintain eloquence under fire. Learn techniques for handling difficult questions, unexpected challenges, and high-stakes moments while preserving clarity, composure, and professional credibility.

Ready to Command the Room?

Start with Lesson 1 and progress through the series at your own pace. Each lesson builds on previous concepts to develop your complete toolkit of advanced discourse strategies.
