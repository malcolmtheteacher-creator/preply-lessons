Food and Drink

B1 Level · Describing Data & Making Comparisons

Vocabulary: Food & Drink

Click each card to reveal the definition.

amount The quantity of something uncountable (e.g., food, water)
number The quantity of things that can be counted (e.g., restaurants)
choice A variety of options to select from
meal A portion of food eaten at a particular time
soft drink A non-alcoholic beverage, often fizzy or sweetened
foreign restaurant A dining establishment serving cuisine from another country

Comparison Language for IELTS

Use these phrases to describe data and make comparisons.

Amount Comparisons

Show Examples
• There is a much greater amount of choice now than there was 50 years ago.
• The amount of food I eat on weekends is greater than on weekdays.
• I drink more in summer than in winter.

Number Comparisons

Show Examples
• The number of foreign restaurants in my town has increased significantly.
• There are far more options now compared to ten years ago.
• The number of popular restaurants ten years ago was small.

Describing Superlatives

Show Examples
• The largest meal I ever ate was at my cousin\'s wedding.
• The food that I like the least is seafood.
• My favorite drink is the one I consume most regularly.

Speaking Practice

Complete these speaking tasks to practice your language.

Task 1: Food Preferences

Compare what you eat at the weekend with what you eat on weekdays. Use the comparison language above.

Task 2: Drinking Habits

Compare the amount you drink in winter with the amount you drink in summer. Why might this differ?

Reflection:

What changes do you think will happen to food and drink choices over the next 20 years?

Writing Task: Describe Data

Your task is to describe and compare two sets of data about food and drink over different time periods.

What to Include

Show Checklist
✓ An introduction identifying what the data shows
✓ Key features: the largest/smallest amounts
✓ Comparisons between the two time periods
✓ Use of comparative language (more, less, greater, etc.)
✓ A conclusion summarizing the main changes
