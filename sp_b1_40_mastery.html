The Mastery Test

You'll complete 3 speaking challenges that integrate all your B1 skills. There's no "pass" or "fail" - this is about demonstrating how far you've come and gaining confidence!

Skills You'll Demonstrate:

  • Fluency: Time-buying, fillers, self-correction, hesitation markers
  • Interaction: Active listening, follow-up questions, agreeing/disagreeing nuances
  • Discourse: Story structure, three-part organization, cause & effect, linking ideas
  • Opinion: Opinions with reasons, hedging language, expressing certainty levels
  • Description: Describing photos, processes, experiences, comparisons
  • Advanced Skills: Hypothetical scenarios, extended thinking, justifying examples

Test Format

Test 1: Discussion (4 minutes)

Conversational discussion with follow-up questions

Test 2: Extended Response (5 minutes)

Tell a story with opinion and detailed explanation

Test 3: Integration Challenge (6 minutes)

Complex scenario requiring multiple skills

Total speaking time: ~15 minutes

TEST 1
Career Change Discussion
⏱️ 4 minutes
You're discussing job satisfaction and career changes with someone. They say: "I'm thinking about changing careers, but I'm worried about starting over." Respond with follow-up questions, empathy, and advice.

Skills to demonstrate: Active listening (ask follow-ups), extended thinking (explore the situation), opinions with reasons (give advice), hedging language (show uncertainty where appropriate), interaction phrases (acknowledge their concern)

Conversation Checklist:

  • ☐ Ask at least 2 follow-up questions to understand their situation
  • ☐ Acknowledge their concern with empathy
  • ☐ Express an opinion about career changes
  • ☐ Give reasons for your opinion (with "because" or similar)
  • ☐ Use at least one hedging phrase to show nuance
  • ☐ Speak naturally with some fillers (um, well, you know)

Test 2: Extended Response

Story + Opinion Timer

5:00
TEST 2
Story + Opinion Challenge
⏱️ 5 minutes
Tell a story about a time you had to make a difficult decision. Then explain what you learned and your opinion about decision-making in general.

Skills to demonstrate: Story structure (beginning/middle/end), cause & effect (why you decided that way), time-buying phrases (Well, you know, let me think), self-correction if you make mistakes, transition words (linking ideas), expressing your opinion, giving reasons, showing certainty levels

Storytelling Checklist:

  • ☐ Clear beginning - set the scene
  • ☐ Middle - describe the problem and your decision process
  • ☐ End - explain the outcome
  • ☐ Use transition words (then, after that, so, therefore, as a result)
  • ☐ Use time-buying phrases when thinking (let me see, well...)
  • ☐ Share your opinion about the experience or decision-making
  • ☐ Give reasons for your opinion
  • ☐ Show some hedging or uncertainty (I think, I might, perhaps)

Test 3: Integration Challenge

Integration Timer

6:00
TEST 3
Complex Scenario Challenge
⏱️ 6 minutes
Scenario: You've just found out you're being offered a promotion that would require relocating to another city. Discuss:
  • What would you do in this situation?
  • What are the pros and cons?
  • How would you decide? What factors matter most?
  • Share your personal opinion on work vs. personal life balance

Skills to demonstrate: Hypothetical scenarios ("If I were in that situation..."), extended thinking (exploring multiple angles), discussing pros/cons (hedging, mixed feelings), expressing degrees of certainty/agreement, describing priorities, justifying with examples, complex sentence structures, fluency markers

Integration Checklist:

  • ☐ Use hypothetical language ("If I were in that situation...", "Imagine if...")
  • ☐ Explore multiple perspectives (pros, cons, different viewpoints)
  • ☐ Express mixed feelings or partial agreement on trade-offs
  • ☐ Show different levels of certainty about decisions
  • ☐ Use time-buying and fluency phrases naturally
  • ☐ Link ideas with discourse markers (however, because, as a result)
  • ☐ Express a clear personal opinion with reasoning
  • ☐ Speak for 6 minutes with fluency and natural pauses
