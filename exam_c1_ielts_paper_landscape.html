Key words

overview (noun) — a short summary of the main patterns, without details. Example: "Her overview named the two biggest changes and gave no figures."
trend (noun) — the general direction in which something changes over time. Example: "The upward trend in online news continued for twenty years."
fluctuate (verb) — go up and down repeatedly, without a clear direction. Example: "Visitor numbers fluctuate from year to year but stay close to fifty thousand."
plateau (noun) — a period when a figure stays level after rising. Example: "Sales reached a plateau in 2015 and hardly moved for five years."
peak (noun) — the highest point that a figure reaches. Example: "Television viewing hit its peak in 2010 and fell afterwards."
overtake (verb) — pass something and become bigger than it. Example: "Online news will overtake television if the lines keep moving this way."
marginal (adjective) — very small, hardly worth noticing. Example: "There was only a marginal difference between the two years."
respectively (adverb) — in the same order as the things just named. Example: "Newspapers and television stood at 62% and 70% respectively."
