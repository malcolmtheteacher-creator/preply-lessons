About this series

Ten lessons for B2 and C1 students who would rather talk about something genuinely interesting than work through another textbook unit. Each one takes a single, slightly counter-intuitive idea from the borderlands of psychology, philosophy and everyday life — and uses it as the scaffolding for a careful piece of language work.

Every lesson follows the same five-tab pattern: a hook and a piece of discussion, eight pieces of C1-flavour vocabulary that all reappear in the reading, a five-paragraph passage with five comprehension questions, a focused grammar peg chosen to fit the topic, and a five-minute timed speaking task that pulls everything together. The reading also carries small pronunciation cues for live work with the teacher — phrasing, stress, linking, intonation, shadow reading.

You can take them in any order. Each is self-contained.
