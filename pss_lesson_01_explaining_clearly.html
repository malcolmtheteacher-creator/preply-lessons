Professional Speaking Skills

Lesson 01 — Explaining Clearly

1. Input: Reading

Last week’s team meeting focused on a delay in the product launch. The project manager explained that the delay was caused by a technical issue discovered during final testing. Because the issue affected data security, the team agreed that releasing the product on time would be risky. As a result, the launch was postponed by two weeks. During the meeting, some team members asked for clarification, as they were unsure whether the delay would affect marketing deadlines. The project manager reassured them that the marketing plan would remain unchanged.

Comprehension
  • Why was the launch delayed?
  • What decision was made?
  • What concern did some team members have?
Notice: Underline the phrases the speaker uses to explain cause and result.

2. Language Focus

1. “The reason for X is…”
Used to introduce a clear cause.
2. “This means that…”
Used to explain consequences.
3. “As a result,”
Signals outcome or decision.
4. “To clarify,”
Used when restating information.
5. “In short,”
Used to summarise an explanation.
Say it: Complete orally.
“The reason for the delay is…”

3. Controlled Practice

Replace the underlined phrase orally:
Because the issue affected security, the team delayed the launch.
Start with “As a result,” and restate the decision.

4. Guided Speaking

Use the same situation. Explain the delay using:
  • one cause phrase
  • one result phrase
Start with: “The reason for the delay is…”

5. Performance Task

Explain the situation to a colleague who missed the meeting.
Time: 2 minutes
Checklist:
  • Clear cause
  • Clear result
  • Short summary at the end

6. Review

Complete orally:
  • I can explain a reason clearly.
  • I can signal results and decisions.
