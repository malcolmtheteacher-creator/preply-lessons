What is liturgical worship?

🔒Reveal answer

Answer: Formal, structured worship using set prayers, rituals, and hymns (e.g., Catholic Mass, Anglican Eucharist).

Define non-liturgical worship.

🔒Reveal answer

Answer: Informal worship without set scripts; prayer and praise directed by the Holy Spirit (Pentecostal, Baptist traditions).

What is the Eucharist (Communion)?

🔒Reveal answer

Answer: Sacrament remembering Jesus' last meal; bread and wine represent his body and blood; Catholics believe in transubstantiation.

Why is Baptism important to Christians?

🔒Reveal answer

Answer: Initiation sacrament; washes away sin; symbol of new life in Christ; marks entry to the church community.

What is a Christian pilgrimage?

🔒Reveal answer

Answer: Journey to a holy site (e.g., Jerusalem, Lourdes) for spiritual renewal, prayer, and devotion.

Name two Christian celebrations and their significance.

🔒Reveal answer

Answer: Christmas: celebrates Jesus' birth; Easter: celebrates resurrection; both commemorate key salvation events.

What is Christian mission?

🔒Reveal answer

Answer: Sharing the gospel message and serving others; fulfils Jesus' Great Commission (Matthew 28:19-20).

Define prayer.

🔒Reveal answer

Answer: Communication with God; can be petition, thanksgiving, praise, or intercession; central to Christian practice.

What role does the church play in community?

🔒Reveal answer

Answer: Spiritual guidance, support networks, charity work, education, social action, belonging and identity.

What does 'worldwide church' mean?

🔒Reveal answer

Answer: The universal body of Christians across all nations, denominations, and cultures united in faith.

The 6-Mark Question Format

Paper 1 Section B (Practices) often has 6-mark questions:

Structure of a 6-Mark 'Explain' Question

Example: "Explain two Christian practices and how they express belief. (6 marks)"

Marking:

  • Knowledge (2 marks): describe each practice clearly
  • Application (2 marks): explain how it shows belief
  • Analysis (2 marks): evaluate significance or connection

Guidance

6-Mark Strategy: Two practices, fully explained. Don't rush; use 4-5 sentences per practice.

Quick-Fire Practice

What is transubstantiation?

Answer: Catholic belief that bread and wine physically become Christ's body and blood in the Eucharist.

Name a Christian pilgrimage site and explain its significance.

Answer: Jerusalem (Jesus' birthplace); Lourdes (healing miracles); Canterbury (medieval saint's shrine); Rome (Pope, St. Peter's).

What is the Great Commission?

Answer: Jesus' command to his disciples to 'go and make disciples of all nations' (Matthew 28:19-20).

Exam Tip: Link Practice to Belief

Guidance

Examiners want to see that you understand why Christians do what they do. Every practice answers a 'why' question:

  • Baptism: Why? Cleanses sin, initiates covenant with God.
  • Prayer: Why? Direct relationship with God; expressing dependence and gratitude.
  • Pilgrimage: Why? Deepens faith, seeks healing, honours saints.
