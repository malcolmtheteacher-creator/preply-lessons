Learn English from the very beginning — one story at a time.

Meet Mia 👋

Mia has just arrived in a new city, and she is learning English from zero — just like you. In each short lesson you follow a little more of her story, learn a few new words, and practise speaking. Nothing is too fast, and every win is real.

📖 Follow the storyRead and say a short, warm story about Mia's new life.
🗂️ Learn a few wordsJust eight each time — with a picture for every one.
🔁 Review brings them backYour words return at the right moment, so they stick for good.
