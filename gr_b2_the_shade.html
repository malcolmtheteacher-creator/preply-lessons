The difference between the right word and the almost-right word

"Slim" and "skinny" both mean thin. But call someone "slim" and they smile. Call them "skinny" and they don't. The meaning is the same — the shade is completely different.

This game is about those shades — the tiny differences in feeling, formality, and connotation between words that a dictionary says mean "the same thing."

3 rounds

Round 1 — Rank: Put synonyms in order from most positive to most negative. Discuss disagreements.
Round 2 — Scenarios: A situation appears. Which synonym fits best? Sometimes you'll disagree — that's the point.
Round 3 — Speed: Situations fly at you. Pick the right shade FAST. No time to overthink.

Reflection

Which word group surprised you the most? Were there shades you hadn't thought about before?
In your language, do similar words have these same shade differences? Give an example of two words that mean the same thing but feel different.
Which 3 new words or shades do you want to remember from today? Use each one in a sentence about your own life.
