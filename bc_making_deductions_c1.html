Making Deductions and Recommendations

Level C1 | Professional Writing & Analysis

Key Vocabulary

Click each card to flip and reveal the definition.

Collocation Two or more words that commonly appear together (e.g., "significant rise")
Deduction A conclusion reached by logical reasoning based on evidence
Modal verb Verbs like might, could, must that express possibility, certainty, or obligation
Plummet To fall sharply or rapidly in value or amount
Shortfall A deficit or the amount by which something falls short of what is needed
Bankruptcy Legal status when a person or organization cannot pay their debts

Making Deductions About the Past

Use modal verbs to make deductions about past events:

Examples:

  • Must have → indicates high certainty ("The company must have faced serious competition")
  • Could have → indicates possibility ("Poor investment choices could have caused the pension shortfall")
  • May have / Might have → indicates lower certainty ("They might have underestimated their costs")
  • Can't have → indicates certainty it didn't happen ("They can't have planned this bankruptcy")

Collocations for Change

Positive change:

  • Rise significantly / slightly / sharply
  • Increase dramatically / gradually
  • Growth in revenue / sales / demand

Negative change:

  • Plummet / Fall / Decline sharply
  • Drop in profits / share price
  • Slump in sales

Practice: Make deductions about this scenario

Scenario: A company's costs have risen dramatically while sales have fallen. The share price has collapsed.

Possible deductions:

  • The company must have faced severe financial stress.
  • Management could have made poor strategic decisions.
  • They may have failed to adapt to market changes.
  • Investors must have lost confidence quickly.

Analyzing a Company Report

You are reading feedback from management consultants about Glaxanon, a pharmaceutical company facing financial difficulties.

Main Findings Summary

Finding 1 - Rising Costs: There has been a significant rise in costs at Glaxanon for many years, and in the last two years, we have seen a huge rise in several key areas. Labour costs, in particular, have risen dramatically.

Finding 2 - Pension Deficit: Generous pension provisions and poor investment choices from pension fund managers have directly resulted in a serious pension fund shortfall that is currently being paid for by the company.

Finding 3 - Share Price Collapse: The share price of Glaxanon has been falling for several years but due to incredibly negative press following its decision to file for Chapter 11 Bankruptcy the share price has plummeted.

Finding 4 - Sales Decline: Parallel to the above increase in costs, Glaxanon has seen sales plummet in the last five years due to competition from other producers. However, the release of their new product line offers some hope.

Finding 5 - Tax Changes: Corporate tax rates have increased dramatically following the change of government. The move from taxation of profits to taxation of turnover has significantly increased the company's tax burden.

Comprehension: What factors contributed to Glaxanon's problems?

Key factors:

  • Rising operational costs (especially labour)
  • Poor pension fund management decisions
  • Declining sales due to increased competition
  • Negative publicity from bankruptcy filing
  • Unfavorable changes in tax policy

What deductions can you make about Glaxanon's leadership?

Consider:

  • They must have struggled to control operational costs.
  • The board could have been slow to respond to competition.
  • Management may have underestimated tax changes.
  • They can't have anticipated the bankruptcy's impact on share price.

Timed Speaking Task

Task: You are presenting recommendations to Glaxanon shareholders. Speak for 10 minutes outlining how the company could improve its situation.

Time remaining: 10:00

Framework for your presentation:

  1. Opening (1 min): Summarize the five main findings
  2. Analysis (3 mins): Explain why each finding matters
  3. Recommendations (4 mins): Propose 3-4 actionable solutions
  4. Closing (2 mins): Summarize your recommendations and timeline

Use this language:

"Based on these findings, I would recommend..." "This could help by..." "The company must address..." "As a result of..."

Notes during your presentation:

Discussion Questions

Question 1: Real-World Application

Have you read or written reports similar to this? What makes a business report credible and persuasive?

Question 2: Strategic Analysis

If you were Glaxanon's CEO, which problem would you tackle first: rising costs, sales decline, or the pension deficit? Why?

Question 3: Language and Certainty

Why is it important to use the right modal verbs (must, could, might) when making deductions in professional writing? What happens if you are too certain or too vague?

Question 4: Writing Task

Draft a 200-word recommendation section addressing one of Glaxanon's five findings. Use modal verbs and change collocations to express your deductions and suggestions.

Key Takeaways

  • Use modal verbs strategically to express different levels of certainty in deductions
  • Precise collocations strengthen your analysis of business data
  • Professional reports require evidence-based reasoning, not speculation
  • Recommendations must follow logically from analysis
