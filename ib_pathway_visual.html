What The Exam Demands

1

A guided textual analysis of an unseen non-literary text. Students analyse how authorial choices shape meaning for a reader — and support every claim with precise textual reference.

Guiding Question

?

Each text comes with a focusing question — e.g. "How does the writer portray the relationship between..." — that directs the analysis. Students must answer it, not just describe the text.

What Band 7 Looks Like

7

Insightful, convincing, nuanced analysis of authorial choices. Evaluation demonstrates critical awareness. Every claim supported by well-chosen references. The writer sounds in control.

The PACER Paragraph — a closer look

The single structural move that unlocks Band 6 and above. Every body paragraph follows this shape.

P
Point
One clear argument about the text.
A
Analysis
Short quotation, named technique.
C
Comparison
Second text or second moment. Linked.
E
Effect
A precise effect verb — unsettles, amplifies, destabilises.
R
Reader
Why this matters to the reader's experience.

Where the Pathway Ends

A student who has completed all six lessons can walk into the Paper 1 exam, open an unseen text, and — in 75 minutes — produce a five-paragraph response with a clear argument, three analysed moves, precise effect verbs, and a self-mark audit already running in their head.
