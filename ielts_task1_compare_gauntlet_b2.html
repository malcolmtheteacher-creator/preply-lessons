The Write — full Task 1 description

Read the chart, plan for 2 minutes, write for 18. Aim for 150+ words. Use a mix of comparative, superlative, and proportion shapes. Then check yourself with the scorecard in Tab 4.

The chart shows the percentage of household income spent on five categories by an average UK family in 2024.

Housing 35% · Food 18% · Transport 15% · Leisure 12% · Savings 8% · Other 12%

Words: 0

Model (172 words)

The bar chart shows the breakdown of an average UK family's monthly spending across six categories in 2024.

Overall, housing was by far the largest expense, taking up more than a third of total income, while savings accounted for the smallest share. The remaining four categories — food, transport, leisure, and other — fell into a fairly tight middle range of 12% to 18%.

Housing dominated the budget at 35%, almost twice as much as the next biggest category, food (18%). Transport followed closely at 15%, only marginally lower than food. Leisure and "other" spending were identical, at 12% each.

At the bottom of the chart, savings made up just 8% — far less than any other category and less than a quarter of what households spent on housing. Taken together, the three largest categories (housing, food, and transport) accounted for almost 70% of all spending, leaving roughly a third for the remaining items.

Target language: by far the largest (superlative + intensifier), almost twice as much as (multiple), marginally lower than (size modifier + comparative), far less than (intensifier + comparative), accounted for (proportion verb), a third / a quarter (proportion phrases). All four shapes used.
