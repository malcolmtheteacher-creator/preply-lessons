American Studies 101

A first-year course in the history, politics and voices of the United States — taught as a course in English.

Department of American Studies · Autumn term

Welcome to the course. Each week takes one big topic from American history, government or literature and turns it into a 50-minute English lesson: words to unlock the topic, a short lecture to read, a piece of grammar that the topic runs on, and a seminar where you do the talking.

The lessons are designed to be taken in order, because each week looks back at the one before — but any week stands on its own.

How each lesson works

Key WordsGuess the meaning from context, check it, then sort the words into groups out loud.
The LectureThree short parts. After each: answer from memory, then reveal, then discuss.
GrammarOne structure the topic needs, practised in gaps, rewrites and error-hunts.
SeminarLook back, choose your questions, speak against the clock, teach it back.
