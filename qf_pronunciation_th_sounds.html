👅 The TH Sounds /θ/ & /ð/

A1 • Universal • Master the hardest English sound

🔍 Let's notice what we hear

Listen to these common mistakes:

❌ "I sink it's free o'clock"

Should be: "I think it's three o'clock"

❌ "Ze bruzzers"

Should be: "The brothers"

What's wrong? Why do they sound wrong? What sound are they replacing?

🎯 The Problem:

English has TWO special TH sounds that are VERY HARD for most languages:

🔵 /θ/ (voiceless) — think, three, bath, thank, thick, month

🔴 /ð/ (voiced) — the, this, brother, father, weather, smooth

Most speakers replace them with: /s/, /t/, /z/, or /d/ — because their language doesn't have these sounds!

🛠️ How to make the TH sounds

🔵 /θ/ VOICELESS

θ
Examples:
think, three, bath, thank, mouth, teeth
How to make it:
• Put your tongue BETWEEN your teeth
• BLOW AIR out
• Vocal cords DON'T vibrate (touch your throat — no vibration)

🔴 /ð/ VOICED

ð
Examples:
the, this, brother, father, mother, smooth
How to make it:
• Put your tongue BETWEEN your teeth
• VIBRATE your vocal cords (touch your throat — you WILL feel vibration!)
• Same position as /θ/, just with voice
💡 The vibration test:

Hold your hand on your throat. If you feel vibration (like a bee), it's /ð/ (voiced). If no vibration (just breath), it's /θ/ (voiceless).

✏️ Guided practice — Say these words

Pronounce (out loud!) these VOICELESS /θ/ words:

🔵 think → t-HINK (tongue between teeth, blow air, no voice)

🔵 three → T-HREE (tongue between teeth, blow air)

🔵 thank you → T-HANK you (practice the beginning sound)

🔵 thick → T-HICK (tongue between teeth!)

Pronounce (out loud!) these VOICED /ð/ words:

🔴 the → DHE (with vibration in your throat)

🔴 this → D-HIS (tongue between teeth, WITH voice)

🔴 brother → BRUD-ER (the end sound has voice)

🔴 father → FAD-ER (tongue between teeth at the end)

🚫 Common mistakes to AVOID

❌ sink instead of think (using /s/ instead of /θ/)

❌ tree instead of three (using /t/ instead of /θ/)

❌ ze instead of the (using /z/ instead of /ð/)

❌ dat instead of that (using /d/ instead of /ð/)

✏️ Self-check: Pronunciation practice

Say this sentence out loud (3 times):

"I think the weather this month is beautiful."

Focus on: think (voiceless), the (voiced), this (voiced)

Key points:

✓ think = /θ/ (no vibration, blow air)

✓ the = /ð/ (vibrate your throat)

✓ this = /ð/ (same position, with voice)

Say this sentence (3 times):

"My father and brother are thinking about three things."

Key points:

✓ father = /ð/ (voiced, at end)

✓ brother = /ð/ (voiced, at end)

✓ thinking = /θ/ (voiceless, at start)

✓ three = /θ/ (voiceless, at start)

✅ I can produce both TH sounds /θ/ and /ð/ correctly
