Explicit vs. Implicit Meanings

Some words have positive definitions, but can imply negative meanings in certain contexts.

Jacinda Ardern Quote

"One of the criticisms I've faced over the years is that I'm not aggressive enough or assertive enough or maybe somehow, because I'm empathetic, it means I'm weak."

What are the implicit meanings here?

Empathetic (explicit: able to understand others' feelings) → (implicit: weak, not tough enough)

Not aggressive (explicit: calm approach) → (implicit: lacking power or strength)

Not assertive (explicit: not forceful) → (implicit: unable to command respect)

Reflection

Can these same qualities be viewed positively? How can a leader be both empathetic and strong?

  • Empathy enables better decision-making that considers diverse perspectives
  • Strength comes from conviction and commitment, not just aggression
  • The best leaders balance compassion with decisive action

Speaking Task: Leadership Reflection

Time Remaining
10:00

Your Task

Speak for 10 minutes about the following topic. You can speak continuously or pause between points.

Topic: Discuss what qualities you believe are essential for political leadership. Use examples from the lesson and your own knowledge. How do these qualities differ based on different cultural contexts or political systems?

Points to Consider

  • What leadership qualities are most valuable?
  • How do gender expectations affect leadership perception?
  • What would an ideal political leader be like?
  • How do different countries value different leadership styles?

Discussion Questions

Consider these questions about women in political leadership.

Key Questions

1. Gender and Double Standards

Women in politics are often held to higher standards than men. Do you think there's a genuine gender-based double standard, and how does it impact women's political careers?

2. Different Perspectives

Do women bring a different perspective to issues like national security and defence? Give examples if you can think of any.

3. Effectiveness in International Relations

Can a female leader be as effective as a male leader in navigating complex international relations and negotiations? What challenges might women face?

4. Personal Reflection

Think of a political leader you admire. What qualities do they have? How do they inspire you?

Imagine a World

Imagine a world where all political leaders are women. How would this change policies or international relations? How might this alter our perception of leadership?
