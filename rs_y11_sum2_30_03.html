What is Just War Theory?

🔒Reveal answer

Answer: Augustine & Aquinas: war justified if (1) just cause (self-defence), (2) right intention, (3) last resort, (4) legitimate authority, (5) reasonable chance of success, (6) discrimination (don't harm civilians), (7) proportionality.

What are Jus in Bello criteria?

🔒Reveal answer

Answer: During war: (1) Discrimination — distinguish combatants/civilians, (2) Proportionality — force matches threat, (3) No prohibited weapons, (4) Respect surrender. These limit warfare.

What is Jihad in Islamic teaching?

🔒Reveal answer

Answer: Greater Jihad = internal spiritual struggle against sin. Lesser Jihad = physical struggle/warfare. Jihad must be defensive, declared by authority, protects combatants, forbids harming civilians.

What are conditions for Islamic warfare?

🔒Reveal answer

Answer: Last resort, declared by legitimate authority, just cause (self-defence, protect faith), discrimination (no civilians, women, children, elderly), proportional response, strict Shariah rules.

What is Christian pacifism?

🔒Reveal answer

Answer: Some Christians (Quakers, Mennonites) teach war always wrong. Jesus: 'Love enemies' (Mt 5:44), 'Turn other cheek' (Mt 5:39). Violence contradicts Christ's message.

What are weapons of mass destruction?

🔒Reveal answer

Answer: Nuclear (destroy cities, radiation), Chemical (nerve agents), Biological (pathogens). All prohibited by international law. Indiscriminate and disproportionate.

What is terrorism?

🔒Reveal answer

Answer: Deliberate attacks on civilians (not combatants) to cause fear for political/religious aims. Islam forbids killing innocents (Q6:151). Christianity also rejects civilian attacks.

What is Christian view on nuclear weapons?

🔒Reveal answer

Answer: Most oppose: indiscriminate (kill civilians), disproportionate (destroy cities). Violate Just War principles. Pope called them immoral.

What peace organisations promote non-violence?

🔒Reveal answer

Answer: Quakers, Pax Christi, Christian Aid, UN, Red Cross/Crescent. Promote dialogue, humanitarian aid, conflict resolution without violence.

What do Muslims teach about refugees?

🔒Reveal answer

Answer: Care for vulnerable is Islamic duty (Zakat). Qur'an protects refugees. Prophet Muhammad welcomed asylum seekers. Muslims obligated to help persecuted persons.

Just War Theory

War can be morally justified if: (1) just cause, (2) right authority, (3) last resort, (4) reasonable success. During war: don't harm civilians, use proportional force.

Islamic Jihad

Greater Jihad = spiritual struggle. Lesser Jihad = warfare with strict rules. Must be defensive, declared by authority, never harm civilians. Shariah strictly regulates jihad.

Christian Pacifism

Some traditions (Quakers, Mennonites) reject all violence. Jesus taught love enemies, turn other cheek. Christ's kingdom is 'not of this world.' Violence contradicts gospel.

WMDs & International Law

Nuclear/chemical/biological weapons are indiscriminate (kill all), disproportionate (destroy cities), cause long-term harm. Both religions condemn them. International treaties ban them.

Case Study: Nuclear Weapons & Just War

Nuclear weapons violate Just War principles: (1) Can't discriminate between combatants/civilians — destroys everything in wide radius. (2) Disproportionate — destruction far exceeds military objective. (3) Long-term harm: radiation, genetic damage for generations. Most Christian ethicists and Islamic scholars: nuclear weapons cannot be used justly.

Difference: Greater vs. Lesser Jihad?

Answer: Greater Jihad = internal spiritual struggle against sin (Hadith). Lesser Jihad = external warfare. Greater Jihad considered 'true' jihad in Sufi Islam.

Do nuclear weapons fit Just War?

Answer: No. Violate discrimination (indiscriminate killing) and proportionality (destruction exceeds military need). Most Christian ethicists and Islamic scholars reject them.

What do Quakers teach on war?

Answer: Absolute pacifism — all violence wrong. 'There is that of God in everyone,' even enemies. They refuse military service. Promote conflict resolution through dialogue.

Watch Out

Don't confuse Jihad with terrorism — Jihad has rules, terrorism targets civilians. Not all Christians support war — many are pacifist. Know Just War criteria completely. Use case studies (WMDs, drone strikes, refugee crisis).
