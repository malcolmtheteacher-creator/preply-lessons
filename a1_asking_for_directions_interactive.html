Lesson 17: Asking for Directions - Finding Your Way

Master the essential skill of asking for and understanding directions

Retrieval + Framework

Warm-up Question: What do you do when you are lost? How do you ask for help?

Think about your answer. Now let's learn the framework for asking for directions in any situation!

ASK-UNDERSTAND-THANK Framework

🗣️
ASK
Politely ask for directions
→
👂
UNDERSTAND
Listen & check you understand
→
🙏
THANK
Thank and confirm

Why This Matters:

When traveling or living in a new place, you will often need to ask for directions. This skill helps you:

  • Navigate confidently to new places
  • Communicate politely with local people
  • Understand basic location vocabulary
  • Ask clarifying questions to prevent getting lost

Deep Dive: Each Component

STEP 1: ASK (Politely ask for directions)

Common Phrases

Excuse me, where is the bank?

Can you help me? I'm looking for the train station.

Sorry, how do I get to the restaurant?

Is there a supermarket near here?

STEP 2: UNDERSTAND (Listen carefully + Key Location Vocabulary)

Turn left
Go to your left side
Turn right
Go to your right side
Go straight
Continue ahead, don't turn
Next to
Beside something; at the side
Opposite
Across from something; on the other side
Between
In the middle of two things
First street
The first road on your left or right
Minute (5 minutes)
Time to walk or travel

Phrases to Check Understanding:

What to Say

Can you repeat that, please?

Did you say turn left?

Is it next to the bank?

So I go straight, then turn right?

How many minutes?

STEP 3: THANK (Say thank you + Confirm)

How to Thank

Thank you!

Thank you very much.

Thanks for your help!

Have a good day!

Examples: Model Conversations

Read these conversations and identify the ASK-UNDERSTAND-THANK parts.

Conversation 1: Finding the Bank

Person A:
Excuse me, where is the bank? [ASK]
Person B:
The bank? Go straight, then turn right on 1st Street. It's next to the supermarket.
Person A:
So I go straight, then turn right? And the bank is next to the supermarket? [UNDERSTAND]
Person B:
Yes, that's right!
Person A:
Thank you very much! [THANK]
Person B:
You're welcome!

Conversation 2: Finding the Restaurant

Person A:
Can you help me? I'm looking for the Italian restaurant. [ASK]
Person B:
The restaurant? Turn left at the bank. Go straight for 5 minutes. It's opposite the cafe.
Person A:
Turn left at the bank? And it's opposite the cafe? How many minutes? [UNDERSTAND]
Person B:
Yes, 5 minutes. It's a short walk.
Person A:
Thank you for your help! [THANK]
Person B:
Have a good day!

Conversation 3: Finding the Train Station

Person A:
Sorry, how do I get to the train station? [ASK]
Person B:
The station? Go straight. Turn right on 2nd Street. The station is between the hotel and the park. 10 minutes.
Person A:
Can you repeat that, please? [UNDERSTAND]
Person B:
Sure! Go straight. Then turn right on 2nd Street. You'll see the station on your right. Between the hotel and the park.
Person A:
Got it! Thank you so much! [THANK]
Person B:
Good luck! Enjoy your trip!
