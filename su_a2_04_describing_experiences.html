Present Perfect Simple

We use the present perfect to talk about experiences in our life. We don't mention a specific time.

Grammar Rule

  • Form: have / has + past participle
  • Signal words: ever, never, before
  • Use: General experiences (not specific time)

Follow-up Questions with Past Simple

After a "Have you ever...?" question, we use past simple questions to get more details.

Question Pairs

Click each card to see example follow-up questions.

Have you ever played a team sport? ▼

Follow-up questions:

• When was it?

• Do you still play it?

• What sport did you play?

Have you ever travelled by boat? ▼

Follow-up questions:

• Where did you go?

• When was the last time?

• How long was the journey?

Have you ever studied another language? ▼

Follow-up questions:

• Which language did you study?

• Can you speak it well?

• How long did you study it?

Have you ever won a prize? ▼

Follow-up questions:

• What competition was it?

• What did you win?

• How did you feel?
