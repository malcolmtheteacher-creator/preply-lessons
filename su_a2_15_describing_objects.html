Adjectives for Describing Objects

Here are useful vocabulary words to describe objects by different qualities:

SIZE

large, small, tiny, huge, compact

SHAPE

round, square, rectangular, curved, flat

MATERIAL

wooden, metal, glass, plastic, leather, ceramic

COLOUR & PATTERN

bright, dark, plain, patterned, striped

QUALITY & STYLE

modern, traditional, stylish, elegant, practical, durable, lightweight, sturdy, delicate

Useful Phrases for Describing Objects

Use these phrases to help you describe objects in natural, complete sentences:

"It's made of..." — Talk about the material
Example: It's made of solid oak wood.
"It's used for..." — Explain the purpose
Example: It's used for storing dishes and keeping them clean.
"It's suitable for..." — Say who or what it's good for
Example: It's suitable for modern kitchens with limited space.
"It looks like..." — Describe appearance
Example: It looks like a sleek rectangular box with a stainless steel finish.
"It comes in different..." — Mention variations
Example: It comes in different colours: white, black, and silver.
"The best thing about it is..." — Highlight a feature
Example: The best thing about it is how quiet it is when running.

Sales Challenge

You want to sell some objects. Pick 4 objects from the original list and describe them to make them sound attractive to a buyer.

What to include in each description:
  • What does it look like? (size, shape, colour)
  • What is it made of? (material)
  • What is special about it? (quality, style, unique features)
  • Why should someone buy it? (use the useful phrases)
