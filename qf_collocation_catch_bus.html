🎯 Catch / Take the Bus

Transport collocations | A2 Level

Let's see what you notice...

Read this sentence. What feels wrong?

I need to pick the bus to work.

What's the correction?

✓ Correction

I need to catch the bus to work.

📌 The Rule

CATCH = urgency (might miss it). TAKE = neutral, planned. Never 'pick the bus'.

Common L1 confusion. 'Get the bus' is OK informally but can be ambiguous.

📚 The Rule (Dual Coding)

CATCH (urgency!)

🏃

Catch the bus!

I almost missed — I caught it!

Run or we'll miss it!

TAKE (planned/neutral)

🚌

I take the 8am train every day

Take the metro to the centre

We took a taxi home

💡 Tip: 'Get the bus' is informal and OK: 'I get the bus to work every day.'
