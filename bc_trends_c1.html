Key Vocabulary

Click each card to reveal the definition.

shoot up to increase rapidly and suddenly
plummet to fall or decrease very quickly
plateau to reach a level and stay there without changing
rocket to increase very quickly and suddenly (like a rocket)
slump to fall suddenly and significantly
tail off to gradually decrease in quantity or intensity
creep up to increase very slowly and gradually
stabilise to become stable or stop changing significantly

Expressing Uncertainty

Use these phrases when you're not sure about data or trends:

I am/was under the impression that…
It looks like/as if…
I take/took it that…
To the best of my knowledge…

Practice: Match Line to Trend Description

Which verb best describes each movement?

Read the trend descriptions and decide which of the verbs you learned (shoot up, plummet, plateau, rocket, slump, creep up, tail off, stabilise) fits best.

1. A value that rises from 20 to 95 in one year: shoot up / rocket

2. A value that stays at 45 for several years: plateau / stabilise

3. A value that falls from 80 to 15 suddenly: plummet / slump

4. A value that rises from 10 to 35 very gradually over time: creep up

5. A value that decreases slowly from 60 to 40 over a long period: tail off

Understanding Data

Analyse the Trends

Consider crime statistics and happiness trends. For each scenario below, choose the best explanation.

Why might crime rates show significant variation over decades? Changes in economic conditions, policing effectiveness, demographic shifts, improved reporting systems, or changes in legislation and law enforcement priorities.

If happiness decreased relative to higher expectations, what might explain this? Unmet expectations, slower social change than anticipated, increased awareness of remaining inequalities, or external pressures like economic crises.

How can we evaluate the reliability of self-reported data? Self-reported data can be influenced by mood, external events, personal bias, and changing definitions of happiness. Multiple data sources and longitudinal studies provide better insight.

Presentation Task

Prepare an informative talk about trends using the structure below:

Step 1: Select Your Data

Choose an interesting trend or dataset that interests you (climate, technology adoption, population changes, education levels, etc.).

Step 2: Describe the Trend

Use the vocabulary you learned (shoot up, plummet, plateau, creep up, tail off, stabilise, etc.) to describe what the data shows.

Step 3: Interpret the Data

Suggest reasons for the trend. Use phrases for expressing uncertainty:

  • "It looks like..."
  • "I take it that..."
  • "To the best of my knowledge..."

Step 4: Reflect on Your Presentation

Consider these questions:

Which trend verbs did you use most naturally?

How confident were you in interpreting the data?

What additional information would make your interpretation stronger?
