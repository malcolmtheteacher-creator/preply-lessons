Today's aim: Today we're learning to explain how things move and stop, so you can say what happens and why.

Words you'll need

force (n) — a push or a pull on something. Example: "A force can make a ball move or stop."
push (v) — to press something so that it moves away from you. Example: "Push the box across the floor."
pull (v) — to move something towards you. Example: "Pull the door to open it."
gravity (n) — the force that pulls things down towards the Earth. Example: "Gravity pulls the apple down from the tree."
friction (n) — the force that slows things when two surfaces rub together. Example: "There is a lot of friction on a rough road."
speed (n) — how fast something moves. Example: "The car increased its speed on the empty road."
surface (n) — the outside or top part of something. Example: "Ice is a very smooth surface."
slide (v) — to move smoothly over a surface. Example: "The box slides a long way on ice."
seatbelt (n) — a strap in a car that keeps you in your seat. Example: "Always wear a seatbelt in a car."
heavy (adj) — weighing a lot. Example: "A heavy box is hard to push."

A question to start

You hold a heavy ball and a light ball at the same height. You let go of both at the same time.

Say it aloud: Which ball hits the ground first? Why do you think so?

In most cases they land at the same time. Gravity pulls all things down at the same rate. If a light thing falls slowly (like a feather), it is because air pushes against it. On the Moon, there is no air, so a hammer and a feather fall together.

Pushes, pulls and friction

🤜 Push or pull

A force is a push or a pull. A force can start something moving, stop it, or change its direction.

⬇️ Gravity

Gravity pulls things down. It is why you fall, and why a dropped cup hits the floor.

🧱 Friction

Friction slows things when two surfaces rub together. Rough surfaces have a lot of friction. Smooth surfaces have little.

Language for explaining

Giving a reason: because and so

The ball stops because there is friction.
There is friction, so the ball stops.

because + the reason  ·  so + the result

Comparing: faster, slower, more, less

A bike is faster than a person who walks.
A feather is lighter than a hammer. A rocket is more powerful than a car.

If sentences

If you push harder, the box moves faster.
If the floor is wet, you slide more.

Practice 1: because or so?

It is hard to walk on ice there is very little friction.

The bike is heavy, it is hard to push up the hill.

I wear a seatbelt I want to stay safe in an accident.

There is no air on the Moon, a feather falls as fast as a hammer.

Practice 2: choose the best word

A rock falls than a leaf in the air.

Ice has friction than carpet.

A heavy box is to push than an empty box.

A ball stops on grass than on a smooth floor.

Why do you move forward when a car stops?

A car goes fast. Then the driver brakes hard. The car stops, but your body keeps moving forward. Your body wants to keep its speed. This is why we wear a seatbelt. The seatbelt is a force that stops you.

1 · Moving
🚗💨 The car and you move at the same speed.
2 · Braking
🛑 Friction slows the car quickly.
3 · Seatbelt
🧍 The belt pulls you back. You stop safely.
