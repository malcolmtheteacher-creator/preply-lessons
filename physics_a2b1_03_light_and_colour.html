A2–B1 Level · Shadows, mirrors and colour

Today's aim: Today we're learning to explain light, shadows and colour, so you can say what you see and why.

Words you'll need

light (n) — what lets us see; it comes from the sun, lamps and fires. Example: "Light comes from the sun."
shadow (n) — a dark shape made when something stops the light. Example: "My shadow is long in the evening."
mirror (n) — a glass that shows your picture. Example: "I look in the mirror every morning."
reflect (v) — to send light back. Example: "A mirror reflects light."
colour (n) — red, blue, green and so on. Example: "What is your favourite colour?"
rainbow (n) — an arch of many colours in the sky. Example: "We saw a rainbow after the rain."
bright (adj) — giving a lot of light. Example: "The sun is very bright."
dark (adj) — with little or no light. Example: "It is dark at night."
torch (n) — a small lamp you hold in your hand. Example: "I use a torch in the dark."
shine (v) — to give out light. Example: "The sun shines in summer."

A question to start

You stand outside on a sunny day. In the morning your shadow is very long. At midday it is short.

Say it aloud: Why is your shadow sometimes long and sometimes short? What do you think?

When the sun is low in the sky, your shadow is long. When the sun is high, it is short. A shadow is the dark place where your body stops the light.

Light, shadows and mirrors

☀️ Light

Light comes from the sun, lamps and torches. Light travels in straight lines.

🌑 Shadow

A shadow is a dark place where something stops the light.

🪞 Mirror

A mirror reflects light. It sends the light back, so you can see yourself.

Try it: shadows and coloured lights

Part 1. A torch shines on an object. The object makes a shadow on the wall. Say aloud what will happen to the shadow when you move the object closer to the torch.

Part 2. Three coloured lights shine on a dark stage. Turn them on and off. What colour do you see when they mix?

Colour in the middle: –
Say it aloud:

1. Move the object close to the torch, then far away. What happens to the shadow?
2. Turn on red and green together. What colour do you see?
3. Turn on all three lights. What colour is it?
4. Finish: "When the object is close to the torch, the shadow is … because …"

What you just saw

1 · Light goes straight
Light travels in straight lines. An object stops some of it.
2 · Close means big
When the object is near the torch, it stops more light, so the shadow is bigger.
3 · Colours mix
Red, green and blue light mix to make other colours. All three make white.

A shadow shows where the light cannot go.

Language for explaining

Facts: the present simple

Light travels in straight lines.
A mirror reflects light.

Giving a reason: because and so

You see a shadow because the object stops the light.
The object stops the light, so there is a shadow.

If sentences

If you put your hand in front of a lamp, you make a shadow.
If the sun goes behind a cloud, your shadow disappears.

Practice 3: write it

Make an If sentence with each set of words. Start with If. Say it, then write it.

2. Words: it, sunny, you, see, your shadow

2. If it is sunny, you see your shadow.

How does a rainbow happen?

Sunlight looks white, but it has many colours in it. When sunlight goes through raindrops, the drops split the light into colours. We see a rainbow.

1 · Sun and rain ☀️🌧️
The sun shines on the raindrops.
2 · Splitting 💧
Each drop splits the white light into many colours.
3 · Rainbow 🌈
We see red, yellow, green, blue and more.
