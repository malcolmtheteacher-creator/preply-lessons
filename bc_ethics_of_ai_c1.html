The Ethics of AI

C1 Level · Artificial Intelligence, Ethics & Decision-Making Focus

A Question for the Future

"When will we draft an artificial intelligence bill of rights?" — Gray Scott

What Does This Question Mean?

Should AI have legal rights or protections? Should there be international laws to regulate AI development? Who decides what is ethical?

Key Questions to Consider:

1. How much AI is already part of your daily life?

(Social media algorithms, shopping recommendations, voice assistants, email filters)

2. Do you trust AI to be fair and objective?

(Think about job hiring, loan approval, criminal justice)

3. In which areas should AI never be used?

(Healthcare decisions, legal verdicts, military operations, hiring)

4. Who should control AI development?

(Tech companies, governments, international bodies, or citizens)

Your task: Form your own opinions on these questions before moving to the next section.

Essential AI & Ethics Vocabulary

Click each card to reveal the definition. You'll use these terms in your discussion.

algorithmic bias Unfair outcomes created by patterns in data or flawed design of AI systems
machine learning The process of computers learning from data and improving themselves automatically
autonomous systems Systems that can make decisions without human control or intervention
predictive analytics Using data to forecast behavior or future outcomes
ethical dilemma A moral conflict where there is no clear right or wrong answer
human oversight Supervision by people to ensure AI systems work as intended and safely
accountability Responsibility for decisions and ability to explain why something happened
transparency Openness about how AI systems work and the data they use

Third Conditional: Talking About Past Mistakes & Regret

Use the third conditional to discuss how things could have been different if decisions had been made differently.

Structure:

If + had + past participle, would/could/might have + past participle

Real Examples:

Example 1:

"If the developers had tested the system more thoroughly, it wouldn't have failed."

Example 2:

"We might have avoided the scandal if we'd had proper human oversight."

Example 3:

"If the company had been more transparent, users would have trusted the system more."

Practice: Complete These Sentences

1. If the AI hadn't made that error, _____________.

Show Answer
people would have trusted the system / the company wouldn't have lost customers

2. The situation would have been different if _____________.

Show Answer
they had listened to ethical concerns / there had been more regulation

3. If the company had been more transparent, _____________.

Show Answer
people would have understood how their data was used / controversy could have been avoided

4. We might have avoided the issue if _____________.

Show Answer
we had tested the system with diverse data / we had included ethicists in development

Ethical Scenario: The Emergency Room Decision

The Scenario:

A hospital wants to use AI to prioritize patients in emergency rooms based on survival predictions.

The AI analyzes patient data and decides who gets treatment first, based on who is most likely to survive. This could save resources and lives — or it could mean people with less hope of recovery get no treatment at all.

Your Task as an Ethics Committee:

You and your classmate(s) must decide:

  • Is this ethical or unethical?
  • What are the benefits?
  • What are the dangers?
  • What improvements or regulations would you suggest?

Language Requirements:

In your discussion, use:

✓ At least 2 vocabulary terms from the AI vocabulary section

✓ At least 1 third conditional sentence to talk about consequences

✓ Clear reasoning and specific examples

Speaking Time:

Time for ethics committee: 10:00

Sample Discussion Starters:

"I think this system would be unethical because of algorithmic bias. If the AI hadn't been trained on diverse patient data, it might discriminate against certain groups."

"While I understand the efficiency argument, human oversight is crucial here. If doctors had final say over AI recommendations, the system would be more ethical."
