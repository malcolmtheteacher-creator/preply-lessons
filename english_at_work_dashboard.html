English at Work

Speaking lessons for the moments of working life that actually decide things — meetings, introductions, and every high-stakes conversation in between.

How it works 💼

Each lesson drops you into a real working moment — getting your point into a fast meeting, working a conference room, holding your nerve when you're put on the spot — and hands you the language that carries your expertise across into English. You read a real conversation, learn the phrases that make it work, then play both sides yourself.
