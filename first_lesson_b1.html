First Impressions

"You never get a second chance to make a good first impression."
— Will Rogers
This means: The first time you meet someone is very important. You cannot do it again.

Let's discuss:

  • When you meet someone new, what do you notice first?
    • Think about: their face, their clothes, how they speak...
  • Are first impressions usually correct, or sometimes wrong?
    • In your experience...
  • Can you remember a time when your first impression of someone was wrong?
    • Maybe you thought someone was unfriendly, but they were actually nice?
  • What impression do you think other people have of you when they first meet you?
Useful phrases:
"When I first meet someone, I usually notice..."
"I think first impressions are (not) always right because..."
"I remember when I met... I thought they were... but actually..."

Language Focus

Next lesson, we're going to discuss important books and inventions. To talk about imaginary situations, we need a useful structure.

The Second Conditional

Talking about imaginary situations
If + past simple, ... would + verb
We use this to talk about things that are not real or not true now.
"If I had more time, I would read more books."
(= I don't have much time, so I don't read much)
Without + noun
We can also use "without" to imagine a different situation:
"Without computers, life would be very different."
"Without the internet, we wouldn't be able to have this lesson online."

Practice: Complete with your own ideas

1. If I could live anywhere in the world, I would...
Where would you choose? Why?
2. If I had more free time, I would...
What would you do?
3. Without my phone, I wouldn't be able to...
What do you use your phone for?
4. If I could meet anyone in history, I would meet...
Who would you choose? What would you ask them?
5. Without coffee/tea, my mornings would be...
How would you feel?
