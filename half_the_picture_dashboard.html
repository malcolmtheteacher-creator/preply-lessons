Speaking games for two

Half the Picture

You each hold half the information. Nobody can see the whole picture, so the only way to finish is to talk.

← All lessons

How it works

1

One person starts a game and gets a four-digit game number. Every game is shuffled, so nobody knows the answers in advance.

2

The other person joins with the same number. You each get a different card: Card A or Card B.

3

Talk until you've pieced it together. Then put the two halves side by side and see what you missed.

The games

Game 1B2

The Hiring Panel

Three candidates, one job. You each read different CVs, references and interview notes. Agree who to hire.

Reporting · weighing up · agreeing
Game 2C1

Cold Case

Something has gone missing from a country house. You each hold different statements and clues.

Deduction · must have, can’t have
Game 3C1

The Deal

A negotiation where each side has a secret brief, a bottom line and points to win.

Bargaining · conditions · softening
Game 4C1

Two Newspapers

One event, two reports. Find what's different, and how each paper tells it.

Reporting verbs · bias · neutral wording
Game 5A2

Spot the Difference: The Flat

Two pictures of the same flat. Eight differences. Describe until you find them all.

There is / there are · where things are
Game 6B1

The Weekend Plan

Two diaries and a stack of timetables. Find a time and a plan that works for you both.

Making plans · suggesting · saying no
Game 7B1–B2

Lost Luggage

An airport baggage desk and a lost-property office. Describe your bag precisely, spell your details, and find the right one.

Describing things · spelling · desk English
