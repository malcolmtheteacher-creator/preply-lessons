Key Technical Terms

Click on each term to reveal its meaning. These terms are essential for discussing app development and UX/UI design.

App Design and Functionality
Mock-up A visual representation of an app's design without the actual functionality
UXD User Experience Design — how an app looks and feels to the user
Visual Hierarchy The arrangement of elements to guide the user's attention on the screen
Progressive Disclosure Revealing information gradually to avoid overwhelming the user
Data and Information Systems
Data Raw facts and figures collected from various sources
Information Processed data that provides meaning and context
Database An organized collection of data stored electronically
Algorithm A step-by-step procedure for solving a problem or completing a task
User and Interaction Terms
User Interface The point of interaction between user and app (buttons, menus, etc.)
Functionality What the app can do and how it performs its tasks
Feature A specific capability or tool within an application

Explaining Technical Terms

Learn how to explain complex concepts clearly to people unfamiliar with the terminology.

Model Dialogue

Developer: John promised to get us a mock-up of the new app in a couple of weeks.

Investor: A mock-up being…?

Developer: A mock-up just shows what an app will look like without having to actually build it.

Expressions for Explaining

EXPLANATION PHRASE
That's basically…
Use when giving a simplified definition of a technical term
EXPLANATION PHRASE
Basically, it [verb]…
Give a simple explanation of what something does or means
EXPLANATION PHRASE
It basically means how you [action/feature]…
Explain a term by describing what it involves or enables
EXPLANATION PHRASE
I'm no expert, but I think it [simple explanation]…
Offer a humble explanation to show willingness to help
EXPLANATION PHRASE
So, [term] would be when [situation/example]…
Define a term by providing a concrete example or context

Reacting to Explanations

REACTION
You lost me there.
Politely indicate that you didn't follow the explanation
REACTION
A [term] being…?
Ask for a brief, simple explanation of an unfamiliar term
REACTION
I'm afraid I haven't heard of…
Formally acknowledge unfamiliarity with a term
REACTION
Gotcha. Makes sense.
Signal that you now understand the explanation

Connected Speech

In natural speech, sounds between words connect smoothly. Notice where sounds link in these sentences to improve your pronunciation fluency.

Linking Patterns

Sentence 1: I've asked Fiona to look_into this.

The final /k/ of "look" links smoothly to the vowel at the start of "into"

Sentence 2: These are all problems that we can work_around.

The final /k/ of "work" connects to the vowel at the start of "around"

Sentence 3: Makiko is our head_designer.

The final /d/ of "head" links to the vowel at the start of "designer"

Sentence 4: This will be a fitness app that people can count_on.

The /t/ of "count" connects to the vowel at the start of "on"

Sentence 5: The solution was to everyone's satisfaction.

The final /t/ of "to" links to the vowel of "everyone's" — smooth elision between sounds

Role-Play: Pitch to Investors

You are presenting a fitness app to potential investors at a business lunch. You need to explain the technical features in accessible language.

App Details to Cover

UXD (User Experience Design)
Explain how the app looks and feels. What makes it appealing and easy to use? How does it guide the user through features?
Visual Hierarchy
Describe how users' attention is guided on the screen. Which elements stand out? How does this help them find what they need?
Progressive Disclosure
Explain how information is revealed gradually so users aren't overwhelmed. What basic features appear first? What advanced features are revealed later?
Additional Features
What other features or technical aspects make this fitness app unique? Consider data tracking, algorithms, user interaction, or integration capabilities.
