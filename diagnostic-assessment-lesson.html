Welcome 👋

Today's session is designed to understand exactly how you communicate — not to test you, but to identify the subtle patterns that make the difference between "good English" and effortlessly fluid English.

What We'll Do Today

You'll speak for 3-5 minutes on a work-related topic while I listen for specific patterns. Then we'll build your personalised development profile together.

Why This Matters

If you're here, your English is likely already functionally strong. What we're looking for today are the micro-patterns that separate "competent" from "commanding" — the subtle differences that make speech feel effortless rather than effortful.

The Speaking Challenge

Choose one of the prompts below, then speak for 3-5 minutes without stopping. This isn't about being perfect — it's about showing me how you naturally communicate under mild pressure.

Select Your Topic

💼 Your Work

"Explain what you do in your job to someone who knows nothing about your field. What makes it challenging or interesting?"

📊 A Recent Project

"Describe a project or task you worked on recently. What was the goal, what challenges did you face, and what was the outcome?"

🔮 Looking Ahead

"What changes do you see coming in your industry or field over the next few years? How should people prepare?"

💡 Tips

Speak as you would in a real meeting. Don't rehearse or script — I want to see your natural communication style. It's okay to pause and think. If you run out of things to say, try to extend by giving examples or considering different perspectives.

      Development Roadmap

      A personalised 8-12 week journey from "functionally excellent" to "effortlessly commanding."

      Weeks 1-3

      Foundation: Awareness Building

      Focus on recognising patterns in your own speech and building a toolkit of alternatives.

      • Record and analyse 2-minute work explanations
      • Build awareness of hesitation triggers
      • Introduce signposting language frames
      • Practice hedging expressions in context
      Weeks 4-6

      Integration: Controlled Practice

      Apply new patterns in structured speaking tasks with increasing complexity.

      • Stakeholder explanation simulations
      • Data presentation with structured frameworks
      • Opinion + evidence + speculation drills
      • Pace and rhythm exercises
      Weeks 7-9

      Fluency: Real-World Application

      Transfer skills to authentic workplace scenarios with minimal scaffolding.

      • Mock client consultations
      • Impromptu Q&A handling
      • Meeting contribution strategies
      • Real-time self-monitoring
      Weeks 10-12

      Mastery: Automatic Processing

      Internalise patterns so they become your default communication style.

      • High-pressure presentation practice
      • Complex topic navigation
      • Style refinement and personal voice
      • Final diagnostic comparison
