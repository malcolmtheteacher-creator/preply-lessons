At the Vet

Your dog is not eating. He is quiet and tired. He can't tell the vet what is wrong — so you must do it, in simple, clear words.

This is the easier version. Want the harder one? → Full B2/C1 version →

The Scene 🎬

Your dog is called Biscuit. He loves his food — he eats everything, every day. But this morning, he did not eat. At lunch, he did not eat again. Something is wrong.

Biscuit is very quiet. He is tired, and he does not want to play. You are worried. But Biscuit can't talk, so he can't say what hurts. You must tell the vet. This is not easy — but simple, clear words help a lot.

You call the vet, and they say, "Come now." Soon, you are in the waiting room with Biscuit on your lap. The vet is kind and calm. Now it is your turn to speak.

Warm up — talk for one minute

  1. Do you have a pet, or did you have one? What is its name? What is it like?
  2. When you feel ill, is it easy to tell the doctor what is wrong? Why, or why not?

Who's who

You
The Worried Owner 🐾

You love Biscuit. You are worried. You must say what is wrong, answer the vet's questions, ask about the price, and say thank you.

The other person
The Vet 🩺

Kind and calm. The vet asks simple questions, looks at Biscuit, and tells you what to do. The vet wants to help.

Next: learn the phrases you need in the next tab. Then see them in a real conversation. It is OK to speak slowly. It is OK to say "I'm not sure." You can also ask the vet to say it again.

The Conversation 💬

Read it out loud, one person each, then change. Find the phrases from your toolkit. Listen for the simple, clear words.

VetHello, come in. So this is Biscuit! What is the problem today?
YouHello. Thank you for seeing us. My dog is not well. He is not eating, and he is very quiet.
VetOh, poor Biscuit. When did it start?
YouIt started yesterday. In the morning he was fine, but in the evening he was tired and quiet. He was sick one time in the night.
VetOK. Did he eat something bad? Maybe something on a walk?
YouI'm not sure. Maybe. On the walk yesterday, he put his nose in a bush for a long time.
VetGood to know. Let me look at him. … His tummy is a little sore here, and he needs more water. But it is not very bad.
YouThat's good to hear. So it is not serious?
VetI think he has a small stomach problem. But I want to do a blood test, to be sure.
YouOK. How much does it cost?
VetThe blood test is ninety pounds. An X-ray costs more. Let's do the blood test first, and then we can decide.
YouYes, good idea. Let's do the blood test first.
VetGood. At home, give him small meals and lots of water. If he is sick again, please call me.
YouThank you very much for your help. Come on, Biscuit, let's go home.

From memory — don't look up!

1. What is wrong with Biscuit? Say two things.

2. What does the vet want to do first — the blood test or the X-ray?

3. The vet gives the owner two things to do at home. What are they?

1. Biscuit is not eating, and he is very quiet and tired. (He was also sick one time in the night.)
2. The vet wants to do the blood test first. The X-ray costs more, so they wait and decide later.
3. Give Biscuit small meals and lots of water — and call the vet if he is sick again.

The Echo 🕰️

In an old story by Rudyard Kipling, a big flood carries a small, wet animal — a mongoose — into a family's garden in India. The family take him in. They dry him, feed him, and give him a name: Rikki-Tikki-Tavi.

Soon, Rikki is not just an animal — he is family. And when two big snakes come, Rikki fights them to keep the family safe. He gives them everything.

It is the same with Biscuit. We take a little animal into our home, we feed it and love it, and it becomes family. So when Biscuit will not eat, you stop everything and take him to the vet. That is what family does.

Two questions to talk about

  1. Rikki-Tikki-Tavi helps the family that took him in. Do you think a pet can love a person? Why, or why not?
  2. People spend a lot of time and money on a pet that can't say "thank you". Why do we do it? What does a pet give us?
