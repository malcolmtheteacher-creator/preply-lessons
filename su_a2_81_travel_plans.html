Why Do We Travel?

Travel helps us explore the world, learn about new places, and experience different cultures. Here are some famous quotes about travel:

Travel Quotes

  • "The world is a book, and people who do not travel read only one page." - St Augustine
  • "To travel is to discover that everyone is wrong about other countries." - Aldous Huxley
  • "The traveller sees what he sees. The tourist sees what he has come to see." - G.K. Chesterton

Discussion:

What do you think these quotes mean? Do you agree with them?

How to Express Future Plans

In English, we have different ways to talk about future travel plans. Each way shows different levels of certainty or intention.

The Three Main Structures

Less Certain / Tentative

We might hitchhike.
We'll probably go hiking.
We hope to catch a train

More Definite / Planned

We're going to take a flight.
We'll definitely go by ship.
We plan to go on a cruise.

The Definite Plan

We'll go by bus!

Three Structures You Need

  • will/might + infinitive: We'll probably go. / We might hitchhike. (Use "probably" or "definitely" for certainty)
  • going to + infinitive: We're going to take a flight. (Shows a plan)
  • plan/hope + to + infinitive: We plan to go on a cruise. / We hope to catch a train. (Shows intention or wish)

Notice: Contractions are important! "We're" = "We are", "We'll" = "We will"

Plan a Trip

Use all the language you've learned to help plan a trip together. Think about these elements:

Planning Checklist

  • Destinations: Where will you go?
  • Dates: When will you travel?
  • Transport: How will you get there? (train, plane, bus, ship, hiking, etc.)
  • Accommodation: Where will you stay?
  • Sightseeing activities: What will you see and do?
  • Other activities: Relaxation, shopping, sports, etc.
  • Food to try: What local dishes will you eat?
  • Other things to do: Museums, nature, nightlife, etc.

Use these structures as you discuss:

"We'll probably go by train." / "We're going to stay in a hotel." / "We plan to visit the museums."

Present your plan

Use the language you've learned to describe your travel plans. Include:

  • Where you will go and when
  • How you'll travel (using different transport vocabulary)
  • What you'll do there
  • Different structures: "We'll...", "We're going to...", "We plan to...", "We hope to..."

Remember: Make your plan interesting! Use vivid descriptions and show enthusiasm about the trip.
