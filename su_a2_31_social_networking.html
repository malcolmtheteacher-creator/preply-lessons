Example Sentences

Post (noun/verb)

Meaning: Share content online; a piece of content shared online

Examples:

  • "I post every two months."
  • "I post once a week on Instagram."
  • "That's a great post! Really funny."

Tag (verb)

Meaning: Mention someone or identify them in a photo or post

Examples:

  • "I tagged my friend in the photo."
  • "Don't tag me in that picture!"

Hashtag

Meaning: A word or phrase starting with # used to organize posts by topic

Examples:

  • "Use the hashtag #LondonFood to find restaurant posts."
  • "I always put #TravelDiary on my photos."

Mention (verb)

Meaning: Write someone's name in a post, usually with @ symbol

Examples:

  • "I mentioned you in my story."
  • "Can you mention the restaurant in your post?"

Like (verb/noun)

Meaning: Show approval of a post by clicking the like button; the action itself

Examples:

  • "Everyone liked her photo."
  • "I got 50 likes on that post!"

Reach (noun)

Meaning: The number of people who see your post

Examples:

  • "That post had an amazing reach - 10,000 people saw it!"
  • "Hashtags help increase your reach."

Share (verb)

Meaning: Post or repost someone else's content (also called "re-post")

Examples:

  • "I shared my friend's post on my timeline."
  • "That video is funny - I'm going to share it!"

Pros & Cons Language

These phrases help you talk about advantages and disadvantages. Click each one to expand.

Advantage Phrases

Use these when talking about positive things:

Advantage 1
"It's great for keeping in touch."
Use this when social media helps you stay connected.
Advantage 2
"That's the good thing about it."
A simple way to introduce a positive aspect.
Advantage 3
"YouTube is really useful for learning."
Show how something is helpful.
Advantage 4
"That's one of the advantages of Instagram."
More formal way to discuss benefits.
Advantage 5
"One of the merits of social media is connecting with people."
"Merit" = advantage or positive quality.

Disadvantage Phrases

Use these when talking about negative things:

Disadvantage 1
"One of the problems is that it wastes time."
Good for introducing a major issue.
Disadvantage 2
"That's one of the downsides."
A natural, conversational way to mention negatives.
Disadvantage 3
"That's one of the disadvantages of TikTok."
More formal version.
Disadvantage 4
"It's not good for concentration."
Simple and direct.
Disadvantage 5
"The problem with Facebook is privacy."
Name a platform or app and describe the issue.

Pronunciation: Stress Patterns

Click each word to see which syllable is stressed (in capitals).

Words with Stress

cy·BER·bul·ly·ing

Stress on BER - the second syllable.

CEL·e·bri·ty GOS·sip

Stress on CEL in celebrity, GOS in gossip.

TROLL·ing

Stress on TROLL - the first syllable. Definition: posting mean comments to upset people online.

TIRE·less or TIR·ed·ness

Stress on TIRE - the first syllable. Using social media makes many people feel tiredness.

READ·ing the NEWS

Stress on READ and NEWS - both are important words in the phrase.

a WASTE of TIME

Stress on WASTE and TIME - "Some people think social media is a waste of time."

KEEP·ing in TOUCH

Stress on KEEP and TOUCH - "Social media helps you keep in touch with friends."
