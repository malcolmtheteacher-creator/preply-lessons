Brick Builders

Sixteen brick models, chopped into pieces and tipped into the parts bin. Snap them back together, beat the clock, and beat your brother.

Who's building?

👑 Daily challenge

gets the harder version (same model, cut into more, smaller pieces).

How to play

Drag a piece out of the grey parts bin. It lifts to the height where it belongs, and a see-through grid shows that layer. Slide it over the model and let go near the right spot: it clicks into place. You can build in any order.

Stuck? Press Hint (costs 10% of the points) or wait 20 seconds: a glowing ghost shows where a piece goes, and that piece bounces in the bin. Drag empty space to spin the view, and pinch or use the mouse wheel to zoom.

Points: Easy 100 · Medium 200 · Hard 350 · Expert 500, plus a time bonus in Timer race. Head-to-head winners get +100.
