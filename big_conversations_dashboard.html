Big Conversations

The big topics of ordinary life — fear, time, work, wonder — each told as a true story that opens into an hour of real conversation.

Some subjects don't belong to business, science or literature — they belong to everyone: why we watch frightening films, where our free time went, and the other questions people actually argue about at kitchen tables. Each lesson here takes one of those subjects and tells its true story — with real dates, real people and real numbers — because opinions get better when they have a story to stand on.

The format is the house formula: ten key words unlocked first, the story in three parts with quick checks, one grammar point the topic itself teaches, a Going Deeper page that argues more than one side, and timed speaking tasks — including debates built for two voices. B2 level, 50 minutes, and the collection grows whenever a new big question comes along.
