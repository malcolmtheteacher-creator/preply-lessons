Life in the Future

What will the world be like in 50 years? Nobody knows — so let's guess! Today we learn may, might and could.

The future is a mystery

We can't see the future, but it's fun to guess! Let's start with some easy questions.

Let's talk:
  • What do you think the world will look like in 50 years? Better or worse?
  • Do you like new technology, or do you prefer old, simple things?
  • When you were a child, what did you think the future would be like? Were you right?
  • What is one machine or invention you really want in the future?

Sure, or not sure?

Some things about the future are easy to guess. Some things are a big surprise. When we are not sure, English has three nice little words: may, might and could. Let's learn them now.

May, might, could — for things that are possible

1. What they mean

We use may, might and could to talk about things that are possible — when we think “maybe yes, maybe no”. For A2, they all mean almost the same thing:

may / might / could + verb  =  perhaps this will happen

Robots might cook our food.  (maybe yes, maybe no)

We could live on the moon one day.  (it's possible)

It may rain tomorrow.  (I'm not sure)

2. The form is very easy

Just put may / might / could + the verb. No to. No -s. It is the same for I, you, he, she, we and they.

✓ Correct✗ Not correct
She might come.She might to come / might comes.
They could win.They could to win.

Negative: use may not or might not. — “I might not have time tomorrow.”

3. Sure or not sure?

How sure?WordExample
Sure ✅willCars will be electric.
Not sure 🤔may / might / couldCars might fly!

Future words — click each card

Six easy words for talking about the future. Tap to see the meaning and an example.

the future
noun
The time that has not happened yet — tomorrow, next year, in 50 years.
"In the future, life might be very different."
a robot
noun
A machine that can move and do jobs, sometimes like a person.
"A robot could clean my house one day!"
technology
noun
Modern machines and computers that help us do things.
"Technology might change very fast."
a machine
noun
A thing with parts that does a job for us.
"This machine may help doctors in the future."
to invent
verb
To make something completely new for the first time.
"Someone might invent a flying car."
to change
verb
To become different.
"The world could change a lot in 30 years."
