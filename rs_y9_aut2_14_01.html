What is the Design Argument?

🔒Reveal answer

Answer: The idea that the complexity and order in the universe suggests an intelligent designer (like God). Examples: the precise balance of the universe, beauty of nature.

What is the First Cause Argument?

🔒Reveal answer

Answer: The argument that everything needs a cause, so the universe must have been caused by God, who is the "uncaused cause."

What is Religious Experience as evidence for God?

🔒Reveal answer

Answer: Personal experiences of God (prayer, feeling of peace, answered prayers) that some people claim show God is real.

What is the Problem of Evil argument against God?

🔒Reveal answer

Answer: If God is all-powerful and all-good, why does evil and suffering exist? This challenges belief in God.

The Conflict Thesis

For much of history, people have seen science and religion as fundamentally opposed. This is called the "Conflict Thesis."

Key claim: Religion asks about why things exist and what gives life meaning. Science asks how things work. These are completely different questions.

Historical examples of conflict:

  • Galileo (1564–1642): Italian scientist who used the telescope to observe the sky. He supported the heliocentric model (sun at centre, Earth orbits). The Catholic Church said no—Earth is the centre. Galileo was tried and put under house arrest for the rest of his life.
  • Evolution debate: When Darwin published On the Origin of Species (1859), many Christians said it contradicted Genesis. Some still do today.
  • Medical treatments: Some faith communities reject vaccines or medical care, trusting only in prayer.

These conflicts made people think religion and science are enemies forever.

Four Models of Religion & Science

But modern scholars have identified four different ways these can relate:

CONFLICT Religion and science are enemies. Cannot both be true.
INDEPENDENCE Religion and science answer different questions. Both valid, but separate realms.
DIALOGUE Religion and science can learn from each other and have conversations.
INTEGRATION Religion and science are complementary. Together they give a fuller picture.

Most scientists today believe science and religion are either independent or can have dialogue.

Are Scientists Religious?

Religious Scientists Today

Francis Collins (born 1950) is a leading geneticist who led the Human Genome Project—one of the biggest scientific achievements of the 21st century. He is also a devout Christian. He sees no contradiction: "To me, DNA is the language in which God spoke life into being."

Georges Lemaître (1894–1966) was a Belgian priest and cosmologist. He developed the Big Bang theory—the leading scientific explanation for how the universe began. His discovery came from pure science, not religion. But as a priest, he also saw God's hand in creation.

About 45% of scientists today say they believe in God or a higher power. This includes physicists, biologists, and astronomers.

Christianity & Science

Christian scientists argue:

  • God created the laws of nature that science discovers
  • Studying science is studying God's creation
  • Evolution and God can both be true

Islam & Science

Islamic tradition has strong scientific roots:

  • The Qur'an encourages study of the natural world
  • Medieval Islamic scientists made major advances in maths, medicine, astronomy
  • Many Muslim scientists see science as deepening faith

Buddhism & Science

Buddhist philosophy aligns with science in some ways:

  • Buddhism emphasises observation and testing ideas
  • The Dalai Lama encourages dialogue with science
  • No creation story to contradict evolutionary science

Humanism & Science

Humanists see science as the best tool for understanding:

  • Science, not revelation, is the path to truth
  • No conflict—just prioritise what can be tested
  • Religion may be valuable for ethics, but not truth claims

Key Concepts

What was the Conflict Thesis?

Answer: The historical view that religion and science are enemies and cannot both be true.

What did Galileo discover, and why was it controversial?

Answer: He proved the Sun is at the centre (heliocentric), not Earth. The Church said Earth was the centre because of the Bible.

What are the four models of religion & science?

Answer: Conflict (enemies), Independence (separate realms), Dialogue (learn from each other), Integration (complementary).

Name a modern scientist who is religious.

Answer: Francis Collins (geneticist, Christian) or Georges Lemaître (priest & cosmologist who proposed Big Bang).

Can a person be both scientific and religious?

Answer: Yes—many scientists are religious. They see science as studying how the world works, religion as addressing meaning and purpose.
