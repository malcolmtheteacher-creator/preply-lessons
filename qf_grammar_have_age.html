🎯 I Have 20 Years → I Am 20 Years Old

Age uses BE not HAVE in English | A2 Level

Let's see what you notice...

Read this sentence. What feels wrong?

I have 20 years.

What's the correction?

✓ Correction

I am 20 years old.

📌 The Rule

In English, we use BE + age (not HAVE).

In Italian (Ho 20 anni), Spanish (Tengo 20 años), French (J'ai 20 ans), Portuguese (Tenho 20 anos) — you use HAVE. But English uses BE.

📚 The Rule (Dual Coding)

❌ Your Language (HAVE)

🇮🇹🇪🇸🇫🇷🇵🇹

🇮🇹 Ho 20 anni

🇪🇸 Tengo 20 años

🇫🇷 J'ai 20 ans

🇵🇹 Tenho 20 anos

All use HAVE + number

✅ English (BE)

🇬🇧

I am 20 years old.

She is 35.

How old are you?

He was 10 when...

Always BE + number (+ years old)

💡 Note: "Years old" is optional: "I'm 20" and "I'm 20 years old" are both correct!
