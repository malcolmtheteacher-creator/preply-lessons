Europe: One Continent, Many Cultures

“Europe” sounds like one place. But there are about fifty countries and many languages.

Today: you will learn about the countries and languages of Europe, and use there is and there are.
Talk first:
  • Which European countries do you know?
  • Which languages do you hear in Europe?
  • Is Europe one place or many places?

Key words

Read the sentence. What does the bold word mean? Then click Reveal.

1. Europe is a continent.
continent (n) — one of the very big areas of land in the world. Example: “Europe is a continent.”
2. The border is in the mountains.
border (n) — the line between two countries. Example: “The border is in the mountains.”
3. They speak a different language there.
language (n) — the words people speak in a country. Example: “They speak a different language there.”
4. Spain is a member of the European Union.
member (n) — a person or country in a group. Example: “Spain is a member of the European Union.”
5. Every country is different.
different (adj) — not the same. Example: “Every country is different.”
6. The name “Europe” comes from a Greek myth.
myth (n) — an old story, often about gods. Example: “The name “Europe” comes from a Greek myth.”
Talk it through:
  • Which word is new for you? Say a sentence with it.
The stereotype: “All Europeans are the same.”

The story

Part 1 — How many countries?

The name “Europe” comes from a Greek myth. About 45 to 50 countries are in Europe. About 740 million people live there. The east border of Europe is the Ural Mountains.

Quick check:

1. Where does the name “Europe” come from?

2. How many people live in Europe?

1. From a Greek myth.

2. About 740 million.

Discuss:
  • Can you name five European countries?

Part 2 — Many languages

Europeans speak many languages. Spanish and Italian are Romance languages. German and Swedish are Germanic languages. Polish and Czech are Slavic languages. The European Union has 27 members and 24 official languages.

Quick check:

1. How many members does the European Union have?

2. Name two Romance languages.

1. 27.

2. Spanish and Italian.

Discuss:
  • How many languages do you speak?

Part 3 — What they share

Europeans share a lot. They have a long history of trade and travel. In the Schengen area, people can cross borders without a passport check. Since 1987, millions of young people have studied in another country with the Erasmus programme.

Quick check:

1. Can people cross borders easily in the Schengen area?

2. What is Erasmus?

1. Yes.

2. A programme for studying in another country.

Discuss:
  • Would you like to study in another country?

Grammar — there is and there are

There is + one thing: “There is a border in the mountains.”
There are + many things: “There are about fifty countries.”
Negative: There isn't / There aren't.

1. Choose the word

1. about fifty countries in Europe. (There is / There are)

2. a big mountain on the border. (There is / There are)

3. 24 official languages in the European Union. (There is / There are)

1. There are

2. There is

3. There are

2. Make your own

1. In my country, there is ______.

2. In my country, there are ______.

3. In my town, there isn't ______.

1. ...a big river. / ...a famous city.

2. ...many mountains. / ...two languages.

3. ...an airport. / ...a train station.
