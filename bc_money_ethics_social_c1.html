Debate Collocations

These word combinations are frequently used in debate contexts.

Solid evidence Strong, reliable proof or data supporting a claim
Sweeping generalization A broad, oversimplified statement without adequate specificity
Strong argument A convincing case supported by relevant evidence
Valid point A legitimate, well-reasoned observation or statement
Core issue The central, most fundamental aspect of a problem
Opposing view An alternative or conflicting perspective on a topic
Justify position To provide valid reasons or evidence for a viewpoint
Address concern To directly respond to or handle a worry or objection

Debate Topics

Prepare to discuss these current issues from multiple perspectives.

Topic 1: Artificial Intelligence

Statement: Children should learn how to use AI to help them with their schoolwork.

Consider: What are the benefits? What are the concerns? How can technology be used responsibly in education?

Topic 2: Digital Entertainment

Statement: Video games are a waste of time.

Consider: What educational value might games have? How much screen time is healthy? What are the social benefits or drawbacks?

Topic 3: Technology in Education

Statement: Children need to learn how to use technology at an early age.

Consider: What technical skills are essential? Are there age-appropriate considerations? What about digital literacy and safety?

Topic 4: AI Governance

Statement: AI technology should be controlled by governments.

Consider: What role should government play? What about innovation and competition? How do we ensure ethical development?
