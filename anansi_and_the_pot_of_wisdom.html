A famous old story from West Africa

The Story

Read it slowly. Read it out loud. The pictures help you. Stuck? Click 🇫🇷 for that part.

🕷️
1. Long ago, there was a clever spider. His name was Anansi. Anansi wanted all the wisdom in the world. He wanted it only for himself.
Il y a très longtemps, il y avait une araignée très maligne. Elle s'appelait Anansi. Anansi voulait toute la sagesse du monde, rien que pour lui.
🫙💡
2. So Anansi walked everywhere. He took a little wisdom from here, and a little wisdom from there. He put it all in a big pot.
Alors Anansi marcha partout. Il prit un peu de sagesse ici, et un peu de sagesse là. Il mit tout dans un grand pot.
🌳
3. "Now I am the cleverest in the world!" he said. "I will hide my pot at the top of a tall tree. Then nobody can take it."
« Maintenant, je suis le plus malin du monde ! » dit-il. « Je vais cacher mon pot en haut d'un grand arbre. Comme ça, personne ne pourra le prendre. »
🧗🫙
4. Anansi tied the pot to the front of his body. He started to climb. But the pot was in the way! It pushed him back. He tried again and again. He could not climb.
Anansi attacha le pot devant lui et commença à grimper. Mais le pot le gênait ! Il le repoussait. Anansi essaya encore et encore, mais il n'arrivait pas à grimper.
👦
5. His small son watched him from the ground. The boy said: "Father, it is easy. Tie the pot on your back. Then you can climb the tree."
Son petit garçon le regardait depuis le sol. Il dit : « Papa, c'est facile. Attache le pot dans ton dos. Comme ça, tu pourras grimper à l'arbre. »
😦
6. Anansi stopped. His little son was right! But Anansi had all the wisdom in his pot… so how did the boy know? Anansi felt very silly.
Anansi s'arrêta. Son fils avait raison ! Mais toute la sagesse était dans son pot… alors comment le garçon savait-il ça ? Anansi se sentit très bête.
😡⬇️💥
7. Anansi was so angry that he dropped the pot. It fell down, down, down — and it broke! All the wisdom flew up into the wind and went all over the world.
Anansi était si en colère qu'il laissa tomber le pot. Il tomba, tomba, tomba — et il se cassa ! Toute la sagesse s'envola dans le vent et partit dans le monde entier.
🌍😊
8. And that is why no one person has all the wisdom today. Everybody — big and small, young and old — has a little piece. And that is a good thing.
Et voilà pourquoi personne n'a toute la sagesse aujourd'hui. Tout le monde — grand et petit, jeune et vieux — en a un petit morceau. Et c'est une bonne chose.

Did you understand?

True or false? Say your answer, then check. (🇫🇷 helps you read the question.)

a) Anansi wanted to share the wisdom with everybody.
False. He wanted all the wisdom only for himself.
Anansi voulait partager la sagesse avec tout le monde. (Vrai ou faux ?)
b) Anansi put the wisdom in a pot.
True. He put all the wisdom in a big pot. 🫙
Anansi a mis la sagesse dans un pot. (Vrai ou faux ?)
c) Anansi tied the pot on his back, so it was easy to climb.
False. He tied it on his front, so he could not climb. His son told him to use his back.
Anansi a attaché le pot dans son dos, alors c'était facile de grimper. (Vrai ou faux ?)
d) At the end, everybody in the world has a little wisdom.
True. The wisdom went all over the world, so everybody has a little piece. 🌍
À la fin, tout le monde a un peu de sagesse. (Vrai ou faux ?)

Easy grammar: talking about the past

The story happened a long time ago. So we use the past. Many verbs change in the past.

L'histoire s'est passée il y a longtemps, alors on utilise le passé. Beaucoup de verbes changent au passé : certains ajoutent -ed, d'autres sont irréguliers (on les apprend par cœur !).
now: wantpast: wanted ✅ (add -ed)
now: trypast: tried ✅
now: gopast: went ⭐ (special)
now: saypast: said ⭐
now: takepast: took ⭐
now: havepast: had ⭐
now: fallpast: fell ⭐
now: breakpast: broke ⭐

⭐ = a special verb. We just learn it! And the verb to be: I/he/she/it → was, you/we/they → were.

Now you try

Choose the past verb. Say the sentence, then check.

1. Anansi __________ all the wisdom. (want)
Anansi wanted all the wisdom.
2. He __________ the wisdom in a pot. (put — this one is the same!)
He put the wisdom in a pot. (put → put, no change!)
3. His son __________ , "Tie the pot on your back." (say)
His son said, "Tie the pot on your back."
4. The pot __________ down and __________ . (fall / break)
The pot fell down and broke.
5. Anansi __________ to climb, but he could not. (try)
Anansi tried to climb, but he could not.
6. The little boy __________ a very good idea. (have)
The little boy had a very good idea.

Let's talk about it

Easy questions. Just say what you think. (🇫🇷 helps you understand the question.)

  • Do you know this story? Is it famous in your family?
    Connais-tu cette histoire ? Est-elle connue dans ta famille ?
  • Do you know another story about a clever animal? 🦊 🐢 🕷️
    Connais-tu une autre histoire avec un animal malin ?
  • Anansi wanted everything for himself. Was that a good idea? Why / why not?
    Anansi voulait tout pour lui. Était-ce une bonne idée ? Pourquoi, ou pourquoi pas ?
  • The little boy was clever too. Can young people teach older people things? Tell me one!
    Le petit garçon était malin lui aussi. Les jeunes peuvent-ils apprendre des choses aux plus grands ? Donne un exemple !
  • What is one thing you are good at, that you can teach someone?
    Quelle est une chose que tu sais bien faire et que tu pourrais apprendre à quelqu'un ?

Not sure what to say? Start like this: "I think…", "In my family…", "One clever animal is…", "I can teach…".
