My Words

Every lesson teaches you new words. My Words keeps them in one place and helps you remember them, so they don't disappear between lessons.

Open My Words

How it works

  1. Open a lesson. A bar at the bottom of the page says how many words from the lesson are saved. You don't need to do anything.
  2. Practise. Press Practise them in the bar, or open My Words at any time. Each review takes a few minutes: choose the meaning, fill the gap in a sentence, or type the word.
  3. Come back often. Words you get right return after a day, then a few days, then weeks. Words you find difficult return sooner. This is how words move into your long-term memory.
  4. Add your own words. Heard or read a word you want to keep? Use + Add a word in the bar, or the Add a word tab in My Words. Write the word, what it means and, if you can, an example sentence.

Two ways to play

Standard is a calm review with a progress bar, points and a daily streak. Challenge mode is a timed-feeling round with three strikes and a count of correct answers in a row. Switch at any time with the button at the top of My Words.

Keep your words safe

Your progress is saved in your browser on this device. Once a week or so, open the Backup tab in My Words and press Save backup file. If you change computer or your browser clears its data, press Restore from file and everything comes back.

On a phone, add My Words to your home screen so it opens like an app.
