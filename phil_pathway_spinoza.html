Baruch Spinoza

1632-1677 · Amsterdam

A Dutch-Jewish lens grinder who said God and Nature are the same thing.

Guess first

Spinoza earned his living by...

A life in a few steps

  1. 1632Born in Amsterdam into a Portuguese-Jewish family.
  2. 1656Expelled from the Jewish community because of his ideas.
  3. Earns his living grinding glass lenses for microscopes and telescopes.
  4. 1670Publishes a book on religion and politics without his name on it.
  5. 1677Dies at 44. His great book, Ethics, is published after his death.

Why Spinoza still matters

He argued for freedom of thought and for a God who is the whole of nature, not a person in the sky.
