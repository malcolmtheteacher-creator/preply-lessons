Plato

c. 428-348 BC · Athens

Socrates' student, who wrote down his teacher's conversations and founded the Academy.

A life in a few steps

  1. c. 428 BCBorn into a rich Athenian family.
  2. As a young man he becomes a follower of Socrates.
  3. 399 BCSocrates is executed. Plato leaves Athens and travels.
  4. c. 387 BCFounds the Academy, a school in Athens that lasts for centuries.
  5. c. 348 BCDies, aged about 80.

Why Plato still matters

His story of prisoners in a cave, who think shadows are the real world, is still one of the most famous ideas in philosophy. One modern thinker said all Western philosophy is 'a series of footnotes to Plato'.
