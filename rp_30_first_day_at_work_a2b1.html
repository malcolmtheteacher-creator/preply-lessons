First Day at Work

New place, new people, new coffee machine. It is your first day at a new job — time to say hello, be friendly, and ask for help.

The Scene 🎬

It is Monday morning. Today is your first day at a new job. You are a little nervous, but you are also happy. You came early, because you did not want to be late.

Nobody expects you to know everything today. That is normal — you are new! But you want to be friendly and easy to work with. So you will say hello, tell people your name, and ask for help when you need it.

A colleague comes to meet you. Their name is Priya. She is kind, and she works here for a long time. She will show you your desk and help you start. You just need to be friendly and polite.

Warm up — talk for one minute

  1. How do you feel on a first day — at a job, a school, a new place? Happy? Nervous? Both?
  2. When you don't know something, is it easy or hard for you to ask for help? Why?

The Conversation 💬

Read it out loud, one person each, then change. Find the phrases from your toolkit. Listen for the friendly, polite words.

ColleagueGood morning! Are you the new person? I'm Priya. Welcome!
YouGood morning, Priya. Yes, I'm Sam. Today is my first day. Nice to meet you.
ColleagueNice to meet you too! Come with me. This is your desk. Your computer is here.
YouThank you. I'm new, so I have a lot of questions. Is that OK?
ColleagueOf course! Ask me anything. It is always better to ask.
YouThank you. Can you show me how to log in? I have a password, but I don't know where to start.
ColleagueSure. Click the blue icon, then type your password. That's it — you're in.
YouGreat, thank you. One more question — where is the kitchen? I would like a coffee later.
ColleagueHa! Good question. It is down the hall, on the left. And the coffee is free.
YouPerfect. How long have you worked here, Priya?
ColleagueSix years! I started at this desk too. It is a nice place. You will like it.
YouThat's good to hear. Can I help you with anything this morning? I want to be useful.
ColleagueThat's very kind! Not now — read the handbook first. I will find you a job later.
YouOK. Thank you for your help today, Priya. You made my first day easy.

The Echo 🕰️

About 2,200 years ago, the first emperor of China was afraid of dying. So he made a big army from clay — about 8,000 soldiers — to go with him. Each soldier had a different face.

These clay soldiers were new too. They stood in long lines, ready for their first day — but then they waited under the ground for 2,000 years. People found them in 1974, near the city of Xi'an.

Your first day is much easier! No clay army, no waiting 2,000 years — just a new desk, a new computer, and a coffee to find. And best of all, you have a kind person to help you.

Two questions to talk about

  1. The emperor made 8,000 soldiers to feel ready for his big new start. What helps you feel ready for a big new day?
  2. Every clay soldier had a different face. At a new job, is it better to be the same as everyone, or to be yourself? Why?
