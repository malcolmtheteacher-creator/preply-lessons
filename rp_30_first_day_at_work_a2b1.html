First Day at Work

New place, new people, new coffee machine. It is your first day at a new job — time to say hello, be friendly, and ask for help.

This is the easier version. Want the harder one? → Full B2/C1 version →

The Scene 🎬

It is Monday morning. Today is your first day at a new job. You are a little nervous, but you are also happy. You came early, because you did not want to be late.

Nobody expects you to know everything today. That is normal — you are new! But you want to be friendly and easy to work with. So you will say hello, tell people your name, and ask for help when you need it.

A colleague comes to meet you. Their name is Priya. She is kind, and she works here for a long time. She will show you your desk and help you start. You just need to be friendly and polite.

Warm up — talk for one minute

  1. How do you feel on a first day — at a job, a school, a new place? Happy? Nervous? Both?
  2. When you don't know something, is it easy or hard for you to ask for help? Why?

The Conversation 💬

Read it out loud, one person each — then change. Listen for the friendly, polite words.

ColleagueGood morning! Are you the new person? I'm Priya. Welcome!
YouGood morning, Priya. Yes, I'm Sam. Today is my first day. Nice to meet you.
ColleagueNice to meet you too! Come with me. This is your desk. Your computer is here.
YouThank you. I'm new, so I have a lot of questions. Is that OK?
ColleagueOf course! Ask me anything. It is always better to ask.
YouThank you. Can you show me how to log in? I have a password, but I don't know where to start.
ColleagueSure. Click the blue icon, then type your password. That's it — you're in.
YouGreat, thank you. One more question — where is the kitchen? I would like a coffee later.
ColleagueHa! Good question. It is down the hall, on the left. And the coffee is free.
YouPerfect. How long have you worked here, Priya?
ColleagueSix years! I started at this desk too. It is a nice place. You will like it.
YouThat's good to hear. Can I help you with anything this morning? I want to be useful.
ColleagueThat's very kind! Not now — read the handbook first. I will find you a job later.
YouOK. Thank you for your help today, Priya. You made my first day easy.

Useful Phrases 🧰

Eight phrases for your first day at any job. Say each one out loud BEFORE you look at the answer. Guessing first helps you remember.

Say it with a smile. On a first day, a friendly voice is very important. You do not need long sentences. Short and warm is perfect. And it is always OK to ask for help — good colleagues like it.

🚪 Saying hello

Phrase 1

You meet a colleague for the first time. You want to say your name and that you are new. Which one sounds friendly and clear?

A. "New employee. First day."
B. "Hi, I'm Sam. I'm new here — today is my first day."
C. "You don't know me."
B — "Hi, I'm Sam. I'm new here — today is my first day."
Say your name first, then say you are new. It is warm and easy. A sounds cold, and C sounds strange. Your name makes you a person, not just "the new one".
Phrase 2

Someone tells you their name. You want to answer in the normal, polite way. Complete it: "Nice to ___ you."

"Nice to meet you."
This is the normal thing to say when you meet a person for the first time. You can also say "Nice to meet you too" when they say it first.

🎣 Asking for help

Phrase 3

You don't know how the computer works. You want a colleague to explain it, step by step. Complete it: "Can you ___ me how it works?"

"Can you show me how it works?"
"Can you show me…?" is a friendly way to ask for help. Use it for anything new: "Can you show me how to log in?", "Can you show me where it is?"
Phrase 4

You want to find the kitchen, but you don't know the way. Put the words in order to ask:

where isthekitchen?
"Where is the kitchen?"
"Where is the…?" is the simple way to find any place: "Where is the toilet?", "Where is the meeting room?", "Where is your desk?"

🌤️ Small talk — being friendly

Phrase 5

A colleague asks, "How is your first day?" You want to sound happy and friendly, not just say one word. Which answer is best?

A. "OK."
B. "Fine."
C. "It's good, thank you. Everyone is very friendly."
C — "It's good, thank you. Everyone is very friendly."
Say a little more than one word, and say something nice about the people. A and B are not wrong, but they are cold. Warm words make people like you.
Phrase 6

You want to ask your colleague about themselves — a friendly question. Complete it: "How long have you ___ here?"

"How long have you worked here?"
Ask people about themselves — it is a great way to make friends. Other easy questions: "Do you like it here?", "What do you do here?"

💛 Helping & saying thank you

Phrase 7

You want to be useful, not only ask for things. You offer to help. What can you say? (Say it out loud first, then look.)

"Can I help you with anything?"
On your first day, this is a lovely thing to say. It shows you want to work and to help. You can also say: "Is there anything I can do?"
Phrase 8

The end of the day. Your colleague helped you a lot. You want to say a warm thank you. What do you say? (Say it out loud first.)

"Thank you very much for your help today."
Say "thank you" and say why ("for your help today"). This makes it warm and real. A smile helps too!

🔑 Make them yours

These phrases work at work, at school, in a shop — everywhere. Write your own sentences. Say each one out loud first.

"Hi, I'm ___. I'm new here." — Say hello with your own name.

"Can you show me ___?" — What do you want someone to show you? (how to log in, where the kitchen is…)

"How long have you worked here?" — Think of a friendly question to ask a new colleague.

Quick test: close your eyes and say your whole first morning out loud — say hello → ask for help → make small talk → offer to help → say thank you. When it is easy, go to Your Turn.

Your Turn 🎭

Now it is your first day. Say your answers out loud FIRST, then write them. Use the timer if you like.

Round 1 — you are the new person 🪪

Your colleague speaks — you answer. Be friendly: say hello, ask for help, say thank you.

ColleagueGood morning! Are you the new person? I'm Priya. Welcome!
ColleagueNice to meet you! This is your desk. Your computer is here.
ColleagueThere you go — you're logged in. Anything else you're not sure about?
ColleagueDown the hall, on the left. Easy! So, how is your first day?
ColleagueEight years! I like it here. Settle in — shout if you need anything.
ColleagueThat's very kind! Read the handbook for now. See you at lunch.

Round 2 — now YOU are the colleague 🤝

Change places. It is your office and your team. Be kind and friendly. You remember your own first day! Help the new person feel welcome.

New personHi, I'm Alex. Today is my first day. Nice to meet you.
New personThank you! Can you show me how to log in? I don't know where to start.
New personGreat, thank you. And where is the kitchen? I would like a coffee.
New personPerfect. Can I help you with anything this morning?
The Twist

🌩️ You forget a name

One more time — no help this round. A colleague comes to say hello and tells you their name. Two minutes later, another person asks, "What is their name?" — but you forgot it! What do you say? Stay friendly and honest. You can say: "Sorry, I forgot — it is my first day! Can you tell me again?" It is OK to forget on day one. Just smile and ask again. Start the timer and speak!

Before you finish…

Think about what you said. Can you say "yes" to these?

  • I said hello and gave my name clearly.
  • I asked for help in a friendly way ("Can you show me…?").
  • I asked a friendly question about my colleague.
  • I offered to help, not only asked for help.
  • I said "thank you" warmly at the end.

Can't say yes to one? That is your practice — try Round 1 again.

The Echo 🕰️

About 2,200 years ago, the first emperor of China was afraid of dying. So he made a big army from clay — about 8,000 soldiers — to go with him. Each soldier had a different face.

These clay soldiers were new too. They stood in long lines, ready for their first day — but then they waited under the ground for 2,000 years. People found them in 1974, near the city of Xi'an.

Your first day is much easier! No clay army, no waiting 2,000 years — just a new desk, a new computer, and a coffee to find. And best of all, you have a kind person to help you.
