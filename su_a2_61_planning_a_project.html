Making Predictions with "Will"

Learn different ways to express certainty and uncertainty about the future.

Strong Certainty

I'm certain that...

I'm certain the locals will love it.

I'm certain the swimming pool will bring more tourism.

I'm quite sure / definitely...

Tourists will definitely see our town as a good location.

The restaurant will probably bring more business.

Medium Certainty

I think... will probably...

The swimming complex will probably bring more tourism.

I think the highway will probably bring more business.

Uncertainty

I'm not quite sure... / may...

I am not quite sure how successful we will be.

The highway may bring more business to our town.

Contractions with "Will"

In spoken English, we often shorten "will" to 'll.

I will → I'll bring more tourists

You will → You'll see the benefits

He/She will → He'll/She'll be happy

We will → We'll make it work

They will → They'll visit us

Project Ideas for Your City

Read about each project idea. Which one do you think is the best? Which would you like to have in your city?

New Swimming Complex

What it is: Modern sports and swimming centre with Olympic-size pools.

Benefits: Brings tourism, attracts young people, improves health and exercise.

Question: Will it be popular with the locals?

New Restaurant Quarter

What it is: Area with many new restaurants serving different foods from around the world.

Benefits: Attracts tourists, creates jobs, brings evening entertainment.

Question: Will small local restaurants compete?

New Museum

What it is: Modern museum showing local history and art from around the world.

Benefits: Brings educated tourists, improves the city's culture, creates jobs.

Question: Is there enough space downtown?

New Highway Connection

What it is: Fast road connecting the city to nearby towns and cities.

Benefits: Brings business visitors, connects communities, helps trade.

Question: Will it create pollution and noise?

New Public Park

What it is: Large green space with gardens, walking paths, and play areas.

Benefits: Good for health, brings families, attracts tourists, peaceful.

Question: Is a park more popular than commercial development?

Your Project Presentation

Choose one project or create your own idea. Prepare to discuss and present it.

What you need to do:

  • Choose a project for your city
  • Explain what it is and where it will be
  • Say who will benefit from it
  • Predict if it will be successful
  • Answer questions about your idea

Structure for Your Talk

1. Introduce: "I think we should build a new..."

2. Describe: "It will have... / It will be..."

3. Benefit: "This will bring... / The locals will love..."

4. Predict: "I'm quite sure it will... / I'm not sure..."
