Future: Using "Will"

We use "will" to talk about the future. Learn these structures.

Positive Predictions

Subject + will + verb

A new park will bring more families.

Tourists will want good restaurants.

It will + verb + object

It will make the city nicer.

It will help local business.

Expressing Certainty

I am certain / sure + will

I am certain the locals will love it.

I am quite sure it will work.

...will probably / definitely...

The swimming complex will probably bring more tourism.

Tourists will definitely see our town as a good place.

I am not quite sure + will

I am not quite sure how successful we will be.

I am not sure it will bring more business.

Prediction Structures

Very sure about the future — use "definitely"
QUITE SURE

PROBABLE

Think it is likely, but not 100% sure
POSSIBLE

NOT SURE

City Project Ideas

Read about these project ideas for different cities. Which ones are good? What new project would you suggest?

New Park Project

Idea: Build a big green park in the city centre with trees, gardens, and a play area.

Benefits: Families will visit. Better air. Nice place to rest.

Cost: Expensive

Sports Centre

Idea: Build a sports centre with gym, swimming pool, and sports courts.

Benefits: People will exercise more. More jobs. Healthy city.

Cost: Very expensive

Restaurant Area

Idea: Create a nice food and restaurant area by the river or square.

Benefits: Tourists will come. Good for local business. Nice atmosphere.

Cost: Medium

Museum or Cultural Centre

Idea: Build a museum or arts centre to show local history and culture.

Benefits: Tourists will visit. Education. City looks modern.

Cost: Expensive

Better Transport

Idea: Build a bus system or train line to connect the city better.

Benefits: Easier to move around. Less cars. Saves money for families.

Cost: Very expensive

Speaking Task

Present your project idea to your partner. Explain why it is good for your city.

What to include:

  • What is your project idea?
  • Why is it good for the city?
  • What will happen after the project?
  • How certain are you it will work?

Remember to use:

  • "Will" structure: "It will... / They will... / This will make..."
  • Certainty phrases: "I am certain...", "probably", "definitely", "not quite sure"
  • City vocabulary: park, restaurant, sports centre, museum, highway
