Adverbs of Frequency for Future Plans

Use these adverbs to talk about how often you"ll do things in the future:

From most frequent to least frequent:

  • quite often — regularly, many times
  • sometimes — not regularly, occasionally
  • occasionally — not very often
  • rarely — not often, almost never
  • never — not at all

Example sentences:

"I"ll quite often work on projects with other team members."

"I"ll sometimes take on new challenges in my role."

"I"ll rarely avoid working under pressure."

Pronunciation: Natural Pauses

Native speakers use natural pauses when speaking. A short pause (/) comes at commas, and a longer pause (//) comes at the end of thoughts or sentences.

"I"m keen to learn, / and I work conscientiously // in all my projects."

"I"ve developed my leadership skills / and I"m ready for a new challenge. //"
