Topic 1: Home & Living

10 minutes

Home & Living

Questions about where you live, your house/apartment, and your neighborhood

Key Vocabulary (click to reveal meaning)

apartment / flat
A home inside a building with other homes
"I live in an apartment on the 5th floor."
neighborhood
The area around your home
"My neighborhood is very quiet."
convenient
Easy and comfortable to use/reach
"It's convenient because the supermarket is nearby."
peaceful / noisy
Quiet and calm / Loud with lots of sounds
"The area is peaceful at night."
grow up
To become an adult (childhood to now)
"I grew up in a small town."
move (to)
To change your home to a new place
"I moved to Hanoi three years ago."

Common IELTS Questions - Practice with Sentence Builders

Where do you live?
Build your answer:
I live in city/town, in which part. + REASON I like it because reason. + EXAMPLE For example, specific detail.
Model Answer (Band 5)
I live in Hanoi, in a busy area near the center. I like it because it's very convenient for shopping and eating out. For example, there's a great coffee shop just two minutes from my apartment.
Do you live in a house or an apartment?
Build your answer:
I live in a house / an apartment. It has description. + REASON I chose it because reason. + EXAMPLE Like, example.
Model Answer (Band 5)
I live in an apartment. It's on the 8th floor and has a nice view. I chose it because the rent is affordable for me. Like, I only pay about 5 million dong per month, which is good for this area.
What do you like about your neighborhood?
Build your answer:
I really like that positive thing. + REASON It's great because why it matters to you. + EXAMPLE For instance, specific example.
Model Answer (Band 5)
I really like that my neighborhood is peaceful and has many trees. It's great because I can relax after a busy day at work. For instance, there's a small park nearby where I sometimes go for evening walks.

Topic 2: Work & Studies

10 minutes

Work & Studies

Questions about your job, what you study, and your daily work life

Key Vocabulary (click to reveal meaning)

work for / work at
Work for = the company; Work at = the place
"I work for Samsung. I work at their office in District 7."
responsible for
In charge of; your job is to do this
"I'm responsible for customer service."
colleague
A person you work with
"My colleagues are very friendly."
flexible hours
You can choose when to start and finish work
"I have flexible hours, so I start at 10am."
challenging / rewarding
Difficult but interesting / Makes you feel good
"The job is challenging but rewarding."
major in
Your main subject at university
"I majored in business administration."

Common IELTS Questions - Practice with Sentence Builders

What do you do? / Do you work or study?
Build your answer:
I work as job title at company/place. + EXTEND I'm responsible for what you do. + REASON I enjoy it because reason.
Model Answer (Band 5)
I work as an accountant at a trading company in Ho Chi Minh City. I'm responsible for checking invoices and preparing monthly reports. I enjoy it because I like working with numbers and it's a stable job.
Do you like your job? / Is your job interesting?
Build your answer:
Yes, I like/enjoy my job. + REASON The main reason is why. + EXAMPLE For example, specific thing you like.
Model Answer (Band 5)
Yes, I quite like my job. The main reason is that I have good colleagues and a nice boss. For example, we often have lunch together and help each other with difficult tasks.
What did you study at university?
Build your answer:
I studied subject at university name. + REASON I chose it because reason. + EXAMPLE Actually, how it connects to now.
Model Answer (Band 5)
I studied economics at Hanoi University. I chose it because I was interested in how businesses work. Actually, it really helped me get my current job in the finance department.
Now You Try!

Answer this question out loud using all 3 strategies:

"Would you like to change your job in the future?"

Topic 3: Free Time & Hobbies

10 minutes

Free Time & Hobbies

Questions about what you do when you're not working, your hobbies and interests

Key Vocabulary (click to reveal meaning)

spend time (+ ing)
How you use your time
"I spend time watching movies."
keen on / into
Really interested in something
"I'm really into photography."
hang out (with)
Spend relaxed time with friends
"I like to hang out with my friends on weekends."
take up
Start a new hobby or activity
"I took up yoga last year."
relax / unwind
Rest and feel calm after stress
"Reading helps me unwind after work."
prefer... to...
Like one thing more than another
"I prefer staying home to going out."

Common IELTS Questions - Practice with Sentence Builders

What do you do in your free time?
Build your answer:
In my free time, I usually activity. + REASON I enjoy it because reason. + EXAMPLE For example, last weekend I specific example.
Model Answer (Band 5)
In my free time, I usually watch Korean dramas or go to coffee shops. I enjoy it because it helps me relax and forget about work stress. For example, last weekend I watched a new drama for about 5 hours - I couldn't stop!
Do you prefer indoor or outdoor activities?
Build your answer:
I prefer indoor/outdoor activities. + REASON This is because reason. + EXAMPLE Like, I really enjoy specific activity.
Model Answer (Band 5)
I prefer indoor activities, to be honest. This is because the weather in Vietnam is often very hot, and I don't like sweating too much. Like, I really enjoy playing video games or reading at home with air conditioning!
Have you taken up any new hobbies recently?
Build your answer:
Yes, I recently started new hobby. + REASON I decided to try it because reason. + EXAMPLE So far, your experience.
Model Answer (Band 5)
Yes, I recently started learning to cook. I decided to try it because I want to eat healthier and save money. So far, I can make about 5 dishes, and my favorite is fried rice with vegetables.

"Is there a hobby you would like to try in the future?"
