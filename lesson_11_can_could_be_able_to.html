🔍 Discover the Grammar

Look at these sentences. What patterns do you notice?

📚 Can - Present Ability

NOW Things you are able to do right now

"I can speak three languages."
✓ I have the ability now. It's a general fact about me.
"She can't drive yet - she's only 15."
✓ She doesn't have this ability right now.
"Can you play the piano?"
✓ Asking about a general ability.

📚 Could - Past Ability

PAST Things you were able to do before

"I could run very fast when I was young."
✓ General ability in the past - I had this skill.
"She couldn't swim until she was 10."
✓ She didn't have this ability until age 10.
"Could you speak English as a child?"
✓ Asking about past general ability.

📚 Be Able To - All Times!

FLEXIBLE Works in past, present, AND future

"I am able to help you tomorrow."
✓ Present form - same as "can" but more formal.
"She was able to finish the project on time."
✓ Past - she succeeded in doing it (specific situation).
"Will you be able to come to the party?"
✓ Future - asking about future ability. (Can't say "will can"!)
"I've been able to save money this year."
✓ Present perfect - with perfect tenses. (Can't say "have could"!)

💡 Key Discovery Question

Discuss with your teacher:

Why do we need "be able to" when we already have "can" and "could"?

Hint: Think about future tense and perfect tenses...

⚖️ Could vs Was Able To

This is the tricky part! There's an important difference in the PAST.

🔴 Important Rule!

Could Was/Were Able To
General ability in the past
"I had this skill"		 Specific achievement - one time success
"I managed to do it"
"I could swim when I was 5."
(general skill I had)		 "The boat sank, but I was able to swim to shore."
(I succeeded in that moment)
"She could speak French fluently."
(general past ability)		 "She was able to convince them to help."
(she succeeded once)

🎯 Quick Check - Which is Correct?

1. "When I was a child, I climb trees really well."

a) could
b) was able to

2. "The exam was hard, but I pass it."

a) could
b) was able to

3. "My grandmother cook amazing food."

a) could
b) was able to

4. "The traffic was terrible, but we arrive on time."

a) could
b) were able to

5. "By next year, I speak English fluently."

a) will can
b) will be able to

📊 Summary Table

Time Can/Could Be Able To
Present can / can't am/is/are able to
Past (general) could / couldn't was/were able to
Past (specific success) ❌ Don't use "could" ✅ was/were able to
Future ❌ No "will can" ✅ will be able to
Perfect tenses ❌ No "have could" ✅ have been able to

    📝 Lesson Review

    🔑 Key Points

    What We Learned Today:

    Form Use Example
    can / can't Present ability "I can swim."
    could / couldn't Past general ability "I could run fast as a child."
    was/were able to Past specific achievement "I was able to pass the exam."
    will be able to Future ability "I will be able to drive soon."
    have been able to Perfect tense ability "I've been able to save money."

    ⚠️ Common Mistakes

    ❌ WRONG: "I will can come tomorrow."

    ✅ RIGHT: "I will be able to come tomorrow."

    ❌ WRONG: "I have could finish the project."

    ✅ RIGHT: "I have been able to finish the project."

    ❌ WRONG: "After trying hard, I could pass the test." (specific achievement)

    ✅ RIGHT: "After trying hard, I was able to pass the test."
