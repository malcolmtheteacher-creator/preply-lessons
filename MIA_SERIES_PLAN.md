# Mia's English — Series Plan, Lessons 7–50

One continuing story: Mia's first year in her new town. Cast: **Mia** (hero), **Tom** (kind neighbour, 50s), **Rosa** (café owner), **Ana** (Mia's younger sister — first appears Lesson 15, returns for the finale), plus the café cat (from Lesson 9). Grammar climbs gently from A1 to early A2. Each lesson = one story beat + ONE tiny grammar pattern + 8 new words + 2 scene images.

Build each lesson per `MIA_A1_LESSON_SPEC.md` (clone `a1s_03_where.html`; engine byte-identical; own `LESSON_NAME`).

Format: `N || slug || title || grammar pattern || story beat || image1 :: scene || image2 :: scene`
Character tokens for image scenes: {MIA} {TOM} {ROSA} {ANA}.
