Grammar: spelling, numbers and floors

At a hotel you say your name letter by letter, and you hear room numbers, floors and times. Slow and clear wins.

Can you spell that? Say one letter at a time: "N-A-V-A-R-R-O." Two letters the same? "double R". The hard letters: A (ay), E (ee), I (eye), G (jee), J (jay), R (ar), W (double-u), Y (why).
Room numbers: say the numbers one by one: room 512 = "five one two", room 308 = "three oh eight". Check: "Sorry — five, one, two?"
Floors: the ground floor (0), the first floor (1), second (2), third (3), fourth (4), fifth (5), tenth (10). "My room is on the fifth floor."
Times: "Breakfast is from seven to ten." "Check-out is at eleven." Questions: "What time is breakfast?" "What time is check-out?"
