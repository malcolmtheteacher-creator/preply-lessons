Easy start

A relaxed warm-up. Answer each question in two or three sentences, not one word. Every question has a way to begin. If you get stuck, press the button for an example, then say your own version.
What did you do last weekend?

Start with: On Saturday I…, and then…

On Saturday I met some friends for lunch, and then we walked by the river. On Sunday I stayed at home and rested, because I was tired.
What is a normal morning like for you?

Start with: I usually… first, and after that…

I usually get up at seven. First I have a coffee, and after that I check my messages. Then I leave for work at about eight.
What do you do to relax?

Start with: To relax, I… It helps me because…

To relax, I go for a long walk. It helps me because I can stop thinking about work.
Tell me about a place you like.

Start with: I like… because… You can…

I like a small park near my home because it is quiet. You can sit under the trees and read.
What are you looking forward to?

Start with: I am looking forward to… because…

I am looking forward to my holiday next month because I need a rest and I want to see my family.
What was the last good thing that happened to you?

Start with: Last…, I… I felt…

Last week I finished a difficult project. I felt very proud, and my boss said thank you.
Make every answer longer with the 3-part trick: answer → because / for example → one more detail.

Tell me more

Choose a topic, or let the page choose for you. Prepare for one minute with key words only. Then tell your story. Your listener asks follow-up questions to help you say more.
Press a button to get a story topic.

Tell it in five steps

  1. Set the scene. It was… / This happened when I was… / A few years ago, I…
  2. Say who was there. I was with… / There was a man who…
  3. Say what happened. First…, then…, suddenly…
  4. Say how you felt. I felt… / I was surprised / I could not believe…
  5. Say how it ended. In the end… / Looking back, I… / I learned that…

Say it better

Each pair: say the short one, then say the longer one, then make your own.

Better: I like it because…, and on top of that, …

Better: To begin with, …, but in the end, …

“I do not agree.”

Better: I see your point, but…

Better: It is difficult because…, especially when…

Opinion chat

Opinions are easier when you start with your own life and then widen the idea. Choose a question, or let the page choose.
Press a button to get a question.

Build your answer in five parts

  1. Say what you do or think. I usually… / I would say… / For me, …
  2. Give a reason. …because… / The main reason is…
  3. Give an example. For example, last year I…
  4. Show the other side. On the other hand, … / But sometimes…
  5. Finish. So overall, … / A bit of both works best.
Useful words: it depends · advantage · disadvantage · stress · flexible · organised · safe · expensive · convenient · the problem is…

An example you can copy and change

Question: Do you plan your holidays, or just go and see?

“I’m the kind of person who plans a little. For example, on holiday I book the hotel first, because I don’t want any stress. But on the other hand, I leave the days free, so I can be spontaneous. For me, a bit of both works best.”

Then, if you feel ready

Now the bigger idea: is it better to plan everything or to improvise? Use the same five parts.
