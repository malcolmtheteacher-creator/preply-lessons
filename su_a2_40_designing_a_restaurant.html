Superlatives Grammar

Superlatives describe the highest or lowest level of a quality. We use them to compare three or more things.

Superlative Rules

Rule 1: Short adjectives (1 syllable)

Add -est to the adjective

cheap → the cheapest | fast → the fastest

Rule 2: Adjectives ending in -y

Change y to -iest

friendly → the friendliest | healthy → the healthiest

Rule 3: Long adjectives (2+ syllables)

Use "most" + adjective

comfortable → the most comfortable | delicious → the most delicious

Rule 4: Irregular adjectives

Special forms

good → the best | bad → the worst

Useful Language

How to talk about your restaurant experiences:

"Where have you experienced the slowest service?"

"The friendliest waiter I ever had was at..."

"The most delicious food I've ever eaten was..."

"This restaurant had the best atmosphere of any place I've visited."

"The prices were the most expensive I have ever paid."

Design Your Restaurant

A company wants to open a new restaurant in your area. Your job is to design and plan it!

Answer each question below to create your restaurant concept:

1. Restaurant Name

What will you call your restaurant?

2. Theme / Style

What style or theme? (Examples: Italian, Japanese, futuristic, traditional, street food, fusion, etc.)

3. Type of Food and Drink

What cuisine will you serve? What drinks will you offer?

4. Menu Highlights

What are 3-5 special dishes or drinks you will serve?

5. Atmosphere and Decoration

Describe the atmosphere: lighting, music, furniture, colors, decoration

6. Staff Uniform

What will your staff wear? Describe their uniforms.

7. Price Range

Is your restaurant budget, mid-range, or luxury?

8. Target Customers

Who will eat at your restaurant? (Examples: families, couples, business people, students, tourists, etc.)

9. What Makes It Special?

Why would people choose YOUR restaurant? What is unique about it?
