Key words — learn them, then make your guess

Read each sentence and guess the meaning of the word in bold. Then click Reveal to check. Say each word out loud.

1. The two countries were enemies for a hundred years. They never talked and they often fought.
enemy (noun) — a person or group who hates you and wants to hurt you. Example: "Cats and dogs are not always enemies."
2. Nobody knew about their plan. It was a secret.
secret (noun) — something that you do not tell other people. Example: "Can you keep a secret?"
3. She stood on the balcony outside her bedroom and looked down at the garden.
balcony (noun) — a small open area on the outside wall of a building, above the ground. Example: "We had breakfast on the balcony."
4. A friar lives a simple life in a church community and helps people. He can also marry couples.
friar (noun) — a kind of Christian holy man, like a monk, who lives simply and helps people. Example: "The friar listened to their problem."
5. The king was angry, so he decided to banish him: he had to leave the city and never come back.
banish (verb) — to send someone away from a place as a punishment. Example: "The king banished him from the city."
6. Don't drink that! It's poison. It can kill you.
poison (noun) — something that makes you very ill or kills you if you eat or drink it. Example: "Some mushrooms contain poison."
7. When people from the family died, they were put in the family tomb, a stone room under the church.
tomb (noun) — a large stone place where dead people are put. Example: "The king's tomb is in the old church."
8. He was too late. When he arrived, the train had gone. He missed it by one minute.
miss (verb) — to be too late for something, so it happens without you. Example: "Hurry, or we'll miss the bus!"

The story, in three parts

William Shakespeare wrote Romeo and Juliet as a play more than 400 years ago. Here is the story in simple English. Read each part, answer the quick check, then talk.

Part 1 — Love at a party

In the Italian city of Verona there were two rich families, the Montagues and the Capulets. They were enemies. Nobody remembered why. Their young men often fought in the streets, and the Prince of Verona was very angry about it.

One night the Capulets had a big party. Everybody wore a mask. Romeo, a young Montague, went to the party with his friends. It was dangerous, but with a mask nobody knew him.

While the music was playing, Romeo saw a beautiful girl. He fell in love at once. They talked, and they kissed. Then he learned her name: Juliet, the daughter of the Capulets. His enemy's daughter!

Later that night Romeo climbed into the Capulets' garden. Juliet was standing on her balcony. She didn't know that Romeo was listening. "Oh Romeo, Romeo," she said, "why are you a Montague? A name is not important. A rose with a different name still smells sweet." Romeo answered her, and they decided to marry. The next day, Friar Laurence married them in secret. He hoped that the marriage could end the war between the families.

Quick check — answer from memory, then reveal:

1. Why was it dangerous for Romeo to go to the party?

2. Why did Friar Laurence agree to marry them?

1. Because it was the Capulets' party, and Romeo was a Montague, their enemy.

2. He hoped the marriage could end the fighting between the two families.

Talk:
  • Romeo and Juliet decided to marry after one night. Is that a good idea? Why or why not?

Part 2 — A fight in the street

An hour after the wedding, Romeo met Tybalt, Juliet's cousin. Tybalt wanted to fight him. But Romeo didn't want to fight. Tybalt was his family now! Romeo's best friend, Mercutio, was angry, so he fought Tybalt himself.

While they were fighting, Romeo stepped between them. Tybalt's sword went under Romeo's arm and killed Mercutio. Romeo was so angry that he fought Tybalt and killed him.

The Prince was furious. He banished Romeo: Romeo had to leave Verona and never come back. Romeo went to live in another city, Mantua.

At the same time, Juliet's father had a plan. He wanted Juliet to marry a rich young man called Paris, in three days. Juliet said no, but her father shouted at her. She couldn't tell him the truth: she was already married.

Quick check — answer from memory, then reveal:

1. Why did Romeo kill Tybalt?

2. What was Juliet's new problem?

1. Because Tybalt killed Mercutio, Romeo's best friend, and Romeo was very angry.

2. Her father wanted her to marry Paris, but she was already married to Romeo.

Talk:
  • What could Juliet do now? Think of three ideas.

Part 3 — The letter that never arrived

Juliet ran to Friar Laurence. He gave her a small bottle. "Drink this before the wedding," he said. "You will sleep very deeply, and everybody will think you are dead. Your family will put you in the tomb. I will write to Romeo. He will come, and when you wake up, you can go away together."

Juliet drank it, and the plan worked. Her family cried and put her in the tomb. But the friar's letter never arrived in Mantua. Instead, Romeo's servant came with terrible news: "Juliet is dead!"

Romeo bought poison and rode back to Verona. He went into the tomb and saw Juliet. She looked beautiful, as if she was sleeping. He kissed her and drank the poison. A few minutes later, Juliet woke up. She saw Romeo dead next to her. She took his knife and killed herself.

In the morning the two families came to the tomb. They were crying, and at last they shook hands. The war between the Montagues and the Capulets was over, but their children were dead.

Quick check — answer from memory, then reveal:

1. What was the friar's plan?

2. What went wrong?

1. Juliet would drink something to sleep and look dead. Romeo would get a letter, come to the tomb and take her away when she woke up.

2. The letter never arrived. Romeo heard that Juliet was dead, so he drank poison. Juliet woke up a few minutes too late.

Talk:
  • At the end, the families stop fighting. Is this a happy ending or a sad ending? Why?

Grammar — Was Doing / Did

Stories often have a long action in the background, and a short action that stops it or happens in the middle of it.

Past continuous (was / were + -ing) = the long background action.
Past simple = the short action that happens in the middle.
• "I was cooking dinner when the phone rang."
• "While we were walking home, it started to rain."
Use while before the long action. Use when before the short action.

1. Choose the correct form

1. Romeo (dance / was dancing) when he first saw Juliet.

2. While Juliet (stood / was standing) on the balcony, Romeo was listening in the garden.

3. Mercutio was fighting Tybalt when Romeo (stepped / was stepping) between them.

4. Juliet (slept / was sleeping) in the tomb when Romeo arrived.

1. was dancing · 2. was standing · 3. stepped · 4. was sleeping

2. Complete with the verbs in brackets

1. While the families (sleep), the friar (write) a letter to Romeo.

2. Romeo (live) in Mantua when his servant (bring) the bad news.

3. Juliet (wake up) while the friar (run) to the tomb.

1. were sleeping · wrote

2. was living · brought

3. woke up · was running

3. Your turn — finish the sentences about your life

1. Yesterday I was … when …

2. While I was … , …

Explain it in your own words:
  • Which action is long and which is short in your two sentences? Why do we use while in one place and when in the other?

Going deeper

Talk about these questions. There are no right answers, only good reasons.

Useful language: "I think…", "In my opinion…", "I agree, because…", "I'm not sure. Maybe…", "It's their fault because…".
1 · Love at first sight

Romeo and Juliet fell in love in one minute. Do you believe in love at first sight? Do you know a real story like this?

2 · Whose fault?

Who is most responsible for the deaths: Romeo, Juliet, the friar or the parents? Why?

3 · Parents and children

Juliet's father chose her husband. In the past, this was normal in many countries. Should parents help their children choose a husband or a wife? Why or why not?

4 · A modern story

If Romeo and Juliet lived today, the letter would be a text message. What problems could a modern Romeo and Juliet have? Would the story end differently?
