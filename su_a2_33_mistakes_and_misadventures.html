Telling Stories: Past Simple & Past Continuous

When we tell stories about mistakes, we use two important verb tenses:

Past Simple

Use for finished actions in a story:

Example:
"The family made the big decision, and in May they all travelled to New York."

Past Continuous

Use for an action that was interrupted by another action:

Example:
"Paul was walking home from work when he saw the old man."

Or use for two things happening at the same time:

Example:
"I was working hard while my cat was sleeping on my desk."

Practice: Choose the Correct Form

Click "Show answer" for each sentence:

1. We played / were playing tennis when John hurt / was hurting his ankle.

Answer: were playing / hurt

2. Susie watched / was watching a film when she heard / was hearing the noise.

Answer: was watching / heard

3. He worked / was working in a bank when he met / was meeting his wife.

Answer: was working / met

4. What did you do / were you doing when I called / was calling you last night?

Answer: were you doing / called

5. When I left / was leaving the house, it snowed / was snowing.

Answer: left / was snowing
