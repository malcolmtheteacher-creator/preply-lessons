🎯 Articles: A, An, The

Master the three English articles | A1 Level

Let's see what you notice...

Read this sentence. What's missing?

She is teacher.

What's the correction?

✓ Correction

She is a teacher.

📌 Why?

In English, singular countable nouns need an article: a, an, or the.

This is a problem for Russian and Polish speakers—your languages don't have articles at all!

Use "a" or "an" when you mention a job, person, or thing for the FIRST time.

🌍 Why this is tricky for you:

Russian and Polish speakers: Your languages have NO articles, so you might think the sentence is fine without one.

📚 The Traffic Light System

Think of articles like a traffic light: green = new/first mention, yellow = specific/known, red = general.

🟢 A / AN

First mention

or professions

"I'm a doctor"

🟡 THE

Specific/known

only one, or already mentioned

"The moon is bright"

⭕ ZERO

General plural

or uncountable general

"Dogs are loyal"

Practice: Fill in a, an, the, or nothing

1. nurse works in hospital.

2. sun is very hot.

3. I saw elephant at zoo yesterday.

4. apples are fruit.

5 (Bonus): He is engineer at company.

💡 A vs AN: Use "an" before vowel sounds (a, e, i, o, u): an apple, an engineer, an orange. Use "a" before consonant sounds: a dog, a teacher, a hospital.
