Meet the thinker · B1

Jean-Jacques Rousseau

1712-1778 · Geneva and France

A Swiss-born writer who said: 'Man is born free, and everywhere he is in chains.'

Guess first

'Man is born free, and everywhere he is...'

A life in a few steps

  1. 1712Born in Geneva. His mother dies a few days later.
  2. 1728Runs away from Geneva at 15.
  3. 1750Wins an essay prize by arguing that civilisation makes people worse.
  4. 1762The Social Contract and Emile. Both books are banned and he has to flee.
  5. 1778Dies near Paris.

Why Rousseau still matters

His ideas about freedom and the will of the people inspired the French Revolution, and his ideas about childhood changed education.

Main works

The Social ContractEmileConfessions

Did you know?

He wrote a famous book about how to bring up children, but he left his own five children at an orphanage.
