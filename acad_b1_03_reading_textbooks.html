Reading Textbooks Effectively

Master skimming, scanning, and using textbook features to study smarter

5:00

Reading Strategies

Skimming

What: Reading quickly to get the general idea

When: Before detailed reading; to decide if text is relevant

How: Read titles, headings, first sentences of paragraphs, summaries

Time: Usually 2-3 minutes for a page

Scanning

Looking for Specific Information

What: Reading quickly to find particular information

When: You need a specific fact, date, or definition

How: Move your eyes quickly; use keywords; look at bold text

Time: Usually 1-2 minutes to find specific information

Using Textbook Features

Tools That Help You Learn

Table of Contents: Understand the structure and find topics

Chapter Headings: Show main ideas and organisation

Bold Words: Indicate key vocabulary and definitions

Summaries: Review main points quickly

Glossary: Find definitions of difficult words

Index: Locate specific topics throughout the book

Questions: Test your understanding and guide your reading

Active Reading

Engage With the Text

Highlight: Mark key ideas and vocabulary (don't highlight too much!)

Annotate: Write notes in margins

Ask Questions: What does this mean? Why is it important?

Connect: Link new information to what you already know

Summarise: Write brief summaries of each section

Step-by-Step Reading Method

1. Skim First: Read the title, introduction, headings, and summary

2. Decide: Is this section relevant to your topic?

3. Set Questions: What do you want to learn from this section?

4. Read Actively: Highlight, annotate, and engage with the text

5. Summarise: Write a brief summary in your own words

6. Review: Check your understanding with questions at the end

Key Takeaways

✓ Skim first to understand structure and relevance

✓ Scan to find specific information quickly

✓ Use textbook features like headings, glossary, and index

✓ Active reading helps you remember better

✓ Don't highlight everything — only key ideas

✓ Review frequently to reinforce learning
