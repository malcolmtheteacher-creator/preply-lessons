The reductio move

If a principle is really true, it has to hold even when you push it hard. Reductio ad absurdum means: take the rule, follow it as far as it goes, and see whether it still makes sense at the edges. If it doesn't — the rule needs more nuance.

For each take below, click each step to reveal a harder version. Defend it for 30 seconds. If you can't — that's the breaking point. Find it.
