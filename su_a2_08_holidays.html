Holiday Activities with "Go"

How do we talk about holiday activities? We use different forms of "go" depending on the activity.

Go + verb-ing

bowling
windsurfing
sightseeing
hiking

Go to + place

the beach
a show
a restaurant
a casino
the theatre
a café

Go on + activity

a boat tour
a guided tour
a bus trip

Now you try

Which of these activities do you like doing on holiday?

Think about your favourite activities. Would you like to go bowling? Would you like to go to the theatre? Would you like to go on a boat tour?

Tell Your Holiday Story

First, make some notes. Then you'll describe your holiday using 3 minutes to speak.

📝 Make Notes

Where did you go? Who did you go with? What did you do? Did you enjoy it?

3:00

Describe your holiday. Use these questions to help you:

  • Where did you go?
  • Who did you go with?
  • What did you do there?
  • What was your favourite activity?
  • Did you enjoy it? Why or why not?
  • Would you go back? Why?
