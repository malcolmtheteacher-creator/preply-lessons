Fitness Vocabulary

Click each card to reveal the definition.

Cardiovascular Relating to the heart and blood vessels; aerobic exercise that strengthens the heart
Endurance The ability to sustain physical effort over a long period without getting tired
Stamina Physical or mental strength and energy to sustain prolonged effort
Flexibility The ability of a joint or muscle to move through its full range of motion
Strength training Exercise using resistance (weights, bands) to build muscle and bone density
Aerobic exercise Physical activity that increases heart rate and breathing, like running or cycling
Hydration Drinking enough water or fluids to maintain optimal body function
Recovery The process of allowing muscles and body to rest and repair after exercise

Practice: Complete the sentences

1. Regular ________ exercise is essential for heart health.

2. Building ________ requires consistent strength training.

endurance / stamina

3. Stretching improves your ________ and reduces injury risk.

4. Proper ________ and recovery are as important as the workout itself.

Key Discussions

1. Fitness Activity Fair

Imagine you are at a Fitness Activity Fair where different activities are on display. What would be the most popular activity and why?

2. Individual Differences

People have different fitness goals and abilities. How would you adapt fitness advice for someone who is elderly, injured, or a beginner?

3. Barriers to Fitness

What are the biggest obstacles people face when trying to stay fit? How can these be overcome?

4. Persuasive Language

You need to persuade someone to try a new fitness activity. What persuasive expressions would you use? Create your own sentences.

  • "It's absolutely essential that..."
  • "You simply must try..."
  • "Without a doubt, this activity will..."
  • "I'd strongly urge you to..."

5. Reflection

What have you learned about different fitness approaches? How might this help your own fitness journey?
