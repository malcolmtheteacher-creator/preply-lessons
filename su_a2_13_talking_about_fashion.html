Talking About Fashion

A2 Level English

How Has Fashion Changed?

Fashion is always changing. Let's think about how the world of fashion has changed over the past 30 years.

How has fashion changed in the last 30 years in the world?

Think about: clothing styles, accessories, tattoos, piercings, and what teenagers or young people wear.

How has fashion changed in your country over the last 30 years?

Are the changes the same as in the rest of the world? What is different?

When you answer, notice: Are you talking about the past or the present? How do you explain changes?

Grammar: "Used to" vs Present Perfect

When we talk about fashion changes, we use two different grammar structures. Let's look at the difference.

Three Groups of Sentences

Group A: What was true in the past (but not now)
Use "used to" + infinitive
"When I was young only a few people used to have tattoos"
"It used to be mostly men that had tattoos"
"Piercing used to be very dangerous"
Group B: Action that started in the past and continues now
Use "have/has" + past participle
"People like David Beckham have had beards for about 5 years"
"Tattoos have been fashionable for centuries"
"Piercing has been part of different cultures for years"
Group C: Change that has happened recently
Use "have/has" + past participle
"Beards have become very fashionable"
"The tattoo has become very popular recently"
"Celebrities have made tattoos popular"

Key Grammar Rules

used to + infinitive = a situation in the past that is no longer true

have/has + past participle = an action that started in the past and affects the present (duration or recent change)

Why do we use different forms?

"Used to" tells us something ended in the past. "Have/has" tells us something connects to now — either it continues or it just changed.

Practice: Discussion Questions

Use "used to" or "have/has" to answer these questions. Try to use the target language naturally.

When you were young, what did teenagers use to wear?
Example: "When I was young, teenagers used to wear baggy jeans, oversized t-shirts, and trainers. But now they wear tight jeans and branded clothes."
When you were younger, what did your parents use to wear?
Example: "My parents used to wear more formal clothes. My father used to wear a tie every day. Now they wear more casual clothes."
Do you think people in the past used to look better than today? Or worse?
Example: "I think people today look better because they have more choices. But people in the past used to have better quality clothes that lasted longer."
