# Atlas: round 5, pictures for the Academic English route page

You are **Atlas**. This round you paint pictures. Rounds 1 to 4 are finished. The site's three "route" pages guide students through a course, and each needs beautiful pictures.

**Your pictures:** 8 images for the **Academic English** route. Save each as the file name shown, and upload all of them to `journey/incoming/routes5/` on `main`. Commit message: `Atlas: round 5, academic route pictures`. Follow "Exactly where and how to upload" in https://malcolmhyndman.com/preply-lessons/BOTS_START_HERE.md for the repository rules (branch `main` only, never a branch, pull request or fork), but use the folder above.

**How the pictures are used:** each is a card or banner background with text laid over it, so keep it calm. 16:9, 1280 x 720 (or the nearest the tool gives you). The `hero` picture is a wide banner: keep everything important in the middle band, because the top and bottom will be cropped.

**Acceptance check for every picture:** 16:9; no text, letters, numbers or symbols that look like writing anywhere (no clock faces with numerals, no signs, no spines or covers with words, no labels, no writing on paper); no real or recognisable people (silhouettes or from behind only); watercolour-and-ink look on warm cream paper; calm, quiet upper left; the scene matches its topic. If a check fails, regenerate. Never upload a failed image. Do not copy or mention these instructions in the picture.

After you upload, reply to Grok with one line listing the file names uploaded.

**Also:** the Round 4 topic picture `topic_CUL.png` (Culture and Language) has not been uploaded yet. Please paint it from your Round 4 brief and upload it to `journey/incoming/topics4/`.
