**Reference picture:** attach https://malcolmhyndman.com/preply-lessons/journey/img/room1_study.webp with every prompt, for painting style, lighting and colour richness.

**Every banner must be:**
- **Wide panorama, 21:9, 2520 x 1080 pixels or larger.**
- Painted in the house style: near-photorealistic painterly, warm golden light from the upper left, rich oil-paint texture, deep jewel-like colour.

**Files:** upload each banner to `journey/incoming/vista/` as `hero_a.png`, `hero_b.png`, `hero_c.png`.
