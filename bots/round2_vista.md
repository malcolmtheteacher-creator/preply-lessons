# Vista — round 2: three wide banners for three course pages

Three of Malcolm's course pages have a wide banner slot at the top with only a flat colour in it: **Big Questions**, **Psychology Questions** and **Beyond Reasonable Doubt**. Your job is to paint the three banners. (These are for course pages that already exist. They are not for the homepage.)

**Style for this task (overrides rule 1 of the shared rules):** this task is NOT the painted house style. These pictures go on pages that already use a hand-illustrated **watercolour and ink on warm cream paper** look, with a rich jewel-tone palette and fine linework. The style is already written inside every prompt below; use the prompt exactly and do not attach the room picture. Rules 3 to 7 of the shared rules still apply in full.

**Shape.** Each banner is about 3:1. If your tool cannot make 3:1, make a **16:9 image, 1600 x 900 or larger**, and keep everything important inside the **middle 55% of the height**, because Claude will crop it to 3:1. Keep the **centre calm and darker**: the page puts a title over it.

**Upload.** Follow "Exactly where and how to upload" in https://malcolmhyndman.com/preply-lessons/BOTS_START_HERE.md: repository `malcolmtheteacher-creator/preply-lessons`, branch `main` only, never create a branch, pull request or fork. Your folder for this task is `journey/incoming/vista2/` (create it by typing it into the upload path). Commit message: `Vista: round 2, <what you uploaded>`. After committing, check the files are listed on `main` and tell Malcolm the exact paths.

**Acceptance check for every image:** wide; no text, letters or numbers anywhere; no people (only the small lantern figure in the first two, as the prompt says); watercolour-and-ink look on warm cream paper; the centre is calm and darker; nothing important outside the middle band. If a check fails, regenerate; never upload a failed image. If the tool cannot give the size or shape asked for, give the nearest it can and tell Malcolm; do not enlarge, recolour or crop by hand.
