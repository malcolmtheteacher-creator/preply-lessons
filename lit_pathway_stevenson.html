Meet the writer · B1

Robert Louis Stevenson

1850-1894 · Scotland

A Scottish writer of adventure and horror who gave us Treasure Island and Dr Jekyll and Mr Hyde.

Stevenson spent his last years living...

A life in a few steps

  1. 1850Born in Edinburgh. He is often ill as a child.
  2. 1883Treasure Island is published as a book.
  3. 1886Strange Case of Dr Jekyll and Mr Hyde is a huge success.
  4. 1890Settles on Samoa, an island in the Pacific Ocean, for his health.
  5. 1894Dies on Samoa, aged 44.

Why people still read Stevenson

Pirates with parrots and treasure maps come from Treasure Island. And 'a Jekyll and Hyde' is now an everyday phrase for a person with two very different sides.
