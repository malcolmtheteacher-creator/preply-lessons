Common Abbreviations

Symbols and Short Forms

w/o = without

→ = leads to, causes, results in

← = comes from, is caused by

↑ = increases, rises

↓ = decreases, falls

∴ = therefore

∵ = because

e.g. = for example

etc. = and so on

approx. = approximately

vs/v = versus, against

Note-Taking Structure

Organizing Your Notes

Date & Topic: Always write the date and lesson topic at the top

Main Points: Use bullet points or numbers for main ideas

Indentation: Indent supporting details under main points

Underline Key Terms: Emphasise important vocabulary

Leave Space: Leave margins for additions and reviews

Mark Questions: Use ? for things you need to clarify later

Essential Vocabulary

Concise Brief and clear, using few words
Abbreviation A shortened form of a word or phrase
Legible Easy to read and understand
Retrieve Find and get information again
Highlight Mark or emphasise important information
Indent Start a line further to the right than usual
Margin The blank space around the edge of a page
Synthesis Combining different ideas into one whole

Exercise 3: Structure Notes

Organise this information into note form:

"The main point is that renewable energy is becoming more important. For example, solar power and wind energy are growing rapidly. This is because fossil fuels are running out and climate change is a serious issue."

• Renewable energy ↑ imp. | e.g. solar, wind power ↑ | ∵ fossil fuels ↓ + climate change
