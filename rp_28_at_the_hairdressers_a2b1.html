At the Hairdresser's

You know what you want. Now you must say it — in easy, polite English — and stop the hairdresser gently if the cut goes wrong.

This is the easier version. Want the harder one? → Full B2/C1 version →

The Scene 🎬

You have a big week. Maybe a party, maybe a new job, maybe a first date. So today you go to a new hairdresser near your home. It is your first time here. Nobody knows your hair.

You know what you want: a small, tidy cut. Not too short. You want to keep the length. This is not easy to say! The picture is in your head, but you must put it into simple words.

The hairdresser is friendly and likes to talk. They are fast with the scissors. But sometimes they cut a little too much. When that happens, you must stop them — in a kind, polite way, before it is too late.

Warm up — talk for one minute

  1. Do you like going to the hairdresser? Do you talk a lot, or do you sit quietly?
  2. Do you like your hair short or long? When did you last have a haircut?

Who's who

You
The Customer 💺

You want a small cut. You want to keep the length. You are polite, but you say "stop" nicely when the cut goes wrong.

The other person
The Hairdresser ✂️

Friendly and fast. They like to talk. They want you happy — but sometimes they cut too much. Tell them, kindly.

First: read the conversation in the next tab. Look at the polite words: please, thank you, I would like, sorry, can you…? These small words help a lot. It is OK to speak slowly.

The Conversation 💬

Read it out loud, one person each — then change. Listen for the polite words.

HairdresserHello! Come and sit down. What would you like today?
YouHello. Just a small cut, please. I want to keep the length.
HairdresserOK, no problem. Your hair is very long. And the fringe? Do you want me to cut it?
YouYes, please. But just out of my eyes. Not too short.
HairdresserOf course. So, are you going on holiday this year?
YouHa, I hope so! Nothing booked yet. And you?
HairdresserMe? I love the beach! Now, I will cut the back a little short…
YouSorry, can you stop for a moment? Please don't cut the back too short.
HairdresserNo problem! I will just tidy the ends. Is that OK?
YouYes, that's good. Thank you.
HairdresserNow look in the mirror. Do you like it?
YouHmm, this side is a little long. Can you make it the same, please?
HairdresserYes, of course. There. Is it better now?
YouIt's perfect! Thank you so much. I love it.

From memory — don't look up!

1. At the start, what two things does the customer want?

2. The hairdresser starts to cut. What does the customer ask them to stop doing?

3. At the end, the customer looks in the mirror. What small problem do they see?

1. A small cut, and to keep the length. (Also: the fringe cut, but just out of the eyes — not too short.)
2. Cutting the back too short. The customer says "please don't cut the back too short", so the hairdresser only tidies the ends.
3. One side is a little longer than the other. The customer asks the hairdresser to make it the same.

Useful Phrases 🧰

Eight phrases you can use at any hairdresser. Say each one out loud BEFORE you look at the answer. Guessing first helps you remember.

Say it with a smile. A friendly voice and the words please and thank you do a lot. It is OK to say "sorry" and to ask them to wait. Short and polite is perfect.

✂️ Saying what you want

Phrase 1

You sit down. You want only a small cut, not a big change. Which is the best, polite thing to say?

A. "Cut it all off."
B. "Just a small cut, please."
C. "Do what you want."
B — "Just a small cut, please."
This is clear and polite. A is a big change — dangerous! C gives all the power to the hairdresser. "Just a small cut" tells them: not too much.
Phrase 2

You do not want your hair shorter. You want the same length. Complete it: "I want to ___ the length."

"I want to keep the length."
"Keep" means "don't change it, leave it the same". You can also say "keep it long" or "don't take too much off".

💇 The fringe, and "not too short"

Phrase 3

You are worried they will cut too much. Ask them softly, with "please". Put the words in order:

pleasedon't cut ittoo short
"Please don't cut it too short."
"Please don't…" is a kind way to say "no" to something. "Too short" means shorter than you want. You can say it about anything: "please don't cut the back too short".
Phrase 4

You want your fringe short enough to see, but no shorter. Complete it: "Just out of my ___, please."

"Just out of my eyes, please."
"Out of my eyes" means: short enough so the hair is not in front of my eyes — but not shorter. It is a small, clear picture the hairdresser can see.

🪞 When it goes wrong

Phrase 5

The hairdresser starts to cut too much. You need to stop them, but stay friendly. Which is best?

A. "Stop! No!"
B. "Sorry, can you stop for a moment?"
C. "You are cutting it wrong."
B — "Sorry, can you stop for a moment?"
"Sorry" and "can you…?" make it soft and kind. A sounds scared and loud. C sounds angry. B stops them, but keeps everyone happy.
Phrase 6

You look in the mirror. It is shorter than you wanted. Say it in a soft, calm way. Complete it: "That is a little too ___."

"That is a little too short."
"A little" makes it soft and kind — not "that is very bad!". You can use it for other things too: "a little too fast", "a little too dark".

🌟 Make it right, and say thank you

Phrase 7

One side is longer than the other. You want both sides the same. Put the words in order:

can you make itthe same,please?
"Can you make it the same, please?"
"The same" means: both sides equal, not one long and one short. "Can you…?, please" is a polite way to ask for a small fix.
Phrase 8

You love your new hair! Say a warm, happy thank you at the end. (Say it out loud first.)

"It's perfect! Thank you so much. I love it."
Add "so much" to make your thank you bigger and warmer. "I love it!" makes the hairdresser happy — and they will look after you next time.

🔑 Make them yours

These phrases work at the hairdresser, in shops, and in cafés. Write your own sentences. Say each one out loud first.

"I would like ___, please." — What do you want to ask for next time you go out? (a small cut, a coffee, a bag…)

"Please don't ___." — When do you need to say "please don't" to someone? Write one example.

"Thank you so much for ___." — Who helped you this week? Say a warm thank you.

Quick test: close your eyes and say the whole visit out loud — sit down → say what you want → stop them nicely → look in the mirror → say thank you. When it is easy, go to Your Turn.

Your Turn 🎭

Now it is your haircut. Say your answers out loud FIRST, then write them. Use the timer if you like.

Round 1 — you are the customer 💺

The hairdresser speaks — you answer. Be polite: use please, thank you, and I would like.

HairdresserHello! Come and sit down. What would you like today?
HairdresserOK! And the fringe — do you want me to cut it?
HairdresserOf course. So, are you going anywhere nice this year?
HairdresserRight, I will take a little more off the back now…
HairdresserNow, have a look in the mirror. Do you like it?
HairdresserThere. Is it better now?

Round 2 — now YOU are the hairdresser ✂️

Change places. Now you cut hair. Be friendly and kind. Say "Hello", "Of course", "No problem", "Sorry".

CustomerHello. Just a small cut today, please. I want to keep the length.
CustomerJust out of my eyes with the fringe, please. Not too short.
CustomerSorry, can you stop for a moment? Please don't cut the back too short.
CustomerThis side is a little long. Can you make it the same, please?
CustomerIt's perfect! Thank you so much.
The Twist

💇 It is too short!

One more time — no help this round. You asked for a small cut. But you look in the mirror, and it is much shorter than you wanted! The hairdresser did not hear you well. Stay calm and polite. Tell them, in a kind way, that it is too short. Ask what you can do now. Do not get angry — if you are angry, the hairdresser stops helping you. Start the timer and speak!

Before you finish…

Think about what you said. Can you say "yes" to these?

  • I said what I wanted clearly — a small cut, keep the length.
  • I used "please" and "I would like" to ask for things.
  • I stopped the hairdresser in a kind, calm way.
  • I asked for a small fix politely ("Can you make it the same?").
  • I said "thank you" warmly at the end.

Can't say yes to one? That is your practice — try Round 1 again.

The Echo 🕰️

Long ago in Japan, a samurai's hair was very important. He wore his hair in a special knot on the top of his head. It told everyone who he was — his job, his family, his place in the world.

Then the government said: cut the knot off. For the samurai, this was very hard. It was not just a new haircut. It felt like losing who they were. Some men cried.

So a haircut is never only a haircut. When you sit in the chair, you give a stranger your hair — and you hope they give you back the person in your head. That is why it feels big!
