My Story Arc — Limits Are Only in the Mind

The clumsy kid → the goalkeeper match → thrown into logistics → asking for help → the gift

  • Only a few friends

  • Jealousy, but quiet admiration
2The Crack
Where were you? What happened?
  • A P.E. match at school
  • Tried to copy a famous goalkeeper
  • "I am good at this!"
  • The impulse to change — a subconscious trigger
  • Limits are only in my mind
  • Ended up playing in the school team
3The Struggle
What was hard? What did it cost?
  • Started working in logistics
  • Educational background unrelated to it
  • Opportunity given — but no scaffolding
  • Thrown in like a child into deep water
  • Survive or thrive
  • Felt like I was sinking at the time
4The Becoming
How did it shape who you are today?
  • Accepted I needed to ask for help
  • Being vulnerable was not easy
  • But it pushed me forward
  • Towards real understanding
  • Limits are only in the mind — again
5The Gift
Why should the listener care?
  • Limits are only in the mind!
