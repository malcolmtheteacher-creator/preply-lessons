Future Forms - Three Ways to Talk About the Future

How do we arrange meetings and talk about future plans? Read this dialogue about scheduling a meeting.
Nick:
"Actually, I'm meeting the customer service manager in the afternoon and then I'm going to play golf."
Paul:
"Friday?"
Nick:
"I'll probably be out of the office in the early morning and I'm going to be quite busy in the afternoon. Let me check my diary, hold on… OK, I'm giving a presentation that afternoon but I'll be free after five."
Paul:
"Oh, I probably won't be here after five o'clock. What about early on Monday then?"
Nick:
"Paul, I'm so sorry, I'm meeting with another customer, but after about three o'clock I'll be free."
Paul:
"Great! That suits me. So Monday at about three, in meeting room four?"
Nick:
"Great, I'll note it in my diary now. Thanks Paul, see you then."

1. WILL + verb — "I'll note it in my diary now"

✓ Use this for a decision made NOW (at the moment of speaking). There is NO plan before this moment.

Examples: "I'll help you." "I'll call you tomorrow." "We'll meet here."

2. GOING TO + verb — "I'm going to play golf"

✓ Use this for a decision made in the PAST. There IS a plan before now.

Examples: "I'm going to the beach." "They're going to start the project next month."

3. PRESENT CONTINUOUS — "I'm meeting the customer service manager"

✓ Use this for a SCHEDULED event (a fixed plan, appointment, or booking).

Examples: "I'm catching the 3pm train." "She's giving a presentation tomorrow." "We're having lunch at 12."

Understanding the Difference

Read these sentences from the dialogue:

  • "I'm giving a presentation" = scheduled, fixed appointment
  • "I'm going to be quite busy" = plan made before, not fixed/scheduled
  • "I'll be free after five" = decision at moment of speaking
  • "I'm meeting another customer" = scheduled appointment

Language for Arranging Events

  • "I need to talk to you about…"
  • "Can we arrange a meeting for…?"
  • "Let me check my diary…"
  • "Great, I'll note it in my diary now."

Time Phrases to Remember

  • on time, in time, time off, time out
  • kill time, save time, waste time, spend time
  • pressed for time

Future Forms Summary

  • Will: Decision at this moment → "I'll note it down"
  • Going to: Plan from before → "I'm going to be busy"
  • Present continuous: Scheduled/fixed → "I'm meeting the customer"
