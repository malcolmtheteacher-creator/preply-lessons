    phrases: Sorry, can I just come in here? | If I could just finish that thought… | That's a good question — let me think for a moment. | How shall I put this… | I don't know the exact word, but it's the thing that…"  /  "You know when…"  /  "What I'm trying to say is… | From where I sit, the bigger risk is waiting. | I take your point, but I see it slightly differently. | When you say 'premature', what exactly do you mean? | Can I come back to you on that? | So, if I've got this right, the proposal is now a soft launch.
- **Working the Room** — Turn a coffee break into real contacts.  [skill: networking]
    phrases: Mind if I join you? | How are you finding it so far? | I'm Sofia, by the way. | I help [who] [do what]." → e.g. "I help mid-size companies move their products across borders without losing their money. | Oh, interesting — how did you get into that? | That's funny you say that, because… | Shall we swap details? | I'll drop you a line next week. | I don't want to monopolise you… | This has been the best conversation of the morning — talk soon.
- **The Presentation** — Survive the questions after your talk.  [skill: presenting & Q&A]
    phrases: I'll keep this brief — three things, then I'll take questions. | So — I'm happy to open it up. What have you got? | Good question — just so I answer the right thing, do you mean…? | Let me take that in order." / "Give me a second to do that justice." / "That's worth a proper answer — one moment. | I hear you, and it's a fair challenge — the way I'd look at it is… | You're right that we can't run the team hot forever — and that's exactly why… | I don't have that figure to hand — let me follow up with you after. | I'd rather not guess at a number in a room like this." / "I'd rather give you something accurate than something quick." / "A wrong figure helps nobody — let me get you the real one. | If there's nothing else, let me leave you with one thought. | The headline isn't the number — it's that we now know how to…
- **The Difficult Conversation** — Give a colleague hard feedback, kindly.  [skill: feedback]
    phrases: Can we talk about something? I'd rather be straight with you than let it sit. | …something I'd rather talk about properly than let it become an awkward thing. | In the last three [meetings / reports / weeks], X happened, and the effect was Y. | The last few times, your part has come in after the deadline" / "I've noticed a pattern I wanted to flag" / "It's happened three times now, so I don't think it's a one-off. | Help me understand what's going on for you. | I'm not questioning your effort — I'm flagging the impact. | That's fair — and it's still landing on the rest of us, so I'd like to sort it. | What would help you get there? | Let's try it for the next report and check in — no drama either way. | I'm really glad we talked.
- **Negotiating** — Trade instead of caving; land a deal.  [skill: win-win deals]
    phrases: Let's find something that works for both of us. | Before we talk price, can I ask…? | That's a bit further than I can go — help me understand what's driving the number. | This is the price that lets me do the job properly." … then say nothing.  /  "I've priced this as fairly as I can." … then wait.  /  "That's where I need to be on this." … and let it sit. | Help me understand what's driving the number. | Rather than just the price, let's look at what's inside the price. | If you can do X, then I can move on Y. | If you can supply the photography and give me two more weeks, then I can bring the price down to your ceiling."  /  "I can lower the price, provided you cover the photos and we push the deadline out a little. | So, just to confirm what we've agreed, the second language stays in and you supply the photos. | Pleasure doing business — this is exactly the kind of project I enjoy, and I'm glad we sorted it together.
- **The Interview** — The pitch, the weakness, and the curveball.  [skill: interviews]
    phrases: In a sentence, I'm someone who… | …this role looks like the same work on a bigger stage, which is why I applied. | Rather than just say I'm organised, let me give you an example. | I'd rather show you than tell you — last quarter I presented our results to the board and turned a nervous room into a yes."  /  "A number might say it better: I cut our reply time to clients from two days to two hours. | Honestly, my weakness is X — and here's what I'm doing about it. | a real weakness plus the work you're doing on it. | Let me walk you through a specific example. | What I did was, I split the order across two suppliers… | Can I ask what success looks like in this role after a year? | Thank you for your time — I've come away more interested in the role, not less, and I'd be glad to take it further."  /  "This has only made me keener; I'd really welcome the next step.
- **Asking to Work From Home** — Ask your manager for two days at home.  [skill: making a case]
    phrases: There's something I'd like to put to you — nothing dramatic. | I want to talk about how I work best, not about doing less. | What I'd like to propose is two fixed days at home — Tuesday and Thursday. | Over the last quarter, almost all the deep work has happened on the days I've been at home. | I know the worry is the team, so let me get ahead of that. | Monday and Friday stay office days precisely because that's when we plan and hand over. | Could we trial it for a month? | If it slips, we drop it. No argument. | That's fair — what would need to be true for it to work? | Can I put a short note together and we look at it properly next time?
- **Chairing the Meeting** — Run the room; finish on time.  [skill: running the room]
    phrases: …here's how I'd like to use it. | Marcus, can you take us through the first one — three minutes? | Marcus — let me jump in there, only because I want Yuki's view before we lose time. | That's a real issue, but it's a different meeting. Can we park it? | Yuki, you've been closest to this — what are we missing? | Let Yuki finish. | Where are we — is everyone happy, or is anyone uneasy? | So what I'm hearing is… | So that's you, Marcus, by Thursday — is that realistic? | We said half an hour and we're at twenty-eight. Thank you — that was a good one.
- **The Performance Review** — Take the criticism; ask for what you want.  [skill: taking it well]
    phrases: Before you start — I'd rather have the real version than the kind version. | Can you give me an example? I want to be picturing the same thing you are. | Let me sit with that for a second. | The part I recognise is the last-minute thing. That's real, and it's mine. | Where I'd push back, gently, is… | It might be worth knowing that… | What would 'better' look like by the summer? | There's something I'd like to put on the table for this year. | What would I need to show you to be in the frame for that? | Thank you for being straight with me. I'd rather this than a pat on the back.
- **The Handover** — Hand over four years in forty minutes.  [skill: letting go of it]
    phrases: I could talk for three hours, so stop me — what do you actually need? | There are three things that'll bite you. Everything else is in the folder. | This is the one that looks fine and isn't. | If it goes wrong, it'll go wrong here, and it'll be a Thursday. | The thing that isn't in any document is that it all runs on Ferran. | Officially it's a two-week process. It isn't. | I never got to this, and I should have. | Don't inherit my way of doing it just because it's mine. | It's yours now — you don't need my permission to change it. | Ring me for a month. After that, honestly, don't.
- **Saying No to Your Boss** — Say no to more work, without saying no.  [skill: declining upward]
    phrases: Can I look at what I've got on and come back to you in ten? | I don't want to say yes and then let you down quietly. | I can do it — but something has to give. Which? | If I take that on, the Renner thing lands late. Is that alright? | You'd know better than me what matters most this week. | I'm not saying no. I'm asking you to pick. | Realistically I've got about a day and a half spare. | I could do a rough version by Tuesday or a proper one by Friday. | I promised Alina Thursday, and I'd rather not be the reason that slips. | That works. Can we say that out loud so we both remember it?
- **The Changing Brief** — Charge for the extra work.  [skill: holding a line]
    phrases: Yes — easily. Let me just tell you what it costs. | I'd rather flag it now than surprise you at the end. | On its own it's nothing. It's the fifth one, though. | Can I show you where we've drifted from the original? | That's about another two days, so it'd be an extra six hundred. | Or we swap it for something we've already agreed — your call. | This isn't a complaint. The project's better for half of these. | I should have said this at tweak two, honestly. That's on me. | Shall we do it this way from now on — you ask, I price it, you decide? | Nothing changes today. I just wanted it on the table.
- **The Mistake** — Tell your boss you've broken something.  [skill: owning an error]
    phrases: I've made a mistake and you need to know about it now, not later. | It's mine. I did it. Nobody else touched it. | Here's exactly what's happened, and here's what I don't know yet. | Ninety-one people, outside the company, forty minutes ago. | I've started the recall, and I've stopped it going any further. | I haven't rung the clients — that's your call, and I didn't want to make it worse. | There's a reason it happened but I'm not going to use it as an excuse. | Tell me off later. Right now, what do you need from me? | I'd rather be the one who tells you than the one you find out about. | It won't happen again, and that's not a promise, it's a change to how I do it.
- **Taking the Credit** — Claim work someone else presented as theirs.  [skill: claiming your work]
    phrases: Can I say something slightly awkward? It'll take a minute. | I'm not making a thing of this. I just don't want to say nothing. | That was my analysis, Ana. The model, the returns idea — that was mine. | You said 'we' once and 'I' for the rest of it. I counted, which tells you how it felt. | I don't think you did it on purpose. I do think you did it. | 'Everyone knows it was a team effort' — they don't, though. That's the point. | I'd like you to say my name to Priyesh. That's all. | Next time, I'd rather be in the room than in the footnotes. | That's it. That's the whole thing. We're fine. | I'd want you to tell me if I'd done it to you.
