Watch It Happen

Step 1: IDEA (Keywords only)

technology + education + better + access

Step 2: BASIC SENTENCE (Simple and correct)

Technology makes education better. More people can access it.

Band 6: Correct but basic

Step 3: TRANSFORM (Apply power-ups)

[Nominalization] The integration of technology in education has improved accessibility.

Step 4: POLISH (Add detail)

The integration of technology in education has significantly improved accessibility, particularly for students in remote areas who previously had limited learning opportunities.

Transformation Chains

Build sentences step by step. At each stage, choose how to upgrade your sentence.

Chain 1: Opinion Essay

Essay Question:

Some people believe that social media has a negative impact on society. To what extent do you agree?

Step 1: Basic idea

Social media is bad for society because people compare themselves to others.

Band 5-6: Simple but basic

Step 2: Choose a nominalization

The negative effects of social media
The harmful impact of social media
The detrimental influence of social media

Step 3: Add a complex noun phrase

users who constantly compare themselves
individuals comparing themselves to unrealistic ideals
the tendency to compare oneself to others

Step 4: Choose a connector

are primarily due to
stem largely from
can be attributed to

Chain 2: Problem/Solution Essay

Essay Question:

What problems does plastic pollution cause? What solutions can governments implement?

Step 1: Basic idea

If governments made stricter laws, plastic pollution would decrease.

Step 2: Choose your conditional structure

Were governments to implement
Should governments introduce

Step 3: Specify the action

comprehensive bans on single-use plastics
stricter regulations on plastic manufacturers

Step 4: Choose the result

plastic waste could be significantly reduced
this growing problem could be effectively addressed

Paragraph Building

See how Band 6 transforms to Band 8, then build your own paragraph.

Band 6 → Band 8 Transformation

BAND 6

Technology has made communication easier. People can talk to anyone in the world. But there are problems. People spend too much time on phones. They don't talk face-to-face. This is bad for relationships.

BAND 8

The rapid advancement of communication technology has fundamentally transformed human interaction, enabling instant connections across the globe. However, this growing reliance on digital communication has led to concerns about relationship quality. Faced with constant notifications, many individuals find themselves neglecting face-to-face interactions. Were people to consciously limit their screen time, the negative social effects could be significantly mitigated.

Sentence 1: Complex noun phrase + nominalization

Sentence 2: Nominalization (reliance, concerns)

Sentence 3: Participle clause (Faced with...)

Sentence 4: Advanced conditional (Were...to)

Your Turn: Build a Paragraph

Essay Question:

Many cities face traffic congestion. What solutions can be implemented?

Build a SOLUTIONS paragraph using at least 3 different structures.

Sentence 1: Introduce solution (nominalization)

Sentence 2: Explain (conditional)

Sentence 3: Add detail (participle/complex NP)

Sentence 4: Emphasise (inversion - optional)

The expansion of public transport networks would provide a practical alternative to private vehicles. Were governments to invest in efficient metro systems, commuters would be more likely to abandon their cars. Coupled with incentives such as subsidised fares, the appeal of public transport could be enhanced. Only by making public transport affordable and convenient can cities realistically expect citizens to change their habits.

Write ONE body paragraph (View A: Free education). Use at least 3 structures.

Self-Assessment:

Those who advocate for free university education argue that the removal of tuition fees would create a more equitable society. Were higher education to be funded entirely by the government, students from disadvantaged backgrounds would no longer face financial barriers. Faced with crippling student debt, many graduates currently struggle to buy homes, which has broader economic implications. The provision of free education could therefore be seen not merely as a cost, but as an investment in the nation's future workforce.
