Joining a Gym

You want to get fit. Today you go into a new gym to look. You ask about the price and the classes — and you learn to say "no" in a nice way.

The Scene 🎬

There is a new gym near your home. You walk past it every day. Today, you go in to have a look. You want to get a little fitter.

You want to know some things first: how much it costs, what classes there are, and if you must pay for a whole year. You are not sure yet. You do not want to say "yes" today.

A person from the gym comes to say hello. They are friendly. But their job is to make you join today. So you must ask your questions, get the facts, and — if you are not ready — say "no" in a kind way.

Warm up — talk for one minute

  1. Do you go to a gym, or do you do sport at home? What do you like to do?
  2. Is it easy or hard for you to say "no" to a salesperson? Why?

Who's who

You want to get fit, but slowly. You ask about the price and the classes. You are friendly, but you do not want to say "yes" today.

The other person
The Gym Worker 📋

Friendly and quick. They want you to join today. They have a "special price, only today". They have an answer for everything.

The Conversation 💬

Read it out loud, one person each, then change. Find the phrases from your toolkit. Listen for the polite words.

Gym WorkerHello, welcome! Is this your first time here? Do you want to join today?
YouHello. I am just looking today. I would like some information, please.
Gym WorkerOf course, no problem! Let me show you the gym. What do you want to do — lose weight, get strong?
YouNothing big. I just want to get a little fitter. How much is it a month?
Gym WorkerOur best plan is forty-five pounds a month. But today, only today, it is thirty-two!
YouOK. Is there a cheaper plan?
Gym WorkerYes, there is a plan for twenty-six pounds. But you can only come in the day, not the evening.
YouI see. What classes do you have?
Gym WorkerLots! We have yoga, spin, and dance. There is a class every day.
YouThat's nice. One question — do I have to sign for a year?
Gym WorkerFor the special price, yes, one year. Sign today and I take away the joining fee too! But only today.
YouThank you, but I need to think about it. Can I take the prices home?
Gym WorkerOf course. Here is the paper, and my card. Have a good day!
YouThank you very much. You were very helpful. I will come back soon.

From memory — don't look up!

1. The new person asks about three things. What are they?

2. Why is the cheap plan (twenty-six pounds) not perfect?

3. The gym worker says "sign today". What does the new person say?

1. The price (how much it is a month), the cheaper plan, and the classes. They also ask about signing for a year.
2. With the cheap plan, you can only go to the gym in the day, not in the evening.
3. They do not say "yes". They say they need to think about it, and they ask to take the prices home.

The Echo 🕰️

Today you can choose: join a gym, or not. You pay some money, and you go when you want. If you don't like it, you stop.

A long time ago, in a city called Sparta, it was very different. Boys had to train hard from a young age. Being strong was not a choice — it was the law. There was no "special price" and no "maybe next week".

You only have to say "no" to a friendly salesperson. A Spartan child had no gym worker with a clipboard — and no way to say "no" at all.

Two questions to talk about

  1. The Spartans had to train. We choose to. Which is better — being made to do sport, or choosing to? Why?
  2. Some people join a gym but never go. Why do you think this happens? What helps a person keep going?
