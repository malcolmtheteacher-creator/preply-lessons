Joining a Gym

You want to get fit. Today you go into a new gym to look. You ask about the price and the classes — and you learn to say "no" in a nice way.

The Scene 🎬

There is a new gym near your home. You walk past it every day. Today, you go in to have a look. You want to get a little fitter.

You want to know some things first: how much it costs, what classes there are, and if you must pay for a whole year. You are not sure yet. You do not want to say "yes" today.

A person from the gym comes to say hello. They are friendly. But their job is to make you join today. So you must ask your questions, get the facts, and — if you are not ready — say "no" in a kind way.

Warm up — talk for one minute

  1. Do you go to a gym, or do you do sport at home? What do you like to do?
  2. Is it easy or hard for you to say "no" to a salesperson? Why?

Who's who

You
The New Person 👟

You want to get fit, but slowly. You ask about the price and the classes. You are friendly, but you do not want to say "yes" today.

The other person
The Gym Worker 📋

Friendly and quick. They want you to join today. They have a "special price, only today". They have an answer for everything.

First: read the conversation in the next tab. Look at the polite words: can I, how much, I would like, I need to think. It is OK to speak slowly. It is OK to say "no".

The Conversation 💬

Read it out loud, one person each — then change. Listen for the polite words.

Gym WorkerHello, welcome! Is this your first time here? Do you want to join today?
YouHello. I am just looking today. I would like some information, please.
Gym WorkerOf course, no problem! Let me show you the gym. What do you want to do — lose weight, get strong?
YouNothing big. I just want to get a little fitter. How much is it a month?
Gym WorkerOur best plan is forty-five pounds a month. But today, only today, it is thirty-two!

Gym WorkerYes, there is a plan for twenty-six pounds. But you can only come in the day, not the evening.

Gym WorkerLots! We have yoga, spin, and dance. There is a class every day.

From memory — don't look up!

1. The new person asks about three things. What are they?

2. Why is the cheap plan (twenty-six pounds) not perfect?

3. The gym worker says "sign today". What does the new person say?

1. The price (how much it is a month), the cheaper plan, and the classes. They also ask about signing for a year.
2. With the cheap plan, you can only go to the gym in the day, not in the evening.
3. They do not say "yes". They say they need to think about it, and they ask to take the prices home.

Useful Phrases 🧰

Eight phrases you can use in a gym — or any shop. Say each one out loud BEFORE you look at the answer. Guessing first helps you remember.

Say it in a nice way. You do not need to buy anything today. It is your money and your time. Ask your questions slowly and clearly. Saying "no, thank you" is polite — you do not need to feel bad.

🚪 Coming in to look

Phrase 1

You walk in. You want to look, not to buy. You want some facts. Complete it: "I would like some ___, please."

"I would like some information, please."
"I would like…" is the polite way to say "I want". "Information" means the facts you need. You can use this everywhere: "I would like some information about the classes."
Phrase 2

The worker asks "Do you want to join today?" You are not ready. Which answer is friendly, but says "not now"?

A. "Yes, I join now!"
B. "No! Go away."
C. "I am just looking today, thank you."
C — "I am just looking today, thank you."
"I am just looking" is a soft, friendly way to say "I am not ready to buy". A says "yes" too fast. B is not polite. C is calm and kind.

💷 Asking about money

Phrase 3

You want to know the price for one month. Put the words in order:

how mucha month?is it
"How much is it a month?"
"How much is it?" asks the price. Add "a month" for things you pay every month. You can also ask: "How much is it a week?" or "How much is it a year?"
Phrase 4

The first price is too high for you. You want to ask for a lower price. Complete it: "Is there a ___ plan?"

"Is there a cheaper plan?"
"Cheaper" means "not so expensive". "Is there a…?" is a nice way to ask. Try: "Is there a cheaper ticket?", "Is there a smaller size?"

🏋️ Asking about the gym

Phrase 5

You want to know about the classes — yoga, dance, and so on. Complete it: "What ___ do you have?"

"What classes do you have?"
"What … do you have?" asks someone to tell you the list. Use it a lot: "What sizes do you have?", "What times do you have?"
Phrase 6

Before you say "yes", you want to know: must you pay for a whole year? Which question asks this clearly?

A. "Do I have to sign for a year?"
B. "I love the gym!"
C. "Where is the toilet?"
A — "Do I have to sign for a year?"
"Do I have to…?" asks if something is a rule. "Sign for a year" means you promise to pay for twelve months. This is a very important question before you join!

🛡️ Saying "not today"

Phrase 7

The worker says "Sign today, it is cheaper!" You are not sure. You want time. Which answer is polite but firm?

A. "OK, I sign now."
B. "I need to think about it."
C. "Stop talking to me."
B — "I need to think about it."
"I need to think about it" is a kind way to say "not now". You are not saying "no" — you are saying "give me time". A says "yes" too fast. C is not polite.
Phrase 8

You want to take the prices home and think. What can you ask? (Say it out loud first.)

"Can I take the prices home?" / "Can I take this paper with me?"
"Can I…?" is a polite way to ask. This lets you leave, think in a calm place, and choose with no pressure. That is a very good idea!

🔑 Make them yours

These phrases work in a gym, a shop, or on the phone. Write your own sentences. Say each one out loud first.

"How much is it a ___?" — Think of a thing you pay for often. Is it a week, a month, a year?

"Is there a cheaper ___?" — What do you want for a lower price next time you shop?

"I need to think about it." — When was the last time you wanted more time to decide? Write the shop or the thing.

Quick test: close your eyes and say the whole visit out loud — walk in → ask the price → ask about classes → say "I need to think about it". When it is easy, go to Your Turn.

Your Turn 🎭

Now it is your gym visit. Say your answers out loud FIRST, then write them. Use the timer if you like.

Round 1 — you are the new person 👟

The gym worker speaks — you answer. Be friendly: use please, thank you, and I would like. Get the facts. Do not say "yes" too fast.

Gym WorkerNo problem! What do you want to do — lose weight, get strong?

Gym WorkerOf course. Here is the paper. Have a good day!

Round 2 — now YOU are the gym worker 📋

Change places. You work at the gym. Be friendly and quick. Invent the plans and the prices. But do not push too hard!

New PersonHello. I am just looking today. Can you tell me about the gym?
New PersonNothing big. I just want to get a little fitter. How much is it a month?
New PersonIs there a cheaper plan? And what classes do you have?

The Twist

🏋️ Your friend works there

One more time — no help this round. This time the gym worker is your friend. They want you to join today, and they say: "Come on, do it for me! Just say yes!" You like your friend, but you are still not sure, and the price is high. What do you say? Be kind, but do not say "yes" only to make your friend happy. Say "I need to think about it" in a warm way. Start the timer and speak!

Before you finish…

Think about what you said. Can you say "yes" to these?

  • I said "I am just looking" in a friendly way.
  • I asked the price with "How much is it?"
  • I asked about the classes and the year.
  • I said "I need to think about it" — I did not rush.
  • I stayed friendly the whole time.

Can't say yes to one? That is your practice — try Round 1 again.

The Echo 🕰️

Today you can choose: join a gym, or not. You pay some money, and you go when you want. If you don't like it, you stop.

A long time ago, in a city called Sparta, it was very different. Boys had to train hard from a young age. Being strong was not a choice — it was the law. There was no "special price" and no "maybe next week".

You only have to say "no" to a friendly salesperson. A Spartan child had no gym worker with a clipboard — and no way to say "no" at all.
