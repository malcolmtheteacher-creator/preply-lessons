🧊 Quick Fix: Uncountable Nouns — No A / An

Information, advice, news, furniture, luggage — no article, no plural

Spot the error:

"Can you give me an information about the course?"

Correct version:
"Can you give me some information about the course?"

The Rule

Uncountable nouns have no plural and cannot take a/an. Use "some" or nothing instead.

Key uncountable nouns:

information • advice • news • furniture • luggage/baggage • equipment • knowledge • progress • research • traffic • weather • work (as a concept)

More examples:

❌ a furniture → ✓ some furniture / a piece of furniture

❌ a news → ✓ some news / a piece of news

❌ an advice → ✓ some advice / a piece of advice

❌ Countable Trap

a chair
a bag
a fact

✓ Uncountable Reality

furniture
luggage
information

Memory tip: To count them, use "a piece of": a piece of advice, a piece of furniture, a piece of news.

Rewrite the incorrect part:

1. "She gave me a very useful advice."

2. "We don't have many informations about the case."

3. "He bought a new furniture for his flat."

4. "The luggages were very heavy."

Rewrite each sentence using the correct uncountable form:

1. "I need an information about the trains."

2. "She gave me two good advices."

3. "All the furnitures in the room were old."

4. "We made a big progress this week."
