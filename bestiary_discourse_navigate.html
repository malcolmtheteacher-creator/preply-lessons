Your NAVIGATE Chain
Five tools for handling any disagreement professionally.
The Diplomacy Escalation

These five escalate gently: acknowledge → offer alternative → push back → provoke → close. You choose the right level for the situation.

When to Use Each One
Seahorse — "I see where you're coming from, but..." The softest disagreement. You validate the other person first. Use when you want to maintain rapport while changing direction. Essential in client meetings and team discussions.
Angel — "That's one way to look at it. Another perspective might be..." Introduces an alternative without attacking the original. Use when you want to broaden the conversation rather than shut someone down. Especially powerful in group discussions.
Puffin — "I'd push back slightly on that..." Firm but measured. You're signalling disagreement openly but without hostility. "Slightly" does the heavy lifting — it softens the blow while making your position clear.
Platypus — "Playing devil's advocate for a moment..." Lets you argue a position you might not hold. Perfect for testing ideas, stress-testing assumptions, or raising an uncomfortable point that needs raising. The disclaimer protects you.
Agaric Fungi — "We may need to agree to disagree on..." The professional exit. Not every disagreement needs resolution. This phrase closes the loop respectfully so everyone can move on. Essential for preserving working relationships.
