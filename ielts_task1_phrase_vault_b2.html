Two ways to say every trend

This is the single biggest move for Band 7 in Task 1: show the examiner that you can express the same idea with a verb + adverb AND with an adjective + noun. You don't need new ideas — just a new grammatical shape.

ChangeVerb + adverbAdjective + noun
↑ big riseSales rose dramatically.There was a dramatic rise in sales.
↑ sudden risePrices increased sharply.Prices saw a sharp increase.
↓ big fallProfits fell significantly.There was a significant fall in profits.
↓ gradual fallAttendance declined steadily.Attendance showed a steady decline.
↓ tiny fallNumbers dropped slightly.There was a slight drop in numbers.
↔ no changeFigures remained stable.Figures remained at the same level.
↕ up & downPrices fluctuated considerably.There was considerable fluctuation in prices.

The three linking verbs that let you switch to noun form

Memorise these — they are the bridge:

  • There was / there were + adjective + noun → There was a sharp rise in sales.
  • saw / experienced / witnessed + adjective + noun → Sales saw a sharp rise. / The company experienced a sharp rise in sales.
  • showed + adjective + noun → Sales showed a sharp rise.

Mini-quiz — rewrite using "There was…"

Tourism grew rapidly.

There was a rapid growth in tourism. Note: growth (noun) from grew (verb). Same idea, different shape.

Now rewrite using "saw"

Unemployment fell sharply.

Unemployment saw a sharp fall. (Or: Unemployment experienced a sharp fall.)

Pick the right strength word

Every change is big, medium, small, or almost nothing. The adverb (or adjective) has to match the size of the change. Pick wrong and the examiner marks you down for lexical accuracy.

Tiny / small
slightly · marginally
slight · marginal
Moderate / steady
steadily · gradually · moderately
steady · gradual · moderate
Big / clear
significantly · considerably · substantially
significant · considerable · substantial
Huge / sudden
dramatically · sharply · rapidly · steeply
dramatic · sharp · rapid · steep

Now say each sentence the OTHER way

Sentence 1 was: "Housing rose considerably from 18% to 32%." — rewrite with saw + noun.

Housing saw a considerable rise from 18% to 32%.

Sentence 2 was: "Food saw a dramatic fall from 22% to 11%." — rewrite with a verb + adverb.

Food fell dramatically from 22% to 11%. (Or: Food dropped sharply from 22% to 11%.)
