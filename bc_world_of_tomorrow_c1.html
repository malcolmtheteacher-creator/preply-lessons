Passive Reporting Verbs: Sophisticated Expression

Reporting verbs allow you to present information with appropriate distance and nuance. Transform these sentences using passive structures.

Study the Pattern:

Active to Passive Transformation

Active: Everyone knows that the deadline has passed.

Passive (Pattern A): It is known that the deadline has passed.

Passive (Pattern B): The deadline is known to have passed.

Your Transformation Tasks:

1. We understand that only the most exceptional candidates will be selected for the mission to Mars.

It is understood that only the most exceptional candidates will be selected for the mission to Mars. OR Only the most exceptional candidates are understood to be selected for the mission to Mars.

2. NASA expects over a million people to observe the launch of the satellite.

It is expected by NASA that over a million people will observe the launch. OR Over a million people are expected to observe the launch of the satellite.

3. Experts believe that the anonymous source provided fabricated evidence regarding the project's viability.

It is believed that the anonymous source provided fabricated evidence. OR The anonymous source is believed to have provided fabricated evidence.

4. Certain stakeholders allege that the mission launch has been postponed due to fiscal misconduct by senior executives.

It is alleged that the mission launch has been postponed due to fiscal misconduct. OR The mission launch is alleged to have been postponed due to embezzlement by senior executives.

5. Research indicates that substantial aqueous resources exist on Mars.

It is indicated that substantial aqueous resources exist on Mars. OR Mars is indicated to have substantial aqueous resources.

Candidate Selection Task

Your assignment: rank the candidates in descending order of suitability for the Mars colonisation mission. Synthesise your assessment across multiple dimensions: technical competence, psychological resilience, interpersonal compatibility, and motivational authenticity.

Evaluation Framework:

  • Technical expertise and scientific contribution
  • Psychological resilience in isolated, confined environments
  • Collaborative capability and interpersonal compatibility
  • Authenticity and sustainability of motivation
  • Adaptability to unprecedented circumstances

Linguistic Strategies:

EXPRESSING RANKED JUDGMENT
I would position Candidate X as most suitable, principally because...
Establishes hierarchical reasoning structure; 'principally' signals primary justification.
ARTICULATING CONCERNS
Conversely, one might contend that Candidate Y's motivation is insufficiently sustainable...
Acknowledges alternative perspectives while maintaining evaluative position.
USING IDIOMS NATURALLY
Candidate Z has arguably bitten off more than they can chew by proposing such experimental objectives...
Integrates challenge-related idioms into sophisticated discourse seamlessly.
PASSIVE REPORTING INTEGRATION
It is generally understood that candidate compatibility is paramount; moreover, research suggests that psychological stability transcends technical expertise...
Employs passive structures to present assertions with appropriate academic distance.
