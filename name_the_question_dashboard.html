Name the Question

Every hard question is one of a few kinds. Hear which kind it is, and you know how to think about it — and what to say.

Click a circle, or any label, to open it.

Experienced people do something before they speak that you rarely see: they recognise the kind of question they have been asked. Should we…? is not the same question as Why…?, and each has a way of thinking that works.

1 · Hear it

Listen for the question word.

2 · Name it

Find its circle on the map. Beware of look-alikes.

3 · Think it

Run that kind’s lens. It leads to a short list of answers.

4 · Say it

Point, reason, stop. Then hold it under pushback.
