Today's aim: Today we're learning to explain how a simple circuit works and how to save electricity, so you can say what happens and why.

Words you'll need

electricity (n) — the energy that makes lights and machines work. Example: "Electricity comes through the wires in the wall."
battery (n) — a small thing that stores electricity. Example: "The torch needs a new battery."
circuit (n) — a complete path for electricity. Example: "A circuit goes from the battery, round the wires and back."
switch (n) — a button that turns electricity on or off. Example: "Press the switch to turn on the light."
bulb (n) — the glass part of a lamp that gives light. Example: "The bulb is broken."
wire (n) — a thin piece of metal that carries electricity. Example: "Do not touch the wire."
plug (n) — the part you put in the wall to get electricity. Example: "Take the plug out of the wall."
socket (n) — the place in the wall where you put a plug. Example: "There is a socket near the bed."
save (v) — to use less of something. Example: "We save electricity when we turn off the lights."
waste (v) — to use something badly or too much. Example: "Do not waste electricity."

A question to start

You press the light switch and the light comes on. You press it again and it goes off.

Say it aloud: What do you think happens inside the wall when you press the switch?

A switch opens or closes a circuit. A circuit is a path for electricity. When the switch is on, the path is complete, so electricity flows to the bulb. When it is off, the path has a gap, so the bulb goes out.

Batteries, circuits and switches

🔋 Battery

A battery gives the electricity a push.

🔁 Circuit

Electricity needs a complete path. It goes from the battery, through the wires and the bulb, and back to the battery.

🔘 Switch

A switch makes a gap in the circuit. With a gap, electricity cannot flow.

What you just saw

1 · A gap stops it
When the switch is off, there is a gap in the circuit, so electricity cannot flow.
2 · In a line
Bulbs in a line share the same electricity, so each bulb is dimmer.
3 · Side by side
Each bulb has its own path, so each bulb stays bright.

Electricity needs a complete circuit. Any gap, or a broken wire, stops it.

Language for explaining

Facts: the present simple

A battery gives electricity.
A switch opens the circuit.

Giving a reason: because and so

The lamp is off because the plug is out of the wall.
The plug is out of the wall, so the lamp is off.

If sentences

If you press the switch, the light comes on.
If you touch a hot wire, it is dangerous.

How can we save electricity at home?

Electricity costs money, and making it can hurt the planet. We can save electricity in easy ways. We turn off lights when we leave a room. We use LED bulbs. We take chargers out of the wall.

1 · Lights off 💡
We turn off lights when we leave a room.
2 · LED bulbs 💡
An LED bulb uses much less electricity.
3 · Unplug 🔌
A charger in the wall still uses a little electricity.
