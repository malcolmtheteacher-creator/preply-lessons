The Story

Read it slowly. Read it out loud. The pictures help you. Stuck? Click 🇫🇷 for that part.

🍲
1. One day, Anansi the spider cooked a big meal. It smelled wonderful. Anansi was very happy.
Un jour, Anansi l'araignée prépara un grand repas. Ça sentait délicieux. Anansi était très content.
🐢
2. Then Turtle came. He was tired and hungry after a long journey. "Hello, friend! That food smells good," he said.
Puis Tortue arriva. Il était fatigué et il avait faim après un long voyage. « Bonjour mon ami ! Cette nourriture sent bon », dit-il.
🤝
3. In Anansi's country, you share your food with a visitor. But Anansi was greedy. He did not want to share his meal.
Dans le pays d'Anansi, on partage sa nourriture avec un visiteur. Mais Anansi était gourmand. Il ne voulait pas partager son repas.
🃏🧼
4. So Anansi made a trick. "Oh, Turtle! Your hands are dirty," he said. "We do not eat with dirty hands. Go and wash them in the river."
Alors Anansi inventa une ruse. « Oh, Tortue ! Tes mains sont sales », dit-il. « On ne mange pas avec les mains sales. Va les laver dans la rivière. »
🐢💧
5. Turtle walked all the way to the river and washed his hands. But on the slow walk back, his hands got dirty again — and Anansi was eating fast!
Tortue marcha jusqu'à la rivière et se lava les mains. Mais sur le chemin du retour, lent, ses mains se salirent encore — et Anansi mangeait vite !
😋
6. "Your hands are STILL dirty! Wash again," said Anansi. When Turtle came back, the plate was empty. Anansi ate everything. Turtle said "thank you" and went home hungry.
« Tes mains sont ENCORE sales ! Relave-les », dit Anansi. Quand Tortue revint, l'assiette était vide. Anansi avait tout mangé. Tortue dit « merci » et rentra chez lui le ventre vide.
🌊🪨
7. Some weeks later, Turtle said: "Come and eat at MY house — at the bottom of the river!" Anansi was too light to stay under the water. So he put heavy stones in his pockets and sank down to the table.
Quelques semaines plus tard, Tortue dit : « Viens manger chez MOI — au fond de la rivière ! » Anansi était trop léger pour rester sous l'eau. Alors il mit des pierres lourdes dans ses poches et descendit jusqu'à la table.
⬆️😮
8. But Turtle said politely: "We do not wear jackets at the table. Take off your jacket." Anansi took off his heavy jacket — and floated up to the top! When he came back, the food was gone. Now Anansi knew how Turtle had felt.
Mais Tortue dit poliment : « On ne porte pas de veste à table. Enlève ta veste. » Anansi enleva sa veste lourde — et remonta tout en haut ! Quand il redescendit, la nourriture avait disparu. Maintenant, Anansi savait ce que Tortue avait ressenti.

Did you understand?

True or false? Say your answer, then check. (🇫🇷 helps you read the question.)

a) Anansi was happy to share his meal with Turtle.
False. Anansi was greedy. He did not want to share.
Anansi était content de partager son repas avec Tortue. (Vrai ou faux ?)
b) Anansi told Turtle to wash his hands in the river.
True. It was a trick, so Anansi could eat the food alone. 🃏
Anansi a dit à Tortue de se laver les mains dans la rivière. (Vrai ou faux ?)
c) At Anansi's house, Turtle ate a big, happy meal.
False. The plate was empty. Turtle went home hungry.
Chez Anansi, Tortue a mangé un grand et bon repas. (Vrai ou faux ?)
d) At Turtle's house, Anansi's jacket floated up, so he got no food.
True. Turtle paid Anansi back with the same kind of trick. 🐢
Chez Tortue, la veste d'Anansi est remontée, alors il n'a pas eu de nourriture. (Vrai ou faux ?)

Put the story in order 🔢

These six things are mixed up! Say the right order (1–6), then check.

A. Anansi ate all the food, and Turtle went home hungry. 😋
B. Turtle invited Anansi to eat at the bottom of the river. 🌊
C. Turtle came to Anansi's house, tired and hungry. 🐢
D. Anansi's jacket came off and he floated up. ⬆️
E. Anansi told Turtle to wash his hands in the river. 🧼
F. Anansi put heavy stones in his pockets and sank down. 🪨
C → E → A → B → F → D

Easy grammar: the past (again!)

Yesterday we started the past. Today we practise more. Some verbs add -ed. Some are special ⭐ — we just learn them.

Hier, on a commencé le passé. Aujourd'hui, on s'entraîne encore. Certains verbes ajoutent -ed ; d'autres sont irréguliers (on les apprend par cœur !).
now: cookpast: cooked ✅ (add -ed)
now: washpast: washed ✅
now: walkpast: walked ✅
now: eatpast: ate ⭐ (special)
now: comepast: came ⭐
now: takepast: took ⭐
now: gopast: went ⭐
now: saypast: said ⭐

And remember the verb to be: I/he/she/it → was, you/we/they → were.

Let's talk about it

Easy questions. Just say what you think. (🇫🇷 helps you understand the question.)

  • Was Anansi kind to Turtle? Why / why not?
    Anansi a-t-il été gentil avec Tortue ? Pourquoi, ou pourquoi pas ?
  • Turtle paid Anansi back with the same trick. Was that fair?
    Tortue a rendu la pareille à Anansi avec la même ruse. Était-ce juste ?
  • In your family or country, do you share food with visitors? 🍲
    Dans ta famille ou ton pays, partage-t-on la nourriture avec les visiteurs ?
  • Tell me about a time you shared something — or a time someone was greedy!
    Parle-moi d'une fois où tu as partagé quelque chose — ou d'une fois où quelqu'un a été gourmand !
  • Which do you like more — Anansi or Turtle? Why?
    Qui préfères-tu — Anansi ou Tortue ? Pourquoi ?

Not sure what to say? Start like this: "I think…", "In my family…", "One time…", "I like… because…".
