👀 Preview: B2 Level (Lessons 26-40)

In B2, you'll learn:

  • Advanced Tenses: Past perfect, future perfect continuous
  • Advanced Modals: Must have, should have, can't have
  • Sophisticated Structures: Inversion, cleft sentences, participles
  • Complex Topics: Technology, education, work, culture, identity
  • Higher Fluency: 10-15 minute presentations and debates

🏠 Before Starting B2...

  • Practice B1 grammar daily for 2 weeks
  • Watch English TV shows or movies (with subtitles is OK!)
  • Read English articles or short stories
  • Speak English for at least 10 minutes every day
  • Review Lessons 11-25 quickly before moving forward
