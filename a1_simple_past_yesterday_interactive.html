Time Words for Past

These words tell us we're talking about the past:

• Yesterday - the day before today 📅
• Last night - the night before today 🌙
• Last week - the week before this week 📆
• Last weekend - the weekend before this weekend 🎉
• Last month - the month before this month 📅
• Last year - the year before this year 🎊

Question Form

What did you do + time word?
Examples:
• "What did you do yesterday?" → "I played video games."
• "What did you do last weekend?" → "I went to the cinema."
• "Did you study last night?" → "Yes, I did!" / "No, I didn't."

Regular Verbs - Add -ED

Most verbs in the past are REGULAR - just add -ED to the end!

Regular Past Tense = Verb + ED

play → played
watch → watched
listen → listened
PLAY → PLAYED
Now: I play video games every day.
Past: Yesterday, I played video games. 🎮
WATCH → WATCHED
Now: I watch Netflix every evening.
Past: Last night, I watched Netflix. 📺
LISTEN → LISTENED
Now: I listen to music.
Past: Yesterday, I listened to music. 🎵
VISIT → VISITED
Now: I visit my grandmother on Sundays.
Past: Last Sunday, I visited my grandmother. 👵
STUDY → STUDIED
Now: I study English every day.
Past: Yesterday, I studied English. 📚

Irregular Verbs - Special Forms

Some important verbs are IRREGULAR - they don't add -ED!
You need to learn these special forms.

These verbs don't follow the -ED rule. You need to memorize them!

Past: Yesterday, I did my homework. ✏️

What Did You Do Yesterday?

Tell your teacher about your day yesterday!
Use past tense verbs.

Your Yesterday

1. What time did you wake up yesterday?
Example: "Yesterday, I woke up at 7:00 AM."
2. What did you eat for breakfast yesterday?
Example: "I ate cereal and drank orange juice."
3. What did you do at school yesterday?
Example: "I studied math and English. I talked with my friends."
4. What did you do after school yesterday?
Example: "I went home and did my homework. Then I played video games."
5. What did you do in the evening yesterday?
Example: "I ate dinner with my family. Then I watched Netflix and went to bed at 10:00 PM."

Now Tell the Complete Story!

Describe your WHOLE day yesterday from morning to night!
Use past tense verbs: woke up, ate, went, did, played, watched, etc.

What Did You Do Last Weekend?

Tell your teacher about last weekend!
What did you do on Saturday and Sunday?

📅 Saturday

What did you do on Saturday?
Think about Saturday morning, afternoon, and evening!
Example: "On Saturday morning, I woke up late. I got up at 10:00 AM! In the afternoon, I played football with my friends. In the evening, I watched a movie with my family."

📅 Sunday

What did you do on Sunday?
Describe your Sunday!
Example: "On Sunday, I visited my grandmother. We ate lunch together. Then I came home and did my homework."

Combining Past, Present, and Future!

Now you can talk about ALL times!

PAST: "Last weekend, I went to the cinema." 📅
PRESENT: "I usually go to the cinema on Saturdays." ⏰
FUTURE: "This weekend, I'm going to go to the cinema again!" 🔮

Speaking Challenge!

Compare last weekend with this weekend!
Example:
"Last weekend, I stayed home and played video games. But this weekend, I'm going to go out with my friends! We're going to go shopping and watch a movie!"

More Discussion Questions

1. What was the best thing you did last week?
Example: "The best thing was when I went to the cinema with my friends!"
2. What did you do last night before bed?
Example: "Last night, I watched YouTube and listened to music. Then I went to bed at 11:00 PM."
3. Did you study English last week? When?
Example: "Yes! I studied English on Tuesday and Thursday with you!"
