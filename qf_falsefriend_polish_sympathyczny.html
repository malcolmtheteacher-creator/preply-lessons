😊 Nice vs Sympathetic

B1 • Polish/Russian speakers • False friends: sympatyczny ≠ sympathetic

🔍 Let's notice what we see

Look at this sentence. What's the problem?

"He is very sympathetic person."

(But they mean he's a nice, likeable person, not necessarily understanding problems!)

⚠️ The False Friend:

Polish "sympatyczny" LOOKS like English "sympathetic", but they mean DIFFERENT things!

In Polish: sympatyczny = nice, likeable, pleasant (on jest sympatyczny = he is nice)

In English: sympathetic = feeling sorry for someone, understanding their problems

In English: nice or likeable = pleasant, friendly

🛠️ Understand the difference

📊 Two different personality traits:

🇵🇱 Polish "sympatyczny"

😊 NICE / LIKEABLE

Pleasant, friendly, attractive personality

"On jest sympatyczny" = He is nice

🇬🇧 English "sympathetic"

💛 COMPASSIONATE

Understanding others' problems, feeling sorry

"She was sympathetic about my situation" = She understood

🎯 Key Rule:

Polish sympatyczny → English NICE / LIKEABLE

English sympathetic → Polish współczujący (compassionate)

Appearance/personality: NICE. Understanding problems: SYMPATHETIC.

🚀 Now you try — no help!

Choose the correct word. Check your answers when you're done.

1. "I really like Sarah — she's so _____ and friendly." (nice / sympathetic)
Answer: "She's so nice."

→ Personality = nice.

2. "My boss was very _____ when I told her I had a family emergency."
Answer: "My boss was very sympathetic."

→ Understanding problems = sympathetic.

3. "He's such a _____ guy — everyone wants to be his friend."
Answer: "He's such a nice guy."

→ Likeable = nice.

4. "The counselor was _____ about my anxiety and helped me feel better."
Answer: "The counselor was sympathetic."

→ Understanding feelings = sympathetic.

✅ I can say 'nice' or 'likeable' instead of 'sympathetic' when describing a pleasant person
