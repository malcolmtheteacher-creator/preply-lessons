Choose your level to start:

Your last few days

Think about the last few days. Tell your teacher three things. Short and simple is fine: work, family, food, the weather — anything.

Since we last spoke

Pick three moments from the last few days. Don't just list them — tell them, with a detail or two and how you felt.

Something that went well

Start like this: The highlight of my week was probably…
"The highlight of my week was probably Saturday. We finally tried that new restaurant, and it actually lived up to the hype."

Something that annoyed you

Start like this: What really got on my nerves was…
"What really got on my nerves was a delivery that was promised for Tuesday. It turned up on Friday, and nobody answered the phone in between."

Something on your mind

Start like this: I've been wondering whether…
"I've been wondering whether it's time to change my car. It still works, but it's started making some worrying noises."

One good thing

Start like this: Something good: on ___ I ___.
"Something good: on Saturday I went to the cinema with my wife. The film was very funny."

One bad or annoying thing

Start like this: Something bad: ___.
"Something bad: my car broke down on Tuesday. I waited two hours for help. I was so angry."

One normal thing

Start like this: Something normal: ___.
"Something normal: this morning I took the dog for a walk. It was very cold."
