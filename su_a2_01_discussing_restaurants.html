Comparing Restaurants

Learn how to compare two restaurants using these structures.

Comparison Structures

… is/are + comparative adjective + than …

This restaurant is bigger than that one.

The fish here is fresher than at the other place.

… is/are + more + adjective + than …

This place is more popular than the café down the road.

Their service is more expensive than ours.

… is/are + less + adjective + than …

The small shop is less busy than the big one.

Pizza House is less formal than The Hills.

… is/are (not) + as + adjective + as …

Tony's Eatery is not as expensive as The Hills.

The food is just as good as at the other restaurant.

Making and Responding to Suggestions

Click on each suggestion to see what it means and how to use it. Notice the different ways to make a suggestion and respond.

Making Suggestions

SUGGESTION
Where shall we go?
Ask the group what place they prefer
SUGGESTION
I would suggest Chez Med.
Formal way to recommend a restaurant
SUGGESTION
Why don't we try Pizza House?
Polite way to propose an idea
SUGGESTION
How about trying Seafood Sam's?
Casual, friendly way to suggest

Responding to Suggestions

AGREEING
Okay, why not? Let's give it a go.
Express enthusiasm and agreement
INTERESTED BUT UNCERTAIN
I'd like to try it, but…
Show interest while raising a concern
OFFERING AN IDEA
Maybe they have a vegetarian option.
Suggest a possibility that solves a problem
PRACTICAL QUESTION
Should we phone and book a table?
Suggest a practical next step

Five Restaurants to Consider

Read about each restaurant. Which one sounds best to you? Which ones match different reasons for eating out?

Tony's Eatery

Popular with locals. Basic everyday food, low prices. Menu: sandwiches, soup, pasta, salads.

Note: Old decoration, less comfortable than usual — no cushions on the chairs!

The Cavern Carvery

Underground café. Cooler in summer, warmer in winter. Choice of meat from around the world: Brazilian beef to Chinese chicken.

Chef: Jim welcomes you with a smile. Small garden with barbecue.

Note: Paradise for meat-lovers, but don't expect a salad.

The Hills

Out-of-town, fine dining in great luxury. Each table has a view over the city.

Ideal for: Impressing partners — business or personal — if you can afford it.

Speciality: Greek salad and moussaka are delicious. Chef is from Athens.

The Golden Crown

Traditional restaurant pub. Famous for relaxed atmosphere and homemade beer.

Food: Large plates like grandma cooked — hearty and traditional.

Note: Quiet corners available, but arrive early — gets noisy when the band starts!

The Old Library

Café restaurant in the main square. Sit by the window and watch tourists pass.

Menu: Coffee/tea menu is longer than the food menu. Home-baked cakes for sweet tooths.

Atmosphere: Jazz music, good for chatting — but main courses are small. You might go home hungry!

Your Decision

Imagine you are going for a meal next month. Look at all five restaurants and decide which one is the best choice.

What you need to decide:

  • Which restaurant is the best choice?
  • Why is it better than the others?
  • What day and time will you go?

Remember to use:

  • Comparisons: "… is bigger than …", "… is more expensive than …"
  • Suggestions: "Why don't we try…?", "How about…?", "I would suggest…"
  • Responses: "Okay, why not?", "I'd like to try it, but…", "Maybe they have…"
