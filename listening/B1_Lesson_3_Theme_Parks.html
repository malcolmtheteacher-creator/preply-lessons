📚 Vocabulary A: Core Words
1. Attractions
Things that interest people and make them want to visit a place. In a theme park, attractions include rides, shows, and experiences designed to entertain visitors. Popular attractions draw large crowds and are often the main reason people visit.
NOW YOU TRY: "The main attractions at this theme park are the space-themed rides and..."
2. Rollercoaster
A fast amusement park ride that moves on tracks with steep drops, sharp turns, and loops. Rollercoasters are designed to give riders excitement and thrills. They come in many different styles and levels of intensity.
NOW YOU TRY: "I'm scared of rollercoasters because they go so fast and have very steep drops."
3. Lined up
Planned or arranged for the future. When something is "lined up," it means it has been scheduled or arranged to happen. For example, new attractions can be "lined up" to open soon at a theme park.
NOW YOU TRY: "The park has several exciting new rides lined up for next year, including..."
4. Expanding
Growing bigger or becoming larger. When a theme park is expanding, it means they are adding new areas, rides, or attractions. This makes the park bigger and offers visitors more things to do.
NOW YOU TRY: "The theme park is expanding to include a new Harry Potter area that will be open in..."
5. Animatronic
Robots or mechanical figures that look and move like real creatures or people. Animatronics are used in theme parks to create magical and immersive experiences. They are computer-controlled and can perform realistic movements and sounds.
NOW YOU TRY: "The animatronic dinosaurs in the Jurassic Park ride look so realistic that..."
📚 Vocabulary B: Practice & Review
Review: Attractions
Usage: "The theme park has many popular attractions for all ages."
Collocations: main attractions, major attractions, tourist attractions, new attractions
Related words: attract (verb), attractive, attraction park
Review: Rollercoaster
Usage: "This new rollercoaster has the fastest drop in the world."
Collocations: ride a rollercoaster, scary rollercoaster, wooden rollercoaster, steel rollercoaster
Related words: coaster, roller coaster (two words), thrill ride
Review: Lined up
Usage: "We have great entertainment lined up for the festival."
Collocations: have something lined up, lined up for, lined up to, activities lined up
Related words: line up (phrasal verb), queue, plan, arrange
Review: Expanding
Usage: "The company is expanding its operations to new countries."
Collocations: rapidly expanding, expanding areas, expanding into, expanding market
Related words: expand (verb), expansion (noun), expanded
Review: Animatronic
Usage: "The animatronic characters greet visitors at the entrance."
Collocations: animatronic figures, animatronic creatures, animatronic robots, animatronic technology
Related words: animatronics (noun), robotic, mechanical, automated
