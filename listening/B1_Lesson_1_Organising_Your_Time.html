🤔 Think About This

Francesco Cirillo was a university student who struggled to focus. He found a tomato-shaped kitchen timer and challenged himself: "Can I focus for just 10 minutes?" That simple question changed everything.

The word "pomodoro" means "tomato" in Italian! 🍅

🍅 The Pomodoro Technique - Summary

  1. Write a to-do list of tasks
  2. Set the timer for 25 minutes
  3. Work with intense focus
  4. Take a 3-5 minute break
  5. Tick off completed items
  6. Repeat! (Take a longer break after 4 sessions)
