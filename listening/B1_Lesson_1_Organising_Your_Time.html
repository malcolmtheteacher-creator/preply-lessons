📚 Before You Listen

🎯 Topic Introduction

Do you ever find it hard to focus when studying? Many students struggle to concentrate for long periods. Today you'll hear about a simple technique that has helped millions of people study more effectively.

💡 Did You Know?

The Pomodoro Technique was invented by an Italian university student in the 1980s. He used a tomato-shaped kitchen timer — and "pomodoro" is the Italian word for tomato! 🍅

📝 Key Vocabulary Preview

🍅 The Technique Summary

  1. Write a to-do list
  2. Set the timer for 25 minutes
  3. Work with intense focus
  4. Take a 3-5 minute break
  5. Tick off completed items
  6. Repeat!
