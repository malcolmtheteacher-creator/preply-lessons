The Best Job in the World

Tourism Australia Competition offering Six Dream Jobs

Let's Get Started!

Imagine being paid to work in one of Australia's most beautiful and exciting locations. Would you like to explore the Outback? Work with wildlife? Take photos while travelling? Or perhaps taste incredible food?

In this lesson, you'll learn about a unique Tourism Australia campaign that offered six amazing dream jobs to people from around the world. Let's discover what these exciting positions entailed.

🎧 Listen to the Description

Before you listen: What do you think would be the "best job in the world"? What skills or interests would you need?

Six Dream Jobs Overview

The campaign featured six different positions available in Australia:

1. Outback Adventurer

Location: Northern Territory. A position for someone who loves adventure and the rugged Australian wilderness.

2. Park Ranger

Location: Queensland. Working to protect and manage Australia's natural parks and wildlife.

3. Wildlife Caretaker

Location: Kangaroo Island. Looking after and working with Australia's unique animals.

4. Lifestyle Photojournalist

Location: Melbourne. Capturing Australia's culture and lifestyle through photography.

5. Taste Master

Location: Western Australia. Exploring and promoting Australia's amazing food and wine.

6. Chief Funster

Location: Sydney. Creating fun experiences and promoting tourism in Australia's most iconic city.

Listening Task: As you listen, note the specific responsibilities and requirements for each job position.

Vocabulary Set A (Click to Reveal)

Learn these key words before listening to the programme.

Stunning
extremely beautiful; taking your breath away; so impressive that you are surprised or amazed
Promote
to help something become more popular or successful; to advertise or publicise something to encourage people to use or visit it
Drawback
a disadvantage or negative aspect of something; something that makes something less good or desirable
Foodie
a person who loves food and cooking; someone who is very interested in trying new foods and culinary experiences
Appeal to
to be attractive or interesting to someone; to have a quality that makes someone like or want something

Discussion & Critical Thinking

Use these prompts to discuss the lesson with your teacher or classmates.

Discussion Question 1

Which of the six dream jobs would appeal to you the most? Explain why and what skills or qualifications you would need.

2-3 minutes discussion

Discussion Question 2

What do you think would be the biggest drawbacks of each job? Consider factors like climate, isolation, or physical demands.

3 minutes discussion

Discussion Question 3

Do you think this campaign was successful in promoting tourism to Australia? Why or why not? What impact would such a campaign have on tourists?

3-4 minutes discussion

Discussion Question 4

If you created a similar "best job in the world" campaign for your own country, what six positions would you include? Where would they be located?

4-5 minutes discussion
