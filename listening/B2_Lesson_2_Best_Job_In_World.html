Let's Get Started!

Imagine being paid to work in one of Australia's most beautiful and exciting locations. Would you like to explore the Outback? Work with wildlife? Take photos while travelling? Or perhaps taste incredible food?

In this lesson, you'll learn about a unique Tourism Australia campaign that offered six amazing dream jobs to people from around the world. Before you listen to the description, let's prepare by learning about the topic and the vocabulary you'll encounter.

Did You Know?

Did You Know?

Australia's "Best Job in the World" campaign became one of the most successful tourism marketing campaigns in history. It received over 34,000 applications from people around the globe!

Did You Know?

The six dream job positions offered a salary of approximately AU$150,000 (about 100,000 USD) for a six-month contract, making them genuinely attractive opportunities, not just marketing gimmicks.

Did You Know?

The campaign was launched during the Global Financial Crisis, when it was challenging for young people to find employment. This made the campaign even more inspiring to millions of viewers worldwide.

Vocabulary Explanations

Click on each word to see its definition and how it's used in the context of the lesson.

Stunning
Extremely beautiful and impressive; so remarkable that you are surprised or amazed.
Promote
To help something become more popular or successful; to advertise or publicise something to encourage people to visit or use it.
Drawback
A disadvantage or negative aspect of something; something that makes something less good or desirable.
Foodie
A person who loves food and cooking; someone who is very interested in trying new foods and having culinary experiences.
Appeal to
To be attractive or interesting to someone; to have a quality that makes someone like or want something.

Lesson Summary

Here's what we covered in this lesson about Australia's dream jobs campaign:

The Six Dream Jobs

  • Outback Adventurer - Northern Territory: Adventure and wilderness exploration
  • Park Ranger - Queensland: Nature protection and wildlife management
  • Wildlife Caretaker - Kangaroo Island: Animal care and conservation
  • Lifestyle Photojournalist - Melbourne: Cultural documentation through photography
  • Taste Master - Western Australia: Food and wine promotion
  • Chief Funster - Sydney: Tourism and entertainment

Key Vocabulary Review

  • Stunning - Extremely beautiful and impressive
  • Promote - To help something become more popular
  • Drawback - A disadvantage or negative aspect
  • Foodie - A person who loves food and cooking
  • Appeal to - To be attractive or interesting to someone
