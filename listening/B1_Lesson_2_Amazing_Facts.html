🎯 Pre-Listening: Get Ready!

In this lesson, you will listen to fascinating facts about the human body. Before you listen, let's prepare by thinking about what you already know and previewing the key vocabulary.

💡 Did You Know?
Fact 1: Your Body is Mostly Not You
Only 10% of the cells in your body are actually human cells. The other 90% are bacteria living inside and on your body. Most of these bacteria are helpful and harmless!
Fact 2: Trillions of Cells
Your body contains approximately 37.2 trillion cells! Most of them are red blood cells that carry oxygen throughout your body. That's an enormous number working together to keep you alive.
Fact 3: Empty Space Everywhere
Did you know that atoms (the tiny particles that make up everything) are 99.9% empty space? The rest is just energy. This means you're mostly empty space too!

1. Bacteria
Tiny living things that live everywhere, including in and on your body. Most bacteria are harmless and actually help your body work properly. Some bacteria can cause disease, but many are essential for your health.

2. Cells
The smallest living parts of all living things. Your body is made of billions of cells that work together. Different types of cells do different jobs - for example, nerve cells send messages, and muscle cells help you move.

3. Digest
To break down food in your body so your body can use the nutrients. Your stomach and intestines work together to digest food into smaller pieces that your body can absorb and use for energy.
