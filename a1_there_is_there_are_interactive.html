🏠 There is / There are

Describe your room, your school, and your neighborhood!

📚 How to Say "There is" and "There are"

When you want to describe what exists in a place (your room, your school, your city), English uses "There is" and "There are". This is different from French! 🇫🇷

☝️

SINGULAR (1 thing)

There is + ONE thing

"There is a TV in my room."

"There is a park near my house."

✌️

PLURAL (2+ things)

There are + MANY things

"There are two windows in my room."

"There are many shops in my town."

🎯 The Pattern

There is + a/an + singular noun

There is a bed. There is an apple.


There are + number/some/many + plural noun

There are three books. There are some chairs.

❌ Negative Form

There isn't = There is not (singular)

"There isn't a balcony in my room."


There aren't = There are not (plural)

"There aren't any parks near my school."

💡 French Comparison

In French, you use "Il y a" for both singular AND plural.
In English, you MUST choose: "There is" (1) or "There are" (2+)

Il y a un lit = There is a bed

Il y a des livres = There are books

💡 Question Forms

Is there...? → Yes, there is. / No, there isn't.

Are there...? → Yes, there are. / No, there aren't.

How many ... are there? → There is/are + number

🗣️ Describe Your World!

🛏️ Describe Your Room

Tell your teacher about your room. Use "There is" and "There are":

  • Is there a bed? A desk? A TV?
  • Are there posters on the walls?
  • How many windows are there?
  • What else is there in your room?
  • What ISN'T there? (There isn't... / There aren't...)

🏘️ Describe Your Neighborhood

Tell your teacher about where you live:

  • Is there a park near your house?
  • Are there shops on your street?
  • Is there a school nearby?
  • Are there restaurants? Cinemas? Sports facilities?
  • What ISN'T there that you wish existed?

🏫 Describe Your School

Tell your teacher about your school:

  • Is there a library? A gym? A cafeteria?
  • Are there computers in your classroom?
  • How many classrooms are there?
  • Is there a sports field?

🏆 Final Challenges

🎯 Challenge 1: The Perfect Room

Describe your DREAM room! What would be there?

Use: There is... / There are... / There isn't... / There aren't...

Example:

"In my dream room, there is a huge TV. There are three gaming consoles. There is a mini fridge! There isn't any homework... 😄"

🎯 Challenge 2: Compare Places

Compare your room and your classroom:

Example:

"In my room, there is a bed. In my classroom, there isn't a bed!"

"In my classroom, there are 20 desks. In my room, there is only one desk."

🌟 SUPER CHALLENGE: Full Description!

Use EVERYTHING you've learned to describe a place!

Combine:

  • ✅ Lesson 10: There is / There are
  • ✅ Lesson 1: I like... (what you like about the place)
  • ✅ Lesson 3: How often (how often you go there)
  • ✅ Lesson 6: I can... (what you can do there)

MEGA Example:

"There is a park near my house. There are many trees and there is a football field. I usually go there on weekends with my friends. I can play football and I can ride my bike. I like going there because it's fun!"
