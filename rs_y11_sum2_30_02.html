What is the Big Bang theory?

🔒Reveal answer

Answer: Universe began ~13.8bn years ago from hot, dense point. Expanding ever since. Supported by cosmic background radiation. Scientific explanation for universe origin.

What is the Christian creation account?

🔒Reveal answer

Answer: Genesis 1-2: God creates world in six days, rests on seventh. Humanity made in God's image (Imago Dei). Some literal, some metaphorical reading.

What is the Islamic creation account?

🔒Reveal answer

Answer: Qur'an: Allah created world and humans. Humans created from clay (Q32:7). Some Muslims accept theistic evolution, all affirm Allah as Creator.

What does 'sanctity of life' mean?

🔒Reveal answer

Answer: Human life is sacred, infinite value. God-given. Underpins teaching on abortion, euthanasia, capital punishment in Christianity and Islam.

What is Christian teaching on abortion?

🔒Reveal answer

Answer: Catholic: always immoral (ensoulment at conception). Protestant: more divided, allow if mothers life at risk, rape/incest. All: life in womb is significant.

What is Islamic teaching on abortion?

🔒Reveal answer

Answer: Permitted before 4 months (before ensoulment — Q23:12-14). After 4 months: forbidden. If mothers life at risk: her life takes priority.

What is euthanasia and Christian views?

🔒Reveal answer

Answer: Deliberately ending life to relieve suffering. Most Christians oppose: life is God's gift. Passive euthanasia (allowing death) more widely accepted.

What is Islamic view on euthanasia?

🔒Reveal answer

Answer: Forbidden — Q4:29 'Do not kill yourselves.' Suffering has spiritual purpose. Active killing prohibited. Withdrawing life support debated.

What is Christian view on afterlife?

🔒Reveal answer

Answer: Resurrection and eternal life with God (Heaven) or separated from God (Hell). Jesus' resurrection is foundation. Some believe in Purgatory (Catholic).

What is Islamic view on afterlife?

🔒Reveal answer

Answer: Day of Judgement (Yawm al-Qiyamah): all resurrected and judged. Paradise (Jannah) and Hell (Jahannam). Akhirah is Article 5 of Islamic faith.

Theme B Paper 2 Format

Q1: 1 mark origin | Q2: 1 mark life issue | Q3: 4 marks explain two teachings | Q4: 6 marks evaluate (e.g., 'Sanctity of life never permits abortion') | Q5: 12 marks essay

Key Concepts

Creation (Big Bang vs. Religious), Sanctity of life, Abortion (when, why), Euthanasia (passive vs. active), Afterlife beliefs

Creation: Science & Religion

Big Bang is HOW universe began. Christianity/Islam can accept Big Bang while affirming God as Creator. Not conflict if understood correctly.

Sanctity of Life Ethics

Human life sacred because God-made (Gen 1:27) or created by Allah. Infinite value. Grounds opposition to abortion, euthanasia, capital punishment in religious ethics.

Abortion Complexity

When does life begin? Conception (Christian)? Ensoulment 4 months (Islamic)? Viability (some)? Does mothers autonomy override fetal rights? Medical exceptions?

Afterlife: Christian vs. Islamic

Christian: Heaven/Hell, resurrection of body. Islamic: Jannah/Jahannam, Day of Judgement. Both: actions in life determine afterlife. Belief shapes present ethics.

6-Mark Evaluation Technique

Structure: (1) One viewpoint (2 marks) | (2) Different viewpoint (2 marks) | (3) Your conclusion (2 marks). Example: 'Some say sanctity forbids all abortion. However, Islam allows before 4 months if mother at risk. Therefore sanctity doesn't absolutely prohibit abortion in all cases.'

Can Christians accept the Big Bang?

Answer: Yes. Big Bang describes HOW universe began. Many believers see it as God's mechanism. Science and faith need not conflict.

Key difference: Christian vs. Islamic abortion?

Answer: Catholic: always wrong. Muslim: permitted before 4 months if needed (mothers health, rape). Protestant: varies.

Why most Christians oppose euthanasia?

Answer: Sanctity of life (God's gift), suffering may have spiritual purpose, violates 'Thou shalt not kill.' Passive euthanasia more accepted.

Watch Out

Don't oversimplify: specify Catholic vs. Protestant. Science and faith aren't enemies. Islamic teaching allows abortion before 4 months — know this. Passive ≠ active euthanasia.
