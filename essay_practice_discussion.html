Understanding Discussion Essays

In a discussion essay, you must present BOTH sides of an argument fairly, then give YOUR opinion.

Today's Question:

Some people believe that working from home is more productive and beneficial for employees. Others think that working in an office is essential for collaboration and career development.

Discuss both views and give your own opinion.

Unlike Opinion Essays where you only argue ONE side, Discussion Essays require you to explain BOTH views before giving your opinion.

Discussion vs Opinion: Key Differences

Opinion Essay Discussion Essay
"To what extent do you agree?" "Discuss both views and give your opinion"
Body 1: Your argument Body 1: View A (not your view)
Body 2: Your second argument Body 2: View B (not your view)
Opinion throughout Opinion mainly in conclusion

Essay Structure for Discussion Essays

Introduction (2-3 sentences)

Paraphrase topic + Acknowledge both views exist + Brief thesis

~40-50 words
Body Paragraph 1: VIEW A (4-5 sentences)

Present the FIRST view fairly + Explanation + Example

~90-100 words
Body Paragraph 2: VIEW B (4-5 sentences)

Present the SECOND view fairly + Explanation + Example

~90-100 words
Conclusion (2-3 sentences)

YOUR opinion + Justification

~40-50 words

Planning This Essay

Before writing, identify the two views and plan your opinion:

View A: Working from home is better

  • No commuting saves time and reduces stress
  • Fewer office distractions, better focus
  • Better work-life balance

View B: Office work is essential

  • Face-to-face collaboration drives innovation
  • Networking and visibility for career growth
  • Clear boundary between work and personal life

My Opinion (for conclusion):

A hybrid approach combining both is ideal - flexibility of remote work with occasional office collaboration.

Model Essay (Band 8)

Read this model essay carefully. Notice how BOTH views are presented fairly before the opinion.

Question:

Discuss both views on working from home vs office work, and give your opinion.

INTRO The question of whether employees should work remotely or in traditional offices has become increasingly relevant in recent years. While some advocate for home-based work, others maintain that office environments remain essential. This essay will examine both perspectives before presenting my own view.
VIEW A Those who support remote working point to several compelling advantages. The elimination of daily commuting not only saves employees significant time but also reduces stress and environmental impact. Furthermore, many workers report higher productivity at home due to fewer interruptions from colleagues. Research from Stanford University found that remote workers were 13% more productive than their office-based counterparts.
VIEW B On the other hand, proponents of office work argue that physical presence in a workplace facilitates collaboration and innovation. Spontaneous conversations and brainstorming sessions are difficult to replicate in virtual environments. Additionally, being visible in the workplace can enhance career prospects, as employees have more opportunities to network with colleagues and demonstrate their capabilities to management.
CONCLUSION In my opinion, a hybrid model that combines both approaches offers the most effective solution. Were companies to implement flexible arrangements, employees could enjoy the focus and convenience of remote work while maintaining the collaborative benefits of regular office attendance.

Band 8 Features Used:

  • Nominalization: "The question of whether...", "The elimination of daily commuting"
  • View A intro: "Those who support remote working point to..."
  • View B intro: "On the other hand, proponents of office work argue that..."
  • Advanced conditional: "Were companies to implement..."
  • Research citation: Stanford University study
  • Balanced presentation: Both views given equal weight
Word count: 272 words. Notice how the opinion only appears clearly in the CONCLUSION.

Write Your Introduction

The introduction has 3 jobs: (1) Paraphrase the topic, (2) Acknowledge both views, (3) Say what the essay will do.

Question:

Discuss both views on working from home vs office work, and give your opinion.

Sentence 1: Paraphrase the Topic (nominalization)

Use this frame:

The [question/issue/debate] of whether [topic rephrased] has [become increasingly relevant/sparked considerable debate] in recent years.

Example: "The question of whether employees should work remotely or in traditional offices has become increasingly relevant in recent years."

0 words

Sentence 2: Acknowledge Both Views

Use this frame:

While some [advocate for/support/believe in] [View A], others [maintain/argue/contend] that [View B].
0 words

Sentence 3: Thesis/Outline

Use this frame:

This essay will examine both perspectives before presenting my own view.

This is a standard thesis for discussion essays - you can use it as-is or adapt slightly.

0 words

Write Body Paragraph 1: View A

Present the view that working from home is better. Present it FAIRLY - even if you disagree!

You are explaining what OTHER PEOPLE think, not your opinion. Use phrases like "Those who support..." or "Proponents argue that..."

Sentence 1: Topic Sentence (Introduce View A)

Use this frame:

[Those who support/Proponents of/Advocates of] [View A] point to [several compelling advantages/numerous benefits].
0 words

Sentence 2: First Advantage (use nominalization)

Use this frame:

[The elimination/removal/absence] of [problem] not only [benefit 1] but also [benefit 2].
0 words

Sentences 3-4: Second Advantage + Evidence

Suggested structures:

Furthermore, [second advantage]. Research from [source] found that [evidence].
0 words

View A Checklist:

Those who support remote working point to several compelling advantages. The elimination of daily commuting not only saves employees significant time but also reduces stress and environmental impact. Furthermore, many workers report higher productivity at home due to fewer interruptions from colleagues. Research from Stanford University found that remote workers were 13% more productive than their office-based counterparts.

(67 words)

Write Body Paragraph 2: View B

Present the OPPOSING view: office work is essential. Less scaffolding this time!

Start with a CONTRAST phrase: "On the other hand," or "Conversely," or "However,"

Suggested structures to use:

  • Topic sentence: "On the other hand, proponents of office work argue that..."
  • Explanation: "...physical presence facilitates collaboration and innovation"
  • Nominalization: "Being visible in the workplace can enhance..."
  • Additional point: "Additionally, employees have more opportunities to..."
0 words (aim for 80-100)

View B Checklist:

On the other hand, proponents of office work argue that physical presence in a workplace facilitates collaboration and innovation. Spontaneous conversations and brainstorming sessions are difficult to replicate in virtual environments. Additionally, being visible in the workplace can enhance career prospects, as employees have more opportunities to network with colleagues and demonstrate their capabilities to management.

(62 words)

Write Your Conclusion

NOW you give YOUR OPINION. This is where you take a position!

In Discussion Essays, the conclusion is where your opinion belongs. Don't just summarise - COMMIT to a view!

Options for your opinion:

  • Hybrid/balanced: "A combination of both approaches is ideal"
  • Favour View A: "Remote work offers more significant benefits"
  • Favour View B: "Office work remains essential for most roles"
  • It depends: "The best approach depends on the nature of the work"
Use an advanced conditional for impact: "Were companies to implement..." or "Should organisations adopt..."
0 words (aim for 40-50)

Conclusion Checklist:

In my opinion, a hybrid model that combines both approaches offers the most effective solution. Were companies to implement flexible arrangements, employees could enjoy the focus and convenience of remote work while maintaining the collaborative benefits of regular office attendance.

(44 words)
