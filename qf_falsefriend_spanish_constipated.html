🎯 Constipado ≠ Constipated (Spanish)

Medical false friend | A2 Level

Let's see what you notice...

Read this sentence. What feels wrong?

I'm constipated so I can't come to work. (meaning: cold)

What's the correction?

✓ Correction

I have a cold so I can't come to work.

📌 The Rule

Spanish constipado = having a cold. English constipated = digestive problem (can't go to the toilet!).

Also Portuguese 'constipado' = cold. This is a very common and embarrassing mix-up.

📚 The Rule (Dual Coding)

🇪🇸 Spanish: CONSTIPADO

🤧

= having a cold

= runny nose, cough

Estoy constipado = I have a cold

🇬🇧 English: CONSTIPATED

😬

= digestive problem

= can't go to the toilet

Very different meaning!

💡 Tip: To say you have a cold: 'I have a cold' / 'I've got a cold' / 'I've caught a cold'.
