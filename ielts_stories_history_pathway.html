IELTS through Stories & History

The same true stories and short fiction — turned into an exam engine. A ready bank of real examples for Writing Task 2, and rich material for Speaking, each lesson tied to a genuine exam question.

Examiners reward two things most students never build: specific real-world examples in Writing Task 2, and the confidence to talk at length about abstract ideas in Speaking. A memorable story gives you both.

Every lesson below is a complete speaking-and-reading class in its own right. But here they are re-sorted for the exam: the histories become a bank of examples you can drop straight into an essay or a Part 2 answer, and a hand-picked set of short stories sharpens the abstract discussion of Speaking Part 3. Work down the themes in order — each is one of the big Task 2 topics — and each card carries a real, standard IELTS question it prepares you for. Every lesson also comes at two levels inside, so you can read up or down.

How to turn a story into a band score

1 · Read

Work through the lesson — the story, the key words, the speaking tasks — as a normal class.

2 · Steal

Note the one real example and 5–6 topic words you could reuse under exam pressure.

3 · Apply

Answer the real Task 2 or Speaking question on the card, out loud or in writing, using them.

4 · Reuse

Bank it. One strong story can serve three or four different essay questions.
