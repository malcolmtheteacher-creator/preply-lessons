Piranesi
Book Club

Four meetings around a strange, short and beautiful novel: a man, an endless house and a sea that comes and goes. Read it alone, in pairs or round a table.

Susanna Clarke's Piranesi (2020) is a short, strange novel about a man who lives alone in a House of endless halls, with statues in every one and the tide moving through the lower floors. It is calm, mysterious and beautifully written, and readers argue about what it means, which makes it ideal for a book club.

Each of the four meetings takes one part of the book and gives you a summary to read, a simple tool for talking about it, the language you need, and plenty of speaking. Use them one-to-one, in pairs or with a group. The meetings discuss the whole book, including the ending, so finish reading before the last one. The summaries are written in our own words, and the book itself is not quoted at length.

Who it is for

Adults who read for pleasure and want to talk about what they read: members of book clubs, one-to-one readers and anyone who wants a real conversation in English about a real book. The language level is C1, and the book itself is easy to read but subtle. You need no knowledge of fantasy or literary terms; each meeting explains what you need.
