What Do You Do at the Weekend?

A2 Level · Daily Life

Your Weekends

Let's talk about what you do at the weekend. Answer these questions:

Question 1: What did you do last weekend?

You can say:

"I went to the park with my friends."

"I stayed at home and watched a movie."

"I did the shopping and cleaned my house."

"I slept in and then had lunch with my family."

Question 2: Are your weekends busy or relaxed?

You can say:

"My weekends are very busy. I have lots to do."

"They're relaxed. I like sleeping and resting."

"A mix. Some activities, some free time."

Question 3: How do you usually spend your free time?

You can say:

"I read books or listen to music."

"I play sports or go to the gym."

"I spend time with family and friends."

"I watch TV or scroll through my phone."

Activity Words

Click on each phrase to see the definition:

Hang Out Spend time with friends, relaxing and talking
Sleep In Stay in bed longer than usual in the morning
Go for a Walk Take a slow journey on foot, usually for exercise
Catch Up Meet someone and talk about recent news
Do Chores Do housework like cleaning, washing, and cooking
Have a Barbecue Cook and eat food outside with friends or family

Present Simple vs Past Simple

Present Simple (habits): "I usually go to the park on Saturdays."

Past Simple (completed actions): "I went to the park last Saturday."

The Perfect Weekend

Two Different Weekends

Carlos's Busy Weekend

Carlos loves activity. Last Saturday, he woke up at 7 o'clock and went for a long run. Then he did the shopping at the supermarket. In the afternoon, he caught up with his friends at a café. They talked and laughed for two hours. In the evening, he had a barbecue in his garden. Ten friends came. They cooked meat and vegetables, and danced to music. Carlos went to bed at midnight. He was very happy but very tired!

Anna's Relaxed Weekend

Anna likes peace and quiet. Last Sunday, she slept in until 9 o'clock. She made coffee and breakfast, then sat in her garden and read a book. She did the housework slowly. In the afternoon, she took a walk in the park. It was calm and quiet. She listened to birds. In the evening, she watched her favourite film and went to bed early. She felt very relaxed and happy.

Comprehension Questions

1. What time did Carlos wake up?

Carlos woke up at 7 o'clock.

2. What did Carlos do in the afternoon?

He caught up with his friends at a café.

3. How many people came to Carlos's barbecue?

Ten friends came.

4. What did Anna do in the park?

She took a walk, listened to birds, and enjoyed the peace and quiet.

5. Are Carlos and Anna similar? Why or why not?

No, they are different. Carlos likes activity and people. Anna likes quiet and peace. But they both enjoy their weekends!

Weekend Discussion

Let's talk about work-life balance and weekends at a simple level.

1. Are weekends important for you?

Do you need rest after work? Is free time important?

2. How much time do you spend with family and friends on weekends?

Who do you see? What do you do together?

3. Do you like your weekends busy or relaxed?

Which is better for you? Why?

4. What's the worst thing about weekends?

Sunday night blues? Not enough time? Too much housework?

5. Do you do the same things every weekend, or do you like to try new activities?

Why?

Weekend Balance Tips

A good weekend includes:

Time to rest and sleep
Time with family or friends
Time for your hobbies (sport, reading, music, etc.)
Time to do necessary housework
Time for activities you enjoy
Time to do something new or different
