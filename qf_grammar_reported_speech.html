📝 Reported Speech Tense Shift

Shift tenses when reporting past speech | B2 Level

Let's see what you notice...

Read this reported speech. What's wrong with the tense?

She said she is tired.

What's the correction?

✓ Correction

She said she was tired.

📌 The Rule

When reporting PAST speech, shift tenses back one level:

Direct: "I am tired" → Reported: "She said she was tired"

Direct: "I will come" → Reported: "He told me he would come"

Direct: "I can help" → Reported: "She said she could help"
