Signs and Symptoms of Stress

Understanding the signs of stress helps you recognize when you need to take action:

Physical Symptoms

  • Headaches or migraines
  • Muscle tension and pain
  • Sleep disturbances (insomnia or oversleeping)
  • Fatigue and low energy
  • Stomach problems or digestive issues
  • Increased heart rate and blood pressure

Emotional & Mental Symptoms

  • Anxiety and nervousness
  • Irritability and mood swings
  • Difficulty concentrating
  • Loss of motivation
  • Feeling overwhelmed or out of control
  • Depression or sadness

Behavioural Symptoms

  • Avoiding work or responsibilities
  • Changes in eating or sleeping habits
  • Increased use of alcohol or caffeine
  • Neglecting exercise or self-care
  • Difficulty making decisions

Strategies to Reduce Workplace Stress

Here are evidence-based techniques to manage stress and increase productivity:

1. Set Boundaries Between Work and Home

Why it matters: Separating work life from home life helps you recharge and prevents burnout.

  • Set specific work hours and stick to them
  • Don't check work emails after hours
  • Create a physical boundary (work area vs. rest area)
  • Use transition activities between work and home time

2. Prioritize Tasks

Why it matters: Focusing on what's important reduces overwhelm and increases efficiency.

  • Identify the most important tasks each day
  • Break large projects into smaller steps
  • Tackle difficult tasks when you have the most energy
  • Learn to say "no" to non-essential requests

3. Take Regular Breaks

Why it matters: Short breaks improve focus and prevent mental fatigue.

  • Take a 5-10 minute break every hour
  • Step away from your desk
  • Go outside or get fresh air
  • Stretch or do light movement

4. Practice Mindfulness or Meditation

Why it matters: These practices reduce anxiety and improve emotional regulation.

  • Try 10 minutes of daily meditation
  • Practice deep breathing exercises
  • Use mindfulness apps for guided sessions
  • Be present in daily activities

5. Exercise Regularly

Why it matters: Physical activity releases endorphins and improves overall wellbeing.

  • Aim for 30 minutes of exercise, 5 days a week
  • Choose activities you enjoy
  • Walk or cycle to work if possible
  • Use exercise as stress relief

6. Maintain Healthy Sleep Habits

Why it matters: Good sleep improves mood, focus, and stress resilience.

  • Aim for 7-9 hours of sleep per night
  • Create a consistent sleep schedule
  • Avoid screens before bedtime
  • Keep your bedroom cool and dark

Stress Management Plan: Speaking Task

You have 5 minutes to develop and discuss a personal plan to reduce stress and increase productivity. Think about current challenges and concrete strategies you can implement.

Time remaining: 5:00

Framework for Your Plan:

1. Identify Your Current Stress

What causes you the most stress at work or in daily life? Describe one or two main stressors.

2. Select Two Strategies

Choose two strategies from the list above that you think would help you most. Why do you think they would be effective?

3. Create an Action Plan

How specifically will you implement these strategies? Think about:

  • When will you start?
  • What will be the first step?
  • What challenges might you face?
  • How will you measure success?

4. Expected Outcomes

How do you think these changes will affect your stress level and productivity? What specific improvements do you hope to see?
