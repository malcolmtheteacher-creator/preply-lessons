🤔 Possibly vs Eventually

B1 • Polish speakers • False friends: ewentualnie ≠ eventually

🔍 Let's notice what we see

Look at this sentence. What's the problem?

"Eventually, we could go tomorrow."

(But they mean: maybe/perhaps we could go tomorrow, not after a long time!)

⚠️ The False Friend:

Polish "ewentualnie" LOOKS like English "eventually", but they mean DIFFERENT things!

In Polish: ewentualnie = possibly, perhaps (ewentualnie moglibyśmy = we could possibly)

In English: eventually = in the end, after a long time

In English: possibly/perhaps = maybe

🛠️ Understand the difference

📊 Two different words:

🇵🇱 Polish "ewentualnie"

🤔 POSSIBLY / PERHAPS

Maybe, it's possible

"Ewentualnie moglibyśmy" = We could possibly

🇬🇧 English "eventually"

⏳ IN THE END

After a long time, at last

"Eventually I found the answer" = After trying

🎯 Key Rule:

Polish ewentualnie → English POSSIBLY / PERHAPS

English eventually → Polish w końcu / ostatecznie (in the end)

Maybe: POSSIBLY. After a long time: EVENTUALLY.

✏️ Guided practice

1. "_____, I found the answer after days of research." (Possibly / Eventually)
2. "We could _____ try that restaurant next week." (possibly / eventually)
3. "If you wait long enough, _____ it will happen." (Possibly / Eventually)
4. "I might _____ go to Paris — I'm not sure yet." (possibly / eventually)

🚀 Now you try — no help!

Choose the correct word. Check your answers when you're done.

1. "_____, they arrived at their destination after weeks of traveling." (Possibly / Eventually)
Answer: "Eventually they arrived."

→ After weeks = eventually.

2. "We could _____ meet up for coffee this weekend."
Answer: "We could possibly meet up."

→ Maybe = possibly.

3. "_____, after years of hard work, she became successful."
Answer: "Eventually she became successful."

→ After years = eventually.

4. "I might _____ change my mind about this job."
Answer: "I might possibly change my mind."

→ Uncertain = possibly.

✅ I can say 'possibly' or 'perhaps' instead of 'eventually' when meaning maybe
