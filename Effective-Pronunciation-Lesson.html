Remember

Try to use a range of pronunciation features while you speak:

  • Chunking – pause between thought groups
  • Sentence stress – emphasise key words
  • Intonation – rise ↗ for "more coming", fall ↘ for "finished"
  • Weak forms – reduce unstressed words
