Key Vocabulary for Summarising

Click each card to see the definition.

summarise to give a short version of the main ideas
main idea the most important point or concept
supporting detail information that explains or proves the main idea
paraphrase to express something using different words
condense to make something shorter or more compact
objective factual and uninfluenced by personal feelings or opinions
omit to leave out or not include something
rephrase to say or write something again in different words

Useful Phrases for Summarising

The main point is... — introduces the central idea

In brief... — used to give a short version

To summarise... — begins a summary statement

The key idea is... — highlights the most important concept

In other words... — introduces a paraphrase or rephrase

Reading Practice: Urban Climate Change

Read the text carefully, then answer the questions below.

Cities are warming faster than rural areas, a phenomenon called the urban heat island effect. Scientists have found that concrete buildings, dark roofs, and limited green spaces trap heat in cities, raising temperatures by 2-5°C above surrounding areas. This affects health, especially for elderly people and those with respiratory problems. Many cities are now planting trees, creating rooftop gardens, and painting roofs white to reduce heat absorption. These solutions are helping to lower city temperatures and improve air quality for residents.

Question 1: What is the urban heat island effect?

The urban heat island effect is when cities are warmer than the countryside around them because of concrete, buildings, and lack of green spaces.

Question 2: How much hotter can cities be than surrounding areas?

2-5°C hotter than surrounding rural areas.

Question 3: What groups of people are most affected by urban heat?

Elderly people and people with respiratory problems.

Question 4: Name three solutions mentioned for reducing city temperatures.

Planting trees, creating rooftop gardens, and painting roofs white.

How to Write a Good Summary

Key Rules:

  • No opinions: Only include facts from the text, not your personal views.
  • Shorter than original: A summary should be about 1/3 the length of the original text.
  • Use your own words: Paraphrase the main ideas. Do not copy sentences directly.
  • Include only main ideas: Leave out examples, minor details, and supporting evidence.
  • Keep the original meaning: Do not change or distort what the author said.

Identify the Main Points

From the urban climate text above, what are the 3 main points?

1. What causes urban heat islands?

Concrete buildings, dark roofs, and lack of green spaces trap heat in cities.

2. Who does it affect?

Elderly people and those with respiratory problems are most affected.

3. What are the solutions?

Cities are planting trees, creating rooftop gardens, and painting roofs white.

While Speaking, Remember:

  • Start with "To summarise..." or "The main point is..."
  • Cover the most important ideas
  • Speak at a natural pace
  • Try to fill the full 60 seconds
  • Use your own words, not memorised text
