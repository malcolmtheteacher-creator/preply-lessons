Grammar Index

Every grammar point in the series, A to Z and by topic. Find the structure you need, then open the story that teaches it.

Each lesson in Short Stories, Long Shadows teaches one grammar structure through its story, and most stories come at two levels with a different point at each. This index lists 104 grammar points in 11 groups, taken from the Grammar tab of all 169 lessons (90 at B2/C1, 79 at A2/B1, including the novella episodes and the stories from the Teens collection).
