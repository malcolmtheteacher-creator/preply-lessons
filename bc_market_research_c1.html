Market Research Vocabulary

Click each card to reveal the definition.

Survey A set of questions asked to collect information from people
Customer A person who buys goods or services from a business
Feedback Information about performance or experience
Opinion A personal belief or judgment not necessarily based on fact
Preferences A greater liking for one alternative over others
Convenience The state of being able to proceed with ease; free of trouble
Frequency How often something happens
Habits Regular behaviors or practices

Asking Survey Questions

Use these question starters to conduct market research effectively.

Question Starter Purpose Example
What...? Ask about things or preferences What do you like about the service?
When...? Ask about timing When do you usually shop here?
Where...? Ask about location Where do you prefer to buy groceries?
How often...? Ask about frequency How often do you use public transport?
Do you ever...? Ask about experience Do you ever buy food at convenience stores?
Do you think...? Ask for opinions Do you think the prices are reasonable?
How could...? Ask for suggestions How could customer service be better?

Polite Opening

"Do you mind if I ask you a few questions?"

This opens a survey politely and asks for permission before proceeding.

Word Stress in Questions

Listen to which words are stressed (emphasized). The stressed words are bolded below.

Example Questions with Word Stress

  1. Do you mind if I ask you a few QUESTIONS?
  2. How OFten do you drink COFfee?
  3. Do you ever buy something with your COFfee?
  4. How MUCH do you PAY for a coffee at a CAfé?
  5. Is it EXPENsive where you LIVE?

Practice

Say each question aloud, stressing the bolded words. Notice how stress helps convey meaning and keeps your listener engaged.

  • Stress makes a word slightly longer and louder
  • Unstressed words are quick and soft
  • Good stress patterns make you sound natural and confident
  • Practice with a recording to hear the difference

Speaking Task: Conduct Market Research

Time Remaining
10:00

Your Task

You are conducting market research. Create a survey about one of these topics and ask questions to learn about customer preferences and habits. Speak for 10 minutes.

Choose one topic:

  • A transport company wants to improve their service
  • An internet provider wants to understand customer needs
  • A supermarket chain wants to know about shopping habits

What to Do

  1. Start with a polite opening: "Do you mind if I ask you a few questions?"
  2. Ask about important features (e.g., price, convenience, quality)
  3. Use varied question starters (What, When, How often, Do you think, etc.)
  4. Make notes on what customers want
  5. Report back what you learned

Reflection Questions

Think about customer service and market research in your own experience.

Question 1: What do you think about customer service in your country or where you live?

Question 2: What is important when it comes to customer service? Name 3–5 factors.

Question 3: How can customer service be better in places you know?

Question 4: Describe a customer service experience you were very happy with. What made it good?

Question 5: Describe a customer service experience you were unhappy with. What did you do?

Question 6: What's the best way to do a market survey—face-to-face, phone, or online? Why?

Final Thought

Do you think companies in your country listen to customer feedback? How could they improve?
