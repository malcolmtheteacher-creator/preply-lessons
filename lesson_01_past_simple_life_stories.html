Why Do We Need Past Simple? 🎯

The Past Simple tells us: "This happened. It's finished."

Use it to talk about completed actions in the past - yesterday, last week, in 2020, when you were a child...

How to Form Past Simple 📝

Regular Verbs: Just Add -ED

work → worked | play → played | watch → watched

Spelling Rule Example Result
Most verbs: + ed work, play, watch worked, played, watched
Verbs ending in -e: + d live, love, dance lived, loved, danced
Consonant + y: y → ied study, try, cry studied, tried, cried
Short vowel + consonant: double consonant + ed stop, plan, travel stopped, planned, travelled

Negative & Questions: Use DID

Form Structure Example
➕ Positive Subject + past verb I went to Paris.
➖ Negative Subject + didn't + base verb I didn't go to Paris.
❓ Question Did + subject + base verb? Did you go to Paris?

⚠️ Common Mistake!

WRONG: Did you went to Paris?

RIGHT: Did you go to Paris?

After DID, use the BASE form of the verb (no -ed, no irregular form!)

Let's Practice! ✏️

Exercise 1: Choose the Correct Form

1. I __________ (go) to the cinema last night.

goed went gone

2. She __________ (not/like) the food at the restaurant.

not liked didn't like doesn't liked

3. __________ you __________ (see) the match on TV?

Did ... see Did ... saw Do ... saw

4. They __________ (study) all night for the exam.

studyed studied studieed

5. We __________ (have) a great time at the party.

haved had has

6. He __________ (not/tell) me the truth.

not told didn't told didn't tell

Story Starters to Help You:

  • "This happened when I was..."
  • "It was [year/day/time]..."
  • "I remember when..."
  • "One day, I..."
  • "The story begins when..."

Useful Verbs for Stories:

Regular: happened, started, decided, wanted, looked, walked, talked, laughed, cried, loved, hated

Irregular: went, saw, met, got, had, made, took, came, thought, felt, said, told, knew

Final Challenge 🏆

The "Life Timeline" Challenge

Create a timeline of 5 important events in your life. For each event, speak for 30 seconds to 1 minute!

👶

Event 1: Early Childhood (0-5 years)

Something that happened when you were very young. Maybe a story your parents told you, or your earliest memory.

I was born in... | When I was 3, I... | My parents told me that I...

🎒

Event 2: School Years

An important memory from your school days. A friend, a teacher, a success, or a funny moment.

When I was in primary school... | I remember one day... | My best friend and I...

🌟

Event 3: A Turning Point

Something that changed your life or the way you think. A decision, an experience, or a realization.

Everything changed when... | I decided to... | That was when I realized...

🎯

Event 4: An Achievement

Something you're proud of. It could be big or small - what matters is that it meant something to you.

I worked hard and... | I finally... | The moment I... was amazing!

🌅

Event 5: Recent Past

Something important that happened in the last few years. How did it shape who you are today?

A few years ago... | Last year, I... | Recently, I...
