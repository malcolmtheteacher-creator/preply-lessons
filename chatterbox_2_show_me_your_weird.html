Certified Weird

No build-up. No apology. Just blurt it out: what is the weirdest habit, taste or opinion you have right now? The thing you'd usually only admit to your closest friend — say it straight, right now.

Go

Say it. One weird habit, taste, or opinion — whatever comes first. Don't explain it yet, don't defend it, just get it out before the timer stops you.

01:00

Origin Story

Every quirk has a history. Pick three of your weird little habits, tastes or opinions — the ones you just admitted, or new ones you're only now remembering — and give each one a proper backstory.

For each of the three, cover: where it came from, when it embarrasses you, and who else you secretly suspect does it too.
Tell the origin story of three of your quirks

Take your time with each one. Don't just list them — tell the story behind each.

03:00
If you dry up, answer these for whichever quirk you're on: Since when has this been a thing for you? What was the worst moment it ever embarrassed you? Who else do you secretly think does this too — and why do you think that?

Secret Superpower

Flip it. Your weirdness isn't a flaw — it's a hidden superpower. Argue that case like you mean it.

Sell your weirdness as a superpower

Take one of your quirks — any of them — and convince someone that it actually makes you better at something, or better company, or just better off than everyone pretending to be normal. Sell it hard.

02:00

Why We Hide It

Here's the strange thing: almost everyone has something like this, and almost everyone hides it. Why?

Make your case

Why do people hide their strange side? What are they afraid of? And what do you think would actually change — for better or worse — if everyone just showed their weirdness openly, all the time?

02:00

The Club

Somewhere out there, there's a whole club of people exactly like you. Time to found it properly.

Pitch the club — cover all four: its name, its motto, its rules (at least two), and what happens at its very first event.
Pitch your club

Give the full pitch, out loud, as if you're recruiting members right now.

02:00
If you dry up: What's the club called, and why that name? What's its motto or slogan? What are the rules for members — what do you have to do, or promise? And what happens at the very first meeting or event — where, what, and what's the highlight?
