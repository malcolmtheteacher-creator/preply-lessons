📌 The Rule

Indirect questions use STATEMENT word order (subject before verb)

Can you tell me where the station IS? (not "is the station")

Do you know what time IT IS? (not "is it")

I wonder where she LIVES. (not "does she live")

After polite phrases like "Can you tell me", "Do you know", "I wonder", use normal statement word order, NOT question inversion.

📚 The Rule (Dual Coding)

🗣️ DIRECT (Inverted)

❓❓

Where IS the station?

What TIME IS IT?

Why DID SHE leave?

Aux + subject (question form)

📝 INDIRECT (Normal order)

💭💭

where THE STATION is

what TIME IT is

why SHE left

Subject + verb (statement form)

💡 Bonus: Start with polite phrases: "Can you tell me...", "Do you know...", "Could you help me...", "I wonder...", "Do you have any idea...", "Could you possibly tell me..."
