Types of Medicine

Herbal medicine Treatment using plants and herbs, alternative to synthetic drugs
Acupuncture Alternative treatment using thin needles inserted into the skin
Yoga Physical and mental practice combining postures, breathing, and meditation
Meditation Mental practice of focusing your mind to achieve relaxation and peace
Conventional medicine Traditional medical treatment using drugs and surgery by doctors
Nutritionist Expert who advises people on diet and healthy eating
Side effects Unwanted effects that happen in addition to the main effect of medicine
Stress relief Methods to reduce mental or emotional tension

Phrases for Arguments For and Against

When discussing alternative medicine, you need to present both sides. Learn these useful phrases:

Arguments In Favour (supporting alternative medicine)

It might help to + verb
"If you're feeling very stressed at work, it might help to do regular exercise."

Can be much more beneficial than
"Learning how to relax can be much more beneficial than taking medication."

Get to the root of the problem
"I think nutritionists often get to the root of the problem and give practical advice."

Arguments Against (criticizing alternative medicine)

A complete waste of time
"A lot of people think herbal products are a complete waste of time."

Only good for + gerund
"Some alternative practitioners are only good for taking your money."

Can be worse than
"The side effects of some drugs can be worse than the illness they treat."

Show Answer
