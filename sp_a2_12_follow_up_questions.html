Today's Goal: Learn 5 types of follow-up questions that keep any conversation flowing naturally.

The 5 Question Types

Good conversationalists ask questions that invite MORE talking. Here are 5 types:

🔍
Details

Who? What? Where? When?

🤔
Reasons

Why? How come?

💭
Feelings

How did you feel?

⭐
Highlights

What was the best/worst?

🔮
Future

Would you...again?

Click to Learn Each Type

1. Detail Questions (Who/What/Where/When)

"Which part...?" / "Who did you go with?" / "When was that?"

Purpose: Get specific information to understand the full picture.

A: "I started a new job."

B: "Oh nice! What company is it?" ← Detail question

More examples: "Where exactly?" / "Who else was there?" / "What kind of...?"

2. Reason Questions (Why/How come)

"Why did you...?" / "How come?" / "What made you...?"

Purpose: Understand motivations and stories behind the facts.

A: "I moved to London last year."

B: "What made you decide to move?" ← Reason question

Tip: "How come?" sounds more casual than "Why?"

3. Feeling Questions

"How did you feel about it?" / "Was it...?" / "Did you enjoy it?"

Purpose: Connect emotionally and show you care about their experience.

A: "I had my driving test yesterday."

B: "Oh! Were you nervous?" ← Feeling question

Variations: "How was it?" / "Was it stressful?" / "Did you have fun?"

4. Highlight Questions (Best/Worst/Favorite)

"What was the best part?" / "What was your favorite...?" / "What was the hardest thing?"

Purpose: Get them to share the most interesting or meaningful part.

A: "I just finished a marathon!"

B: "Wow! What was the hardest part?" ← Highlight question

These work great for: trips, experiences, projects, events

5. Future Questions

"Would you do it again?" / "Are you planning to...?" / "What's next?"

Purpose: Explore their future plans and keep the conversation forward-moving.

A: "I just finished my first painting class."

B: "That's cool! Will you continue with more classes?" ← Future question

More examples: "Do you think you'll...?" / "What are you going to do next?"

Quick Summary

5 Follow-Up Question Types:

  • Details: Who? What? Where? When? Which?
  • Reasons: Why? How come? What made you...?
  • Feelings: How did you feel? Were you...? Did you enjoy...?
  • Highlights: What was the best/worst/hardest/favorite?
  • Future: Would you...again? What's next? Are you planning...?

Real World Practice
