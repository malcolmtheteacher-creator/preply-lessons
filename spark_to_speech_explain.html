2

Grow your topic

One word holds a lot. Answer these out loud — jot a word or two only if it helps. Don't write sentences yet.

In simple terms, what is it?
What is the first thing to understand?
Then what happens — what comes next?
Why does that matter?
What is the result in the end?
What do people usually get wrong about it?
3

Say it

Now pour those ideas into ready-made sentences. Read each one aloud and finish it your way.

Basically, it works like this: …
The first thing to know is …
After that, …, which means …
The reason this matters is …
So, in the end, …
