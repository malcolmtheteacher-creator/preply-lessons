🛑 Stop / Remember (Gerund or Infinitive)

Two forms, two meanings | B2 Level

Let's see what you notice...

Read this sentence. Does it mean the person QUIT or PAUSED?

I stopped to smoke.

What's the difference?

✓ The Ambiguity

"I stopped to smoke" = I paused (in order) to have a cigarette.

"I stopped smoking" = I quit smoking.

📌 The Rule

STOP + -ING = quit the activity (past action)

STOP + TO = pause in order to do something (future reason)

REMEMBER + -ING = recall past action

REMEMBER + TO = don't forget future action

📚 The Rule (Dual Coding)

🔙 -ING (Looking Back)

↩️

stopped SMOKING = quit

remember LOCKING = recall past

The action finished

🔜 TO (Looking Forward)

⏩

stopped TO SMOKE = paused in order to

remember TO LOCK = don't forget future

The action hasn't happened yet

💡 Also applies to: TRY and FORGET. "I tried calling" (past attempt) vs "try to call" (future attempt). "I forgot telling him" (didn't recall) vs "forgot to tell him" (forgot duty).
