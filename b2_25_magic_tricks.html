Modals of Speculation

Modal Verbs for Speculation with Past Events

When speculating about something that already happened:

  • He must have used sleight of hand. (90% sure)
  • She could have hidden the coin. (Possible)
  • It might have been a trick. (Maybe)
  • It may have been coincidence. (Maybe, more formal)
  • They couldn't have done it without practice. (Impossible)

The Bullet Catch Trick

One of the most dangerous tricks in magic history.

The Bullet Catch
A magician stands in front of an audience. A spectator fires a real bullet from a gun. The magician catches the bullet between his teeth. How is this possible?
How might this trick be done? Use modal verbs to speculate. What could the magician have done?

Possible explanations:

  • The magician might have used a duplicate bullet hidden in his mouth beforehand.
  • The gun could have been loaded with a blank cartridge, not a real bullet.
  • The spectator may have been a planted assistant who knew what to do.
  • The magician must have practiced this trick thousands of times.
  • It might have been a combination of sleight of hand and misdirection.

More Famous Tricks

Can you explain how these tricks were performed?

The Levitation Trick
A magician makes a person float in the air, suspended above the ground. The magician waves his hand beneath the person, showing there's nothing supporting them.
How could the magician have done this? Think about hidden supports, angles, lighting, and what the audience can and cannot see.

Most likely explanation: The person could have been supported by a hidden metal frame or pole. The magician's body or positioned objects might have blocked the audience's view. Lighting and angles must have been carefully controlled so shadows didn't reveal the support.

The Disappearing Elephant
A huge elephant stands inside a curtained box on a stage. The curtains close, and when they open again, the elephant has vanished.
How did the elephant disappear? Where could it have gone?

Most likely explanation: The stage must have had a secret trap door or hidden tunnel. The elephant could have been moved during a few seconds when the audience couldn't see. Lighting and mirrors might have been used to create the illusion of an empty space.

The Escape from Chains
A magician is locked inside a wooden box, chains are wrapped around it, and the box is covered with a curtain. After 30 seconds, the magician emerges, but the chains are still locked and the box is still locked.
How could the magician escape? Did he really stay in the box, or could there be another explanation?

Most likely explanation: The magician could have escaped from the box before the curtain was closed. An assistant might have emerged dressed as the magician. Or the box may have had a hidden panel that allowed the magician to slip out. The curtain must have blocked the audience's view of the actual escape.
