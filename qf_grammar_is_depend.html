🎯 It Depends (Not 'Is Depend')

Depend is a verb, not an adjective | B1 Level

Let's see what you notice...

Read this sentence. What feels wrong?

It is depend on your decision.

What's the correction?

✓ Correction

It depends on your decision.

📌 The Rule

Depend is a verb, not an adjective. Say 'it depends' or 'it depends on...' Never 'it is depend'.

Common in spoken English worldwide. Students treat 'depend' like an adjective (similar to 'it is different').

📚 The Rule (Dual Coding)

❌ WRONG

🚫

It is depend on...

Is depend of...

It's depend...

✅ CORRECT

✅

It depends on...

It depends.

That depends on the weather.

Depending on the situation...

💡 Tip: 'Depending on' is useful too: 'Depending on the weather, we might stay home.'
