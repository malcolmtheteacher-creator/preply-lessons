Something to think about

Researchers have linked constant noise with stress and poorer sleep, and many people seek out quiet as a form of rest. Yet silence can feel uncomfortable in a culture used to constant stimulation, and some find that moderate background sound actually helps them concentrate.

Two sides of the same story

Read both, then tell me which argument is stronger and why.

Supporters argue…

Quiet supports rest, health and concentration. Spending time in silence gives space to think, and reducing noise would improve life for many people in cities.

Critics counter…

Silence is not always welcome, and sound can bring comfort, connection and energy. Cities are alive because they are noisy, and one person's noise is another's music.
