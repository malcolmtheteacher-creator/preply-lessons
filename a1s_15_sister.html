Ana arrives 📖

Read it slowly. Say the words out loud.

Big news! Mia's little sister Ana is coming to visit her. Today is the day! 🎉

Mia is at the station. She waits for the train. "Come on, come on!" she says.

The train arrives. The doors open… and there is Ana! "Mia!" "Ana!"

Ana runs to her. She gives Mia a big hug. 🤗

"Welcome!" says Mia. "Give me your suitcase — I can carry it for you."

Ana talks and talks. "Tell me everything! Your café, your friends — I want to see them all!"

At the café, Rosa smiles. "So this is Ana! Come in, come in. Sit with us."

Tom comes too. "Hello, Ana! Mia talks about you every day." Ana laughs. She likes him already. "Good things, I hope!" 😄

Notice: the little words me, her, him, us, them — "Give me your suitcase", "Ana runs to her", "Sit with us". They point at people. Learn the new words next.

The pattern: me, him, her, us, them 🧩

One word does the action. The other word gets the action. Each person-word has two forms:

I → me  ·  Ana hugs me.
he → him  ·  Ana likes him.
she → her  ·  Mia waits for her.
we → us  ·  Rosa says: "Sit with us."
they → them  ·  Ana wants to see them all.
She visits Mia. → She visits her.
I call Tom. → I call him.
Mia shows Ana the town. → Mia shows her the town.
Where does it go? After the verb: "Ana hugs me" ✓ — not "Ana me hugs" ✗. Small word, always after the action.
