〰️ Past Simple vs Past Continuous

Master background actions and interruptions | A2 Level

Let's see what you notice...

Read this sentence. What feels wrong?

I was walking when I was seeing her.

What's the correction?

✓ Correction

I was walking when I saw her.

📌 The Rule

Past Continuous = longer background action (was walking)

Past Simple = shorter action that interrupts it (saw)

When + past simple = the interruption. While + past continuous = the background action.
