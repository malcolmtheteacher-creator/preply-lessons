Home & Shelter

A home is more than just a building. It's a place where we feel safe, express ourselves, and build memories. What makes a house feel like home? Is it the location, the people in it, the memories created there, or the freedom to make it your own?

Let's talk about your living situation:

• Where do you currently live? (City, country, type of home)

• What do you like about your current home?

• What's the biggest challenge living there?

• How long have you lived there?

• Have you lived in many different places? How did it feel to move?

Home & Housing Vocabulary

Detached A house not connected to any other buildings
Spacious Large and having plenty of room
Renovation Restoring and improving an old house or building
Mortgage A long-term loan to buy property; you pay the bank monthly
Neighbourhood An area of a city or town with its own community
Commute Regular journey to work or school; how far you travel
Furnished A place that comes with furniture already included
En-suite A bathroom attached directly to a bedroom

Rooms & Features

Common rooms: Living room, bedroom, kitchen, bathroom, dining room, study, garden, garage, basement, attic.

Features: Central heating, air conditioning, Wi-Fi, outdoor space, balcony, terrace, pool, parking, security system, modern appliances.

Describing locations: Suburban, rural, city center, downtown, near the coast, in the mountains, quiet area, busy area, close to shops, near public transport.

Reflection Questions

1. Would you prefer to rent or own a home? Why?

Consider flexibility, security, building equity, and commitment.

2. How important is proximity to work, family, and friends when choosing a home?

Think about your personal priorities and life stage.

3. Would you ever live in a tiny home or unconventional space?

Consider minimalism, sustainability, and lifestyle preferences.

4. How does your culture influence home design and family living spaces?

Different cultures prioritize different aspects of home and family spaces.
