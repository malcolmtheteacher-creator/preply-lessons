The Simple Phone Comeback

Read the article about how simple phones are popular again. Then answer the questions.

Forget it, Apple. Move over, Samsung. Not everyone wants your hi-tech phones anymore.

A surprising new trend is appearing in the market. More and more people are turning their backs on smartphones. They are saying goodbye to constant messages, notifications, and endless scrolling. They want something simpler.

Simple phones are making a comeback. These basic devices can only call and text. They have no internet, no apps, no constant notifications. Just calls and messages. That is all.

Why is this happening? People say their smartphones cause stress. The constant connection is exhausting. They want to turn off the noise.

The numbers tell an interesting story. Sales of simple phones have gone up by 5% this year. More people are choosing to disconnect from the smartphone world.

"I feel like I can breathe again," says one customer. "My simple phone just lets me talk to the people I love."

Title Matching Exercise

Which title is best for this article?

A) Apple and Samsung Make New Phones
Not the best choice. The article is not about Apple or Samsung making new phones. It is about people choosing simple phones instead of hi-tech phones. The article criticizes these companies, not discusses their new products.
B) People Are Buying Simple Phones Because They Want Less Stress
This is the best choice! The whole article is about why people are choosing simple phones. The article explains that smartphones cause stress and people want to disconnect. The numbers show sales are going up.
C) Simple Phones Have No Internet
Not the best choice. This is true, but it is too small a detail. It is only one sentence of the article. The main idea is about why people want these phones and the trend of simple phones making a comeback, not just what features they do or do not have.

Reading Tips

  • Look for words in the title that appear in the text. In the correct answer "People," "Buying," "Simple Phones," and "Stress" all appear in the article.
  • Focus on the first and last sentences. Often they tell you the main idea.
  • Identify when a title is too small. If it only covers one small part of the article, it is probably not the best title.

Phones Past and Present

Compare old phones with modern phones. Use comparative adjectives and the present perfect tense.

Comparisons

Use these comparatives to talk about differences:

  • Old phones were bigger than modern phones.
  • Modern phones are more expensive than simple phones.
  • Old phone batteries were smaller.
  • Modern phones are more complicated.

Old Mobile Phones (1990s)

Size: Very large and heavy

Features: Only calls and texts

Price: Very expensive

Battery: Small, short life

Screen: No screen or very small

Modern Smartphones (2020s)

Size: Small and light

Features: Apps, internet, camera, GPS

Price: Can be very expensive

Battery: Larger, longer life

Screen: Large, colorful, touchscreen

Changes Over Time – Present Perfect

Use "have / has + past participle" to talk about changes:

  • Phones have become smaller over time.
  • Technology has changed a lot.
  • Features have increased dramatically.
  • Prices have gone up.
  • Battery life has improved.
