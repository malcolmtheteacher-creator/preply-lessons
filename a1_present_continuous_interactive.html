🎬 What Are You Doing?

Talk about what's happening RIGHT NOW!

📚 Present Continuous: RIGHT NOW!

When you want to talk about what is happening at this exact moment, use the Present Continuous. It's like describing a video! 🎥

⏰

RIGHT NOW

Present Continuous

"I am watching Netflix."

"She is eating lunch."

= happening at this moment!

🔄

HABITS / ROUTINES

Present Simple

"I watch Netflix every day."

"She eats lunch at 12."

= happens regularly

🎯 The Formula

Subject + BE + verb-ING

I am playing video games.

You are reading this lesson.

He is sleeping.

📝 The Verb "BE" - Choose the Right One!

Subject BE Short Form Example
I am I'm I'm studying
You / We / They are You're / We're / They're You're learning
He / She / It is He's / She's / It's She's cooking

💡 When to Use Present Continuous

✅ Actions happening NOW: "I'm talking to you!"

✅ Temporary situations: "I'm staying at my grandma's house this week."

✅ Describing pictures/videos: "In this photo, my dog is sleeping."

✍️ Spelling Rules for -ING

Most Verbs

Just add -ING

play → playing
watch → watching
eat → eating

Verbs ending in -E

Remove E, add -ING

make → making
write → writing
dance → dancing

Short verbs (CVC)

Double the last letter

run → running
sit → sitting
swim → swimming

Verbs ending in -IE

Change IE to Y, add -ING

lie → lying
die → dying

💡 Short Answers

Are you...? → Yes, I am. / No, I'm not.

Is he/she...? → Yes, he/she is. / No, he/she isn't.

Are they...? → Yes, they are. / No, they aren't.

🗣️ Describe What's Happening!

⏰ Right Now in Your Life

Tell your teacher what's happening RIGHT NOW:

  • What are you doing? (I'm having an English lesson!)
  • Where are you sitting?
  • What is the weather doing outside?
  • Is anyone else in the room with you? What are they doing?
  • What are you wearing?

👨‍👩‍👧‍👦 Your Family Right Now

Imagine what your family is doing at this moment:

  • What is your mom/dad doing?
  • Are your siblings at home? What are they doing?
  • Is anyone working? Cooking? Watching TV?
  • What is your pet doing? (if you have one!)

🌍 Around the World

Think about different places! What's happening there now?

  • It's morning in New York. What are people doing?
  • It's night in Tokyo. What are people doing?
  • What are students in your school doing right now?
  • What are your friends doing?

🌟 Integration: Present Simple vs Continuous!

Compare what you DO (habits) with what you're DOING (now):

"I usually watch Netflix after school, but right now I'm studying English!"

"My dad works at a hospital. Right now, he's seeing patients."

🏆 Final Challenges

🎯 Challenge 1: Describe a Photo

Think of a photo on your phone. Describe what's happening in it!

Example:

"In this photo, I'm sitting on the beach. My sister is swimming in the sea. My parents are eating ice cream. The sun is shining!"

🎯 Challenge 2: Compare NOW vs USUALLY

Use Present Simple AND Present Continuous together!

Example:

"I usually play video games at 7pm, but tonight I'm studying for a test."

"My mom usually cooks dinner, but today my dad is cooking!"

🌟 SUPER CHALLENGE: Live Commentary!

Describe what YOU are doing, step by step, like a sports commentator!

"OK, so right now I'm sitting at my desk. I'm looking at my computer screen. I'm talking to my English teacher. I'm thinking about what to say next. Now I'm smiling because this is fun! I'm learning a lot today!"
