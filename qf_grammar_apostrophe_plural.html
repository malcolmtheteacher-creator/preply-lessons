✏️ Apostrophes: Plural vs. Possessive

Don't add 's just to make something plural | B1 Level

Let's see what you notice...

Read this sentence. What feels wrong?

Several large company's have moved their operations abroad.

What's the correction and why?

✓ Correction

Several large companies have moved their operations abroad.

📌 The Rule

Apostrophe + s = possession — something belongs to something else

No apostrophe = simply more than one (a plural)

Ask yourself: does something belong to someone here? If not — no apostrophe needed.

📚 The Rule (Dual Coding)

🔑 POSSESSIVE (use apostrophe)

📎

the government's policy

a nation's identity

the company's profits

a worker's rights

→ "the policy OF the government"
(something belongs to something)

🔢 PLURAL (no apostrophe)

📋

three governments

several nations

large companies

many problems

→ more than one
(no ownership involved)

💡 Quick test: Can you rephrase with "of the ___"? (the policy of the government) → you need an apostrophe. Just counting more than one? → no apostrophe.

Practice: Write the correct form

1. Many are struggling with rising debt. (more than one country)

2. The new tax policy has been widely debated. (the policy belongs to the government)

3. Several declined to comment on the report. (more than one company)

4. The profits were higher than expected this quarter. (the profits belong to the company)
