Giving Compliments

Match these compliments with appropriate responses:

Compliment:

Is that jacket new?

Possible responses: "Yes, I bought it last week!" / "Thanks, I'm glad you like it!"

Compliment:

Nice trainers!

Possible responses: "Thanks! I just got them." / "Thanks, they're really comfortable."

Compliment:

You look very smart.

Possible responses: "Thank you!" / "Thanks for the compliment."

Phrases for Giving Advice

Common structures for giving clothing advice:

Complete the phrase:

If I w___ you, I would wear something red.

Answer: were — "If I were you, I would wear something red."

Complete the phrase:

Why d____ you wear jeans with a nice shirt?

Answer: on't — "Why don't you wear jeans with a nice shirt?"

Complete the phrase:

How a____ wearing high-heeled shoes?

Answer: bout — "How about wearing high-heeled shoes?"

Complete the phrase:

A g_____ thing to wear would be a warm jacket.

Answer: ood — "A good thing to wear would be a warm jacket."

Complete the phrase:

You s____ wear a long skirt or trousers.

Answer: hould — "You should wear a long skirt or trousers."

Complete the phrase:

You m_____ want to wear comfortable shoes.

Answer: ay — "You may want to wear comfortable shoes."

Giving Advice: Speaking Task

You have 5 minutes to speak. Your task is to choose one of the scenarios below and give advice on what to wear. Use the language you've learned and explain your reasoning.

Time remaining: 5:00

Scenario 1: Job Interview

Your friend has an important job interview tomorrow. What clothes and accessories would you recommend? Why?

Scenario 2: First Day at a New Job

Someone is starting a new job in an office you know well. What should they wear on their first day? Explain your reasoning.

Scenario 3: Casual Wedding

You're attending a casual wedding in the summer. What outfit would you recommend? Include accessories.

Scenario 4: Important Presentation

You're giving a presentation to senior managers at your company. What would you wear and why?

Speaking Tips

When giving advice about clothes, remember to:

  • Use the advice structures you learned (If I were you... / You should... / How about...)
  • Give specific reasons for your suggestions
  • Consider the context and situation
  • Speak clearly and naturally

Discussion Questions

Think about these questions and prepare your thoughts:

1. Money & Fashion

Do you spend a lot of money on clothes? Why or why not? Do you think clothes are a waste of money?

2. Decision Making

How long does it take you to decide which clothes to wear? Are you someone who plans your outfit or do you choose spontaneously?

3. Sustainable Fashion

Do you buy second-hand clothes? What are the advantages and disadvantages of buying used items?

4. Footwear

How many pairs of shoes do you need or want? What factors influence your shoe purchases?

5. Professional Dress

In your job or field, is there a particular dress code? How does it affect the way you feel about work?

6. Cultural Differences

How do clothing choices differ between cultures or countries? What surprised you about fashion in other places?
