Describing Experiences

Making your stories come alive

🎨 Description

Good vs. Great?

When someone asks about your weekend, your holiday, or a restaurant, do you always say the same thing?

🤔 "How was your holiday?"

❌ "It was good."

❌ "It was nice."

❌ "It was fun."

These words tell us NOTHING! Good how? Nice in what way?

The Problem

Many learners use the same 5 words for EVERY experience: good, nice, fun, interesting, beautiful.

✓ Compare these descriptions:

"The food was good."

"The food was absolutely delicious. The pasta was so fresh, and the sauce was incredibly rich."

Today's Goal: Learn powerful adjectives and intensifiers to describe experiences vividly, making your English more engaging and specific.

Part 2: Better Adjectives

Click each category to reveal vivid alternatives:

Instead of "GOOD" 👍

amazing • fantastic • wonderful • brilliant • excellent

Usage examples:

• "The concert was amazing!" (exciting, impressive)

• "We had a fantastic time." (extremely good)

• "The weather was brilliant." (British English: very good)

With intensifiers: "It was absolutely amazing!" / "really fantastic"

Instead of "BAD" 👎

terrible • awful • disappointing • exhausting • stressful

Usage examples:

• "The film was terrible." (very bad)

• "The traffic was awful." (extremely unpleasant)

• "The meal was disappointing." (not as good as expected)

• "The journey was exhausting." (made me very tired)

Instead of "INTERESTING" 🤔

fascinating • captivating • thought-provoking • eye-opening

Usage examples:

• "The museum was fascinating." (extremely interesting)

• "The documentary was eye-opening." (taught me something new)

• "The book was captivating." (held my attention completely)

Instead of "BEAUTIFUL" ✨

stunning • breathtaking • gorgeous • picturesque • spectacular

Usage examples:

• "The view was breathtaking." (so beautiful it surprised you)

• "The beach was stunning." (extremely beautiful)

• "The village was picturesque." (like a picture, charming)

Note: "Absolutely stunning" ✓ but NOT "very stunning" ✗

Instead of "FUN" 🎉

exciting • thrilling • hilarious • entertaining • memorable

Usage examples:

• "The rollercoaster was thrilling!" (exciting, a bit scary)

• "The comedian was hilarious!" (very funny)

• "It was such a memorable trip." (I'll never forget it)

Guided Practice

Upgrade these boring descriptions! Say your improved version aloud.

Original: "My holiday was good."

Upgrade with: intensifier + powerful adjective + specific detail

Example: "My holiday was absolutely wonderful. The beaches were stunning and the food was incredible."

Original: "The movie was bad."

Upgrade using: terrible / awful / disappointing + explain why

Example: "The movie was really disappointing. The plot was confusing and the acting was terrible."

Original: "The city was nice."

Upgrade with specific adjectives for different aspects:

Architecture? Food? Atmosphere? People? → "The architecture was stunning, the food was delicious, and the atmosphere was so vibrant."

Original: "The party was fun."

Be more specific: What made it fun?

Example: "The party was absolutely brilliant! The music was fantastic and everyone was so friendly. It was really memorable."
