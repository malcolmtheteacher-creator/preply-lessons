The Problem

🤔 "How was your holiday?"

❌ "It was good."

❌ "It was nice."

❌ "It was fun."

These words tell us NOTHING! Good how? Nice in what way?

✓ Compare these descriptions:

"The food was good."

"The food was really delicious. The pasta was so fresh, and the sauce was incredibly tasty."

Today's Goal: Learn powerful adjectives and intensifiers to describe experiences more vividly.

Part 2: Better Adjectives

Stop using "good", "nice", and "fun"! Use these instead:

Instead of... Use these Example
👍 "good" amazing, fantastic, wonderful, brilliant, excellent "The concert was amazing!"
👎 "bad" terrible, awful, disappointing, exhausting, stressful "The film was terrible."
🤔 "interesting" fascinating, surprising, eye-opening, unusual "The museum was fascinating."
✨ "beautiful" stunning, gorgeous, lovely, spectacular "The view was stunning."
🎉 "fun" exciting, thrilling, hilarious, entertaining, memorable "The show was hilarious!"

Elaborate: Extended Descriptions

Now combine everything into longer, richer descriptions. Use the formula:

📐 The Description Formula

Overall feeling + Specific aspect 1 + Specific aspect 2 + Comparison or Effect

Describe your best holiday ever (4+ sentences):

Use the formula: Overall + Place + Food + Activity + Feeling

My best holiday was ___. The ___ was ___. The food was ___. We also ___, which was ___.
Word bank:
absolutely amazingreally wonderfulbeachcitymountainsstunninggorgeousdeliciousfantasticwent hikingvisited a marketwent swimmingexcitingmemorablethrilling
📝 Model answer

"My best holiday was absolutely amazing. The beach was stunning — the water was so clear and warm. The food was really delicious, especially the fresh fish. We also went hiking in the mountains, which was thrilling. It was the most memorable trip I've ever had."

Describe a disappointing experience (4+ sentences):

Use the formula: Overall + What happened + How you felt + Effect

Word bank:
really disappointingquite awfulterribleexhaustingstressfulconfusingboringfrustratedAs a resultsoBecause of this
📝 Model answer

"Last month I went to a new restaurant and it was really disappointing. The service was incredibly slow and the waiter was quite rude. The food was terrible — it was cold and tasteless. As a result, I won't go back there again."

Compare TWO experiences (use L7 comparison structures):

Describe two restaurants, two holidays, or two films — one good, one bad.

Comparison connectors (from L7):
[A] is more...than [B][A] is not as...as [B][A] is different from [B]buthowever
Description vocabulary:
amazingterribledeliciousdisappointingstunningboringfantasticawful
📝 Model answer

"The Italian restaurant was absolutely fantastic — the pasta was delicious and the atmosphere was really lovely. However, the Chinese restaurant was quite disappointing. The food was not as fresh as the Italian place, and the service was much slower."

4. Why can't you say "very amazing"?

"Amazing" is already an extreme adjective. Use "absolutely amazing" ✓ not "very amazing" ✗. "Very" goes with normal adjectives (very good, very bad).
