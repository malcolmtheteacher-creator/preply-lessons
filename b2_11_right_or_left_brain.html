Weak Forms and Connected Speech

In fluent English, some words are pronounced weakly. Notice how these words change in natural speech.

Unstressed Words

Listen to your speaking partner and notice the weak forms. The underlined words are often very quiet and quick:

EXAMPLE 1
It means you produce more of a stored concept of the object.
The vowel sounds become /ə/ (schwa sound). "Concept" is faster and higher-pitched in natural speech.
EXAMPLE 2
I hadn"t tried anything like this before, and it was initially quite difficult to do.
"Initially" becomes less stressed. The "ly" ending is barely pronounced.
EXAMPLE 3
I was particularly pleased with the face, as I usually find faces extremely hard to draw.
"I" is pronounced as /ə/ (like the schwa in "about"). It"s almost inaudible.

The Personality Quiz

Ask your partner these questions and take notes. Then use the key at the bottom to decide if they are a right or left-sided thinker.

1. Are you a right or left-sided thinker? (What"s your guess?)

Note: ______________________

Initial guess indicates what they already think about themselves

2. When you study, do you prefer total silence or background music/noise?

Note: ______________________

Background music = Left-sided thinker

3. If someone brings you a problem, are you more likely to empathise or suggest solutions?

Note: ______________________

Empathise = Right-sided thinker

4. How easily do you lose track of time?

Note: ______________________

Loses track easily = Right-sided thinker

5. Are you more likely to say, "I can"t put a name to a face" or "I can"t put a face to a name"?

Note: ______________________

"Face to a name" = Right-sided thinker

6. Do you tend to use hand gestures when you talk?

Note: ______________________

Uses gestures = Right-sided thinker

7. Which do you trust more: your gut instinct or logic?

Note: ______________________

Logic = Left-sided thinker

8. Is your living space generally neat or cluttered?

Note: ______________________

Neat = Left-sided thinker

9. Do you prefer looking at a map or reading written directions?

Note: ______________________

Map = Right-sided thinker

Analysis

After the quiz, count which side scored higher. Which side describes your partner better? Did your initial guess match the results?

Interview Your Partner

Use the quiz questions to interview your partner. Ask naturally, listen carefully, and take notes on their answers.

What to do:

  • Ask each quiz question naturally, as part of a conversation
  • Make notes of their answers
  • After the quiz, tell them which side of the brain seems dominant
  • Discuss whether the result matches their personality
