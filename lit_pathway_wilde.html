Meet the writer · B1

Oscar Wilde

1854-1900 · Ireland

An Irish writer famous for his wit, his clothes, his comedies and his fairy tales.

Which of these books did Oscar Wilde write?

A life in a few steps

  1. 1854Born in Dublin.
  2. 1874Studies at Oxford, where he is a star student.
  3. 1888Publishes The Happy Prince and Other Tales, fairy tales for children and adults.
  4. 1895The Importance of Being Earnest is a hit. The same year he is sent to prison for two years, because loving men was a crime in Britain then.
  5. 1900Dies in Paris, aged 46, poor and far from home.

Why people still read Wilde

His sentences are short, funny and clever, and people still quote them every day. His fairy tales look simple, but they are about love, kindness and selfishness.
