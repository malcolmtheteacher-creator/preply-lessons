Understanding Cause and Effect

In academic writing, you often explain why something happened and what resulted from it. These connections are crucial for building arguments.

Key Concepts

  • Cause: Why something happened; the reason or trigger
  • Effect: What happened as a result; the consequence or outcome

Example

Cause: "Rising temperatures in the Arctic"
Effect: "Ice sheets are melting at unprecedented rates"
Connection: "Because rising temperatures in the Arctic are warming the ice, ice sheets are melting at unprecedented rates."

Cause and Effect Connector Words and Phrases

Because / Since / As

Introduce the cause first. "Because the economy weakened, unemployment increased."

As a result / Consequently / Therefore

Connect cause to effect. "The budget was cut. As a result, services declined."

Due to / On account of

Show the reason. "Due to climate change, weather patterns are shifting."

Lead to / Cause / Result in

Show the outcome. "Poor nutrition leads to health problems."

As a consequence / In consequence

Formal cause-effect connections. "Inflation rose. In consequence, purchasing power declined."

The reason why / The cause of

Direct statements of cause. "The reason why graduation rates improved was increased funding."

Identifying and Expressing Cause-Effect

For each scenario, identify the cause and effect, then write a sentence connecting them.

Scenario 1

A student attended all classes and studied regularly. Her test scores improved significantly.

Cause: Effect:

Write a sentence using "As a result" or "Due to":

Cause: Attending all classes and studying regularly Effect: Improved test scores Sentence: "Due to her consistent attendance and regular study habits, her test scores improved significantly."

Scenario 2

Social media platforms have made information spread quickly. Misinformation also spreads rapidly.

Cause: Effect:

Write using "Lead to" or "Result in":

Cause: Quick information spread on social media Effect: Rapid misinformation spread Sentence: "The rapid spread of information on social media can lead to the equally quick distribution of misinformation."
