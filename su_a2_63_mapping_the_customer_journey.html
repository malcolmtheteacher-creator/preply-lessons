Understanding the Customer Journey

A customer journey has different stages. Click on each stage to see what happens.

📢 Awareness

This is when the customer first knows about your product. They might see it on social media, hear about it from a friend, or find it in an advert. This is the first touchpoint.

🔍 Consideration

The customer now knows about your product. They think about it. They may look at other products to compare. They read reviews and ask questions. They are deciding if they want to buy.

🛒 Purchase

The customer buys your product. They complete the transaction. This is a crucial moment - the customer is making a decision. We call this a moment of truth. The buying experience must be easy and smooth.

✅ Usage

The customer uses the product. They experience if it works well, if it is good quality, if it does what they expected. This is another important moment of truth.

⭐ Loyalty

If the customer is happy, they may buy again. They may recommend it to friends. They stay loyal to your company. If they are not happy, they may complain or tell others about the bad experience.

The Three Es: Making the Journey Better

When we plan a customer journey, we think about three important things:

😊 Emotion

How does the customer feel?

Are they happy? Do they trust us? Are they excited?

🚀 Ease

Is it easy for the customer?

Can they find what they want? Is the process simple? Is it fast?

💰 Effectiveness

Does it work well?

Do we save time? Do we save money? Do we solve the problem?

Process Mapping Helps Us

Why is process mapping important?

Process mapping helps because it:

  • Shows us exactly what happens at each step
  • Helps us see where customers have problems
  • Helps us find opportunities for improvement
  • Gives us a tool to train new staff
  • Helps us understand the whole customer experience
