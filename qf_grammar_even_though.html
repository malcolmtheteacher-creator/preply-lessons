🎯 Even Though vs Even If

Real contrast vs hypothetical | B2 Level

Let's see what you notice...

Read this sentence. What feels wrong?

Even if she was tired, she finished the work. (= real fact)

What's the correction?

✓ Correction

Even though she was tired, she finished the work.

📌 The Rule

Even though = despite the fact (real, true). Even if = hypothetical, whether or not.

Many students use them interchangeably. 'Even though' = fact. 'Even if' = condition/hypothetical.

📚 The Rule (Dual Coding)

EVEN THOUGH (= real fact)

✓

Even though it rained, we went out

Even though she's busy, she helps

Even though I'm tired (I AM tired)

EVEN IF (= hypothetical)

❓

Even if it rains, we'll go out

Even if she offers, I'll say no

Even if I were rich (hypothetical)

💡 Tip: 'Although' = 'even though' (slightly less emphatic). 'Even if' can combine with all conditionals.

Practice

1. _____ he's rich, he lives simply. (= he IS rich — fact)

2. _____ it rains tomorrow, we'll go. (= it might rain)

3. _____ she was exhausted, she kept working. (= she WAS exhausted)

4. I wouldn't go _____ you paid me! (= hypothetical)
