Train Comfortable; good leg room; stations near city centres; WiFi available; overnight options Takes longer (7 hours); overnight cabins more expensive

Language for Making Comparisons

Study these phrases grouped by type:

Superlative Adjectives

Use when comparing one thing to all others in a group:

Examples:

  • The plane is by far the fastest way to travel.
  • The coach is the cheapest option.
  • The train is the most comfortable way.
  • Flying is the most expensive method.

Comparative Adjectives

Use when comparing two things:

Examples:

  • The train is much faster than the coach.
  • Flying is much more frequent than buses.
  • The coach is a cheaper option than flying.
  • The train journey is much shorter than driving.

Other Comparison Phrases

Additional useful expressions:

Examples:

  • The good thing about the train is the comfort.
  • Another option is to take a coach.
  • The negative thing about flying is the cost.
  • The only other way to travel is by car.
  • The train is only just cheaper than flying.

Practice

Fill in the blanks with comparison phrases:

1. The plane is _______ (by far / much) the fastest option.

Answer: by far

2. Trains are _______ (much more / a lot more) comfortable than buses.

Answer: much more

3. _______ (The good thing / The best thing) about the coach is the price.

Answer: The good thing (or The best thing)

Travel Planning: Speaking Task

You have 5 minutes to plan a travel article comparing ways to get between two cities. Choose a journey you know well and compare at least three transport methods.

Time remaining: 5:00

What to Include:

Introduction:

Introduce the journey and the three transport methods you're comparing.

Compare Each Transport Method:

  • Time: How long does it take?
  • Cost: Is it expensive or cheap?
  • Frequency: How often do they run?
  • Comfort: Is it comfortable?
  • Other advantages/disadvantages: What else makes it good or bad?

Conclusion & Recommendation:

Which transport method would you recommend and why?

Suggested Journey Pairs:

  • Two cities in your country
  • Your home town and the nearest major city
  • A journey you make regularly
  • Two cities you'd like to visit

Discussion Questions

Reflect on these travel-related questions:

1. Personal Preferences

What's your favourite way to travel long distances? Why do you prefer it?

2. Public Transportation

Do you often use public transportation? How important is it in your daily life?

3. Missed Connections

Have you ever missed your last train or bus home? If so, how did you get home that night?

4. Parking Challenges

Is parking a problem in your country or city? How does this affect where people choose to travel?

5. Government Responsibility

Do you think governments should encourage public transportation more? What would be the benefits?

6. Travel Stories

Tell about an interesting or memorable journey you've taken. What made it special?

7. Future Transport

What new forms of transport do you think will exist in the future? How might they change travel?
