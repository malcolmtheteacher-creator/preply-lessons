Pronunciation & Practice

Learn how to use contractions naturally in speech.

Full form: "We are going to"

Contracted: "We're going to" or "gonna" /ˈɡənə/

Full form: "We will"

Contracted: "We'll" /wiːl/

Example Sentences

We're going to take a flight.
We will go by bus!
We're going to have a great time.
We will visit many places.
Practice: Say these sentences using contractions. Listen to how natural they sound.

Plan Your Round-the-World Trip

You have saved a lot of money and have 2 months off. Plan your trip!

Remember: Future Forms

Possible Plan (might, probably, hope to)

"We might go hiking." / "We'll probably take a train."

Solid Plan (going to, will, plan to)

"We're going to visit temples." / "We'll stay in hostels."

Spontaneous Plan (will)

"We will find great restaurants!" / "We'll explore the local markets."

Speaking Task — Present Your Trip (3 minutes)

3:00

After your presentation, discuss:
