Learning Experiences 📚

Level: B1-B2 | Duration: 50 minutes

Warm-up

Think about these questions:

  • What is something you learned as an adult that you wish you had learned as a child?
  • What was the hardest thing you ever learned to do?
  • Are you learning anything new right now?

Vocabulary - Learning & Skills

Activity A: Match Learning Expressions

Match the expressions (1-10) with their meanings (a-j):

Expressions:

1. pick something up
2. get the hang of it
3. trial and error
4. steep learning curve
5. practice makes perfect
6. learn the ropes
7. it's second nature
8. master a skill
9. self-taught
10. hands-on experience		 Meanings:

a. learn by doing, not just reading
b. become very skilled at something
c. start to understand how to do something
d. something very difficult to learn
e. learn informally or easily
f. so automatic you don't think about it
g. the more you do it, the better you get
h. learn by trying different ways
i. learned without a teacher
j. learn the basic procedures

Answers:

1-e (pick something up = learn informally or easily) 2-c (get the hang of it = start to understand how to do something)
3-h (trial and error = learn by trying different ways) 4-d (steep learning curve = something very difficult to learn)
5-g (practice makes perfect = the more you do it, the better you get) 6-j (learn the ropes = learn the basic procedures)
7-f (it's second nature = so automatic you don't think about it) 8-b (master a skill = become very skilled at something)
9-i (self-taught = learned without a teacher) 10-a (hands-on experience = learn by doing, not just reading)

4. Now that I've been driving for 10 years, it's _____________ to me.

Grammar - Past Perfect & Used to

Past Perfect - for actions that happened before another past action:

  • By the time I was 10, I had already learned to swim.
  • I had never driven a car before I moved to the countryside.
  • Had you studied English before you came here?

Used to - for past habits and states that are no longer true:

  • I used to be afraid of speaking English in public.
  • My teacher used to give us homework every day.
  • I didn't use to practice regularly, but now I do.

Grammar Practice

Complete with past perfect or used to:

1. Before I learned to ski, I _____________ (never/be) to the mountains.

2. I _____________ (think) learning English was impossible, but now I speak well!

3. When I started my first job, I _____________ (not/use) a computer before.

4. My grandfather _____________ (tell) me that practice is the most important thing.

5. By the time I was 20, I _____________ (already/learn) three languages.

Speaking Practice

Discussion: Your Learning Journey

Discuss these questions:

Memorable Learning Experiences:

  • What is something you learned that changed your life?
  • Tell me about a time when learning something was really frustrating. What happened?
  • What is the most useful skill you have learned? How did you learn it?
  • Have you ever given up learning something? Why?

Learning Methods:

  • Do you prefer learning from books, videos, or hands-on practice? Why?
  • Are you the kind of person who learns by trial and error, or do you prefer following instructions?
  • Do you learn better alone or with other people?
  • What is your learning style? (visual, auditory, kinesthetic)

Challenges & Success:

  • What makes learning difficult for you?
  • How do you stay motivated when learning something new?
  • What advice would you give someone learning something you already know how to do?
  • Is there anything you would like to learn in the future?

Tell Your Story

Choose ONE of these learning experiences and tell the story in detail:

  • Learning to drive
  • Learning to swim
  • Learning to ride a bike
  • Learning a musical instrument
  • Learning a new language
  • Learning to use new technology
  • Learning a sport or physical skill
  • Learning something for work

Include:

  • When and why did you start learning it?
  • Who taught you? Or were you self-taught?
  • What was the hardest part?
  • How long did it take to get the hang of it?
  • Did you make any funny or embarrassing mistakes?
  • How do you feel about this skill now?

Compare: Then vs Now

Think about learning English. Discuss these comparisons:

When you started learning English:
- What did you use to find difficult?
- What mistakes did you use to make?
- How did you use to practice?
- What were you afraid of?		 Now:
- What is still challenging?
- What have you mastered?
- How do you practice now?
- What are you confident about?

Quick Debate

Topic: "It's too late to learn new skills after the age of 30."

Do you agree or disagree? Discuss:

  • Is it harder to learn when you're older? Why or why not?
  • What are the advantages of learning as an adult?
  • Can you think of examples of people who learned important skills later in life?
  • What role does motivation play in adult learning?

Wrap-up

  • What is one learning expression you will remember from today?
  • What is something you used to find difficult but now feels like second nature?
  • Is there something you want to start learning after today's discussion?

Homework: Write about an important learning experience in your life (150 words). Use past perfect and "used to" at least twice each!
