Around the World: Stereotype vs Reality

Click a region of the map. Every lesson starts with a common stereotype — then finds out what is really true, why the idea exists, and how to get on well with people there.

Each region is a short series: a Background lesson first, then lessons on culture and cultural differences. Every lesson follows the same five steps as the story lessons — key words, a reading in three parts, one grammar point, going deeper, and timed speaking — and ends with practical cross-cultural tips for today.

Stereotypes are handled carefully: we ask where they come from, what the evidence says, and how to talk about differences respectfully — about tendencies, never about every person.
