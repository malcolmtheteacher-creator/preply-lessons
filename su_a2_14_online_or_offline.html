Online learning has changed how people study around the world. Some people love it. Others prefer traditional classroom learning. Many enjoy a mixture of both.

Let's explore the language we use to talk about our likes, dislikes, and feelings about different learning styles.

How to Express Likes & Dislikes

Useful Phrases

  • I like something
  • I hate something
  • I find it difficult to do something
  • I find it easy to do something
  • I love something
  • I feel something (e.g., I feel motivated)
  • I enjoy something
  • I don't mind something

Let's Explore Further

Question 1: What other adjectives can we use instead of "difficult" or "easy"?
Impossible, challenging, enjoyable, useful, interesting, boring, expensive, overwhelming, frustrating, rewarding, stressful
Question 2: How can we say "I find it difficult" differently?
"It's difficult for me" / "I think it's difficult" / "I struggle with it" / "It's a challenge for me"
Question 3: When we say "I don't mind," is that good or bad?
It's positive! When we say "I don't mind," it means it's not bad for us. It's neutral to positive — we're okay with it.
