The Challenge

🎯 Personality Assessment Challenge

Step 1: Choose 6-8 adjectives that best describe YOUR personality.

Step 2: Based on your first impressions of me, choose 6-8 adjectives you think describe MY personality. Think about why you've formed this impression.

Step 3: Tell me what adjectives you chose for me - and why. I'll react and tell you if you're right!

While you're thinking...

  • Do you find it easy or difficult to describe yourself?
  • Are there adjectives you WANT to be true about you, but aren't sure if they are?
  • Do other people see you the same way you see yourself?

Language Focus

Next lesson, we're going to discuss books that changed the world. To talk about the impact of these books, we need some useful structures.

Unreal Conditional Forms

"If it weren't for..."
"If it weren't for the existence of books, our everyday lives would be changed beyond recognition."
= If books didn't exist...
"If it hadn't been for..."
"If it hadn't been for the seminal works of Shakespeare, would literature have developed in the same way?"
= If Shakespeare hadn't written his seminal works...

Complete with your own ideas:

1. If it hadn't been for Martin Luther King,
2. If it weren't for penicillin,
3. We would know much less about genetics if it
4. The town of Guernica wouldn't be so well-known if it
5. If it hadn't been for the invention of the printing press,
6. Life in the twenty-first century would be very different if it

Now create your own:

Think of something or someone that changed the world - or your life. Use "If it weren't for..." or "If it hadn't been for..."

Next time, we'll discuss some of the most influential books in history and debate which ones had the biggest impact. You'll use the structures we just practised to talk about their legacy.

Some of the books we'll discuss:

1984
George Orwell (1949)
The dystopian novel that gave us "Big Brother" and "thought crime".

Charles Darwin (1859)
The book that changed how we understand life itself.
The Communist Manifesto
Karl Marx (1848)
"Workers of the world, unite!" A book that sparked revolutions.
Silent Spring
Rachel Carson (1962)
The book that started the environmental movement.

Questions to consider:

  • What's the book's legacy?
  • Is the book still relevant today?
  • How would the world be different if it hadn't been for this book?
