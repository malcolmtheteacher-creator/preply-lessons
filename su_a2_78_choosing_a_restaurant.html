What Makes a Good Restaurant?

When choosing a restaurant, different things are important to different people. What matters most to you?

Rate These Factors

Put these in order of importance for you (1 = most important, 5 = least important):

  • The atmosphere and environment
  • The quality of food
  • The quality of service
  • The size of portions
  • The price

Think about this:

Talk about one of these factors. Why is it important to you? Have you had a bad experience in a restaurant because of this factor?

Linking Words for Describing Restaurants

When we describe restaurants, we often need to show connections between ideas. Here are four important linking words:

So Shows the result or consequence of something
Because Gives the reason why something happens
But / Although Shows contrast between two ideas (something positive with something negative)
And... too Adds more information; gives additional details

Examples

  • So: The food was delicious and the service was professional, so we will definitely come again.
  • Because: I don't think we will go here again because it wasn't lively enough for us.
  • But: The staff were helpful, but not that fast.
  • Although: Although the service was slow, the portions were huge.
  • And... too: The waiters were slow and rude, and we had to pay a very expensive bill too.

Complete the Sentences

Link each sentence using one of the linking words: too, so, because, although, but

1. I want to keep fit. I go to the gym three times a week.

I want to keep fit so I go to the gym three times a week.

2. I'm saving money. I want to go to Italy for my next holiday.

I'm saving money because I want to go to Italy for my next holiday.

3. I usually finish work at 6 p.m. I sometimes need to do overtime.

Although I usually work at 6 p.m., I sometimes need to do overtime.

4. I usually take the train to work. It's quicker.

I usually take the train to work because it's quicker.

5. I'm allergic to seafood. I'm always very careful when I go out to eat.

I'm allergic to seafood so I'm always very careful when I go out to eat.

6. I'm keen on vegetables. I like fruit.

I'm keen on vegetables, and I like fruit too.

Three Restaurants to Choose From

You have decided to go out to eat. You have to choose from three restaurants:

  • The Blue Dolphin
  • Chequers
  • The Hungry Hound

Your Restaurant Preferences

What you prefer: The Blue Dolphin

  • Last time you were there, you had delicious food
  • The price was really good

What you want to avoid: The Hungry Hound

  • Last time you were there, the waiter was rude
  • The portions were very small

How will you make your case?

Use the linking words you learned to explain why The Blue Dolphin is the best choice and why you don't want to go to The Hungry Hound.

Restaurant Debate

You will discuss which restaurant to choose. Each of you has different preferences.

Time remaining: 3:00

Conversation Points

  • Which restaurant would you choose and why?
  • What was your experience at each restaurant?
  • What is most important to you in a restaurant?
  • Can you persuade the other person to agree with you?

Remember: Use the linking words (so, because, but, although, too) to make your arguments stronger. Use full sentences and explain your reasoning.
