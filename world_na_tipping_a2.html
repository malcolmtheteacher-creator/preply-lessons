Tipping: Generous or Obligatory?

In the USA and Canada, you tip 15 to 20 percent. Visitors find it strange. Why do people tip?

Key words

Read the sentence. What does the bold word mean? Then click Reveal.

1. He left a tip for the waiter.
tip (n) — extra money for good service. Example: “He left a tip for the waiter.”
2. The waiter brought the bill.
waiter (n) — a person who brings food in a restaurant. Example: “The waiter brought the bill.”
3. Please bring the bill.
bill (n) — the paper that shows how much you must pay. Example: “Please bring the bill.”
4. A server's wage can be low.
wage (n) — money for work. Example: “A server's wage can be low.”
5. The service was very good.
service (n) — help from a worker. Example: “The service was very good.”
6. A tip is 15 to 20 percent.
percent (n) — a part of 100. Example: “A tip is 15 to 20 percent.”

  • Which word is new for you? Say a sentence with it.
The stereotype: “Americans tip because they are generous.”

The story

Part 1 — Not really optional

In US and Canadian restaurants, people usually tip 15 to 20 percent. Tips are not a law, but they are a big part of a server's money. In the USA, the national cash wage for tipped workers can be as low as $2.13 an hour, if tips bring them up to the normal minimum wage.

Quick check:

1. How much is the usual tip?

2. What is the lowest national cash wage for tipped workers?

1. 15 to 20 percent.

2. $2.13 an hour.

Discuss:
  • Who should pay a worker's wage: the boss or the customer?

Part 2 — History

Tipping came to the USA from Europe in the 1800s. Many people did not like it. In the early 1900s, several US states made laws against tipping because they thought it was undemocratic. But the laws were hard to enforce, so the states ended them.

Quick check:

1. When did some states make laws against tipping?

2. Why did the states end the laws?

1. In the early 1900s.

2. They were hard to enforce.

Discuss:
  • Why can tipping be unfair?

Part 3 — Tip screens

Today, payment screens in cafés and shops ask for a tip of 18, 20 or 25 percent. Many customers feel pressure, so they press a button. Many Americans think tipping has gone too far. But the habit stays because workers need the money.

Quick check:

1. What do payment screens ask for?

2. Why does the habit stay?

1. A tip of 18, 20 or 25 percent.

2. Workers need the money.

Discuss:
  • Is a tip screen a good idea?

Grammar — because and so

because gives the reason: “People tip because servers need the money.”
so gives the result: “Servers need the money, so people tip.”
Use because + reason and so + result.

1. Choose the word

1. People tip servers need the money. (because / so)

2. Customers feel pressure, they press a button. (because / so)

3. Many people tip they do not want to seem rude. (because / so)

1. because

2. so

3. because

2. Make your own

2. The wage is low, so ______.

3. I did not tip because ______.
