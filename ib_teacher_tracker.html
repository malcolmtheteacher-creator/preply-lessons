Band Descriptor — what the response demonstrates
7–8 Insightful and convincing analysis and evaluation of the ways in which authorial choices shape meaning. Evaluation is nuanced, demonstrates critical awareness, and is fully supported by well-chosen references.
5–6 Appropriate and relevant analysis and evaluation of authorial choices. References support the evaluation. Effects on the reader are identified but may lack depth or range.
3–4 Some analysis with limited evaluation of authorial choices. The response describes choices and effects rather than analysing them. References are present but not always purposeful.
1–2 Mostly descriptive. Little or no analysis of authorial choices. Effects on the reader are not addressed or are asserted without evidence.
