⚖️ Quick Fix: Too vs Enough

Too much of something / not enough of something

What's wrong here?

This coffee is enough hot to drink.

enough hot — This is backwards!

The Rule

TOO comes BEFORE the adjective:

too + adjective/adverb

Examples: "too hot", "too cold", "too quickly"

ENOUGH comes AFTER the adjective:

adjective/adverb + enough

Examples: "hot enough", "cold enough", "quickly enough"

ENOUGH comes BEFORE the noun:

enough + noun

Examples: "enough time", "enough money", "enough coffee"

Correct version:

This coffee is hot enough to drink.

More examples of the error:

  • I am enough tall → I am tall enough
  • He is too much tired → He is too tired (no 'much' with adjectives)
  • There isn't time enough → There isn't enough time

Understanding the Pattern

TOO

😩

Before adjective

Means: more than wanted

too hot
too loud
too expensive

ENOUGH

✅

After adjective
Before noun

Means: sufficient

hot enough
enough time
enough money

Key Structures

too + adjective + TO-INFINITIVE: "This room is too cold to sleep in."

adjective + enough + TO-INFINITIVE: "This room is warm enough to sleep in."
