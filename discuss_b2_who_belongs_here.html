The Pattern

SPEAK: Look at these four arrivals. What do they have in common?
43 AD
Romans — brought roads, baths, wine, law. Left after 350 years. Britain kept everything.
400s
Anglo-Saxons — brought the English language itself. "English" = "Angle-ish."
800s
Vikings — gave us "sky", "egg", "window", "husband." Came as raiders, stayed as neighbours.
1066
Normans — French became the court language. Anglo-Saxon "pig" → French "pork" on the plate.
DISCUSS:
• Every wave arrived as outsiders. What happened next?
• At what point did they stop being "invaders" and become "British"?
• Is that still happening today?
Britain wasn't built by "the British." It was built by everyone who showed up.
