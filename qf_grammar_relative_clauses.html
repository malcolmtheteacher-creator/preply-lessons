👤 Who / Which / That in Relative Clauses

Master relative pronouns | B1 Level

Let's see what you notice...

Read this sentence. What feels wrong?

The man which called you is here.

What's the correction?

✓ Correction

The man who called you is here.

📌 The Rule

WHO = people (The man who called)

WHICH = things (The book which I read)

THAT = both (informal)

WHERE = places

WHOSE = possession

NEVER use "which" for people.

📚 The Rule (Dual Coding)

👤 WHO

👥

PEOPLE ONLY

The woman who lives here

Someone who can help

Students who study hard

Use for human subjects

📦 WHICH

🎁

THINGS ONLY

The car which is blue

A book which I read

Things which matter

Use for objects/ideas

⚡ THAT (Both People & Things)

THAT is informal and can refer to both people and things:

The man that called... (informal)

The book that I read... (informal)

Formal English prefers WHO for people and WHICH for things.

💡 Bonus Relative Pronouns:

WHERE = places (The city where I live)

WHOSE = possession (The student whose book is lost)
