🛏️ Camera ≠ Camera

A2 • Italian speakers • False friends: camera (room) ≠ camera (photo device)

🔍 Let's notice what we see

Look at this sentence. What's the problem?

"I went to my camera to sleep."

(The speaker wants to say they went to their bedroom, not that they got into a photo device!)

Does this sentence sound odd? Why?

⚠️ The False Friend:

The Italian word "camera" looks exactly like English "camera", but they mean DIFFERENT things!

In Italian: camera = room (camera da letto = bedroom)

In English: camera = device for taking photos/video 📷

To say the photo device in Italian: macchina fotografica (literally "photo machine")

🛠️ Understand the difference

📊 Italian camera vs English camera:

🛏️ Italian: CAMERA

  • Bedroom (camera da letto)
  • Living room (camera da soggiorno)
  • Any room in a house
  • A space/chamber

📷 English: CAMERA

  • Photo device
  • Video device
  • Digital camera
  • Takes pictures

🚨 How to say it in English

Common rooms in English:

camera → English: "room"
Example: "Vado in camera" = "I'm going to my room" ✓
NOT "I'm going to my camera" ✗
camera da letto → English: "bedroom"
Example: "La camera da letto è grande" = "The bedroom is large" ✓

🚀 Now you try — no help!

Choose the correct word: room or camera. Check your answers when you're done.

1. "My bedroom is in the upstairs _____."
Answer: "My bedroom is in the upstairs room."

→ This is referring to a physical space (location in the house).

2. "The photographer used a professional _____ to take wedding photos."
Answer: "The photographer used a professional camera to take wedding photos."

→ This is the device for taking photos/video.

3. "She cleaned every _____ in the house before the guests arrived."
Answer: "She cleaned every room in the house before the guests arrived."

→ This is about spaces/areas in a house.

4. "I'll bring my _____ to capture memories at the party."
Answer: "I'll bring my camera to capture memories at the party."

→ This is the device for taking photos.
