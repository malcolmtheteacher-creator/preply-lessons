B1 Focus Diagnostic — Present Perfect vs Past Simple

Welcome to The Timeline Judge. Your job is to decide, for each sentence, whether the action belongs to finished time or time connected to now.

Finished Time

Last week, in 2020, yesterday, when I was a child — times that are closed. Use past simple.

→

Connected to Now

Ever, never, just, already, yet, this week (not finished), since 2020, for three years. Use present perfect.
