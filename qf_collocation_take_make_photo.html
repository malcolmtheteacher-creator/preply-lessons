📸 Take a Photo (Not Make)

Master this essential collocation | A2 Level

Let's see what you notice...

Read this sentence. What's wrong with it?

Can you make a photo of me?

What's the correction?

✓ Correction

Can you take a photo of me?

📌 Why?

English uses TAKE with photos, pictures, and screenshots. Russian, German, and Polish use their word for "make" (делать, machen, robić), but English is different.

Other TAKE collocations: take a break, take a shower, take notes, take a taxi

Remember: This is a "false friend" for Romance and Slavic languages!
