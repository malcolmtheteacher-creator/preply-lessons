Essential Academic Words

These words appear frequently in academic texts across different subjects. Learning them helps you understand and write better.

Common Academic Verbs

  • Analyse: Examine something in detail to understand it
  • Evaluate: Judge the value or quality of something
  • Argue: Present reasons and evidence to support a point
  • Suggest: Propose an idea or possibility
  • Compare: Look at similarities and differences
  • Contrast: Focus on differences between things
  • Demonstrate: Show or prove something clearly
  • Investigate: Research carefully to learn facts

Common Academic Adjectives

  • Significant: Important or meaningful
  • Relevant: Connected to the topic being discussed
  • Substantial: Considerable in size or amount
  • Theoretical: Based on ideas rather than practical experience
  • Empirical: Based on observation and data
  • Fundamental: Basic or essential

Word Families and Collocations

Learning words in families helps you use them accurately in different forms. Collocations are words that often go together.

Evidence (noun) - The facts or information indicating something
Evident (adjective) - Clear or obvious
Evidently (adverb) - Clearly or obviously
Collocation: "provide evidence," "strong evidence," "evidence suggests"
Theory (noun) - An idea or set of ideas to explain something
Theoretical (adjective) - Based on theory rather than practice
Theoretically (adverb) - In theory; based on principles
Collocation: "according to theory," "in theory," "support a theory"
Method (noun) - A way of doing something
Methodical (adjective) - Done in an organized, careful way
Methodology (noun) - The system of methods used in research
Collocation: "research method," "effective method," "describe the methodology"

Vocabulary in Context

Choose the correct academic word for each sentence.

Question 1

The researcher used a qualitative to collect data from interviews. (method / evidence)

Answer: method. Qualitative research requires a method (way of doing something), not evidence (which is the result).

Question 2

To the effectiveness of the program, we need to compare results before and after. (analyse / evaluate)

Answer: evaluate. Evaluate means judge quality or success. Analyse is to examine in detail but doesn't focus on judging effectiveness.

Question 3

According to Darwin's , humans and primates share a common ancestor. (evidence / theory)

Answer: theory. A theory is an idea or set of ideas explaining something. Evolution is Darwin's theory.

Question 4

The study provides strong that exercise improves mental health. (evidence / relevant)

Answer: evidence. Evidence is the facts supporting a claim. "Relevant" is an adjective describing whether information is connected to the topic.
