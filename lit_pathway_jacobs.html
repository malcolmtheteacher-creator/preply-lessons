Meet the writer · B1

W. W. Jacobs

1863-1943 · England

A London writer of funny stories about sailors, and of one of the most frightening short stories ever written.

A life in a few steps

  1. 1863Born in Wapping, near the London docks. His father manages a wharf on the river Thames.
  2. 1879Starts work as a clerk in a government office.
  3. 1896His first book of stories, Many Cargoes, is a success.
  4. 1902'The Monkey's Paw' is published.
  5. 1943Dies aged 79.

Why people still read Jacobs

'The Monkey's Paw' turns one simple idea into a nightmare: be careful what you wish for. It has been copied in plays, films and TV shows for more than a hundred years.

Famous works

Did you know?

Jacobs grew up among sailors and dock workers, and they are the heroes of most of his funny stories.
