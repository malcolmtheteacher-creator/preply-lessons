Why Visit a Museum?

What makes a museum popular? Rate these in order of importance to you (1 = most important):

  • Modern space with good facilities for families and places to sit and rest
  • A good cafe or restaurant and interesting shop for souvenirs
  • Clear information on exhibits in multiple languages
  • Regular guided tours of the exhibits
  • Interactive exhibits

Functional Language for Proposals

These phrases help structure a proposal:

Stating the Purpose:

  • The purpose of this project is to...
  • The aim of this proposal is to outline...
  • This proposal has been written to demonstrate...

Introducing More Information:

  • Another idea would be to...
  • One possibility would be to...
  • It would be useful to...

Concluding:

  • All in all
  • On the whole
  • In conclusion, it appears that...

Linking Words for Contrast and Comparison

These words help connect sentences smoothly in a proposal:

In spite of being old, the museum still attracts a lot of visitors.

In spite of shows unexpected positive result despite a negative factor (age).

Even though the number of visitors has fallen, the museum has plans for future expansion.

Even though shows that something positive happens despite a negative situation.

As well as thinking it was cold and dull, people also thought it was boring.

As well as adds additional information; people had multiple negative opinions.

Whereas some people think it is the best museum in the city, others think it is the worst.

Whereas compares two very different or opposite opinions.

Despite opening a new wing, the number of visitors failed to increase.

Despite shows something negative happened even though an improvement was made.

Practice: Choosing the Right Link

What links these: "Some people are only interested in exhibits. Most people want a rounded experience."

Although some people are only interested in exhibits, most people want a rounded experience.

What links: "Older people prefer cafe and shop. Younger people prefer multimedia displays."

On the other hand connects contrasting preferences: Older people prefer cafe and shop. On the other hand, younger people prefer multimedia displays.

What links: "Publicity is important. The museum should be advertised on the internet."

For this reason shows cause and effect: Publicity is important. For this reason the museum should be advertised on the internet.

Your Proposal Task

Your local council has invited proposals for ideas on how to adapt an empty warehouse in your city to a cultural centre for the community.

What to Include

Think about cultural education and performance space. Consider:

  • Would you include dance, theatre, music, exhibitions?
  • What other features would make it popular with the community?
  • What facilities would visitors need (cafe, shop, rest areas)?
  • How would you attract different age groups?

Proposal Structure

Introduction: State the purpose of the project
Background: Explain why this centre is needed
Recommendations: Describe what the centre will include
Conclusion: Summarise the benefits to the community

Success Criteria for Your Proposal

When you write, make sure you:

  • Include relevant and appropriate information and recommendations
  • Organise the proposal into sections with appropriate headings
  • Write in an appropriately formal and neutral style
  • Use a good range of vocabulary and structures
  • Use a variety of linking phrases to connect ideas
