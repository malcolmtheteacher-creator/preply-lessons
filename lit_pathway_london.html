A life in a few steps

  1. 1876Born in San Francisco to a poor family.
  2. 1890sWorks in a factory, sails on a ship and travels the country as a hobo, jumping onto trains.
  3. 1897Joins the gold rush to the Klondike in the far north of Canada.
  4. 1903The Call of the Wild is a huge success.
  5. 1908'To Build a Fire'.
  6. 1916Dies aged 40.

Why people still read London

He knew hard physical life first-hand. His stories show humans and animals fighting to survive against nature.
