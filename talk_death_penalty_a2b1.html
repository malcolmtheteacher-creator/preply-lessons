Something to think about

In some countries, the worst crimes can lead to the death penalty. Many other countries have stopped using it. Some people say it is a fair punishment for terrible crimes. Other people say it is wrong, because judges can make mistakes and nobody can change a death.

Two opinions

Read both. Which opinion is stronger for you? Why?

Some people say…

Some crimes are terrible, so the punishment must be very hard. It can stop other people from doing the same thing, and families feel that justice is done.

Other people say…

Judges can make mistakes, and nobody can change a death. The state should not decide who lives and who dies.
