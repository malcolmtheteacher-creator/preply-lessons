📚 The PAO System Explained

🎯 PAO = The Complete System

PAO stands for Person - Action - Object. This is what world champions use!

  • Person = Who is doing something?
  • Action = What are they doing?
  • Object = What are they using/interacting with?

🎬 How PAO Works

Each 2-digit number gets a PERSON, their ACTION, and an OBJECT:

Example: 42

Person: David Beckham

Action: Kicking

Object: Soccer ball

Image: I see David Beckham KICKING a SOCCER BALL!

Why 3 components? The OBJECT helps you connect to the NEXT number in long sequences!

💡 The POWER of PAO: Combining Components

Each 2-digit number (00-99) has its OWN Person, Action, AND Object. The magic happens when you MIX components from different numbers!

Your PAO List (examples):

31 = Michael Jackson | Moonwalking | Glove

41 = Donald Duck | Quacking | Sailor hat

59 = Elvis Presley | Singing | Microphone
