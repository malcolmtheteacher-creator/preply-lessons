Role Play: Practice Speaking

Use these scenarios to practise making requests. Speak out loud!

Role Play 1: At Work

The Situation:

You want to ask for more flexible hours because you need to do something important in the mornings.

What are your reasons?

How will you phrase your request? (Write or speak it out loud)

What if the answer is no? What will you say?

Role Play 2: Everyday Life

The Situation:

You're at a restaurant. Your food hasn't arrived after 30 minutes. You want to politely ask about it, and then ask if you can change your order.

First, how will you politely ask about your food?

Then, how will you ask to change your order?

What will you say if they refuse to change it?

Key Phrases to Remember

Making Requests: "Could you...?", "Would you mind...?", "I was wondering if..."

Asking Permission: "Can I...?", "Could I...?", "Would it be possible to...?"

Saying Yes: "Of course!", "Certainly.", "I'd be happy to help."

Saying No Politely: "I'm sorry, but...", "I'm afraid not.", "Maybe another time?"
