Interview with a Musician

Identify the past tense verbs in these sentences from a musician interview. Click on the highlighted words to check.

Sentence 1

I started singing when I was very young when I joined a music academy.

Past tense verbs: started (start), was (be), joined (join)

Sentence 2

How old were you?

Past tense verb: were (be)

Sentence 3

Was anyone else in your family musical?

Past tense verb: was (be)

Sentence 4

We took turns playing.

Past tense verb: took (take)

Sentence 5

They thought it was just a phase.

Past tense verbs: thought (think), was (be)

Sentence 6

He gave me his card and told me.

Past tense verbs: gave (give), told (tell)

Sentence 7

How did the tour go?

Past tense verb: did (do)

Past Simple Question Formation

Remember: Use 'did' + base verb (don't change the verb to past tense).

Unscramble the Questions

Words: When / start / playing the drums?

When did you start playing the drums?

Words: Go to concerts / when / younger?

Did you go to concerts when you were younger?

Words: When / first / perform on stage?

When did you first perform on stage?

Words: How long / your first single / at the top of the charts?

How long was your first single at the top of the charts?

Pronunciation Note

Listen to weak forms:

"I started playing guitar when I was twelve"
/aɪ ˈstɑːtəd ˈpleɪɪŋ ɡɪˈtɑːr wenəwəz twelv/

Notice how "when I was" becomes one flowing sound: /wenəwəz/

Role-Play Interview

Choose a role and practice asking or answering questions about becoming a musician.

Role 1: Interview a Famous Musician

Plan questions about:

  • Childhood and family background
  • Schooling and music education
  • When they started playing music
  • Musical influences and inspiration
  • Training and practice
  • How they became famous

Role 2: Be the Musician

Plan answers about:

  • Your childhood and family
  • Your music education
  • When and why you started
  • Who inspired you
  • Your training journey
  • Your path to success

Instructions: Have a conversation for 3 minutes in one role. Then swap roles and do it again. Use past simple questions and answers.

Reflection & Discussion

Think about these questions:

Question 1:
Can you play an instrument? If yes, when did you start? If no, would you like to learn?
Question 2:
Have you ever been to a concert? Tell me about it. When was it? Which musician or band?
Question 3:
How has your music taste changed over time? What did you listen to as a child? What do you listen to now?
Question 4:
Why do you think music is important to culture and society?
Question 5:
If you could be any musician in the world, who would you be and why?
