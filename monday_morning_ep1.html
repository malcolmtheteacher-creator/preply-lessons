🧠 Surprising Facts

Guess the answer before you click! These facts are strange but true.

Fact 1: How many hours does the average person spend in meetings every week?

Take a guess... 5 hours? 10 hours? 15 hours?

👀 Click to see the answer
15 hours per week! (for managers)
That is almost 2 full working days just sitting in meetings.

💬 Discussion: Does this sound like your week? Are most meetings useful?

Fact 2: What percentage of people check their phone within 5 minutes of waking up?

30%? 50%? 80%?

👀 Click to see the answer
80% of people check their phone within 5 minutes of opening their eyes!

💬 Discussion: Are you one of the 80%? Is this a good or bad habit?

Fact 3: Which day of the week are people most productive?

Monday? Tuesday? Wednesday? Thursday?

👀 Click to see the answer
Tuesday! Studies show people do their best work on Tuesday morning.
Monday is for "warming up" and Friday is for "winding down."

💬 Discussion: Do you agree? When do YOU do your best work?

Fact 4: How long can the average person focus without getting distracted?

5 minutes? 15 minutes? 45 minutes? 90 minutes?

👀 Click to see the answer
Only about 8 seconds! (shorter than a goldfish, which is 9 seconds)
But with practice, you can focus for 90-minute blocks.

💬 Discussion: What distracts you most when you are working?

🤷 Two Truths and a Lie

This is a classic game! Here is how it works:

The Rules

1. You tell me THREE things about yourself

2. TWO things are TRUE

3. ONE thing is a LIE

4. I try to guess which one is the lie!

Then we swap - I tell you three things and you guess!

Need Ideas? Try These Topics:

Travel

Places you have been

Food

Strange things you have eaten

Childhood

Things from when you were young

Skills

Unusual things you can do

Famous People

Anyone famous you have met

Fears

Things you are scared of

Example

"Okay, here are my three things:

1. I have been to 15 different countries.

2. I can play the piano.

3. I have never broken a bone in my body."

Useful phrases:

"I think number [X] is the lie because..."

"That sounds too [strange/normal] to be true..."

"Are you serious? Really?"
