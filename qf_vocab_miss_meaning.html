🎯 Miss — Multiple Meanings

Fail to attend vs feel sad about absence | A2 Level

Let's see what you notice...

Read this sentence. What feels wrong?

I missed the bus but I miss my family.

What's the correction?

✓ Correction

Both correct! Miss = fail to catch/attend OR feel sad about absence.

📌 The Rule

Miss has several meanings: 1) Fail to catch (miss the bus). 2) Not attend (miss class). 3) Feel sad about absence (miss my family). 4) Not hit (miss the target).

Spanish 'perder/echar de menos', Italian 'perdere/mancare', French 'rater/manquer' use different verbs. English uses 'miss' for all.
